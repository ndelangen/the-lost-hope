# Original illustration prompts

## Style for new creature portraits

Use the campaign event illustrations for lighting and paint treatment. The Lake Serpent Disrupts the Crossing is a reference for cool light on creatures. The Party Plays Three Rounds with the Fiddler is a reference for warm light on cloth, wood and metal. The Party Rescues Roberto from a Faceless Shadow is a reference for outdoor light and material texture.

Paint the creature in full color with clear directional light and deep, readable shadows. Keep the square format, a single complete silhouette and blank margins. Use the shop illustrations for the muted gray-tan paper color. Review each portrait at thumbnail size and retain credited source art in its gallery.
