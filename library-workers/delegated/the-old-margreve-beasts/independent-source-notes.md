## Alligator — p. 215

Medium Beast, CR 1/4, AC 13 natural, HP 14, speed 20 ft. and swim 30 ft., Perception 10, Stealth 12, resistant to grappled. Hold Breath lasts 15 minutes. Bite is +4 at reach 5 ft. for 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing, with grapple escape DC 12 and restraint during the grapple. The alligator cannot bite another target while it holds that grapple. High Walk is a bonus-action Dash, unavailable while swimming. The lore describes an aquatic ambush predator with broad jaws. The nearby reach 10 ft. slashing Bite belongs to the turtle.

## Alligator Turtle — p. 215

Medium Beast, CR 1/4, AC 15 natural, HP 12, speed 20 ft. and swim 30 ft., Perception 11, Stealth 13, resistant to prone. Its motionless open-mouth pose underwater resembles a rock with a worm, using its tongue as the lure. Hold Breath lasts 1 hour. Bite is +4 at reach 10 ft. for 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing, with grapple escape DC 12. It can only bite the held target until the grapple ends. The Spiky Shell reaction triggers on a hit by a visible attacker within 5 ft., reducing damage by 5 (2d4) and dealing 2 (1d4) piercing to the attacker. The book describes a long flexible neck and beak-like jaws.

## Baboon, Howler — p. 232

Book heading `Howler Baboon`. Medium Beast, CR 1/4, AC 12, HP 15, speed 30 ft. and climb 30 ft., Perception 13, Stealth 12, resistant to deafened. Pack Tactics requires a non-incapacitated ally within 5 ft. of the target. Fist and Rock are each +4 for 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning. Fist reach is 5 ft. and Rock uses ranged distances 20/60 ft., though the extracted line labels it “reach.” Challenging Howl is a bonus action against a visible creature within 30 ft. that makes a DC 12 CHA save. On a failure, the baboon chooses disadvantage on the target’s next attack roll or requires its next attack to target the baboon. Both options expire at the start of the baboon’s next turn. The lore describes territorial troops in forest and hills.

## Bat, Strossen — p. 239

Book heading `Strossen Bat`. Small Beast, CR 1/4, AC 13, HP 14, speed 5 ft. and fly 40 ft. Echolocation disables keensense while deafened. Heightened Hearing sets Perception to 17 for hearing. Bite is +5 at reach 5 ft. for 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing, then attaches. The attached bat cannot attack. Its target loses 5 (1d4 + 3) HP at the start of each of the bat’s turns. The bat moves with the target and can detach for 5 ft. of movement. It detaches after draining 15 HP or when the target dies. A creature can use its action to remove it. Haunting Screech is a bonus action, one target within 10 ft. that can hear it, DC 13 WIS or frightened through the end of that target’s next turn. The mirror says “attached bag” once in this block. The surrounding sentences identify the bat. It has black fur and a white streak across the eyes. The lore traces it from Castle Shadowcrag through the wider Margreve Forest.

## Beetle, Giant Bombardier — p. 226

The prose heading is `Giant Bombardier Beetle` and the stat block is `Bombardier Beetle`. Medium Beast, CR 1/4, AC 14 natural, HP 12, speed 30 ft., Perception 10, Stealth 12, vulnerable to frightened, resistant to fire, keensense 30 ft. Forest Camouflage raises Stealth to 17 in forests. Bite is +4 at reach 5 ft. for 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing. Spray recharges on 5–6 and targets one visible creature within 15 ft. for 5 (2d4) fire on a failed DC 12 DEX save, half on success. At 7 HP or fewer, taking damage lets it recharge and immediately use Spray as a reaction. The lore describes a forest-floor herbivore that uses boiling spray to escape predators.

## Bull — Appendix

Large Beast, CR 1/2, AC 11, HP 25, speed 40 ft., Perception 9, Stealth 11. Charge needs 20 ft. of straight movement followed by a Gore hit that turn, adds 3 (1d6) piercing, and forces creatures to make a DC 13 STR save or fall prone. The excerpt I could retrieve stops before the Gore damage line and page footer. The adjacent Acid-Coated Tendril belongs to the Bulbous Violet. The bull is a bulky horned quadruped. In *Hollow*, a magical song drives a prized bull into a frenzy.

## Forest Falcon — p. 226

Medium Beast, CR 1/2, AC 13, HP 25, speed 20 ft. and fly 60 ft., Perception 14, Stealth 13, vulnerable to blinded. Heightened Sight sets Perception to 19 for sight. Beak is +5 at reach 5 ft. for 8 (2d4 + 3) piercing. The extraction places the Beak and Heightened Sight lines above the Forest Falcon heading, beside Shadow Fey Guardian rules. The falcon hunts under dense forest canopy and dives from a high perch.
