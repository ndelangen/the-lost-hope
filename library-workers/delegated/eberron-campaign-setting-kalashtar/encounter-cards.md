# Kalashtar encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. They are Medium humanoids with 30-ft. land speed. Under *Expanded Psionics Handbook*, their racial psi-like ability gives them the Psionic subtype. Both gain +2 racial on saves against mind-affecting spells and abilities and against possession. Both sleep and are immune to *dream*, *nightmare*, and effects that rely on their ability to dream. ## Seravakri, witness mediator

Telepath Psion 1. Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 11, touch 11, flat-footed 10. HP 5, initiative +1. Fort +1, Ref +1, Will +3. BAB +0, grapple −1. STR 8, DEX 13, CON 12, INT 16, WIS 12, CHA 13. Common, Quor, Draconic, Riedran.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Light crossbow | +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment |
| Dagger | −1 melee, 1d4−1 piercing or slashing, minimum 1, 19–20/×2 |

Bluff +7, Concentration +5, Diplomacy +7, Knowledge (psionics) +7, Listen +3, Psicraft +7, Spot +3. Listen and Spot include Alertness.

Manifester level 1. Six power points per day, two base, one from Intelligence, one racial, and two from Psionic Talent. Spend one point at most on a manifestation. The racial use of *mindlink* is separate. [3.5 SRD, Psion](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/psionic/classes/psion.htm) · [Psionic Talent](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/psionic/psionicFeats.htm#psionicTalent)

| Known power | 1-point effect |
| --- | --- |
| *Inertial armor* | AC 15 and flat-footed 14 for 1 hour |
| *Mind thrust* | 1d10 to one creature within 25 ft., mind-affecting, Will DC 14 negates, power resistance applies |
| *Mindlink* | Use the psionics version below. Spend 1 point when manifesting it as a known power. |

Seravakri asks each witness for permission before opening a link. She can compare the accounts while the party checks the warehouse ledger. If threatened, she puts up *inertial armor* and withdraws to the public room.

## Tarenkhad, quay interpreter

Expert 1. Provisional CR 1/2, ECL 1. AC 13, touch 11, flat-footed 12. HP 7, initiative +1. Fort +1, Ref +1, Will +4. BAB +0, grapple +0. STR 10, DEX 13, CON 12, INT 12, WIS 14, CHA 14. Common, Quor, Riedran.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Club | +0 melee, 1d6 bludgeoning, ×2, 5-ft. reach |
| Light crossbow | +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment |

Appraise +5, Bluff +8, Decipher Script +5, Diplomacy +11, Disguise +6 or +8 to impersonate a human, Knowledge (local) +5, Sense Motive +6. Diplomacy includes Skill Focus.

Naturally Psionic gives him one power point. His daily racial *mindlink* leaves the reserve intact. He saves the link for a willing passenger the dock crew cannot understand and asks the party to hold the berth while he traces the missing luggage.

## Racial mindlink, once per day each

| Rules in use | At these NPCs’ level |
| --- | --- |
| *Expanded Psionics Handbook* | Standard action, effective manifester level 1. One willing creature with Intelligence 3 or higher within 25 ft. Duration 10 minutes. No save for the willing target. Power resistance applies as harmless. |
| Setting book fallback | One willing creature with Intelligence 3 or higher within 30 ft. Duration one round. No saving throw or spell resistance. |

Both are mind-affecting. The resulting link works over any distance on the same plane and carries communication without a shared language.
