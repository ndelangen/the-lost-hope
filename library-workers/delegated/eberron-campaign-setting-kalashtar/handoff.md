# Kalashtar for D&D 3.5

One playable Kalashtar version from the June 2004 first printing of *Eberron Campaign Setting*. Naturally Psionic adds one power point per character level. Racial *mindlink* is once per day. With *Expanded Psionics Handbook*, its effective manifester level is half the Kalashtar’s Hit Dice, minimum 1. The setting book also prints a fallback with 30-ft. initial range and one round per character level. [Original traits, pp. 17–18]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Seravakri is an original Telepath Psion 1 at provisional CR 1. Her card shows six power points, a one-point cap per manifestation, and her separate daily racial *mindlink*. Tarenkhad is an original Expert 1 at provisional CR 1/2. He has one racial power point and a daily racial *mindlink*. His build has no class manifesting.
