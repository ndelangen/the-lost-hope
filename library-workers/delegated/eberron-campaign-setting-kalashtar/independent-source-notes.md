# Independent Kalashtar reviews

The source reviewer checked the June 2004 first printing. Beet drew the Kalashtar on p. 18. The description on p. 17 gives a humanlike, slightly taller build and angular features. The traits span pp. 17–18. The February 2006 official errata does not change them. [Original appearance]() · [Wizards art gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20161101150242/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/eb/20040618a) · [Official errata]()

The racial *mindlink* in this printing uses half Hit Dice, minimum manifester level 1. The two rules modes have distinct range and duration. The 3.5 FAQ distinguishes the racial ability’s effective level from actual manifester levels. *Expanded Psionics Handbook* explains the shared power point reserve on p. 17, *mindlink* on pp. 120–121, and the subtype and psi-like rules on p. 184. [Original traits and fallback]() · [Expanded Psionics Handbook]() · [Wizards FAQ archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The original book presents Halharan, Psion 4, in a narrative example on p. 40. Later *Races of Eberron* gives separate full encounter stat blocks on pp. 69–70. Its full-HD *mindlink* on p. 125 belongs to an optional Telepath substitution. [Halharan]() · [Later source]()

The second reviewer checked Seravakri’s 20 skill points, three known powers, six-point reserve, and one-point spending cap. Tarenkhad uses 28 skill points and one racial point. Exact publication matching keeps both builds on the 2004 profile. The reviewer also confirmed that publication sorting will make the staged 2004 profile and its portrait the default unless integration pins the current preferred version.
