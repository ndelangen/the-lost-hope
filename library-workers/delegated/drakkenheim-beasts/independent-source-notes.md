# Dungeons of Drakkenheim — Big Linda

The original English *Dungeons of Drakkenheim* gives Big Linda as a variant in Chapter 8, printed p. 163. I visually checked the v1.1 book mirror at viewer p. 167 and compared the text with two independent extracts. Ghostfire labels the book as 5E 2014. The existing *Monsters of Drakkenheim* version belongs to a separate 5.5E book. [Ghostfire 2014 product](https://ghostfiregaming.com/product/dungeons-of-drakkenheim-book/) · [Original page image](https://online.anyflip.com/wuzfu/tkrx/files/large/83da799e4554cb327ae575969f9fd9e7.webp) · [Original text]()

The encounter directs the reader to use the 2014 Tyrannosaurus Rex statistics with CR increased to 9, acid immunity and blindsight 30 ft. Acid Spew recharges on 5–6. Each creature in its 30 ft. cone makes a DC 15 DEX save for 49 (11d8) acid damage on failure or half damage on success. It does not replace an existing attack. The original encounter gives no regeneration or rejuvenation trait.

The 2014 baseline is a Huge unaligned Beast with AC 13 (natural armor), HP 136 (13d12 + 52), walking speed 50 ft., and Perception +4 (passive 14). Multiattack makes one Bite and one Tail against different targets. Both are +10 and have reach 10 ft. Bite deals 33 (4d12 + 7) piercing. A Medium or smaller creature it hits is grappled with escape DC 17 and restrained during the grapple. While holding that target, Big Linda cannot bite another. Tail deals 20 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning. Keep the inherited attack bonuses and grapple DC, as the encounter states no changes to them. [Wizards SRD 5.1, viewer p. 279]()

Her bone-strewn den adjoins the Court of Thieves arena, where she fights as the Queen of Thieves’ champion. The Queen has permanently charmed her, usually controls her with spoken commands, and calls her out with a whistle. The original describes knobbly flesh, shimmering back scales, useless forearms, sharp teeth and a spined tail on a wingless, dragon-like body. The illustration beside the entry depicts a thief.
