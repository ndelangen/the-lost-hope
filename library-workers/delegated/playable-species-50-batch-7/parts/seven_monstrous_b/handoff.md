# Basilisk, Behir, Beholder and Blight

These are Tyler Kamstra's player conversions from *Monstrous Races*, published on DMs Guild in 2018 for the 2014 fifth-edition rules. I used the official preview for Basilisk and Behir and the v2 text mirror for the folios beyond the preview. Each NPC uses the published player traits and a 2014 class. [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [Official preview]() · [V2 text mirror]()

| Profile | Printed folios | NPC and manual calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basilisk | Official preview p. 19 | Dapplecairn, Barbarian 2. AC 17 = 15 + 2 Dexterity. HP 25 = 12 + 3 + 7 + 3. Bite +5 = 3 Strength + 2 proficiency. Gaze DC 13 = 8 + 3 Constitution + 2 proficiency. |
| Behir | Official preview p. 20 | Serrask, Ranger 2. AC 15 = 13 + 2 Dexterity. HP 20 = 10 + 2 + 6 + 2. Bite +4 = 2 Strength + 2 proficiency. Breath DC 12. Ranger spell DC 11. |
| Beholder | V2 pp. 21–22 | Tallyglass, Spectator Monk 1. AC 14 by either unarmored formula. HP 9 = 8 + 1 Constitution. Bite +4 through Dexterity and Martial Arts. Chill touch +4 at this table. |
| Blight | V2 pp. 23–24 | Hedgewrit, Vine Blight Fighter 2. AC 14 = 12 + 2 Dexterity. HP 20 = 10 + 2 + 6 + 2. Quarterstaff +5 to hit with 1d6+5 from Strength and Dueling. Entangling Plants DC 12. |

The published Basilisk preview makes averting eyes a reaction for a creature that is not surprised. I used its six-stage exhaustion track and one-stage-per-round recovery after the first successful save. The Behir preview scales Lightning Breath at levels 5, 11 and 17. The v2 mirror differs on both points. Lightning resistance appears in design commentary and the trait box omits it. [Official preview]()

The Beholder profile keeps Eye Tyrant, Death Tyrant and Spectator together. Tallyglass uses Intelligence and eyestalk motions for the racial cantrips. The printed Eye Rays describe focus use but leave the ability and somatic method open. The Blight profile keeps Needle, Twig and Vine together. Hedgewrit uses the printed 25-ft. Vine Blight speed and rolls the initial Entangling Plants save because the Plant exemption applies to difficult terrain.
