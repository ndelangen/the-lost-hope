# Monstrous Races player conversions

Four profiles and linked NPCs use Tyler Kamstra's 2018 Dungeon Masters Guild supplement for the 2014 fifth-edition rules. The official product preview covers all four. It gives no revision number, so the evidence calls it the official preview. The page numbers below are the printed folios. Preview links start one viewer page earlier. [Official preview]() · [Publication listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races)

| Profile | Printed pages | Worked build |
|---|---|---|
| Aboleth | 9–10 | Orzhal Lowwater, Evocation Wizard 3. AC 13 from 12 + 1 Dex or 14 with Mage Armor. HP 20 from 8 + 6 + 6. Spell DC 13 and attack +5 from Int +3 and proficiency +2. |
| Animated Object | 11–14 | Brass Sentry, Animated Armor Fighter 2. AC 20 from fixed AC 18 and a shield. HP 20 from 12 + 8. The sword uses +4 to hit and deals 1d8+4 with Dueling. The Flying Sword and Rug of Smothering stay in this profile. |
| Ankheg | 14–15 | Flintjaw, Barbarian 1. AC 13 from either natural armor or Unarmored Defense. HP 14 from 12 + 2 Con. Bite +4 to hit uses Str +2 and proficiency +2. Acid Spray DC 12 uses Con +2 and proficiency +2. |
| Banshee | 17–18 | Maelin Reednote, flying Banshee Bard 1. AC 13 from leather and Dex +2. HP 9 from 8 + 1 Con. Wail DC 13 and Corrupting Touch +5 use Cha +3 and proficiency +2. The corpse-body variant stays in this profile. |

The official preview gives Flying Sword AC 15 + Dex and increases Ankheg Acid Spray damage at levels 5, 11, and 17. A mirror labeled v2 gives AC 17 + Dex and levels 6, 11, and 16. The profiles use the official preview's values. [Official preview, Animated Object]() · [Official preview, Ankheg]() · [v2 mirror]()

The builds use a grasping tentacle for Orzhal's spell components and 5-ft. reach for Maelin's Corrupting Touch. Flintjaw applies the Strength modifier and Rage bonus to the slashing part of the bite. The Sentry and Maelin heal by resting. The supplement proposes optional repair spells for constructs and undead, and neither NPC uses them.
