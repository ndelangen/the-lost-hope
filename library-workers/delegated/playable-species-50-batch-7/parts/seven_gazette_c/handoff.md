# Gate Pass Gazette profiles

Five original Level Up heritages from EN Publishing. Each heritage has one encounter NPC with its own gift, culture, background, and devotion. Subtypes and paragon options stay in the parent heritage. The source records identify the original Gazette issue and link to the supporting rules.

| Heritage | NPC and gift | Manual arithmetic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pantheran | Tavi Screefoot, Wildkin Rogue 1 | Padded leather 12 + 3 Dex gives AC 15. HP 8 + 2 Con = 10. Finesse claws and sling hit at +5. Initiative adds the heritage expertise die. |
| Pode | Sounding-at-First-Light, Mature Fighter 1 | Hide armor 13 + 1 Dex + 2 shield gives AC 16. HP 10 + 2 Con = 12. Club hits at +5. Ink Cloud, twice per long rest, uses 8 + 2 Con + 2 proficiency for DC 12. |
| Shadowcast | Nera Chalkthread, Shadow Walker Rogue 1 | Padded leather 12 + 3 Dex gives AC 15. HP 8 + 2 Con = 10. Rapier hits at +5. Teleport twice per long rest and keep the human counterpart's Fast Learner and Intrepid base traits. |
| Spiderfolk | Rell Threadwick, Spiderling Fighter 1 | Chitin 12 + 3 Dex + 2 shield gives AC 17. HP 10 + 2 Con = 12. Shortsword hits at +5 for 1d6+5 with Dueling. Only the primary arms hold the shield and sword. |
| Zevite | Selka Amberwake, Hopeful Foresight Rogue 1 | Padded leather 12 + 2 Dex gives AC 14. HP 8 + 1 Con = 9. Shortsword hits at +4. The hopeful markings give a Persuasion expertise die and impose disadvantage on Insight. |

The Spiderfolk page has a stray Zevite sentence above the correct trait paragraph. Its Spinner-Touched duration says proficiency modifier. I read that as proficiency bonus and recorded it in the profile. Rell uses Spiderling. The Pode's knockdown applies to the land ink, where the rules describe difficult terrain. Selka reads the Zevite text's expertise as an A5E expertise die. [Spiderfolk](https://a5e.tools/rules/spiderfolk) · [Pode](https://a5e.tools/rules/pode) · [Zevite](https://a5e.tools/rules/zevite)
