# Crawling Claw, Cyclops, Darkmantle, and Demilich

These profiles use Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races*, a DMs Guild player conversion for the 2014 fifth edition. I read the v2 traits and used the 2014 Basic Rules for class levels, equipment, combat, and spellcasting. Every build links to the exact species, publication, and edition keys in the roster. [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [v2 text]() · [2014 Basic Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014)

| Profile and v2 pages | Original NPC | Manual build check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crawling Claw, pp. 36–37 | Pell Stitchfinger, Fighter 2 | Tiny studded leather, Dexterity, and Defense give AC 16. HP 20 comes from 12 at first level and 8 at second. A Strength-based claw hits at +4. |
| Cyclops, pp. 37–38 | Ora Cairnstep, Barbarian 2 | Printed natural armor gives AC 16. HP 25 comes from 15 at first level and 10 at second. A greatclub hits at +5 and deals 1d8+3, or 1d8+5 with Rage. |
| Darkmantle, pp. 38–39 | Lint Bellows, Fighter 2 | Light leather, Dexterity, and Defense give AC 14. HP 20 comes from 12 at first level and 8 at second. Strength and proficiency give Crush +5 and Athletics +5. |
| Demilich, pp. 41–42 | Aster Siltglass, Sorcerer 5 | Draconic Resilience gives AC 15 and adds 5 HP across five levels for 37 total. Charisma +4 and proficiency +3 make the spell attack +7 and save DC 15. The level-five slot counts are 4, 3, and 2. |

Pell applies the quarter-weight armor and carry rules on p. 8. The claw attacks unarmed and uses tools only on a stationary turn. Both undead builds use the healing rules on p. 7 and can recover through rest. The book presents repair spells as an optional addition.

Lint uses the 30-ft. walking speed in the player traits. The design notes reference the encounter creature's 10-ft. movement. Crush permits a tentacle grapple after the unarmed hit. Extra reach, larger targets, and freedom from the usual grapple contest would take a separate rule.

Aster uses the Small size in the player traits. The design notes mention Tiny. Aster can speak spell words, make anatomy-appropriate somatic movements, and use the skull as a focus for materials without a price or consumption requirement. The spells in the build fit those limits. The phylactery follows the printed 1d10-day return and loss of location and travel memory. The copper tally sealed in a silted bell is this NPC's artifact.
