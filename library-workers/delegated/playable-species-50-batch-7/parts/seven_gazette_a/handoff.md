# Gazette heritages, first group

Five profiles use EN Publishing's original Gazette heritage rules, with one first-level NPC each. I checked the heritage gifts, backgrounds, cultures, destinies, equipment, and attacks against the publisher's rules. The examples use A5E class advancement and the standard ability array with background increases.

| Heritage | NPC | Manual calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birdfolk | Tern Flintpin, Rogue 1 | AC 15 from padded leather 12 + Dex 3. HP 9 from 8 + Con 1. Dagger and finesse talons attack at +5. Aspect of the Hunt is the original issue #5 gift. |
| Cervid | Alder Tine, Herald 1 | AC 14 from leather brigandine 13 + Dex 1. HP 12 from 10 + Con 2. Spear and piercing antlers attack at +5. Charisma gives DC 12 and two prepared first-level spells. |
| Chrysalian | Luma Mothreed, Healer Cleric 1 | AC 15 from 10 + Dex 2 + Wis 3. HP 10 from 8 + Con 2. Wings carry 20 lb. and allow two consecutive rounds of hovering before fatigue. Healing Touch restores 1 hp independently of spell healing. |
| Constructed | Rivet Reedgate, Rogue 1 | AC 15 from padded leather 12 + Dex 3. HP 9 from 8 + Con 1. Boot dagger attacks at +5. Fire planarite and the Service Frame apply. |
| Dreamborn | Vesper Sprig, Druid 1 | AC 15 from Nature Skin 13 + Dex 2. HP 10 from 8 + Con 2. Shillelagh attacks at +5 for 1d8+3. Spell save DC is 13. |

The publisher's Birdfolk page also prints later Flightless and Mystic variants. This profile uses the initial issue #5 gifts and paragon only. The Constructed page specifies healing and fuel rules but supplies no creature type override. Rivet uses the ordinary Humanoid type for the worked encounter. The source record also documents the reading used for the malformed Social Grace sentence. [Birdfolk](https://a5e.tools/rules/birdfolk) · [Constructed](https://a5e.tools/rules/constructed)

The five 1254 × 1254 originals were made with built-in ImageGen. Big Linda was the paint and paper reference. I checked the full-size images and 96 px previews for legibility and plain parchment. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json)
