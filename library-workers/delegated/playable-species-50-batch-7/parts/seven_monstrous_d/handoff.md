# Monstrous Races player conversions

Chuul, Cloaker, Cockatrice and Couatl each have one playable profile, one linked NPC and one square portrait. The profiles use Tyler Kamstra's 2018 DMs Guild supplement for the 2014 fifth-edition rules. I read the v2 player trait text and kept the roster's exact publication and edition keys. [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [Author's publication history](https://rpgbot.net/announcing-monstrous-species-for-dnd-5e/) · [v2 text]()

## Manual rules check

| Profile | Source | NPC and arithmetic |
|---|---|---|
| Chuul | p. 32, Chuul Traits | Keth of the Sill, Barbarian 2. Natural AC 16 = 14 + 2 Dex. HP 25 = 12 + 3 Con at 1st level and 7 + 3 at 2nd. Pincer +5 and Athletics +5 each use +3 Str and +2 proficiency. Rage adds 2 to pincer damage. |
| Cloaker | p. 33, Cloaker Traits, and p. 7 for natural weapons | Hush-in-the-Fold, Monk 1. Natural AC 16 = 13 + 3 Dex. HP 9 = 8 + 1 Con. Martial Arts gives the bite +5 to hit and 1d8+3 damage. The bonus Martial Arts attack is a conventional body impact for 1d4+3. |
| Cockatrice | p. 34, Cockatrice Traits | Tack, Rogue 2. AC 13 = 10 + 3 Dex. HP 17 = 8 + 2 Con at 1st level and 5 + 2 at 2nd. Finesse bite +5, Sneak Attack 1d6, Petrifying Bite DC 12 = 8 + 2 Con + 2 proficiency. |
| Couatl | p. 35, Couatl Traits | Iriam of the Second Dawn, Life Cleric 1. Natural AC 15 = 13 + 2 Dex. HP 9 = 8 + 1 Con. Spell DC 13 = 8 + 3 Wis + 2 proficiency. Healing Word restores 1d4 + 3 Wis + 3 Disciple of Life. |

For Chuul and Cloaker I followed the printed trait lists where the design notes differ. The Chuul keeps AC 14 + Dexterity. Cloaker uses its bite, grapple, damage split and False Appearance. Its trait list omits Moan, Phantasms and Sunlight Sensitivity. On odd damage totals, Hush takes the rounded-down half and the grappled creature takes the remainder.

The Cockatrice uses effective 2014 exhaustion levels for its bite's mixed fatigue and exhaustion wording. Its sixth failed save causes the printed petrified outcome. Iriam uses Divine Mandate as an ordinary focus and leaves somatic spells off its action list under the 2014 hand rule.
