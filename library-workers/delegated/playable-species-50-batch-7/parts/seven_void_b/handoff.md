# Voidrunner's Codex heritages

Keridani, Naato, and Sipher use EN Publishing's original *Voidrunner's Codex* heritage rules. Each profile keeps its gifts and level 10 paragon choices together. The NPCs use A5E ability scores, origins, and classes, with equipment from the same setting. [Publisher book](https://enpublishingrpg.com/products/vo) · [Official rules index](https://a5e.tools/rules/voidrunners-codex)

The Keridani page uses “proficiency modifier” for Ancestral Awakening uses. I read that as proficiency bonus. The Sipher page puts Planetary Invocation in the Hivebound paragraph without a separate heading. I kept the feature there and flagged it for a print-layout check. Lio uses Energy Bolt, so that choice does not affect the NPC. [Keridani original traits](https://a5e.tools/rules/keridani) · [Sipher original traits](https://a5e.tools/rules/sipher)

Built-in ImageGen produced three square 1254 px portraits with Big Linda as the paint and paper reference. I reviewed each original and the 96 px circular preview.
