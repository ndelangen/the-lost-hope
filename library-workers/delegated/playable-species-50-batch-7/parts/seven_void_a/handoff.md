# Voidrunner's Codex

The four profiles use EN Publishing's A5E heritages from *Voidrunner's Codex* (2025). Each NPC uses the Codex's origins, class rules, and equipment. The stat blocks use the A5E standard array and apply the two bonuses from each background. [Publisher rules index](https://a5e.tools/rules/voidrunners-codex) · [Character creation](https://a5e.tools/rules/character-creation)

| Heritage | NPC | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Axon | Tovin Breakwire, Scout 1 | AC 14 from Artful Defense. HP 9 from 8 + 1 Con. Claws +4 to hit for 1d8+2. Dastardly Gambit uses two d6s per long rest, one per reaction. Hearing or smell raises passive Perception from 12 to 15. |
| Grey | Iri Vale, Psion 1 | AC 14 in synthweave. HP 7 from 6 + 1 Con. Wisdom gives +5 psionic attack and DC 13 for powers and the held-object Telekinesis save. Telekinetic Shield grants 6 temporary HP per use. |
| Houseki | Sorel Wellglass, Scientist 1 and Engineer | Small at Tier 1. Canvas AC 12. Engineer Hit Die gives HP 10. Intelligence gives +5 to hit and DC 13 for praxes. Dexterity gives Analytical Attack its separate maneuver DC 11. |
| Hurroc | Dara Hitch, Trooper 1 | AC 17 from the aramid vest, Dexterity, and medium shield. HP 12. Horns +5 to hit for 1d6+3 and DC 13 to resist being knocked prone. One Slugger hit can add Favored Ordnance damage before Dara starts her next turn. |

The Grey's Telekinesis entry describes what it can move without specifying a turn cost. Iri uses that trait outside combat. Binding has its own combat action and resource. Grey is the profile key, and the trait text includes the alternate spelling Gray for search. Sorel's custom blaster and contraptions come from Scientist praxes, and the engineer's toolbox comes from the archetype.

All portraits are original 1254 × 1254 PNGs created with built-in ImageGen. I inspected the plain parchment, silhouettes, and identifying anatomy at full size and 96 px. The comparison shows Big Linda followed by Axon, Grey, Houseki, and Hurroc.
