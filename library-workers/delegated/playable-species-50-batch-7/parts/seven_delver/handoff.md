# Dungeon Delver's Guide

Five heritages use the EN Publishing rules for Level Up: Advanced 5th Edition. Each profile keeps its gifts and paragon options together. The linked NPCs use native Level Up classes, cultures, backgrounds, and destinies. [Publisher book index](https://a5e.tools/rules/dungeon-delvers-guide)

| Heritage | NPC and manual checks |
| --- | --- |
| Motley | Jessa Bentreach, Fighter 1. Padded leather 12 + Dexterity 2 + medium shield 2 gives AC 16. Fighter 10 + Constitution 2 gives 12 HP. The one-handed spear is +5 to hit and deals 1d6 + 5 piercing with Dueling. Long Arms extends reach on Jessa's turn. [Publisher traits](https://a5e.tools/rules/motley) |
| Mycelial | Luma Chalkcap, Fighter 1. Padded leather 12 + Dexterity 1 + shield 2 + Defense 1 gives AC 16. Fighter 10 + Constitution 3 gives 13 HP. Her Spore Cloud uses Constitution DC 13 and sits where she releases it for this build. The source does not say that it follows her. [Publisher traits](https://a5e.tools/rules/mycelial) |
| Oozefolk | Silt, Rogue 1. The first d8 makes Silt Small. Padded leather gives AC 15 with Dexterity 3. Rogue 8 + Constitution 2 gives 10 HP. The Oozefolk trait introduction has a stray Motley label. The paragon changes how an existing climb speed works. Silt's climb speed comes from Agile Athlete. [Publisher traits](https://a5e.tools/rules/oozefolk) |
| Ratling | Tivin Crumbwick, Rogue 1. Padded leather and Dexterity give AC 15, with 10 HP. Dexterity 3 + proficiency 2 gives the light crossbow +5 to hit. Keen Smell raises passive Perception from 14 to 17 for scent. [Publisher traits](https://a5e.tools/rules/ratling) |
| Rockborn | Orven Veinlock, Fighter 1. Padded leather 12 + Dexterity 1 + shield 2 gives AC 15. Fighter 10 + Constitution 2 + Carven 1 gives 13 HP. His Roll uses maneuver DC 13 and the shield bonus drops away when curled. [Publisher traits](https://a5e.tools/rules/rockborn) |
