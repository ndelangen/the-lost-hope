# Gazette heritages

Five original Level Up heritages, five linked level 1 NPCs, and five square portraits. I read the publisher's trait pages and confirmed the issue numbers against its magazine index. Each NPC uses an A5E culture, background, destiny, class, and heritage gift. ## Table readings

- Ila's Protective Petals ends at the start of her next turn. The published duration is one round, with no use limit.
- Rending Bite omits who makes the first Constitution save. Nerev's target makes it. I apply the ongoing damage before the repeat save at the end of each affected turn.
- Tavin uses the Garoul claw's written 1d4 damage. The claw entry omits a damage modifier, and Finesse still applies to the attack.
- The Madrai trait introduction says Mycelial. The surrounding heading and the rest of the entry identify Madrai. I read Boon Companion's proficiency modifier as proficiency bonus.
- The Oxfolk rules give height without a size category. Mara uses Medium and counts as Large for the specified carrying and lifting rules. The official paragon entry gives the three benefits in the plural, while general character creation describes choosing a paragon gift. The profile preserves that source wording, and Mara's level 1 build avoids the conflict.
