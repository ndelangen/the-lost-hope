# Deep Imaskari

This batch adds the playable Deep Imaskari from the October 2003 *Underdark*. Neris Vell, a vault surveyor, and Serran Thale, a waystation warden, are original NPCs. Neris’s plain-parchment portrait represents the 2003 profile.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and both encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/deep-imaskari-draft.png). The [style comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Neris beside the approved Big Linda portrait.

In a temporary copy, search found the profile by name, Spell Clutch, low-light vision, and both NPC roles. The portrait resolved to the staged original and became the list thumbnail. The patch applied on its own and after preceding staged batches. [Verification](verification.json)
