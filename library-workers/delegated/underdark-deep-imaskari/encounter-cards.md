# Deep Imaskari encounters

Both have low-light vision and gain +4 on Hide checks underground. Once per day, Spell Clutch lets them spend a standard action to prepare again a 1st-level spell they previously prepared and cast. They cast it separately.

## Neris Vell, vault surveyor

**Wizard 1, CR 1.** AC 11, HP 6, initiative +5. Fortitude +2, Reflex +1, Will +3. Light crossbow +1 for 1d8 piercing. *Mage armor* increases AC to 15 for 1 hour. *Grease* lasts 1 round and has Reflex DC 15.

Neris scouts the lower vault with *mage armor* already active. If pursued, she casts *grease* in the first doorway, takes cover, and uses Spell Clutch to ready it for the second. Her Hide modifier is +5 underground.

## Serran Thale, waystation warden

**Cleric 1, CR 1.** AC 16, HP 10, initiative +0. Fortitude +4, Reflex +0, Will +5. Light mace +1 for 1d6+1 bludgeoning. *Cure light wounds* heals 1d8+1.

Serran casts *bless* to get the refugees past the first barricade, then restores the spell in the lull. He saves the second casting for the defense of the inner doors. Each casting lasts 1 minute and grants a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls and saves against fear.
