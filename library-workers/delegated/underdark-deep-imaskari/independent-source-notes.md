# Deep Imaskari rules sources

- The detailed racial traits give +2 Intelligence and −2 Dexterity. Table 1–1 lists a Strength penalty instead. The catalog uses the racial text, following Wizards’ text-over-table rule. The collected errata have no *Underdark* file and the June 2008 FAQ has no Deep Imaskari or Spell Clutch entry. [Table 1–1, p. 6]() · [Racial traits, p. 9]() · [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)
- Spell Clutch restores one 1st-level spell the character previously prepared and cast, once per day. The core supernatural-ability rule makes the activation a standard action that does not provoke. Casting the prepared spell is a separate action. [Spell Clutch, p. 10]() · [Monster Manual, p. 315]()
- The book describes a Medium human subrace and lists no racial Hit Dice or positive level adjustment. The builds use Humanoid (Human) and ECL 1. Both choose Deep Imaskar for regional bonus languages. [Racial traits, pp. 9–10]() · [Deep Imaskar region, p. 23]()

Neris’s marble-veined skin, dark greatcoat, and rings follow the written description. The artist on the original book illustration is Vance Kovacs. [Art and description, pp. 8–9]()
