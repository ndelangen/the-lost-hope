# Sharakim encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Human subtype, darkvision 60 ft., a 30-ft. land speed, and +1 natural armor. Both are ECL 2 and provisional CR 2. [Playable rules, printed pp. 104–105]()

| NPC | AC (touch, flat-footed) | HP | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maren Eldermere, Wizard 1 | 11 (10, 11) | 5 | +0 | +1, +0, +3 |
| Oswin Fenlock, Rogue 1 | 14 (11, 13) | 7 | +1 | +1, +3, +1 |

Add +2 to Hide, Move Silently, Search, and Spot in darkness or shadowy illumination. Take −1 on attack rolls in bright sunlight or within a *daylight* spell. Attacks against orcs and half-orcs gain +1. Apply both modifiers when both conditions are present.

## Maren Eldermere, seed vault keeper

Someone entered the seed vault before a spring shipment was due to be reopened. Maren can examine the damaged catches if the party keeps the room clear and brings back the missing inventory board.

Concentration +5, Decipher Script +7, Spellcraft +10, Knowledge (arcana) +7, and Knowledge (architecture and engineering) +7. Search +3 and Spot +1 in normal light, or +5 and +3 in shadow. Spell component pouch, quarterstaff, and light crossbow.

- **Quarterstaff** +1 melee using one end in two hands, 1d6+1 bludgeoning, critical ×2. **Light crossbow** +0 ranged, 1d8 piercing, critical 19–20/×2, 80-ft. range increment.
- **Prepared cantrips.** *Detect magic*, *mage hand*, and *ray of frost*. The ray is +0 ranged touch within 25 ft. for 1d3 cold. Bright sunlight makes it −1 and an orc or half-orc target makes it +1. Both conditions together leave it at +0.
- **Prepared 1st-level spells.** *Grease* covers a 10-ft. square for one round within 25 ft. Grounded creatures caught in it fall on a failed DC 14 Reflex save. *Magic missile* deals 1d4+1 force within 110 ft. and avoids the attack-roll penalty because it requires no attack roll. Spell resistance applies to *magic missile* and *ray of frost*. [Wizard class rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/sorcererWizard.htm#wizard)

## Oswin Fenlock, cistern inspector

The cistern gate keeps jamming before the morning draw. Oswin can inspect the shadowed maintenance steps if the party keeps the main lever still.

Search +9, Disable Device +6, Open Lock +5, Hide +5, Move Silently +5, and Spot +5. In darkness or shadowy illumination, Search +11 and Hide, Move Silently, and Spot +7. Trapfinding and ordinary thieves' tools are included.

- **Short sword** +2 melee, 1d6+2 piercing, critical 19–20/×2.
- **Light crossbow** +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, critical 19–20/×2, 80-ft. range increment. Reload as a move action.
- Add 1d6 Sneak Attack on qualifying hits. Ranged Sneak Attack is limited to 30 ft.
