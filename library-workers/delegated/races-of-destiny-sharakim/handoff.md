# Sharakim for D&D 3.5

One playable Sharakim entry from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*. Sharakim have the Human subtype, 60-ft. darkvision, and level adjustment +1. Shadow Affinity boosts four skills in darkness or shadowy illumination. Light Sensitivity gives −1 on attack rolls in bright sunlight or within a *daylight* spell. Both attack modifiers are shown alongside the racial +1 against orcs and half-orcs. [Printed pp. 104–105]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

The full-body plain-parchment portrait depicts **Maren Eldermere**, an original Wizard and seed vault keeper. **Oswin Fenlock** is an original Rogue and cistern inspector. Both have provisional CR 2. Maren's card distinguishes a ray attack from *magic missile*, and Oswin's card gives his four shadow-adjusted skill totals. [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [NPC build records](npc-builds.json)

The printed appearance reference describes gray-to-black skin, small curved temple horns, and lower tusks. The book's hunter Sharak appears in an origin legend without encounter statistics. [Printed description and art, p. 103]() · [Origin legend, p. 23]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)
