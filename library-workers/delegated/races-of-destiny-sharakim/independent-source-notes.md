# Independent checks

The direct matches the December 2004 first printing. Its viewer is one page ahead of the printed folios in the Sharakim chapter. The printed entry gives 60-ft. darkvision and the Human subtype. It limits Light Sensitivity to attacks and lists one +1 attack bonus against orcs and half-orcs. The source review found no statted Sharakim encounter and no *Races of Destiny* entry in the publisher's collected errata. [Printed Sharakim traits, pp. 104–105]() · [Publisher errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Eric Polak's Sharakim illustration is on printed p. 103. The figure beside the remaining trait list on the later page illustrates the Skulk entry. Maren's portrait is original. [Printed artwork]()

The catalog review checked Maren's prepared spells, spellbook, save DCs, and attack totals. It also checked Oswin's skill ranks and four Shadow Affinity adjustments, and confirmed that both original NPCs attach to one Sharakim entry. After moving the NPC insertion point, the Catfolk and Sharakim patches applied in either order to temporary files.
