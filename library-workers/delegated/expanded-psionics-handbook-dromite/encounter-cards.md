# Dromite encounter cards

These original D&D 3.5 NPCs are Small Monstrous Humanoids with the Psionic subtype, 20-ft. land speed, darkvision 60 ft., and bonus Blind-Fight. Scent works at 30 ft., 60 ft. upwind, or 15 ft. downwind. Strong and overpowering odors extend those distances. A move action finds the direction. Within 5 ft., the Dromite can pinpoint the source. Each NPC's racial ray has its own daily use and costs no power points. [Species traits, p. 8]() · [Energy Ray, p. 103]()

## Sura Reedcord

The floodgate is jammed and water is backing into an occupied alley. Sura can tell which release pin was replaced if someone holds the upstream walkway. Provisional CR 1/2 and ECL 2. Cold resistance 5.

Sura wears leather and carries a small dagger, a small light crossbow, and ordinary thieves' tools. Disable Device +8, Open Lock +6, and Search +5 cover the gate investigation. Fall back behind the ratchet housing if the party draws trouble.

- Dagger +0 melee, 1d3−1 piercing or slashing, minimum 1, critical 19–20/×2.
- Light crossbow +3 ranged, 1d6 piercing, critical 19–20/×2, 80-ft. range increment. Reload as a move action.
- Cold Energy Ray once per day. Standard action, +3 ranged touch within 25 ft., 1d6+1 cold. No save. Power resistance applies.

## Jori Hearththread

Jori has three spooked pack beetles and a blocked tunnel. A clear lane around the bend is enough to move the caravan if the group keeps the noise down. Provisional CR 1 and ECL 2. Fire resistance 5.

Jori wears studded leather and carries a light wooden shield and a small shortspear. Handle Animal +5 and Climb +3. Get the pack animals around the stone bend before using the ray.

- Shortspear +4 melee or +4 thrown, 1d4+1 piercing, critical ×2. Thrown range increment 20 ft.
- Fire Energy Ray once per day. Standard action, +3 ranged touch within 25 ft., 1d6+1 fire. No save. Power resistance applies.
