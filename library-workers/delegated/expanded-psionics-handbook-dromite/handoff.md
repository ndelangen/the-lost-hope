# Dromite for D&D 3.5

One playable Dromite entry contains four selectable castes from the April 2004 first printing of *Expanded Psionics Handbook*. Fire is the default. All four share a portrait of an Ice Caste Dromite on plain parchment. The armor, colors, and equipment are original illustration choices. [Caste descriptions, p. 6]() · [Playables, p. 8]() · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg)

Each caste pairs a resistance of 5 with its matching daily Energy Ray. Fire and Ice add one damage per die. Glimmer improves the attack against metal armor and the check against power resistance. Voice reduces damage per die and ignores object hardness. The ray uses ranged touch at every caste and allows no saving throw. [Energy Ray, p. 103]() · [Psi-like abilities, p. 184]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Independent review](independent-source-notes.md)

Sura Reedcord is an Ice Caste Expert who can investigate a jammed floodgate. Jori Hearththread is a Fire Caste Fighter trying to move a stranded beetle caravan. Both are original encounters with provisional CRs and links to their respective versions. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [NPC build data](npc-builds.json)
