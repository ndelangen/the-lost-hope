# Fourth-edition beast errata source notes

Wizards’ August 2012 *Rules Update* prints revised headers on p. 77 for Pseudodragon and p. 82 for Ambush Vine. These use the same one-based numbering as the viewer. The `4e Updates - ` inside Wizards’ ZIP matches the local reference byte for byte. Both entries require the book for the full rules. I found no surviving official D&D Compendium page for either creature. [Wizards support index](https://dnd-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000962623-Dungeons-Dragons-3rd-3-5-and-4th-Edition-Rules-Questions) · [Official errata ZIP](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/4ECollectedErrataFAQ.zip)

## Pseudodragon

The update changes Tiny to Small. The Deluxe book already shows Small natural beast (reptile), Level 3 Lurker, 150 XP. The block, tactics and Nature DC 15 lore share printed p. 91, viewer p. 92. The labeled illustration and the level 4 encounter group on the next page are on printed p. 92, viewer p. 93. [Deluxe Monster Manual stat block]() · [Illustration and encounter]()

The Deluxe scan shows recharge dice 4, 5 and 6 and marks both Bite and Sting as melee basic attacks. Each is +8 vs. AC for 1d8 + 4. Sting also deals ongoing 5 poison damage (save ends). Flyby Attack allows one melee basic attack anywhere during a flight of up to 8 squares, so Sting qualifies when ready. The protection from opportunity attacks applies while moving away from the target of that attack. Taking damage recharges Invisibility, and moving ends it.

The text supports a wild predator and a pet valued by humanoids. It describes animal vocalizations and a poisonous sting. The labeled illustration shows a small reddish winged drake. The drakes at the top of printed p. 91 have their own caption with different names.

## Ambush Vine

The first printing gives the adult 1,400 XP. The update gives 2,800 XP for the Large fey beast (plant), Level 16 Elite Controller. Printed p. 204 has both stat blocks, the illustration and Nature DC 20 lore. Further lore and a sample encounter appear on p. 205. The Housegriffon copy uses the same viewer page numbers as the book. In the Manuals.plus copy, these are viewer pp. 205–206. [Monster Manual 2 stat blocks]() · [First-printing alternate copy]() · [Lore]()

The Housegriffon page image confirms the damage dice, reach and Shoot stats. Poison Lash has reach 2 and hits Reflex for 1d8 + 6 plus ongoing 5 poison (save ends). Foot Snare is a minor action with reach 4 and hits Fortitude for 1d8 + 6 and prone. Both attacks are +20. Lashing Vines is a close burst 4 against enemies, +20 vs. Reflex for 2d8 + 6. Each hit on an immobilized target deals an extra 1d8 and heals the vine 5 HP. Rapid Growth spawns two shoots the first time the adult is bloodied. Sprout Vine is an at-will minor action that deals 10 damage to the adult to spawn one shoot. Both spawn within 4 squares and act immediately after the adult on its initiative count.

The Shoot is a Level 15 Minion Controller worth 300 XP, with 1 HP and no damage on a missed attack. It has AC 29, Fortitude 27, Reflex 30, Will 23, initiative and Perception +14, tremorsense 20, speed 6 and burrow 6. Wrapping Vines is a melee basic attack that hits at reach 2, +20 vs. Fortitude for a flat 12 damage and restrained (save ends). Enwrap imposes −2 on adjacent enemies’ saves against immobilized and restrained. The lore places these vines in Feywild wilderness and in deep forests and swamps in the natural world, where they can hide underground or blend in with plants.
