# Urdunnir encounter cards

## Breka Flintvein

Expert 2. Provisional CR 2, ECL 6. AC 13, HP 15, initiative +1, speed 20 ft. Fort +3, Ref +1, Will +4. Add +4 against poison or +2 against spells and spell-like abilities when applicable.

- Light mace +2, 1d6+1 bludgeoning. Add +1 to the attack against orcs and goblinoids.
- Craft (gemcutting) +12. Search +9 for unusual stonework.
- Stone Walk to a known air pocket. Breka's racial payload limit is 432 lb. Everyone in the stone holds their breath.
- Use Stone Shape to reshape a landing. Shape Metal uses melee touch +2. Fortitude DC 14 where applicable.

Breka asks the party to mark the known exit before he crosses the seam.

## Dorrin Slatebrace

Fighter 2. Provisional CR 3, ECL 6. AC 18, HP 21, initiative +5, speed 20 ft. Fort +6, Ref +1, Will +1. Add +4 against poison or +2 against spells and spell-like abilities when applicable.

- Dwarven waraxe +6, 1d10+3 slashing, critical ×3. Add +1 to the attack against orcs and goblinoids.
- Stone Walk by the shortest known section of the collapsed passage. Dorrin's racial payload limit is 460 lb.
- Shape Metal takes a full round. Against a shield, weapon, or metallic creature, it deals 4d6 damage on a successful use. Melee touch +5, Fortitude DC 15 where applicable.

Dorrin asks the party to hold the approach while he reaches a bent fitting on the far side. [Complete builds](npc-builds.json)
