# Urdunnir sources

The March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn* covers the playable Urdunnir on printed pp. 20–23. The viewer is one page ahead. The traits on p. 22 grant +4 on poison saves, Stone Walk, at-will Stone Shape and Shape Metal, favored class Expert, and level adjustment +4. The Urdunnir has no racial Hit Dice. [Original playable rules]()

Stone Walk carries up to twice the dwarf's body weight. Both the dwarf and any passenger must hold their breath in stone or earth. The entry gives no separate movement speed, so the encounter cards use short routes to known openings and the dwarf's ordinary movement. [Stone Walk]()

The 3.5 core Dwarf supplies stability, martial weapon familiarity, and 20-ft. movement under armor or load. At caster level 8, Stone Shape can reshape 18 cubic feet of stone. The Urdunnir's Shape Metal uses the 5-cubic-foot cap and full-round action from p. 22, with touch, Fortitude, spell resistance, and combat effects from the appendix on p. 191. The appendix also describes readying a touch in response to a strike. The encounter cards use full-round activation and leave that timing interaction to the DM. [3.5 Dwarf](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/races.htm#dwarves) · [Stone Shape](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/stoneShape.htm) · [Shape Metal]()

The original species grants Dwarven and Undercommon. Both NPCs use Oldonnar for regional bonus languages and Common from the revised Realms rules, while retaining the original grant of Undercommon. [Original region, printed p. 23]() · [3.5 regional table, printed p. 19]()

Wayne England illustrated the Urdunnir in the sourcebook. Breka is an original ImageGen portrait, using the book's description and the plain parchment from Big Linda and Sael. [Appearance and book illustration]()
