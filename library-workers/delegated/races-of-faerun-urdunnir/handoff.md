# Urdunnir

This batch adds the Urdunnir orecutter dwarf from the March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn*. It carries the book's Stone Walk, Stone Shape, and Shape Metal into a 3.5 profile.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species, Breka Flintvein, and Dorrin Slatebrace. It is based on catalog commit `e15e857`. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) links Breka's [square portrait](portraits/urdunnir-draft.png) to this publication.

The original rules, NPC builds, and thumbnail were reviewed by hand. Search, portrait loading in the app, and automated verification are deferred.
