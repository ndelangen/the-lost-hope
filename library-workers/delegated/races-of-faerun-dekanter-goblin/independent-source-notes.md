# Dekanter Goblin sources

The March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn* gives the playable Dekanter Goblin on printed p. 137. The viewer is one page ahead. It gives Medium Monstrous Humanoid, two racial d8s, fast healing 3, cold resistance 5, and level adjustment +4, for ECL 6 before classes. The original entry gives Common, Goblin, and Undercommon as automatic languages. Proficiency covers simple weapons, and Barbarian is the favored class. [Original player rules]()

In the player rules the horn is primary and has a ×3 critical. Both claws are secondary. With a manufactured weapon, add a single secondary horn or claw on a full attack. The two NPCs are original builds under the player entry. Tavra uses the 2000 Barbarian rules and their Wilderness Lore skill. Both CRs are provisional estimates. [Player attack rules]() · [Original Player's Handbook]()

The 2001 encounter monster was published in *Monsters of Faerûn*, printed p. 53. The 2004 conversion lists Humanoid (Goblinoid), a +3 level adjustment, Will +1, and the horn or claw pair as alternate attacks. The patch adds the original playable profile only. Vince Locke illustrated the lineup on printed p. 135. Orrik is an original ImageGen portrait based on the book's orange-red hide, wiry dark mane, and single horn at the tip of the snout.
