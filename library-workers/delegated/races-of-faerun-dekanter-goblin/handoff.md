# Dekanter Goblin

This batch adds the playable Dekanter Goblin from the March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn*. It is indexed under D&D 3.0 and retains the original Monstrous Humanoid type, level adjustment +4, and horn and claw routine. The 2004 revision documents a separate encounter monster. [Original player rules]() · [3.5 monster update]()

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species, Orrik Flintnose, and Tavra Slatecall. It is based on catalog commit `76e36d1`. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) maps Orrik's [square portrait](portraits/dekanter-goblin-draft.png) to that publication.

Manual review covered the rules, both builds, and the [portrait thumbnail](portrait-thumbnails.jpg). Search, in-app portrait loading, and automated verification are deferred.
