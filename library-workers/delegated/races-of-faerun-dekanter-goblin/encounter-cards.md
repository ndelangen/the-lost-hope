# Dekanter Goblin encounter cards

## Orrik Flintnose

Two racial Hit Dice. Provisional CR 2, ECL 6. AC 16, HP 22, initiative +2, speed 20 ft. Fort +4, Ref +5, Will +5. Fast healing 3 and cold resistance 5.

- Horn +7, 1d6+5 piercing, critical ×3.
- Full attack adds two secondary claws at +2 for 1d4+2 each.
- Climb +9, Listen +5, Spot +5.

Orrik signals from the high gallery that a rope is missing from the lift shaft.

## Tavra Slatecall

Two racial Hit Dice and Barbarian 1. Provisional CR 3, ECL 7. AC 19, HP 32, initiative +6, speed 30 ft. Fort +6, Ref +5, Will +5. Fast healing 3 and cold resistance 5.

- One-handed battleaxe +9, 1d8+6 slashing, critical ×3. On a full attack, add one horn at +4 for 1d6+3 or one claw at +4 for 1d4+3.
- Rage 1/day, 9 rounds. AC 17, HP 38 at full health, Fort +8, Will +7. Axe +11 for 1d8+8. The secondary horn or claw attacks at +6, with 1d6+4 or 1d4+4 damage respectively.
