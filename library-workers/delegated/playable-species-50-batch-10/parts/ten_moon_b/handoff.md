# The Crooked Moon, pages 8–11

Four player profiles, four original NPCs, and four square portraits for the 2024 rules. The species rules use the printed types. The also offers a Humanoid variant for groups that want one. [Player v1.1]() · [Avantris 2024 edition]()

- **Gnarlborn, p. 8.** Larchwall uses Nature as the current Elderwood Whispers choice. Grasping Branches uses Constitution for DC 11, and its successful initial save halves Speed. The restrained creature's later successful save simply ends the effect.
- **Graveborn, p. 9.** Mera Flintjaw uses the printed Undead type. Infused Drakkonite applies to a damage event, and Devour Corpse heals without spending a die.
- **Harvestborn, p. 10.** Wick Furrow uses a shield and one sickle. The build records Nick training without granting an extra attack from one weapon. Gift of the Green uses the target's Hit Point Die and Wick's proficiency bonus. Wick carries phosphorus for Dancing Lights.
- **Plagueborn, p. 11.** Nix Wattle is Small and chooses Deception. Plague Bearer is a separate magical effect with a DC 12 Constitution save. Born in Filth grants advantage on the specified saves.

I checked the stat lines against the 2024 class, origin, equipment and spell rules. The fighters have 11, 12 and 14 HP in the order above. Chain shirt, shield and Defense give AC 18, 17 and 17. Nix has 17 HP, AC 14 and Initiative +5 with Alert. These are character-level builds with no assigned CR. [2024 D&D rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024)
