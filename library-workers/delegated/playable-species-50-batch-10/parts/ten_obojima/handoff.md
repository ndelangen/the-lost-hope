# Obojima profiles

This group stages two playable profiles and two original NPCs. I read the rendered pages of the July 2025 second printing of the 5.5e , ISBN 978-1-954777-16-3. The Dara forms and trait table are on pp. 163–165. The Nakudama block is on p. 168. I used the island’s language list on p. 197 for the examples. [Publisher edition listing]() · [Rendered book](https://online.anyflip.com/dexhd/qfnf/mobile/index.html#p=83)

- **Dara.** This profile groups both colors. Selka Dara is a Blue Dara Rogue 1. The color descriptions give Blue Dara one Knowledge talisman per day and Red Dara one Might talisman per day in addition to other talismans. The formal Create Talisman trait says it can be used once per Long Rest. Selka uses the formal limit of one creation, with Knowledge among her options. A table that reads the color description as granting a separate daily creation can give Selka a Knowledge talisman alongside the regular use. Impart Knowledge starts at level 10.
- **Nakudama.** Wick Tidecup is a Nakudama Fighter 1. His Grasping Tongue save DC is 12, from base 8 + Strength 2 + proficiency 2. Latching Tongue moves Wick toward a larger target. Amphibious gives him underwater breathing. The printing lists a 30-ft. Speed and supplies no separate Swim Speed.

I checked the example builds against the 2024 class and equipment rules. Selka has AC 14, 9 HP, and +5 to hit with Dexterity weapons. Her Investigation +6 and Stealth +7 use Expertise. Wick has AC 16, 11 HP, +5 with Dexterity weapons, and two uses of Second Wind. [Class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes) · [Equipment rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/equipment)

I generated both 1254 × 1254 portraits with built-in ImageGen, using Big Linda as the paper and brushwork reference. In the comparison sheets, Big Linda is left, Selka is center, and Wick is right. I checked the original images and the 96-pixel thumbnails. Both silhouettes and identifying eyes are legible at thumbnail size.
