# Four playable beasts from Monstrous Races

[Species profiles](book-species.json) · [NPC builds](npc-builds.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

The player rules come from Tyler Kamstra's v2 printing for 2014 fifth edition. The builds use 27-point buy and fixed HP after 1st level. [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [v2 text]() · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Playable profile | NPC | Printed trait pages | Manual rules check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Owl | Reedwatch, Rogue 1 | 240–241 | Unarmored AC 13, HP 10, Talons +5. The printed finesse rule qualifies Talons for Sneak Attack. Expertise gives passive Perception 16, or 21 for sight and hearing. Flyby uses a bonus action. |
| Giant Poisonous Snake | Siltcoil, Monk 1 | 241 and Monk exception on 7 | Unarmored AC 15, HP 10. Bite +5 deals 1d4+3 piercing plus 1d4 poison. The bonus strike follows Kamstra's Monk exception and deals 1d4+3 bludgeoning. |
| Giant Spider | Loomstep, Fighter 2 | 244 | Natural AC 14, HP 20, Second Wind 1d10+2. Web escape DC 12 is 8 + proficiency 2 + Constitution 2. The build treats Web as a proficient natural attack, for +5 to hit. |
| Giant Wasp | Cinderwing, Barbarian 1 | 246 | Unarmored AC 15, HP 14. Strength-based Sting is +4 to hit and gains 2 piercing damage during Rage. Dexterity Sting is +5 to hit and keeps its usual damage during Rage. |

The Giant Owl's No Hands trait refers to tentacles. The book describes an ordinary owl body elsewhere, so Reedwatch uses its talons and leaves manual equipment out of the build. For the Giant Spider, the printed Web trait leaves proficiency and recharge unstated. Loomstep's Web uses Dexterity and proficiency for the attack. The trait's Strength check, escape DC, and web object statistics are carried through as printed. The snake, spider, and wasp use the player venom dice on the hit. Cinderwing has no paralysis rider.

The four square PNGs use Big Linda as the paper and paint reference. I inspected the portraits at source size and at 96 pixels. The comparison shows Big Linda, Reedwatch, Siltcoil, Loomstep, and Cinderwing from left to right. I regenerated Cinderwing to correct the leg count. [Portrait files and credits](portrait-files.json)
