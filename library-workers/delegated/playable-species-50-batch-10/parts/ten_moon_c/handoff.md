The folios above are printed in the 2024 Player v1.1. The builds use the 2024 core rules and the creature types in their individual trait blocks. [Authored player text]() · [Avantris 2024 release]() · [D&D Beyond 2024 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes)

Penn uses the core reaction timing for Silver Bulwark. His resistance begins immediately after the triggering damage. The printed trait leaves the triggering damage implicit, so the source evidence records the table ruling. Ione's Moment of Remembrance recharges only after the d4 turns a failure into a success. Her Dance of Death uses a Hit Point Die without expending it. Pella has Guidance at level 1 and two reaction uses per long rest. Her species spells at levels 3 and 5 stay in the species profile. [2024 reaction timing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game#Reactions)
