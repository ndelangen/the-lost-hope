# Ryoko’s Guide v1.00, group A

Five playable profiles, each with an original first-level NPC and square parchment portrait. I read the final player trait blocks on the rendered folios. The builds use the 2014 core class and equipment rules. [Final v1.00, chapter 8](https://anyflip.com/qwvsj/izqk/basic/101-150) · [Loot Tavern](https://loottavern.com/ryokos-guide/) · [2014 core classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

- Both Enkoh forms share the profile. Toma uses Hulking, with the flexible adjustments from p. 117. His quilted traveling coat covers his leather armor.
- The heading on p. 121 reads Fuyōren. Fuyōhren and Fuyohren are retained as lookup variants in the evidence and profile text. Reedstep spent one Water Magic use before the encounter.
- Sumi uses Unarmored Defense for AC 15. Rugged is a separate AC calculation of 14. Essence Capture takes a reaction and makes any copied ability score temporary.
- Kae’s Clay Companion uses the 2014 Mastiff statistics, with the Haniwa rules for creature type and Soul Shell. Kae’s Grave Guardian entry uses Wisdom DC 12 and the 2014 errata for sanctuary. Earthen uppercut is on p. 281. [Mastiff](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-m#Mastiff) · [2014 Player’s Handbook errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/players-handbook-2014)
- Mako’s Waterborne trait allows water breathing and removes underwater disadvantage from weapon attack rolls. The player entry lists a walking speed. Fortune Favoured has one use at proficiency +2. At odd proficiency bonuses, the 2014 rounding rule rounds the printed half down.

I checked class saves, AC, HP, proficiency, damage and recharge math by hand. I compared each 1254 × 1254 ImageGen portrait with Big Linda and checked a 96 px circular preview. I revised Mako’s portrait to show the four vestigial appendages and the antennae described on p. 128.
