# Crooked Moon player species, pages 3 through 7

These five profiles use the 2024 Player v1.1. I read each species' player trait block and used the 2024 core rules for the example NPCs. [Player v1.1]() · [Avantris edition]() · [2024 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes) · [2024 spells](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spell-descriptions)

| Species | Printed traits | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ashborn | p. 3 | Nix Coppermark, Rogue 1. AC 14 in leather, HP 10, initiative +5 with Alert. The Dexterity sting hits at +5 and deals 1d4+3 piercing, plus an optional 1d6 poison. |
| Azureborn | p. 4 | Vell Sableflight, Ranger 1. AC 14 in leather, HP 11, shortbow +5 for 1d6+3 piercing. Glimpse Fate can give two creatures advantage on initiative. |
| Bogborn | p. 5 | Orruk Fenpeg, Fighter 1. AC 16 with hide, shield, and Defense, HP 12. Spear +5 for 1d6+3 piercing with Sap. Regeneration uses Orruk's d10 Hit Point Die. |
| Curseborn | p. 6 | Sella Ragwort, Barbarian 1. AC 13 without armor, HP 16 with Tough. The Strength claws hit at +5 for 1d6+3 slashing, rising to 1d6+5 during Rage. |
| Deepborn | p. 7 | Maar Siltglass, Warlock 1. AC 15 with Mage Armor, HP 9, and DC 13 for Eldritch Gibbering. Each affected creature rolls its own result. |

Nix's tail does not qualify for Sneak Attack. Vell gains Augury at character level 3. Wings along the arms are visible in the portrait. Vell's flight has no printed armor restriction and the build uses leather. Orruk tracks Regeneration and Second Wind separately. Sella's claws use the 2024 Rage rule for Strength-based unarmed damage. Maar only expends Eldritch Gibbering if at least one target fails the save.

All five level-one builds use the standard array with ability increases from their chosen backgrounds. I checked AC, hit points, saves, skill bonuses, attack bonuses, save DCs, and recharge timing by hand. Guide and Farmer use the published 2024 background options.
