# Shadow, Blood Hawk, Flying Snake and Giant Centipede

Four playable profiles and four original NPCs are staged with square parchment portraits. The rules follow the v2 player trait blocks in Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races* and the 2014 class rules. [Printed v2 rules]() · [DMs Guild listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Profile | Trait page | NPC and manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shadow | 185 | Sill, Fighter 1. AC 13, 12 HP. Strength Drain uses +4 to hit and a DC 12 Strength save on a natural 20. The separate shortsword attack uses +5 to hit and 1d6 + 5 piercing with Dueling. |
| Blood Hawk | 224 | Bramble-Eye, Rogue 2. AC 13, 17 HP. Finesse bite is +5 for 1d4 + 3 slashing and can trigger Sneak Attack. Pack Tactics and Cunning Action each spend the bonus action. |
| Flying Snake | 231 | Copper Sedge, Monk 1. AC 15, 10 HP. Bite and tail slap use +5 to hit. The tail slap uses a monk die and deals bludgeoning damage under the supplement's rule on bonus-action strikes. |
| Giant Centipede | 234 | Thimbleback, Rogue 1. AC 14 from Natural Armor, 10 HP. Finesse bite uses +5. The printed trait gives 60 ft. of blindsight. |

The Flying Snake's design commentary mentions Flyby, which the player trait block omits. The Giant Centipede commentary also gives a different blindsight distance. The staged entries follow the printed traits in both cases. For the snake and centipede builds I assigned the Dexterity damage modifier to the piercing portion of the bite. Thimbleback's Sneak Attack adds piercing damage.

I inspected all four built-in ImageGen originals alongside Big Linda at full size and at 96 px. Each is 1254 × 1254. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [96 px comparison](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [Generation prompts](portrait-prompts.json)
