# Ryoko v1.00, group B

Four playable profiles, four original NPCs, and four square portraits. I read the printed chapter 8 folios in the v1.00 digital book and used the 2014 class, equipment, and spell rules for the builds. [Published folios](https://anyflip.com/qwvsj/izqk/basic/101-150) · [Loot Tavern digital edition](https://loottavern.com/product/ryokos-guide-to-the-yokai-realms-digital/) · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Playable profile | Printed folios | NPC | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kitsune | 129–131, traits on 131 | Sumi Wickstep, Rogue 1 | Leather AC 14, fox-form AC 13, HP 10, shortbow +5 for 1d6+3. One tail. Fey. |
| Oniborne | 132–135 | Iori Reedjaw, Blue Oniborne Fighter 1 | Chain mail, shield, and Defense AC 19. HP 13. Bite +5 for 1d6+3, and a separate reaction bite against a qualifying attacker with a critical range of 18–20. |
| Ryokido | 136–137 | Gen Tidemark, Fighter 1 | Tough Shell and shield AC 16 from 12 + 2 Constitution + 2 shield. HP 12. One-handed spear with Dueling +5 for 1d6+5. |
| Tatsumi | 138–141, traits on 139 and 141 | Nami Reedscale, white-primary Nishikin Fighter 2 | Leather AC 14, HP 20, shortbow +7 for 1d6+3. The character has the two Hit Dice Moonblessed requires. |

- The final Kitsune is a Fey. Ascendant Form lasts up to one hour and can assist a failed save or missed attack with a reaction. Language of Motion works out to 60 ft. The young courier has one tail.
- The Red Oniborne text continues onto p. 134 before the Blue heading. Weeping Magic belongs to Red. The Blue NPC uses Compassionate Heart, Crunching Maw, Gift of Empathy, and Savage Bite. All three colors stay on one profile.
- Ryokido is the printed name. The book uses lion turtle as a common name. Ryōkido and Lion Turtle are lookup aliases. The final Heavy Shell requires a bonus action to stand from prone alongside the usual movement cost.
- Nishikin and Ryūjin stay on the Tatsumi profile. The latter can be reached at level 7 or through a significant personal event at the GM's discretion. Nami's Moonblessed spends two Hit Dice and adds Constitution once. Her white-primary Slumbering Dragon applies to Wisdom rolls.
- Each build spends 27 points before its species adjustments. The starting score arrays in Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma order are Sumi 8/15/14/10/10/14, Iori 15/10/14/10/14/8, Gen 15/10/15/8/13/10, and Nami 8/15/13/10/15/10.
