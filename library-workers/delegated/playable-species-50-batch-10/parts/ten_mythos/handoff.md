# Mythos playable profiles

Four player profiles and four original NPCs for the 2014 fifth-edition rules. I read the Chapter 2 trait blocks in the 2018 Petersen Games printing with ISBN 978-0-9995390-4-0. The original options stay grouped under their species. [Printed rules](https://www.scribd.com/document/521806750/Sandy-Petersen-s-Cthulhu-Mythos-5e) · [Publisher preview]()

| Playable profile | Trait pages | Original NPC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dreamlands Cat | 11–13 | Moth Ledger, Terran monk 1 |
| Mythos Ghoul | 19–20 | Veller Dross, raised by Ghouls, wizard 1 |
| Gnorri | 26–27 | Seln Brineglass, blue fighter 1 |
| Zoog | 34 | Latch Quickroot, rogue 2 |

Moth uses the printed Nine Lives trait's 1d3-day rest in the Dreamlands. The introductory text describes a separate 4d6-hour return as a mundane cat. Veller is a living Monstrosity. Seln's two-armed weapon damage includes the printed +1 bonus. Latch uses a dagger for Sneak Attack, and Trap Culture never adds proficiency twice.

I checked the standard-array ability scores, hit points, Armor Class, skills, saves, spell DCs, and attack modifiers by hand. I compared the four square originals with Big Linda and checked their silhouettes and faces at 96 pixels. The Zoog portrait needed a correction to give it the book's four paws.
