# Giant Eagle, Giant Frog, Giant Hyena, and Giant Octopus

These four profiles use Tyler Kamstra's printed v2 player rules. The 2014 class builds use the standard array, fixed hit point increases, and proficiency +2. [Monstrous Races v2 text]() · [DMs Guild listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 combat](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

| Profile | Printed traits | Worked example |
|---|---|---|
| Giant Eagle | p. 235 | Slatebeak, Rogue 1. AC 13, HP 10. Talons +5 for 1d8+3, with 1d6 Sneak Attack when eligible. |
| Giant Frog | pp. 236–237 | Silt, Barbarian 1. AC 14, HP 14. Bite +5 for 1d8+3 before Rage, with a bonus-action grapple at Athletics +5 after a hit. |
| Giant Hyena | p. 238 | Rattlejaw, Barbarian 2. AC 13, HP 23. Bite +5 for 1d8+3 before Rage. Rampage can move 15 ft. and deal another bite after reducing a creature to 0 HP in melee. |
| Giant Octopus | pp. 239–240 | Dunewick, Monk 1. AC 13, HP 9. Tentacle +5 for 1d8+3, with a Martial Arts bonus-action headbutt at +5 for 1d4+3. |

Slatebeak flies 60 ft. and Rattlejaw walks 30 ft., as stated in their player traits. The playable Giant Frog's trait block leaves out the darkvision and standing leap discussed in the design notes. Silt uses the bite to grip and rolls the usual Athletics contest. The playable Giant Octopus's tentacle attack has no built-in grapple. Its Ink Cloud does not specify how to activate it. Use an action for Dunewick, matching the published encounter ability on which it was based. Dunewick's Martial Arts bonus strike follows Kamstra's monk rule on p. 7.
