# Heliana, five playable profiles

Five profiles, five original NPCs and five matching square PNGs for the 2014 rules. I read the first-printing player trait pages and checked the October 2024 errata for the five entries. None required an erratum. [Chapter 6](https://anyflip.com/ltplu/rswq/basic/151-200) · [Publisher listing]() · [Official errata]()

| Printed traits | NPC | Table details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cnidaran, p. 152 | Senn Brinecord, Nematocyst Fighter 1 | AC 17, HP 13, rapier +5 for 1d8 + 3. Neurotoxin has two uses per long rest and a DC 13 Constitution save. Nematocyst and Shimmerskin share one species card. |
| Cyclopian, p. 154 | Avel Pagehook, Wizard 1 | AC 12 or 15 with mage armor, HP 8, spell attack +5 and save DC 13. Savant of Secrets lasts a minute. Thought for Food depends on the GM recognizing a significant discovery. |
| Gobboc, p. 156 | Pip Clattercomb, Rogue 1 | AC 14, HP 9, rapier +5 for 1d8 + 3. Coward’s Creed requires a successful melee hit. Gallus Domesticus uses the Chicken stat block printed on the same page. |
| Golynn, p. 158 | Daro Flintdrum, Fighter 1 | AC 17 with natural armor and shield, HP 12, one-handed warhammer +5 for 1d8 + 5. Drill Dervish uses a DC 13 Strength save. |
| Lotol, p. 160 | Tavi Ripplegill, Life Cleric 1 | AC 17, HP 10, spell attack +5 and save DC 13. Aqueous Adaptation and Prehensile Tongue are the two chosen adaptations. Life domain raises 1st-level healing word to 1d4 + 6. |

The printed Gobboc illustration has a chicken-like beak, comb and plumage. I followed that anatomy in place of the roster’s tentative corvid description. The Gobboc and Chicken transformation share one species profile. The Cyclopian is a Humanoid and counts as an Aberration for qualifying effects and prerequisites. Cnidaran, Gobboc, Golynn and Lotol use the 2014 default Humanoid type because those player trait blocks supply no alternate type.

I checked ability increases, proficiencies, AC, hit points, attack bonuses, damage, spell slots, saves and species DCs by hand. The NPCs are original. [2014 core classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [Equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment) · [Spellcasting](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/spellcasting)

I compared each portrait with Big Linda at working size and at 96 pixels. I revised Avel’s portrait to show the printed Cyclopian’s hulking shoulders and crouch. The other portraits show the Nematocyst’s barbed tendril, the Gobboc’s comb and beak, the Golynn’s digging claws or the Lotol’s gills.
