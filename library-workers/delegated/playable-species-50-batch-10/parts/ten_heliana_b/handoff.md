# Heliana’s Guide to Monster Hunting, chapter 6

Five first-printing player profiles, each with one original NPC and a square parchment portrait. The NPCs use the 2014 fifth-edition rules and can be placed outside the published setting.

## Rules and builds

I read the printed pages in the April 2023 first edition, ISBN 978-1-9990962-8-1. The cover sheet adds one to the printed page number in the viewer. The builds use 2014 class features and level-one HP, with fixed HP for later levels. [Chapter 6 page images](https://online.anyflip.com/ltplu/rswq/mobile/index.html#p=163) · [Publisher listing]() · [2014 Basic Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014)

| Profile | Printed pages | NPC and checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mycelian | 162–163 | Fen Stitch, Fighter 1. A Medium dwarf host supplies the 25-ft. walk, darkvision and Dwarven Resilience. AC 17 is 12 + 2 Dexterity + 2 shield + 1 Defense. HP 13 is 10 + 3 Constitution. Spore Spray DC 13. |
| Ombrask | 165 | Aster Sill, Rogue 2. Former elf, +1 Intelligence, one retained voice. AC 14 is 11 + 3 Dexterity. HP 15 is 8 + 5 + 2 Constitution. Stealth +7 comes from Trackless and Expertise. |
| Oozekin | 167 | Brine Button, Champion Fighter 3. AC 16 is 12 + 2 Dexterity + 2 shield. HP 28 is 10 + 6 + 6 + 6 Constitution. Acid Splash DC 12, Engulf DC 13. The build records how Brine frees a hand for spellcasting while holding a target. |
| Opteran | 169 | Bobbin Rush, Bard 1. Larval form, with each life stage recorded under the same profile. AC 13 is 11 + 2 Dexterity. HP 9 is 8 + 1 Constitution. String Shot DC 11, spell save DC 13. |
| Rakin | 171–173 | Tansy Bolt, Rogue 1. Posskin, with Urkin and Tanukin kept under Rakin. AC 14 is 11 + 3 Dexterity. HP 10 is 8 + 2 Constitution. Play Dead triggers at 5 HP or below and uses Constitution (Deception) +6 with Expertise. The book’s errata supplies the casual-glance wording. |

## Portraits

Five original 1254 × 1254 PNGs generated individually. I compared them to Big Linda and checked the 96-pixel circle crops. Fen’s shelf fungi, Aster’s fading edges, Brine’s translucent body, Bobbin’s larval form, and Tansy’s bare tail distinguish the five NPCs. Bobbin’s tail end clips slightly in the round preview.
