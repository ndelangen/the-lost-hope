# Dungeon of the Mad Mage beasts

Five records checked against the November 2018 first printing. Four encounters use blocks from the 2014 Monster Manual with the modifications listed in the adventure. Haungharassk’s statistics and abilities come directly from the Runestone Caverns encounter. [Publisher index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/wdotmm), [Monster Manual errata]()

| Beast | First-print folio | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crow | [302](https://online.anyflip.com/tqblu/aefj/files/basic-html/page303.html) | Uses Raven’s statistics and bonds with a randomly chosen character. |
| Giant Flying Spider | [150](https://online.anyflip.com/tqblu/aefj/files/basic-html/page151.html) | Uses Giant Spider’s statistics with wings and a 40-ft. fly speed. |
| Giant Lizard (variant) | [147](https://online.anyflip.com/tqblu/aefj/files/basic-html/page148.html), [150](https://online.anyflip.com/tqblu/aefj/files/basic-html/page151.html) | Applies the Spider Climb sidebar trait to Giant Lizard. |
| Haungharassk | [258](https://online.anyflip.com/tqblu/aefj/files/basic-html/page259.html) | No attacks and 0 XP. The live snail’s touch can grant temporary HP and remove curses. A pound of salt deals 1d6 acid damage. |
| Space Hamster | [251](https://online.anyflip.com/tqblu/aefj/files/basic-html/page252.html) | Uses Rat’s statistics with no attacks and 0 XP. It can be befriended with food. |

Giant Flying Spider and Haungharassk have new square ImageGen portraits on plain tan parchment. The spider has a distinct pair of wings and four leg attachments on each side. One rear foot is obscured by the pose. The snail has the encounter’s golden shell. Both images fit a round crop. The Giant Lizard reuses its existing portrait, the Crow uses the roster’s Raven image as a corvid stand-in, and the Space Hamster shares the new ordinary-looking Giant Space Hamster portrait from part b14_05.
