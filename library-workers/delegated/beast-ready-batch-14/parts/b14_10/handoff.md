# Call of the Netherdeep beasts

I checked the March 2022 first printing against the rendered pages. The publisher's public index links to the relevant chapter and appendix. Those links lead to the purchase page without access. I found no applicable publisher errata. [Publisher's source index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/cotn) · [Official errata index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae)

- Bristled Moorbounder and Moorbounder have separate Large Beast blocks on p. 203. This printing uses Claw in the singular. Both have Standing Leap. The bristled creature also has Bladed Hide and can make one Blades attack and one Claw attack with Multiattack. The two D&D Beyond creature links lead to *Explorer's Guide to Wildemount*, so the evidence includes the adventure appendix link and the printed page. [Adventure appendix](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/cotn/creatures#Moorbounders)
- Moonshark inherits the 2014 Huge Beast Giant Shark block. The embedded spear gives the shark light and lets it swim up to 25 ft. as a bonus action after it uses its action to Bite and misses. Removing the spear ends both effects. The original encounter on pp. 33–34 also gives the Emerald Eye rope's AC and HP and the race pace rules. [Adventure chapter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/cotn/a-fateful-competition#TheEmeraldGrotto) · [Giant Shark base block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16894-giant-shark)
- Young Horizonback Tortoise inherits the 2014 Huge Beast Ankylosaurus block. Its variant on p. 26 adds amphibious breathing, Goblin comprehension, and a piercing Bite with the same reach and prone effect as the Ankylosaurus's Tail. The adventure expressly gives Bite but does not say whether to remove the base Tail, so the source notes include the parent action. [Adventure chapter](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/cotn/a-fateful-competition#AdventureStart) · [Ankylosaurus base block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17088-ankylosaurus)

Four new 1254 × 1254 portraits use the same plain matte tan parchment. Both moorbounders have upward tusks, and the bristled variant has rows of blade-shaped bristles. The Moonshark's silver shaft enters the flank separately from the amulet rope.
