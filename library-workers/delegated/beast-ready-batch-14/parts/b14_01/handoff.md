# Beast-ready batch 14, part b14_01

I verified six entries in the September 2016 first printing of *Storm King's Thunder* and checked the official errata. Giant Raven and Yak use the published Giant Vulture and Elk references. Hulking Crab has its own Appendix C stat block. The Ice Spiders apply the cold and web changes from the Eye of the All-Father. The queen has a fixed 44 HP, and the Pig has no effective attacks and awards 0 XP. [Adventure](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/skt) · [Adventure errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/storm-kings-thunder) · [2014 Monster Manual errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)

The Ice Spider and its queen share the white-furred, red-eyed portrait. Pig and Yak have their own new portraits. I checked the reused Raven and Crab against the roster hashes. The crab portrait shows its exposed shell, which can carry debris in the adventure. All five distinct portraits use plain tan parchment and fit within their round previews.
