# Six Waterdeep Dragon Heist beasts

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Three portrait originals](portrait-files.json)

I checked the September 2018 first printing and the cited 2014 *Monster Manual* base blocks. All six entries use a Beast stat block. The publisher’s errata index gives no changes to these six adaptations or their base blocks. [Adventure listing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/wdh) · [Errata index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae) · [2014 Monster Manual errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)

| Entry | Adventure folio | Base block | Printed changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awakened Rat | [102](https://online.anyflip.com/degf/fwng/mobile/index.html#p=103) | Rat, p. 335 | Intelligence 10 and spoken Common. |
| Diatryma | [191](https://online.anyflip.com/degf/fwng/mobile/index.html#p=192) | Axe Beak, p. 317 | Beak deals piercing damage. The summoned mount follows the feather’s duration and command rules. |
| Falcon | [53](https://online.anyflip.com/degf/fwng/mobile/index.html#p=54) | Hawk, p. 330 | The Gralhund family’s falcon is kept hooded in a cage. It uses the Hawk block. |
| Maxeene | [37](https://online.anyflip.com/degf/fwng/mobile/index.html#p=38) | Draft Horse, p. 321 | Intelligence 10 and spoken Common. The Harper mission gives separate checks to find her and gain her trust. |
| Obliteros | [66](https://online.anyflip.com/degf/fwng/mobile/index.html#p=67) | Giant Shark, p. 328 | Chaotic evil, Intelligence 10, and spoken and understood Aquan. |
| Sylgar | [220](https://online.anyflip.com/degf/fwng/mobile/index.html#p=221) | Quipper, p. 335 | Removes Blood Frenzy. Water Breathing and the fixed 1 piercing Bite remain. |

Three separate built-in ImageGen calls used Big Linda as the plain parchment reference. The subject prompts called for a colorful flightless diatryma with a heavy beak, a falcon wearing the hood described in the adventure, and Sylgar as a common goldfish. The original silhouettes and parchment were checked in square and round previews. [Sylgar’s goldfish description](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/xgte)

Awakened Rat, Maxeene, and Obliteros reuse matching portraits. All six image checksums match the roster or new-art manifest.
