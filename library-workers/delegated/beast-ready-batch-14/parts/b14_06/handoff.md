The book's credits identify the August 2021 first printing. The publisher's errata index has no entry for this adventure. [Book index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/twbtw) · [Publisher errata index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae)

- Giant Dragonfly has a 120-ft. audible Drone and can halve attack damage with Uncanny Dodge when it sees the attacker. The printed fly speed is 60 ft.
- Giant Snail gains +4 AC in its shell and can emerge as a bonus action. Salt Osmosis covers contact at the start of a turn and a separate action to apply a pound of salt. The 80-ft.-per-round racing movement on p. 47 applies to the Chapter 1 event. The block on p. 234 gives 10-ft. walking and climbing speeds.
- Giant Swan uses the 2014 Giant Eagle statistics under the instructions on p. 38. It makes two Beak attacks as one action, has no Talons attack and speaks Common and Auran. The entry supplies no swim-speed value. Its stat block inherits Keen Sight and the individual Beak attack from the Giant Eagle. [2014 Giant Eagle](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16881-giant-eagle)
- Pollenella is the orange-sized queen bee described on p. 134. Her p. 135 Sting deals a flat 3 piercing damage. The adjacent stat block belongs to Amidor.

The reused dragonfly and three new ImageGen portraits are square at 1,254 pixels per side. The wings, feet, antennae and shells remain visible in the circular crops. The contact sheet follows the table order. [Square and round portrait review]()

## Portrait direction

The three built-in ImageGen prompts used Big Linda as the style reference for a single realistic, painted beast on flat matte tan parchment. The prompts called for a square canvas with the whole silhouette inside a centered circular crop and no environment, props, or lettering.

- Giant Snail. A large land snail with a sturdy light-brown spiral shell, dark cinnamon band, russet-brown skin, long upper eye stalks and a wide muscular foot. Low three-quarter view with a soft contact shadow.
- Giant Swan. A white swan standing in three-quarter view with an upright S-curved neck, folded feathers, a black-based orange bill and dark webbed feet. A faint contact shadow.
- Pollenella. An orange-sized queen honeybee in low hover, with a long tapered amber-and-brown abdomen, fuzzy compact thorax, fine legs and veined translucent wings.
