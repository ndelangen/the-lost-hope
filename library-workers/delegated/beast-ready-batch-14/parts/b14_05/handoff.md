# Batch 14, part b14_05

Staged Giant Space Hamster, Space Eel, Space Guppy, Space Mollymawk and Space Swine as Beasts. The rules come from the August 2022 first printing of *Boo’s Astral Menagerie*, a volume in *Spelljammer: Adventures in Space*. The printed profiles are on pp. 55–57, corresponding to sheets 123–125 of the consolidated facsimile. The official errata lists no correction for these entries. [First-print folios](https://online.anyflip.com/lqark/wnzg/mobile/index.html#p=123) · [Publisher index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sais/bam) · [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/spelljammer-adventures-in-space)

The Space Eel’s Bite attaches on a hit and deals damage at the start of the eel’s turns while attached. Its Tail Spine links paralysis to the one-minute poison. The Space Guppy’s Air Envelope supports itself and one other Tiny creature in its space while in a vacuum. The Space Swine has AC 11, or AC 16 with half-plate barding.

The hamster portrait represents both the bare Giant Space Hamster and the matching body shape of the separate Space Hamster entry in part b14_04. The other four species each have their own original. The Space Swine portrait shows the default unbarded form. All five portraits were checked in square and 64 px circular crops. The swine and mollymawk were reframed to keep their wing tips inside the circle.
