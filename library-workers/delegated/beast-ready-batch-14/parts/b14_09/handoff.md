# Batch 14 Beast rules

Verified Giant Two-Headed Goat, Goat, Wild Dog and Wild Dog Alpha on p. 14 and Jackal on p. 29 of Shawn Merwin's September 2015 *Harried in Hillsfar*, DDEX3-1. The summaries include the encounter instructions from pp. 10–13 and 27–28. [Adventure facsimile](), [Wizards Adventurers League index]()

The Wild Dog Alpha's printed Pounce calls the attacker a lion in its final sentence. The summary reads that subject as the alpha. The check to spot the hiding alpha has no printed DC.

Generated the bright-red Giant Two-Headed Goat with built-in ImageGen on Big Linda's plain tan parchment. The final square portrait leaves both sets of horns and all four hooves inside the circle crop. The four reused portraits match the roster hashes. The ordinary Goat portrait remains cream, and the encounter text records the red-furred goats at the farm.
