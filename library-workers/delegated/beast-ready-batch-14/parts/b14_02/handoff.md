# Batch 14, part b14_02

Staged six Beast entries from the May 2019 first printing of Ghosts of Saltmarsh. Barnacle Bess uses the printed Giant Crab adjustments. Fish uses Quipper statistics with the printed attack prohibition. The Giant White Moray Eel inherits its 10-ft. Bite from the Giant Constrictor Snake and uses the encounter’s movement and Stealth changes. The official errata lists no changes to these six entries. [Wizards errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/ghosts-of-saltmarsh)

Six individual built-in ImageGen portraits use plain tan parchment. The two eels have distinct colors and silhouettes. The Sahuagin Hatchling Swarm shows a cohort of small finned creatures.
