# Batch 14, part 08

Verified five stat blocks in Shawn Merwin’s 2016 Suits of the Mists. Printed folios match the page numbers. The local Wizards supplies all five blocks, and the league FAQ confirms the adventure’s place in the 2016 season. I found no standalone publisher errata for the module. [Original listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/177576/DDAL401-Suits-of-the-Mists-5e) · [Wizards league FAQ]()

- Fire Stirge and Ice Cat are on p. 14. Flare asks for a DC 10 Dexterity save in an adjacent 5-foot square and deals no damage on a success. The damage clause omits a type, while the opening sentence describes fire. Ice Walk and Pounce are both printed.
- Mastiff is on p. 35. The appendix gives CR 1/8 and 25 XP. The adventure awards 50 XP per mastiff on p. 34. The p. 31 Cooshee encounter uses this profile for green-brown elven hounds.
- Elk is on p. 36. Charge deals an additional 7 (2d6) damage, with the type unspecified in the trait. The herd’s gold coloration is described in the encounter.
- Giant Bat is on p. 42. Its appendix block includes both Echolocation and Keen Hearing.

All five reused portraits match their roster hashes and are recognizable in circular crops. The Fire Stirge’s wing tips and the Ice Cat’s tail run close to the crop edge. The Mastiff art depicts the appendix profile.
