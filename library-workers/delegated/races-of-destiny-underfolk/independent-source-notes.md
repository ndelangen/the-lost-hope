# Independent checks

The December 2004 traits are on printed p. 110, viewer p. 111. The contents point to p. 109. The printed list gives darkvision 60 ft., no natural armour, +4 racial Hide increasing to +10 in rocky terrain, and a dazzled condition under Light Sensitivity. The normal Hide requirements still apply. Drow appears in the racial language list, while Undercommon is in the language prose. The chapter gives no statted Underfolk encounter. Wizards' collected errata contains no *Races of Destiny* entry. [Printed traits]() · [Language prose]() · [Publisher errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Jeff Miracola drew the unnamed Underfolk barbarian on p. 109. Berren's portrait is original and follows the printed description's long silver hair, large eyes and ears, and rock-toned skin pigment. [Printed description]() · [Printed illustration]()

The catalog review checked both sets of class and racial skill points, the pick proficiency and attacks, all Hide totals with and without Camouflage, and the exact dazzled penalties. The Underfolk and Sea Kin patches applied in either order in temporary files.
