# Underfolk for D&D 3.5

One playable Underfolk entry from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*. The profile uses the printed 60-ft. darkvision and lists no racial natural armour. Camouflage is a +4 racial Hide bonus that becomes +10 total in rocky terrain. True seeing removes the bonus. [Printed traits, p. 110]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Light Sensitivity causes dazzled in bright sunlight or within a *daylight* spell. The encounter cards give the resulting penalties to attacks, Search, and Spot. The source notes also preserve Drow as the printed bonus language, with Undercommon mentioned in the cultural description. [Core condition](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/conditionSummary.htm#dazzled) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

**Berren Shalebank**, an original Expert and ore assayer, appears in the full-body plain-parchment portrait. **Lysa Chalkmark**, an original Ranger, keeps watch in a stone gallery. Berren is provisional CR 1/2 and Lysa is provisional CR 1. Their cards include ordinary and rocky-terrain Hide totals and tell you what changes under true seeing or bright light.
