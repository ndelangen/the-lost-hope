# Underfolk encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Human subtype, 60-ft. darkvision, and 30-ft. land speed. Camouflage gives each Hide +9 in ordinary terrain or +15 in rocky terrain. True seeing reduces either total to +5. Darkvision alone leaves the bonus in effect. Ordinary Hide requirements apply. [Playable Underfolk, printed p. 110]() · [Core Hide rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/skills/hide.htm)

Bright sunlight or a *daylight* spell causes dazzled. Subtract 1 from attack rolls as well as Search and Spot. Neither NPC's Listen total changes. [Dazzled](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/conditionSummary.htm#dazzled)

## Berren Shalebank, ore assayer

A disputed shipment is about to be mixed with the rest. Berren can compare the samples under the counting room's awning if the party finds the original merchant seal. **Provisional CR 1/2.**

Appraise +8, Craft (stonework) +5, Knowledge (local) +5, Listen +6, Search +5, and Spot +4. Leather armour and a light crossbow. Weapon Familiarity makes the heavy pick a simple weapon, which Berren can use as an Expert.

- **Heavy pick** +1 melee, 1d6+1 piercing, critical ×4, 5-ft. reach.
- **Light crossbow** +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, critical 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment. Reload as a move action.

## Lysa Chalkmark, gallery watch

An old stone sentry keeps passing the counterweight the repairers need to reach. Lysa can listen for its tread and guide the party along the ledge between passes. **Provisional CR 1.**

Listen +10, Spot +8, Search +4, Move Silently +5, and Survival +6. Alertness, Track, and Wild Empathy +0. Favoured enemy constructs raises weapon damage and relevant Bluff, Listen, Sense Motive, Spot, and Survival checks by +2 against constructs. Studded leather, light pick, and longbow.

- **Light pick** +2 melee, 1d4+1 piercing, critical ×4, 5-ft. reach.
- **Longbow** +3 ranged, 1d8 piercing, critical ×3, 100-ft. increment.
