# Derro rules sources

- *Underdark* lists 3d8 racial Hit Dice, LA +1, favored class Sorcerer, and ECL 4. Its adventurer description supports retaining the *Monster Manual*’s Madness trait. The *Monster Manual* gives a sane Derro LA +2, which starts at ECL 5. This profile uses the *Underdark* advancement. The collected errata mention Diirinka’s deity rules and give no correction to Derro advancement. [Underdark, pp. 19–20]() · [Monster Manual, p. 49]() · [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)
- The core monster-to-character conversion yields +4 Dexterity and +2 Constitution after backing out the sample’s Wisdom and Charisma adjustments. Madness then applies −6 Wisdom and +6 Charisma once and uses Charisma on Will saves. Both builds assume Common and buy Undercommon with two racial skill points because these entries give no dedicated language table. Dorr spends his other ten racial points on Bluff 3, Hide 3, Listen 2, and Move Silently 2. Mira spends hers on Bluff 3, Hide 4, and Move Silently 3, then spends her two Sorcerer skill points on Concentration. Both CRs are provisional encounter estimates. [Monster Manual, p. 49]() · [Core monster character rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monstersAsRaces.htm)
- The racial spell-like abilities have fixed caster level 3 and use Charisma for save DCs. Mira casts Sorcerer spells at caster level 1. Sunlight deals 1 Constitution damage per hour. Constitution 0 is fatal. A Derro recovers 1 point per 24 hours spent underground or otherwise sheltered. [Monster Manual, p. 49]()

Dorr’s dwarflike build, bulbous white eyes, coarse white hair, and drooping mustache follow the written description. Vance Kovacs illustrated the Derro in *Underdark*. [Description]() · [Illustration, p. 18]()
