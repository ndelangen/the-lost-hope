# Derro encounters

Both NPCs have 60-ft. darkvision, a 20-ft. land speed, and spell resistance 15. They use Charisma on Will saves. For every hour in sunlight, mark 1 Constitution damage. They recover 1 point per 24 hours underground or otherwise sheltered.

## Dorr Palevein, tunnel guide

**3 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 3.** AC 18, HP 23, initiative +7. Fortitude +3, Reflex +6, Will +6. Light crossbow +7 for 1d6 piercing. Hide +14 and Move Silently +9.

Dorr scouts from cover and uses *ghost sound* to draw attention away from the crossing. The illusion is Will DC 13 if someone interacts with it. Once per day he can use *sound burst* for 1d8 sonic damage and a DC 15 Fortitude save against its one-round stun.

## Mira Chalkwick, gallery archivist

**Sorcerer 1 with 3 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 4.** AC 16, HP 28, initiative +7. Fortitude +3, Reflex +6, Will +9. Light crossbow +7 for 1d6 piercing.

Mira checks the gallery with *mage armor* active, increasing AC to 20. If pursued, she uses her racial *sound burst* at caster level 3 for 1d8 sonic damage and Fortitude DC 16 against its one-round stun. Her Sorcerer *color spray* uses caster level 1 and Will DC 15.
