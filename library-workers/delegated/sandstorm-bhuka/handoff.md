# Bhuka from Sandstorm

This batch adds the D&D 3.5 Bhuka from the March 2005 first printing with two original encounters. Water Sense uses Survival to detect drinkable water within 100 ft., with the DC set by depth. Sure Feet makes shallow sand cost one square of movement and deep sand cost two. The encounter cards show both rules with numbers. Akari’s portrait uses the approved plain tan parchment and keeps her splayed feet visible. The temporary copy found the Bhuka by name, trait, and encounter role, returned both builds from both catalog commands, and resolved Akari’s portrait as the new list thumbnail.
