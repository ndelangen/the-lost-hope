# Bhuka encounters

Both are Medium Humanoids with the Goblinoid subtype, provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. Glare Resistance prevents dazzling from bright sunlight. They have +2 racial on Constitution checks or Fortitude saves against heat and dehydration. Heat Endurance separately gives +2 on saves against fire effects and heat protection level 1. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

Water Sense uses Survival to detect a drinkable source within 100 ft. Use the source’s depth below ground to choose the DC. [Player rules, p. 41]()

| Depth | Survival DC |
| --- | ---: |
| Surface | 10 |
| Up to 10 ft. below ground | 12 |
| 11–20 ft. | 15 |
| 21–40 ft. | 20 |
| 41–70 ft. | 25 |
| 71–100 ft. | 30 |

## Akari of the Reed Well, Druid 1

Akari leads the party to a bend in the dry channel. A drinkable seep lies 30 ft. below her and within range. Her Survival +12 meets DC 20 on an 8 or higher.

- HP 10, AC 14, initiative +2, land speed 30 ft. Knowledge (nature) +9.
- Quarterstaff +0 for 1d6 bludgeoning with one end.
- Create water produces up to two gallons for immediate relief. Entangle requires vegetation and has Reflex DC 14. Cure light wounds restores 1d8+1 HP.

## Taren of High Shelf, Ranger 1

Taren scouts the dune’s shallow shoulder before the wind covers the trail. A drinkable source 15 ft. down and within range uses DC 15. His Survival +6 succeeds on a 9 or higher.

- HP 10, AC 15, initiative +3, land speed 30 ft. Knowledge (nature) +6.
- On a straight move with 30 ft. of movement available, cross six shallow-sand squares or three deep-sand squares.
- Shortspear +2 for 1d6+1 piercing. Shortbow +4 for 1d6 piercing, or +5 for 1d6+1 within 30 ft. Favored enemy animals gives no bonus on the Water Sense check.
