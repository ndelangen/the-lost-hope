# Bhuka source check

The March 2005 first printing describes Bhuka on pp. 39–41 and gives the player rules on p. 41. The counter runs one ahead of the printed folios. The separate Warrior on p. 141 confirms Medium size and the Goblinoid subtype. It has printed CR ½. The staged builds are original first-level characters with provisional CR 1. [Player rules]() · [Warrior]()

Sure Feet treats shallow sand as normal and deep sand as shallow. The movement costs follow the terrain rules on p. 18. Water Sense uses a Survival check within 100 ft. The entry gives depth bands and DCs and supplies no action or retry limit. Glare Resistance applies to bright sunlight. The independent review checked the two builds’ Survival examples, attacks, saves, and skill ranks. [Sand travel]() · [Heat Endurance]()

Wizards’ collected archive has no Sandstorm errata file. The June 2008 FAQ has no Bhuka entry. Mitch Cotie illustrated the Bhuka elder on p. 40. The book’s long lashes, small neck frill, and splayed feet informed Akari’s portrait. Her clothing, pose, and staff are original.
