# Troglodyte rules sources

- *Underdark* lists two racial Hit Dice, LA +2, ECL 4 before class levels, and favored class Cleric. It refers to the *Monster Manual* for the detailed rules. The racial traits are −2 Dexterity, +4 Constitution, −2 Intelligence, 90-ft. darkvision, and +6 natural armor. Color-changing skin gives +4 to Hide, rising to +8 in rocky or underground surroundings. [Underdark, pp. 19–20]() · [Monster Manual, pp. 246–247]()
- Anger or fear triggers Stench, which reaches living creatures within 30 ft. Troglodytes and creatures immune to poison are exempt. Poison resistance applies its normal save bonus. Failure sickens for 10 rounds. Success grants 24-hour immunity to the same individual’s Stench. Both *delay poison* and *neutralize poison* remove the sickness. The sample has Constitution 14 and DC 13. The two builds recalculate DC from their Constitution modifiers and two racial Hit Dice. [Stench, p. 246]() · [Core special attack DCs]()
- The playable entry grants Multiattack and one racial Hit Die feat of choice. The sample monster chooses Weapon Focus (javelin). Both encounter CRs are provisional estimates. The official core errata cover Laogzed’s deity entry and leave the Troglodyte character rules as printed. [Troglodyte Characters, p. 247]() · [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Sethrik’s beady eyes, tapered tail, small claws, and head-to-neck frill follow the book’s description. The source illustration is signed “SW,” consistent with Sam Wood’s listing in the interior art credits. [Description and illustration, p. 246]()
