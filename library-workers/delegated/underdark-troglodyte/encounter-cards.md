# Troglodyte encounters

Both NPCs have 90-ft. darkvision, a 30-ft. land speed, and +6 natural armor. Anger or fear triggers Stench. Living creatures within 30 ft. roll Fortitude, including allies. Troglodytes and creatures immune to poison are exempt. Failure causes sickness for 10 rounds. Success protects against that individual’s Stench for 24 hours. *Delay poison* or *neutralize poison* removes the sickness.

## Sethrik Chalkscale, cistern guide

**2 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 1.** AC 17, HP 19, initiative +5. Fortitude +6, Reflex +1, Will +1. Stench DC 14. Hide +14 in rocky or underground surroundings.

Sethrik follows the dry seam along the cistern wall. If danger corners the group, he calls for companions who can be affected by his Stench to keep beyond 30 ft. On a full attack he uses two claws at +3 for 1d4+2 each and a bite at +1 for 1d4+1.

## Vess Siltcrest, grate warden

**Fighter 1 with 2 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 2.** AC 20, HP 31, initiative +4. Fortitude +9, Reflex +0, Will +0. Stench DC 15. Javelin +3 for 1d6+3 piercing.

Vess watches the grate with a javelin and shield. He switches to his club when a foe closes. The club attacks at +5 for 1d6+3 bludgeoning. On a full attack with the club and shield he adds a bite at +3 for 1d4+1.
