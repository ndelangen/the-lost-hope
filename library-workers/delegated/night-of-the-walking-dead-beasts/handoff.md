# Night of the Walking Dead beasts

Four AD&D 2e encounters from Bill Slavicsek's original 1992 TSR printing, RQ1. The selected rules follow the printed encounter blocks. The later Wizards online issue matches the local reference byte for byte and has different pagination and some expanded stats. [1992 print scan](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/vsya/) · [Archived online issue]() · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | 1992 print | Wizards online issue | HP in the encounter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crocodile | pp. 7–8 | pp. 7–8 | 14 |
| Giant Frog | p. 8 | p. 8 | 5, 8, and 10 |
| Giant Bat | p. 30 | p. 22 | 1d4 per bat |
| Giant Rat | p. 30 | p. 23 | 1d4 per rat |

The original bat and rat blocks give rolled HP and leave Hit Dice unstated. The staged patch adds optional rolled HP to the library's book profiles so both can display 1d4. The rat retains the printed neutral evil alignment, which the online issue omits. Its encounter names disease without giving a procedure. The other profiles keep the swamp surprise penalties, the frog's tongue attack bonus, and the bat's penalty to incoming missile attacks while flying. [Exact pages and image hashes](source-evidence.json) · [Rules and loader patch](rules.patch)

Four new square paintings use plain parchment. The crocodile and bat were reframed to leave wider margins around the snout, tail, and wings. [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [96-pixel previews](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json) · [Portrait index additions](portrait-index-preview.json)

In a temporary library copy, all four print profiles were searchable by name and encounter text and loaded the expected version portraits. Existing versions and galleries matched the baseline. An independent check verified that the loader accepts fixed HP, Hit Dice alone, and rolled HP and rejects malformed or conflicting HP values.
