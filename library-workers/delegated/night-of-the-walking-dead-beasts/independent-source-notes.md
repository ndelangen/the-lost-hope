# Night of the Walking Dead beast source check

Bill Slavicsek’s *Night of the Walking Dead* was published by TSR in 1992 as RQ1, product 9352, ISBN 1-56076-350-7. It is an AD&D 2nd Edition Ravenloft adventure. The original credits name Robh Ruppel for the cover and Stephen Fabian for interior art. The printed encounter pages give no creature-specific art credits or colors. [1992 print credits](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/vsya/files/large/8daff849ab33501816f8ed5bbf1020a8.webp) · [TSR’s contemporary announcement in Dragon 179]()

The 1992 print pages govern these profiles. In the scan viewer, page 8 shows print p. 7, page 9 shows print p. 8, and page 31 shows print p. 30. The local 29-page is the Wizards/Kargatane online issue, dated 2000 by its metadata. Its file matches the archived Wizards download byte for byte, SHA-256 `388126d23e743c8506b22961bbbab599bd7b477a7b5fbe997fa6bf2db0590ff5`. That issue repaginates the two mausoleums to pp. 22–23 and adds an errata page at p. 29 concerning continuity. The errata page does not document the stat presentation differences below. ## Crocodile

- Print p. 7 introduces the encounter and p. 8 gives the block. The online issue places the same material on pp. 7–8.
- Print values are AC 5, MV 6/12, HD 3, 14 hp, two attacks, damage 2–8/1–12, special defense surprise, alignment N, 65 XP each and THAC0 16. The scene has a single crocodile despite the experience line’s “each.” The online issue writes damage as 2d4/1d12 and adds Large size, average morale (9) and animal intelligence (1).
- The crocodile lurks among floating logs in the Souragne swamp. Probing the logs gives a 20% chance to spot it. Passing the logs without checking imposes a −2 penalty to the party’s surprise roll. It targets one character at the edge of the raft and tries to pull the victim into the water and flee. The adventure supplies no separate grab procedure and does not assign each damage figure to a particular body part. [Print p. 7](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/vsya/files/large/6e5a6a706573d43057464b50623c92f0.webp) · [Print p. 8](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/vsya/files/large/65e03aa82382f95fefecf01b7fcf2d.webp)

## Giant Frogs (3)

- Print p. 8 and online p. 8. Print values are AC 7, MV 3/9, HD 2, 5, 8 and 10 hp for the three, one attack, damage 1–6, special attack tongue, alignment nil, 175 XP each and THAC0 16. The online issue writes 1d6 damage and adds Medium size, average morale (8) and nonintelligence (0).
- Each frog measures four feet long and weighs 150 pounds. The long, sticky tongue gains +4 to attack and deals no damage. A hit reels the victim toward the bite. An attack roll of 20 can swallow a small-sized victim whole, including demihumans under four feet tall. The adventure gives no further swallowing procedure or escape rule. At their swamp feeding ground, the party rolls surprise at −3. Preserve that as the encounter’s opening condition. [Print p. 8](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/vsya/files/large/65e03aa82382f95fefecf01b7fcf2d.webp)

## Giant Bats (1d6 appear)

- Print p. 30, area 4, Decrepit Mausoleum, and online p. 22. Print values are AC 8, MV 3/18, random 1d4 hp each, one attack, damage 1–2, special attack nil, −3 to missile attacks against them while flying, alignment nil, 35 XP each and THAC0 20. The print gives no HD. The online issue clarifies movement as 3 and Fl 18, writes damage as 1d2, and adds HD ½, Medium size, unsteady morale (6) and animal intelligence (1).
- They live in the crumbling mausoleum and attack if the tomb is disturbed. The encounter rolls 1d6 for how many appear, then hp for each. The flight defense applies to incoming missiles. [Print p. 30](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/vsya/files/large/afd312e1d5c8dbb69a6b2478cb28f2e7.webp)

## Giant Rats (3d6 appear)

- Print p. 30, area 6, Tomb of Rats, and online p. 23. Print values are AC 7, MV 12/6, random 1d4 hp each, one attack, damage 1–3, special attack disease, special defense nil, alignment NE, 15 XP each and THAC0 20. The print gives no HD. The online issue writes damage as 1d3 and adds HD ½, Tiny size, unsteady morale (5) and semi-intelligence (3). The online block omits alignment and special defense.
- The rats defend their mausoleum, leaping from open burial vaults when the party enters. The encounter rolls 3d6 for how many appear, then hp for each. Both issues list disease without a type, likelihood, save or other procedure. [Print p. 30](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/vsya/files/large/afd312e1d5c8dbb69a6b2478cb28f2e7.webp)

All four creatures fit the cross-edition Beast category as animal forms. The module uses AD&D stat conventions and prints no 5e creature type or challenge rating. The cemetery encounters describe resident animals defending their lairs.
