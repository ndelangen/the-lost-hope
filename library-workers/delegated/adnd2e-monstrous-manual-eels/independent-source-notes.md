# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual eel source check

TSR’s *Monstrous Manual*, product 2140, was first published in 1993. The scanned physical copy identifies itself as the January 1994 third printing. Printed p. 117 (viewer 120) carries the four stat rows, Electric prose and the start of Giant prose. Printed p. 118 (viewer 121) finishes Giant and carries Marine and Weed. The reflowed electronic edition splits the corresponding material over pp. 351–353. ## Individual rules

- **Electric Eel.** The jolt affects creatures in a 15-foot radius around the eel. Damage is 3d8 at less than 5 feet, 2d8 from 5 through 10 feet and 1d8 farther away within range. The paragraph requires one hour of recharge between attacks in the context of its discharge. It gives no discharge attack roll or saving throw. The eel is immune to electrical effects. [Printed p. 117][117]
- **Giant Eel.** Teeth deal its listed damage. The eel has no electrical discharge and receives a +1 bonus to initiative rolls for its fast strike. The text gives no swallow trigger for the eel. [Printed p. 117][117] · [Printed p. 118][118]
- **Marine Eel.** The discharge has a 15-foot range. Damage is 6d6 at less than 5 feet, 4d6 from 5 through 10 feet and 2d6 farther away within range. A victim saves versus paralyzation or is stunned for a number of rounds equal to the electrical damage sustained. The save concerns the stun. The text specifies electrical immunity and gives no recharge time or separate discharge attack roll. [Printed p. 118][118]
- **Weed Eel.** A bitten victim who fails a save versus poison dies in 1d4 rounds. Weed Eels inhabit fresh and salt water 25–40 feet deep. The colony’s central cave measures roughly 30 feet long by 20 feet wide and high. The eels collect small stones, coins and gems there. Six-foot-diameter passages radiate to holes 6–8 inches across, which house individual eels. They defend the colony, especially the central nursery. The lair description supplies no attack, poison or defense modifiers. [Printed p. 118][118]

## Habitat and artwork

The Fresh Water summoning table lists Electric Eel under Monster Summoning II, slot 1. Marine Eel appears under VI, slot 1 in both the Salt Water and Fresh Water tables. Those are summoning associations. The dedicated Electric and Marine descriptions provide no natural range or water salinity limits. The Fresh Water V, slot 2 label is “Eel, giant electric.” The Fish entry explicitly gives Giant Eel no discharge. The appendix supplies no separate stat row or explicit link between these names. Giant Eel’s swim movement establishes an aquatic setting. Weed Eel has an explicit habitat in its own entry. [Printed p. 378][378] · [Printed p. 117][117] · [Printed p. 118][118]

The two Fish pages supply no eel illustration, color, markings, body measurements or eel-specific art credit. Page 117 has no creature art. The illustration on p. 118 depicts a shark. Giant Eel’s teeth and Weed Eel’s colony setting are direct visual cues. [Printed p. 117][117] · [Printed p. 118][118]

## Catalog and Corsairs overlap

The primary bestiary TSV has no matching *Monstrous Manual* row for these four names, in either natural or comma order. The staged *Corsairs of the Great Sea* Electric Eel is a separate AD&D 2e encounter profile. Its p. 24 (p. 25) supplies an individual 16 HP, a 9-foot length, size Large, neutral alignment and nonintelligence. Its radius, dice and once-per-hour timing correspond to the Manual’s Electric Eel. At exactly 5 feet, the *Corsairs* wording overlaps its two bands. The Manual puts that distance in the 2d8 band. The *Corsairs* block does not specify the Manual’s electrical immunity.
