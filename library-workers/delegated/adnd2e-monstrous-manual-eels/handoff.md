# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual eels

Electric, Giant, Marine, and Weed Eel come from the Fish entry on pp. 117–118 of the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. All four add Beast entries to the base catalog. The Electric Eel shares its catalog identity with the separately staged *Corsairs of the Great Sea* encounter, which has its own publication version and 16 HP. Both patches introduce Electric Eel at the same TSV insertion point. [Printed page 117](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bf2949b86e3fe804af0c021045ab0d44.webp) · [Printed page 118](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/981f6c8811613d3c1f2b6bf18210924c.webp) · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Eel | Printed rules |
| --- | --- |
| Electric | A discharge reaches 15 feet and deals 3d8, 2d8, or 1d8 according to distance. It takes an hour to recharge. The eel is immune to electricity. |
| Giant | Bites for 3–18 and gets +1 to initiative. Its description explicitly rules out an electrical discharge. |
| Marine | The ordinary attack has separate 6, 7, and 8 HD variants. Its discharge deals 6d6, 4d6, or 2d6 according to distance. A failed save vs. paralyzation stuns for rounds equal to damage. The text gives no recharge interval. |
| Weed | Its bite calls for a poison save. Failure kills in 1d4 rounds. Colonies occupy both fresh and salt water at 25–40 feet. The table prints 1−1 HD. |

The summoning appendix lists Electric Eel under Fresh Water and Marine Eel under both Fresh Water and Salt Water. Those are summoning associations. The appendix's separate “giant electric” name remains unassigned to the non-electric Giant Eel profile. [Summoning appendix, p. 378](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/86d43ae12f3d55ecb443ba0531c3dea8.webp)

The portraits show the entire body on plain parchment. The printed Fish entry has no eel illustration or assigned colors. The palettes and proportions are illustration choices. [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [96-pixel previews](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

Combining the handbook and *Corsairs* catalog rows in the temporary copy verified that the Electric Eel versions remain separately selectable.
