# Buomman source and review notes

The July 2004 first printing of *Planar Handbook*, ISBN 0-7869-3429-8, gives Buomman lore and traits on pp. 8–10. The printed page numbers match the scan. The November 2004 errata has no Buomman correction. Steve Prescott drew the unnamed Buomman monk on p. 9. The text gives a long face, elongated fingers and toes, and long hair and heavy brows in males. [Publisher preview]() · [Original profile]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The vow allows ritual singing. Page 10 names both Silent Spell and Nonverbal Spell as ways to cast while keeping it. The latter changes the words to another audible sound and remains subject to magical silence. Silent Spell removes the verbal component through metamagic. The encounter builds use the Material Plane to make the Extraplanar subtype’s application explicit. [Spellcasting and the vow, p. 10]() · [Nonverbal Spell, pp. 40–41]()

Independent review checked both skill budgets, the Monk’s post-vow changes, the Cleric’s domains and spell slots, and publication scoping. The two names are nicknames, following the book’s description of names sung in Buommi.
