# Buomman encounters

Both NPCs use D&D 3.5 rules and are provisional CR 1, ECL 1. They have low-light vision, speak Buommi and understand Common. Both are Humanoid (Extraplanar) for these encounters on the Material Plane. [Original traits, pp. 9–10]()

## Morrow, north gate listener

Lawful good Monk 1. HP 10, AC 15 (touch 15, flat-footed 13), speed 30 ft., initiative +6. Fort +4, Ref +4, Will +5. Listen +9, Perform (sing) +5, Sense Motive +7, Tumble +6.

- **Unarmed strike** +2 melee, 1d6+2 bludgeoning, ×2. Flurry is a full attack at +0/+0, 1d6+2 each. The flurry penalty applies to his other attacks before his next turn.
- **Stunning Fist** once per day. Declare before rolling. On a hit, Fortitude DC 13 or stunned for one round. A miss expends the use. Creatures immune to critical hits, constructs, oozes, plants, undead, and incorporeal creatures are immune.
- **Shuriken** +2 ranged, 1d2+2 piercing, ×2, range increment 10 ft.

After a first vow breach, Wisdom is 15. Use AC 14 (touch 14, flat-footed 12), initiative check +4, Fort +2, Ref +2, Will +2, Listen +6, and Perform (sing) +3. Unarmed and shuriken attacks are +0, and flurry is −2/−2. Stunning Fist drops to DC 12 until Wisdom recovers. Further breaches deal another point of Wisdom damage and extend the −2 penalty for another 24 hours.

Morrow points late visitors toward the sheltered gate and signals their keeper with a Buommi departure song. If a pursuer reaches him, he tries a stunning strike before retreating. [Vow rule, pp. 9–10]()

## Lumen, cairn road healer

Neutral good Cleric 1 of Fharlanghn, Protection and Travel domains. HP 10, AC 17 (touch 11, flat-footed 16), speed 30 ft., initiative +1. Fort +4, Ref +1, Will +5. Concentration +6, Heal +7, Knowledge (religion) +5. She also knows Terran.

- **Heavy mace** +1 melee, 1d8+1 bludgeoning, ×2. She keeps a hand free when a spell needs gestures. Nonverbal Spell lets her use audible song for verbal components without using a higher-level slot. Magical silence stops those castings.
- **Cantrips prepared.** *Detect magic*, *guidance*, and *light*, one use each. Caster level 1.
- **Bless** affects allies within a 50-ft. burst for one minute, granting +1 morale on attacks and saves against fear. **Shield of faith** gives +2 deflection AC for one minute. On Lumen it gives AC 19, touch 13, flat-footed 18. Each is prepared once. Lumen can spend either prepared spell instead on *cure light wounds* for 1d8+1 HP by touch.
- **Longstrider** occupies the separate Travel domain slot and gives land speed 40 ft. for an hour. Travel’s freedom of movement activates automatically against magical impediments for up to one round per day. Protection’s ward is a standard action by touch, once per day, for +1 resistance on the next save within one hour.
- **Turn undead** twice per day. Roll 1d20−1 on the turning check and 2d6 for total Hit Dice affected.

Lumen follows the cairns to reach an injured surveyor. She can cast *longstrider* to get there sooner and keep both ordinary 1st-level slots available for healing. [Nonverbal Spell, pp. 40–41]() · [Cleric class rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/cleric.htm)
