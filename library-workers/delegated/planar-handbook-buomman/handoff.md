# Buomman for D&D 3.5

The July 2004 first printing of *Planar Handbook* describes the Buomman vow against speech. Buommans communicate through Buommi ritual songs and understand Common. Breaking the vow deals 1 Wisdom damage and imposes −2 on attacks, saves, and checks for 24 hours. Another breach adds another point of Wisdom damage and extends the penalty. [Original rules, pp. 8–10]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Morrow**, Monk 1, has separate post-vow values for attacks, initiative, AC, saves, and Stunning Fist.
- **Lumen**, Cleric 1, casts with Nonverbal Spell and audible Buommi song. This keeps her vow and uses the normal spell slot. Magical silence stops the casting.

Both are provisional CR 1, ECL 1. Their encounters take place on the Material Plane, where both have the Extraplanar subtype. Morrow is the subject of the full-body parchment portrait.
