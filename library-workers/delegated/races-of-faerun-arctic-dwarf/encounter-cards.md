# Arctic Dwarf encounter cards

## Irsa Whitecurl

Ranger 1. Provisional CR 2, ECL 3. AC 14, HP 11, initiative +1, speed 20 ft. Fort +5, Ref +3, Will +1. Immune to cold.

- Small quarterstaff +6, 1d4+6 bludgeoning when using one end in two hands. Small sling +3, 1d3+4 bludgeoning, 50-ft. range increment.
- Favored enemy giants adds +2 damage with either weapon and raises Survival to +7 when tracking giants. Listen and Spot are +9 against giants. Dwarf dodge AC is 18 against giants while eligible for a Dexterity bonus.
- Irsa finds tracks beside cracked ice and asks the party to wait until she checks the crossing.

## Merec Iceknot

Fighter 2. Provisional CR 2, ECL 4. AC 18, HP 21, initiative +4, speed 20 ft. Fort +6, Ref +0, Will +1. Immune to cold.

- Small heavy pick +9, 1d4+5 piercing, critical ×4. With full Power Attack, +7 for 1d4+7.
- Small shortspear +3 thrown, 1d4+5 piercing, 20-ft. range increment. Dwarf dodge AC is 22 against giants while eligible for a Dexterity bonus.
- Merec asks the party to watch the flank until the last sled clears the pass.
