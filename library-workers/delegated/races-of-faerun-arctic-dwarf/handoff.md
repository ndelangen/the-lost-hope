# Arctic Dwarf

This batch adds the playable Arctic Dwarf, or Inugaakalikurit, from the March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn*. The 3.5 adaptation retains Small size, cold immunity, and favored class Ranger. It uses the revised Dwarf rules and the Great Glacier language table. [Original traits]()

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species, Irsa Whitecurl, and Merec Iceknot. It is based on catalog commit `9526861`. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) maps Irsa's [square portrait](portraits/arctic-dwarf-draft.png) to this publication.

Manual review covered the rules, both NPC builds, and the [thumbnail](portrait-thumbnails.jpg). Search, in-app portrait loading, and automated verification are deferred.
