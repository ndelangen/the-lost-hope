# Arctic Dwarf sources

The March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn* covers the Arctic Dwarf on printed pp. 9–11. The viewer is one page ahead. Page 10 gives Small size, +4 Strength, −2 Dexterity, +2 Constitution, −2 Charisma, immunity to cold, Ranger as favored class, and level adjustment +2. The entry gives no racial Hit Dice. Cold immunity comes from the player trait. The entry lists no Cold subtype or fire vulnerability. [Racial traits]()

The adaptation uses 3.5 Dwarf stability, dwarven weapon familiarity, and the 20-ft. movement rule for armor and loads. The Great Glacier table gives Dwarven and Uluik, with Common granted to PCs by the setting's general language rule. It offers Aquan, Auran, Damaran, Draconic, and Giant as bonus languages. The original Arctic Dwarf region repeats Common among its bonus choices. Irsa's Intelligence bonus takes Giant from the revised list. [3.5 Dwarf](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/races.htm#dwarves) · [Original regional rules]() · [Revised Great Glacier table]()

The 2001 *Monsters of Faerûn* encounter reference gives a sample CR ½ and lists advancement by character class. Irsa and Merec are original builds under the later player rules, with provisional CRs. *Frostburn* points to the *Races of Faerûn* Arctic Dwarf as an option in place of its separate Glacier Dwarf.
