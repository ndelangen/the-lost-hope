# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual giant beetle source check

TSR’s *Monstrous Manual* is an AD&D 2nd Edition rulebook, product 2140, first published in 1993. The inspected print scan’s credits page states third printing, January 1994, with the 1993 TSR copyright. In that scan, printed pp. 18–19 are one-based viewer pages 21–22. The complete table and the Bombardier and Boring descriptions are on p. 18. The Fire description and the family illustration are on p. 19. The reflowed electronic edition spreads the same material across pp. 78–80. The first-print catalog record is public, but its full scan is restricted. ## Giant Bombardier Beetle

- Any forest, common, solitary, day active, carnivore, Non- (0) intelligence, no treasure, neutral, 3–12 appearing. AC 4, movement 9, HD 2+2, THAC0 19, one attack for 2–12 damage, special attack acid cloud, special defense fire cloud, magic resistance nil, Small (4 ft. long), morale elite (13), 120 XP.
- An attacked or disturbed beetle has a 50% chance per round to turn its rear on the attacker and discharge an 8-foot spherical cloud of foul-smelling, reddish acid vapor from its abdomen. The discharge can occur every third round and at most twice in eight hours. Creatures in the cloud take 3d4 damage per round. The prose gives neither a cloud duration nor whether eight feet describes radius or diameter. It supplies no independent effect for the table’s fire cloud label.
- The sound has a 20% chance to stun creatures that can hear within 15 feet, followed by a 20% chance of deafening those that were not stunned. Stunning lasts 2d4 rounds and is followed by 2d4 rounds of deafness. Deafness from the separate check lasts 2d6 rounds. The source gives no saving throw for these effects.
- It lives above ground in woods, feeding mainly on offal and carrion and laying eggs in accumulated heaps. The vapor is reddish. There is no body color specified for this form. [Printed p. 18](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bf979c3e2feda7a98b369f4c896f4541.webp)

## Giant Boring Beetle

- Any land, common, solitary, night active, omnivore, Animal (1) intelligence, treasure C, R, S and T, neutral, 3–18 appearing. AC 3, movement 6, HD 5, THAC0 15, one attack for 5–20 damage, special attacks and defenses nil, magic resistance nil, Large (9 ft. long), morale elite (14), 175 XP.
- Its large mandibles deliver a powerful bite. It feeds on rotted wood and similar matter. The prose places individuals inside huge trees and groups in underground tunnel complexes. It describes humanlike communal reasoning as a rumor about whole nests. The individual stat remains Animal (1). Underground groups cultivate molds, slimes and fungi. Shrieker fungi serve as food and warn of intruders, to which the beetles respond quickly.
- The text gives no shell color for this form. [Printed p. 18](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bf979c3e2feda7a98b369f4c896f4541.webp)

## Giant Fire Beetle

- Any land, common, solitary, night active, omnivore, Non- (0) intelligence, no treasure, neutral, 3–12 appearing. AC 4, movement 12, HD 1+2, THAC0 19, one attack for 2–8 damage, special attacks and defenses nil, magic resistance nil, Small (2½ ft. long), morale steady (12), 35 XP.
- It bites with oversized mandibles and has no fire attack. The prose compares the bite loosely with three daggers. Use the table’s 2–8 damage.
- Two glands above its eyes and one near the back of its abdomen glow red and shed cold light in a 10-foot radius. The glands continue to glow for 1d6 days after removal. The light produces no heat. This is the smallest form in the table and occurs above and below ground. The glow is red and the source gives no body color. ## Art and catalog identity

All three use the family anatomy of paired wings, six bristly legs, chewing mandibles and a hard chitinous shell. The text says the wings cannot be used for flight. The signed family illustration on p. 19 shows a horned beetle carrying a rider. Tony DiTerlizzi identifies it in his art order and describes using a Venezuelan rhinoceros beetle as his pose reference. That image supports the overall silhouette and jointing of a giant beetle. These three profiles have no individual illustrations in the spread. [Printed illustration](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/2d16ae154a6a7a8025654797682b1bd3.webp) · [DiTerlizzi’s retrospective](https://diterlizzi.com/behind-the-monstrous-manual-part-8/) · [Original art order]()

The current primary catalog has no exact TSR 2140 match for these three forms. Giant Fire Beetle has detailed profiles for the 2024 *Monster Manual*, the 2018 *Basic Rules* and the Revised v3.5 SRD. Giant Bombardier Beetle has one in the Revised v3.5 SRD, and *The Old Margreve* has a separate *Beetle, Giant Bombardier* record. Giant Boring Beetle has no exact-name match. Beast describes their animal forms in the cross-edition catalog. The AD&D table supplies neither a modern creature type nor a challenge rating.
