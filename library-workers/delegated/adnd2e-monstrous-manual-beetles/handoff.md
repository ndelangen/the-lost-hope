# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual beetles

Three base profiles from the Giant Beetle table in TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. The checked pages are from the January 1994 third printing of the 1993 book. The table and the electronic text agree. [Printed table, p. 18](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bf979c3e2feda7a98b369f4c896f4541.webp) · [Continuation, p. 19](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/2d16ae154a6a7a8025654797682b1bd3.webp) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | AC | HD | Damage | Distinctive rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Bombardier Beetle | 4 | 2+2 | 2–12 | Its acid discharge has a printed cadence and separate stun and deafness checks. The table also lists a fire-cloud defense without a separate procedure. |
| Giant Boring Beetle | 3 | 5 | 5–20 | The book describes fungus farms and attributes reputed human-level reasoning to a whole nest. An individual has Animal intelligence (1). |
| Giant Fire Beetle | 4 | 1+2 | 2–8 | Three glands give cool red light. Its only printed attack is the bite. |

The book gives group counts of 3–12, 3–18, and 3–12 respectively. The profiles preserve their individual Hit Dice, THAC0, and XP. [Exact printing evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

Tony DiTerlizzi confirms that he illustrated the original family entry using a rhinoceros beetle as reference. These three plain parchment portraits are new illustrations specific to their forms. The Fire Beetle's red glands follow the text, and the other shell colors are illustration choices.
