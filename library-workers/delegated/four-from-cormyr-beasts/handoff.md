# Four from Cormyr beasts

Pernicon and Rot Grub come from John Terra's 1997 AD&D 2e adventure *Four from Cormyr*. Both appear in Area 12 of “The Bibliophile,” printed page 16 and page 18. The archived Wizards ZIP contains the same as the local reference, byte for byte. [Archived publisher download](https://web.archive.org/web/20101014100000id_/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/files/2/fr_downloads/tsr9531.zip) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

Searching the rubbish has a cumulative 10% encounter chance per turn. An encounter has a 50% chance of being 50 Pernicons and a 50% chance of being 10 Rot Grubs. Both creatures have 1 HP and award 15 XP per individual. The Pernicon swarm makes one attack at THAC0 20, with 1d10 damage and 1 point of Constitution drain on a hit. Rot Grubs use their own burrowing checks. The existing 5e Swarm of Rot Grubs keeps its versions and portraits. [Exact source pages and hash](source-evidence.json)

The new paintings depict individual creatures on plain parchment. The Pernicon's grasshopper-like body, colors, and rear pincers follow the earlier *Fiend Folio* description. The adventure does not give a Rot Grub color, so its pale larval palette is an art choice.
