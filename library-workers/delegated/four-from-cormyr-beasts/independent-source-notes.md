# Four from Cormyr beast source check

## Printing and encounter

John Terra’s *Four from Cormyr* is a 1997 TSR Forgotten Realms adventure for AD&D 2nd Edition, product 9531, ISBN 0-7869-0646-4. The credits and copyright are on printed p. 2 (page 4). Both creature blocks are on printed p. 16 (page 18), in Area 12, Ruined Corridor, in “The Bibliophile.” The visually checked is a digital facsimile of the 1997 print, with a local SHA-256 of `8c5f470dae14747f8be9c03b87fe22992eb16beae61226fcdc4dd828cb4bd7c5`. Organic and inorganic debris litters the corridor. Searching or handling it brings a cumulative 10% encounter chance per turn, with a 50% chance of either creature group each time an encounter occurs. Handling the debris becomes unavoidable on the route south. These triggers and group counts belong to Area 12. The adventure calls both creatures insects and gives them Tiny size, neutral alignment and nonintelligence. Beast fits the catalog’s cross-edition classification. These blocks predate 5e creature types and challenge ratings. The existing catalog’s three matching rot-grub names are 5e *Swarm of Rot Grubs* records from other publications.

## Pernicons (50)

- AC 3, MV 12, HD listed as 1 hp, 1 hp each, THAC0 20, one attack, 1d10 damage and Constitution drain, Tiny (1 inch long), morale unreliable (4), intelligence non- (0), alignment neutral, 15 XP each.
- Each successful swarm hit deals 1d10 damage and drains 1 Constitution point. At Constitution 2 the victim falls unconscious and at 0 the victim dies. Lost Constitution returns at 1 point per day. The adventure assigns hp and XP to individual insects and makes the attack a group action. It gives neither pooled swarm hp nor a rule for splitting the 50 individuals into multiple attacking groups.
- *Four from Cormyr* gives the size and debris encounter. The 2e *Monstrous Manual* describes large mandibles in its “Insect” entry. The earlier 1e *Fiend Folio* Pernicon has a grasshopper-like body, long water-sensing antennae, rear abdominal pincers and a bright red, yellow, ochre and light-blue palette. These details can guide the illustration. Use the adventure’s one-inch size, compared with the older entry’s two inches. [2e Monstrous Manual scan]() · [1e Fiend Folio scan, printed p. 72]()

## Rot Grubs (10)

- AC 9, MV 1 and Br 0, HD listed as 1 hp, 1 hp each, THAC0, number of attacks and damage inapplicable, Tiny (1 inch long), morale unsteady (5), intelligence non- (0), alignment neutral, 15 XP each. The special attack is burrowing into exposed flesh. The special weaknesses are fire and *cure disease*.
- A grub burrows into exposed flesh on a percentile chance equal to the target’s base AC. Fire kills 2d10 grubs if applied before 1d6 rounds of burrowing have elapsed. *Cure disease* kills all of them. Left unchecked, they reach the heart and kill in 1d3 turns. The ten grubs use individual rules and values. The module’s abbreviated procedure leaves the armor calculation and any further timing detail unstated.
- The adventure gives the length and refuse habitat. The “Worm” entry in the 2e *Monstrous Manual* describes maggot-like creatures found in refuse and supplies extra rules for that reference. The module and the manual give no color for the Rot Grub. A pale larval palette would be an illustration choice. [2e Monstrous Manual scan]()

The module credits Ciruelo Cabral for the cover and Ned Dameron for interior illustrations. The encounter page has decorative border art and no illustration of either creature. [Publisher-authored credits in a reference copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/544844515/Four-From-Cormyr)
