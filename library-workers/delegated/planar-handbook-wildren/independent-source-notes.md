# Wildren source check

The July 2004 printing introduces Wildren on pp. 16–17, with racial traits continuing onto p. 18. The November 2004 errata has no Wildren correction. Page 18 completes the automatic badger communication rule and lists the bonus languages, favored class, and +1 level adjustment. [First printing]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The two primary claws add full Strength to each damage roll. The core rules give claws both piercing and slashing damage. The independent review caught the draft’s incorrect damage type, now corrected in both builds. The reviewer also checked the skill ranks, attack bonuses, armor, stacking, and nine-round rage duration. [Natural weapon rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#naturalWeapons) · [Barbarian rage](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/barbarian.htm#rage)

The portrait follows the book’s description of dark fur, a broad white stripe, muscular forearms, and long claws. David Roach illustrated the unnamed Wildren on p. 17. Rusk’s clothing and pose are original. [Book illustration]()
