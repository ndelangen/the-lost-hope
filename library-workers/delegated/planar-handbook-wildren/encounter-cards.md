# Wildren encounters

Both encounters take place on the Material Plane, where Wildren have the Extraplanar subtype. Each is provisionally CR 2 and ECL 2. Both have darkvision 60 ft., scent, a 10-ft. burrow speed, and +4 to ability checks against bull rush and trip while firmly on the ground. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

Fury is available once per day. After a Wildren takes at least 1 point of damage, it can use Fury at the start of its next turn. It gains +4 Strength and takes −2 AC for one round. It cannot end Fury early.

## Rusk Ashbank, Barbarian 1

Rusk has the scent of a missing woodcutter. The bank is giving way, and he needs someone to keep the surface path open while he follows the trail.

- HP 16, AC 13, initiative +1, land speed 30 ft. in leather armor with a light load.
- Make one claw attack as a standard attack or both as a full attack. Each claw attacks at +4 for 1d4+3 piercing and slashing.
- Fury alone or rage alone gives +6 to hit for 1d4+5 with each claw and drops AC to 11. With both active, each claw attacks at +8 for 1d4+7 and AC drops to 9 for the round of overlap.
- Barbarian rage lasts 9 rounds if Rusk keeps it active for its full duration. Rage adds 2 HP for the duration and changes Fortitude to +8 and Will to +3. He is fatigued for the rest of the encounter when rage ends.

## Dara Cutbank, Fighter 1

Dara can ask the local badgers which branch is blocked. She guards the open tunnel while the party escorts the trapped workers out. In loose soil she can dig a warren large enough for one Medium creature in an hour.

- HP 14, AC 14, initiative +0, land speed 20 ft. in scale mail.
- Make one claw attack as a standard attack or both as a full attack. Weapon Focus (claw) gives +4 to hit with each claw for 1d4+2 piercing and slashing.
- During Fury each claw attacks at +6 for 1d4+4 and AC drops to 12 for one round.
