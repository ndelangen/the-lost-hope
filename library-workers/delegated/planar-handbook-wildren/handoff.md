# Wildren from Planar Handbook

This batch adds the D&D 3.5 Wildren from the July 2004 first printing and two original encounters. Fury starts at the beginning of the Wildren’s next turn after taking damage. It lasts one round and can overlap barbarian rage. The two primary claws can both attack in a full attack. [Book, pp. 16–18]() · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

Rusk Ashbank shows what happens when Fury and rage overlap. Dara Cutbank keeps her 20-ft. land speed in scale mail and can ask badgers about the blocked tunnel. Both builds are provisionally CR 2 and use one class level with LA +1. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

Rusk’s square portrait uses the same plain parchment as Big Linda. The temporary catalog found Wildren by name, rules text, and NPC role. Both CLI commands returned the two builds, and the new profile supplied the verified portrait and list thumbnail.
