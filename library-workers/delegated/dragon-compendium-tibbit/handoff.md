# Tibbit for D&D 3.5

The October 2005 first printing of *Dragon Compendium, Volume I* gives Tibbits a Small humanoid form and a Tiny house-cat form. Paizo’s errata corrects the subtype to Shapechanger. Taking cat form is a standard action. Returning takes a full-round action and starts a one-hour wait before the next change. [First printing, p. 25]() · [Paizo errata]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Pell Fenwick**, Rogue 1, scouts as a cat and changes back to use his thieves’ tools. His Hide bonus rises from +10 to +16 after the change.
- **Rusk Brindle**, Fighter 1, can attack with Weapon Finesse in cat form. His AC drops from 19 to 16 as his armor and shield meld, even though his Dexterity and size bonuses increase.

Both encounters are provisional CR 1, with separate numbers for each form. The plain-parchment portrait shows Pell in both forms. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Parchment comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [96 px preview](portrait-thumbnails.jpg)

The temporary copy found Tibbit by name, traits, and both NPC roles. Both encounter commands returned the full builds. The new species supplies its own browse thumbnail. Existing Cat beast, Cat monster, and Cat Form monster profiles matched the baseline. The Tibbit patch also applied after Dragonborn of Bahamut.
