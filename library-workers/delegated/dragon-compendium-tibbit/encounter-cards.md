# Tibbit encounters

Both NPCs are D&D 3.5, provisional CR 1 and ECL 1. Both forms keep darkvision 60 ft. Cat form gains scent and melds armor and weapons. It prevents speech, fine manipulation, spell components that require speech or gestures, and magic-item activation. Enter cat form as a standard action. Return as a full-round action, then wait one hour before changing again. Tiny cat form has 0-ft. reach. Entering an enemy’s space to attack normally provokes. [Feline Transformation, p. 25]()

## Pell Fenwick, mews courier

Chaotic good Rogue 1. HP 8 in either form.

| Form | AC (touch, flat-footed) | Speed | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will | Hide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small humanoid | 17 (14, 14) | 20 ft. | +7 | +2, +5, +1 | +10 |
| Tiny cat | 16 (16, 12) | 30 ft. | +8 | +2, +6, +1 | +16 |

- **Humanoid.** Small dagger +1 for 1d3 piercing or slashing, 19–20/×2. Small light crossbow +4 for 1d6 piercing, 19–20/×2, range increment 80 ft. Move action to reload. Search and Disable Device +5, Open Lock +7. Trapfinding applies, including magical traps.
- **Cat.** Claw −2 for 1 damage, or full attack with two claws at −2 and bite −7 for 1 damage each. Move Silently +8, Escape Artist +10.
- **Sneak attack +1d6.** Cat form cannot flank with 0-ft. reach and can still sneak attack a target denied Dexterity to AC. Ranged sneak attack requires a target within 30 ft.

Pell is retrieving a misdelivered letter. He scouts past the porter as a black cat, changes in an alcove, and takes out his tools to open the tack-room lock.

## Rusk Brindle, grain loft watch

Chaotic neutral Fighter 1. HP 11 in either form.

| Form | AC (touch, flat-footed) | Speed | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will | Jump |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small humanoid | 19 (14, 16) | 20 ft. | +7 | +3, +3, +1 | +0 |
| Tiny cat | 16 (16, 12) | 30 ft. | +8 | +3, +4, +1 | +5 |

- **Humanoid.** Small longsword +5, 1d6+3 slashing, 19–20/×2. Small javelin +5, 1d4+3 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft.
- **Cat.** Weapon Finesse puts the primary claws at +7 and secondary bite at +2. A claw hits for 1 damage. On a full attack use both claws and the bite, which deals 1d3−1 damage, minimum 1.

Rusk watches the grain loft after closing. He uses scent if something moves near the loft opening and changes behind the sacks if the storekeeper needs an armed guard.
