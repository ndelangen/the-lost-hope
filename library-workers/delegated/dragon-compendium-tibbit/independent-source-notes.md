# Tibbit source and review notes

Scott Bennie’s Tibbit first appeared as an AD&D monster in *Dragon* #135 (July 1988), printed p. 14. Its falling immunity, psionics, and rear-claw follow-up belong to that version. The playable D&D 3.5 rules used here come from the October 2005 first printing of *Dragon Compendium, Volume I*. Printed pp. 21–25 correspond to pages 25–29 in the stored source. [1988 entry]() · [2005 chapter]()

The first printing uses “shapeshifter,” and Paizo’s errata supplies Shapechanger. The type stays Monstrous Humanoid in both forms. The darkvision paragraph has a stray reference to Lupins after explicitly granting Tibbits 60-ft. darkvision. [Printed traits]() · [Paizo errata]()

The p. 22 paired portrait is a source for the humanoid and cat proportions. Tom Fowler is credited in the margins accompanying the adjacent illustrations. Pell’s deep brown skin, black hair, pointed ears, amber eyes, and black house-cat form are an original combination. [Paired source portrait]()

An independent rules review checked both skill budgets, normal and cat-form AC, saves, grapple and attacks, and the 0-ft.-reach restriction on flanking. The temporary fixture also tested the raw patch, exact version portrait, and the separate Cat and Cat Form identities.
