# Independent Warforged reviews

The source reviewer checked the June 2004 first printing. Steve Prescott drew the Warforged on p. 21. The original rules describe a constructed humanoid with fitted plates and fibers at the joints. The playable traits span pp. 23–24. [Original artwork]() · [Wizards art gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20161101150242/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/eb/20040618a)

The February 2006 errata restricts the ban on natural healing to lethal damage and confirms that the p. 46 Craft repair procedure requires a trained skill. Page 46 allows taking 10 and bars taking 20. The FAQ clarifies that Adamantine Body carries the printed heavy-armor restrictions and does not require Heavy Armor Proficiency to avoid extra penalties. The ordinary natural slam is distinct from a gauntlet or an unarmed strike. [Official errata]() · [Wizards FAQ archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Published profiles include Cutter, a CR 1 Barbarian with Mithral Body, on pp. 308–309, and Saber, a CR 3 Fighter with Adamantine Body, on p. 317. The errata lists Cutter’s rage at 7 rounds and Saber’s touch AC at 11. Swage and Keel are original builds. [Cutter]() · [Saber]()

The second reviewer checked Swage’s 24 skill points, three infusions, 20 XP craft reserve, scroll-specific Use Magic Device bonus, and both repair methods. The review also checked Keel’s skill budget, armor, armor check penalty, speed, and slam. Exact publication matching attaches both NPCs to the 2004 profile.
