# Warforged for D&D 3.5

One playable Warforged version from the June 2004 first printing of *Eberron Campaign Setting*. The profile covers Living Construct, Composite Plating, Light Fortification, the slam, injury states, and the optional Adamantine Body feat. The February 2006 errata clarifies natural healing and trained Craft repair. Nonlethal damage can recover naturally. Healing-subschool spells and qualifying supernatural healing work at half effect, and *repair light damage* works at full effect. [Printed rules, pp. 23–24]() · [Official errata]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Swage is an original Artificer 1 and latch maker with ordinary Composite Plating. Keel is an original Fighter 1 and drain gate watch with Adamantine Body. Both are provisional CR 1. Their cards show the repair amounts, plated defenses, and slam totals. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [NPC builds](npc-builds.json)

Swage’s full-body portrait is on plain parchment, with fitted plates, exposed binding at the joints, and a brass hinge. [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Style comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [96 px thumbnail](portrait-thumbnails.jpg)

The temporary copy checks search by name, trait, and role, both encounter lookups, and version portrait resolution. The two fifth-edition profiles match their baselines. Under the current sort, the data and portrait make the 2004 profile the default and Swage the list thumbnail. The prior default was *Eberron: Forge of the Artificer*.
