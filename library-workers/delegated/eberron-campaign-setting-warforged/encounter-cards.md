# Warforged encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. Both are Medium Constructs with the Living Construct subtype. They gain Light Fortification, a 25% chance to roll normal damage instead of the critical hit or sneak attack. They function without food, sleep, or air. [Original rules, pp. 23–24]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Swage, latch maker

Artificer 1. Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 13, touch 11, flat-footed 12. HP 8, initiative +1, speed 30 ft. Fort +2, Ref +1, Will +2. BAB +0, grapple +0. STR 10, DEX 13, CON 14, INT 15, WIS 10, CHA 10. Common.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Slam | +0 melee, 1d4 bludgeoning, 5-ft. reach |
| Light crossbow | +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment. Reload as a move action. |

Concentration +6, Craft (armorsmithing) +9, Disable Device +6, Search +6, Spellcraft +6, Use Magic Device +4 or +6 when activating scrolls. Scribe Scroll and 20 XP in the craft reserve. Artificer Knowledge +3, DC 15 after one minute examining an item. Disable Trap covers difficult Search checks and disabling magic traps.

Three 1st-level infusions per day at caster level 1. Two are base and one is from Intelligence. Composite Plating imposes 5% failure on arcane spells. Infusions are neither arcane nor divine. [Artificer rules, pp. 30–31]()

| Option | At Swage’s level |
| --- | --- |
| *Repair light damage* | Standard action, touch, instant, 1d8+1 HP. Ordinary construct needs at least 1 HP. Living construct needs at least −9 HP. No save or spell resistance. |
| *Skill enhancement* | One round, touch an item using fine black thread. The wearer or wielder gets +2 circumstance on checks with a specified skill for 10 minutes. |
| Trained Craft repair | Eight hours. Taking 10 with Craft +9 gives 19, restoring 4 HP. Swage can use it on themself. |

Swage needs the worn hinge to make a replacement that fits the lift cage. The party can retrieve it while Swage repairs the lift attendant. [Craft repair, p. 46]() · [*Repair light damage*, p. 114]()

## Keel, drain gate watch

Fighter 1. Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 21, touch 11, flat-footed 20. HP 13, initiative +1, speed 20 ft. Fort +5, Ref +1, Will +0. BAB +1, grapple +3. STR 15, DEX 12, CON 16, INT 12, WIS 10, CHA 8. Common.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Warhammer | +4 melee, 1d8+2 bludgeoning, ×3, 5-ft. reach |
| Javelin | +2 ranged, 1d6+2 piercing, ×2, 30-ft. increment |
| Primary slam with a free arm | +3 melee, 1d4+3 bludgeoning, 5-ft. reach |

Climb −1, Craft (armorsmithing) +5, Intimidate +3. Adamantine Body grants +8 armor AC and DR 2/adamantine. The heavy steel shield adds +2 AC. Combined armor check penalty −7. Body plating has a maximum Dexterity bonus of +1 and 35% arcane spell failure. The warhammer’s attack includes Weapon Focus. [Adamantine Body, p. 50]()

Keel can remain below the waterline to hold the drain gate open. The party can lead the crew to the landing while Keel guards the opening.

## Injury and healing

At 0 HP either NPC gets one move or standard action per round, without the usual risk of losing HP from exertion. From −1 to −9 HP, they are inert, unconscious, helpless, and stable until harmed again. They can recover nonlethal damage naturally. Healing-subschool spells and supernatural effects that heal HP or ability damage work at half effect. *Repair light damage* uses the full rolled amount.
