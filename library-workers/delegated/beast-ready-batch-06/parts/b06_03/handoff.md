# Cave Bear, Giant Lizard variants, diseased Giant Rat, and Saber-Toothed Tiger

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Reused Cave Bear portrait](../../../adnd2e-monstrous-manual-bears/portraits/cave-bear-draft.png)

I read the September 2014 first-print *Monster Manual* scan and checked the cited folios visually. The counts the cover as page 1, so its page numbers are one ahead of the book folios. The only change applied from the official errata is the diseased rat's Bite. [Official Monster Manual errata]()

| Existing catalog scope | Printed folio | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cave Bear | [334](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page335.html) | The sidebar gives coarse dark hair and darkvision 60 ft. The rest comes from Polar Bear, including swim 30 ft., AC 12, 42 HP, and CR 2. |
| Diseased Giant Rat and Giant Rat (Diseased) | [327](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page328.html) | Both spellings use the same sidebar. The corrected Bite is +4 for 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing, replacing the first print's +3 for 3 (1d4 + 1). Its DC 10 Constitution save and 24-hour maximum HP loss follow the printed sidebar. |
| Giant Lizard (Hold Breath and Spider Climb) | [326](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page327.html) | This existing scope applies both traits. The lizard can hold its breath for 15 minutes, swim 30 ft., and climb difficult surfaces and ceilings without a check. |
| Giant Lizard (variant) | [326](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page327.html) | The generic scope allows one or both published traits. Only Hold Breath adds the swim speed. The Bite and variant box continue at the top of the right column. |
| Saber-Toothed Tiger | [336](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page337.html) | Pounce uses a 20-ft. approach, a Claw hit, and a DC 14 Strength save. Both damage formulas retain the printed +5 even though Strength is +4. |

The Cave Bear reuses the 1254 × 1254 portrait staged for the AD&D *Monstrous Manual*. Its dark coat matches the 2014 variant description. The reused art hashes match the roster.
