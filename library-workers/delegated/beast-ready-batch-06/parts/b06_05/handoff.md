# Five swarms

I checked the September 2014 *Monster Manual* scan and its official errata. All five swarms resist bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage. The errata adds immunity to grappled alongside the printed condition immunities. The Swarm trait lets another creature share the swarm's space, lets it pass through openings that fit one Tiny member, and prevents regaining HP or gaining temporary HP. [Monster Manual errata, p. 2]()

The snake swarm deals the same 14 (4d6) poison damage at either health tier, halved on a successful DC 10 Constitution save. The raven swarm's Beaks lists a 5-ft. reach and specifies a target in its own space. The other four attacks list 0-ft. reach.
