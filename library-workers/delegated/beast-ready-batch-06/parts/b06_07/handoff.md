# Spider and wasp swarms

Four encounter summaries retain the two variant names and their parenthesized catalog labels. The *Monster Manual* prints the Swarm of Insects stat block and the adjacent *Variant: Insect Swarms* sidebar on p. 338. Both variants use AC 12, 22 HP (5d8), and CR 1/2 (100 XP). The official erratum adds immunity to the grappled condition to every swarm. [Printed folio 338](https://online.anyflip.com/sqwmo/hxog/mobile/index.html#p=339) · [Monster Manual errata, p. 2]()

- Spiders keep the base walking and climbing speeds of 20 ft. each and blindsight 10 ft. The sidebar adds Spider Climb, Web Sense, and Web Walker.
- Wasps walk 5 ft. and fly 30 ft. The sidebar removes the base climbing speed. Blindsight stays 10 ft.
- Both retain Bites at +3 to hit, reach 0 ft. and one target in the swarm's space. A hit deals 10 (4d4) piercing, or 5 (2d4) at half HP or fewer. Neither variant gets a poison rider.

I read the original folio and its sidebar side by side, checked the erratum against the first-print immunity line, and matched both indexed portrait hashes to the roster. The reused art supplies the appearance details. The habitat tags are editorial.
