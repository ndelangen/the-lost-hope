# Warhorse barding

All seven records use the Warhorse and Warhorse Armor table on printed p. 340 of the September 2014 first printing. The table supplies each barding AC and keeps the base Warhorse challenge rating. The official errata correct Hooves from +4 to +6 to hit. [Printed folio](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/index.html#p=341), [Monster Manual errata, p. 2]()

| Barding | AC |
| --- | ---: |
| Chain Mail | 16 |
| Leather | 12 |
| Plate | 18 |
| Ring Mail | 14 |
| Scale Mail | 15 |
| Splint | 17 |
| Studded Leather | 13 |

Each variant has 19 HP, 60 ft. speed, CR 1/2 (100 XP), and Hooves at +6 to hit for 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning. Trampling Charge retains its 20-ft. straight movement requirement, DC 14 Strength save against falling prone, and bonus Hooves attack against the prone target.

The armor rules give Stealth disadvantage to chain, plate, ring, scale, and splint barding. The Warhorse's Strength 18 meets every listed Strength threshold. [2014 equipment rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment)

All seven reuse the verified `riding-warhorse` portrait from batch 05. It depicts a light-bay horse on plain parchment. The individual records name the fitted barding.
