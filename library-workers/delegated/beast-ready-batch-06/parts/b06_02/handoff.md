# Eight beasts from the 2018 Basic Rules

| Beast | and print page | Encounter check |
|---|---:|---|
| Crocodile | 122 | Holds its breath for 15 minutes. Bite grapples on a hit, with escape DC 12. The target remains restrained while grappled. |
| Elk | 125 | Charge adds 2d6 after a straight 20-ft. approach and Ram hit. A creature hit makes a DC 13 Strength save or falls prone. Hooves requires a prone target. |
| Lion | 142 | Pounce uses DC 13. Running Leap allows a 25-ft. long jump after a 10-ft. start. |
| Mammoth | 143 | Trampling Charge uses DC 18. Gore and Stomp continue in the right column above Manticore. |
| Panther | 148 | Climb 40 ft. and Pounce DC 12. Pounce can grant a bonus-action Bite against the prone target. |
| Saber-Toothed Tiger | 151 | Pounce uses DC 14. Both attacks are +6 to hit with a printed damage modifier of +5. |
| Weasel | 158 | Fixed 1 piercing damage on a Bite hit. Skills, senses, traits, and Bite continue in the right column above Werewolf. |
| Wolf | 159 | Pack Tactics and Bite at +4 for 2d4+2. A creature hit makes a DC 11 Strength save or falls prone. |

I read all eight entries in the November 2018 v1.0 . The tiger's indexed name retains the roster's exact punctuation. I also checked that the MM errata was not applied to this Basic Rules scope.
