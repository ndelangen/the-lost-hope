# Beast-ready batch 06, group 06

Five scoped *Monster Manual* records are staged. I checked the September 2014 first printing, the insect swarm sidebar on printed p. 338, and the official 2018 errata. The base stats are AC 12, HP 22 (5d8), and CR 1/2 (100 XP).

| Roster names | Printed rules |
| --- | --- |
| Swarm of Insects | Base block. Walk and climb 20 ft. |
| Swarm of Beetles, Swarm of Insects (Beetles) | Use the Swarm of Beetles sidebar. Add a 5-ft. burrow speed. |
| Swarm of Centipedes, Swarm of Insects (Centipedes) | Use the Swarm of Centipedes sidebar. A creature the swarm reduces to 0 HP becomes stable and remains poisoned for 1 hour even if it regains HP. It is paralyzed while poisoned this way. |

All five retain the printed Bites damage and gain grappled immunity under the errata. Each alias keeps its roster name and points to the same printed variant rules. The three reused portraits matched their roster SHA-256 values.
