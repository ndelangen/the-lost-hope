# Seven beasts from the 2018 Basic Rules

I checked all seven entries against the November 2018 v1.0 . Page numbers are the printed folios and match the viewer. [Wizards of the Coast ]()

| Beast | Page | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ape | 117 | AC 12, 19 HP. Multiattack uses two Fist attacks. Rock is a separate +5 ranged attack at 25/50 ft. |
| Axe Beak | 118 | AC 11, 19 HP, 50-ft. speed. Beak +4 deals 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing. |
| Baboon | 118 | AC 12, 3 HP, 30-ft. walk and climb. Pack Tactics and Bite +1 for 1 (1d4 - 1) piercing. |
| Badger | 118 | AC 10, 3 HP, 5-ft. burrow, 30-ft. darkvision and Keen Smell. Its +2 Bite continues at the top of the right column and deals 1 piercing. |
| Boar | 120 | AC 11, 11 HP. Charge adds 3 (1d6) slashing to a qualifying hit and calls for a DC 11 Strength save against prone if the target is a creature. Relentless recharges after a short or long rest. |
| Camel | 121 | AC 9, 15 HP, 50-ft. speed. Bite uses the printed +5 attack and 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage. |
| Cat | 121 | AC 12, 2 HP, 40-ft. walk and 30-ft. climb. Keen Smell and Claws +0 for 1 slashing. |

All seven selected portrait SHA-256 hashes matched the roster. Cat uses the indexed portrait and the other six use the batch 02 portraits. The habitat tags are editorial suggestions.
