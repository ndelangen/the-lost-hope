# Hoard of the Dragon Queen beasts

Four stat blocks verified against the August 8, 2014 version 1 Online Supplement. The local printer-friendly matches the publisher’s archived file byte for byte. Its pages and printed folios use the same numbers. [Official version 1 ]()

| Beast | Folio | Table details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Frog | 10 | Bite grapples on a hit, with escape DC 11. Swallow requires a Bite against a grappled target no larger than Small. Once swallowed, the creature takes acid damage at the start of the frog’s turns. If the frog dies, the creature can spend 5 ft. of movement to exit prone. |
| Giant Lizard | 10 | AC 12, 19 HP and 30-ft. climb. The supplement gives the base Bite block. |
| Giant Spider | 10 | Bite uses a DC 11 Constitution save for poison damage. If that poison damage reduces the target to 0 HP, it becomes stable and poisoned for 1 hour, with paralysis while poisoned this way even after it regains HP. Breaking Web uses a DC 12 Strength check. |
| Stirge | 17 | Blood Drain starts with a piercing hit and attachment. Later feeding causes hit-point loss at the start of the stirge’s turns. It detaches after draining 10 HP or when the target dies, and any creature can detach it as an action. |
