# Hoard of the Dragon Queen Online Supplement beasts

Four Beast overlays use the supplement released August 8, 2014, version 1. I compared the supplied local with the publisher's download. The files have different hashes and identical extracted text on the three folios in scope. I read each printed block in its page layout. The official adventure errata lists no change to these entries. [Supplement ]() · [Adventure errata]()

| Beast | Printed and page | Checked core values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crocodile | [7]() | AC 12, HP 19 (3d10 + 3), CR 1/2, 100 XP. Bite +4 for 7 (1d10 + 2), grapple and restraint, escape DC 12. |
| Deer | [7]() | AC 13, HP 4 (1d8), CR 0, 10 XP. Bite +2 for 2 (1d4) piercing. |
| Elk | [8]() | AC 10, HP 13 (2d10 + 2), CR 1/4, 50 XP. Ram and Hooves both +5. Charge adds 2d6 damage and can knock a creature prone on a failed DC 13 Strength save. |
| Giant Centipede | [9]() | AC 13, HP 4 (1d6 + 1), CR 1/4, 50 XP. Bite +4 for 4 (1d4 + 2), then DC 11 Constitution or 10 (3d6) poison. |

The Elk's extra Charge damage has no type specified in this release. Hooves is an action against a prone creature. The Giant Centipede's hour-long poison and paralysis apply when its poison damage reduces the target to 0 HP. I checked that wording in the right column of p. 9, next to the separate Ettercap block.

All four reused portraits matched the roster SHA-256 values. I reviewed the square images and their 96-pixel circular previews. The new portrait manifest is empty. Appearance and habitat tags are editorial.
