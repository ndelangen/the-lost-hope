## Lizard, Fire

- Printed p. 19, page 20, Crystal Labyrinth encounter 2, *Where There’s Smoke*. The scene has one awake fire lizard. AC 3, MV 9, HD 10, 60 hp, THAC0 11, three attacks, damage 1–8/1–8/2–16, magic resistance nil, Gargantuan (30 ft.), morale steady (11), animal intelligence (1), neutral and 3,000 XP.
- Its attacks are two raking claws and a bite. It can breathe fire simultaneously. The cone is 5 feet wide at the mouth, 10 feet wide at the far end and 15 feet long. It deals 2–12 fire damage, halved on a successful save vs. breath weapon. The lizard is immune to fire-based attacks. The adventure gives no reuse limit or recharge.
- In the maze’s chamber with opaque walls, the party interrupts the lizard eating a gibberling. It follows if the party retreats immediately and withdraws to nurse its wounds if it comes under serious attack. Those responses belong to the scene.
- The adventure describes reddish scales, a bright red underside, massive fangs and flames around its mouth. The 2e *Monstrous Manual* classifies the creature under “Lizard” and describes a wingless red-dragon-like silhouette with a gray base, mottled red and brown back and reddish underside. Use the adventure’s brighter colors for this specimen. The p. 19 illustration shows adventurers and a victim caught in wisps, consistent with the preceding crimson death encounter. There is no identified fire lizard illustration on that page. Animal intelligence and its placement in the 2e Lizard family support the Beast classification, with “false dragon” as a species nickname. [Adventure p. 19]() · [2e Monstrous Manual, Lizard]()

## Bookworm (50)

- Printed p. 28, page 29, Area 2B, *The Study*. The block gives AC 2, MV 12, Br 3, 1 hp per worm across 50 worms, THAC0 nil, zero attacks and damage, special attack nil, magic resistance nil, Tiny (1 inch long), morale unreliable (2), nonintelligence (0), neutral and XP 15. The HD field is blank in print. The 2e *Monstrous Manual* gives its generic Bookworm ¼ HD and XP 15, which can be cited separately. Preserve the adventure’s printed 1 hp each.
- The worms change color to match their surroundings and impose −6 on opponents’ surprise rolls. The number 50 belongs to the study contingency, which releases the worms into the affected shelf and its contents if a living being touches something on a shelf. The party has about five rounds to save 1d12 books, tomes, parchments or maps, with only 1d4 of the recovered materials in non-illithid languages. The block describes 50 individual worms with no attack and provides no pooled swarm statistics. Their effect on the library comes from their feeding and the scene’s trigger.
- The study page has no creature art. The 2e *Monstrous Manual* places Bookworms among long, cylindrical burrowers and gives them a normal gray color. They consume pages and bindings, can burrow through dead wood and leather, cannot eat living matter and try to avoid capture. These details can guide appearance. Its “Worm” illustration depicts a purple worm. [Adventure p. 28]() · [2e Monstrous Manual, Worm table]() · [2e Monstrous Manual, Bookworm description]()
