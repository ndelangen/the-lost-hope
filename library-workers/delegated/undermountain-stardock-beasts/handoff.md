# Undermountain: Stardock beasts

Two AD&D 2e encounter profiles from Steven E. Schend's *Undermountain: Stardock*, TSR 9538, copyright 1996. The in the archived Wizards ZIP matches the local reference byte for byte. [Archived publisher download](https://web.archive.org/web/20101014100000id_/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/files/2/fr_downloads/tsr9538.zip) · [Publisher preview]() · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Print and pages | Encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire Lizard | p. 19, p. 20 | One 60 HP lizard in the Crystal Labyrinth. It can breathe fire while attacking with its claws and bite. |
| Bookworm | p. 28, p. 29 | The shelf contingency releases 50 worms with 1 HP each. The printed block leaves Hit Dice blank. |

The Fire Lizard's cone and retreat behavior are in its encounter profile. The Bookworm keeps its camouflage bonus and the study's roughly five-round document-rescue window. [Exact source pages and hashes](source-evidence.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

The module credits Nicholas Jainschigg for its cover and Earl Geier for interior art. The Fire Lizard page illustrates the preceding encounter, and the Bookworm page has no identified creature art. The two plain parchment paintings are new. The Fire Lizard uses the adventure's reddish scales and bright underside and has extra room around its tail. The Bookworm's gray coloring follows the general 2e creature description.
