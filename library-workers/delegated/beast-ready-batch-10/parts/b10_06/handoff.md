# Beast-ready batch 10, group 06

Five revised Monster Manual profiles are staged from their individual publisher pages. Each includes its printed initiative bonus and score, all six saves, proficiency bonus, and habitat. The current official errata has no changes for these creatures.

[Stat blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

All three swarms have 5-ft. Bites reach. The insect swarm’s DM chooses climbing or flying, and Spider Climb applies to the climbing form. Piranhas roll with advantage against a wounded target and use their reduced damage while the swarm itself is Bloodied. The Spider's publisher page credits the Monster Manual, and the publisher lists the matching block in its 2024 Basic Rules. [Swarm of Insects](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195224-swarm-of-insects) · [Swarm of Piranhas](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195227-swarm-of-piranhas) · [Spider](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775844-spider)

I checked the five selected portraits against the roster checksums and inspected the depicted creatures. The Piranhas use the existing toothed-fish school image.
