# Five Beasts from Volo’s first printing

I checked the original November 2016 pages in the local book facsimile. The four dinosaur stat blocks share printed p. 140, with descriptions on p. 139. The swarm and its lore are on p. 133. The viewer adds one to each printed folio. The official v3.0 errata lists no changes for these creatures. [Volo’s official errata]()

| Beast | Printed folio | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hadrosaurus | 139–140 | Tail does bludgeoning damage. The p. 139 training note allows a Small or Medium rider when the dinosaur is raised from a hatchling. |
| Quetzalcoatlus | 139–140 | Flyby, 30-ft. flying dive, and 10-ft. Bite reach. The first printing gives the dive’s extra damage as 10 (3d6) without separately naming its damage type. |
| Stegosaurus | 139–140 | Natural armor, 76 HP, and the 10-ft. Tail attack for piercing damage. |
| Swarm of Cranium Rats | 133 | HP-gated psionics and Bites, the bonus-action light settings, shared-space movement, damage defenses, and Telepathic Shroud. The block grants self-range divinations and immunity to all divination spells. Both lines are retained because the printing gives no self-cast exception. |
| Velociraptor | 139–140 | Tiny size, Pack Tactics, and a Multiattack with one Bite and one Claws attack. |

The four reused files match their roster hashes. The new 1254 × 1254 swarm depicts seven separate brain-crowned rats on plain tan parchment. I compared the square original and 96-pixel circle to the individual Cranium Rat and Big Linda references.
