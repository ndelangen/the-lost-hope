# Batch 10, part b10_05

Staged Reef Shark, Rhinoceros, Riding Horse, Saber-Toothed Tiger and Scorpion under the exact Monster Manual (2024) scopes. Their dedicated D&D Beyond entries credit pp. 368–369. The official errata lists no changes for these entries. [Publisher errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

The Rhinoceros deals extra piercing damage and applies Prone when its Gore hits a Large or smaller creature after a straight 20-ft. approach immediately before the hit. The Saber-Toothed Tiger has two Rend attacks and can Disengage or Hide as a bonus action. The Scorpion’s sting applies poison damage on a hit. Riding Horse uses its published Hooves action and shares its portrait with Warhorse.

The five reused square portraits depict the roster subjects on plain parchment and match the roster SHA-256 hashes.
