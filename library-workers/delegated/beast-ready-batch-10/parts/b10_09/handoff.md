# Batch 10 Beast rules

Verified Wolf on D&D Beyond, *Monster Manual* p. 373. In this printing, a Bite hit knocks a Medium or smaller creature Prone. [Wolf stat block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775850-wolf), [Monster Manual errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

Verified Cranium Rat on p. 133 and Brontosaurus, Deinonychus and Dimetrodon on p. 139 of the November 2016 first printing of *Volo's Guide to Monsters*. The v3.0 errata has no change for these entries. Volo's Appendix C places Cranium Rat in the Underdark and in urban areas and Dimetrodon on coasts. It lists no habitat for Brontosaurus or Deinonychus. [Volo's Guide to Monsters errata]()

All five portraits reuse approved staged artwork. I restored the Dimetrodon file from the batch 08 temporary validation copy after a preview command overwrote it. The restored file matches the exact roster SHA-256.
