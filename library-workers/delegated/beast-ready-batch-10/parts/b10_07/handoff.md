# Batch 10 · Three swarms, Tiger, and Triceratops

The five records use the revised Monster Manual. D&D Beyond credits pp. 370–372. The publisher’s version 1.0 errata lists no changes for these entries. [Publisher errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

The swarms retain their shared-space, Tiny-opening, and healing rules. Ravens keep Cacophony with its Recharge 6, single target, and short duration. Venomous Snakes keep the full poison damage on any Bites hit, including while Bloodied.

All five reused parchment portraits match the roster hashes and their subjects. The revised Swarm of Venomous Snakes uses the existing Swarm of Poisonous Snakes portrait.
