# Five revised Monster Manual beasts

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

I checked five public D&D Beyond stat blocks credited to the revised Monster Manual and found no changes to them in the official errata. The roster calls this scope *Monster Manual (2024)* because the volume uses the 2024 rules and was released in 2025. [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

| Entry | Folio | Initiative | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Octopus | [365](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775830-octopus) | +2 (12) | Ink Cloud is a once-per-day reaction triggered at the end of a nearby creature's turn underwater. Tentacles deals a fixed 1 bludgeoning damage. |
| Owl | [366](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775831-owl) | +1 (11) | Flyby applies when the owl flies out of an enemy's reach. Talons deals a fixed 1 slashing damage. |
| Panther | [366](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775832-panther) | +3 (13) | Nimble Escape allows Disengage or Hide as a bonus action. |
| Piranha | [366](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195160-piranha) | +3 (13) | The printed land speed is 5 ft. Bite gains Advantage against a target below its HP maximum and deals a fixed 1 piercing damage. |
| Plesiosaurus | [366](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195166-plesiosaurus) | +2 (12) | Hold Breath lasts 1 hour. Bite has 10 ft. of reach. |

All five reused portraits depict the rostered subjects and match their SHA-256 hashes. The Piranha portrait from Batch 01 shows one fish. Its encounter rules come from the revised Monster Manual.
