# Batch 10, part b10_02

Lion, Lizard, Mammoth, Mastiff and Mule are staged under the exact Monster Manual (2024) scopes. Their public D&D Beyond pages credit pp. 364–365. The 2025 errata lists no changes to these five records. [Publisher errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

The mammoth keeps the published Initiative +2 (12), DC 18 bonus-action Trample and the separate 10-ft. Gore and 5-ft. Trample ranges. The mastiff applies Prone on a bite hit against a Medium or smaller creature. The lion’s Roar can replace one Rend, and its Frightened effect ends at the start of the lion’s next turn.

All five square parchment portraits match their roster SHA-256 values. The portrait subjects are a maned lion, mottled lizard, shaggy tusked mammoth, fawn mastiff, and long-eared mule.
