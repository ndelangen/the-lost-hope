# Beast-ready batch 10, part b10_01

Five revised Monster Manual entries are staged. I checked Hippopotamus, Hyena, Jackal, and Killer Whale against their public D&D Beyond creature pages. I read Giant Squid on printed folio 360, which appears on sheet 363 in the facsimile. The public D&D Beyond link for Giant Squid redirected to the book marketplace. The official errata lists no corrections for these five entries. [Printed folio](https://anyflip.com/acscv/ypya/mobile/index.html#p=363) · [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

Giant Squid's Tentacle grapples a Huge or smaller creature from one of two tentacles and allows a straight pull of up to 10 feet. Ink Cloud is a once-per-day reaction after the squid takes damage underwater. The staged Underwater habitat is inferred from the printed Water Breathing trait and swim speed.

I inspected the five reused portraits for species and parchment style and matched all five SHA-256 hashes to the roster. The portrait file manifest is empty because the batch references existing images. The batch-level search and portrait checks belong to the temporary app integration.
