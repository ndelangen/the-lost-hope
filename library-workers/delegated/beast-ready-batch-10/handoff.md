# Beast batch 10

Staged 50 Beast encounter profiles against `26acbda`. The roster completes the 41 remaining revised *Monster Manual* records found in the scan and adds nine from the November 2016 first printing of *Volo's Guide to Monsters*. Each profile retains its own edition's mechanics and source references. [Roster](roster.tsv) · [Source and portrait manifest](manifest.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch) · [Portrait index](portrait-index-preview.json)

Giant Squid uses printed p. 360 because the canonical D&D Beyond creature link redirects during anonymous access. Its overlay retains the two tentacles, optional straight pull, and once-per-day Ink Cloud reaction. Weasel's verified print folio is p. 373. The publisher's digital byline says p. 372. The Volo Swarm of Cranium Rats can cast while above 18 HP and deals reduced Bites damage at 18 HP or less. The handoff for its part records how the printed Telepathic Shroud intersects with two self-range divinations. [Giant Squid printed folio](https://anyflip.com/acscv/ypya/mobile/index.html#p=363) · [Weasel evidence](parts/b10_08/source-evidence.json) · [Volo swarm evidence](parts/b10_10/source-evidence.json)

The bundle uses 48 existing portrait files and one new 1254 × 1254 PNG for Swarm of Cranium Rats. The individual rat reuses the matching portrait from the earlier playable bundle. Riding Horse and Warhorse share a portrait. The T. rex reuse keeps its warmer parchment and tighter crop. Two preview commands briefly overwrote staged copies of Dimetrodon and Triceratops. Both were restored and verified against their original SHA-256 values. [Comparison with Big Linda](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Portrait sheet 1](portraits-1.jpg) · [Portrait sheet 2](portraits-2.jpg)

In a temporary Library copy, I found the swarm by name and by “exposed brains,” opened the credited Marco Nelor illustration, and checked its spellcasting and Bites thresholds. Giant Squid loaded its portrait and showed the printed Tentacle and Ink Cloud rules. The browser console was clean. The independent catalog review matched all 50 runtime lookup keys and all 49 image files.
