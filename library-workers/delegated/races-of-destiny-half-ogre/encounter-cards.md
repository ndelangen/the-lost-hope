# Half-Ogre encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. The Half-Ogre traits come from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*. Each NPC is a Large Giant with a 10-ft. space, 10-ft. natural reach, darkvision 60 ft., and low-light vision from the Giant type. Both speak Common and Giant. [Original traits, pp. 97–98]() · [3.5 SRD]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Bram Reedbar, Fighter 1

Provisional CR 2, ECL 3. AC 18, touch 10, flat-footed 17. HP 13, initiative +1, speed 30 ft. Fort +5, Ref +1, Will +0. BAB +1, grapple +10. STR 20, DEX 12, CON 16, INT 10, WIS 10, CHA 8.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Large glaive | +6 melee, 2d8+7 slashing, ×3. Hits at 15 or 20 ft. |
| Large spiked gauntlet | +5 melee, 1d6+5 piercing, ×2. Hits within 10 ft. |

Climb +7, Jump +7. Weapon Focus is included in the glaive attack. If Bram uses Power Attack, subtract 1 from melee attack rolls and add 2 to glaive damage.

The water is about to cover the last height mark and the crew is still in the channel. Bram lends the key to the old inspection stair if the party brings the crew to higher ground. If someone forces the gate, he guards the steps with his glaive and switches to his gauntlet within 10 ft.

## Dessa Tallowbeat, Barbarian 1

Provisional CR 2, ECL 3. AC 16, touch 9, flat-footed 16. HP 15, initiative +4, speed 40 ft. Fort +5, Ref +0, Will +1. BAB +1, grapple +10. STR 21, DEX 10, CON 16, INT 8, WIS 12, CHA 8.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Large greatclub | +5 melee, 2d8+7 bludgeoning, ×2, 10-ft. reach. Rage gives +7 to hit and 2d8+10 damage. |
| Large javelin | +0 ranged, 1d8+5 piercing, ×2, 30-ft. increment. Rage increases damage to 1d8+7. |

Climb +8, Jump +12, Listen +5. Fast Movement adds 10 ft. before armor and load modifiers. Dessa moves at 40 ft. in her listed light gear, and Jump includes the +4 speed bonus.

Rage once per day and at most once per encounter, lasting up to 8 rounds. While raging, HP 17, AC 14, touch 7, flat-footed 14, grapple +12, Fort +7, Ref +0, Will +3. When rage ends, remove the 2 bonus HP and apply fatigue for the rest of the encounter. Fatigue gives −2 STR and DEX and prevents running and charging.

Dessa’s partner missed the last drum reply in a whiteout. Dessa keeps calling from the ridge while the party searches the downhill forks. She uses rage if the group is trapped.
