# Half-Ogre for D&D 3.5

One playable Half-Ogre profile from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*. The entry begins on p. 96, and the racial traits span pp. 97–98. The profile has Large Giant size and type, 10-ft. natural reach, +4 natural armor, and level adjustment +2. A Half-Ogre with one class level has one class Hit Die and effective character level 3. [Original traits]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Bram Reedbar is a Fighter 1 who keeps the levee gate. His glaive reaches 15 or 20 ft., and his spiked gauntlet covers up to 10 ft. Dessa Tallowbeat is a Barbarian 1 who guides people along a ridge by drum. Her card gives both ordinary and raging totals. Both are original NPCs at provisional CR 2.
