# Independent Half-Ogre reviews

## Original printing

The first-print entry starts on p. 96. Ron Spencer drew the unnamed Half-Ogre barbarian on p. 97. The traits span pp. 97–98. The reviewer verified Large Giant, 10-ft. reach, +4 natural armor, 60-ft. darkvision, and LA +2. The entry grants no racial Hit Dice and prints no encounter stat block or CR. Low-light vision and weapon proficiencies come from the general Giant type. The optional p. 150 variant adds the human subtype at the DM’s discretion and keeps the Giant type. The earlier *Savage Species* uses Giant Blood and LA +1. Its example with one class level is ECL 2. The block beside it on p. 218 is a Desmodu. The reviewer found no *Races of Destiny* entry in Wizards’ collected errata. [*Savage Species*, pp. 217–218]() · [Wizards’ errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

## NPCs and catalog

The second reviewer checked each Large weapon’s damage and reach, skill ranks, armor, saves, and rage totals. Bram’s glaive hits at 15 or 20 ft. and his spiked gauntlet covers his 10-ft. natural reach. The review corrected Dessa’s Fast Movement wording. The class adds 10 ft. before armor and load modifiers. Dessa moves 40 ft. in the gear on her card.
