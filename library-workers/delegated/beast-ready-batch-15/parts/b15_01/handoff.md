# Beast-ready batch 15, part b15_01

I verified all five entries in the local *Mulmaster Undone* V2 . Giant Vulture’s attacks continue onto p. 76. The Young Bulette has its own Beast block on p. 57, with burrow speed, tremorsense, Claw, and Charge. The adventure is listed as DDEP2 in the publisher’s index. [Adventure index]()

The Giant Badger’s stat block lists 50 XP. The adventure’s combat awards list 100 XP per foe. The Wolf appendix prints a Scimitar action, an apparent copying error. The staged record preserves that action and its evidence. I found no publisher correction for either discrepancy.

Three new portraits show the giant badger’s obsidian-like teeth and claws and opal-like eyes, the young bulette’s opalescent eyes, and the wolf’s studded leather barding. The shark and vulture reuse their species portraits. I checked all five portrait hashes and round previews.
