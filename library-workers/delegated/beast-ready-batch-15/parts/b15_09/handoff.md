# Batch 15 Beast rules

Verified Black Bear on p. 304 and Cat and Constrictor Snake on p. 305 of the August 2014 first printing of the *Player's Handbook*. The first printing uses +3 for both Black Bear attacks. The official v3.0 errata changes both to +4. Constrict gives no fixed escape DC in this printing. [Player's Handbook contents](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2014), [Wizards errata]()

Verified Giant Weasel on p. 26 and Spider on p. 31 of the original *Outlaws of the Iron Route*. The local copy matches Wizards' public byte for byte. Both entries include the Kobold Caverns encounter instructions from p. 15. [Wizards adventure ]()

All five reused portraits match the roster hashes and show the expected animals on muted tan parchment.
