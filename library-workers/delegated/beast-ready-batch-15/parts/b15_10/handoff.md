# Curse of Strahd and Earthspur Mines beasts

I checked the March 2016 first printing of *Curse of Strahd* and the April 2015 *Mayhem in the Earthspur Mines* release. The evidence records the adventure pages and the 2014 *Monster Manual* pages the three Barovian creatures inherit. [Curse of Strahd source index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/cos) · [Earthspur Mines release](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/170478/DDEX204-Mayhem-in-the-Earthspur-Mines-5e)

- Armored Saber-Toothed Tiger has AC 17 in its fitted half plate, 84 HP, and CR 3 on p. 115. The official errata narrows its training target to Strahd's Vistani servants and removes the recognition detail from p. 115. The supporting notes preserve the difference with the original p. 116 running text. [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/curse-of-strahd)
- Piccolo uses the Baboon block. Page 118 identifies him as Blinsky's pet monkey and describes the custom ballerina tutu. [Vallaki](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/cos/the-town-of-vallaki#AreasofVallaki)
- Sangzor uses the Giant Goat block with 33 HP, Intelligence 6, chaotic evil alignment, CR 1, and resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing attacks. Its encounter and pelt effects are on p. 160. The printed gray pelt called for a separate portrait from the white Mountain Goat. [Tsolenka Pass](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/cos/tsolenka-pass#SpecialEvents)
- Lion's p. 26 stat block gives CR 1 and 200 XP. The p. 23 Combat Awards table gives 100 XP per lion for session rewards. Both values appear under their printed uses.

I checked all four portraits in square and round crops. The three new 1254 × 1254 portraits show the tiger's fitted armor, Piccolo's tutu, and Sangzor's gray pelt against plain tan parchment. The reused Lion matches its roster checksum.
