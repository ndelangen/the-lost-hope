# Adventurers League beasts

Five records checked against the 2016 . The Dark Lord’s cover identifies version 1.0. The Seer’s cover has no version number. No official correction to these standalone appendix blocks was located. [Wizards’ adventure index]()

| Adventure | Beast | Stat block | Encounter |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| The Darklord | Brown Bear | 30 | Replaces Bertock for a Very Weak party. Very Strong adds the bear to the fight. |
| The Darklord | Giant Ape | 32 | Bertock’s default animal form. The adventure gives separate Strong and Very Strong adjustments. |
| The Darklord | Giant Crocodile | 32 | Replaces the Giant Ape for a Weak party. This block has a 40-ft. walking speed. |
| The Seer | Brown Bear | 18 | Four bears ambush the party. The encounter gives the surprise exceptions and the drumming that causes the bears to flee. |
| The Seer | Dire Wolf | 18 | Full appendix block and 200 XP. The forest narrative refers to lurking wolves without specifying their number or placement. |

The Darklord’s Brown Bear Bite prints 8 piercing damage and the inconsistent expression 1d8 + 8. The record uses the printed fixed value and retains the expression in its source notes. Both adventures print +5 to hit for their Brown Bears. The +6 correction applies to the Monster Manual’s own entry. [The Dark Lord, p. 30](), [Monster Manual errata]()

The portraits reuse the roster’s existing natural animals. Bertock’s animal form uses those appearances, and the encounter notes describe the change back to a stuffed toy on death. All four distinct portrait files match their roster hashes.
