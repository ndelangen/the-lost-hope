# Five Adventurers League beast entries

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

I checked the printed stat blocks and encounter rules in the original 2015 release . Their covers date *City of Danger* to April 1 and *Bounty in the Bog* to May 1. The Adventurers League FAQ changes *Bounty in the Bog*’s per-character completion award from 450–600 XP to 900–1,200 XP. The printed creature awards remain 50 XP for Giant Badger and 100 XP for Giant Wasp. [Publisher adventure index]() · [Official Adventurers League FAQ]()

| Adventure | Entry | Stat block | Encounter folios | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| City of Danger | Blood Hawk | 12 | 10–11 | The summoned hawks use Pack Tactics in pairs. |
| City of Danger | Giant Badger | 25 | 22–23 | A successful DC 15 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check gives the enchanted badgers disadvantage on attacks until the end of their next turn. |
| City of Danger | Giant Frog | 32 | 30–31 | Bite grapples and restrains. Swallow requires a Small or smaller target already grappled by the frog. |
| Bounty in the Bog | Giant Badger | 22 | 14–15, 18 | The grotto badger attacks the closest target. It uses the ordinary Giant Badger block. |
| Bounty in the Bog | Giant Wasp | 21 | 10–11, 13, 18 | Sting’s poison stabilizes, poisons, and paralyzes a target it drops to 0 HP. The nest encounter adds repellent and carrying rules. |

Four entries reuse earlier muted-parchment portraits. The *City of Danger* Giant Badger shares the Batch 15 obsidian portrait for the black teeth and claws described on p. 22. The shared portrait’s faint opalescent eyes come from the other adventure’s description.
