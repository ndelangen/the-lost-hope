# Batch 15, part b15_02

Six Beast entries use the original 2016 module . The Executioner supplies Black Bear and Blood Hawk on p. 23 and Raven on p. 24. The main module for The Beast supplies Blood Hawk on p. 27 and Dire Wolf and Elk on p. 29. [Wizards adventure index]()

The Executioner prints 100 XP in the Black Bear stat block and 200 XP in its combat rewards table. Both Blood Hawk blocks grant advantage on sight checks under the heading “Keen Hearing and Smell.” The Beast prints Dire Wolf Bite +4 and has an apparent vulture copy error in Pack Tactics. The summary reads the ally clause as referring to the wolf. The official FAQ changes The Executioner’s adventure-wide XP bounds and leaves the creature statistics as printed. [Wizards FAQ]()

The five reused records cover Blood Hawk, Raven, Dire Wolf, and Elk. Both Blood Hawk records share one portrait. The Executioner’s new Black Bear portrait depicts the thin individual described in the encounter. All five distinct images use plain muted tan parchment.
