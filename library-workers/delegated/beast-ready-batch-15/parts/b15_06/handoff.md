- The Riding Horse has the printed +2 to hit with Hooves. The Draft Horse has AC 10 and +6 to hit. Half-plate barding, the harness cover and the tight four-horse formation belong to the chase scene on p. 11.
- The Cooshee's stat block gives Keen Hearing and Smell. Frederick has Keen Smell. Their appearances follow the encounter prose. The Cooshee is a greenish elven hound mottled with brown. Frederick is Scartia Krutz's massive basset hound.
- The Giant Wolf Spider block begins at the bottom of p. 26's left column and continues above Needle Blight on the right. The continuation calls the spider a swarm and labels its attack Bites. This summary uses the named spider as the subject and retains the printed traits, action heading, +3 attack bonus and DC 11 Constitution save. The separate Swarm of Spiders block on pp. 27–28 has a different attack. [2014 comparison block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-g#GiantWolfSpider)

## Portrait direction

Built-in ImageGen used Big Linda as the style reference for realistic painted animals on muted, matte tan parchment. The Cooshee prompt called for a heavy, strong-jawed hound with moss-green fur and irregular brown patches. Frederick's prompt called for a stocky basset hound with short legs, long ears, loose jowls and a muted tricolor coat. Both prompts asked for the entire animal inside a centered circular crop.
