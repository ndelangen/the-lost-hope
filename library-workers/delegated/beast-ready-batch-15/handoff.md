# Beast readiness batch 15

This batch stages 50 Beast publication versions across 24 publications against archive `eaeee0b`. The parts cite the original Adventurers League , *Player's Handbook*, and *Curse of Strahd*. They identify inherited blocks and keep published errata separate from the first-printing notes. An independent reviewer checked the roster against the archive and the preceding 701 staged scopes. [Wizards errata index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae)

- [Beast-only roster](roster.tsv) · [Manifest and source evidence](manifest.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch) · [Preview portrait index](portrait-index-preview.json)
- The patch adds 50 stat blocks. The catalog TSV is byte-for-byte unchanged. The *Pool of Radiance Resurgent* tiger keeps its indexed name and can also be found by searching `Saber-Toothed Tiger`.
- Thirty earlier portraits and 15 new built-in ImageGen PNGs cover the batch. The two adventures that describe obsidian teeth and claws share one Giant Badger portrait. The two octopuses have distinct portraits for *The Waydown*'s albino specimens and the green tentacle in *Dark Pyramid of Sorcerer's Isle*. The armor, costume, coat, and anatomy differences behind the other new portraits are documented in the parts. [Big Linda style comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Contact sheet 1](portraits-1.jpg) · [Contact sheet 2](portraits-2.jpg) · [Contact sheet 3](portraits-3.jpg)
- The original *Dark Pyramid of Sorcerer's Isle* Boar appendix omits its attack. The batch omits that candidate and stages the *Embers of Elmwood* Mastiff in its place. The Mastiff keeps the appendix's AC 12 and separately shows the encounter's leather barding and AC 13.
- The first-printing PHB Black Bear retains its printed +3 attack bonus and labels +4 as later errata. The Darklord Brown Bear preserves the printed fixed 8 damage and flags its contradictory dice. [PHB Black Bear erratum](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/players-handbook-2014)

In the disposable copy, I checked search, rule display, edition selection, and portrait loading on both octopuses and three Giant Badger versions. The obsidian badgers resolved to the same image, and the *Bounty in the Bog* badger kept its ordinary portrait. I also opened the *Pool of Radiance Resurgent* tiger from the standard spelling search. The browser console showed no warnings or errors. [The Waydown before and after](waydown-octopus-before-after.jpg)

The bundle is staged locally. This batch made no tracked edits to the primary checkout and no changes to R2.
