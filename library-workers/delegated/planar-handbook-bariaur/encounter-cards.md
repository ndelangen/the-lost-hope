# Bariaur encounters

Both NPCs are Medium Outsiders on the Material Plane and have the Extraplanar subtype. Each is provisionally CR 2 and ECL 2. Both wear studded leather barding and move 40 ft. while lightly loaded. Both have darkvision 60 ft. and SR 12. For a spell that allows spell resistance, roll 1d20 + caster level against 12 before any applicable saving throw. Lowering spell resistance takes a standard action. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Mara Sedgehill, Ranger 1

A messenger disappeared on the switchback above the ford. Mara can follow the trail, cover the bend with her bow, or charge along the straight approach.

- HP 10, AC 15, initiative +2. Fortitude +4, Reflex +4, Will +1, or +3 against spells and spell-like abilities that require a Will save.
- Longsword +4 for 1d8+3 slashing. Longbow +3 for 1d8 piercing, or +4 for 1d8+1 within 30 ft.
- On a charge, ram once at +6 for 2d6+4 bludgeoning. A full-round charge needs a clear path and 10–80 ft. of movement. AC drops to 13 until the start of her next turn.
- Strength-based checks to resist bull rush or trip are +7. Her light-load limit is 114 lb.

## Kel Stoneford, Fighter 1

Kel keeps the ford clear while livestock cross. A hostile caster can test his spell resistance or target the crossing. Kel can lower his resistance ahead of an allied spell if he has time.

- HP 12, AC 14, initiative +1. Fortitude +4, Reflex +1, Will +3, or +5 against spells and spell-like abilities that require a Will save.
- Greatsword +6 for 2d6+6 slashing. On a charge, choose a greatsword attack at +8 for the same damage or a ram at +7 for 2d6+6 bludgeoning. AC drops to 12 until the start of his next turn.
- Strength-based checks to resist bull rush or trip are +8. His light-load limit is 150 lb.
