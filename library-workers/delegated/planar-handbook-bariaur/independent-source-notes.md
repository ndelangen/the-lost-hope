# Bariaur source check

The July 2004 printing shows a Bariaur on p. 7 and gives the Medium player profile on p. 8. The same page calls the larger Book of Exalted Deeds form an Exalted Bariaur. The November 2004 errata has no Bariaur correction. [First printing]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Page 8 gives Powerful Charge one ram attack for 2d6 bludgeoning plus 1½ times Strength modifier. The normal +2 attack bonus and −2 AC for charging apply. The trait gives no standalone ram attack. The same profile gives SR 11 + class level and a separate +2 racial bonus on Will saves against spells and spell-like abilities. [Charge rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/combat/specialAttacks.htm#charge) · [Spell resistance rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#spellResistance)

The independent review confirmed the attack and damage rolls, skill budgets, carrying limits, and SR. Both builds use the Medium nonhumanoid armor pricing rule. Their studded leather barding costs 50 gp and weighs 20 lb. The 5e player profile comes from D&D Beyond Drops and has its own rules. [Armor rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/equipment/armor.htm#armorForUnusualCreatures) · [D&D Beyond announcement](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2282-d-d-beyonds-november-drop-champions-of-ysgard)

David Martin illustrated the unnamed Bariaur on p. 7. The description and illustration informed Mara’s ram horns, cloven hooves, and golden-brown wool. Mara’s clothing and pose are original. [Book illustration]()
