# Bariaur from Planar Handbook

This batch adds the 2004 D&D 3.5 Bariaur profile and two original encounters. Powerful Charge gives one ram attack during a charge. Spell resistance uses 11 + class level, so both first-level builds have SR 12 even though their ECL is 2.
