# Northlands Worldbook beasts

This patch covers all three beast source lookups in the Tales of the Valiant *Northlands Worldbook*. The credits page identifies ISBN 978-1-964506-38-8. The same mirror ends with back-cover text for the D&D 2024 edition, so the profiles use the ToV credits and the Black Flag stat blocks on printed p. 264. [Book text](https://www.scribd.com/document/1066608060/ToV8-Northlands-Worldbook-TOV) · [Publisher listing](https://koboldpress.com/kpstore/product/tales-of-the-valiant-northlands-worldbook/)

- **Moose**, CR 1/2. Its Hooves attack gains damage and knocks down a Large or smaller target after 10 ft. of straight movement immediately before the hit. Fury is a reaction to taking at least 5 damage.
- **Reindeer**, CR 1/4. The index rounds this to 0.3. Its Hooves line in the extracted text gives 7 (1d8 + 2), although the dice average 6 when rounded down. Both values are recorded, pending a page image to confirm the discrepancy. [Publisher index](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)
- **Sea Serpent**, CR 3. A Bite hit can trigger Constrict as a bonus action. A failed DC 14 Strength save deals damage and grapples the target, with escape DC 16.

The Reindeer portrait is for this printing, and the Sea Serpent has a revised blue-green, pale-bellied portrait draft. The reused Moose still needs more room above its antlers and cooler, quieter parchment to match the new drafts. Its source remains intact.
