# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual crocodile source check

TSR’s *Monstrous Manual*, product 2140, was first published in 1993. The checked copy identifies itself as the January 1994 third printing. The table, both descriptions and the family illustration are on printed p. 49, viewer page 52. The linked page image has SHA-256 `2448199e5dbbe373af0943b1ce25007ea36d21e508ca258cd1846f69a1a19297`. ## Crocodile

- Common, 3–24 appearing, AC 5, HD 3, THAC0 17, attack damage 2–8 and 1–12, Large (8–15 ft. long), morale average (9), 65 XP. The entry supplies no fixed hp value.
- Its bite deals 2d4 and its tail deals 1d12. When it lies in wait, opponents take −2 to their surprise roll. It can resemble a floating log and notices movement in the water. It seizes prey and can drag it underwater or to its lair, and may overturn boats. The profile gives no separate grab or drowning rule. Below 40°F it moves at half normal speed.
- It spends most of its time submerged in tropical or subtropical swamps, rivers and other large bodies of water. Groups sometimes congregate and fight over food, while the prose says that they do not cooperate when hunting. The 3–24 count is an encounter figure. The body is long and squat, with short strong legs, a powerful tail, tough hide and conical teeth. Only its eyes and nose need break the surface when it floats.

## Giant Crocodile

- Frequency reads **Very rare to common** and number appearing reads **1 to 2–12**. The entry gives no rule assigning either endpoint to salt water or a prehistoric setting. AC 4, HD 7, THAC0 13, attack damage 3–18 and 2–20, Huge (21–30 ft. long), morale steady (11), 1,400 XP. The prose describes them as generally rarer than the smaller form.
- It typically inhabits salt water or prehistoric settings and may prey on sharks, small whales and small boats. It rams small vessels to capsize or break them and may eat the catch while leaving the crew alone. The text supplies neither vessel statistics nor a special ramming roll.
- Surprise is explicit in the Giant column. Only the general crocodile combat paragraph describes the −2 surprise modifier and cold penalty, and the Giant subsection supplies no alternate rule. The Giant’s damage order matches a bite and tail as in the family profile and the older EX1 encounter, but the Giant subsection gives only the table ranges and does not independently specify dice or damage labels.

## Adventure comparisons

- **Night of the Walking Dead (1992, AD&D 2e).** Printed pp. 7–8 describe one Crocodile in Souragne. It has THAC0 16 and 14 hp, with Surprise listed as a special defense. The adventure keeps AC 5, 3 HD, 2–8/1–12 damage and 65 XP. It has a 20% detection chance if the party probes the logs and a −2 surprise penalty if the party ignores them. Use those numbers for its own raft encounter. - **Dungeonland (EX1, 1983, AD&D 1e).** P. 5 has one Giant Crocodile at 42 hp, with surprise on 1–3 on a d6, a bite for 3–18 and a tail smash for 2–20. Its submerged pool encounter adds a stabbing-weapon restriction, −2 to hit, a 2–4-round drowning window on a captive, release after 10 cumulative damage, and a rear tail attack arc. Those procedures are specific to the adventure source. [EX1 source notes]()
