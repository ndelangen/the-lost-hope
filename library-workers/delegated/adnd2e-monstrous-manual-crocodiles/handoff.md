# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual crocodiles

Page 49 of the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual* gives distinct Crocodile and Giant Crocodile profiles. The climate line spans both columns. The prose places ordinary crocodiles in swamps, rivers, and other large waters, and typically places giant crocodiles in salt water or prehistoric settings. The 1992 *Night of the Walking Dead* Crocodile and the 1983 *Dungeonland* Giant Crocodile remain separately selectable when their patches are applied. | Creature | AC | HD | THAC0 | Printed damage |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Crocodile | 5 | 3 | 17 | 2–8 / 1–12. The prose identifies a 2d4 bite and 1d12 tail lash. |
| Giant Crocodile | 4 | 7 | 13 | 3–18 / 2–20. The Giant paragraph describes ramming boats and trying to break the hull with its jaws. |

Both columns list surprise. The main combat text gives prey a −2 surprise penalty when the crocodile lies in wait and halves ordinary crocodile movement below 40°F. The Giant profile attributes the −2 to the main text. Its own paragraph gives no separate surprise modifier or cold rule. The Giant's frequency is printed as “very rare to common” and its encounter count as “1 to 2–12.” The prose calls the Giant much rarer than the ordinary crocodile. The table leaves the frequency and encounter ranges unallocated.

Both portraits sit on plain parchment with the full snout and tail in frame. The ordinary Crocodile follows the long, low shape described in the book. The Giant has a broader body and heavier jaws. Colors and the relative bulk are illustration choices. The printed family illustration is unsigned and carries no specific form label.
