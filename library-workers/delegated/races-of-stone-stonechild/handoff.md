# Stonechild for D&D 3.5

One playable Stonechild profile from the August 2004 first printing of *Races of Stone*. The full racial package gives two Outsider Hit Dice and level adjustment +4, for effective character level 6 before taking any class levels. The optional six-level monster class reaches the same package in stages. Its third level has one racial Hit Die and one daily use of *magic stone*. The second Hit Die and Blind-Fight arrive at level 4. [Original traits, pp. 93–94]() · [Progression, p. 94]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Miren Slatefold is an original cartwright with two racial Hit Dice and Fighter 1. She is provisional CR 4 and ECL 7, and appears in the full-body plain-parchment portrait. Nalin Flintwell is an original quarry marker at Stonechild monster-class level 3. He is provisional CR 1 and ECL 3, using the printed CR 1 benchmark for that progression level.
