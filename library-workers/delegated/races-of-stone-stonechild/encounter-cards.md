# Stonechild encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. Both are Medium Outsiders with the Earth subtype, 30-ft. speed, darkvision 60 ft., and immunity to acid and poison. They are Extraplanar in these Material Plane encounters. [Original rules, pp. 93–94]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Miren Slatefold, cartwright

Two Outsider Hit Dice and Fighter 1. Provisional CR 4, ECL 7. AC 21, touch 11, flat-footed 20. HP 37, initiative +1. Fort +11, Ref +4, Will +4. BAB +3, grapple +9. STR 23, DEX 12, CON 22, INT 12, WIS 12, CHA 8. Common, Terran, Dwarven.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Warhammer | +10 melee, 1d8+6 bludgeoning, ×3, 5-ft. reach |
| Javelin | +4 ranged, 1d6+6 piercing, ×2, 30-ft. increment |
| Enchanted pebble, thrown | +5 ranged, 1d6+1, or 2d6+2 against undead, 20-ft. increment |

Appraise +6 or +8 for related stonework, Climb +8, Craft (stoneworking) +10, Intimidate +0, Knowledge (history) +6, Knowledge (the planes) +6, Listen +6, Search +6, Spot +6, Survival +6. Survival gains +2 to follow tracks and +2 on other planes. Blind-Fight, Skill Focus (Craft), Power Attack, and Weapon Focus (warhammer). With the hammer in one hand, subtract up to 3 from melee attack rolls and add that amount to damage if using Power Attack.

*Magic stone* three times per day at caster level 3. Miren needs two stone bearings to fix the axle before the wagon can cross the ford. She guards the crew if trouble reaches the riverbank.

## Nalin Flintwell, quarry marker

Stonechild monster-class level 3, one Outsider Hit Die. Provisional CR 1, ECL 3. AC 16, touch 11, flat-footed 15. HP 13, initiative +1. Fort +7, Ref +3, Will +2. BAB +1, grapple +4. STR 17, DEX 12, CON 20, INT 14, WIS 10, CHA 8. Common, Terran, Dwarven, Ignan.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Longspear | +4 melee, 1d8+4 piercing, ×3, hits at 10 ft. |
| Heavy mace | +4 melee, 1d8+3 bludgeoning, ×2, 5-ft. reach |
| Enchanted pebble, thrown | +3 ranged, 1d6+1, or 2d6+2 against undead, 20-ft. increment |

Appraise +6, Climb +6, Craft (stoneworking) +6, Intimidate +3, Knowledge (history) +6, Knowledge (the planes) +6, Listen +6, Search +6, Spot +6, Survival +4. Listen and Spot include Alertness. At this stage natural armor is +2 and *magic stone* is available once per day at caster level 3. The printed table gives CR 1 for level 3. [Progression, p. 94]()

Someone moved a stone marking the boundary of the quarry. Nalin asks the party to walk the line before the next shift starts. If threatened, he watches from behind the boundary wall with enchanted pebbles at hand.

## Magic stone

Each use affects up to three pebbles. The effect lasts 30 minutes or until they’re discharged. Use normal ranged attack rolls with the +1 enhancement bonus included above. A sling uses its normal 50-ft. increment. The tables use the spell’s printed damage. The earlier *Mayhem & Madness* pregenerated sheet adds Strength once to each thrown hit, including hits against undead. Under that convention, Miren’s figures are 1d6+7 and 2d6+8, and Nalin’s are 1d6+4 and 2d6+5. [3.5 SRD spell](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/magicStone.htm) · [Pregenerated sheet]()
