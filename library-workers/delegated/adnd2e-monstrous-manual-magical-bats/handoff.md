# AD&D 2e Azmyth, Night Hunter, and Sinister

These are the three remaining columns of the Bat entry in the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. The catalog had no entries for the three names or the Night Hunter alias Dragazhar. This patch also applies alongside the separately staged Bat, Giant Bat, and Mobat patch. [Printed page 15](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bdd98c9a7333e11f12af09bcf01273ad.webp) · [Printed page 16](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/4ef87b704dca99fd16240b23494fd32e.webp) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Profile | Catalog type | Printed rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Azmyth | Magical Beast (Monsters) | 2 HD, THAC0 19, 40% magic resistance. Its twice-daily shocking grasp can add 1d8+6 damage to a physical attack. |
| Night Hunter | Animal (Beasts) | 2+2 HD, THAC0 19. Bite, two claw rakes, and one tail attack chosen between a 1d6 slash and a 3d4 stab. |
| Sinister | Magical Beast (Monsters) | 4+4 HD, THAC0 17, 70% magic resistance, natural levitation, a continuous missile-blocking field, and hold monster once per day. |

The type labels are catalog choices. The AD&D table groups all three under Bat and gives no formal creature type. Azmyth and Sinister use magic. Night Hunter hunts with physical attacks and has no listed magic resistance. The 2004 *Forgotten Realms Monster Update* classifies Night Hunter and Sinister as Magical Beasts under that edition's rules. [Publisher's 3.5e update]()

The Night Hunter table gives 1–30 in a lair. The prose says lairs usually hold more than 30. The Sinister table gives lawful evil, while the facing prose describes the creature as non-evil. An earlier source prints lawful neutral. Both 1994 disagreements are visible in the profiles. [The Drow of the Underdark, p. 113](https://www.scribd.com/document/1058602637/ADnD-FOR2-Forgotten-Realms-Drow-of-the-Underdark-2nd-Edition)

The portraits use plain parchment. Azmyth has an emerald crest, white eyes, a bearded chin, and a pointed tail. Night Hunter has orange eyes and a triangular tail blade. Its velvet-black color comes from *The Drow of the Underdark*, which supplies the color word missing from the 1994 prose. Sinister has a broad manta-like outline with smooth wing membranes and no distinct head or neck. The 1994 family illustration has no confirmed attribution to a specific form or artist.
