## Azmyth

- Any land, rare, solitary, any activity, omnivore, High intelligence (13–14), treasure nil, chaotic neutral, one appearing. AC 2, movement 3 and Fl 24 (A), 2 HD, THAC0 19, two attacks for 1 and 1–2 damage, special attacks and defenses magic use, magic resistance 40%, Small (3 ft.), elite morale (14), 650 XP.
- It bites for 1 point and stabs with its needle-sharp tail for 1d2. Twice per day it can use a *shocking grasp* attack for 1d8+6 electrical damage on direct contact. That damage may accompany a physical attack. It can *know alignment* three times per day, turn itself invisible once per day for up to six rounds or until it lands a successful attack, and use *silence, 15-foot radius* centered on itself once per day. It has 90-ft. infravision and 60-ft. telepathy.
- Solitary wanderers return to favored haunts and sometimes form lasting partnerships with humanoids. The partnership does not give the direct control associated with a familiar. They eat flowers, small plants and insects. The prose gives crested heads, bearded chins, pupil-less white eyes and leathery skin that can be gray, mauve or emerald green.

## Night Hunter

- Any land, uncommon, pack, activity night/any, carnivore, Average to High intelligence (8–14), treasure M, O and Z in lair, neutral evil, 1–12 appearing and a printed parenthetical of 1–30 in lair. AC 6, movement 2 and Fl 18 (A), 2+2 HD, THAC0 19, four attacks, special attacks, special defenses and magic resistance nil, Medium (7 ft.), steady morale (11), 175 XP.
- The alias is *dragazhar*, after the adventurer who first domesticated one. The four attacks are bite 1d4, two wing-claw rakes at 1d2 each, and a tail slash for 1d6 or stab for 3d4. The slash and stab are alternate versions of the tail attack. It has 120-ft. infravision. It hunts from ambush by flying low behind terrain but rarely surprises opponents because excitement may produce echoing, loon-like screams. The entry provides no numerical surprise modifier.
- It is nocturnal on the surface and active at any time underground. Typical lairs are double-ended caves or tall, dense woods, with prey usually consisting of small beasts. It can subsist on carrion and sometimes attacks larger prey when desperate. The p. 16 prose says lairs usually contain **over 30**, in tension with the p. 15 table’s **1–30 in lair**. Keep the table figure and the conflicting prose visible.
- The text specifies a dexterous, triangular, razor-sharp tail and violet, orange or red eyes. The manual literally says the bodies and claws are “velvet in hue,” without a named color. The earlier *Drow of the Underdark*, p. 113, says velvet black even to the claws, which supplies a separately sourced palette. ## Sinister

- Any land, rare, band, any activity, omnivore, Average to Exceptional intelligence (8–16), treasure nil, **lawful evil as printed in the table**, 1d6 appearing. AC 3, movement 2 and Fl 21 (A), 4+4 HD, THAC0 17, one attack for 2–5 damage, special attack magic use, special defense energy field, magic resistance 70%, Large (9 ft.), champion morale (15–16), 2,000 XP. The prose gives no anatomical description for the attack.
- It levitates precisely and can hang motionless. Its always-present, five-foot-deep field turns aside ordinary missiles and absorbs *magic missile* and *Melf’s acid arrow*. It gives no general protection from other spells or melee. The table’s 70% magic resistance is a separate defense. It casts *hold monster* once daily, generally to escape a stronger creature and sometimes to hunt when ravenous. It has 160-ft. infravision and limited telepathy with its own kind within 20 feet.
- It is jet black and manta-like, with no distinct head or neck and muscular wings lacking the usual visible bat finger bones. It is silent, likes vocal or instrumental music, and may gather around or follow a bard without harassing the performer unless attacked. It prefers hunting at night above ground, eats carrion when other food is unavailable, and also eats flowers and seed pods.
- The p. 16 prose explicitly says sinisters are not evil, contrary to the p. 15 lawful evil table entry. The earlier TSR *Drow of the Underdark*, p. 113, gives lawful neutral, which agrees with the tone of the prose. Retain the 1994 print table reading alongside this unresolved internal discrepancy. ## Illustration and catalog

The p. 16 image is an unlabeled bat with a conventional head, large ears, visible fangs and fingered wings. Its silhouette is unlike the textual Sinister. The print offers no named species or confirmed illustrator for the image. Use the three prose descriptions for visual identity. [Bat family image, printed p. 16](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/4ef87b704dca99fd16240b23494fd32e.webp)

The source’s native heading is Bat and it supplies no modern creature type or challenge rating. Azmyth and Sinister have spell-like abilities and telepathy. Night Hunter has higher-than-animal intelligence and infravision, but its attacks are physical and its special-attack, defense and magic-resistance rows say nil. No exact matching names were found in the primary index or staged profile files. Common Bat, Large Bat and Mobat are separate columns already handled in the preceding batch.
