# Maenad for D&D 3.5

One playable Maenad entry from the April 2004 first printing of *Expanded Psionics Handbook*. Maenads have two racial power points, a separate once-daily sonic Energy Ray, and a separate once-daily Outburst. The sonic ray uses ranged touch, allows no save, and ignores object hardness. [Playables, p. 14]() · [Energy Ray, p. 103]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Outburst gives +2 Strength and −2 Intelligence and Wisdom for up to four rounds. The printed trait leaves activation unstated. The two NPC cards explicitly use a standard action under the original rule for activated extraordinary abilities. [Published warrior, pp. 203–204]() · [Core special abilities](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#extraordinaryAbilities) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

The portrait depicts **Vara Flintshore**, an original Wilder and shoal rescuer, with dark braids and subtle crystal flecks on plain parchment. **Toren Glasswake** is an original Expert who repairs ship hulls. Vara is provisional CR 1 and Toren is provisional CR 1/2. Their cards keep the racial abilities separate from Vara's Force Screen, Wild Surge, and daily power-point reserve.
