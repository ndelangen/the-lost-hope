# Independent checks

The source review compared the playable rules on p. 14 with the sample warrior on pp. 203–204. Both support the once-daily sonic ray and the once-daily Outburst. The ray's generic Charisma-based save line has no effect because Energy Ray allows no save. The 2006 errata contains no Maenad, Outburst, or Energy Ray correction. [Playable chapter]() · [Energy Ray]() · [Sample warrior]() · [Publisher errata]()

The April 2004 text does not state how long Outburst takes to activate. These cards use the original core default for an activated extraordinary ability. The 2007 *Rules Compendium* gives a different general default for extraordinary abilities, so the standard action is a disclosed choice for these original-rules encounters. [Core special abilities](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#extraordinaryAbilities) · [Rules Compendium, p. 118]()

The p. 203 Maenad illustration is by Monte Moore. Vara's staged portrait is original. It follows the p. 13 description of dark braids and skin flecked with living crystal. [Physical description]()

The catalog review checked both builds in and out of Outburst, including their saves, skills, attacks, and Vara's Wild Surge and psychic enervation. It also verified that the two NPCs attach to one Maenad entry and that Maenad and Xeph apply in either order to temporary files.
