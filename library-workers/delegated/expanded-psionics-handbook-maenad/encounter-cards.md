# Maenad encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Maenad and Psionic subtypes, ordinary vision, and a 30-ft. land speed. Each has ECL 1. [Playable Maenad, p. 14]()

| NPC | AC (touch, flat-footed) | HP | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will | Power points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vara Flintshore, Wilder 1 | 13 (11, 12) | 8 | +1 | +2, +1, +1 | 5 |
| Toren Glasswake, Expert 1 | 14 (11, 13) | 7 | +1 | +1, +1, +2 | 2 |

Each has one **Sonic Energy Ray** per day. Standard action with an audible scream, +1 ranged touch within 25 ft., 1d6−1 sonic, ignores object hardness. No save. Power resistance applies. The ability spends no power points. [Energy Ray, p. 103]()

Each has one **Outburst** per day for up to four rounds. The cards use a standard action to start it under the original 3.5 rules for activated extraordinary abilities. The first printing gives the Strength, Intelligence, and Wisdom changes and leaves activation unstated.

## Vara Flintshore

A rising tide is about to cover the shelf where a pilot is trapped. Vara can swim a depth line across if someone keeps it secured on shore. **Provisional CR 1.**

Concentration +6, Profession (sailor) +3, Spot +3, Swim +8. In Outburst, Swim +9, Spot and Profession +2, and Will +0.

- Shortspear +1 melee or thrown, one-handed damage 1d6+1 piercing, critical ×2, thrown range increment 20 ft. In Outburst, melee +2 and damage 1d6+2 on either attack.
- **Force Screen**. Spend 1 PP as a standard action. AC 17 (touch 11, flat-footed 16) for one minute. The +4 shield bonus also applies against incorporeal touch attacks.
- **Wild Surge +1** can extend Force Screen to two minutes at the same cost. Roll a 5% chance of psychic enervation immediately afterward. On enervation, lose 1 PP and become dazed until the end of the next turn. [Wilder, pp. 30–31]() · [Force Screen, p. 108]()

## Toren Glasswake

A grounded ship needs its keel inspected. Toren can identify the right timber and direct the lift if someone steadies the brace. **Provisional CR 1/2.**

Craft (shipbuilding) +8, Knowledge (architecture and engineering) +5, Climb +5, Swim +4, and Use Rope +5. Use Outburst for the lift after the inspection. While active, Climb +6 and Swim +5, but Craft drops to +7, Knowledge to +4, and Will to +1.

- Club +2 melee, one-handed damage 1d6+2 bludgeoning, critical ×2. In Outburst, +3 melee and 1d6+3 damage.
- Light crossbow +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. range increment. Reload as a move action.
