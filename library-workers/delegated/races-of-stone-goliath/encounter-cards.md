# Goliath encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. They begin as Medium Monstrous Humanoids with 5-ft. space and natural reach and darkvision 60 ft. Both have +4 on grapple size from Powerful Build in the listed totals. Each has one class Hit Die and level adjustment +1. [Printed traits, p. 56]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Kethan Linekeeper Varukutai, Expert 1

Provisional CR 1, ECL 2. AC 13, touch 11, flat-footed 12. HP 8, initiative +1, speed 30 ft. Fort +2, Ref +1, Will +2. BAB +0, grapple +8. STR 18, DEX 12, CON 14, INT 12, WIS 10, CHA 8. Common, Gol-Kaa, Dwarven.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Large longspear | +4 melee, 2d6+6 piercing, ×3. Hits at 10 ft. |
| Large club | +4 melee, 1d8+4 bludgeoning, ×2, 5-ft. reach. Can throw it at +1 with a 10-ft. increment. |

Climb +8, Craft (ropemaking) +5, Jump +8, Listen +4, Sense Motive +9, Spot +4, Use Rope +5. Sense Motive includes Skill Focus and the species’ +2 bonus. Kethan can move 15 ft. with an accelerated Climb check as a move action at his full +8.

The pass will ice over soon. If the party checks the downhill anchors, Kethan can jump the break and rig a crossing. If someone corners him on the path, he uses the longspear at 10 ft. and switches to the club up close.

## Rova Stonecaller Athamavi, Barbarian 1

Provisional CR 2, ECL 2. AC 14, touch 11, flat-footed 13. HP 14, initiative +5, speed 40 ft. Fort +4, Ref +1, Will +1. BAB +1, grapple +9. STR 19, DEX 12, CON 15, INT 10, WIS 12, CHA 8. Common and Gol-Kaa.

| Attack | Ordinary | In Mountain Rage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large greatsword, 19–20/×2 | +5 melee, 3d6+6 slashing, 5-ft. reach | +7 melee, 3d6+10 slashing, 10-ft. reach |
| Large javelin, ×2, 30-ft. increment | +2 ranged, 1d8+4 piercing | +1 ranged, 1d8+7 piercing |

Climb +7, Jump +11, Knowledge (nature) +4, Listen +5, Sense Motive +3. Jump includes a +4 bonus for moving at 40 ft. in light gear. Improved Initiative is included. Barbarian illiteracy applies.

Mountain Rage is available once per day and at most once per encounter for up to 7 rounds. Rova becomes Large with 10-ft. space and natural reach. STR 25, CON 19, HP 16, AC 11, touch 8, flat-footed 10, grapple +12, Fort +6, Ref +1, Will +3, Climb +10, Jump +14. Weapon size stays Large and the grapple size modifier stays +4. On ending the rage, remove 2 bonus HP. Fatigue lasts for the rest of the encounter and gives −2 STR and DEX and prevents running or charging. [Mountain Rage, pp. 150–151]()

Rova is trying to bring a quarry crew inside before the next rockfall. She asks the party to count the crew at the path marker, and uses Mountain Rage if a creature follows them to the shelter.
