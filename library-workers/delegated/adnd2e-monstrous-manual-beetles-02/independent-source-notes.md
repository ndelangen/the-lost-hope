# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual giant beetles, second group

The full table is on printed p. 18 (viewer page 21), and the Rhinoceros, Stag and Water descriptions and the illustration are on printed p. 19 (viewer page 22). ## Giant Rhinoceros Beetle

- Any jungle, uncommon, solitary, any activity, herbivore, Non- (0) intelligence, no treasure, neutral, 1–6 appearing. AC 2, movement 6, HD 12, THAC0 9, two attacks for 3–18 and 2–16 damage, special attacks, special defenses and magic resistance nil. Large (12 ft. long), morale elite (14), 4,000 XP.
- The prose specifies 3d6 damage from the mandibles and 2d8 from the horn. Horn length is about six feet. It roams tropical and subtropical rainforests searching for fruit and vegetation and crushing things in its path. The description gives no separate trampling action or damage. Its shell is often bright or iridescent, and intact shells are prized by clerics as temple ornaments.

## Giant Stag Beetle

- Any forest, common, solitary, any activity, herbivore, Non- (0) intelligence, no treasure, neutral, 2–12 appearing. AC 3, movement 6, HD 7, THAC0 13, three attacks for 4–16/1–10/1–10 damage, special attacks, special defenses and magic resistance nil. Large (10 ft. long), morale elite (13), 975 XP.
- The prose gives two horns usually at least eight feet long and up to 10 damage per horn. The two 1–10 slots match these attacks. The family’s mandibles suggest the remaining 4–16 attack, but the Stag paragraph does not name that slot or specify dice for it. Use the printed range.
- These woodland beetles favor grains and growing crops. They fight when attacked or when they find organic material they regard as food. A raid can strip a farm of crops and stampede livestock. Hungry beetles can also eat livestock. The table retains herbivore as the diet field, and the livestock detail belongs in the behavior text. The description gives no shell color.

## Giant Water Beetle

- Fresh water, common, solitary, any activity, omnivore, Non- (0) intelligence, no treasure, neutral, 1–12 appearing. AC 3, movement 3 and Sw 9, HD 4, THAC0 17, one attack for 3–18 damage, special attacks, special defenses and magic resistance nil. Medium (6 ft. long), morale elite (14), 120 XP. The print gives the damage range without specifying dice.
- Its freshwater habitat is at least 30 feet deep. It finds prey by scent and vibration and eats nearly anything, including animals. It is slow on land and faster in water. In navigable rivers or lakes it may attack and sink a vessel to reach prey aboard. The source gives no special vessel attack or damage procedure.
- It breathes air and carries a bubble under its wings to stay submerged for extended periods. It can bring that bubble into a cave or other cavity able to hold it. The source gives no duration and specifies no shell color or specialized swimming-leg shape.

## Illustration and catalog

All three have the family’s chitinous shells, six bristly legs, chewing mandibles, and two wing pairs that do not provide flight. Tony DiTerlizzi’s signed p. 19 illustration shows a mounted, long-horned beetle in profile. His 1993 commission lists it as “Beetle, Giant,” and his retrospective identifies a Venezuelan rhinoceros beetle as the pose reference. The horn and placement support using the image for the Rhinoceros Beetle’s anatomy. The rider, gear, exact palette and second visible projection are details of the illustration. The Rhinoceros stat block gives one horn attack and one mandible attack. The spread gives no dedicated Stag or Water portrait.
