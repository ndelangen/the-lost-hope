# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual beetles, second batch

The three remaining forms from the Giant Beetle table in TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. The table and descriptions were verified in the January 1994 third printing of the 1993 book. [Printed table, p. 18](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bf979c3e2feda7a98b369f4c896f4541.webp) · [Descriptions, p. 19](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/2d16ae154a6a7a8025654797682b1bd3.webp) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Printed HD | Printed attacks | Distinctive behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Rhinoceros Beetle | 12 | 3d6 mandibles and 2d8 horn | Jungle forager with a horn roughly six feet long. |
| Giant Stag Beetle | 7 | 4–16 for the first attack, plus two horns for 1–10 each | Crop raider. The prose also describes hungry beetles eating livestock. |
| Giant Water Beetle | 4 | One attack for 3–18 | Freshwater hunter that breathes air carried under its wings. |

The Stag Beetle's first attack stays unnamed. The Water Beetle's text describes attacking and sinking vessels without a separate damage rule and gives no duration for the air bubble. [Exact printing evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

The family illustration on p. 19 is by Tony DiTerlizzi, who describes using a rhinoceros beetle as his model. The three new portraits are on plain parchment and distinguish the single horn, paired stag horns, and Water Beetle's paddle legs. The Water Beetle's appearance draws on real diving beetles. [DiTerlizzi's account](https://diterlizzi.com/behind-the-monstrous-manual-part-8/) · [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [96-pixel previews](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

All three profiles passed search and portrait loading in a temporary copy. The existing 3.5 Giant Stag Beetle monster retained its default version and gallery. Its new AD&D counterpart opens under the Beast category with its own portrait. Both staged beetle patches applied together in either order and retained all six profiles.
