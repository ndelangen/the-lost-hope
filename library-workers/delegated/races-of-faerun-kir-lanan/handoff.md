# Kir-Lanan

This batch adds the playable Kir-Lanan from the March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn*. The profile uses the 2004 revision for type, space, and reach. It keeps the original Negative Energy Touch damage, save, and healing. [Original traits]()

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species, Varek Ashveil, and Sira Duskmere. It is based on catalog commit `8bafc0f`. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) maps Varek's [square portrait](portraits/kir-lanan-draft.png) to this publication.

Manual review covered the rules, both builds, and the [portrait thumbnail](portrait-thumbnails.jpg). Search, in-app portrait loading, and automated verification are deferred.
