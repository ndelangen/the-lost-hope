# Kir-Lanan encounter cards

## Varek Ashveil

Four racial Hit Dice. Provisional CR 3, ECL 8. AC 16, HP 29, initiative +3. Land 30 ft., fly 90 ft. with good maneuverability. Fort +3, Ref +7, Will +4.

- Claw +9, 1d4+4 piercing and slashing. Two claws on a full attack.
- Negative Energy Touch 3/day. Touch +8 or deliver through a claw hit. Deals 2d6 hit-point damage and 1 Strength damage. Fortitude DC 13 negates the Strength damage. Varek heals the energy HP damage dealt up to his maximum.
- Ray of Enfeeblement 3/day. Ranged touch +7 within 35 ft., Strength penalty 1d6+2 for 4 minutes, no save. Spell resistance applies.
- Rebuke or command undead 3/day as Cleric 4.

Varek waits on a shuttered ledge to see who comes out of the tower.

## Sira Duskmere

Four racial Hit Dice and Fighter 2. Provisional CR 4, ECL 10. AC 19, HP 50, initiative +6. Land 30 ft., fly 90 ft. with good maneuverability. Fort +7, Ref +6, Will +4.

- Longsword +12, 1d8+5 slashing, critical 19–20/×2. Full attack adds the sword at +7 and a claw at +6 for 1d4+2 piercing and slashing.
- Negative Energy Touch 3/day. Touch +11 or deliver through a claw hit. The Strength damage save is Fortitude DC 12.
- Ray of Enfeeblement 3/day, ranged touch +8. Rebuke or command undead 3/day as Cleric 6.

Sira holds the ledge until the last evacuee reaches the rookery exit. [Complete builds](npc-builds.json)
