# Kir-Lanan sources

The March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn* covers Kir-Lanan on printed pp. 137–140. The viewer is one page ahead. Its player traits give four racial Hit Dice, level adjustment +4, and ECL 8 before class levels. Common and Kir-Lanan are automatic. Fighter is the favored class. The 2004 *Monster Update* confirms Monstrous Humanoid type, 5-ft. space and reach, and level adjustment +4. [Player traits]() · [Monster Update, p. 2]()

Negative Energy Touch can be delivered by a melee touch attack or through a claw hit against normal AC. Its Fortitude save is DC 12 plus Charisma modifier and applies to the point of Strength damage. The Kir-Lanan heals only the hit-point damage dealt by the energy. The 2001 *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting* gives a sample CR 2 monster, and the errata corrects its claw delivery to use normal melee AC. Both encounters here are original builds with provisional CRs. [Player traits]() · [Earlier monster and errata](https://anyflip.com/njwsm/eaxu/basic/301-336)

Both builds use the 3.5 default of piercing and slashing for claws. Ordinary flight uses the core light-load limit. [3.5 natural weapons and movement](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#naturalWeapons)

The build uses the 3.5 Ray of Enfeeblement rules at the printed caster level 4. The effect is a Strength penalty. Rebuke Undead scales with total Hit Dice. The Kir-Lanan's positive-energy weakness and divine-magic restrictions come from the player entry. [Ray of Enfeeblement](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/rayOfEnfeeblement.htm) · [Player traits and restrictions]()

Adam Rex illustrated the sourcebook's Kir-Lanan. Varek is an original ImageGen portrait based on the book's dark scales, batlike wings, blunt temple horns, and cloth wrappings threaded with ivory or metal. Big Linda and Sael supplied the parchment reference. [Appearance and illustration]()
