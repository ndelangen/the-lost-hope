# Player’s Guide 2 beasts

This batch covers all five outstanding beast entries in the 2026 Tales of the Valiant *Player's Guide 2*. The book credits and publisher's sell sheet agree on ISBN 978-1-964506-24-1. The source text comes from a third-party extraction of the book, with rules checked against printed pp. 142–145. [Book text](https://www.scribd.com/document/1015989165/ToV-Players-Guide-2) · [Publisher sell sheet]()

| --- | --- | --- |
| Cavalry Camel | 1/2 | Barding, Spit, Defensive Kick, and handling |
| Hunting Hound | 1/4 | Tracking, the Bite grapple, Flushing Bark, and Swift Hunter |
| Oliphaunt Warbeast | 6 | Siege powers, charge, and Trunk Sweep |
| War Pig | 1/2 | Charge, keen smell, and Frightful Squeal |
| Homing Pigeon | 0 | Three trained destinations, message delivery, and its distinct training rule |

The Hound's printed CR 1/4 takes precedence over the index's rounded 0.3. The printed *Oliphaunt* spelling is recorded against the existing *Oliphant Warbeast* catalog entry. The handling paragraph specifies a DC 10 WIS (Animal Handling) check, then calls its failure a failed save. The profiles follow the named check, and the pigeon uses its separate destination training.
