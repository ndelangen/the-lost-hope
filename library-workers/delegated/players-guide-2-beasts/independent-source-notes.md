## Shared combat animal rules — pp. 142–143

A designated handler uses an action and a DC 10 WIS (Animal Handling) check to command Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide or Use an Object. The next sentence calls failure a failed save, although the rule names only a check. On failure the animal defends itself and otherwise takes no actions. Use an Object can deliver something or fetch something from a willing creature or from where it sits unworn and uncarried. In mounted combat the animal follows controlled-mount rules. Each of up to four handlers trains with the animal for a week. A fifth handler replaces the one that commanded it least recently. At the GM’s discretion, a strong bond can prevent replacement and cause that training to fail. The pigeon has its own rules.

## Cavalry Camel — p. 143

Large Beast, CR 1/2, AC 12 with studded leather barding, HP 25, speed 40 ft., Perception 9, Stealth 10. Resistant to blinded and exhaustion. Nonmagical sand does not slow it. Bite +5, reach 5 ft., 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning. Spit is a bonus action, recharge 4–6, one visible creature within 15 ft., DC 13 CON or blinded through the end of its next turn. Defensive Kick reacts when a visible creature within 5 ft. damages the camel or rider. DC 13 STR or pushed up to 10 ft. and knocked prone. The reaction specifies no damage. Cost 200 gp, capacity 480 lb. Most are single-humped dromedaries, with double-humped camels also in service.

## Homing Pigeon — p. 143

Tiny Beast, CR 0, AC 10, HP 8, speed 10 ft. and fly 50 ft., Perception 11, Stealth 10. The war animal table’s 50 ft. is its flying speed. Peck +2, reach 5 ft., 2 (1d4) piercing. Training takes one week per humanoid or location, up to three remembered. A fourth replaces the one visited least recently. Messenger gives direction and approximate distance on the same plane. Any humanoid can attach a message tube and specify a remembered recipient or place. The pigeon flies directly there and resists exhaustion while carrying the tube. Resolute Delivery is a bonus-action Disengage or Dash. With a tube, it can move 15 extra feet before or after that action. Cost 8 gp, capacity 15 lb.

## Hunting Hound — p. 144

Small Beast, CR 1/4, AC 12, HP 15, speed 40 ft., Perception 13, Stealth 12. Diligent Tracker grants advantage on WIS (Survival) checks to track creatures. Hearing or smell raises Perception to 18. Bite +4, reach 5 ft., 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing, and grapples Medium or smaller creatures (escape DC 12). The hound cannot bite another target during that grapple. Flushing Bark is a bonus action affecting creatures hiding or behind cover in a 15 ft. cone. DC 12 WIS or stop hiding or move up to 10 ft. out from cover, avoiding opportunity attacks and damaging terrain on forced movement. Swift Hunter is a bonus action moving up to its speed toward a visible hostile creature. Cost 50 gp, capacity 210 lb. The prose describes a scent tracker and gives no breed or coat color.

## Oliphaunt Warbeast — p. 144

Huge Beast, CR 6, natural AC 14, HP 133, speed 40 ft., Perception 13, Stealth 9. Resistant to bludgeoning, frightened and prone. Siege Monster doubles damage to objects and structures. Destroying a Small or larger object forces hostile creatures within 10 ft. to make a DC 16 CHA save or be frightened through the end of their next turns. Trampling Charge requires 20 ft. of straight movement toward a creature and a Gore hit that turn. DC 16 STR or prone. It can make a bonus-action Stomp against the prone target. Multiattack is one Gore and one Stomp or two Gores. Gore +9, reach 10 ft., 20 (4d6 + 6) slashing. Stomp +9, reach 5 ft., prone creature only, 22 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning. Trunk Sweep is a bonus action, 15 ft. cone, DC 16 STR or prone. Keep listed attack and damage numbers alongside STR +9. Cost 2,400 gp, capacity 1,560 lb. The smaller war breed is described with a thick gray-brown hide, four curved tusks, a short trunk and a toothed maw.

## War Pig — lore p. 144 and stat block p. 145

Medium Beast, CR 1/2, AC 10, HP 27, speed 30 ft., Perception 11, Stealth 10. Resistant to grappled. Heightened Smell raises Perception to 16. Gore +4, reach 5 ft., 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing. Charge adds 5 (2d4) piercing to a Gore hit after 20 ft. of straight movement toward the target that turn and forces a creature to make a DC 12 STR save or fall prone. Frightful Squeal is a bonus action. Each Beast and Humanoid within 10 ft. makes a DC 12 WIS save or is frightened through the end of its next turn. The rule gives no hearing requirement or ally exemption. Cost 50 gp, capacity 210 lb. The prose gives bristly brown and gray hair and two long, curved tusks.

## Monster Vault 2 overlap

The publisher index gives separate Monster Vault 2 entries for Cavalry Camel, Oliphaunt Warbeast and War Pig with matching categories, sizes and CRs. Hunting Hound and Homing Pigeon appear under Player’s Guide 2. The supplement establishes the shared handler rules and lists its own prices and capacities. I found no complete public Monster Vault 2 page for a line-by-line comparison, so stat differences between the books remain unverified.
