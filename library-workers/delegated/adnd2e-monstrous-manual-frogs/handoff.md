# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual frogs

Giant, Killer, and Poisonous Frog come from page 119 of the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. Giant Frog adds a historical version to an existing catalog entry. The separately staged *Night of the Walking Dead* Giant Frog remains a distinct adventure version. Killer and Poisonous Frog add two Beast entries. [Printed page 119](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/eb733e29de9e9cd310bd83dcba021125.webp) · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Rules preserved |
| --- | --- |
| Giant Frog | The printed HD cell says 1+3. The profile uses the 1, 2, and 3 HD versions described in the prose and supported by the THAC0 and XP rows. The leap depends on weight. The tongue gains +4 to hit, and the prey's weight determines how quickly it can be reeled in. Swallowing uses the printed roll of 20 and the under-three-foot limit. |
| Killer Frog | Three attacks with front talons and teeth. The entry excludes swallowing whole. |
| Poisonous Frog | Skin contact and a bite both call for a save at +4. The entry leaves the failed-save effect unspecified. The printed length reads 6′–1′. The profile keeps the Tiny category and records the malformed range. |

The three portraits sit on plain parchment with the full feet in view. The Giant is an enlarged ordinary frog, the Killer shows its teeth and forefoot talons, and the Poisonous is a small ordinary frog. Their colors and poses are illustration choices. The printed family illustration has no confirmed variant label or artist. [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [96-pixel previews](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

A temporary copy verified all three profiles in search by name, appearance, habitat, and rules text. Each selected version loaded its own portrait. Giant Frog kept its Basic Rules (2018) default and thumbnail. The new profile and the older adventure version were both selectable when the two patches were applied together.
