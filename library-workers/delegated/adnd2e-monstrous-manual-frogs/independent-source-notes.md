All three inhabit any fresh water, have pack organization, can be active at any time, have Non- (0) intelligence, and list nil treasure, alignment and magic resistance. Pack is their organization label. The Giant’s prose describes groups without a social structure.

## Giant Frog

- Uncommon, carnivore, 5–40 appearing. AC 7, movement 3 and Sw 9, one attack, special attacks tongue and swallow whole, special defenses nil, Tiny to Medium (2–6 ft. long), morale average (8). The print’s Hit Dice cell reads **1 + 3**. The adjoining THAC0 and XP entries and the bite prose establish three forms at 1, 2 and 3 HD. The electronic table gives **1–3**. At 1 HD use THAC0 19, bite 1–3 and 120 XP. At 2 HD use THAC0 16, bite 1–6 and 175 XP. At 3 HD use THAC0 16, bite 2–8 and 270 XP.
- A two-foot frog weighs 50 lb. and each additional foot adds 50 lb., up to 250 lb. at six feet. The prose says 1 HD frogs are two feet long, 2 HD frogs are two to four feet, and 3 HD frogs exceed four feet. The exact two-foot case therefore appears in two bands. Maximum forward jump length is 180 ft. at 50 lb., decreasing by 20 ft. per additional 50 lb. It can jump 30 ft. straight up and cannot jump backward or directly to either side.
- Its camouflage imposes −3 on opponents’ surprise rolls in its natural habitat. The sticky tongue extends up to three times the frog’s length, attacks at +4 and deals no damage. A caught victim can make one attack against the tongue before the frog reels it in. A hit makes the frog release that victim and refrain from attacking it again.
- If the victim misses and weighs less than the frog, it reaches the frog’s mouth that round. A heavier victim takes an extra round and receives a second chance to strike the tongue. The exact equal-weight case is unspecified. A victim weighing more than twice the frog cannot be drawn in and is released on the third round after capture even if it never strikes the tongue.
- A successful bite in the first round the victim is in range deals maximum damage. An attack roll result of 20 can swallow a target shorter than 3 ft. A swallowed victim has three rounds before asphyxiation and can escape with a sharp-edged weapon on an attack roll result of 18 or better. Successful escape kills the frog. Damage to the frog while it has swallowed someone has a 33% chance of also affecting that victim. The printed mechanics describe roll results without adding a qualifier about unmodified dice.
- Giant frogs resemble common frogs apart from size and have camouflage suited to their habitat. They eat insects, fish and small mammals. They fear fire and always retreat from it. [Printed p. 119](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/eb733e29de9e9cd310bd83dcba021125.webp)

## Killer Frog

- Very rare, carnivore, 3–18 appearing. AC 8, movement 6 and Sw 12, HD 1+4, THAC0 18, three attacks for 1–2/1–2/2–5, special attacks and defenses nil, Small (3 ft. long), morale unsteady (6), 35 XP.
- This smaller Giant Frog relative fights with its front talons and sharp teeth. The text expressly excludes swallowing whole and gives it an appetite for human prey. The table supplies its attack ranges without a separate tongue or jump rule. The description gives no fixed colors. [Printed p. 119](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/eb733e29de9e9cd310bd83dcba021125.webp)

## Poisonous Frog

- Rare, insectivore, 2–12 appearing. AC 8, movement 3 and Sw 9, HD 1, THAC0 19, one attack for 1 damage, special attacks nil, special defense poison, morale unsteady (6), 65 XP. The printed size is **T (6′–1′ long)**, an internally reversed measurement. The description calls it a rare type of normal frog. Six inches to one foot is a plausible reading, while Tiny is the unambiguous printed category.
- Both skin contact and a bite expose creatures to its fictional contact poison. The text gives victims +4 to their saving throws and supplies no poison type, failed-save outcome, onset or duration. The earlier 1e *Monster Manual* describes its poison as potentially fatal, which is a rule from that separate source. The 2e entry gives no fixed color or warning markings.
