# Proposed playable profiles, batch 11

50 claims for overlap review from Tyler Kamstra’s Monstrous Races (2018), v2 , for the 2014 fifth-edition rules.

I compared canonical names against the ten prior staged batches and catalog commit `5f228a2`. All 50 names match existing encounter entries and have no playable profile in that snapshot.

I checked the printed player trait headings and page numbers directly. When the book sends a creature to another entry for its traits, both names share one playable claim. For example, Brown Bear uses Black Bear, and Draft Horse, Riding Horse and Warhorse all use Pony. Giant Lizard’s three printed forms likewise share one profile. The TSV gives the alias or grouping and its source for each entry.

The printed trait block governs each build. The author’s optional adjustments in Design Notes are separate. See pp. 7–8 for this supplement’s general rules, including Tiny characters, natural weapons and somatic components.
