# Owl, Panther, Phase Spider, Poisonous Snake, and Polar Bear

Five playable profiles from Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races* v2. Each has one original 2014 character build and one square portrait. [Printed player text]() · [DMs Guild listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 combat rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

| Profile | Printed traits | NPC | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owl | p. 255 | Pip Brasscall, Rogue 2 | AC 13, HP 17, talons +5 for 1d4+3, Perception expertise +6. |
| Panther | pp. 255–256 | Coalwhisk, Rogue 1 | AC 13, HP 10, bite or claw +5 for 1d8+3, Pounce Athletics +7. |
| Phase Spider | pp. 256–257 | Seamstep, Barbarian 1 | Unarmored Defense AC 15, HP 14, bite +5 for 1d4+3 piercing and 1d4 poison. Rage adds 2 piercing. |
| Poisonous Snake | p. 257 | Rushcoil, Monk 1 | Unarmored Defense AC 15, HP 10, Dexterity bite +5 for 1d8+3 poison and bonus headbutt +5 for 1d4+3 bludgeoning. |
| Polar Bear | p. 258 | Driftmark, Barbarian 2 | Unarmored Defense AC 15, HP 25, bite or claw +5 for 1d8+3. Rage adds 2 damage. |

Pip is Tiny. Flyby gives a bonus-action Disengage, which overlaps with Cunning Action. Coalwhisk's Pounce uses the 2014 shove contest after a qualifying hit. Both animals have finesse in their printed natural strike traits and can use Sneak Attack when eligible.

Seamstep is an intelligent Monstrosity and uses nonlinguistic signals, consistent with the explanatory text on p. 257. Its Ethereal Jaunt lasts until its next turn starts. The player traits allow movement through webs without adding a web attack.

Rushcoil uses the Medium size stated in the player traits. The design commentary mentions Tiny during its derivation. Venomous Snake is a descriptive search term for this profile. Martial Arts provides Dexterity for the bite and the conventional bonus headbutt follows the supplement's p. 7 restriction. Driftmark uses the printed swim speed and has no species trait for resistance to cold damage.

I checked 27-point buy, ability modifiers, proficiency, attack math, movement, and defenses by hand. I inspected each original 1254 × 1254 PNG and compared its silhouette and face with Big Linda at 96 pixels. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [Generation prompts](portrait-prompts.json)
