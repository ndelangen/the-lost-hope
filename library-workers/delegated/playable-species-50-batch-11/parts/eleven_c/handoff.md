# Five playable beasts from Monstrous Races

[Species profiles](book-species.json) · [NPC builds](npc-builds.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

The builds use Tyler Kamstra's v2 player traits, 2014 fifth-edition class rules, and 27-point buy. I checked the attack bonuses, class features, saves, AC, and HP by hand. [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [v2 text]() · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Playable profile | NPC | Printed pages | Rules check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crab | Clatterclaw, Monk 1 | 227 and Monk exception on 7 | AC 14, HP 10, Claw +4. The Monk bonus strike uses the shell for 1d4+2 bludgeoning. |
| Crocodile | Mirejaw, Barbarian 1 | 227 and Giant Crocodile on 235 | AC 14, HP 14, Bite +5. An Attack-action hit opens a bonus-action grapple attempt at Athletics +5. Rage uses that same bonus action on the turn it begins. |
| Deer | Tawnbell, Druid 1 | 228–229 and somatic exception on 8 | AC 13, HP 10, spell save DC 13. Two 1st-level slots and four prepared spells. All six selected spells avoid material components. |
| Eagle | Brackenpin, Rogue 2 | 229 | AC 13, HP 17, Talons +5. Printed finesse allows Sneak Attack. Perception expertise gives a passive score of 17, or 22 when Keen Sight grants advantage. |
| Elephant | Kettlebrow, Fighter 2 | 230 and Mammoth on 252 | AC 15, HP 22, Gore or Stomp +5. A 15-ft. straight approach and hit opens a bonus-action shove attempt at Athletics +5. |

The Crab and Crocodile design notes differ from the traits. Crab uses Small size and 60 ft. blindsight from its printed block. Both builds use ordinary breath-holding rules because neither printed block gives a trait that changes them. Crocodile's printed Bite gives a grapple attempt, handled with its jaws and the regular 2014 contest. Mirejaw uses no equipment. [2014 combat rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

The printed Giant Crocodile cross-reference is grouped under Crocodile and Mammoth is grouped under Elephant. Each grouped profile has one NPC and one portrait showing the base form.
