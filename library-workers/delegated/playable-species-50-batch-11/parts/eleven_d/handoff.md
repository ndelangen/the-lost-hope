# Elk, Giant Badger, Giant Bat, Giant Crab, and Giant Goat

These are playable species from Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races* v2. Each build uses the 2014 core rules. [Printed player text]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 core rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014)

| Profile | Printed player source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Elk | v2 pp. 230–231. Giant Elk refers here from p. 236. | Copper Tine, Barbarian 2. Unarmored AC 13, HP 21, ram +5 for 1d8+3 before Rage or Charge. |
| Giant Badger | v2 p. 232 | Lint Digger, Barbarian 1. Unarmored AC 14, HP 14, claws or bite +5 for 1d8+3 before Rage. |
| Giant Bat | v2 p. 233 | Rafter Click, Rogue 2. Unarmored AC 13, HP 15, finesse bite +5 for 1d8+3 and a qualifying Sneak Attack adds 1d6. |
| Giant Crab | v2 pp. 234–235 | Notch Shell, Monk 1. Shell AC 16, HP 9, claw +5 for 1d6+3 piercing and Martial Arts bonus kick +5 for 1d4+3 bludgeoning. |
| Giant Goat | v2 p. 237. Goat refers here from p. 248. | Fennel Cragstep, Druid 1. Natural AC 12, HP 9, ram +5 for 1d8+3 before Charge and spell DC 13. |

The profile text includes the printed Giant Elk and Goat aliases. Each points to its source's shared Medium player trait block and a single portrait of the assigned form. I used the printed 1d8 Giant Bat bite and kept the design-note-only No Hands wording outside the Giant Badger player traits. The Giant Badger build uses no gear. The Giant Crab's Monk bonus-action strike uses the class die and bludgeoning under v2 p. 7. Fennel's spell choices use vocal and form-appropriate somatic components under v2 p. 8. The druid cantrip is Druidcraft with no hand-dependent spell effect. [2014 Druidcraft](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2080-druidcraft)

I checked ability scores, AC, HP, senses, attack and skill bonuses, triggers, and spell numbers by hand. I reviewed all five square PNGs at full size and at 96 px beside Big Linda. The circular preview clips the outermost elk antler and bat wing tips, with both faces and the main silhouettes visible. The square PNGs keep the complete creatures.
