# Monstrous Races v2, group G

Five playable Beast profiles, each with one linked NPC using 2014 fifth edition and one original portrait. The player trait blocks come from Tyler Kamstra's v2 text. [Authored rules mirror]() · [Publisher listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Profile | Printed page | NPC | AC | HP |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Killer Whale | 251 | Bellfin, Barbarian 2 | 13 | 21 |
| Lion | 251 | Kedge, Barbarian 1 | 13 | 13 |
| Mastiff | 253 | Cinder, Monk 2 | 15 | 15 |
| Mule | 253 | Beam, Fighter 2 | 15 | 20 |
| Octopus | 254 | Knots, Rogue 2 | 13 | 17 |

- Bellfin's printed bite deals slashing damage. The natural armor and Keen Hearing mentioned in the design commentary do not appear in the player traits. Orca is included for search.
- Kedge's Pounce uses the 2014 shove rules. Pounce, Pack Tactics, and starting Rage compete for the same bonus action. [2014 shove rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat#ShovingaCreature)
- Cinder's action bite uses the racial 1d8 die. The bonus-action forepaw thumps follow Kamstra's monk clarification on p. 7 and deal 1d4 bludgeoning plus Dexterity.
- Beam's AC includes fitted hide barding and the Defense style. Sure-footed applies against being knocked prone. The pack saddle sits over the barding. [2014 barding rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment)
- Knots uses the printed Medium size and finesse-qualified tentacle strike. I set the Ink Cloud release to an action because the printed trait leaves its cost open and the 2014 encounter ability uses an action. The radius and recharge come from the player's trait. Octopuses and octopi appear in the searchable profile text. [2014 encounter octopus](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-o#Octopus)

I checked the builds' AC, HP, saves, movement, skills, attacks, and action costs by hand. I compared the five ImageGen PNGs against Big Linda at full size and checked the 96 px circular thumbnails. I revised Knots's portrait so all eight arm tips are visible.
