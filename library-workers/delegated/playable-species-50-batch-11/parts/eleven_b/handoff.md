# Black Bear, Boar, Camel, Cat, and Constrictor Snake

The profiles use Tyler Kamstra's printed v2 player traits. The NPCs use the 2014 fifth-edition rules, standard array, and fixed hit point increases. Brown Bear, Giant Boar, and Giant Constrictor Snake appear in their base profiles as exact names from the printed cross-references. Polar Bear has its own rules. Gravelroot's Charge adds damage once per turn, and Relentless applies to any reduction to 0 HP that does not kill outright. Rillglass uses the supplement's conventional bonus-strike rule on p. 7. The Cat's printed trait block says Small and omits Keen Smell. Loamcoil uses the printed walking speed and the standard grapple contest. The constrict hit has no automatic restraint.
