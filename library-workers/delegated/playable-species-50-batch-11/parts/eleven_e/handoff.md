# Monstrous Races v2, five giant beasts

Five player profiles from Tyler Kamstra's second printing for 2014 fifth edition. The NPCs use the printed player traits and standard starting ability scores. [Publisher listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [v2 rules text]()

- **Giant Lizard, pp. 238–239.** Gecko, Iguana and Salamander share one profile. Bramblepad is a Gecko Monk with Spider Climb. The elemental Salamander is a separate entry.
- **Giant Rat, p. 242.** Cobbledot uses the bite's printed finesse rule for Sneak Attack. The player trait block grants Keen Smell and leaves out the Darkvision mentioned in the design notes.
- **Giant Scorpion, pp. 242–243.** Keelpin rolls a separate grapple contest after a claw hit. The player text gives the claw die without a damage type. This build uses bludgeoning.
- **Giant Sea Horse, p. 243.** Siltcrest uses Strength for a ram that triggers Charge. A Dexterity-based ram is available and leaves Charge inactive. The build is for underwater play.
- **Giant Vulture, p. 245.** Shearshade reads and understands Common and signals through wing dips. Monk Martial Arts and Pack Tactics compete for one bonus action.

The rat, scorpion and sea horse trait blocks describe object manipulation using tentacle wording. The summaries record that permission and the builds use ordinary animal anatomy, with no hand-operated equipment in their actions.

## Manual checks

I checked saves, skills, armor, hit points and attack totals against the 2014 rules. In roster order, the builds have AC 13, 13, 14, 13 and 13, with HP 10, 17, 14, 13 and 10. Every listed primary attack has +5 to hit. Both monks take ordinary bludgeoning bonus strikes under the supplement's p. 7 rule.
