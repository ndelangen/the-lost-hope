# Tiger, Weasel, Winter Wolf, Wolf, and Worg

Five playable profiles, five original NPCs, and five original parchment portraits. The profiles use Tyler Kamstra’s v2 player trait blocks for the 2014 fifth-edition rules.

[Player profiles](book-species.json) · [NPC builds](npc-builds.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait files](portrait-files.json)

## Printed rules and build checks

| Profile | v2 folio | NPC and calculations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiger | 265, with the Saber-Toothed Tiger reference on 262 | Saffron Step, Rogue 1. AC 13 and HP 9. The printed finesse property allows a 1d6 Sneak Attack rider on a qualifying bite or claw hit. Pounce uses Athletics +7 after the charge and hit. |
| Weasel | 267 | Hush Thread, Rogue 2. AC 13 and HP 15. Bite +5 for 1d4 + 3 and a possible Sneak Attack rider. Passive Perception 16, or 21 for smell or hearing. The v2 Tiny rule reduces carry capacity to 30 lb. |
| Winter Wolf | 267–268 | Rime Knell, Barbarian 2. AC 14 and HP 23. Cold Breath uses DC 12 and 2d6 cold damage, with half damage on a successful save. The racial bite qualifies for Rage and Reckless Attack. |
| Wolf | 268, with the Dire Wolf reference on 229 | Alder Pace, Monk 1. AC 15 and HP 9. Uses the d4 Martial Arts die for body strikes. Pack Tactics, a second Martial Arts strike, and Trip each use the bonus action. |
| Worg | 269 | Gravel Jaw, Monk 2. AC 14 and HP 17. Unarmored Movement raises the walk to 50 ft. Flurry of Blows uses two conventional d4 bludgeoning strikes. |

The Tiger and Wolf profiles spell out their published cross-references for search. Each uses the base form in its NPC and portrait. The Wolf and Worg design notes mention a d8 natural strike that their printed traits omit. Both builds use Monk strikes with the printed rules for class bonus attacks on p. 7. The Wolf’s printed traits also omit a No Hands paragraph. Alder has a natural quadruped body and uses no handheld gear. The Winter Wolf uses the player block’s breath, snow camouflage, and languages.

## Portraits

The five originals are 1254 × 1254. I compared their paint and plain paper to Big Linda and checked their faces and outlines in 96-pixel circular previews. Rime’s ivory coat and heavy ruff, Alder’s narrow gray-brown silhouette, and Gravel’s dark shoulders and blocky jaw distinguish the three canines.
