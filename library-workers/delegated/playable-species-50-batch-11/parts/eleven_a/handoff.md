# Ape, Awakened Tree, Baboon, Badger and Bat

Five player profiles, linked NPCs, and plain parchment portraits are staged for batch 11. I checked the v2 trait blocks against the printed pages and used the 2014 rules for the builds. [Monstrous Races v2]() · [2014 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment)

| Profile | Printed pages | NPC and manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ape | 219, 232 | Flint Palm, Barbarian 1. AC 15, 15 HP. Fist +5 for 1d6 + 3 bludgeoning, or 1d6 + 5 during Rage. The worked Rock attack uses Dexterity and proficiency for +4 and 1d6 + 2 bludgeoning. |
| Awakened Tree | 220 | Split Willow, Monk 1. AC 14, 10 HP. Both attacks use +5. Slam deals 1d6 + 3 bludgeoning, and the conventional Martial Arts root kick deals 1d4 + 3 under the supplement's rule on bonus strikes. |
| Baboon | 221 | Spindle, Fighter 1. AC 13, 12 HP. Shortbow +7 with Archery, 1d6 + 3 piercing. Strength-based bite +2, 1d6 slashing. Pack Tactics and Second Wind each spend a bonus action. |
| Badger | 221 | Ashsnout, Barbarian 1. AC 14, 14 HP. Bite or claw +4 for 1d6 + 2, or 1d6 + 4 during Rage. Keen Smell can raise passive Perception from 13 to 18. |
| Bat | 222 | Eavespin, Rogue 2. AC 13, 17 HP. Bite +5 for 1d4 + 3 piercing. The printed finesse rule permits 1d6 Sneak Attack when the conditions are met. Echolocation disables blindsight while deafened. |

The Ape profile includes the printed Giant Ape cross-reference under the same player rules. Flint Palm depicts an ordinary Ape. The Awakened Tree is Small, the Baboon uses a 1d6 bite, the Badger is Small, and the Bat walks 30 ft. These details follow the printed player traits. The design commentary differs on all four.
