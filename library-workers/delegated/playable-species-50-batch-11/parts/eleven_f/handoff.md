# Monstrous Races v2, group F

Five playable profiles, five original NPCs and five 1254 × 1254 portraits are staged together. I checked the player traits, class rules, build arithmetic and portraits by hand.

Giant Shark points to the Hunter Shark player rules in the book and is named in the Hunter Shark profile for search. The Hunter Shark size field is blank. Its design notes explicitly reduce the player build to Medium, which is the size used here. The printed bite uses Strength. Blood Frenzy requires a hit made as part of the Attack action and spends the same bonus action Slate would use to start Rage.

The trait blocks give Giant Weasel 35 ft. walking speed and Hawk 60 ft. flight. Cinder's Monk movement adds 10 ft. His action bite uses the printed slashing die. His Martial Arts and Flurry bonus strikes use the Monk's ordinary bludgeoning die under the supplement's p. 7 clarification. Gale uses the printed talon die and the Tiny carrying rule on p. 8. The Jackal's printed kit includes Pack Tactics and omits the keen senses mentioned in its design discussion.

Each portrait uses plain parchment, with Big Linda as the paint and paper reference. I checked the facial or anatomical details at full composition and the reduced portraits at 96 pixels.
