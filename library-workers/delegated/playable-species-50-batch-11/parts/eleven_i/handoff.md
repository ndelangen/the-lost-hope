# Monstrous Races v2, five playable beasts

Five profiles, five class-built NPCs and five original parchment portraits. The printed player traits come from Tyler Kamstra’s Monstrous Races v2 and use the 2014 rules. I also consulted the supplement’s general rules on pp. 7–8. [Authored rules text]() · [Publisher listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races)

| Printed player traits | NPC and table details |
| --- | --- |
| Pony, p. 258 | Chaffstep, Barbarian 2. AC 14, HP 23, hooves +5 for 1d8 + 3 bludgeoning, or 1d8 + 5 during Rage. |
| Quipper, p. 259 | Coppergill, Rogue 2. AC 13, HP 17, bite +5 for 1d4 + 3 piercing and conditional Sneak Attack. Blood Frenzy and Cunning Action compete for the bonus action. |
| Reef Shark, p. 261 | Slatewake, Barbarian 1. AC 13 from Unarmored Defense, HP 13, bite +5 for 1d8 + 3 piercing, or 1d8 + 5 during Rage. Pack Tactics applies to one attack and costs a bonus action. |
| Rhinoceros, pp. 261–262 | Graysill, Monk 1. AC 14 from natural armor, HP 9, gore +5 for 1d8 + 3 piercing. Charge adds 1d6 and can enable a contested bonus-action shove. The Martial Arts bonus kick deals 1d4 + 3 bludgeoning. |
| Scorpion, p. 262 | Tallowpin, Rogue 1. AC 13, HP 9, sting +5 for 1d8 + 3 poison and conditional Sneak Attack. |

Draft Horse, Riding Horse and Warhorse point to Pony on pp. 229, 262 and 266. The names appear in the Pony profile, which has one NPC and one portrait. The other player profiles use their own printed traits. Both fish need water to breathe. The Tiny equipment and carrying rules apply to Coppergill and Tallowpin, and their printed natural damage dice apply as written. All five builds use the supplement’s No Hands restriction.

I checked ability increases, armor formulas, hit points, skill and save proficiency, attack rolls, damage, and bonus-action timing by hand. I used the contested 2014 shove for Charge. For Graysill’s Martial Arts bonus attack, I followed the supplement’s p. 7 distinction between an ordinary kick and a natural weapon. [2014 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 combat](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

The five 1254 × 1254 PNGs use Big Linda’s plain warm parchment. I reviewed the figures at working size and 96 pixels. I regenerated the Scorpion from a higher angle so that all eight walking legs read separately from the two pincers.
