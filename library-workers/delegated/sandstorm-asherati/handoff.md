# Asherati from Sandstorm

This batch adds the D&D 3.5 Asherati from the March 2005 first printing and two original encounters. Rashen sandswims 30 ft. in leather with a light load. Neral’s breastplate limits him to 5 ft. Both can use Body Lamp to navigate loose sand. The separate once-daily flash can dazzle any creature within 30 ft., including allies.
