# Asherati encounters

Both are Medium Humanoids, provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. They have +1 natural armor and Heat Endurance. They need one-quarter the usual drinking water. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

Sandswim works through sand, ash, dust, and softsand. Slipsand, packed dirt, and rock stop it. Heavier armor or a medium load drops Sandswim to 5 ft. A heavy load stops movement through sand. Asherati breathe normally under sand. Immersion in water immediately calls for Constitution checks against drowning. When completely wet, apply −1 to attack rolls, ability checks, and skill checks.

Body Lamp glows at will. It provides bright light to 60 ft. and shadowy illumination to 120 ft. In loose sand the Asherati can pick out solid objects within 60 ft. to navigate. Other creatures see the sand glow. The Asherati cannot Hide while glowing. Once per day the Asherati can flash as a free action. A failed Fortitude save dazzles a creature within 30 ft. for 1 minute. The area includes allies.

## Rashen Dustline, Rogue 1

Rashen follows the sand beside a jammed sandlock. He lights his skin to find the release frame, then extinguishes the glow to hide on the surface.

- HP 8, AC 16, initiative +3. Land and Sandswim 30 ft. in leather with a light load.
- Body Lamp flash at Fortitude DC 12. Move Silently +9. Hide +9 normally and +11 in sand while unlit.
- Short sword +1 for 1d6+1 piercing. Shortbow +3 for 1d6 piercing. Add 1d6 sneak attack against an eligible target without concealment.

## Neral Glassbank, Fighter 1

Neral guards the dry landing beside the cistern overflow. He can approach beneath the sand beside the landing, then take the dry path around the spill.

- HP 12, AC 17, initiative +5. Land 20 ft. and Sandswim 5 ft. in breastplate.
- Body Lamp flash at Fortitude DC 11. Warn allies before using it.
- Eagle’s claw +5 for 1d6+3 slashing and piercing, critical 18–20/×2. Asherati treat it as a martial weapon. Javelin +2 for 1d6+3 piercing.
