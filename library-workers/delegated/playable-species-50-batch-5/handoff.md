# Playable species batch 5

This batch stages 50 playable profiles, 50 linked NPC builds, and 50 original portraits. All 50 are new publication and edition records. Each addition identifies its exact source and edition. 7 named lineages sit under their canonical species. The manifest records one owner for each species group.

[Catalog patch](rules.patch) · [Source and ownership manifest](manifest.json) · [Portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) · [Approved roster](claim-list.md)

The patch is based on catalog commit `e1116fb`. It covers species entries, local rules, and NPCs tied to those profiles. The portrait preview names the destination keys. The manifest and individual handoffs locate the staged raster originals.

## Review

PC1 groups Faun under Satyr because the original book uses both names. The Faun profile and NPC still use the exact PC1 publication key. [PC1 sources](parts/five_wee_a/handoff.md)

The Samsaran profile records Everborn as the printed trait name. Hagborn uses the attributed open-content reprint. Its original trait pages remain unverified. [Heroic Races sources](parts/five_heroic/handoff.md)

Authors checked the rules, NPC mechanics, and portraits by hand. Separate spot reviews covered ownership, publication boundaries, representative rules, and encounter math. In-app search and portrait loading remain unverified. No automated tests ran. No changes were made to the primary checkout or R2.
