# Krugel Orc, Malpheggi Lizardman, Kogolor Dwarf and Gully Dwarf

Four playable profiles, four original NPCs and four 1254 × 1254 portraits. The 96 px comparison runs left to right from Big Linda to Krugel Orc, Malpheggi Lizardman, Kogolor Dwarf and Gully Dwarf. The portraits use warm parchment, subdued pigments and a faint contact shadow. I revised the Krugel’s tabarko and sun hat, the Kogolor’s coat and cane, and the Gully Dwarf’s silhouette and beard after checking the published descriptions.

## Sources and build checks

- Khar Amberrein uses the Hollow World Krugel Orc class at level 1, with 0 XP, 8 hp from a 7 on the d8 and +1 Constitution, and descending AC 7 in leather. His fighter saves are 12/13/14/15/16. At base THAC0 19 he needs a raw 14 against AC 4 with Strength +1 on his melee attack. A thrown spear uses Dexterity to hit and Strength for damage. The player profile’s printed pages 41–42 are facsimile pages 43–44. [Hollow World Player’s Guide, Krugel Orcs](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/nhmz/#p=43)
- Sseth Reedwake uses the Malpheggi Lizardman class at level 1, with 0 XP, 8 hp from a 7 on the d8 and +1 Constitution, natural descending AC 5 without a shield, and cleric saves 11/12/14/16/15. Each claw rolls separately with +1 to hit and +1 damage for Strength. The player profile’s printed pages 45–46 are facsimile pages 47–48. [Hollow World Player’s Guide, Malpheggi Lizard Men](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/nhmz/#p=47)
- Korin Switchback uses the standard Dwarf class at level 1, with 0 XP, 7 hp from a 6 on the d8 and +1 Constitution, and dwarf saves 8/9/10/13/12. Scale mail gives AC 6 and his shield improves it to 5. His armor, shield and cane weigh 300 + 100 + 50 = 450 cn, in the 90 ft. normal and 30 ft. encounter movement band. The player profile’s printed pages 39–40 are facsimile pages 41–42. [Hollow World Player’s Guide, Kogolor Dwarves](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/nhmz/#p=41)
- The BECMI builds use standard 1d6 side initiative each round and the level-1 character attack row. For the two new race-as-class options, the Player’s Guide gives save and XP schedules. I used the Rules Cyclopedia character attack row, which starts its listed classes at THAC0 19. The build records use 0 for the numeric initiative field, with the roll explained under traits. I chose standard weapon damage without optional Weapon Mastery and assigned morale 8 to each build. [Rules Cyclopedia, pp. 9–10, 23–24, 62, 67, 81–84, 87, 102–106]()
- Nib Patchfoot uses the Aghar traits and Rogue 1 with ECL 1. A first-level d6 plus Constitution gives 8 hp. AC 16 is 10 + 2 leather + 3 Dexterity + 1 Small. Initiative +7 is Dexterity +3 and Improved Initiative +4. His seven skills with four ranks each spend all 28 skill points. The small club attack adds size +1 and the sling also adds Dexterity +3. The profile’s printed pages 42–45 are pages 43–46.
