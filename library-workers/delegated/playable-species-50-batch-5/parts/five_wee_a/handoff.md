# PC1 woodland creature classes

Five profiles, five linked original NPCs and five 1254 × 1254 PNGs are staged in this folder. The portraits use Big Linda as a paint-and-paper reference. The comparison shows the approved reference beside each new portrait at 96 px. Each portrait has an isolated full figure, warm plain parchment, muted color and a faint contact shadow.

The evidence links to the complete PC1 facsimile with a text layer. Its page is two ahead of the printed DM’s booklet page. The unnumbered advancement foldouts have separate page numbers in the evidence. I checked the printed progression and movement tables against the scan images and used the Rules Cyclopedia for spell effects, attack rows, saves, ability adjustments and ordinary weapon damage.

All five HP totals are sample rolls with no Constitution modifier. Fen adds +1 to melee attack and damage from Strength. Linden and Sedge apply the Wisdom bonus to relevant saves vs. spells. Each build uses side initiative and the light-load speeds from PC1. PC1 leaves the faun’s musical range and lingering duration to adjudication. The sample uses audibility and treats the faun’s own saving throw as a save vs. spells.

The Redcap appears within Brownie and the clurachaun within Leprechaun. Each gets the ordinary form’s portrait and playable NPC for this batch. PC1 also calls fauns satyrs and older fauns sileni, so the Faun profile is grouped under Satyr while Fen’s concept links to the exact PC1 Faun printing.

Browser search and portrait route checks are part of the aggregate temporary-copy verification.
