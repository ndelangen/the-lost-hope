# Four playable profiles from Races of Ansalon

| Profile | Original NPC | Rules used | Printed folios |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kender | Nim Alderloop | True Kender Rogue 1. The profile also covers afflicted kender. | [144–146]() |
| Tarmak | Kered Vann | Fighter 1, using the racial fighter feat and four physical skill points. | [18–20]() |
| Kyrie | Rill Cliffcall | Kyrie racial tier 1, one racial Hit Die and gliding. The profile also records the full racial package. | [207–210]() |
| Phaethon | Sera Cinderstep | Core Monk 1, with Flaming Wings. | [210–211]() |

I used the 2007 first printing. The viewer number is one higher than the printed folio. All four NPC links use the exact edition and publication keys. I checked the skills, attacks, armor, and saves against the printed traits and the 3.5 SRD. [Core rules]()

Rill keeps the common Kyrie land speed, size, and darkvision. The first racial tier gives Rill a domain power. I used caster level 1 for its duration because spellcasting starts at tier 2. Sera uses the monk level as an encounter CR estimate. The +2 level adjustment raises Sera’s effective character level to 3.

The four square portraits are original. I reviewed each at full size and at 96 pixels. The comparison begins with the approved Big Linda reference, followed by Kender, Tarmak, Kyrie and Phaethon. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg)
