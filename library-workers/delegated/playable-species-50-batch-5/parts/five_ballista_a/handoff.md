# Top Ballista creature heroes

The four profiles use the 1989 creature-hero rules. The printed Background Booklet pages are three pages earlier than the viewer numbers. [Original TSR 9255 scan]()

| Profile | Printed pages | pages | Encounter NPC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Faenare | 6–10 | 9–13 | Teren Amberwake, mature windsinger |
| Skygnome | 10–12 | 13–15 | Nell Springline, level 2 flight fitter |
| Gremlin | 13–16 | 16–19 | Pip Tonguetack, level 3 stagehand |
| Nagpa | 20–22 | 23–25 | Avar Foldquill, level 1 manuscript mender |

Skygnome belongs under Gnome in the catalog. Nell’s NPC concept links directly to the Skygnome profile. All four use descending AC and 1d6 side initiative. The NPCs use the original monster attack brackets and their class-based save brackets.

Apply Teren’s permanent ward to two damage dice per separate lightning attack. PC2 does not specify the ward’s damage allowance or refresh. Gremlin ability V repeats “horizontal” for the shorter jumps. The paired labels from ability III support reading those shorter jumps as vertical. Pip’s large-target Leg Whip save applies both the tier bonus and the spell’s large-target bonus.

The [96 px portrait strip](portrait-comparison.png) shows Big Linda, Faenare, Skygnome, Gremlin and Nagpa from left to right. Each 1254 × 1254 PNG was visually reviewed at original size and at 96 px. All four have clear silhouettes, plain warm parchment and faint shadows. The Nagpa’s narrow build takes up less horizontal space by design.
