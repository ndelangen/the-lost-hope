The publisher preview's introduction names Hagborn and Samsaran and marks racial traits as open content. Hagborn's trait pages fall outside the preview. I used the attributed Open Gaming Network reprint for its three subtypes and could not confirm their printed folios. [Publisher preview]() · [Hagborn reprint](https://www.5esrd.com/database/race/hagborn)

The preview's printed p. 5 calls the playable race Everborn. It gives the full Companion and Dreaming traits, including 60-ft. darkvision and Common and Everborn languages, and p. 6 describes its appearance. The linked wizard uses those printed rules. The original title listing and introduction use Samsaran, which remains the approved catalog key. The reprint and licensed 2018 conversion use Everborn. [Printed traits]() · [Original listing](https://www.opengamingstore.com/collections/jon-brazer-enterprises/products/book-of-heroic-races-player-races-1-5e?lshst=collection) · [Licensed conversion](https://www.fantasygrounds.com/store/product.php?id=JBE04035E)

I checked both builds against the 2014 fifth-edition rules. Tessa uses one claw attack per Attack action with a shield in her other hand. Nerin has two 1st-level slots, two additional unprepared rituals from the wizard spellbook, and History advantage from the Dreaming lineage. The checks were manual.
