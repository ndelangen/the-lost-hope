# Pegataur, Sphinx, Tabi and Pterran

Four standalone player species each have one linked encounter NPC and one original square PNG. The 96 px comparison runs left to right from Big Linda to Pegataur, Sphinx, Tabi and Pterran.

## Rules checked

- Beryl Tailwind uses 5d8 at 10,000 XP, 23 HP, base THAC0 15 and the fighter saves from *Top Ballista* Table 8. I used the Pegataur chapter’s natural AC 5 over the shared table’s AC 6. The original *Creature Catalogue* supplies ground movement and hoof damage. The encounter uses the core spear damage and one level-one elf spell.
- Istran uses the 4d8, −2,400,000 XP row, 19 HP, AC 4, THAC0 16 and Fighter 8 saves. Table 9 gives that row 1d8 rounds of fear, while the prose groups it with 1d4 rounds. The build follows the table and applies its −1 modifier to the separate deafness save as well. All three zones remain cumulative.
- Neri Inkthumb uses the 32,000 XP level-one row, 5d8, 22 HP, AC 7, THAC0 15, Magic-User 5 saves and 45%/50%/50% thief skills. The *Top Ballista* poison save takes precedence over the earlier monster entry’s paralysis save. The normal-monster skill cell printed as 4 is irrelevant to the level-one profile.
- Othakk uses the 1995 revised Dark Sun racial tables and an unspecialized third-level fighter. AC 8, 19 HP, THAC0 18 and the Warrior 3 save row match the sources. The setting explicitly exempts spears from substitute-material penalties. The named NPC has no wild talent. Player characters use the boxed set’s wild-talent requirement.

The portrait inspection included the full composition and 96 px copies. Each figure has one warm parchment field and a faint contact shadow. The Pegataur’s four legs and feathered wings, Sphinx’s human head and lion body, Tabi’s leather wings and grasping paws, and Pterran’s rear fin and nonflying shoulder stubs remain visible.
