# Dragonlance player profiles

Thanoi, Ursoi and Draconian each have one linked original NPC and one square parchment portrait. All three use D&D 3.5. The Draconian profile holds the Baaz and Kapak options together. Bress Tarn uses the Baaz rules and carries the longsword and wooden shield shown in his portrait.

| Profile | Printed rules | NPC | Encounter benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thanoi | *Races of Ansalon*, pp. 214–217 | Orruk Hollowtusk, Thanoi racial class 5 | 3 racial Hit Dice, ECL 5, printed CR 2 |
| Ursoi | *Races of Ansalon*, pp. 217–221 | Saltlistener, Ursoi racial class 7 | 5 racial Hit Dice, ECL 7, printed CR 4 |
| Draconian | *Dragonlance Campaign Setting*, pp. 33–37 and 217 | Bress Tarn, Baaz with 2 dragon Hit Dice and Fighter 1 | ECL 4, estimated CR 3 |

Both viewers place the printed folio one page later. Orruk uses the tusk routine in the Thanoi entry and counts the printed +8 Swim bonus once. Saltlistener uses scent 20 ft. from the Ursoi racial table because the scent paragraph gives conflicting level guidance. Bress's CR uses the printed Baaz CR 2 and treats Fighter 1 as an associated class level. [Races of Ansalon]() · [Dragonlance Campaign Setting]() · [Revised 3.5 SRD](https://www.d20srd.org/)

I checked the skill pools, Hit Dice, AC, attack routines, and saves by hand. The portrait strip starts with Big Linda, then shows Thanoi, Ursoi and the Baaz Draconian at 96 pixels. The draft Baaz had a ground-length tail. The revised portrait shows the short tail described in the book. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg)
