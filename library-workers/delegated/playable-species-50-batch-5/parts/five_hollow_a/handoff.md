# Hollow World player profiles

Staged four original NPCs and four square parchment portraits. The figures are Kisita, a barefoot jackal-headed Hutaakan courier, Gat-Kor, a Brute-Man scout in a hide vest, Vanorva, a Kubitt with blackened mail and a painted shield, and Elthir, a pale Schattenalfen scout with a hood and gloves.

| Profile | Player’s Guide folios | pages | Worked build |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hutaakan | 36–37 | 38–39 | Hutaakan 1, AC 8, HP 4, staff 1d6 |
| Brute-Man | 31–32 | 33–34 | Brute-Man 1, AC 7, HP 8, stone spear 1d6+1 |
| Kubitt | 43–44 | 45–46 | Fighter 1, AC 3, HP 6, miniature short sword 1d3+1 |
| Schattenalfen | 56–57 and 6–8 | 58–59 and 8–10 | Warrior-Elf 1, AC 7, HP 5, short sword 1d6+1 |

Manually checked each build’s ability adjustments, HP, armor, movement, save order, weapon rolls, initiative and General Skill choices against the original player booklet and supporting Rules Cyclopedia. The Hutaakan and Brute-Man use the core first-level attack matrix for these examples since their player entries do not give individual matrices. The Kubitt’s movement uses full-size coin weights for chain, shield and short sword because the player option gives no miniature weights. Its Dexterity modifier for initiative applies only if the table uses that optional rule.

Inspected the complete portraits and the 96-pixel comparison. The thumbnail strip shows Big Linda, Hutaakan, Brute-Man, Kubitt and Schattenalfen from left to right. Each has a plain warm parchment field, no scenery, muted pigments and a faint contact shadow. The Kubitt received one edit to blacken the original mail, boots and shield and add personal heraldry. The Schattenalfen portrait shows Elthir in shade, with the hood loose so the face reads at thumbnail size. Elthir’s encounter profile calls for covering all exposed skin before entering direct sun.
