# Dragonkin, Shadow Fey, Dust Goblin and Sahuagin

| Profile | Linked build | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dragonkin | Karrash Emberseal, Flame Dragonkin Fighter 1 | *Midgard Heroes* (2015), pp. 5–6 |
| Shadow Fey | Vesha Gloamchart, Shadow Fey Rogue 2 | *Midgard Heroes* (2015), pp. 15–16 |
| Dust Goblin | Kethrek Flintstep, Dust Goblin Rogue 2 | *Unlikely Heroes* (2016), pp. 8–10 |
| Sahuagin | Vekatal Saltmark, Sahuagin Fighter 2 | *Unlikely Heroes* (2016), pp. 17–19 |

I checked the original player traits against the 2014 class, combat, equipment, and spell rules, then checked each build’s ability scores, AC, HP, saving throws, skills, attack modifiers, damage, and limited uses. [Midgard Heroes publisher preview](), [Midgard Heroes booklet](https://anyflip.com/usqxr/igmp/basic/), [Unlikely Heroes booklet](https://anyflip.com/lphgc/hojw/basic), [Unlikely Heroes page transcription](https://www.scribd.com/document/424641862/Unlikely-Heroes), [2014 Basic Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014).

- Dragonkin includes all five printed subraces. Karrash uses Flame.
- Shadow Fey is a separate catalog entry. The 2015 Traveler in Darkness covers both fey roads and shadow roads.
- Dust Goblin is grouped under Goblin. Kethrek links to the 2016 Dust Goblin entry. Twisted triggers on an attack from hiding, including a miss.
- Vekatal uses the player Sahuagin’s natural armor and a shield. His bite and claws use the printed damage die and the standard melee Strength modifier. Both fighters use Dueling on their one-handed spears, including when thrown. [2014 Sage Advice Compendium](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sac/sage-advice-compendium).
