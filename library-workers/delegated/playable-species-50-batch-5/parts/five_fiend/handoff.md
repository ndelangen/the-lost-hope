# Fiend Folio profiles and portraits

Six standalone species profiles, six original NPCs and six square portraits are staged here. The NPC concepts use the exact claim names and point to their matching builds. The Selkie profile and NPC both cover land and seal forms.

| Profile | Printed pages | NPC | Racial HD and effective character level |

The source is the April 2003 first printing from Wizards of the Coast. The cited page indexes match the printed page numbers. The builds use the book’s racial adjustments and the original 3.0 class, spell and equipment rules. [Fiend Folio first printing](), [Savage Species, character construction](), [3.0 System Reference Document](https://a.osmarks.net/content/dandwiki_en_all_maxi_2021-03/A/3e_System_Reference_Document)

- The Dark Stalker and Yurian entries give contradictory level adjustments and effective character levels. The builds keep the printed racial Hit Dice and follow each dedicated player character example. The Dark Stalker’s Shadow Cloak uses a standard action and lasts ten rounds as a table convention. The book supplies neither detail.
- The Kaorti stat block and player example agree on two racial Hit Dice. The resin suit sidebar refers to a one-Hit-Die Kaorti. The build uses two Hit Dice and the 3.0 spell rules for the appearance disguise, color spray, ray of enfeeblement, reduce and spider climb.
- The Ophidian player paragraph gives no numerical adjustment. Sesis uses the printed racial Hit Dice and fighter levels for combat rolls.
- The Selkie speed line lists land and swim movement together. The build uses the 30-ft. land speed in humanoid form and the 90-ft. swim speed in seal form. The portrait shows Mara in humanoid form.
- Keth uses Wilderness Lore as the 3.0 skill corresponding to the April book’s Survival entry. Keth and Brakk use the original Power Attack, which trades attack and damage bonuses at one for one.

The full portraits and 96-pixel comparison were visually reviewed. The comparison begins with Big Linda, followed by Dark Stalker, Kaorti, Khaasta, Ophidian, Selkie and Yurian. The images are original 1254 × 1254 PNGs. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg), [generation prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

The rule check was manual. I checked the six source identities, racial and class Hit Dice, ability adjustments, armor, attack bonuses, damage, saves and listed ability effects against the print and the 3.0 rules.
