# The Sea People

Five standalone Basic D&D (BECMI) playable profiles, five original encounter NPCs and five square parchment portraits. Encounter movement is in parentheses. Each playable profile links to its NPC by exact species, publication and edition.

| Profile | NPC | XP and HD | AC | HP | THAC0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kna | Brasscrest Driftledger | 0 XP, 7 HD | 4 | 40 | 13 |
| Kopru | Terazz Cindercoil | 0 XP, 8 HD +4 hp before Constitution | 2 | 47 | 11 |
| Merrow | Mareva Greenwake | 1,000 XP, 2 HD | 6 | 11 | 18 |
| Sea Giant | Tavar Deepstep | 0 XP, 9 HD | 0 | 54 | 11 |
| Shark-Kin | Ssekk Gutterfin | 0 XP, 2 HD | 5 | 12 | 18 |

I checked the printed PC3 profiles, advancement tables, character creation and equipment rules against the scan. The player booklet begins at viewer page 32. I used the Rules Cyclopedia for the monster attack and fighter save tables, missile modifiers, weapon timing and Resist Fire. The PC3 p.18 example adds Constitution once to the initial pool and again each time the character gains a die. The dice totals before fixed and Constitution modifiers were 34, 37, 9, 42 and 10 hp in the order above.

The Kopru uses one combined three-tail damage roll and a separate bite. A successful save against a fresh attempt grants lasting immunity. A monthly save breaks control and leaves a target eligible for another attempt. The Sea Giant uses the species table on p.26 for XP, since the p.18 worked example uses different thresholds. Ssekk uses the developed-leg outcast form. The 10-, 40-, 50- and 80-foot encounter moves follow the profiles' parenthetical values.

All five 1254 × 1254 PNGs were visually reviewed at original size and against Big Linda at 96 pixels in [portrait-review.jpg](portrait-review.jpg). The Merrow has two finned tails as in PC3. I revised the Shark-Kin's head and tail tip for a clearer aquatic silhouette. The five PNG hashes and exact file paths are in portrait-files.json. The prompt record includes both Shark-Kin passes.
