# PC1 woodland creature heroes

Five playable profiles, five original NPCs and five square PNG portraits. The basis is the 1989 rules booklet, including its fold-out advancement and movement tables. Pages 4–6 cover creation and combat. Pages 24–25 cover the common fairy rules. The 1991 *Rules Cyclopedia* supplies the standard saving throws, attacks, equipment, spells and thief skills. The source index records printed folios separately from page numbers. [PC1 rules booklet scan]() · [Rules Cyclopedia scan]()

| Profile | Worked NPC | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pooka | Brindlewake, a black hare who trusts the ferryman | PC1 pp. 34–36 and Table 14. At 0 XP, 9 HP from 2d8, AC 6, base THAC0 18, thief saves 13/14/13/16/15. The hare has no assigned combat attack. The rules give normal animal forms broadly and put active shapechanging at 10th level. |
| Sidhe | Ivara Reedglass, a warrior guarding a sluice | PC1 pp. 37–38, 41 and 49, Tables 15–16. At 2,500 XP, 7 HP from the two d4 rolls, counting as one Hit Die. The bronze chain shirt uses standard chain mail statistics, producing AC 4 with Dexterity. Base THAC0 19, sword THAC0 18, fighter saves 12/13/14/15/16. The profile covers both paths. |
| Sprite | Tansy Duskwick, an orchard lookout | PC1 pp. 31–33 and 41, Tables 12–13 and 18a. At 2,000 XP, 5 HP from 2d4, AC 7, base THAC0 18, elf saves 12/13/13/15/15. The single fairy charm is Magic Missile. Four more sprites are required for the cooperative curse. |
| Wood Imp | Nettle Bracken, an archer protecting spider eggs | PC1 pp. 18–22 and 49, Tables 7 and 18. At 800 XP, 5 HP from 2d4, AC 7, base THAC0 18, fighter saves 12/13/14/15/16. For the miniature bow, damage drops to 1d4 and the range stays standard. The light kit meets the 25 coin-weight maximum for its listed movement. |
| Woodrake | Clove Barrow, a locksmith with three forms | PC1 pp. 39–40 and Tables 17–18a. At 0 XP, 18 HP from 4d8, drake AC 1, unarmored humanoid AC 8, base THAC0 16, magic-user saves 11/12/11/14/12. Use 5th-level thief skills and the optional two rounds of spell immunity. Changing shape takes one round as a table ruling. |

Saving throw values follow this order. Death or poison, wands, paralysis or stone, breath, rod or staff or spell. The initiative field uses an unmodified 1d6 for each side. These are tabletop builds using the monster attack matrix by actual Hit Dice.

The Woodrake’s juvenile XP thresholds conflict between Table 17 and adjacent prose. The build uses the 0 XP tier, where they agree. The Pooka’s time stop description gives a use formula without a printed unlock level. Both readings are recorded with the individual sources.

Each portrait was compared with Big Linda at 96 pixels and checked at its original size. The Sprite has pale green gossamer wings. The Woodrake uses the small-dragon silhouette described by PC1 and the green scales specified by the *Rules Cyclopedia*. The comparison sheet places Big Linda at left.
