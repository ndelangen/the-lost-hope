# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual bear source check

TSR’s AD&D 2nd Edition *Monstrous Manual*, product 2140, was first published in 1993. The inspected print scan’s copyright page identifies it as the January 1994 third printing. The bear table, all four combat descriptions and the family illustration share printed p. 17, viewer page 20. ## Shared rules

All four are daytime omnivores in family groups, with Semi- (2–4) intelligence, neutral alignment, no treasure and average morale (8–10). They have three attacks and no listed special defenses or magic resistance. A successful paw hit on an attack roll of 18 or better adds the form’s hug damage. The shared threshold applies to a single paw hit. The prose gives no separate grapple or ongoing hold for this attack.

The entry describes dense fur, powerful claws, strong hearing and smell, and relatively poor eyesight. Most bears avoid humans unless provoked, with more aggressive behavior described for individual forms. They live in small family groups with protective mothers.

## Black Bear

- Temperate land, common, 1–3 appearing, AC 7, movement 12, HD 3+3, THAC0 17, damage 1–3/1–3/1–6, Medium (6 ft. or more tall), 175 XP. A qualifying paw hit adds 2–8 (2d4) hug damage. The Black Bear has no post-zero fighting rule in the entry.
- The prose gives fur ranging from black to light brown, and describes the form as smaller than the Brown Bear and the most widespread. It typically attacks when pressed.

## Brown Bear

- Temperate land, uncommon, 1–6 appearing, AC 6, movement 12, HD 5+5, THAC0 15, damage 1–6/1–6/1–8, Large (9 ft. or more tall), 420 XP. A qualifying paw hit adds 2–12 (2d6) hug damage.
- It continues fighting for 1–4 melee rounds in the 0 through −8 hp band and dies immediately at −9 hp or lower. The grizzly is its best-known variety in the text. It is aggressive and leans more toward meat than a Black Bear, with deer and elk named as prey. The text supplies no exact shade for this form.

## Cave Bear

- Any land, uncommon, 1–2 appearing, AC 6, movement 12, HD 6+6, THAC0 13, damage 1–8/1–8/1–12, Huge (12 ft. or more tall), 650 XP. A qualifying paw hit adds 2–16 (2d8) hug damage.
- It continues fighting for 1–4 melee rounds in the 0 through −8 hp band and dies immediately at −9 hp or lower. It may attack well-armed groups without provocation. The general ecology text describes a preference for meat, while the table gives omnivore. The entry specifies no coat color and lists any land as habitat.

## Polar Bear

- Any cold, rare, 1–6 appearing, AC 6, movement 12 and Sw 9, HD 8+8, THAC0 11, damage 1–10/1–10/2–12, Huge (14 ft. or more tall), 1,400 XP. A qualifying paw hit adds 3–18 (3d6) hug damage.
- It continues fighting for 2–5 rounds in the 0 through −12 hp band and dies immediately below −12 hp. The text calls it a powerful swimmer that feeds mostly on marine animals. The table gives omnivore. The entry has no Polar Bear coat description.

## Illustration and catalog

The single unlabeled image shows a russet-brown bear on all fours, facing left, with a heavy shoulder hump, small rounded ears, visible teeth and claws. It supports a grizzly-like Brown Bear interpretation, though the book does not label it by form. The credits list Dave Simons for ordinary and near-ordinary animals and Les Dorscheid for much of the coloring. Those general credits suggest possible contributors. No page signature or figure-specific credit confirms either assignment.
