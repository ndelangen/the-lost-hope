# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual bears

Black, Brown, Cave, and Polar Bears from the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. No exact matches for this publication and edition appeared in the primary catalog or staged source evidence. Existing names occur in other editions, including the separately staged Black Flag bear profiles. [Printed entry, p. 17](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/ec893f6a034f2d89f8f5f17847760e6d.webp) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Printed habitat | HD | Hug damage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Black Bear | Temperate land | 3+3 | 2d4 |
| Brown Bear | Temperate land | 5+5 | 2d6 |
| Cave Bear | Any land | 6+6 | 2d8 |
| Polar Bear | Any cold terrain | 8+8 | 3d6 |

Each hug requires a successful paw hit with an attack roll of 18 or better. Brown and Cave Bears can fight for 1–4 melee rounds at 0 through −8 HP. Polar Bears can fight for 2–5 rounds at 0 through −12 HP. The Black Bear has no such rule in the entry. The profiles preserve the individual movement, attacks, THAC0 and XP from each column. [Rules patch](rules.patch) · [Exact printing evidence](source-evidence.json)

The four new plain parchment portraits distinguish the smaller dark Black Bear, the grizzled Brown Bear, the heavy-headed Cave Bear, and the long-muzzled Polar Bear. The printed illustration is an unlabeled brown-colored bear. Its species and artist remain unconfirmed. The Cave and Polar Bear portrait details include illustration choices where the printed entry gives no color.
