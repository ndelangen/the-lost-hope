# Goblinoid and lizardfolk player variants

Five D&D 3.5 player profiles, five linked original NPCs and five square parchment portraits. Catalog grouping uses the parent in the table. Each NPC's species link targets the named variant and its exact printing.

| Player profile | Catalog parent | Original NPC | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flind | Gnoll | Kavra Flintjaw, Fighter 1 with 2 racial HD | *Monster Manual III*, p. 62 |
| Forestkith Goblin | Goblin | Vek Branchfall, Fighter 1 | *Monster Manual III*, pp. 64–65 |
| Poison Dusk Lizardfolk | Lizardfolk | Tress Siltstep, Ranger 1 | *Monster Manual III*, pp. 96–97 |
| Viletooth Lizardfolk | Lizardfolk | Reth Blackreed, Fighter 1 with 2 racial HD | *Dragon Magic*, p. 9 |
| Sunscorch Hobgoblin | Hobgoblin | Rava Cinderwatch, Fighter 1 | *Dragon Magic*, pp. 9–10 |

The Forestkith NPC uses the enumerated player traits. I left the sample creature's climb speed and bite out of the build. For Tree Shape, I treated the form as Small with gear melded and used the spell's 12-hour limit alongside the species' early end triggers. Poison Dusk's ranger carries ordinary arrows, and the build treats a narrow belt and carrying strap as leaving the camouflage scales exposed. Viletooth and Sunscorch inherit the core 3.5 player traits that their *Dragon Magic* sections leave in place.
