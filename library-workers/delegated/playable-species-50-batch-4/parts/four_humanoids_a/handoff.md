# PHBR10 playable humanoids

Five AD&D 2e playable profiles, five linked NPCs, and five original square portraits. I checked the playable entries in TSR's 1993 *The Complete Book of Humanoids* (PHBR10, TSR 2135). The scan's page is one higher than the printed number. The NPCs use the core 2e class, weapon, save, and initiative rules. [PHBR10]() · [Player's Handbook]()

| Profile | PHBR10 printed pages | Linked NPC | AC | HP |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Aarakocra | 16–17 | Rusk Pennon, Fighter 1 | 5 | 8 |
| Alaghi | 18–19 | Venn Alderhand, Fighter 1 | 4 | 18 |
| Beastman | 19–20 | Tavin Mossstripe, Fighter 1 | 5 | 7 |
| Advanced Bullywug | 22–24 | Krev Siltbank, Fighter 1 | 4 | 9 |
| Fremlin | 25–27 | Siv Roofnook, Mage 1 | 3 | 3 |

The source records list the companion TSR creature entries used for movement rates where the player book gives none. Chapter 2 sets the class-specific caps in the profiles. PHBR10's appendix differs on a few limits and multiclass options. The linked characters use classes and levels the chapter and appendix both permit.

The aarakocra uses the core javelin and PHBR10's two-javelin dive. The alaghi example uses one fist attack at the first-level fighter rate because the player text gives fist damage without a separate attack count. For the advanced bullywug, the *Monstrous Manual* gives rates in the main Bullywug entry and gives no different rates in the Advanced subsection. Krev uses the unarmored rates and slings the shield while swimming.

I checked the NPC numbers by hand. I reviewed the five 1254×1254 originals at full size and at 96 pixels against Big Linda. The frog's spear point sits close to the top edge and remains inside the frame. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg)
