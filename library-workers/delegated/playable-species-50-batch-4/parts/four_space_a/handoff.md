# Four AD&D Spacefarer's Handbook profiles

| Playable profile | Linked NPC | Original player entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dracon | Perrin Broadwake, Fighter 1 and gangway keeper | pp. 16–17 |
| Giff | Leda Caskmark, Fighter 1 and quartermaster | pp. 17–18 |
| Grommam | Serren Knotwright, Fighter 1 and topside rigger | pp. 18–19 |
| Hurwaeti | Venn Reedstep, Fighter 1 and salvage guard | pp. 20–21 |

All four use TSR's 1992 printing. I checked racial ability requirements, adjustments and class limits on pp. 15–16, and the thief skill table on p. 27. The facility and crew roles are original hooks. [Spacefarer's Handbook original scan]()

Core fighter Hit Dice, THAC0, saves, equipment, and weapon damage by target size come from the AD&D Player's Handbook. I checked each build by hand against the ability tables. The Spelljammer companion entries supply Dracon's natural AC and the other three ground movement rates, plus the Grommam's tree and Hurwaeti's swim rates. [Player's Handbook facsimile]() · [Original Spelljammer creature entries](https://www.scribd.com/document/1054555911/TSR2109-Spelljammer-MC7-Monstrous-Compendium)

## Table rulings

- Dracon uses the companion's natural AC 5 with the normal shield adjustment and the player's class Hit Dice. Its shoulder check adds 4 to the roll-under success target.
- Giff uses the printed head-butt's 2d6 as fixed damage. Strength applies to the melee attack roll.
- Grommam uses the species passage's explicit two-weapon penalty waiver. The NPC carries a smaller off-hand dagger in keeping with the core off-hand size rule.
- Hurwaeti's racial fog gives the area and daily limit. The build uses the PHB spell's range, initiative modifier and a seven-round duration based on the first level able to cast the spell.
