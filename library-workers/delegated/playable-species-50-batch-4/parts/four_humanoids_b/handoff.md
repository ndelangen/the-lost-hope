The four saurial lineages share one playable profile. The NPC and portrait depict a finhead. Each NPC links to its exact publication and edition.

For the AD&D builds, the [Monstrous Manual]() and [Monstrous Compendium Annual Volume Three](https://anyflip.com/cmjgl/qcnv/basic/51-100) supply ground speeds omitted by the player profiles. Harn's table uses Fighter 3 for the duration of his unlocked detect magic. Idren's huge bow uses the PHB sheaf-arrow range bands plus the printed 50% increase. Letha takes a full round to change forms, a table convention because the entry leaves timing open.

Beresht has effective character level 20 and encounter CR 11. His sand blaster uses DC 23, based on the same racial Hit Dice formula that fits both printed examples. The build uses the MMIII glossary's standard action for Statue and records the timing discrepancy with the generic SRD rule.
