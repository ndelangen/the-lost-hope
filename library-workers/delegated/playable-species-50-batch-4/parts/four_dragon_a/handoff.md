The viewer page is one higher than each printed Dragon Magic page. Its subtype rules are on printed p. 4. The full drow uses the Monster Manual base. The half-drow uses the Races of Faerûn base, including the language options of a northern expatriate. The existing Drow Half-Elf group includes the Sword Coast Adventurer’s Guide printing and serves as the catalog group only. [Dragon Magic pp. 6–8]() · [Races of Faerûn pp. 62–63]() · [3.5 SRD]()

I used maximum first-level Hit Points and accounted for class skill ranks, bonus languages, class saves, armor and weapon modifiers by hand. Ered has one class Hit Die and level adjustment +2, so his ECL is 3 and his caster level for racial powers is 1. Nessa keeps the base Gather Information bonus and her Diplomacy bonus comes from Negotiator. Mara’s Feather Fall uses the spell's free-action timing. The Forestlord pact applies to both builds, and both keep magical sleep immunity.

Built-in ImageGen generated the five 1254 × 1254 PNGs. I reviewed the full images and the 96-pixel comparison against Big Linda. The prompt set records the approved parchment reference.
