# Five Dragon Magic lineages

Each lineage has a 3.5 playable profile, an original NPC and a version-specific portrait. The profile points to the existing parent species. The NPC's species name points to the exact lineage from the September 2006 first printing.

| Lineage | Parent species | Linked NPC | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fireblood Dwarf | Dwarf | Bera Cinderbrace, Fighter 1 | [Printed p. 7]() |
| Stonehunter Gnome | Gnome | Idrin Copperstep, Ranger 1 | [Printed pp. 8–9]() |
| Glimmerskin Halfling | Halfling | Janna Goldseed, Rogue 1 | [Printed p. 9]() |
| Frostblood Orc | Orc | Hurg Rimeshield, Fighter 1 | [Printed p. 10]() |
| Frostblood Half-Orc | Half-Orc | Ilyra Coldbriar, Ranger 3 | [Printed p. 10]() |

The viewer is one page ahead of the printed folio. All five retain the level adjustment of their 3.5 parent species, +0. The player orc rules supply Hurg's light sensitivity. Ilyra uses the Player's Handbook half-orc traits and takes Precise Shot when Ranger 3 would grant her Endurance a second time. Bera uses Dragon Dodge only against creatures of the dragon type. Janna applies Touch of Luck before a saving throw is rolled.
