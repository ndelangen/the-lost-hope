# Six playable creature profiles

Each profile has one original D&D 3.5 NPC and one square portrait. The portrait strip begins with Big Linda, then follows the rows below.

| Playable profile | Linked NPC | Character build | Player rules |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Armand | Bram Shelldrift | 5 racial HD, Fighter 1, LA +3, ECL 9 | [Monster Manual III, pp. 10–11]() |
| Harssaf | Vek Emberglass | 6 racial HD, Fighter 1, LA +4, ECL 11 | [Monster Manual III, pp. 82–83]() |
| Ibixian (Goatfolk) | Tavra Flintbrow | 3 racial HD, Barbarian 1, LA +1, ECL 5 | [Monster Manual III, p. 63]() |
| Quaraphon | Koth Reedbank | 9 racial HD, Fighter 1, LA +5, ECL 15 | [Monster Manual III, pp. 128–129]() |
| Witchknife | Kesh Redreed | 9 racial HD, Rogue 1, LA +3, ECL 13 | [Monster Manual III, pp. 194–195]() |
| Jaebrin | Fenn Copperlaugh | Sorcerer 1, LA +1, ECL 2 | [Monster Manual V, pp. 92–93](https://anyflip.com/myubd/sdgs/basic/51-100) |

The July 2006 errata clarifies Witchknife proficiency and Quaraphon ability adjustments, skill terminology, and hoof attacks. The Harssaf sample pulse has DC 16 after the errata. Vek's seven HD and Constitution score give DC 18. [Monster Manual III errata]()

Vek's aura uses the first entry or start of turn as the once-per-round damage point. His Sand Form uses *gaseous form*'s 10-foot movement rate along the ground. Fenn uses the Jaebrin player entry's class-based Hit Dice and adds half his class level to the Constitution-based Will Sapper DC. [D&D 3.5 SRD]() · [Monster Manual V, p. 93]()

I checked the source traits, NPC arithmetic, and spell resources by hand. I inspected each portrait at full size and 96 px. The Quaraphon shows four eyes, two stacked mouths, and four hooves. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg)
