The Skullcrusher, Naztharune and Crystalline versions sit under Ogre, Rakshasa and Troll. The NPC links retain the variant's exact source name. Ogre Mage and Irda have their own entries.

I applied the 2006 errata to Naztharune sneak attack and Crystalline Troll natural weapons. Brann uses the player-profile rules and his own scores. His CR follows the racial-only printed benchmark, as do Sahir's and Hrak's. Treat Serein's class-based CR as a baseline since Change Shape and racial magic can change the encounter. Serein's favorite disguise keeps her encounter math simple. I use the standard supernatural-action timing for the Irda's Change Shape.

The Ogre Mage table header reads Ogre Mace, while the contents and entry heading read Ogre Mage. Novak uses the tier 4 rows and the original 3.0 spell effects. He has three racial Hit Dice at that tier. The 3.0 Monster Manual's free flight toggle applies once Table A–39 grants his flight.

The profile traits, NPC builds, source notes and portrait inventory are separate JSON files. The PNGs are in portraits/. No automated tests ran.
