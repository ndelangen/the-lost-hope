# Fiend Folio playable profiles

Six standalone profiles use the April 2003 printing. Each has a linked original NPC and one square ImageGen portrait. The spell and weapon mechanics use the original 3.0 rules. [Printed source]()

| Profile | Printed rules | Original NPC | Effective character level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feytouched | p. 71 | Reva Thornstep, rogue 1 | 2 |
| Maeluth | pp. 136–137 | Brena Coalward, fighter 1 | 2 |
| Mechanatrix | p. 137, shared traits p. 136 | Darsa Wheelmark, fighter 1 | 2 |
| Shyft | p. 138, shared traits p. 136 | Pell Greywick, rogue 1 | 4 |
| Wispling | p. 139, shared traits p. 136 | Fera Copperfin, rogue 1 | 2 |
| Spriggan | pp. 162–163 | Orro Rootcuff, 5 racial Hit Dice and rogue 1 | 8 |

The five one-Hit-Die examples exchange the sample monster’s Hit Die for their first class Hit Die. Shyft keeps its printed +3 level adjustment. Reva uses the Feytouched entry’s printed charm save DC, while Fera’s Change Self interaction save uses DC 11 because the Wispling entry leaves it open. Common and Sylvan for Reva and Common for Darsa are example language choices. [One-Hit-Die guidance]()

Orro’s two forms share one profile and portrait. His attack, AC and saves use his own scores, the printed size changes and the chain shirt’s Dexterity cap. Both forms are runnable from the stat block. The enlarged form suppresses spell-like abilities and all sneak attack for this example. Portraits show the NPC’s natural form.

The outlines and page references were checked manually. The six 1254 × 1254 PNGs were inspected alongside Big Linda, including at 96 pixels. The strip shows Big Linda first, then the six portraits in the table order. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg)
