# Rastipede, Scro, Xixchil, and Dracotaur

| Playable profile | Original NPC | Printed player rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rastipede | Chirr Ambercoil, Mage 4 and freight-lock pilot | [Complete Spacefarer's Handbook, pp. 22–23]() |
| Scro | Garrun Flintjaw, Fighter 3 and dock marshal | [Complete Spacefarer's Handbook, pp. 23–24]() |
| Xixchil | Vesh Claspneedle, Mage 2 and hull surgeon | [Complete Spacefarer's Handbook, pp. 24–26]() |
| Dracotaur | Drezhal Stonewake, Fighter 1 and marsh outrider | [Monster Manual III, pp. 42–43]() |

The Spacefarer's scan numbers the front cover as page 1, so viewer pages run one ahead of the printed folios. The three builds use the AD&D 2e core class tables. The Rastipede's helm operates as if the character were a 12th-level mage, while its spell list stays at fourth level. The Scro and Xixchil use standard unencumbered movement 12 because their player entries supply no replacement rate. [AD&D 2e Player's Handbook]()

The Xixchil treats the anesthetic save as unmodified. The separate anesthetic paragraph supplies that save with no penalty, unlike the poison paragraph. The encounter uses the same delivery options and applies bite poison after physical bite damage. The optional Body Manipulation proficiency is for downtime.

The Dracotaur uses the July 2006 erratum's +6 Constitution, 3 racial Hit Dice and a +5 level adjustment. Fighter 1 brings it to four actual HD and ECL 9. The tail uses the damage multiplier shown in the printed examples. [Player's Handbook v.3.5]()

I checked each build's arithmetic against the printed class rules and reviewed the four original images at full size and 96 px. The first Rastipede drafts showed a surplus walking leg. The staged version has four walking legs on each side and two separate grasping arms. The Xixchil has two blade arms, two smaller manipulators and two walking legs.
