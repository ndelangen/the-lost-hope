# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual spider source check

TSR’s *Monstrous Manual* is an AD&D 2nd Edition rulebook, product 2140, ISBN 1-56076-619-0, first published in 1993. The full table and the base Large, Huge and Giant Spider descriptions appear on printed p. 326. The rendered copy used here is the third printing, January 1994, as stated on its credits page. Printed pp. 326 and 327 are viewer pages 329 and 330. The public reflowed electronic edition spreads these passages over pages 897–901. Its relevant values and rules agree with the checked print scan. The Internet Archive catalog identifies a 1993 copy, but that item’s scan is access-restricted and its p. 326 was not directly checked. ## Large Spider

- Any nonarctic land, common, organization swarm, activity any, carnivore, intelligence Non- (0), treasure J–N, neutral, 2–20 appearing. AC 8, movement 6 and Wb 15, HD 1+1, THAC0 19, one attack for 1 damage, special defenses nil and magic resistance nil, Small (2 ft. diameter), morale unsteady (7), 175 XP.
- These web-spinners lurk by sticky webs. Strength 19 or higher lets a creature pass through. At lower Strength, breaking free takes one round per point below 19. The spiders crowd the trapped target, attacking at +4, and that target loses all Dexterity adjustments to Armor Class.
- The bite’s Type A poison takes effect after 15 minutes and deals 15 damage. A successful save vs. poison with a +2 bonus prevents the poison damage.

## Huge Spider

- Any nonarctic land, common, organization pack, activity any, carnivore, intelligence Animal (1), treasure J–N and Q, neutral, 1–12 appearing. AC 6, movement 18, HD 2+2, THAC0 19, one attack for 1–6 damage, special defenses nil and magic resistance nil, Medium (6 ft. diameter), morale average (8), 270 XP.
- The hunting spider ambushes from camouflaged tunnels and holes and can leap up to 30 feet. Opponents take −6 to surprise rolls when it attacks this way.
- Its Type A poison has the Large Spider’s 15-minute onset and 15 poison damage, prevented by a successful save. The Huge Spider’s save bonus is +1. The watch spider is a separate, specially bred form with its own venom.

## Giant Spider

- Any nonarctic land, uncommon, organization nest, activity any, carnivore, intelligence Low (5–7), treasure C, chaotic evil, 1–8 appearing. AC 4, movement 3 and Wb 12, one attack for 1–8 damage, special defenses nil and magic resistance nil, Large (8–12 ft. diameter), morale elite (13).
- Both base Hit Dice options are in the same table column. HD 3+3 uses THAC0 17 and XP 420. HD 4+4 uses THAC0 15 and XP 650. The other XP lines name the flying, steeder and trap door forms and are outside this profile.
- The prose describes the ordinary giant spiders as larger versions of the web-spinning Large Spider. The Giant profile has Type F poison, which causes immediate death on a failed save. It gives no separate save modifier. Its web behavior is supported by the reference to the Large Spider, whose distinct Type A poison applies to that smaller form.

## Appearance, art and scope

The entry describes eight legs and eyes. The Large and Giant web-spinners have bulbous abdomens and thin, sleek legs. The Huge hunting spider has a relatively smaller body, larger head and fangs, and hair on its body and legs. The base forms have no specified color. Printed p. 326 has no art. The unlabeled illustration on p. 327 sits above continuation of the Sword Spider text and depicts long legs with blade-like spines. Its placement and anatomy support the Sword Spider identification. The book has general interior illustrator credits and no confirmed individual attribution for that piece. [Printed p. 327](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/20047e15403c5bfd4751dd371400f5b9.webp) · [Print credits](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/efaf128f9ea2ed636f64e32847d3e254.webp)

The three profiles fit the library’s cross-edition Beast category as arachnid forms. The Giant Spider retains its printed Low intelligence and chaotic evil alignment. The Large Spider’s “swarm” is the printed organization label, with individual stat values and a separate number appearing. No exact name-and-publication records for these three are present in the primary catalog. Other sources’ Giant Spider profiles and the *How the Mighty Are Fallen* Large Spider encounter use separate source keys.
