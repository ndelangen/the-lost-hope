# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual spiders

Three base profiles from TSR's *Monstrous Manual*, p. 326. The checked page is from the January 1994 third printing of the 1993 book. Its figures and descriptions match the electronic edition. [Printed entry](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/b2128e061e816acd3711c7fd7f9085db.webp) · [Printing and credits](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/efaf128f9ea2ed636f64e32847d3e254.webp) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Printed size | Rules preserved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large Spider | Small, 2 ft. across | Web escape based on Strength, and a +2 save bonus against Type A poison. |
| Huge Spider | Medium, 6 ft. across | A leap of up to 30 ft., a −6 surprise penalty during that ambush, and a +1 save bonus against Type A poison. |
| Giant Spider | Large, 8–12 ft. across | Type F poison. The 3+3 HD option has THAC0 17 and 420 XP. The 4+4 HD option has THAC0 15 and 650 XP. |

The Giant Spider retains both printed pairs in the same edition profile. The three base entries have no fixed HP or challenge rating. [Exact printing evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

The three square portraits are newly generated on plain parchment. The printed description guided the slender web-spinners and the stockier, hairy hunting spider. The colors are illustration choices. The facing illustration appears to depict the Sword Spider, and no individual artist credit was verified for an illustration of these three forms.
