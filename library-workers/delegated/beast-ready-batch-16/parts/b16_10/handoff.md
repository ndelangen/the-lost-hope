# Batch 16 beast sources and portraits

- Clawfoot and Fastieth both use the standalone Medium Beast blocks on p. 289 of the November 2019 first printing of *Eberron: Rising from the Last War*. The Clawfoot's printed Charisma is 6 (−2). The indexed value was 10. The Fastieth's Quickness recharges on 5–6 and lets it Dodge as a bonus action. The earlier Deinonychus portrait has the Clawfoot's bipedal profile, sharp teeth, and sickle toe claws. The new Fastieth shows its larger eyes and patterned scales against flat tan parchment. [Publisher source index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/erftlw) · [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/eberron-rising-from-the-last-war)
- Two-Headed Crocodile uses the standalone Large Beast block on p. 89 of the original *Infernal Machine Rebuild* and the guardroom context on p. 29. A successful bite grapples and restrains its target and prevents that head from biting another target until the grapple ends. The public publisher index confirms the type and title. I verified the original text and folio breaks from a text facsimile. A rendered page was unavailable. The new portrait shows both heads in the circular crop, which clips a little at the outer edge. [Publisher source index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/imr) · [Publisher creature list](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters?page=166)
- Chimeric Fox uses the standalone Tiny Beast block on sheet 8 of the unnumbered 2021 NERDS . The encounter is on sheet 5 and the credits are on sheet 12. Its color-changing fur supports reusing the existing Frostmaiden Chimeric Fox portrait. [Original promotional mirror]()
