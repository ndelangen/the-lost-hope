# Batch 16, part b16_02

Staged printed rules for seven 2024 Player’s Handbook Beast records. Primal Companion appears on pp. 122–124. Summon Beast and Bestial Spirit appear on pp. 322–323. The three Bestial Spirit forms each derive from that one printed block. Their AC, HP, and attacks still scale by spell slot. [2024 Player’s Handbook](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024)

The existing Beast of the Sea recipe lists Piercing only. The first printing allows Bludgeoning or Piercing, chosen when summoned, and sets the grapple escape DC to the ranger’s spell save DC. Its printed ability scores match the existing registry. An early digital version swapped the Sea and Sky arrays, which D&D Beyond corrected in February 2025. [D&D Beyond correction](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/bugs-support/215922-2024-primal-companion-stat-block-discrepancy)

The Primal Companion portraits are shared with the 2014 forms, which also have primal markings. Bestial Spirit appearances are examples the caster may choose. The parent and Land use Wolf, Air uses Giant Eagle, and Water uses the Crocodile portrait from playable-species batch 11, with the snout and tail inside the circular crop. The three source portraits match the roster hashes and use muted tan parchment.
