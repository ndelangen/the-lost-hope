# Four beasts from the original books

I checked each stat block against the printed page. The local *Tales Trees Tell* matched Wizards' English release byte for byte. The books' credits pages identify the November 2021 first printing of *Strixhaven* and the May 2021 first printing of *Van Richten's Guide*. I found no creature-specific correction in the publisher's errata index or changelog. [Errata index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae) · [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog/)

- **Panther, *Tales Trees Tell* p. 26.** The appendix has the full block. Pounce requires the straight approach and Claw hit on the same turn. The Bite is a bonus action against a prone target. [Original Wizards ]()
- **Relic Sloth, *Strixhaven* p. 210.** Claws can grapple up to two targets altogether. Slow but Sturdy is a reaction to movement or being knocked prone. [Printed page](https://online.anyflip.com/usqxr/xjis/files/large/4b4ebc7af65997f4196bafa5ebd13056.webp)
- **Swarm of Maggots and Swarm of Scarabs, *Van Richten's Guide* p. 247.** Both are Medium swarms of Tiny Beasts, and their printed headings leave alignment unspecified. Infestation has a disease save and three effects rolled at the end of long rests. Ravenous Bites can leave embedded scarabs that deal damage at the start of each turn until removed. [Printed page](https://online.anyflip.com/npkza/fwfi/files/mobile/248.webp)

The Panther portrait's checksum matches the batch 2 original. I compared all three new 1254 × 1254 portraits with Big Linda at full size and in round 96-pixel crops. The sloth's long foreclaws stay visible. I gave the maggots soft skin and small mouth hooks so they read separately from the armored Rot Grubs.
