# Giant Insect forms and the Yûlash mastiff

[Scalable encounter prose](scalable-rules.json) · [Mastiff encounter block](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

The four *Player’s Handbook* entries use the shared spell creature on p. 279. The September 2024 first printing adds 10 HP per spell level. The publisher’s erratum applies that increase only for levels above 4. The staged descriptions use the corrected formula, share the caster’s initiative count, and preserve each form’s actions. [First-print folio](https://online.anyflip.com/acscv/btgw/mobile/index.html#p=280) · [Corrected spell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618944-giant-insect) · [Publisher errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/players-handbook)

| Form | Rules checked |
| --- | --- |
| Giant Insect | The caster chooses one form. It uses the caster’s proficiency bonus and spell attack modifier. At spell level 4 it has AC 15, 30 HP, and two attacks. At spell level 6 it has AC 17, 50 HP, and three attacks. |
| Centipede | Venomous Spew is a bonus action. It targets one creature the centipede can see within 10 ft. A failed Constitution save leaves that creature Poisoned until the start of the insect’s next turn. |
| Spider | Web Bolt uses the caster’s spell attack modifier at 60 ft. A hit sets the target’s Speed to 0 until the start of the insect’s next turn. |
| Wasp | Fly speed 40 ft., alongside the shared walk and climb speeds. |

The SRD 5.2.1 independently confirms the corrected spell on pp. 135–136. Its separate publication scope is already in the earlier standalone handoff. [SRD 5.2.1]()

The March 2016 release of *Death on the Wall* prints the Mastiff on p. 23. Bite deals 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage, with a DC 11 Strength save against falling prone for creatures it hits. The patrol and kennel details come from pp. 11–12. [Publisher adventure index]()

All five portrait hashes and square dimensions match. The round preview clips a few outer appendages in the three-form comparison image, but the forms remain identifiable.
