The source is the October 2008 first printing. Each stat block classifies its creature as a natural beast. The adventure's page in the August 2012 collected errata changes the Eladrin Ring of Passage rarity. The three creature blocks have no listed corrections. [Publisher preview]() · [Official 4e errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/4ECollectedErrataFAQ.zip)

- Dire Bear's Maul makes two Claw attacks. Both must hit the same target before the bear rolls the secondary attack against AC to grab. Ursine Crush deals 4d8 + 6 damage to a grabbed creature without another attack roll.
- Redspawn Firebelcher has resist 20 fire and gives allies resist 10 fire in aura 5. Fire Burst recharges on 5–6 and omits the ongoing damage on a miss.
- R3 uses three wyverns with the same stat block. Claws requires flight and knocks the target prone on a hit. Sting's secondary attack is against Fortitude. Flyby preserves its particular opportunity attack exception. Aerial Agility grants +2 to all defenses to a rider of level 10 or higher while flying.

The new Dire Bear uses the pale projections visible in Book One's Mound of Skulls illustration. The Firebelcher and Wyverns reuse the selected roster portraits. The Wyverns plate shows two views of the same creature type, with the three-creature count recorded in the encounter entry. Its wing tips are cropped in the circular preview and fully visible in the square. [Portrait review]()

## Portrait direction

Built-in ImageGen took Big Linda as a style reference for a single naturalistic brown bear on a square of matte tan parchment. The prompt called for a standing three-quarter pose, heavy brown-bear shoulders, broad paws, and a short row of small ivory projections along the upper back. The second pass extended the parchment into the corners and preserved the bear.
