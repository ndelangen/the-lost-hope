# Batch 16, part b16_05

Staged four natural-beast profiles from the September 2008 first printing of *Scepter Tower of Spellgard*. Adventure Book Two gives Fire Beetles on p. 7, Spitting Drake on p. 8, Gray Wolves on p. 11 and matching Deathjump Spider blocks on pp. 41, 59, 60 and 63. The official collected 4e updates and FAQ list no correction for these entries. [Publisher preview]() · [Official updates and FAQ](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/4ECollectedErrataFAQ.zip)

The fire beetles’ torchlike light and the wolves’ chain-grab procedure are printed encounter rules. The wolf portrait shows three members of the pack without leashes. The kennel encounter still calls for five and provides its own leashes. At the outer edge of the circular crop, small parts of the existing beetle and wolf group portraits are trimmed.

The new bronze Spitting Drake was generated with built-in ImageGen and saved as `portraits/spitting-drake.png`. All four feet and the tail remain visible in square and 64 px circular crops. The three reused portraits match the roster hashes.
