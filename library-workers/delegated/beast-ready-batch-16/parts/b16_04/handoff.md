# Winter Wolf in The Raven

The 2016 version 1.0 adventure gives Winter Wolf a standalone Large Beast block at p. 22. It has AC 13, 75 HP, cold immunity and CR 3 (700 XP). The adventure block governs this encounter. [Publisher preview]()

The printed Bite gives 11 piercing damage with the inconsistent expression 2d6 + 3. The profile uses the fixed 11 and preserves the printed expression. Cold Breath gives 18 (4d8) cold damage, DC 12 Dexterity for half, and no recharge. Strong and Very Strong raise the DC to 13 and set damage to 27. The Very Weak adjustment refers to Dire Wolves, while the main encounter gives six Winter Wolves. [The Raven text](https://www.scribd.com/document/496189802/DDAL04-12-The-Raven-5e-9367545)

The source describes a shaggy wolf with burning red eyes. The staged ImageGen edit changes the eyes to muted ember red and keeps the indexed portrait’s anatomy and plain parchment. The round crop preserves the face and red eyes. It clips the far-left front paw, as the original framing did. [Before and after](portrait-comparison.jpg)
