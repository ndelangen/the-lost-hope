# Batch 16 · Steeders and adventure-specific Beasts

The six records use the original adventure . The Encounters female steeder jumps up to 90 ft. with a 30-ft. minimum speed. The Maerimydra female steeder jumps up to 120 ft. and the text gives no minimum speed. The Encounters male jumps up to 60 ft. with a 30-ft. minimum speed. Its attacks continue on p. 63. [Wizards Encounters listing](https://wpn.wizards.com/en/products/out-of-the-abyss)

Spider King keeps 44 fixed HP and the changes in Dire Den on p. 74, alongside the Giant Spider's attacks. The Young Bulette uses the Rhinoceros Beast proxy directed by the Bulette Kennels, with Bite in place of Gore. The Golden Stag uses Elk statistics for the normal-animal branch and records the corrected encounter title. [Out of the Abyss errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/out-of-the-abyss) · [Princes of the Apocalypse errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/princes-of-the-apocalypse) · [Hoard of the Dragon Queen errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/hoard-of-the-dragon-queen)

The two new built-in ImageGen portraits use Big Linda's plain muted parchment. Spider King has two clearly visible heads. Golden Stag has shimmering golden fur and pale platinum-colored antlers. The versioned rules share the existing female steeder portrait. Male steeder and young bulette also reuse verified portraits.
