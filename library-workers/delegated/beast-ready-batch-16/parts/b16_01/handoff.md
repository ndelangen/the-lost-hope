# Beast-ready batch 16, part b16_01

I verified the three Primal Companion profiles on printed p. 61 and the single Bestial Spirit block with Air, Land, and Water forms on pp. 109–110. The November 2020 first-print copy includes the spell’s higher-level clause at the top of p. 110. The December 2021 errata contains no change to these rules. [Tasha’s Cauldron of Everything](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/tcoe) · [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/tashas-cauldron-of-everything)

The existing numeric formulas match the print. At ranger level 3 and PB +2, Land and Sea have AC 15 and 20 HP with three d8 Hit Dice. Sky has AC 15 and 16 HP with three d6 Hit Dice. A 4th-level Summon Beast has AC 15, 30 HP in Air or 40 HP in Land or Water, and two 1d8 + 8 piercing Maul attacks.

The three concrete Bestial Spirit recipes omit the printed caster PB. Land Charge and Sea Binding Strike use the caster spell-save DC in print. Their typed entries carry damage and conditions, and the source sections give the save and escape procedures. The source sections also give the owner-turn rules, ranger revival and replacement, and the spell’s command and disappearance conditions. The recipe file hash is recorded in the source evidence, and the part contains no fixed stat blocks.

The three primal portraits portray a wolf, crocodilian, and hawk with subtle markings. Both Tasha’s p. 61 and the 2024 *Player’s Handbook* p. 122 specify marks on the chosen companion animal. The Bestial Spirit selector and Land form reuse the wolf. Air reuses the Giant Eagle, and Water reuses the crocodile. The animal choices are illustrative.
