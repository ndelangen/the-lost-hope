# Batch 16, part 08

Verified five Beast profiles across four exact publication scopes.

- The 2014 Dungeon Master’s Guide prints Giant Fly with Ebony Fly on p. 169. The creature box has no CR, XP, or listed attacks. The profile displays challenge as “Not listed.” The 2024 version on p. 261 prints CR 0, 0 XP, and Initiative +1 (11). The 2024 item specifies that the fly acts immediately after its user on the same initiative count. [2014 item](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4831-figurine-of-wondrous-power-ebony-fly) · [2024 item and creature block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228613-figurine-of-wondrous-power-ebony-fly)
- Wolf of the Overworld has the same p. 7 rules in Mojang’s original and revised . The portrait is a naturalistic interpretation of the source’s gray-furred taiga wolf. The source illustration uses block-shaped anatomy. [Original Minecraft ]() · [Revised Minecraft ]()
- Black Flag v0.2 places Hippocampus across pp. 305–306 and Orca on p. 365. Shared Breath applies to an air-breathing rider while the hippocampus is neither fighting nor moving fast. Orca’s Tail Slap adds stun when the save fails by 5 or more. The XP values come from the same version’s p. 229 table. [Black Flag Roleplaying v0.2]()

All reused portraits matched the roster hashes and were checked in square and circular crops. The two fly versions share the same portrait. Its wing tip approaches the edge of the circular crop, as does the orca’s tail. Hippocampus retains its warmer orange parchment.
