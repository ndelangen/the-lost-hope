# Beast readiness batch 16

Batch 16 stages 50 Beast publication versions against archive `fc571f1`. It adds 32 fixed stat blocks and adds printed rules and folios to 18 existing companion and summon profiles. [Roster](roster.tsv) · [Manifest and source evidence](manifest.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch) · [Portrait index](portrait-index-preview.json)

The 2024 *Player’s Handbook* Beast of the Sea now offers bludgeoning or piercing damage. Its other scaling rules and the other profiles’ formulas carry forward. The Giant Insect rules display the corrected hit-point rule. [2024 *Player’s Handbook* errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/players-handbook)

The batch reuses 22 matching portrait files and adds 16 originals. The three marked ranger companions share portraits across the two editions. *The Raven*’s Winter Wolf gets a red-eye edit because the adventure describes burning red eyes. Some reused images lose peripheral details in round crops. The Hippocampus retains its warmer parchment. [Winter Wolf before and after](parts/b16_04/portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Big Linda style comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Contact sheet 1](portraits-1.jpg) · [Contact sheet 2](portraits-2.jpg)

The batch omits *Arcana Unleashed* and Spiderfrog because their original blocks could not be verified.

The temporary Library copy returned Spitting Drake and Giant Fly in search, loaded their square portraits, and displayed the Giant Fly’s missing challenge rating in the 2014 guide and CR 0 in the 2024 guide. Both Beast of the Sea printings used the same marked crocodile portrait and showed their own folios and rules. The 2024 book’s shark is still available in the image carousel. The browser console was clear. [Beast of the Sea before and after](beast-of-the-sea-before-after.jpg) · [Spitting Drake preview](spitting-drake-preview.jpg)

I ran two manual command-line checks. At ranger level 5 with Wisdom modifier +3, the 2024 Beast of the Sea returned AC 16, 30 HP, and 1d6 + 5 with both damage types available. A fourth-level slot gave the Giant Insect’s Spider form AC 15 and 30 HP. The catalog reviewer checked all 50 portrait lookups and verified that only the 2024 Sea companion’s damage choices changed in the scaling recipes. The bundle is staged locally, with the primary checkout’s tracked files and R2 untouched.
