# Mongrelfolk for D&D 3.5

One playable Mongrelfolk profile from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*. The entry begins on printed p. 98, and the playable rules span pp. 99–100. The catalog also has the fifth-edition *Curse of Strahd* monster under its own entry. [Appearance, p. 98]() · [Traits, pp. 99–100]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

Emulate Race automatically meets the humanoid race requirement on a magic item. The +4 racial bonus applies to a trained Use Magic Device check to emulate a nonhumanoid race. Sound Imitation has a fixed DC 16 Will save. Diffuse Blood adds +2 on saves against spells that target or exclude any of the seven listed ancestry races. [Species rules](book-species.json)

Mara Fir Vale, a pawnbroker and Rogue 1, appears in the full-body portrait on plain parchment. She is provisional CR 1. Tavin Nek Reed, a ferry bellkeeper and Expert 1, is provisional CR 1/2. Both NPCs are original builds.
