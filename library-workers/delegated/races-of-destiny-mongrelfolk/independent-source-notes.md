# Independent Mongrelfolk reviews

## Original printing

The source review confirmed that the contents direct readers to p. 97, where the Half-Ogre entry continues. Mongrelfolk begins on p. 98. Eric Polak's illustration shows an unnamed rogue, with no accompanying stat block. The racial traits span pp. 99–100. The source reviewer found no entry for *Races of Destiny* in Wizards' collected errata. [Printed pp. 98–100]() · [Wizards' errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The reviewer confirmed automatic Emulate Race for humanoid magic item restrictions, +4 racial for a trained check to emulate a nonhumanoid race, the seven-race limit on Diffuse Blood, and the fixed DC 16 Will save against Sound Imitation. The earlier *Fiend Folio* publishes a CR 1/3 monster profile on pp. 125–126. [3.5 SRD](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/skills/useMagicDevice.htm#emulateaRace) · [*Fiend Folio* scan](https://anyflip.com/myubd/ovic/basic/101-150)

## NPCs and catalog

The second reviewer checked class skill budgets, racial bonuses, combat math, and magic item restrictions. Mara spends 32 skill points. Her Use Magic Device modifier is +6 normally and +10 for nonhumanoid race emulation. Tavin spends 24 skill points, with Alertness already included in Listen and Spot.

The catalog uses separate species and monster IDs. The NPC lookup matches species, edition, and publication, which lets both new NPCs resolve under *Races of Destiny* while the fifth-edition *Curse of Strahd* monster keeps its own detail.
