# Mongrelfolk encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. The racial traits come from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*, pp. 99–100. [Printed rules]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Mara Fir Vale, Rogue 1

Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 14, touch 12, flat-footed 12. HP 9, initiative +2, speed 30 ft. Fort +3, Ref +4, Will +0. BAB +0, grapple +0. Low-light vision. STR 10, DEX 15, CON 17, INT 10, WIS 10, CHA 8.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Rapier | +0 melee, 1d6 piercing, 18–20/×2 |
| Shortbow | +2 ranged, 1d6 piercing, ×3, 60-ft. increment |

Sneak attack adds 1d6 when the target is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC or is flanked. A ranged sneak attack requires a target within 30 ft. Appraise +5, Disable Device +4, Hide +10, Move Silently +7, Search +5, Sleight of Hand +10, Spot +5, Use Magic Device +6.

Mara can demonstrate an elf-restricted magic clasp without rolling to meet the race requirement. Apply any other requirements as usual. If she tries a nonhumanoid restriction, roll Use Magic Device at +10 against DC 25. She wants the clasp returned to the person named in her ledger before someone redeems it with a copied ticket. If cornered, she hides behind the counter and heads for the back stair. [3.5 SRD, Use Magic Device](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/skills/useMagicDevice.htm#emulateaRace)

## Tavin Nek Reed, Expert 1

Provisional CR 1/2, ECL 1. AC 13, touch 11, flat-footed 12. HP 10, initiative +1, speed 30 ft. Fort +4, Ref +1, Will +3. BAB +0, grapple +1. Low-light vision. STR 12, DEX 13, CON 18, INT 10, WIS 12, CHA 6.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Club | +1 melee, 1d6+1 bludgeoning, ×2 |
| Sling | +1 ranged, 1d4+1 bludgeoning, ×2, 50-ft. increment. Loading takes a move action. |

Craft (carpentry) +4, Listen +8, Profession (sailor) +5, Search +5, Sense Motive +5, Spot +8. Listen and Spot include Alertness.

Someone rang the bell after the ferry tied up for the night. Tavin asks the party to watch the landing. He imitates the sound of the cracked bell from shore to see who comes for the crossing. A listener detects the trick with a DC 16 Will save.

## Racial saves and sound

Either NPC can imitate a sound they have heard. Listeners need a DC 16 Will save to recognize the ruse. Both NPCs are immune to sleep spells and similar magical effects. Apply +1 racial on saves against enchantment, illusion, or poison. Diffuse Blood gives +2 racial on the save when a spell targets or excludes humans, halflings, dwarves, elves, gnomes, goblins, or orcs. Use the spell's normal targeting rules.
