# Dragonborn of Bahamut source and review notes

The January 2006 first printing of *Races of the Dragon*, ISBN 0-7869-3913-3, gives the Rite of Rebirth and aspects on pp. 8–10. Page 10 preserves the original type. Wizards’ later 3.5 FAQ confirms that nonhumanoids can undergo the rite and keep their type. [Publisher sample]() · [Original Rite, pp. 8–10]() · [Wizards FAQ archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Both sample NPCs appear on p. 134. Vythjhank’s block retains several dwarf bonuses that the conversion rules remove and prints save bonuses against magical sleep and paralysis, where Mind grants immunity. The staged builds follow the player rules. Orra’s Wings aspect keeps her 20-ft. dwarf speed and loses dwarf darkvision and the armored movement perk. Joren’s human bonus feat and skill points are gone. [Mechanics of Rebirth, p. 10]() · [Published profiles, p. 134]()

Emily Fiegenschuh illustrated Kethend emerging from the rite on p. 8. Eric Polak’s p. 9 group shows dragonborn who retain different original builds and statures. Orra’s compact frame follows that distinction, with an original costume and pose. [Wizards art gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20161101032141/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/ag/20060105a)

The integration reviewer checked both skills budgets, armor and saves, Heart’s breath DC, and Wings’ Jump calculation. They also checked the separate species and monster IDs. The playable dive rule explicitly requires a piercing weapon.
