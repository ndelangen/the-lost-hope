# Dragonborn of Bahamut encounters

Both NPCs use D&D 3.5 rules and are provisional CR 1, ECL 1. Each has +2 dodge AC against attacks by Dragon-type creatures, bringing AC to 19 and touch AC to 13 when eligible for that bonus. Both are immune to dragons’ frightful presence. [First-print traits, pp. 8–9]()

## Joren Cindervein, ember road warden

Lawful good Paladin 1, Heart aspect. Former human. HP 13, AC 17 (touch 11, flat-footed 16), speed 30 ft., initiative +1. Fort +5, Ref +1, Will +3. Diplomacy +6, Heal +5. Ordinary vision.

- **Heart breath** takes a standard action and hits a 5-ft. line for 1d8 damage. Choose acid, cold, electricity, or fire. Reflex DC 13 for half. Roll 1d4 for the recharge in rounds.
- **Longsword** +3 melee, 1d8+2 slashing, 19–20/×2. Smite evil once per day for +5 and 1d8+3 against an evil target. An attempt against another alignment spends the use and resolves as a normal attack.
- **Javelin** +2 ranged, 1d6+2 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft. Detect evil at will.

Joren wants to get a wagon of refugees past the dragon’s tribute collectors. He asks the party to keep the wagon moving while he checks the next bend. He saves his breath for a target he can hit without catching anyone from the wagon in the line. [Heart aspect, p. 9]()

## Orra Slatefall, ravine rope guard

Lawful good Fighter 1, Wings aspect. Former dwarf. HP 13, AC 17 (touch 11, flat-footed 16), speed 20 ft., initiative +5. Fort +5, Ref +1, Will +0. Climb +3, Jump +7. Ordinary vision.

- **Glide** at 30 ft. with average maneuverability and a light load. Travel 20 ft. forward per 5 ft. of descent and take no falling damage while gliding. If helpless or unconscious in midair, Orra’s wings slow her fall to 1d6 damage. Powered flight begins at 6 HD.
- **Jump +7** comes from four ranks, +3 Strength, +10 Wings, −4 armor and shield, and −6 for her 20-ft. land speed. Rebirth kept her land speed and lost her old armored movement perk.
- **Longsword** +5 melee, 1d8+3 slashing, 19–20/×2. **Javelin** +2 ranged, 1d6+3 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft.

Orra can glide to the ledge below with a light messenger line. She asks the party to haul the heavier replacement rope around the ridge so it stays out of her glide load. [Wings and Rebirth mechanics, pp. 9–10]()
