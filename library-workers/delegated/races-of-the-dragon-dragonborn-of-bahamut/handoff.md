# Dragonborn of Bahamut for D&D 3.5

The 2006 Rite of Rebirth keeps selected traits of the original race, including size, base speed, ability adjustments, and level adjustment. The dragonborn adds +2 Constitution and −2 Dexterity and chooses Heart, Mind, or Wings once. The species profile covers all three aspects and how their abilities scale with Hit Dice. [Rite and aspects, pp. 8–10]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Joren Cindervein** is a former human and a Heart Paladin. His breath starts as a 5-ft. line with 1d8 damage, Reflex DC 13 for half, and a 1d4-round recharge.
- **Orra Slatefall** is a former dwarf and a Wings Fighter. She retains a 20-ft. land speed and can glide at 30 ft. with a light load. Powered flight starts at 6 HD.

Both builds are provisional CR 1. Orra is the subject of the plain-parchment portrait. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Parchment comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [96 px preview](portrait-thumbnails.jpg)

The temporary copy found the species by name, traits, and both NPC roles. Both encounter commands returned the full builds. The new species supplies its own default portrait and browse thumbnail. The generic Dragonborn species and the same-name *Fizban’s Treasury of Dragons* monster retained their profiles and artwork. The patch uses the baseline containing Spellscale.
