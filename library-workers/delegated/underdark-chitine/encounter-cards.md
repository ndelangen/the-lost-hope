# Chitine encounters

Both are Small Monstrous Humanoids with 60-ft. darkvision, a 30-ft. land speed, and a 20-ft. climb speed. Their hooks let them cross walls and ceilings. They can take 10 on Climb checks even under pressure. In sunlight or bright magical light, take −1 on attack rolls, Search checks, and Spot checks.

## Qid Lowweb, gallery scout

**Rogue 1 with 2 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 2.** AC 17, HP 23, initiative +8. Fortitude +2, Reflex +9, Will +3. Grapple +3. Climb +14 and Hide +14.

Qid crosses the ceiling to a ledge, lands, and draws her short swords. If she attacks once, roll at +7 for 1d4+1 piercing. When she can make a full attack with four swords, roll each at +5. The primary deals 1d4+1 and each off-hand sword deals 1d4. Add 1d6 to each hit that qualifies for Sneak Attack. When dazzled, the single attack falls to +6 and each full-attack swing to +4.

## Awa Spinner, silk rigger

**2 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 1.** AC 14, HP 16, initiative +3. Fortitude +2, Reflex +6, Will +3. Grapple +3. Small short sword +5 for 1d4+1 piercing, reduced to +4 when dazzled.

Awa needs to reach the silk stays before the gangway gives out. He climbs above the break with empty hands and takes 10 for a Climb result of 24. He draws his sword from a stable brace if the crossing comes under threat.
