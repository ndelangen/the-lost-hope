# Diopsid source and review notes

The October 2005 first printing of *Dragon Compendium, Volume I* gives the 3.5 Diopsid on pp. 10–14. Printed pp. 12–14 are pages 16–18 in the saved scan. Paizo’s errata contains no Diopsid correction. [First printing]() · [Paizo errata]()

Johnathan M. Richards’s original AD&D playable Diopsid appeared in *Dragon* #267, pp. 42–45, with Jeff Miracola’s illustration. That version has different size, defenses, and natural attacks. This handoff uses the later 3.5 rules. [Original magazine issue]()

Mike Nelson is credited beside the p. 11 close-up and Kevin Wasden beside the p. 13 battle illustration. The original anatomical description gives two strong upper arms, two much smaller supporting arms, and fixed stalks tipped with multifaceted red eyes. Rivet’s stance and equipment are original. [Compendium artwork]()

The integration reviewer checked both sword routines, skill budgets, armor, rage, LA, and publication scoping. The Large longsword die follows the core weapon-size table. The provisional encounter CR is an estimate for each build. ECL comes from class Hit Dice and level adjustment. [SRD weapon size table](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/equipment/weapons.htm#weaponSize)
