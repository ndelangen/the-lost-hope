# Diopsid encounters

Both NPCs are D&D 3.5, provisional CR 2 and ECL 2. Each has one class Hit Die and LA +1. Both have darkvision 60 ft. and immunity to sleep effects. They can switch their light on or off as a free action. It lights a 10-ft. radius, with shadowy illumination for 20 ft. beyond. While they can act, they can unfurl their wings as an immediate action to descend 60 ft. per round without fall damage. [Diopsid racial traits, pp. 12–14]()

## Rivet, scree hoist watch

Neutral good Barbarian 1. HP 15, AC 14 (touch 10, flat-footed 14), speed 40 ft., initiative +0. Fort +5, Ref +0, Will +1. Climb +7, Jump +11, Listen +7, Survival +5.

- **Large longsword** +4 melee, 2d6+3 slashing, 19–20/×2. One primary hand wields it while its matching lower hand steadies it. **Javelin** +1 ranged, 1d6+3 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft.
- **Rage** once per day, up to eight rounds. HP 17, AC 12 (touch 8, flat-footed 12), Fort +7, Ref +0, Will +3. Longsword +6 for 2d6+5 and javelin +1 for 1d6+5. Climb +9, Jump +13. Lose the 2 added HP when rage ends and apply fatigue through the encounter.

Rivet is trying to get a new guide chain down to a stranded hoist. He can descend with a light line while the party works the upper brake. If a creature comes over the side, he steadies the sword with his lower hand. [Extra Arms, p. 13]() · [SRD weapon damage by size](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/equipment/weapons.htm#weaponSize)

## Tongs, iron gantry guard

Lawful neutral Fighter 1. HP 13, AC 18 (touch 11, flat-footed 17), speed 30 ft., initiative +5. Fort +5, Ref +1, Will +1. Climb and Jump +6. Custom studded leather gives +3 armor with no added racial Dexterity penalty.

- **Single greatsword** +4 melee, 2d6+4 slashing, 19–20/×2.
- **Two greatswords** as a full attack at +0/+0. Each deals 2d6+4 slashing, 19–20/×2. Limb Symmetry lets Tongs take Two-Weapon Fighting at Dexterity 12. The non-light off-hand sword imposes a −4 attack penalty for each weapon, and each sword gets the full two-handed Strength bonus.
- **Javelin** +2 ranged, 1d6+3 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft. Free a hand before throwing it.

Tongs can flash a signal to get the porters moving. On the first round, Tongs can move to the narrow approach and swing once. From there, a full attack uses both swords. [Limb Symmetry, p. 13]() · [SRD two-weapon penalties](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/combat/specialAttacks.htm#twoWeaponFighting)
