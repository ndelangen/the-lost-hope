# Diopsid for D&D 3.5

The October 2005 first printing of *Dragon Compendium, Volume I* gives Diopsids two strong arms and two supporting arms. The smaller arms can steady an oversized weapon or help wield two two-handed weapons. The wings slow a fall as an immediate action. The species has level adjustment +1. [Original rules, pp. 12–14]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Rivet**, Barbarian 1, braces a Large longsword without an oversized weapon penalty. It deals 2d6+3 damage, or 2d6+5 in rage.
- **Tongs**, Fighter 1, uses two Medium greatswords. With Two-Weapon Fighting and a non-light off-hand weapon, the full attack is +0/+0 for 2d6+4 each. A single attack is +4.

Both encounters are provisional CR 2 and ECL 2, with one class Hit Die and LA +1. The plain-parchment portrait shows Rivet using the lower arm to brace his sword.
