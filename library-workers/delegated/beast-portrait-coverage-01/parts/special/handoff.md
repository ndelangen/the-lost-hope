# MCDM Beast portraits

Four square portraits cover five publication versions. The two Mohler entries share one portrait. I found existing publisher illustrations for all four creatures, then checked the catalog and earlier stages for a matching parchment portrait. The new portraits use original poses and match Big Linda's plain matte parchment. [Flee, Mortals!](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/fm) · [Where Evil Lives]()

I checked the source details at full size and in round thumbnails. The Mohler has broad digging claws, pale tusks and small bony growths. The Rotbeast has a faceless fungal head and stag antlers. The isopod and grub keep distinct segmented armor and soft amber larval anatomy. I gave the Rotbeast extra margin so its antlers fit in the round crop.
