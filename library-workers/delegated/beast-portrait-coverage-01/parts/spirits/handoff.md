## Shared prompt

Create one original square bestiary portrait with a plain warm tan matte parchment field edge to edge, matching the attached Big Linda image solely for paper color, matte traditional natural-history paint, restrained details, gentle directional lighting and faint contact shadow. Render an incorporeal dinosaur spirit. Its complete skeleton is visible inside a thin continuous outer silhouette made of translucent pale desaturated blue-green, with subtle darker teal contours around the face, spine and limbs for thumbnail legibility. Keep pale sea-glass cyan and bone ivory, enough contrast to read on tan. The tan field shows through the spectral creature in places, while the species silhouette reads clearly. The paper has uniform unobtrusive tone and very fine grain. Show the entire animal in an original standing pose within the central 69 percent of the canvas, on a horizontal body plan if appropriate, with the head, feet, and complete tail safe inside the 96-pixel circular crop. No environmental scene, horizon, black/dark fill, ground pattern, neon outline, magical particles, smoke, shadows in the corners, vignette, decoration, borders, type, or other subjects.

The translucent teal shape reveals ivory-teal spinal column, ribs and limb bones. Rounded osteoderms and shoulders rise above the back. The heavy bony tail club swings in a low shallow curve toward the viewer, plainly separated from the back legs. Give the low wide skull an ankylosaur's blunt beak. No long frill or face horns.
