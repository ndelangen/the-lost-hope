# Beast portrait coverage

The audit compared 1,164 runnable Beast publication versions with the current portrait index, the local artwork archive, and prior staging. It surfaced 239 publication versions without an exact or general portrait mapping. Equivalent printings already had suitable creature art. Of the 19 catalog names with no same-name generated index entry, eight needed a distinct portrait.

The stage adds eight square, plain-parchment ImageGen portraits for nine publication versions. Mohler shares one portrait across *Flee, Mortals!* and *Where Evil Lives*. The three Dinosaur Spirits have separate silhouettes for their runnable forms. Trinket wears the plate armor specified in *Tal’Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn*, p. 266. Earlier staging already contains the Alligator, Forest Ray, Saragón Lyra Bird, and Skinwing. The generic MCDM Bear, Camel, Griffon, two Bird of Prey sizes, and Wildcat can use existing representative art. The generic Dinosaur Spirit selector still uses its publisher token. The new art covers its three named forms.

All 43 Beast rules holds remain open. The Dinosaur Spirits already had runnable recipes, and this stage supplies artwork only. Their printed rules remain unverified. [Source pages needed](needed-source-pages.md)
