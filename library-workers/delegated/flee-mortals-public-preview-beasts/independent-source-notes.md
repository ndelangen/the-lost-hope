# Flee, Mortals! public preview beast source notes

Both entries are from the MCDM public preview, p. 9, for catalog edition `2014 fifth edition compatible`. The local reference matches the publisher download byte for byte. These notes use the values on that page. [MCDM public preview, p. 9]()

## Swarm of Skitterlings

Catalog entry `beast-5e95b1f48100231371be657a`, preview version `0250c8f4d6420756fe09c3bb`.

- CR 2 Soldier, 450 XP. Medium swarm of Tiny beasts, unaligned. AC 13, HP 36 (8d8), walk 30 ft., fly 60 ft., PB +2. STR 10, DEX 16, CON 10, INT 3, WIS 12, CHA 5. Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11, no languages.
- Resists bludgeoning, piercing and slashing. Immune to Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Prone, Restrained and Stunned. The swarm can share spaces in either direction and pass through gaps a single Tiny skitterling could fit through. It cannot recover HP or gain temporary HP.
- **In Your Face.** An enemy in the swarm's space has disadvantage on attacks aimed elsewhere and takes 3 (1d6) slashing damage if it attacks another creature. **Claws.** +5 melee, 0-ft. reach, one target. The hit deals 14 (4d6) slashing or 7 (2d6) at half HP or less. The target must pass a DC 10 CON save or be blinded until the start of its next turn. Immunity to poison damage or the Poisoned condition grants automatic success. To use the Soldier role, occupy a foe's space so it has a reason to focus on the swarm instead of its allies. [Preview roles, p. 4]() · [Stat block, p. 9]()
- Goblins train these six-legged, winged rodents to go for the face. Each is about the size of a housecat. At Jagged Edge Hideaway, a swarm roosts on the dirt ceiling of area G8 until disturbed or sent after intruders. G8 is a documented underground encounter site. The sources leave the creature's broader natural habitat unspecified. [Creature description, p. 5]() · [Where Evil Lives preview, p. 11]()

## War Spider

Catalog entry `beast-6403554aa0e95adc6d83ebb2`, preview version `9ab6a9e696d34ad0057fb524`.

- CR 3 Brute, 700 XP. Huge beast, unaligned. AC 15 (natural armor), HP 73 (7d12 + 28), walk and climb 40 ft., PB +2. STR 18, DEX 14, CON 18, INT 2, WIS 11, CHA 4. Blindsight 10 ft., darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10, no languages.
- **Baby Burst.** On death, a Swarm of Insects (spiders) emerges and takes the spider's initiative count. **Rider Launcher.** Allied riders can leap 30 ft. horizontally or 15 ft. vertically, with or without a run-up, and gain advantage on their first melee weapon attack that turn. **Spider Climb** permits ceiling travel without a check. **Web Walker** ignores webbing movement restrictions. **Wide Back** allows two Small or Tiny riders per space, with a printed capacity of 18 Small or 64 Tiny.
- **Bite** and **Bladed Leg** both hit at +6. Bite has 5-ft. reach and deals 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing plus 7 (2d6) poison. Bladed Leg has 10-ft. reach and deals 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing. **Web Spray** restrains targets in a 15-ft. cube on a failed DC 13 DEX save. A creature can spend an action and pass a DC 13 STR or DEX check to free itself or a creature within reach. **Trample** recharges on 6, lets the spider move up to its speed through occupied spaces and grants one Bladed Leg attack per creature whose space it enters. A hit prevents reactions until the start of that creature's next turn. The preview gives no special movement cost for those spaces. [Stat block, p. 9]()
- The preview describes a huge, blade-equipped spider with a platform for goblin archers. At Jagged Edge Hideaway, Marrowgnaw is elephant-sized, with gray hair and blades strapped to her legs. She hides on the cavern ceiling when warned, opens with Web Spray, follows with bites and tries to escape with Trample at 20 HP. Those physical details and the retreat threshold describe this named individual. The book establishes the cavern and webbed access shaft as encounter locations. [Creature description, p. 5]() · [Where Evil Lives preview, p. 10]()

## Rules in later sources

The later *Where Evil Lives* preview adds Dazed immunity and explicitly gives Claws a target inside the skitterling swarm's space. Its War Spider carries 72 Tiny riders, uses DC 14 for both Web Spray rolls, treats occupied spaces as difficult terrain during Trample and spawns its own Swarm of Spiders stat block. The two commercial *Flee, Mortals!* D&D Beyond links led to the storefront when checked signed out, so the full commercial blocks remain unverified here.
