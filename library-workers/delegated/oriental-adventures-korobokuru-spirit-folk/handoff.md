# Korobokuru and Spirit Folk playable species

Two missing playable species from the 2001 D&D 3.0 printing of James Wyatt's *Oriental Adventures*. Korobokuru traits span pp. 12–13. Spirit Folk and its Bamboo, River, and Sea lineages span pp. 14–15. The three lineages are selectable profiles on one Spirit Folk entry. They share one portrait because the book gives a shared humanlike appearance. [Publisher preview]() · [Original book scan]() · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Profile | Printed traits to use at the table |
| --- | --- |
| Korobokuru | Small, 20-ft. base movement, 60-ft. darkvision, and specific attack, dodge, save, and Wilderness Lore bonuses. The separate published warrior lists 120-ft. darkvision. |
| Bamboo Spirit Folk | Trackless Step, forest Hide and Wilderness Lore bonuses, specific earth and wood saves, and daily speech with animals. |
| River Spirit Folk | Water breathing, a 30-ft. swim speed, water-related saves, and daily speech with fish. |
| Sea Spirit Folk | Water breathing, a 30-ft. swim speed, saves against fire-descriptor spells, and a weather prediction check. |

Bramble is an original first-level Korobokuru Barbarian who protects an evacuation route. Ilen is an original first-level River Spirit Folk Adept who can lead an underwater rescue and treat the injured. Ilen is listed as a River example anywhere the species' NPC summary appears. The encounter cards include prepared spells, saves, attacks, and what each character wants. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [NPC build records](npc-builds.json)

The portraits have complete figures on plain parchment. The equipment and styling are original choices. The printed p. 8 lineup identifies both species and leaves the Spirit Folk lineage unspecified. [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

In a temporary copy, all four profiles were searchable, and each Spirit Folk lineage resolved to the same image. The catalog's encounter and NPC commands returned both runnable builds. The patch was verified alongside the prior Nezumi and Vanara records. [Before](catalog-before.json) · [After](catalog-after.json) · [Verification](verification.json)
