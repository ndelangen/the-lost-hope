# D&D 3.0 encounter cards

Both NPCs are original builds. Reuse the sheet with a different name, motive, or hook. [Playable rules, pp. 12–15]() · [3.0 Adept class]() · [3.0 Barbarian class]()

## Bramble, Korobokuru pass rescuer

**Barbarian 1, CR 1.** Small Humanoid (Dwarf). AC 14 (touch 12, flat-footed 13), HP 11 (1d12+1 plus Toughness), initiative +1, speed 30 ft. Fort +3, Ref +1, Will −1. Str 13, Dex 12, Con 13, Int 8, Wis 9, Cha 8. Darkvision 60 ft. Speaks Common and Dwarven.

**Skills and traits.** Climb +5, Hide +5, Listen +3, Wilderness Lore +5. Leather armor. Add +2 on saves against poison and +2 on saves against spells and spell-like effects. Add +1 to attacks against goblinoids. Against giants, add +4 dodge AC when the Dexterity bonus to AC still applies.

- **Short sword.** Melee +3, 5-ft. reach, 1d6+1 piercing, critical 19–20/×2. Raging +5 for 1d6+3. Fatigued +2 for 1d6.
- **Thrown dagger.** Ranged +3, 10-ft. range increment, 1d4+1 piercing, critical 19–20/×2. Raging +3 for 1d4+3. Fatigued +2 for 1d4.
- **Rage once per day.** Up to six rounds with Str 17, Con 17, HP 13, AC 12, Fort +5, Will +1. Tasks such as moving silently or spellcasting are unavailable. Remove the two rage HP when it ends. Until the encounter ends, fatigue sets Str 11, Dex 10, AC 13, and Ref +0 and prevents charging or running.

Bramble has rigged a safety line and wants to get a stranded crew off the pass. Someone has to take the loose end across. If the evacuation is threatened, Bramble holds the exposed side until the last traveler reaches cover.

## Ilen, River Spirit Folk ferry healer

**Adept 1, provisional CR 1/2.** Medium Humanoid (Spirit). AC 11 (touch 11, flat-footed 10), HP 4 (1d6), initiative +1, speed 30 ft., swim 30 ft. Fort +0, Ref +1, Will +3. Str 8, Dex 12, Con 11, Int 10, Wis 13, Cha 9. Low-light vision. Speaks Common and Spirit Tongue.

**Skills and traits.** Heal +7 from Skill Focus and ranks. Wilderness Lore +5. Breathes air and water. Ordinary swimming requires no check. Swim +7 for hazards or special actions, with take 10 for a result of 17 even under pressure. Add +2 on qualifying water-related saves from the River profile.

- **Club.** Melee −1, 5-ft. reach, 1d6−1 bludgeoning (minimum 1), critical ×2.
- **Prepared cantrips.** Cure minor wounds twice per day, touch, restores 1 HP per cast. Create water once per day, up to two gallons.
- **Prepared 1st-level spells.** Cure light wounds once per day, touch, restores 1d8+1 HP. Bless once per day, allies in a 50-ft. burst gain +1 morale to attacks and saves against fear for one minute.
- **Speak with animals.** River trait, once per day for one minute, caster level 1, fish only.

Ilen has a missing passenger to find before the current carries them past the landing. Ilen can ask the fish where the passenger last surfaced, lead a swimmer to the pier, and treat the passenger once the party gets them ashore.
