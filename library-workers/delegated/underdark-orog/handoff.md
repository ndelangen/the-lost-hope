# Orog

This batch adds the playable Orog to the 3.5 *Underdark* catalog. The species uses the original deep-orc traits from *Races of Faerûn*, as cited by *Underdark*. Gorvak Ironcleft and Maig Forgechant are original NPCs. Gorvak’s plain-parchment portrait represents the species profile.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and both encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/orog-draft.png). The [comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Gorvak beside Big Linda.

In a temporary copy, search found the species by name, Light Blindness, cold resistance, and both NPC roles. The portrait loaded for the 2003 profile and as the species thumbnail. The existing Orog monster entry, including its versions and portraits, matched the baseline. [Verification](verification.json)
