# Orog encounters

Both Orogs have 120-ft. darkvision, +2 natural armor, fire resistance 5, and cold resistance 5. Sudden bright light blinds them for 1 round. They take −1 on attack rolls, saves, and checks while the bright light lasts.

## Gorvak Ironcleft, forge guard

**Fighter 1, provisional CR 2.** AC 19, HP 12, initiative +0, speed 20 ft. in half-plate. Fortitude +4, Reflex +0, Will +0. Masterwork greatsword +8 for 2d6+7 slashing. Power Attack changes that to +7 for 2d6+9.

Gorvak holds the forge passage until the smiths are clear. If a shutter exposes him to daylight, his normal greatsword attack drops to +7, Craft drops to +6, and his saves become Fortitude +3, Reflex −1, and Will −1 while the light lasts.

## Maig Forgechant, foundry caller

**Bard 1, provisional CR 2.** AC 15, HP 8, initiative +1, speed 30 ft. Fortitude +2, Reflex +3, Will +2. Greatsword +3 for 2d6+4 slashing or throwing axe +1 for 1d6+3 slashing.

Maig beats a drum to gather the workers at the side exit. His Inspire Courage grants +1 on attacks, weapon damage, and saves against fear or charm while the allies hear him and for 5 rounds afterward. He stops playing before casting. He can use his first-level slot for *grease* to block pursuit at the threshold (Reflex DC 14, 1 round) or for *cure light wounds* to heal 1d8+1.
