# Orog rules sources

- The March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn* gives the original playable Orog traits. The 3.5 *Underdark* refers to that book and gives class Hit Dice, LA +2, favored class Fighter, and ECL 3 at first class level. The *Player’s Guide to Faerûn* continues to use the original ancestry traits. [Races of Faerûn, pp. 71–73]() · [Underdark, pp. 19–20]() · [Player’s Guide to Faerûn, pp. 5–6]()
- The Orog racial traits give greatsword and throwing axe proficiency. The orc double axe appears as optional regional equipment in the later guide. Both new builds take Common from the original racial bonus-language list. [Races of Faerûn, p. 73]() · [Player’s Guide to Faerûn, p. 27]()
- Light Blindness blinds an Orog for 1 round on sudden exposure to bright light. The −1 circumstance penalty on attack rolls, saves, and checks applies while bright light persists. Fire resistance 5 and cold resistance 5 are separate traits. The collected errata and official FAQ give no Orog correction. [Races of Faerûn, p. 73]() · [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Gorvak’s larger ears, pale eyes, and half-plate follow the written description. Jim Pavelec illustrated the Orog in the original book. Both staged CRs are provisional estimates for the original NPCs. [Description and illustration, p. 71]()
