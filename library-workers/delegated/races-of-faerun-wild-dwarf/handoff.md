# Wild Dwarf

This batch adds the playable Wild Dwarf, or Dur Authalar, from the March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn*. The profile keeps the original handaxe and blowgun proficiencies, fire resistance, and specific knockout-poison immunity, alongside the revised 3.5 core rules. [Original traits]()

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species, Nima Reedbend, and Perrin Mossline. It is based on catalog commit `e92b216`. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) maps Nima's [square portrait](portraits/wild-dwarf-draft.png) to this publication.

Manual review covered the rules, both builds, and the [portrait thumbnail](portrait-thumbnails.jpg). Search, in-app portrait loading, and automated verification are deferred.
