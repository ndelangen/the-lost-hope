# Wild Dwarf encounter cards

## Nima Reedbend

Barbarian 2. Provisional CR 2, ECL 2. AC 14, HP 24, initiative +1, speed 30 ft. with fast movement. Fort +6, Ref +1, Will +1. Fire resistance 5.

- Small handaxe +7, 1d4+3 slashing, critical ×3.
- Rage 1/day, 8 rounds. AC 12, HP 28 at full health, Fort +8 and Will +3. Handaxe +9, 1d4+5 slashing.
- Uncanny dodge retains the Dexterity bonus when caught flat-footed or attacked by an invisible creature.

Nima holds the washed-out crossing until the others reach firm ground.

## Perrin Mossline

Rogue 2. Provisional CR 2, ECL 2. AC 16, HP 14, initiative +3, speed 20 ft. Fort +2, Ref +6, Will +1. Fire resistance 5.

- Small handaxe +3, 1d4+1 slashing. Small blowgun +5, 1 piercing, 10-ft. range increment. Point Blank Shot raises it to +6 for 2 piercing within 30 ft., before applying range penalties.
- Sneak attack +1d6 when flanking or against a target denied its Dexterity bonus to AC. Ranged sneak attacks must be within 30 ft.
- Trapfinding, evasion, and Disable Device +6.

Perrin shows the party the washed-out trail marker and points out the old crossing. [Complete builds](npc-builds.json)
