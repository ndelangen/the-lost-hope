# Wild Dwarf sources

The March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn* gives playable Wild Dwarves on printed pp. 23–25. The viewer is one page ahead. The player entry grants Small size, fire resistance 5, +3 on poison saves, +4 on disease saves, and immunity to Wild Dwarf knockout poison. Poison Use prevents accidentally poisoning oneself while applying poison to a weapon. The species has no racial Hit Dice or listed level adjustment. [Original player traits]() · [Knockout poison, printed p. 160]()

The 3.5 adaptation inherits Dwarf stability and the armor and load movement rule. The player entry replaces weapon familiarity with handaxe and blowgun proficiency and removes stonecunning and the standard Appraise and Craft bonuses for stone and metal. Perrin's blowgun uses the 3.5 table for the ordinary Small blowgun, with 1 piercing damage, a ×2 critical, and a 10-ft. range increment. He uses uncoated needles. [3.5 Dwarf](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/races.htm#dwarves) · [3.5 Dungeon Master's Guide, Table 5–3]()

Both NPCs use Common, Chultan, and Dwarven from the revised Realms rules and the Chult dwarf region. Perrin takes Goblin as his Intelligence bonus language. The revised Chult table offers Draconic, Goblin, Ignan, Shaaran, and Tashalan. The original Wild Dwarf regional list also includes Yuan-Ti. [Original regional rules]() · [Revised Chult table]()
