# Al-Qadim Caravans beasts

This bundle adds four animals from Rick Swan's 1994 TSR adventure. It uses the *Campaign Guide* for the Burrowing Owl and the named *Adventure Book* encounters for the snakes. The profiles retain AD&D 2e movement, THAC0, and XP. These blocks give no challenge ratings. The local copy matches the inside the archived publisher ZIP byte for byte. [Archived publisher download](https://web.archive.org/web/20101014100000id_/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/files/tsr9459caravans.zip) · [Exact pages and hash](source-evidence.json) · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

| Animal | Stat block | Printed movement | Encounter HP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burrowing Owl | Campaign Guide p. 27 | 1, Fl 27 (D), Br 3 | 1 Hit Die |
| Black Sand Adder | Adventure Book p. 9 | 12 | 21 |
| Dune Viper | Adventure Book p. 10 | 15 | 13 and 15 |
| Giant Cobra | Adventure Book p. 11 | 15 | 27 |

The *Campaign Guide* summary on p. 32 repeats the Black Sand Adder label on a row that matches the Dune Viper's other combat values. Its snake HP values are 15, 9, and 23. The profiles use the named encounters and record the discrepancies. Fayiz's dialogue says the adder is faster than the viper, while their blocks give MV 12 and MV 15 respectively. The cobra's invisibility and the thrown vipers' delay appear under their encounter rules.

I made four square parchment portraits with built-in ImageGen. The owl and snake colors follow the book where it specifies them. The cobra's coloration is an interpretation of the uncaptioned black-and-white hooded-snake scene. The Adventure Book credits Karl Waller for interior art.
