# Al-Qadim Caravans beast source review

Rick Swan wrote *Caravans* for AD&D 2e. The 1994 TSR release is product 9459. The 128-page compiled contains the *Campaign Guide*, followed by the separately numbered *Adventure Book*. Campaign Guide p. 2 (p. 4) and Adventure Book p. 2 (p. 37) credit Karl Waller for interior art. I cross-checked the relevant printed pages against the text. The original Wizards ZIP is listed in the local reference index. A readable copy of the TSR pages is also available from the mirror below. ## Burrowing Owl

- *Campaign Guide* p. 27, p. 29. The block headed “Burrowing owl” is under Helpful Statistics. Animal intelligence (1), neutral, AC 5, move 1, fly 27 (D), burrow 3, HD 1, THAC0 19, three attacks for 1–4/1–4/1–2, Tiny (1 ft. tall), morale 7, 65 XP. The source supplies HD without a fixed HP and gives the damage sequence without separately labeling the three attacks.
- The “Burrowing Owls” prose on printed p. 28, p. 30, places groups of 2–8 on the plains north of the Range of the Marching Camels. This golden-feathered owl has outsized talons, sleeps by day in a 3–5 ft. sand burrow, and claws at travelers who disturb its group while digging for ab’i tubers. This is the desert owl profiled by the guide. [TSR scan, Campaign Guide pp. 27–28]()

## Black Sand Adder

- *Adventure Book* p. 9, p. 44, Area 2c “Shelves.” “Black sand adder” has animal intelligence, neutral alignment, AC 6, move 12, HD 3, 21 HP, THAC0 17, one attack for 1–2 damage, Medium (6 ft. long), morale 9, 270 XP. Poison uses a save versus poison at −1 and a 2d4-round onset. A failed save incapacitates the victim for 24 hours.
- Fayiz describes the dark snake in the lead-up on p. 7 (p. 42). His claim that adders are faster than vipers conflicts with their printed move values, 12 and 15. In Area 2c, the zin enlarges the jug when a character approaches, releasing the snake. The snake strikes someone nearby, then tries to escape under the tent wall if left alone. [TSR scan, Adventure Book pp. 7 and 9]()

## Dune Viper

- *Adventure Book* p. 10, p. 45, Area 2e “Beds.” “Dune viper (2)” starts at the foot of the left column and continues at the top of the right. Animal intelligence, neutral, AC 5, move 15, HD 2+1, encounter HP 13 and 15, THAC0 19, one attack for 1 damage, Small (4 ft. long), morale 8, 175 XP. Poison has a 1d4-round onset and an unmodified poison save. Failure adds 2d4 damage and gives −2 to all the victim’s attack rolls for 24 hours.
- Fayiz describes the vipers as brown and aggressive on p. 7 (p. 42). In Area 2e, the zin’s unseen servant prods the pair under the bed. A viper flees after losing at least half its HP. Area 4d on p. 11 refers back to the same block for the other two vipers. On p. 12 (p. 47), after the zin throws them, the pair takes one to two rounds to get its bearings, attacks creatures nearby, then tries to flee after one or two strikes. The delay belongs to the thrown pair’s encounter. [TSR scan, Adventure Book pp. 7 and 10–12]()

## Giant Cobra

- *Adventure Book* p. 11, p. 46, Area 4b “Cabinet.” “Giant cobra” has animal intelligence, neutral alignment, AC 5, move 15, HD 4+2, 27 HP, THAC0 17, one attack for 1d3 damage, Medium (12 ft. long), morale 10, 420 XP. Poison has a 2d4-round onset and a poison save at −2. Success still deals 10 extra damage. Failure kills. Hypnotic Stare calls for a separate save versus paralyzation and leaves the victim stationary for 1d6 turns on failure. The block gives no special initiative modifier or separate timing for rolling those saves.
- The zin’s invisibility on the snake is part of the cabinet scene. The cobra rises and spreads its hood when disturbed, then becomes visible on its attack against the nearest player character. It tries to immobilize a character with its stare when an opportunity presents itself, concentrates its attacks on that target, and normally fights to the death. On p. 12, a negotiated truce can lead to the zin calming the cobra and sending it away. The adjacent uncaptioned illustration on p. 10 (p. 45) shows a hooded snake confronting armed figures in the tent and fits this scene. The scene gives no color for this cobra. [TSR scan, Adventure Book pp. 10–12]()

## Summary table and catalog check

*Campaign Guide* p. 32 (p. 34) repeats “Snake, black sand adder” with two different stat rows. The first entry is consistent with the named adder. The second matches the named Dune Viper’s AC, move, HD, THAC0, attack count and damage. The guide lists HP 15, 9 and 23 for those two rows and “Snake, cobra,” respectively. The adventure explicitly supplies the named encounter versions with 21, 13 and 15, and 27 HP. Use the encounter entries for those three named forms. The two extra vipers reuse the Dune Viper block. The two young zin are described as blue snakes and belong to the shapeshifting zin’s story. They have no separate animal profile in the scene. None of the four names had an exact match in the primary catalog or publisher-art index. [TSR scan, Campaign Guide p. 32]()
