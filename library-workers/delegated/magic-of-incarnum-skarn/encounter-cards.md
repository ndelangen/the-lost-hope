# Skarn encounters

Both NPCs use D&D 3.5 rules and are provisional CR 1, ECL 1. Their species grants no innate essentia. Corven has none at this level. Sera gains one from Cobalt Power. [Species traits, p. 15]() · [Cobalt Power, p. 37]()

## Corven Ashstep, stone stair marshal

Lawful good Monk 1, first-level Skarn substitution. HP 10, AC 13 (touch 13, flat-footed 12), speed 30 ft., initiative +5. Fort +4, Ref +3, Will +4. Climb +9, Intimidate +5, Jump +7, Tumble +5.

- **Arm spine** +3 melee, 1d6+3 piercing, ×2. **Flurry** is a full attack with two spine attacks, +1/+1 for 1d6+3 each. The −2 penalty also applies to other attacks before Corven’s next turn.
- **Stunning Fist** once per day. Declare it before rolling a spine attack. On a hit, the target must succeed at Fortitude DC 12 or be stunned for one round. A miss spends the use. Constructs, oozes, plants, undead, incorporeal creatures, and creatures immune to critical hits are immune.
- **Shuriken** +1 ranged, 1d2+3 piercing, ×2, range increment 10 ft.

Corven wants to get the last climbers off a cracked landing. He asks the party to guide them down while he clears the stair. If a pursuer reaches him, he tries to stun it long enough for the last climber to pass. [Spine Strike, p. 48]()

## Sera Flintward, lockgate inspector

Lawful neutral Fighter 1. HP 12, AC 17 (touch 11, flat-footed 16), speed 30 ft., initiative +1. Fort +4, Ref +1, Will +1. Climb +5, Intimidate +6.

- **Longsword** +4 melee, 1d8+3 slashing, 19–20/×2. With Power Attack −1 and Cobalt Power invested, +3 for 1d8+5. One extra point of damage comes from Power Attack and one from Cobalt Power.
- **Arm spine** +4 melee in place of the longsword, 1d6+3 piercing, ×2. A spine attack on the sword arm keeps her shield bonus. Using the shield arm drops AC to 15 and flat-footed AC to 14 until her next turn.
- **Javelin** +2 ranged, 1d6+3 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft.
- **Cobalt Power** holds her one point of essentia for 24 hours. It also gives +1 insight on the attack rolls or ability checks she makes to bull rush, overrun, or sunder.

Sera is holding the lockgate while the crew reaches the jammed chain. She can show the party the dry route from the bank. She keeps the shield up with people in the lane and can switch to a spine attack if she loses the sword. [Arm spine and shield rules, p. 15]() · [Cobalt Power, p. 37]()
