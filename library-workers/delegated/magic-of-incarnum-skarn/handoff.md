# Skarn for D&D 3.5

The first printing of *Magic of Incarnum* gives Skarn a 1d6 piercing attack with one arm’s spines per round. Attacking with the shield arm removes that shield’s AC bonus until the start of the next turn. An arms chakra bind makes the spine attacks lawful for overcoming damage reduction. [Original traits, p. 15]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Corven Ashstep**, Monk 1, uses Spine Strike for a two-attack flurry and can deliver Stunning Fist with a spine strike.
- **Sera Flintward**, Fighter 1, uses Cobalt Power to add damage when she takes the Power Attack penalty. A spine attack with the sword arm lets her keep her shield bonus. Using the shield arm drops her AC from 17 to 15.

Both encounters are provisional CR 1. The portrait depicts Corven on plain tan parchment. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Parchment comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [96 px preview](portrait-thumbnails.jpg)

The temporary copy passed species search by name, racial traits, and both NPC roles. Both encounter commands returned the full builds. The new Skarn profile supplies its default portrait and browse thumbnail. The Skarn patch also applies after the separate Rilkan patch.
