# Skarn source and review notes

The September 2005 first printing of *Magic of Incarnum*, ISBN 0-7869-3701-7, gives the playable traits on p. 15. Its description puts distinct rows of spines on the fronts of both forearms, the backs of both calves, and the upper back. Mark Poole illustrated the generic pair on p. 17. Wayne England illustrated a Skarn monk on p. 47. [Original traits]() · [Generic pair]() · [Skarn monk]()

The p. 192 sample Monk 3 uses two spine attacks in its flurry. It prints a 1d8 spine die even though Spine Strike on p. 48 first grants 1d8 at Monk 4. Corven follows the class feature. The p. 193 sample Incarnate declares four essentia but lists five invested, and its feet-bind heading conflicts with the hands bind in the text. The racial spine benefit specifically requires an arms bind. [Spine Strike]() · [Published profiles]()

An independent integration review checked the patch, publication scope, skill budgets, shield AC, Cobalt Power damage, and the Monk flurry and Stunning Fist. A separate temporary fixture checked that Rilkan and Skarn could be applied in sequence.
