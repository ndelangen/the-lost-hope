# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual bat source check

TSR’s *Monstrous Manual*, product 2140, first appeared in 1993. The inspected scan’s copyright page identifies it as the January 1994 third printing. The Bat table and the Common and Large descriptions are on printed p. 15 (viewer page 18). The Mobat description starts on p. 15 and its combat rules and the family illustration are on p. 16 (viewer page 19). The reflowed electronic edition puts the table and these rules on pp. 70–73. ## Bat, Common column

- Any land, common, swarm, nocturnal, omnivore, Animal (1), treasure nil, neutral, 1–100 appearing. AC 8, or AC 4 under ideal flying conditions. Movement 1 and Fl 24 (maneuverability B). The Hit Dice row specifies **1–2 hp**. THAC0 20, one attack for one damage, special attack refers to the prose, special defense and magic resistance nil, Tiny (1 ft.), morale unreliable **2–4** in print and 15 XP. The reflowed edition gives morale 2–5. The publisher’s rules preview also defines Unreliable as 2–4.
- A bat attacks when cornered. Startled bats fly about in confusion. The swarm has a 1% chance per bat encountered per round to extinguish torches. Casting requires a Wisdom roll. The text describes worse weapon use as a −2 THAC0 penalty. It does not give a summed HP pool or replace the individual bite with a group attack. The wording describes a hindrance even though a lower numerical THAC0 normally improves an attack.
- The description gives a small, mouse-like body with membranes stretched across the forelimbs, echolocation in darkness, and roosts in caves, dark buildings and damp crevices. It gives no coat color.

## Giant Bat, Large column

- Any land, uncommon, flock, nocturnal, omnivore, Animal (1), treasure nil, neutral, 3–18 appearing. AC 8, movement 3 and Fl 18 (C). The Hit Dice row gives **1d4 hp or 1 HD**. Only 10% are the more powerful 1 HD form. The table prints THAC0 **19 or 20** without explicitly pairing the numbers with the two forms. The prose gives 1d2 bite damage for a typical bat and 1d4 bite damage for the larger one. One attack, special attack nil, special defense as described below, magic resistance nil, Medium (5–6 ft. wingspan), morale unsteady (5–7), 35 XP.
- While airborne it receives a three-point AC bonus against missiles fired by opponents of Dexterity 13 or less. With descending AC that is effectively AC 5. It must land to bite and usually lands on its target.
- The adventure-game disease rule names rabies. A bite has a 1% infection chance per damage point. The fictional incubation is 1d4+6 days, followed by ten days before death without a listed magical cure. The afflicted character cannot eat or drink. The DM chooses provocation triggers. When provoked, a failed Wisdom check makes the character attack until killed or knocked unconscious. Listed magical cures are *wish*, *alter reality*, *limited wish*, *cure disease* or something similar.
- The prose gives a three-foot body, a five- to six-foot wingspan, dark cavern roosting and sonar use in flight. The prose likens it to the carnivorous variety of the Common Bat. The Large Bat table itself says omnivore, and the form has no specified coat color. [Printed p. 15](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bdd98c9a7333e11f12af09bcf01273ad.webp)

## Mobat, Huge column

- Warm caves, rare, flock, nocturnal, omnivore, Low (5–7) intelligence, treasure C, neutral evil, 1–8 appearing. Movement 3 and Fl 15 (C), one bite for 2d4 damage, special attacks and defenses refer to the prose, magic resistance nil, Huge (12–16 ft. wingspan), morale steady (11–12). The 4, 5 and 6 HD variants give THAC0 17, 15 and 15, respectively, and XP 420, 650 and 975.
- AC 2 when mobile, AC 7 in crowded flight and AC 10 when grounded. Fast, silent flight imposes −3 on victims’ surprise rolls. It uses a piercing screech if prey resists. The radius is 20 feet, and a failed save vs. paralyzation makes a victim cover its ears instead of fighting. The source gives no duration or daily cap.
- It favors warm-blooded prey, uses its fangs, is attracted to shiny objects and needs room to launch. The table diet stays omnivore. The prose assigns no coat color. ## Illustration and catalog

The p. 16 picture shows an unlabeled pale brown bat with a pink cast to its spread membranes, large ears and visible fangs. No reliable signature or individual credit identifies its artist, and the layout does not assign the picture to any one form. It is a family illustration and does not establish a species palette. [Printed art](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/4ef87b704dca99fd16240b23494fd32e.webp) · [Print credits](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/efaf128f9ea2ed636f64e32847d3e254.webp)

The primary catalog has Bat and Giant Bat in other editions, with no exact TSR 2140 collisions and no exact Mobat entry. Independently staged matching names include the Revised v3.5 SRD Bat, Black Flag v0.2 Giant Bat, and the 1992 *Night of the Walking Dead* Giant Bat. The last is an adventure profile with its own values and flying missile wording. The Bat table’s three animal forms use separate source keys. Their inclusion in the cross-edition Beast category leaves the Mobat’s printed intelligence, alignment and screech intact.
