# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual bats

The Common Bat, Large Bat (Giant Bat), and Huge Bat (Mobat) come from pages 15–16 of the January 1994 third printing of TSR’s *Monstrous Manual*. The catalog had Bat and Giant Bat from other publications. The 1992 *Night of the Walking Dead* Giant Bat remains a separate adventure profile. Mobat adds a new catalog entry. [Printed page 15](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bdd98c9a7333e11f12af09bcf01273ad.webp) · [Printed page 16](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/4ef87b704dca99fd16240b23494fd32e.webp) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Printed HP or Hit Dice | Rules preserved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bat | 1–2 HP | AC 4 in ideal flight and the printed swarm rules. The paper table gives morale 2–4. The electronic edition gives 2–5. |
| Giant Bat | 1d4 HP or 1 HD | Must land to bite. In flight it gains a 3-point Armor Class bonus against missiles fired by attackers with Dexterity 13 or less. The print lists THAC0 19 or 20 without assigning the values to the two variants. |
| Mobat | 4–6 HD | AC 2 in open flight, 7 in cramped flight, and 10 on the ground. Each HD value keeps its printed THAC0 and XP. Its screech affects a 20-foot radius. |

The three new portraits use plain parchment and show the entire creature. The Common Bat is small and mouse-like, the Giant Bat banks with darker wings, and the Mobat has a broad chest and visible fangs. The printed family illustration has no confirmed artist attribution or form label.
