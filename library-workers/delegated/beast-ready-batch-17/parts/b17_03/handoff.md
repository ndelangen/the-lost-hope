# Batch 17, part b17_03

I checked all five entries against the first printings. Each uses a base block named in the adventure. The overlays keep the encounter changes and record the inherited block separately. [Avernus folio 50](https://online.anyflip.com/uggd/evkp/mobile/index.html#p=51) · [Avernus folio 95](https://online.anyflip.com/uggd/evkp/mobile/index.html#p=96) · [Avernus folio 195](https://online.anyflip.com/uggd/evkp/mobile/index.html#p=196) · [Acquisitions Incorporated, pp. 93–94 and 185–187]() · [2014 base rules]()

- The Hellwasp Grub uses Giant Centipede. The Peacock uses Vulture. Traxigor has the Archmage's statistics with Tiny Beast, Strength 3, six named languages, and Plane Shift in place of Teleport.
- The Enormous Tentacle uses Giant Constrictor Snake with room-wide reach and no Bite. Its source describes the grapple's stages and leaves the creature beyond the rift unidentified.
- Onyx is Huge from the shrunken party's perspective. I kept the explicitly printed 200-ft. climb speed and the rules for avoiding or distracting her.

I reviewed all five 1254 px portraits in square and round crops. The grub, peacock, robed otter and tentacle have different anatomy or dress from their rules proxies. Onyx uses the existing staged cat as the basis for a warm brown coat and blue eyes. All five art files use built-in ImageGen, with prompts and hashes recorded alongside them.
