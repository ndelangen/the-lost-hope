# Wildemount and Radiant Citadel Beasts

Wildemount p. 295 prints both Moorbounders as Large Beasts. They have the same armor, hit points, speed, and damage as the versions staged from *Call of the Netherdeep*. Wildemount calls the attack Claws. *Netherdeep* calls it Claw. Both versions reuse the matching B14 portrait for each beast. [Wildemount p. 295](https://fliphtml5.com/DnD5E/Explorers-Guide-To-Wildemount/Explorers_Guide_To_Wildemount/296/)

Old Croaker uses the 2014 Giant Toad with cold damage immunity. The DC 15 handling check and the option for Old Croaker to ferry characters belong to the pool encounter. The giant toad portrait fits the description of this individual. [Frozen Sick](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/wa/frozen-sick) · [Old Croaker](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/744327-old-croaker)

Radiant Citadel p. 26 assigns the Giant Sea Horse block to the Koi Prawn. The four prawns defend themselves if damaged and have half cover against attackers outside the tank water. The violet shell, cyan markings, spines, and slender legs in the new portrait follow the book's illustration. Giant Sea Horse and Giant Crayfish artwork depict different animals. [Printed adventure](https://www.scribd.com/document/617503780/qpbh) · [Publisher illustration used for anatomy and color](https://www.thefandomentals.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Journeys-Through-the-Radiant-Citadel-ART8.jpeg)

The Rooster on p. 206 uses Hawk stats with the flying speed removed. Its reused image comes from the Rooster Heraldic Servant art index and depicts an ordinary rooster on warmer parchment. This batch stages only the Radiant Citadel Beast rules for that image.

I checked all four reused portrait hashes and compared the new ImageGen prawn with Big Linda and the reused images at square and round sizes. The prawn's mouth, legs, tail, and antennae fit the round crop.
