# Beast batch 17, part 01

Four *Turn of Fortune's Wheel* profiles and two *Glory of the Giants* profiles are staged. I checked the October and August 2023 first printings respectively, along with the 2014 *Monster Manual* for the four inherited stat blocks. [Wheel first printing](https://www.scribd.com/document/734156838/Turn-of-Fortune-s-Wheel) · [Glory of the Giants scan](https://online.anyflip.com/fmtsi/jmxv/mobile/index.html#p=176)

- Ebonclaw has Intelligence 12 and Charisma 12. Parvaz has Intelligence 16 and Charisma 14. Both speak Common. [Ebonclaw and Parvaz, digital layout](https://online.anyflip.com/kqwdv/igfp/files/large/4e9fb116b3861fcba9f1127fa7488acb.webp)
- Ophelia's passive Perception 12 derives from her Wisdom 14. The 2018 *Monster Manual* errata predates the adventure and gives the base Elephant's attacks their +6 damage modifier. The first *Monster Manual* printing used +5. [Official Elephant erratum](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)
- The ordinary whirlwyrms use Giant Crocodile rules and look like giant eels. The separate elder uses Behir rules and is outside this Beast batch. [Whirlwyrm Attack, digital layout](https://online.anyflip.com/kqwdv/igfp/files/large/b19c2226bd07fb8e24c2bc5886acbddb.webp)

Ebonclaw, Ophelia, and the Whirlwyrm reuse matching portraits. The new art shows Parvaz as an albatross, the Spotted Lion with charcoal flecks in its coat and mane, and the Titanothere with folded hide and forked facial horns. The square view preserves their full silhouettes. The circular preview clips the tips of Parvaz's extended wings and touches the Titanothere's outer horn.
