# Beast readiness batch 17, part 05

Three publication versions match the saved roster.

- Szikzith’s named block appears on sheet 6 of the original *A Web of Lies* . She is a neutral Medium Beast with her own bite and ability scores. The encounter has her negotiate even though the block lists no language. The new portrait uses the round, honey-fed body described on sheet 5 and the token’s reddish brown. [Publisher mirror]()
- The twelve Sled Dogs on p. 27 of *The Rise of Tiamat* use the Wolf block, which p. 26 of the October 2014 online supplement supplies. The dogs’ barking alerts Oyaviggaton. The new black-and-white dog uses the publication token’s coat and harness. [Adventure facsimile](https://anyflip.com/kpthp/zqtr/basic) · [Official supplement]()
- The digital *Welcome to the Hellfire Club* appendix supplies the Giant Spider’s CR 1 block for this catalog scope. Its Web is a DC 13 Dexterity save. Itsy and Bitsy use the same portrait as the earlier Giant Spider. The original-content export identifies the appendix but gives no verified print folio. [Official index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/wthc) · [Digital rules export](https://www.scribd.com/document/981259652/Stranger-Things-Welcome-to-the-Hellfire-Club)

Both built-in ImageGen portraits are 1254 px square on plain tan parchment. I checked each by eye in square and circular crops. The round crop trims the outermost ends of a couple of Szikzith’s legs. The face and rounded abdomen remain clear. [Portrait review](portrait-review.jpg) · [Image prompts](portrait-prompts.json)
