# Beast readiness batch 17

Batch 17 stages 27 verified Beast publication versions against archive `cc052c5`. The requested batch size was 50. The remaining indexed candidates comprise 43 source holds and one SRD Giant Insect already staged separately. [Roster](roster.tsv) · [Manifest and source evidence](manifest.json) · [Source gaps](source-gaps.tsv) · [Rules patch](rules.patch) · [Portrait index](portrait-index-preview.json)

The publisher’s public materials identify the 21 *Monster Vault 2* and 18 *Humblewood Tales* candidates without providing their complete stat blocks. The other holds are Ghamoo-Ra, Spiderfrog, the final-printing Swarm of Contaminated Insects, and the *Dark Pyramid of Sorcerer’s Isle* Boar, whose appendix omits its attack. [Kobold Press preview]() · [Humblewood Tales contents](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/hwt)

The batch reuses 10 existing portrait files and adds 15 plain-parchment illustrations. The two sword spiders share one image, and the two Ravenloft swarms share a study of larvae and winged insects. The Koi Prawn portrait follows the original’s violet-and-blue coloring and keeps the published scene in the Library carousel. Parvaz’s wing tips and a few of Szikzith’s outer legs are trimmed in round crops. [Koi Prawn before and after](koi-prawn-before-after.jpg) · [Big Linda style comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Contact sheet 1](portraits-1.jpg) · [Contact sheet 2](portraits-2.jpg)

The original *Radiant Citadel* encounter uses Giant Sea Horse combat rules for the Koi Prawn and ends the challenge when a prawn reduces a character to 0 HP. Ebonclaw’s printed Intelligence and Charisma are both 12. The Ravager’s Multiattack refers to an undefined Fear aura, which is recorded as a source omission. [Koi Prawn part](parts/b17_02/handoff.md) · [Ebonclaw part](parts/b17_01/handoff.md) · [Ravager part](parts/b17_04/handoff.md)

In a temporary Library copy, I found the Koi Prawn and the Pandarian Dragon Turtle, checked their rules and square portraits, and switched the Bristled Moorbounder between Wildemount and Netherdeep. The console showed no warnings or errors. An independent reviewer checked all 27 installed scopes, the portrait keys and hashes, and the unchanged catalog TSV and pre-existing rules. The rules patch and portrait assets are staged separately. This batch made no tracked edits to the primary checkout and no changes to R2.
