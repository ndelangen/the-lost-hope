# Gray Orc encounter cards

## Maura Stonewick

Cleric 2. Provisional CR 2, ECL 3. AC 15, HP 16, initiative +1, speed 40 ft. Fort +5, Ref +1, Will +6. Scent.

- Light mace +3, 1d6+2 bludgeoning. Light Sensitivity reduces attacks by 1 in bright sunlight or within a daylight spell.
- Bless and shield of faith last 2 minutes. Cure light wounds heals 1d8+3. Domain magic stone gives up to three shots, +3 to hit and 1d6+1 damage each, or 2d6+2 against undead.
- Rebuke or command undead 3/day. Earth domain grants a separate pool of three attempts against air or earth creatures.

Maura asks the party to help an injured porter get inside the wayhouse.

## Keshen Trailmark

Ranger 1. Provisional CR 1, ECL 2. AC 15, HP 9, initiative +2, speed 40 ft. Fort +3, Ref +4, Will +2. Scent.

- Greataxe +4, 1d12+4 slashing. Longbow +3, 1d8 piercing, 100-ft. range increment. Both have ×3 criticals. Add 2 damage against animals. Apply the Light Sensitivity penalty when appropriate.
- Track with Survival +6, or +8 against animals. A fresh scent trail typically starts at DC 10.

Keshen pauses where the trail turns into the wind and marks a path to the crossing. [Complete builds](npc-builds.json)
