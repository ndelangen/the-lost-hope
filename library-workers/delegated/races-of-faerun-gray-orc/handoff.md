# Gray Orc

This batch adds the playable Gray Orc from the March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn*. The 3.5 adaptation keeps the printed 40-ft. land speed, Scent, and attack-only Light Sensitivity. [Original player rules]()

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species, Maura Stonewick, and Keshen Trailmark. It is based on catalog commit `20a2f23`. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) maps Maura's [square portrait](portraits/gray-orc-draft.png) to the 2003 profile.

Manual review covered the rules, both builds, and the [portrait thumbnail](portrait-thumbnails.jpg). Search, in-app portrait loading, and automated verification are deferred.
