# Gray Orc sources

The March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn* gives the Gray Orc on printed pp. 65–67. The viewer is one page ahead. The player list on p. 66 gives 40-ft. land speed, Scent, and proficiency with the greataxe and longbow. Light Sensitivity applies −1 to attack rolls in bright sunlight or within a daylight spell. Orc Blood makes the Gray Orc count as an orc for racial effects. The printed trait list omits darkvision. Favored class is Cleric and level adjustment is +1, with no racial Hit Dice. The 3.5 Scent rule uses a 30-ft. base detection range, adjusted for the source's position relative to the wind. A move action gives direction, and the creature pinpoints a source within 5 ft. Keshen uses Scent with Track to follow a trail, with a typical fresh-trail DC of 10 plus 2 per hour. [Scent](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#scent) · [Track](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/feats.htm#track)

Maura is a neutral evil Cleric with Luthic's Earth and Healing domains. Healing adds one caster level to cure light wounds. Earth supplies magic stone and its own pool of turning or rebuking attempts. Maura rebukes undead and converts prepared non-domain spells to inflict spells. Both builds' CRs are provisional.
