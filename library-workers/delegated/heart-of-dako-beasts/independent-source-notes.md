# The Heart of Dako beast source review

Hit Point Press released *Humblewood: The Heart of Dako* for Free RPG Day 2021. The copyright page (booklet p. 3) identifies the first printing and copyright year 2021. The free publisher downloads are `` and ``, for the 2014 fifth edition rules. Booklet page references below give both the printed number and the one-based viewer page. I checked the two typeset blocks against their duplex card fronts and backs. [Official booklet and reference cards]() · [Publisher’s Free RPG Day listing]()

## Kappu

- Printed p. 24 (booklet p. 25) and card pp. 7–8. Large unaligned Beast, CR 1/4 (50 XP), AC 11, HP 13 (2d10 + 2), speed 60 ft., swim 30 ft. STR 17, DEX 12, CON 12, INT 4, WIS 10, CHA 6. Both print Perception +2 and passive Perception 10. Preserve those values as printed.
- Keen Hearing grants advantage on hearing-based Wisdom (Perception) checks. Bite is +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target, and deals 8 (2d4 + 3) piercing damage. Olivia Rea’s card front shows a stocky tawny rodent with a broad blunt muzzle, rounded ears, thick tapering tail, and riding tack. The booklet describes the tail as furred and useful for propulsion while swimming and balance while running, and places the animal in the marshy rainforests of the Tanglewilds.
- On printed p. 7 (p. 8), four grazing Kappus can be tamed as mounts with a DC 14 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check. On printed p. 8 (p. 9), the party’s Kappus refuse to cross the 5-ft.-wide Redroar rope bridge. The Companions’ mounts are too large to cross safely and become hard to tie up when the nearby fight disturbs them. That behavior belongs to the bridge encounter. ## Swarm of Tanglewasps

- Printed p. 25 (booklet p. 26) and card pp. 9–10. Medium swarm of Tiny Beasts, unaligned, CR 1/2 (100 XP), AC 11, HP 22 (5d8), and speed 30 ft. Both blocks print a single speed. STR 3, DEX 13, CON 10, INT 1, WIS 7, CHA 1. Blindsight 10 ft. and passive Perception 8. Damage resistances are bludgeoning, piercing, slashing and fire. Condition immunities are charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained and stunned.
- Swarm permits shared spaces and passage through gaps sized for a Tiny insect, and prevents hit point recovery and temporary hit point gain. Sting is +3 to hit, reach 0 ft., and can hit one target in its space. It deals 10 (4d4) poison damage, reduced to 5 (2d4) when the swarm has half its hit points or fewer.
- Wrong Step describes a camouflaged 1-ft. mud sphere occupying a 5-ft. space. Spotting a nest takes a DC 17 Wisdom (Perception) check. Stepping on it calls for a DC 14 Strength save. On failure the nest grapples the target (escape DC 14) and deals 5 (2d4) poison damage at the start of each of the target’s turns while grappled. The nest holds one target at a time. Another creature in reach can spend its action and succeed on DC 14 Strength (Athletics) to free the target. The p. 7 jungle encounter (p. 8) separately calls for a DC 17 group Wisdom (Perception) check to avoid disturbing 2d6 swarms.
- The wasps are native to the Tanglewilds rainforests. Their hardened mud nests sink into wet ground and blend in. Julia Metzger’s illustrations show blue-green heads and thoraxes, magenta abdomens, and iridescent wings. [Official booklet and reference cards]()

The booklet also prints Crocodile and Panther as Zenja’s Wild Shape options on p. 26 (p. 27). The Feliad Stalker is a Monstrosity. [Official booklet and reference cards]()
