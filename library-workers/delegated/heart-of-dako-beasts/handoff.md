# The Heart of Dako beasts

This separate bundle adds Kappu and Swarm of Tanglewasps from Hit Point Press's 2021 first printing. The booklet and the matching reference cards agree on both profiles, including the challenge ratings, movement, and action details. [Official booklet and reference cards]() · [Exact pages and file hashes](source-evidence.json) · [Independent source check](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

- **Kappu, CR 1/4.** Booklet p. 24 and card p. 8. The card prints 60 ft. walking and 30 ft. swimming. It also prints Perception +2 and passive Perception 10. The Redroar River rope bridge refusal and the night encounter's taming check are table notes tied to the adventure.
- **Swarm of Tanglewasps, CR 1/2.** Booklet p. 25 and card p. 10. The block prints a single 30-ft. speed. The profile preserves the nest's detection, grapple, and rescue checks, the poison damage timing, and Sting's lower damage at half HP or fewer. The separate jungle travel check is in the table notes.

Both parchment drafts were made with built-in ImageGen. The original Kappu card credits Olivia Rea, and the original Tanglewasps card credits Julia Metzger.
