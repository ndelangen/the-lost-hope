# AD&D 2e piranhas

The January 1994 third printing of the *Monstrous Manual* supplies Piranha, Giant Piranha, and Quipper. The first and third join their existing catalog entries as AD&D 2e versions. Giant Piranha gets its own entry. [Printed table, p. 117](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bf2949b86e3fe804af0c021045ab0d44.webp) · [Printed descriptions, p. 118](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/981f6c8811613d3c1f2b6bf18210924c.webp)

Giant Piranha uses THAC0 19 from the printed table. The reflowed gives 16. The giant shares the ordinary school's frenzy, caps simultaneous attackers at ten per human-sized target, and can leap up to 10 feet once per round. Ordinary schools cap at twenty. The Quipper paragraph identifies a cold-water freshwater piranha and leaves frenzy implicit, so the Quipper profile gives the printed bite and flags the gap.
