# Dark Pyramid Boar and Spiderfrog rules holds

**Boar** · `beast-5d790795330b9003623dd05d` · `4c3222a3e9c8f630a3adfa8a` · 2014 fifth edition

The two local copies of *Dark Pyramid of Sorcerer's Isle* have the same SHA-256, `18edc7399ddea0f747c18b6488969a7100476d41db7eb937147bdb39cd4a8043`. Page 12 describes the mutated boars. Page 33 lists their stats, Charge, and Relentless. Charge refers to Tusk, the only Tusk reference in the adventure. The printed block ends after Relentless. Page 2 asks DMs to have the Basic Rules or Player's Handbook on hand as general preparation. The Boar entry itself gives no instruction to use an external block. The missing evidence is a corrected p. 33 or official direction to use a specific Tusk action. [Local adventure ](< - 5th Edition (2014)/Adventures/DDEX1-11 - Dark Pyramid of Sorcerer's >) · [Official adventure index]()

All printed stats and traits agree with the 2014 Basic Rules Boar. That Boar's Tusk has a +3 attack bonus, 5-foot reach, and 4 (1d6 + 1) slashing damage. It remains a table fallback under the Basic Rules printing. [Basic Rules Boar, p. 120](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16812-boar)

The full block requires access to the book's indexed p. 33 or an official reprint with that block. Secondary transcriptions surfaced in the search and are insufficient for exact-printing verification.
