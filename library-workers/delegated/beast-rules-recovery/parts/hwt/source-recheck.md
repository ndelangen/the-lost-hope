# Humblewood Tales source recheck

All 18 frozen *Humblewood Tales* Beast profiles still need their complete Appendix A blocks from the collected 2014-compatible edition. D&D Beyond publishes the exact section links and catalog metadata. The bestiary pages redirect to the marketplace without access. The collected folios remain unverified. [Official contents](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/hwt) · [Publisher v.18](https://hitpointpress.itch.io/humblewood-tales)

The publisher identified a full editing review after the two earlier backer releases. Its March 2023 update announced that the final bookmarked copies were available in backer accounts. The v.18 download, matching hardcover, or licensed digital Appendix A can provide exact rules. Hit Point Press also distributes a Shard Tabletop edition with the sourcebook and 110 monster records, which offers another route if already licensed. [Editing and distribution update](https://hitpointpress.com/blogs/hot-off-the-press/humblewood-tales-november-update-reward-progress) · [Final backer announcement](https://humblewood-tales.backerkit.com/hosted_preorders/project_updates?page=1) · [Licensed Shard edition](https://marketplace.shardtabletop.com/m/product?id=irw6fsjyxbghxge8)
