# Independent checks

The source review confirmed the glide's descent ratio, 5-HD powered flight, Constitution-based endurance, load restriction, and separate footbow rules. At 10 HD, the player text equates flight exertion to walking or running. The published Jenlisa, Chanil, and Miithi examples still list daily fatigue. The 1-HD Youth Pilgrim also lists a fly speed despite the playable threshold and its own description of gliding. The original builds use the playable traits. [Playable traits]() · [Named examples]() · [Youth Pilgrim]()

The footbow can be drawn one- or two-handed while held in the feet. The two-handed Strength bonus is capped at one and a half times the bow's Strength rating. The ground rule requires prone posture and imposes a −4 penalty. The dive follows the melee charge rules. The catalog review confirmed Avilli's Expert ranks and Tarinel's Fighter feats, flight duration, piercing dive, and footbow totals. The summary now explicitly grants the dive and straight-line flight run from 5 Hit Dice. After adjusting the patch insertion point, both Raptoran and Killoren applied in either order in temporary files.
