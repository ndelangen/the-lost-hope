# Raptoran encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Raptoran subtype, low-light vision, and a 30-ft. land speed. Their glides have 40-ft. speed and average maneuverability, with 20 ft. of forward travel for each 5 ft. of descent. Gliding prevents falling damage when available, cannot hover, and requires a light load. A helpless or unconscious Raptoran in midair descends in a corkscrew and takes 1d6 falling damage. ## Avilli, ropeway rigger

A ropeway is down and travellers are approaching the broken span. Avilli can glide a light messenger line from the upper landing if the party holds the near anchor. **Provisional CR 1/2.**

Climb +7, Jump +15, Spot +6, and Use Rope +9. Gear includes leather armor, a light crossbow, a dagger, and a light messenger line. Her Strength 12 light load is up to 43 lb. She is a 1-HD glider and needs height to cross the gap.

- Dagger +1 melee, 1d4+1 piercing or slashing, critical 19–20/×2.
- Light crossbow +2 ranged, 1d8 piercing, critical 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment. Reload as a move action.

## Tarinel, beacon sentry

Tarinel needs to light a beacon on the far platform before the pass closes. He can use a short powered bout and then glide to a lower perch if the party clears the landing. **Provisional CR 5.**

Climb +10, Jump +18, Spot +3. Chain shirt, masterwork +2 Strength-rated footbow, and shortspear. His Strength 14 light load is up to 58 lb.

- **Flight**. At 5 HD and Constitution 14, fly 40 ft. with average maneuverability for two safe consecutive rounds. He can push to four rounds and becomes fatigued at the end of that flight. More than ten minutes of powered flight in a day also causes fatigue. Glide between bouts. Powered flight requires a light load and freedom from fatigue or exhaustion.
- **Footbow** +10 ranged, 110-ft. increment, critical ×3. Damage is 1d8+4 with one hand drawing or 1d8+5 with both. Within 30 ft., add +1 to attack and damage. Precise Shot removes the usual penalty for shooting at a target engaged in melee.
- **Ground footbow**. Shoot prone at −4. A Rapid Shot full attack from a stable prone firing position gives +4/+4 beyond 30 ft., or +5/+5 within 30 ft.
- **Shortspear** +7 melee wielded two-handed, 1d6+3 piercing, critical ×2, 5-ft. reach. A dive is a charge at +9 melee after moving at least 30 ft. and descending at least 10 ft. It deals double damage on a hit.
