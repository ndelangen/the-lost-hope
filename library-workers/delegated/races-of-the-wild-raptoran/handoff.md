# Raptoran for D&D 3.5

One playable Raptoran entry from the February 2005 first printing of *Races of the Wild*. The profile tracks gliding at low Hit Dice, powered flight from 5 HD, and the 10-HD change in exertion. The full-body plain-parchment portrait depicts Avilli, an original glider with feathered wings and gripping talons. [Playable traits, p. 68]() · [Species rules](book-species.json) · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg)

The playable traits say that flight at 10 HD takes no more exertion than walking or running. Several named encounter blocks at 10 HD or higher still retain daily fatigue after ten minutes. The source evidence preserves that discrepancy. The two original builds follow the playable thresholds, including gliding only for the 1-HD NPC. [Named encounters, pp. 83–87]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

**Avilli** is a ropeway rigger, Expert 1, provisional CR 1/2. **Tarinel** is a beacon sentry, Fighter 5, provisional CR 5. His card gives powered-flight endurance at Constitution 14, footbow attacks from the air or prone on the ground, and a piercing melee dive.
