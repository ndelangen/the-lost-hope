# Quaggoth

This batch adds the playable Quaggoth from the 2003 *Underdark*, using the original *Monsters of Faerûn* entry with its official 2004 conversion. Rusk Chalkback and Borr Sootmane are original NPCs. Rusk’s portrait shows a beast-follower with undyed white fur against plain parchment.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and both encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/quaggoth-draft.png). The [comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Rusk beside Big Linda.

In a temporary copy, search found the species by name, rage, scent, and both NPC roles. The portrait loaded for the 2003 profile and as the species thumbnail. The existing Quaggoth monster entry, including its versions and portraits, matched the baseline. [Verification](verification.json)
