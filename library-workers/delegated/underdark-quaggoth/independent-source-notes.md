# Quaggoth rules sources

- *Underdark* gives the playable Quaggoth three racial Hit Dice, LA +2, ECL 5 before class levels, and favored class Barbarian. It refers to the 2001 *Monsters of Faerûn* entry. The official 2004 *Monster Update* converts that stat block to 3.5, updates its skills and feats, and adds darkvision 60 ft. [Underdark, pp. 19–20]() · [Monsters of Faerûn, p. 75]() · [Monster Update, p. 6]()
- The ability adjustments are derived from the original stat block using the core monster-as-character rule. The original natural attack routine uses two 1d4 claws and a secondary 1d4 bite before Strength modifiers. Its rage checks for a trigger on the round after combat damage. Beast-followers use a 50% chance and magic-followers use one in six. Rage grants +4 Strength and −4 AC and cannot end voluntarily. [Original stat block, p. 75]() · [Core monster character rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monstersAsRaces.htm) · [Natural weapon damage](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#naturalWeapons)
- The 2007 *Drow of the Underdark* gives its Quaggoth four racial Hit Dice and LA +2, 120-ft. darkvision, poison immunity, light sensitivity, and voluntary rage. Those rules belong to the 2007 profile. The 2001 statement about classed Quaggoths and spellcasting applies to that original entry. [Drow of the Underdark, pp. 136–139]()
- Carl Critchlow illustrated the original Quaggoth. Rusk’s tall build, white fur, and natural claws follow that entry’s description of a beast-follower. Both encounter CRs are provisional estimates. [Description and illustration, p. 75]()
