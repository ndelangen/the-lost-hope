# Quaggoth encounters

Both Quaggoths have a 30-ft. land speed and climb speed, darkvision 60 ft., scent, and fear immunity. Claw damage is piercing and slashing. Bite damage is bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing. Their racial rage can trigger the round after they take combat damage. It grants +4 Strength and −4 AC. The raging Quaggoth fights with claws and bite until it or its opponent dies.

## Rusk Chalkback, shaft guide

**3 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 3.** AC 15, HP 26, initiative +5. Fortitude +4, Reflex +4, Will +4. As a full attack, two claws +8 for 1d4+5 each and a bite +3 for 1d4+2.

Rusk takes 10 with Climb +13 for a result of 23 on the shaft wall. Check for rage the round after he takes damage in combat. On 1–3 on a d6, AC becomes 11, both claws become +10 for 1d4+7 each, and the bite becomes +5 for 1d4+3.

## Borr Sootmane, switchback guard

**Fighter 1 with 3 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 4.** AC 17, HP 35, initiative +5. Fortitude +6, Reflex +4, Will +4. Two-handed club +10 for 1d6+7 bludgeoning. Hide +6 rises to +8 in shadow.

Borr uses his dyed fur to blend into the bend. The round after taking damage in combat, roll a d6. On a 1 he drops the club and rages. AC becomes 13, both claws attack at +11 for 1d4+7 each, and his bite attacks at +6 for 1d4+3.
