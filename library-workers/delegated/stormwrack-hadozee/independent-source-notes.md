# Hadozee source check

The August 2005 first printing says average maneuverability for the 40-ft. glide in the player entry. The Warrior and First Mate on p. 151 and the character summary on p. 152 say poor. Page 152 also lists Dodge as a bonus feat and Profession (sailor) as a class skill. Page 41 omits both. The original builds follow p. 41 and Renn chooses Dodge as his first-level feat. [Player rules]() · [Encounter section]()

The separate Spelljammer errata describes a fifth-edition reaction to a fall of at least 10 ft. The Stormwrack rule uses a glide speed and a descent ratio. Wizards’ 3.5 archive has no Stormwrack errata, and the June 2008 FAQ has no Hadozee entry. Independent review checked the staged attack rolls, skill ranks, and glide examples. [5e Spelljammer errata]() · [3.5 errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP) · [June 2008 FAQ]()

Jim Nelson illustrated the Hadozee ranger on p. 42. The portrait uses the book’s depiction of gliding membranes, brown fur, and a sailor’s harness. Renn’s pose and equipment are original. [Book illustration]()
