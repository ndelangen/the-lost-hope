# Hadozee encounters

Both NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Hadozee subtype, provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. Each walks 30 ft. and can glide at 40 ft. with average maneuverability. A glide needs descent and can travel up to 20 ft. horizontally per 5 ft. descended. Using the membranes to glide prevents falling damage. Both keep their Dexterity bonus to AC while climbing and can climb with one hand occupied. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Renn Farwake, Rogue 1

Renn takes a signal cord up to the catwalk to find the jammed release. Once the cord is fixed, he glides from the damaged landing to the lower platform.

- HP 8, AC 15, initiative +4, Balance +12, Climb +9. Dodge is his first-level feat and raises AC to 16 against his designated opponent while he retains his Dexterity bonus.
- Dagger +1 melee or +4 when thrown, for 1d4+1 damage. Add 1d6 sneak attack against an eligible target. Concealment prevents sneak attack.
- A clear 5-ft. drop allows up to 20 ft. across. A clear 10-ft. drop allows up to 40 ft. across at his glide speed.

## Daro Brightkeel, Fighter 1

Daro carries the shortspear in one hand while climbing to the gantry. He glides 20 ft. to the winch platform 5 ft. below and lowers the line for the crew.

- HP 12, AC 14, initiative +6, Balance +6, Climb +11. Daro keeps his +2 Dexterity bonus to AC on the climb.
- Shortspear +5 melee or +4 thrown for 1d6+3 piercing.
