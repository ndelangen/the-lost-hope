# Seacliff Dwarf source check

The August 2005 first printing gives the variant on p. 44, with LA +0 in the p. 34 table. It inherits the standard dwarf and changes two things. Strong Swimmers adds +2 racial on Swim and doubles the Constitution score used for breath duration. The variant loses the +1 racial attack bonus against orcs and goblinoids. It keeps Stonecunning, Stability, weapon familiarity, the save bonuses, and the dodge bonus against giants. The entry grants no swim speed or water breathing. [Stormwrack, p. 44]() · [Core Dwarf traits](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/races.htm#dwarves)

Stormwrack p. 44 explicitly references Dungeon Master’s Guide p. 304, which uses twice Constitution as the ordinary baseline. Stormwrack p. 11 and the printed Player’s Handbook p. 84 give one round per point of Constitution before action deductions. The SRD Swim entry uses twice Constitution. The cards show both resulting totals and keep the racial rule as a doubled score. Independent review checked both outcomes, the skill budgets, armor, and weapon attacks.
