# Seacliff Dwarf encounters

Both are Medium Humanoids with the Dwarf subtype, provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. Both have darkvision 60 ft., land speed 20 ft., and +2 racial on Swim. Each uses ordinary Swim checks to move through water. When fighting underwater, use a piercing weapon and get firm footing or succeed on the Swim check to avoid an attack penalty. [Full builds](npc-builds.json) · [Underwater combat](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/wilderness.htm#underwaterCombat)

Strong Swimmers doubles the Constitution score used for breath duration. Pick the baseline before the dive. The cited Dungeon Master’s Guide baseline gives 4 × actual Constitution in calm rounds. Stormwrack’s p. 11 procedure gives 2 × actual Constitution and deducts one extra round for a standard action. Once the budget is spent, the first Constitution check is DC 10. Increase the DC by 1 for each prior check. [Stormwrack, pp. 11 and 44]() · [Dungeon Master’s Guide, p. 304]()

## Brott Saltbrace, Fighter 1

Brott dives to free a line in the lower harbor passage. He returns to the landing before fighting with his waraxe and braces on the stone to hold the gate.

- HP 13, AC 13, initiative +1. Fortitude +5, Reflex +1, Will +2. Swim +9.
- Constitution 16 gives 64 calm rounds with the cited Dungeon Master’s Guide baseline or 32 with the Stormwrack procedure.
- Dwarven waraxe +5 for 1d10+3 slashing. Shortspear +4 for 1d6+3 piercing. Treat the waraxe as a martial weapon.
- Stability gives +4 on checks to resist bull rush or trip while Brott stands firmly on the ground.

## Vessa Mere, Rogue 1

Vessa finds a recent seam beside the flooded door. She checks it from the passage and returns to air before trying the release.

- HP 8, AC 15, initiative +3. Fortitude +2, Reflex +5, Will +0. Swim +7.
- Constitution 14 gives 56 calm rounds with the cited Dungeon Master’s Guide baseline or 28 with the Stormwrack procedure.
- Search +9 normally, +11 for unusual stonework. Stonecunning gives her a check within 10 ft. as if actively searching.
- Short sword +1 for 1d6+1 piercing. Add 1d6 sneak attack against an eligible target without concealment.
