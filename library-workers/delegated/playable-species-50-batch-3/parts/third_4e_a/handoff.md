| Species | Printed pages | Linked NPC |

| Eladrin | 38–39 | Irian Ashletter, Wizard 1 |

All five builds use level-one player character math and the June 2008 printing. The viewer numbering is one ahead of the printed pages. The August 2012 rules update adds alternate racial ability increases and changes some attack features. The Elf and Halfling examples use the original Careful Attack damage and Sneak Attack frequency. The Fighter follows the official clarification that an enemy must bear his own mark to trigger Combat Challenge. The Half-Elf's Eyebite also qualifies under the later Dilettante wording.

I checked the class defenses, armor and shield modifiers, healing surges, trained skills and attack powers against the book. The spell courier has two daily spells in her book and prepares Sleep for this encounter. Each example includes the racial feature that matters to its encounter.
