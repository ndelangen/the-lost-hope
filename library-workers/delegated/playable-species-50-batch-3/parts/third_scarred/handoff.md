# Scarred Lands playable profiles

These five profiles use the 2016 fifth-edition *Scarred Lands Player's Guide* by Onyx Path Publishing and Nocturnal Media. Page numbers below are printed numbers. The NPCs are original level 2 class builds. Each concept points to its exact species, publication, edition and build. | Species | Printed pages | Original NPC | AC | HP | Primary attack |

| Asaatthi | 20–21 | Kethsar Dunecoil, Fighter 2 | 18 | 20 | Scimitar +5, 1d6 + 5 slashing |
| Hollow Legionnaire | 30–31 | Merel Ironwake, Fighter 2 | 19 | 20 | Spear +5, 1d6 + 3 piercing |
| Ironbred | 34–35 | Umber Kesh, Fighter 2 | 19 | 22 | Flail +4, 1d8 + 2 bludgeoning |
| Manticora | 36–37, 116–117 | Tessu Claypaw, Fighter 2 | 16 | 18 | Claw gauntlet +5, 1d6 + 3 slashing |
| Slitherin | 40–41 | Veshki Reed-Ring, Rogue 2 | 15 | 19 | Shortsword +5, 1d6 + 3 piercing |

- Kethsar uses the guide's finesse bite as a separate proficient weapon. His default weapon modifier is Dexterity +3. Dueling applies to his scimitar while he carries a shield.
- Merel is a Humanoid bound to starting chain mail. Past-Life Knowledge adds Arcana to the fighter skill choices, and Merel uses one class skill on it.
- Kesh is a redeemed sutak. Tough Hide contributes 2 maximum HP at level 2. Fire Born covers extreme heat and thirst. Kesh travels on foot with the caravan.
- Tessu's 40-ft. movement requires both hands empty. This example treats the equipped claw gauntlet as occupying one hand and starts with it stowed when speed matters.
- The 2016 Slitherin rules give one shared profile and a choice between a Dexterity and Intelligence increase. Veshki picks Dexterity and uses the printed 1d3 finesse bite.

I compared armor, attacks, resource use, skills and ability scores by hand. Fighter fixed HP is 16 + twice Constitution modifier, plus Tough Hide for Kesh. Veshki's rogue HP is 13 + twice Constitution modifier. All character builds have proficiency +2.

The original square PNGs use built-in ImageGen and the plain-parchment treatment from Big Linda. I checked the silhouettes and thumbnail contrast at 96 px.
