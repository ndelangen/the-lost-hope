# Five Savage Species profiles

| Playable profile | Original NPC | Class level | Class Hit Dice | CR | Printed rule |

| Triton | Iril Tidepost | 1 | 1 | 1 | [p. 199, Table A–48]() |

Iril uses the first-level swim speed and carries a shell-tipped guide rod for work. Bral has regeneration 1 and remains Medium. Orren uses the first tier that grants a trumpet and prepares spells as a cleric of level 5. Krev uses the Vrock's 3.0 class resistance, spores and lesser powers. His lower-claw rakes follow the original Monster Manual's airborne description. Sillrun uses the Medium tier. The Water Elemental table ends at level 12 even though the prose refers to level 13 and discusses damage reduction. The source note records that discrepancy.
