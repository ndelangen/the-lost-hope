The viewers count the cover. Each link opens one page past the number printed in the book.

I built all five NPCs as level 1 characters. Maris uses the 2008 minor-action Infernal Wrath. Dera uses the original Strength and Dexterity bonuses. Pell uses the printed once-per-round Sneak Attack. The *Monster Manual* recommends DM permission for player Goblins and Orcs.

The encounter records include regular and bloodied HP, healing surges, defenses, and power triggers. I checked attacks, damage, and defenses against the printed class and equipment rules. Korr uses Fast Runner with Running Charge. His charge moves up to 10 squares, and his Warrior’s Surge is its own standard action.

I checked each square PNG at full size and at 96 pixels against Big Linda. The proof sheet shows Big Linda first, then the five profiles in table order. [Portrait comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [96-pixel thumbnails](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

All five profiles, linked NPCs, source notes, and portraits are staged locally. Review was manual.
