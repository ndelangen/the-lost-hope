# Revised Grim Hollow heritages

Each example uses its heritage's eight traditional traits and the 2024 core class rules. The 2025 printing credits SRD 5.2.1. Printed folios fall four pages before their flipbook viewer numbers. [Revised player guide](https://fliphtml5.com/lxnee/xhim/GH2_Players_guide__Digital_Edition/) · [Digital edition](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/ghpg) · [2024 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes)

| Profile | Printed page | NPC | Encounter use |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Dhampir | 25 | Mara Vell, Fighter 2 | Gains temporary HP on fang hits and uses a climbing route to get away. |
| Downcast | 27 | Adra Senn, Cleric 2 | Spends a Hit Die to boost a nearby ally's heal. Touch of Life gives necrotic resistance. |
| Wulven | 29 | Kellan Rook, Ranger 2 | Reaches a stranded scout with Burst of Speed and supports an ally with Pack Hunter. |

The related heritage traits are on pp. 30–47. Mara and Kellan use Strength for the natural attack roll and damage. Natural Attack expressly offers Dexterity for damage. Mara has the base Draining Attack, which gives temporary HP on every qualifying hit. The proficiency-bonus use limit is in the description of the To the Dregs upgrade. Adra's portrait shows travel clothes for a roadside stop. The encounter AC assumes scale mail and shield.
