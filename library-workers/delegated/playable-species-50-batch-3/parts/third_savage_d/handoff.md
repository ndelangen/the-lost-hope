# Five Savage Species profiles

| Profile | Original NPC | Class level | HD | CR |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mummy | Nesek Reedseal, sarcophagus guard | 10 | 6 | 3 |
| Ogre | Rodd Weirpole, bridge steward | 5 | 4 | 2 |
| Stone Giant | Ulden Screeline, quarry lookout | 6 | 5 | 3 |
| Succubus | Vessa Ashscript, treaty broker | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Treant | Bramblewake, orchard boundary keeper | 8 | 5 | 5 |

I checked these levels against the printed player tables and worked the attack, save, skill, and defense totals by hand. The Mummy takes its rot details and the Ogre takes its specific club damage and face from the original 3.0 Monster Manual. The Stone Giant can throw and catch rocks while still Medium. The Succubus can use Suggestion once daily, with three chosen humanoid forms. Its entry uses the sorcerer spell level for the Suggestion DC. [Savage Species, Appendix 1]() · [Original 3.0 SRD](https://www.dragon.ee/30srd/)

I used the printed progression where the Ogre introduction disagrees with the table and where the Treant table grants a feat omitted by the prose. The Treant progression also leaves its slam dice unspecified. The build scales the original Huge treant's 2d6 slam to 1d8 for Large and keeps the paired full attack. Its animated tree uses the same combat numbers and stops at one controlled tree for this encounter. [Savage Species, Table 5–1]() · [Original 3.0 SRD, Treant](https://www.dragon.ee/30srd/monsters_t.html) · [Monster Manual II, Trample glossary]()

## Portraits

I used built-in ImageGen, asking for square, full-body bestiary portraits on plain warm tan parchment. Big Linda supplied the paint and paper reference. The subjects were a linen-wrapped Mummy with a stone pendant, an Ogre in mended cloth with a lowered club, a long-limbed pebbled Stone Giant holding a rock, a winged Succubus in a slate travel coat, and a gnarled oak Treant with root feet. I checked all five 1254 × 1254 originals at full size and at 96 px. [Thumbnail comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [Portrait manifest](portrait-files.json)
