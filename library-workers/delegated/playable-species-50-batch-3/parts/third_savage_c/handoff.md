Harl's paralysis uses the one-minute duration from class level 2. Nella trades on disguise and mimicry at Green Hag 3. Tallowcrest has the printed 50-ft. flight and pounce before advancing to Large size.

Kebr's barbs deal damage through impale after a successful grab and grapple check. The class gives a fear effect on a hit without a separate unlock in the table, so the level-7 NPC uses that effect as written. Spell save DCs follow the sorcerer spell level where one exists, with the original cleric or druid level for the remaining powers. [3.0 Monster Manual, pp. 48–52]() · [Original Spells by Class]()

Pinder's aura has a 5-ft. radius. The book gives a save but leaves timing open, so the encounter calls for a check upon first entry or at the start of a turn, at most once per round. The build resolves burning touch against normal AC using the printed natural-weapon wording.
