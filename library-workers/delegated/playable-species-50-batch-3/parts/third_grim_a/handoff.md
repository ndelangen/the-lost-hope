# Revised Grim Hollow heritages

| Profile | Linked NPC | Key rules | Printed page / viewer page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grudgel | Orven Vale, Fighter 1 | All eight traditional traits. Artisanal Focus selects glassblower's tools and Magical Savvy selects Mending. | 20 / 24 |
| Accursed | Sella Wren, Rogue 2 | Eight individual choices. Two combat, three exploration, and three roleplaying traits. | 23 / 27 |
| Arisen | Tamsin Reed, Fighter 2 | All eight traditional traits. Artificial Form grants the Construct type and Magical Fortification uses Wisdom. | 24 / 28 |

The profiles use the Transformed Edition and retain the catalog's `Fifth edition compatible` key. The NPCs use 2024 core classes, weapon mastery, and origin feats, with Etharis languages chosen for their backgrounds. All three use Medium size and 30 ft. base speed. Tamsin uses workshop-issued armor.

I checked the heritage choices, actions, AC, HP, saves, skills, and attack bonuses by hand. The portraits use built-in ImageGen with Big Linda as the paint and parchment reference. All three are 1254-pixel square PNGs. I inspected the full figures and a 96-pixel strip. Tamsin's seams and Sella's curse marks are easier to see at full size.
