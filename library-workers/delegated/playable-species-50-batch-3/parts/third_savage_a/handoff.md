I checked the original player progressions and the build math by hand. Tavian uses his wings on the ground and his claws in flight, following the original avoral description. Hessik uses the 1-point heat rider from the original azer stat block because the player text leaves the damage amount unstated. Bramble stays at level 3, where the table and prose agree on one web per day. They put the second use at levels 4 and 5 respectively. The encounter uses the original web's Medium target limit and fixed escape DCs. [Monster Manual (2000)]()
