# Kenku, Nycter, Synad, and Savage Coast Tortle

| Profile | Linked NPC | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kenku | Kett Brassring, Rogue 1 | [Monster Manual III, pp. 86–87]() |
| Nycter | Pell Ventmark, 3 racial HD and Fighter 1 | [Monster Manual III, pp. 112–113]() |
| Synad | Ione Lark, Nomad Psion 1 | [Complete Psionic, pp. 139–141]() |
| Tortle | Mera Reedpole, Fighter 1 | [Savage Coast Campaign Book, Part 2]() |

Pell uses the printed Nycter player traits. The monster's hunting cry and natural armor are outside those traits. Ione has one head because the portrait shows her with power points left. Her Multitask action costs one point in addition to the cost of the chosen power. Mera uses the original TSR player tables, with numerical movement from the land Tortle's companion entry. She goes unarmored, and the source evidence records the book's fitted armor examples and its conflicting bite damage types. [Savage Coast Monstrous Compendium, Tortle](https://pandius.com/tortle.html)

I checked the original rules, ability adjustments, AC, saves and attacks by hand. All four square originals passed full-size and 96-pixel review beside Big Linda. The comparison strip shows Big Linda first, then the four profiles in table order. ImageGen prompts and the portrait checksums are included.
