# Mephling from Planar Handbook

This batch adds one D&D 3.5 Mephling profile with the Air, Earth, Fire, and Water ancestries from the July 2004 printing. Each ancestry has an original encounter. All four breath weapons use the same 15-ft. cone and fixed 1d8 damage. The ancestry sets the damage type, and Hit Dice and Constitution set the Reflex save DC. [Original rules, pp. 10–12]() · [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

The sidebar mentions reduced fast healing. The playable traits give no rate, conditions, or fast healing ability, so the builds follow the playable traits. The official errata and FAQ do not supply a rule for it. [Source check](independent-source-notes.md) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

Pip Reedwhistle’s plain-parchment portrait shows the Air ancestry and supplies the new profile’s list thumbnail. The temporary copy found the profile by name, trait, and NPC role. Both catalog commands returned all four builds, the portrait resolved, and the separate Fire Mephit entry matched its before snapshot.
