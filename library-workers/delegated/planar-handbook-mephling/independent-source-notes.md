# Mephling source check

The 2004 first printing introduces Mephlings on p. 10. Racial traits begin on p. 11 and finish on p. 12. Page 11’s sidebar mentions reduced fast healing, while the playable traits give no mechanics for it. The November 2004 errata and 2008 FAQ have no correction for Mephlings. [First printing]() · [Wizards errata and FAQ archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP) · [June 2008 FAQ]()

The book gives all four ancestries fixed 1d8 breath damage and a 15-ft. cone with a Reflex save. The first extra daily use occurs at level 4. Once multiple uses are available, consecutive uses must be separated by 1d4 rounds. The core rules supply the standard action and supernatural classification. The racial formula uses the Mephling’s Hit Dice and Constitution modifier. [Core breath rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#breathWeapon)

Independent review checked all four builds’ skill budgets, saves, breath DCs, and spell slots. Fire ancestry raises burning hands to CL 2 because the spell has the Fire descriptor. Mage armor stays at CL 1. [Burning hands](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/burningHands.htm) · [Mage armor](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/mageArmor.htm)

Steve Prescott’s illustration labels all four ancestries on p. 10. The Air Mephling’s pale skin and small membrane wings informed Pip’s portrait. His clothing, flute, and pose are original. [Book illustration]()
