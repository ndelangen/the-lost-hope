# Mephling encounters

Each of these Small Humanoids has one class level, LA +1, ECL 2, and provisional CR 2. They are Extraplanar on the Material Plane. Each breath attack is a standard action, available once per day in these builds. It deals 1d8 damage in a 15-ft. cone with a Reflex save for half. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Pip Reedwhistle, Air Bard 1

Pip flies through the bell loft hatch and signals the party from the gallery. He waits for the others to clear the exit before using his breath.

- HP 7, AC 17, initiative +3. Land 30 ft., fly 10 ft. with perfect maneuverability.
- Dust-and-grit breath deals piercing damage, Reflex DC 11. His light crossbow attacks at +4 for 1d6 piercing, or +5 for 1d6+1 within 30 ft.
- Inspire courage is available once per day and gives allies who can hear him +1 on attacks, weapon damage, and saves against charm and fear. He has two cantrip casts per day.

## Flint Cutwall, Earth Druid 1

Flint burrows through loose earth beside a blocked cellar. If a foe follows outside, he can use entangle where weeds and roots cover the path.

- HP 10, AC 15, initiative +0. Land 30 ft., burrow 10 ft.
- Rock-shard breath deals bludgeoning damage, Reflex DC 12. His club attacks at +4 for 1d4+3 bludgeoning.
- Entangle is CL 1, Reflex DC 14. Cure light wounds restores 1d8+1 HP. Each is prepared once.

## Cinder Kilnwatch, Fire Sorcerer 1

Cinder guards the kiln passage. Use the bend to keep the kiln hands out of either cone.

- HP 5, AC 14, initiative +7, land 30 ft. Mage armor raises AC to 18 for one hour.
- Flame breath deals 1d8 fire, Reflex DC 11. Burning hands deals 2d4 fire, Reflex DC 14. It uses a spell slot and benefits from CL 2 because it has the Fire descriptor. Mage armor stays at CL 1.
- Cinder has four first-level spell casts per day. Track the single breath use separately.

## Ripple Lockreach, Water Monk 1

Ripple swims a rope to the far sluice ladder. She surfaces before guiding the workers and saves the acid cone for a clear shot on land.

- HP 11, AC 15, initiative +2. Land 30 ft., swim 30 ft. Swim +10 on checks for hazards or special actions. She still needs air.
- Caustic breath deals acid damage, Reflex DC 13. Her unarmed strike attacks at +3 for 1d4+2 bludgeoning. A flurry attacks twice at +1 each.
- Once per day, declare Stunning Fist before an unarmed attack. A target damaged by it must pass Fortitude DC 12 or be stunned for one round.
