# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual scorpions

Large, Huge, and Giant Scorpion come from page 309 of the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. Large and Huge add catalog entries. Giant Scorpion adds a historical version to an existing entry. The separately staged 3.5e *White Plume Mountain* Large Monstrous Scorpion is a different profile. [Printed page 309](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/3107fb0068e280ea17bbff454a5b676b.webp) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | AC | HD | Combat rules |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Large Scorpion | 5 | 2+2 | Type A sting. The victim saves at +2 and takes 15 poison damage on a failure or 0 on a success, in addition to the sting's 1 damage. |
| Huge Scorpion | 4 | 4+4 | Type F sting. A target pinned in its pincers can still fight back. |
| Giant Scorpion | 3 | 5+5 | Type F sting. A held victim takes automatic pincer damage, gets one escape attempt per combat, and is hit automatically if the sting targets them. At 1 or 2 HP, the scorpion uses two sting attempts per round. |

The Huge and Giant profiles use the creature entry's next-round death on a failed Type F save. The *Dungeon Master Guide*'s general poison table gives immediate onset for Type F. The Large Scorpion's specific Type A rule qualifies the lethal-sting sentence in the shared Large and Huge paragraph. [Dungeon Master Guide, table 51](https://www.scribd.com/document/522946282/Dungeon-Master-Guide)

The new portraits use plain parchment and show the full claws and tail. Large is sandy and slender, Huge is dark brown with heavier pincers, and Giant follows the printed green shell, yellowish green pincers, and black tail. The builds of the Large and Huge scorpions are illustration choices within their shared color range. The printed family illustration bears Tony DiTerlizzi's signature and has no size label.
