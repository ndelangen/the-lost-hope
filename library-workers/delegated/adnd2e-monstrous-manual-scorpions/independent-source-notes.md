# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual scorpion source check

The checked copy of TSR’s *Monstrous Manual* (product 2140, first published in 1993) identifies itself as the January 1994 third printing. Printed p. 309 (viewer page 312) contains the full Scorpion table, all three descriptions and the art. The linked scan image has SHA-256 `02e21e45efeb888896e9ad11b424eed238527d54140e0a83cc227cbf1083b7a3`. The reflowed electronic edition spreads the same content across pp. 852–854. ## Shared table fields

All three are carnivorous, active at any time, nonintelligent (0) and neutral. The table gives warm wilderness and subterranean areas, swarm organization, treasure D, three attacks with two pincers and a sting, special attack poison sting, and no special defense or magic resistance. “Swarm” is an organization label. The statistics describe individual scorpions.

## Large Scorpion

- Uncommon, 1–6 appearing, AC 5, movement 9, HD 2+2, THAC0 19, damage 1–4/1–4/1, Small (2 ft. long), morale average (8), 175 XP.
- Its sting has Type A poison, 15 damage on a failed save and zero poison damage on a successful save. The victim gets +2 to the save. The large form’s weaker poison qualifies the sentence about dying the next round in the shared Large and Huge paragraph. The entry gives no separate onset for this Type A damage. The sting still does its listed one point of attack damage.
- The prose describes tan, brown and black color variants and reports rare white scorpions deep underground. It describes pinning explicitly for Huge, without a separate pinning procedure for Large. [Printed p. 309](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/3107fb0068e280ea17bbff454a5b676b.webp)

## Huge Scorpion

- Common, 1–4 appearing, AC 4, movement 12, HD 4+4, THAC0 15, damage 1–8/1–8/1–3, Medium (4 ft. long), morale average (10), 420 XP.
- A failed save against its Type F sting kills the victim the next round in the creature text. It can pin a victim in a manner described as similar to the Giant, but the Huge’s pinned victim can still fight back. Distinguish its 1–8 claw damage from the Giant’s numerical values. The manual does not give the Huge a separately quantified automatic-damage, escape or frenzy procedure.
- The same tan-to-black palette and rumored rare white underground specimens apply. The source also describes scorpions of different sizes fighting each other. [Printed p. 309](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/3107fb0068e280ea17bbff454a5b676b.webp)

## Giant Scorpion

- Uncommon, 1–4 appearing, AC 3, movement 15, HD 5+5, THAC0 15, damage 1–10/1–10/1–4, Medium (5–6 ft. long), morale steady (11), 650 XP.
- It attacks an approaching creature 95% of the time and can attack three opponents at once. After it grabs a target with a pincer, that claw deals an automatic 1–10 damage per round while held. The target can attempt one bend bars/lift gates roll per combat to escape, which must be its sole action that round. The entry gives no separate grab roll or threshold. A sting directed at a target in the pincer hits automatically. An untrapped target requires an attack roll.
- The sting deals 1–4 attack damage and its Type F poison kills on the next round on a failed save. The text says scorpions are susceptible to their own poison. At 1 or 2 hp it uses only the tail with two sting attempts per round. It drags slain prey to its burrow. Underground lairs have a 20% chance to hold 5d4 eggs.
- The prose gives a hard green carapace, yellowish-green legs and pincers and a segmented black tail. It favors deserts and warm lands but can live in colder places such as dungeons. It eats prey including other giant arthropods and may itself be eaten by purple worms. [Printed p. 309](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/3107fb0068e280ea17bbff454a5b676b.webp)

The 2e *Dungeon Master Guide*’s generic poison table lists Type F with immediate onset, while this creature entry delays death to the next round. The scorpion profiles use that explicit next-round wording. The Large entry gives Type A’s failed and successful save outcomes and the +2 bonus without a specific onset. [Dungeon Master Guide, Table 51](https://www.scribd.com/document/522946282/Dungeon-Master-Guide)

## Original art and catalog

The signed p. 309 image shows a rusty-brown and ochre scorpion with extended pincers, raised tail and a small skull, facing right. The stylized `td.` monogram below its walking legs matches Tony DiTerlizzi’s signed beetle image on p. 19. His retrospective identifies a rejected giant-scorpion sketch for the manual as the basis for his distinct *Dragon* #197 illustration. The p. 309 art has no size label. Its colors match the Large/Huge prose better than the explicitly green Giant body and are safest as family art rather than a fixed identity. [Printed scorpion art](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/3107fb0068e280ea17bbff454a5b676b.webp) · [Signed beetle comparison](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/2d16ae154a6a7a8025654797682b1bd3.webp) · [DiTerlizzi’s retrospective](https://diterlizzi.com/behind-the-monstrous-manual-part-10/) · [Dragon #197]()

The primary catalog has no exact TSR 2140 matches for the three names. Giant Scorpion and Scorpion exist in 5e and BFRD. The staged *White Plume Mountain (Revised)* Large Monstrous Scorpion uses 3.5 and its own adventure and baseline references. The separate Man-Scorpion publications are outside this animal family. All three fit the catalog’s Beast grouping as arthropods.
