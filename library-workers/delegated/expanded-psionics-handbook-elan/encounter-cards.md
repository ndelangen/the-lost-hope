# Elan encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Aberrations with the Psionic subtype, a 30-ft. land speed, and ordinary vision. They use the playable version of the racial abilities. Both are ECL 1 and provisional CR 1. [Playable Elan, p. 10]()

| NPC | AC (touch, flat-footed) | HP | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will | Power points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teva Alder, Seer Psion 1 | 11 (11, 10) | 6 | +1 | +2, +1, +3 | 5 |
| Corren Vale, Fighter 1 | 16 (11, 15) | 12 | +5 | +4, +1, +1 | 2 |

Spend 1 PP on **Resistance** as an immediate action for +4 on all saves until the start of the Elan's next action. **Resilience** is also immediate and prevents 2 HP of incoming damage per PP. An Elan who is flat-footed cannot use either defense. **Repletion** costs 1 PP and covers food and water for 24 hours. Every use comes from the current reserve.

## Teva Alder

Someone erased names from a public register. Teva can still see traces of the old ink when the gallery light hits it. Give her a quiet minute before the closing bell and she can try to recover the missing names.

Knowledge (history) +6, Knowledge (psionics) +6, Psicraft +6, Concentration +6, Search +4. Add +4 to Concentration when using Combat Manifestation defensively or while grappling or pinned.

- Light crossbow +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. range increment. Reload as a move action. Dagger −1 melee, 1d4−1 piercing or slashing, minimum 1, 19–20/×2.
- **Inertial Armor**, 1 PP. Standard action, personal, lasts 1 hour. AC 15 (touch 11, flat-footed 14). The force armor also applies against incorporeal attacks.
- **Call to Mind**, 1 PP. After a failed Knowledge check, take 1 minute to retry it with a +4 competence bonus.
- **Detect Psionics**, 1 PP. Standard action to start, 60-ft. cone, concentrate for up to 1 minute. Presence on round one, aura count and strongest aura on round two, each aura's strength and location on round three.

## Corren Vale

Corren is escorting a courier across an unlit causeway ahead of a morning hearing. The old markers are gone on the far stretch. Corren needs someone to relay a hooded lamp signal so the courier does not lose the route.

Climb +3, Jump +3. Chain shirt, light wooden shield, longsword, javelin, and hooded lamp. Corren can spend 1 PP on Repletion before leaving and keep the last point for either immediate defense.

- Longsword +4 melee, 1d8+2 slashing, 19–20/×2, 5-ft. reach.
- Javelin +2 thrown, 1d6+2 piercing, critical ×2, 30-ft. range increment.
