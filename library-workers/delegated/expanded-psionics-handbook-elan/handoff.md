# Elan for D&D 3.5

One playable Elan entry from the April 2004 first printing of *Expanded Psionics Handbook*. Elans look human, have the Aberration type with the Psionic subtype, and have no darkvision. They gain two racial power points at 1st level, which they can spend on Resistance, Resilience, and Repletion. [First printing, pp. 9–10]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

The 2006 errata removes the maximum age and corrects the creature chapter to two racial power points, matching the playable chapter. Resistance follows the playable duration and ends at the beginning of the Elan's next action. The sample warrior says next turn. [Errata]() · [Published warrior, pp. 196–197]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

The full-body plain-parchment portrait shows Teva Alder, an original Seer and archive conservator. Pale skin and red hair reflect a preference of the council described in the book. Elans otherwise vary in appearance as widely as humans. Corren Vale is a separate original Fighter who escorts a courier at night. Both have provisional CRs and runnable power-point budgets.
