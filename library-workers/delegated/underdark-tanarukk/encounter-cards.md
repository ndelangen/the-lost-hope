# Tanarukk encounters

Both Tanarukks have 60-ft. darkvision, a 20-ft. land speed, and fire resistance 10. Each can use Control Flame once per day as a standard action. From within 10 ft. of an existing nonmagical fire, dim it to coals or brighten it to daylight brightness and twice its normal illumination radius. The change lasts 5 minutes.

## Khar Ashridge, coal-gate sentry

**5 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 3.** AC 16, HP 36, initiative +2, SR 14. Fortitude +6, Reflex +6, Will +5. Two-handed battleaxe +10 for 1d8+6 slashing. On a full attack he adds a bite at +4 for 1d6+2.

Khar uses his daily Control Flame on the nearest brazier so his darkvision gives him a clear view of the landing. Against an incoming spell that allows resistance, the caster rolls 1d20 + caster level against SR 14.

## Varka Coalvein, ember-gallery scout

**Rogue 1 with 5 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 4.** AC 20, HP 42, initiative +8, SR 15. Fortitude +6, Reflex +10, Will +4. Rapier +9 for 1d6+3 piercing. On a full attack she adds a bite at +4 for 1d6+1. Sneak Attack adds 1d6 on eligible hits.

Varka uses Hide +12 and Move Silently +13 to reach the signal flame. Once the escape route is open, she approaches within 10 ft. and brightens the flame for 5 minutes. Its heat and fuel use stay the same.
