# Tanarukk

This batch adds the playable Tanarukk to the 3.5 *Underdark* catalog using the player traits in *Races of Faerûn*. Khar Ashridge and Varka Coalvein are original NPCs. Khar’s plain-parchment portrait represents the species profile.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and both encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/tanarukk-draft.png). The [style comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Khar beside Big Linda.

In a temporary copy, search found the species by name, Control Flame, spell resistance, and both NPC roles. Khar’s portrait loaded on the 2003 profile and as its list thumbnail. The existing monster entry, including its versions and portraits, matched the baseline. [Verification](verification.json)
