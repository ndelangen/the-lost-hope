# Independent Illumian reviews

The source reviewer checked the December 2004 printing. Chuck Lukacs drew the sigil study on p. 51. The next page describes pale, often bald, humanlike figures with layered embroidered garments. The playable traits span pp. 53–55. The review checked all six power sigils, all fifteen words, and the duration and per-day limits for activated words. [Original artwork]() · [Rules]()

The second reviewer flagged the p. 54 phrase “2nd level in any class.” The source reviewer also found Bloodwing Zhaolei, Bard 1/Fighter 1, with two +2 power sigils on p. 87. The profile preserves both. The same page says Thulech’s prepared-only casting cannot pay for Uurhoon. [Power sigil rules]() · [Published examples, p. 87]()

The published book has separate Bloodwing NPCs on pp. 83–87 and sample encounters on pp. 189–192. The latter include a Cabal Guard at CR 4, Spy at CR 6, Loremaster at CR 9, and Battle Sorcerer at CR 10. The two new NPCs in this batch are original and carry provisional CRs. The source reviewer found no title-specific errata file in Wizards’ collected archive. [Published encounters]() · [Wizards’ errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The second reviewer checked attack and defense totals, skill budgets, spell slots, and changes when the sigils go dark. Eshara uses 45 skill points and Veyric uses 16.
