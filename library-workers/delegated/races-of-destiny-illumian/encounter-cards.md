# Illumian encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. Their species traits come from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*. Both are Medium humanoids with the human subtype and 30-ft. land speed. [Original traits, pp. 53–55]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Glassweir Eshara, Rogue 2

Provisional CR 2, ECL 2. AC 14, touch 12, flat-footed 12. HP 12, initiative +4 with sigils lit or +2 doused. Fort +1, Ref +5, Will +0. BAB +1, grapple +3. STR 14, DEX 15, CON 13, INT 12, WIS 10, CHA 8. Common, Illumian, Dwarven.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Rapier | +3 melee, 1d6+2 piercing, 18–20/×2 |
| Shortbow | +3 ranged, 1d6 piercing, ×3, 60-ft. increment |

Climb +9, Disable Device +6, Hide +9, Listen +7, Move Silently +9, Open Lock +9, Search +6, Spot +7, Tumble +9. Aesh and Uur each give +2 to the relevant checks. With sigils doused, Climb, Hide, Move Silently, Open Lock, and Tumble fall to +7.

Sneak attack adds 1d6 when the target is flanked or denied its Dexterity bonus to AC. Ranged sneak attacks require a target within 30 ft. While the sigils are lit, Aeshuur gives +2 dodge AC against an opponent Eshara damages with a sneak attack or critical hit, until her next turn begins. Trapfinding covers Search DCs above 20 and magic traps. Evasion negates damage on a successful Reflex save for half in light or no armor.

Eshara will map the bypass hatch if the party keeps the winch still. She douses her lights before hiding and brings them back to work the lock.

## Latchwater Veyric, Wizard 1

Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 11, touch 11, flat-footed 10. With *mage armor*, AC 15 and flat-footed 14. HP 5, initiative +1. Fort +1, Ref +1, Will +3. BAB +0, grapple −1. STR 8, DEX 13, CON 12, INT 15, WIS 12, CHA 10. Common, Illumian, Draconic, Dwarven.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Dagger | −1 melee, 1d4−1 piercing or slashing, minimum 1 damage, 19–20/×2 |
| Light crossbow | +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment. Reload as a move action. |

Concentration +5, Decipher Script +7, Knowledge (arcana) +7, Spellcraft +10. Naen gives +1 to Intelligence checks and skills. With sigils doused, Decipher Script and Knowledge (arcana) drop to +6 and Spellcraft to +9.

Caster level 1, save DC 12 + spell level. Prepare *detect magic*, *light*, and *read magic* at level 0, and *grease* and *mage armor* at level 1. *Mage armor* lasts 1 hour. *Grease* covers a 10-ft. square within 25 ft. for 1 round. Reflex DC 13 or fall. Moving across it takes a DC 10 Balance check at half speed.

Veyric wants the missing rubbing before anyone touches the threshold. If the party has to retreat, he uses *grease* to slow pursuit.

## Shared traits

Lit sigils give candlelight. Douse them as a standard action and restore them as a free action. When the name of a spell includes glyph, rune, sigil, or symbol, a character below the spell’s caster level takes a −4 racial penalty on its saves. At or above that caster level, the character is immune to the spell. Both have +2 racial on saves against spells with the shadow descriptor. At death, Final Utterance can be heard for 2 rounds for Eshara or 1 round for Veyric. [Original traits]()
