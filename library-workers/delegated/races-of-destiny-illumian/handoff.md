# Illumian for D&D 3.5

One playable Illumian profile from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*. It covers all six power sigils and all fifteen words, along with Glyphic Resonance, Final Utterance, and literacy. Dousing the sigils takes a standard action and suspends their benefits. Restoring them is a free action. [Original traits, pp. 53–55]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

The rule on p. 54 gives the second sigil at “2nd level in any class.” The published Bard 1/Fighter 1 on p. 87 already has two +2 sigils and a word. Both passages are recorded in the profile so the multiclass example is easy to find. [Power sigil rule]() · [Published example]()

Glassweir Eshara is an original Rogue 2 and lock surveyor, provisional CR 2. She is the subject of the full-body portrait on plain parchment. Latchwater Veyric is an original Wizard 1 and inscription reader, provisional CR 1. Their cards show the changes when their sigils go dark.
