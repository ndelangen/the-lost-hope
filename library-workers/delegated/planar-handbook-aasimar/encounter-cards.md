# Aasimar encounters

Both are Medium Native Outsiders with darkvision 60 ft. and resistance 5 each to acid, cold, and electricity. Each has one class level, LA +1, ECL 2, and provisional CR 2. Their armor reduces their land speed from 30 ft. to 20 ft. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

Each can use daylight once per day at CL 1. Touch an object as a standard action to shed bright light for 60 ft. and dim light for another 60 ft. The effect lasts 10 minutes and can be dismissed. Apply penalties for bright light. Creatures damaged or destroyed by actual sunlight take no such damage from daylight. [Daylight spell](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/daylight.htm)

## Iona Vale, Paladin 1

On the Material Plane, Iona lights a lantern and hands it to a ferryman to guide the group through the quay passage. She guards the rear.

- HP 12, AC 16, initiative +1. Fortitude +4, Reflex +1, Will +2.
- Longsword +5 for 1d8+3 slashing. Once per day, Smite Evil with that weapon raises the attack to +8 and damage to 1d8+4 against an evil target. An attempt against a non-evil target still spends the use.
- Detect evil at will. Her racial daylight is available at first level.

## Teren Mere, Cleric 1

Teren lights the handrail on a dark Ysgard stair. He is Extraplanar there. He can turn any undead blocking the steps before tending to the injured.

- HP 10, AC 16, initiative +4. Fortitude +4, Reflex +0, Will +5. Heavy mace +1 for 1d8+1 bludgeoning.
- Turn undead five times per day. Roll 1d20+2 to determine the highest individual HD affected and 2d6+3 for the total HD. One attempt can be greater turning, which destroys the undead it would turn.
- Prepared first-level spells are bless and protection from evil. The Healing domain raises cure light wounds to CL 2 for 1d8+2 healing. Racial daylight stays at CL 1.
