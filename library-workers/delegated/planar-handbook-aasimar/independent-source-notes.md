# Aasimar source check

The July 2004 first printing describes the Aasimar on p. 6 and gives the player traits on p. 7. The November 2004 errata has no Aasimar correction. Page 7 gives daylight once per day at caster level equal to character level. The separate Monster Manual warrior has its own combat entry. [First printing]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The book calls Aasimar Native Outsiders from the Material Plane and grants Extraplanar away from it. The core rules give the transitive-plane exception. Both staged builds use the core daylight duration and light radii. The independent review also checked their skill budgets, AC, smite, turning, and Healing domain caster level. [Extraplanar subtype](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/typesSubtypes.htm#extraplanarSubtype) · [Daylight](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/daylight.htm) · [Healing domain](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spellLists/clericDomains.htm#healingDomain)

David Hudnut illustrated the unnamed paladin on p. 6. That page describes Aasimar as human-looking and gives silver hair and gold eyes as signs of celestial ancestry. Iona’s equipment and pose are original. [Book illustration]()
