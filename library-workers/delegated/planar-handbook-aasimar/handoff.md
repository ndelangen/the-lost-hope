# Aasimar from Planar Handbook

This batch adds the 2004 D&D 3.5 Aasimar profile and two original encounters. Iona and Teren each use daylight once per day at caster level 1. Their +1 level adjustment makes them ECL 2 and leaves character level at 1. [Original rules, pp. 6–7]() · [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

Iona is on the Material Plane and Teren is on Ysgard. Teren gains the Extraplanar subtype alongside Native. Both have resistance 5 to acid, cold, and electricity. [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

Iona’s portrait uses Big Linda’s plain tan parchment. The temporary catalog found the new profile by name, rules text, and NPC role, returned both NPCs from both commands, and resolved Iona’s portrait on the 2004 version. Monsters of the Multiverse remains the default, and the list thumbnail stays the same.
