The mechanics were checked against the licensed 2025 Spanish edition of the 2024 *Monster Vault*. The XP values come from printed p. 17. The English catalog supplies names and habitats and rounds the snakes’ CR to 0.3 and 0.1. Kobold Press’s May 29, 2025 errata lists no changes specific to these five blocks. [Nosolorol edition](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/) · [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

- The giant and hunter sharks each make two Bite attacks and can move toward a visible hostile creature with Swift Hunter. The reef shark makes one Bite attack and can Hide as a bonus action. Lightning triggers a Constitution save against incapacitation at DC 15, 12 and 11 respectively.
- The constrictor's grapple and Crush each use DC 12. On a failed Crush save, the target takes bludgeoning damage and starts suffocating until the grapple ends. The flying snake's Reflexive Escape triggers when a visible creature attacks. Its poison calls for a DC 11 Constitution save.
- All five roster portraits match their recorded SHA-256 hashes and are 1,254 by 1,254 pixels. The three sharks have distinct silhouettes and markings. The constrictor is coiled and wingless, and the flying snake has membranous wings. Each uses plain parchment. The [contact sheet]() follows roster order.
