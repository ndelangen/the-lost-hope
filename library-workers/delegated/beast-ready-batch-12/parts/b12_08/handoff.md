# Batch 12, part 08

Verified five Monster Vault profiles in the licensed Spanish Cubil de Monstruos. The print supplies the rules, original challenge fractions, and XP table. The English catalog confirms names and habitats. The May 29, 2025 Monster Vault errata lists no changes for these profiles. [Nosolorol edition](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/) · [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

- Stirge, printed p. 184. Blood Drain attaches and causes further HP loss at the start of the stirge's turns. Reactive Wiggle can redirect an incoming attack to the creature carrying it. The adjacent Shadow Stirge sidebar describes a separate variant.
- Giant Spider and Giant Wolf Spider, printed p. 334. The giant spider restrains with its rechargeable Web bonus action. The wolf spider has Hunter's Leap. Their poison saves have different outcomes on success.
- Tiger and Saber-Toothed Tiger, printed p. 359. The ordinary tiger's two-hit Multiattack can cause one delayed HP loss. The saber-toothed tiger's Bite can open a wound that bleeds each turn until closed. Their Pounce traits grant different bonus attacks.

All five reused portraits match the final roster hashes. Both spiders use the muted Batch 05 assets. The saber-toothed tiger uses the lower saturation Batch 06 asset.
