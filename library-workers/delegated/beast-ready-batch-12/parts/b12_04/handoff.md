# Monster Vault beasts

Five records checked against Nosolorol’s licensed Spanish edition, *Cubil de Monstruos*. The Kobold Press catalog confirms the English names and habitats. All five records reuse portraits verified by subject and SHA-256. [Spanish edition](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant), [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)

| Beast | Printed folio | CR | AC | HP | Combat details |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Quipper, Swarm of Quippers | 351 | 1 | 13 | 34 | A failed DC 13 Dexterity save against Bites causes a bleeding wound. A DC 10 Medicine check as an action or magical healing closes it. |
| Rat | 353 | 0 | 10 | 9 | Swim 20 ft. and fixed Perception 15 by smell. |
| Rat, Giant | 354 | 1/4 | 13 | 14 | Pack Tactics. A failed DC 13 Constitution save against Bite can cause Rat Plague or brief poisoning. |
| Rat, Swarm of Rats | 353–354 | 1/4 | 10 | 14 | Bites affects one creature in the swarm’s space. A failed DC 9 Constitution save can cause Rat Plague or brief poisoning. |
| Raven | 341 | 0 | 11 | 8 | Tracks carrion by scent and repeats up to 20 words in voices it has heard. |

The Giant Rat stat block gives CR 1/4 and the English catalog rounds it to 0.3. The Spanish Beast overview on p. 33 gives 1/8. This profile follows the stat block and awards 50 XP. The Spanish folios govern the staged rules. The Monster Vault errata lists no changes to these five creatures. [Monster Vault errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)
