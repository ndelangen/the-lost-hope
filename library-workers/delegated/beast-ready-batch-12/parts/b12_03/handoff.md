# Five Monster Vault beasts

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

I translated the mechanics from Nosolorol’s licensed Spanish *Cubil de Monstruos* and checked English names, types, challenge ratings, and habitats against the Kobold Press catalog. The Spanish blocks print CR 1/4 for Giant Owl and Panther and CR 1/8 for Pony. The online catalog rounds those ratings. The official errata gives no entry-specific changes for these five. [Spanish edition](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [English catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/) · [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

| Entry | Spanish folio | page | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owl | 335 | 337 | Perception rises to 18 for hearing or sight. Stealth is 18 in flight. |
| Owl, Giant | 335 | 337 | Perception rises to 20 for hearing or sight. Stealth is 19 in flight. It speaks Giant Owl and understands Common, Elvish, and Sylvan. |
| Panther | 349–350 | 351–352 | Pounce requires a 20-ft. straight approach and a Claw hit on the same turn. A prone target allows a bonus-action Bite. Stealthy Hunter grants a bonus-action Hide. |
| Pony | 351 | 353 | Draft Animal lets the pony push, drag, or lift three times its carrying capacity. |
| Quipper | 350–351 | 352–353 | The individual Piraña has land speed 0 ft., swim speed 40 ft., and advantage on melee attacks against creatures below maximum HP. Its Bite appears on the following folio. |

The Spanish Giant Owl entry ends after Talons. Kobold Press’s separate English Black Flag Reference Document v1.0 adds Puffed Up Display there. The encounter block follows the Spanish witness. [Black Flag Reference Document]()

All five reused portraits match their subjects and roster hashes. Quipper uses the individual fish portrait.
