# Batch 12 · Four snake profiles and Spider

The rules come from the licensed Spanish edition of the Monster Vault. The four snake blocks are on pp. 355–356. Spider is on pp. 333–334. The English Kobold Press catalog supplies the display names and habitats. The XP values and fractional challenge ratings follow the book. [Spanish publisher](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [English catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)

The giant constrictor can use Crush on a grappled creature. The two solitary poisonous snakes have different save DCs. A successful save against the giant poisonous snake’s Bite still deals half poison damage. The swarm has a start-of-turn poison effect in addition to Bites. Spider's Web Walker covers nets and webs in the licensed printing. The English Black Flag reference lists webbing. [English rules reference]()

All five reused portraits match the updated roster checksums and their subjects. The three solitary snake portraits and Spider use muted tan parchment. The snake swarm uses gold parchment.
