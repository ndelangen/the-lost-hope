# Batch 12 Beast rules

Verified both vultures on p. 336, both weasels on pp. 340–341 and both wolves on pp. 346–347 of *Cubil de Monstruos*, Nosolorol's Spanish edition of *Monster Vault*. The English catalog confirms the names and terrain labels. The summaries use the book's printed challenge ratings and XP table. [Nosolorol](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant), [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)

Giant Weasel uses CR 1/4 as printed in its stat block and terrain tables. The chapter 3 Beast table lists CR 1/8. The May 2025 errata lists no correction for these six entries. [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

All six reused portraits match the roster hashes. Each has the expected animal subject on plain, muted parchment.
