# Monsters of the Multiverse dinosaurs

Brontosaurus, Deinonychus, and Dimetrodon use the first-printing stat blocks on p. 95. Velociraptor uses the stat block on p. 96 and the creature account on p. 95. I checked the stat blocks against the rendered pages and confirmed the October 2021 printing on the credits sheet. The public D&D Beyond index lists all four. Its chapter and creature links redirected to the purchase page. The stage records the scan and the publisher links separately.

Brontosaurus has 20 ft. of reach on both attacks and its Stomp can knock a target prone. Deinonychus can follow Pounce with a bonus-action Bite against a prone target. Velociraptor's Bite targets one creature, and its Claw targets one target. Dimetrodon's block has the Dinosaur tag and a 20 ft. swim speed. I found no published correction covering these entries in the official errata index or digital changelog. No earlier Beast-ready manifest or roster claimed these four records in this publication and edition.

I inspected the four reused portraits and matched each 1254 × 1254 source against the roster's SHA-256. The existing art paths resolve to the long-necked Brontosaurus, green clawed Deinonychus, sail-backed Dimetrodon, and feathered Velociraptor on plain parchment. The part contains rules and source notes. It adds no portrait file.
