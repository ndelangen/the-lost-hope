# Batch 12, part b12_02

Mastiff, Mule, Octopus, Octopus, Giant, and Orca are staged under their exact Monster Vault scopes. The rules summaries come from the licensed Spanish printing, pp. 347–352. The English catalog supplies the indexed names and habitat labels. [Nosolorol](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)

This printing gives the octopus a 60-ft. swim speed and the giant octopus a 30-ft. swim speed. Orca Tail Slap measures the 15-ft. target range from its tail. The evidence records where the Black Flag Reference Document v0.2 differs.

All five reused square portraits match the roster hashes and depict the corresponding animals on plain tan parchment. The orca uses the existing Killer Whale portrait.
