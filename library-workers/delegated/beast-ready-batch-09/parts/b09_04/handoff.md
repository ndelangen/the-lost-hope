# Monster Manual beasts

Five records verified against the publisher’s 2024-rules Monster Manual entries. The April 2025 errata lists no changes to these five creatures. All five reuse the portraits specified in the roster, with matching SHA-256 hashes. [Monster Manual errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

| Beast | CR | AC | HP | Rules to check at the table |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Crocodile](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775811-crocodile) | 1/2 | 12 | 13 | A Bite hit grapples and restrains a Medium or smaller creature, escape DC 12. |
| [Deer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194966-deer) | 0 | 13 | 4 | Agile applies when it leaves an enemy’s reach. Ram deals 1d4 bludgeoning. |
| [Dire Wolf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775812-dire-wolf) | 1 | 14 | 22 | A Bite hit knocks a Large or smaller creature prone. Pack Tactics uses the updated ally condition. |
| [Draft Horse](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775813-draft-horse) | 1/4 | 10 | 15 | Hooves deal 1d4 + 4 bludgeoning. |
| [Eagle](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194979-eagle) | 0 | 12 | 4 | Talons are +4 to hit. Perception is +6 and Passive Perception is 16. |

The Deer page displays “Darkvision 60” without a unit. Its matching XMM transcription confirms feet. The Crocodile entry cites the general grapple rules for release, escape and the restriction on using the jaws against another target while holding prey.
