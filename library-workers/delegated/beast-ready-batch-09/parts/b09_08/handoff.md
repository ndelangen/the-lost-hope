# Batch 09, part 08

Verified five 2024-rules stat blocks against the publisher's revised Monster Manual. The book was released in 2025. The files retain the catalog's Monster Manual (2024) label. [Publisher's errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual)

- Giant Lizard, Giant Octopus, and Giant Rat appear on p. 358. The lizard has Spider Climb, the octopus releases Ink Cloud as a reaction, and the rat has a +5 Dexterity save. [Giant Lizard](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195022-giant-lizard) · [Giant Octopus](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195023-giant-octopus) · [Giant Rat](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195025-giant-rat)
- Giant Scorpion and Giant Seahorse appear on p. 359. The scorpion's sting adds poison damage on the hit. The seahorse has conditional Ram damage and a Bubble Dash bonus action. [Giant Scorpion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195026-giant-scorpion) · [Giant Seahorse](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775820-giant-seahorse)

The secondary index also associates the seahorse stat block with Player's Handbook (2024) p. 350. The scoped publisher page gives the Monster Manual p. 359 byline.

All five reused portraits match the roster SHA-256 values. I checked the subjects in full frames and circular crops. The Giant Seahorse points to the existing `giant-sea-horse.png` asset.
