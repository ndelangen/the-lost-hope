# Five 2024-rules Monster Manual beasts

I checked five public D&D Beyond stat blocks against their *Monster Manual* page bylines. The catalog labels them *Monster Manual (2024)* for the revised rules. The book was released in 2025. The official errata lists no change for these five entries. [Publisher errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual) · [Errata v1.0]()

| Beast | Folio | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Allosaurus | 348 | Claws can apply Prone and permit one Bite against a Large or smaller creature after a direct approach of at least 30 ft. immediately before the hit. |
| Ankylosaurus | 348 | Two Tail attacks, each +6 for 9 (1d10 + 4). A qualifying hit puts a Huge or smaller creature Prone. The printed Strength save is +6. |
| Ape | 348 | Two Fists for 5 (1d4 + 3) each. Rock recharges on 6 and deals 10 (2d6 + 3), with range 25/50 ft. |
| Archelon | 349 | Huge Beast with the Dinosaur tag, Amphibious, swim speed 80 ft. Multiattack makes two Bites. |
| Baboon | 349 | Pack Tactics depends on an ally within 5 ft. of the target who lacks the Incapacitated condition. Bite +1 deals 1 (1d4 - 1) piercing. The publisher lists Forest and Hill. |

The Ape's public byline gives *Monster Manual* p. 348. The secondary index also cites *Player’s Handbook (2024)* p. 346. The publisher's handbook appendix requires access, so the evidence records that citation as a cross-reference.
