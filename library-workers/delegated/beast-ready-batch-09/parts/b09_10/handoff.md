# Five Beasts from the modern Monster Manual

I checked each exact D&D Beyond stat block and its Monster Manual page byline. The profiles retain the catalog’s Monster Manual (2024) label for the 2024 rules. The book was released in 2025. The current official errata and its version 1 list no changes for these five creatures. [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual) · [Errata ]()

| Beast | Folio | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Wasp | 361 | Flyby and a Sting that deals piercing and poison damage on the same hit. |
| Giant Weasel | 361 | Dexterity 17, Acrobatics +5, Climb 30 ft., and the +5 Bite. |
| Giant Wolf Spider | 362 | Spider Climb, Blindsight and Darkvision, and both damage components of Bite. |
| Goat | 362 | Strength save +2 and Climb 30 ft. Ram uses 2 (1d4) damage in place of 1 damage when the goat moves at least 20 ft. straight toward the target immediately before the hit. |
| Hawk | 362 | Perception +6, Passive Perception 16, and Talons for a fixed 1 damage. |

The habitat tags come from the publisher’s listings. I checked all five reused portraits against their roster hashes and based the appearance text on the animals shown.
