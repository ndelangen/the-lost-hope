# Batch 09, part b09_02

Badger, Bat, Black Bear, Blood Hawk and Boar are staged under the exact Monster Manual (2024) scopes. The individual D&D Beyond pages directly credit pp. 349–350. The [publisher’s Basic Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/creature-stat-blocks) also carry these modern stat blocks. The [2025 errata sheet]() lists no changes to these five entries.

All five square parchment portraits match the roster SHA-256 values. Visual review confirmed the striped badger, membranous bat wings, dark bear, red hawk with hooked bill and bristly tusked boar.
