# Batch 09 · Giant Crocodile through Giant Hyena

Five Beast records use the Monster Manual with the 2024 rules, released in 2025. The D&D Beyond entries credit p. 356 for Giant Crocodile and p. 357 for Giant Fire Beetle, Giant Frog, Giant Goat, and Giant Hyena. The source evidence records the exact publisher pages and the errata.

The current Giant Frog uses +3 to hit with Bite. The first printing used +4. The erratum changes only the attack bonus. The current block retains Stealth +4 and 5 (1d6 + 2) Bite damage. The Swallow acid damage occurs at the end of the frog’s next turn, and the frog disgorges a survivor prone. [Publisher errata ]()

All five portraits reuse the earlier parchment studies. Each SHA-256 matches the roster, and visual inspection confirmed the depicted animal. The part contains no new rasters.
