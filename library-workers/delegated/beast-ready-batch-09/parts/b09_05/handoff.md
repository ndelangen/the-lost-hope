# Five beasts for the 2024 rules

I checked the modern publisher stat blocks and their page bylines. The catalog uses *Monster Manual (2024)* for the 2024 rules, and Wizards released the book in 2025. Both versions of the errata were checked. They list no changes for these five entries. [Online errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual) · [Errata ]()

| Beast | Page | AC | HP | Initiative | CR | XP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Elephant | 353 | 12 | 76 | −1 (9) | 4 | 1,100 |
| Elk | 353 | 10 | 11 | +0 (10) | 1/4 | 50 |
| Frog | 354 | 11 | 1 | +1 (11) | 0 | 10 |
| Giant Ape | 354 | 12 | 168 | +5 (15) | 7 | 2,900 |
| Giant Badger | 354 | 13 | 15 | +0 (10) | 1/4 | 50 |

- Elephant makes two Gore attacks with Multiattack. A charge can knock a Huge or smaller creature Prone. Bonus-action Trample targets a Prone creature within 5 ft. and uses a DC 16 Dexterity save. [Elephant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775814-elephant)
- Elk's charging Ram applies extra damage and Prone to a Large or smaller creature. [Elk](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775815-elk)
- Frog has a +3 Bite that deals 1 Piercing damage, and its Standing Leap works with or without a running start. [Frog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775816-frog)
- Giant Ape's Boulder Toss uses a visible point within 90 ft., a 5-ft.-radius Sphere and a DC 17 Dexterity save. Its bonus-action Leap spends 10 ft. of movement for a jump of up to 30 ft. [Giant Ape](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195010-giant-ape)
- Giant Badger resists Poison damage and has Darkvision to 60 ft. [Giant Badger](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4775817-giant-badger)

All five reused portraits match the roster hashes and subjects. Appearance descriptions follow those portraits, and habitat tags come from the publisher's listings.
