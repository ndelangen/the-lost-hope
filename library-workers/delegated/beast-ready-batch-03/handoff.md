# Beast batch 03

Staged 50 Beast printings from the 2014 *Monster Manual* and the November 2018 *Basic Rules*. The 26 subjects use existing square PNGs from batches 01 and 02. Each version keeps its own rules and source citation. The manifest records the image hashes and the original bundle paths. The files in this bundle can also supply those paths in an independent preview.

Elephant keeps 18 Gore and 21 Stomp damage in the *Basic Rules* . Its *Monster Manual* record uses 19 and 22 from the official errata. Both Frog records preserve the printed 0 XP. [Basic Rules ]() · [Monster Manual errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)

In the temporary Library copy, I found Elephant and Stirge through Beast search. Both sampled Elephant versions loaded the same 1254 × 1254 portrait and showed the correct damage for the selected source. Stirge displayed the initial piercing damage and subsequent blood loss with their separate triggers. The browser console showed no errors or warnings.
