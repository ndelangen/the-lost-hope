# Crab, Mastiff, and Stirge

| Beast | Monster Manual, first printing | Basic Rules v1.0, November 2018 | Encounter check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crab | p. 320 | p. 122 | Claw is +0 to hit for a fixed 1 bludgeoning damage. |
| Mastiff | p. 332 | p. 144 | A creature hit by Bite makes a DC 11 Strength save or falls prone. |
| Stirge | p. 284 | p. 153 | A Blood Drain hit attaches the stirge. The separate HP loss starts on the stirge's turn while attached. After draining 10 HP, or if the target dies, it detaches. Another creature or the target can use an action to remove it earlier. |

I compared all six printed blocks. Each creature's mechanics match across the two printings. Wizards' 2018 *Monster Manual* errata lists no correction for these three entries. Neither Stirge block assigns a damage type or saving throw to the ongoing HP loss, or applies a named condition for attachment. Both distinguish it from the initial piercing damage.
