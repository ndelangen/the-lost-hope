Both printed Frog blocks award 0 XP and give it no effective attack. The D&D Beyond legacy listing shows 10 XP. The Monster Manual text also permits using the Frog block for a toad. The Pony mechanics agree in both printings. The Wolf's Bite knocks a creature prone on a failed DC 11 Strength save.
