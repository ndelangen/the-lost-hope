# Bat, Lizard and Spider

I read all six stat blocks in the November 2018 *Basic Rules* v1.0 and the 11th printing of the 2014 *Monster Manual*. The mechanics match in each pair. Each publication has its own staged entry. The official *Monster Manual* errata has no changes for these blocks. [2018 Basic Rules]() · [2014 Monster Manual, 11th printing](https://anyflip.com/kqwdv/eoae/basic/301-350) · [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)

| Beast | 2018 Basic Rules page | 2014 Monster Manual page | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bat | 119 | 318 | AC 12, 1 HP, 5-ft. walk and 30-ft. flight. Deafened disables its 60-ft. blindsight. Bite +0 targets one creature and deals 1 piercing damage. |
| Lizard | 142 | 332 | AC 10, 2 HP, 20-ft. walk and climb, darkvision 30 ft. Bite +0 deals 1 piercing damage to a single target. |
| Spider | 153 | 337 | AC 12, 1 HP, Stealth +4. Spider Climb, Web Sense and Web Walker. Bite +4 deals 1 piercing on a hit. A failed DC 9 Constitution save adds 2 (1d4) poison damage. |

All six entries are CR 0 and 10 XP. Appearance describes the selected portraits. Habitat tags are editorial encounter suggestions. I verified the three portrait hashes against the roster.
