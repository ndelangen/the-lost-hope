# Elephant, Owl and Weasel

Five encounter blocks for 2014 fifth edition. I read the September 2014 *Monster Manual* pages and checked the official errata. The two *Basic Rules* blocks follow the November 2018 v1.0 . [2018 Basic Rules ]() · [Monster Manual errata]()

| Creature | Source | Page | AC | HP | CR |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Elephant | Basic Rules (2018) | 125 | 12 | 76 | 4 |
| Elephant | Monster Manual (2014) | 322 | 12 | 76 | 4 |
| Owl | Basic Rules (2018) | 147 | 11 | 1 | 0 |
| Owl | Monster Manual (2014) | 333 | 11 | 1 | 0 |
| Weasel | Monster Manual (2014) | 340 | 13 | 1 | 0 |

- **Elephant.** Both blocks use DC 12 Trampling Charge and +8 to hit. The 2018 gives Gore 18 (3d8 + 5) and Stomp 21 (3d10 + 5). The *Monster Manual* first print matches those numbers, and the errata for that book updates them to 19 (3d8 + 6) and 22 (3d10 + 6). [Basic Rules p. 125]() · [Monster Manual errata]()
- **Owl.** The printings agree. Flyby applies when it flies out of an enemy's reach. Talons deal a flat 1 slashing damage. [Basic Rules p. 147]()
- **Weasel.** The *Monster Manual* prints Bite against one creature for a flat 1 piercing damage. The online entry uses the broader wording “one target.” This row follows the printed book. [Official online reference](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17052-weasel)

The three existing artwork files match the roster hashes. Appearance and habitat evidence identifies the editorial details and official online habitat tags.
