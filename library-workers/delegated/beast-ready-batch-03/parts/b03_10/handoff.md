# Jackal and Rhinoceros

Four encounter profiles cover the 2018 Basic Rules and 2014 Monster Manual. Each animal has the same printed mechanics in the two books. The official Monster Manual errata lists no changes for either entry. [2018 Basic Rules]() · [2014 Monster Manual errata]()

| Beast | Basic Rules | Monster Manual folio | Mechanics verified |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jackal | 141 | 331 | Small Beast, AC 12, HP 3 (1d6), CR 0 (10 XP). Keen Hearing and Smell, Pack Tactics, and a +1 Bite for 1 (1d4 − 1) piercing. Passive Perception is 13, or 18 for hearing or smell under the passive-check rule. |
| Rhinoceros | 151 | 336 | Large Beast, AC 11, HP 45 (6d10 + 12), CR 2 (450 XP). Its +7 Gore and the extra 2d8 from Charge both deal bludgeoning damage. The DC 15 Strength save controls whether a creature falls prone. |

I checked the two existing parchment images against the roster hashes. The appearance and habitat search text is editorial and follows the depicted animals.
