# Goat and Quipper

Four encounter stat blocks for two animals in the 2014 *Monster Manual* and November 2018 *Basic Rules* version 1.0. The combat rules agree across the two printings. The publisher's *Monster Manual* errata has no entry for either creature. [November 2018 Basic Rules]() · [2014 Monster Manual errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)

| Record | Basic Rules page | Monster Manual folio | AC | HP | CR and XP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Goat | 138 | 330 | 10 | 4 (1d8) | 0, 10 XP |
| Quipper | 150 | 335 | 13 | 1 (1d4 − 1) | 0, 10 XP |

- Goat's Charge deals extra damage on a qualifying Ram hit. Only a creature target makes the DC 10 Strength save against falling prone. Sure-Footed applies to Strength or Dexterity saves against being knocked prone. The habitat tags are editorial. [Basic Rules p. 138]() · [Monster Manual p. 330](https://online.anyflip.com/sqwmo/hxog/mobile/index.html#p=331)
- Quipper bites for a fixed 1 piercing damage and gains melee attack advantage against creatures missing hit points. The *Monster Manual* names cold underground lakes as one aquatic habitat. The 2018 printing retains Water Breathing and a shorter description. [Basic Rules p. 150]() · [Monster Manual p. 335](https://online.anyflip.com/sqwmo/hxog/mobile/index.html#p=336)

I checked the printed fields by hand and confirmed both selected portrait hashes against the roster.
