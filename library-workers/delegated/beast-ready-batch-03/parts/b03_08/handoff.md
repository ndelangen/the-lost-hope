# Hawk and Rat encounter rules

Four existing Beast entries have rules overlays. Hawk and Rat each have one entry from the 2014 *Monster Manual* and one from the 2018 *Basic Rules*, both under the 2014 ruleset. I checked each printing. The numbers and action effects match between the books.

| Entry | Basic Rules page | Monster Manual printed page | Checked values |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hawk | [139]() | [330](https://fliphtml5.com/vmzaj/Monster-Manual_AddPage/332/) | AC 13, HP 1 (1d4 − 1), CR 0, 10 XP. Talons +5 for 1 slashing damage. |
| Rat | [150]() | [335](https://fliphtml5.com/vmzaj/Monster-Manual_AddPage/337/) | AC 10, HP 1 (1d4 − 1), CR 0, 10 XP. Bite +0 for 1 piercing damage. |

The Hawk's Keen Sight and Rat's Keen Smell apply to checks that use the relevant sense. The overlays retain passive Perception 14 and 10 as printed. The table notes give 19 and 15 for sight- or smell-based passive checks with advantage. The Monster Manual errata changes the separate Diseased Giant Rat. It lists no changes to the ordinary Rat or Hawk. [Passive checks]() · [Monster Manual errata v2.0]()

Appearance and habitat tags are editorial. I matched both previously selected portrait files to the roster hashes. No new artwork was needed.
