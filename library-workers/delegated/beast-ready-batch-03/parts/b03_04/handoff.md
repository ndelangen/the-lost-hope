# Eagle, Octopus, and Vulture

Six encounter blocks cover the 2014 *Monster Manual* and the November 2018 *Basic Rules* v1.0. The two printings agree on all three creatures' encounter rules. I checked the books and the official *Monster Manual* errata, which list no changes to these creatures. [2018 Basic Rules]() · [Monster Manual errata]() · [Detailed sources](source-evidence.json)

| Beast | 2014 MM folio | 2018 page | Printed basics |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Eagle | 322 | 124 | AC 12, HP 3 (1d6), CR 0, 10 XP. Keen Sight and Talons +4 for 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing. |
| Octopus | 333 | 146 | AC 12, HP 3 (1d6), CR 0, 10 XP. Tentacles +4 for 1 bludgeoning and grapple on a hit, escape DC 10. |
| Vulture | 339 | 157 | AC 10, HP 5 (1d8 + 1), CR 0, 10 XP. Keen Sight and Smell, Pack Tactics, and Beak +2 for 2 (1d4) piercing. |

The Octopus releases Ink Cloud as an action underwater. It obscures a 5-ft. radius for 1 minute unless a strong current disperses it sooner. The Octopus can then Dash as a bonus action. The cloud recharges after a short or long rest. I checked the printed monster grapple and obscured-area rules used in the table note. [Octopus block]() · [Monster grapple rules]()

The 2018 folios match their one-based pages. The *Monster Manual* print witnesses show the book folios in the footer, one page lower than the mirror viewer's count.
