# Hyena and Raven

Four verified stat blocks. Each animal uses the same numbers and mechanics in the two printings. Each printing has its own record and page citation. The 2018 Basic Rules numbers match the printed folios. The Monster Manual mirror counts the cover and shows each printed folio one sheet later. I checked the official errata and found neither animal listed. [Monster Manual errata]()

- **Hyena.** CR 0, 10 XP, AC 11, 5 HP and 50-ft. walking speed. Pack Tactics gives the hyena advantage against a creature when a non-incapacitated ally of the hyena is within 5 ft. of that creature. Bite is +2 to hit and deals 3 (1d6) piercing. [Basic Rules p. 141]() · [Monster Manual printed p. 331](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page332.html)
- **Raven.** CR 0, 10 XP, AC 12, 1 HP and 50-ft. flying speed. A listener can recognize Mimicry with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check. Beak is +4 to hit and deals a flat 1 piercing. [Basic Rules p. 150]() · [Monster Manual printed p. 335](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page336.html)
