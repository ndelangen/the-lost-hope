# Dungeonland beasts

Four encounter profiles from Gary Gygax's *Dungeonland*, AD&D 1e module EX1. The archived Wizards download is byte-for-byte identical to the local . Its title page shows 1993. The publisher's current preview gives the original 1983 copyright for the same module and product number. [Archived download](https://web.archive.org/web/20030608000000id_/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/files/2/dungeonland.zip) · [Publisher preview]() · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Printed and page | Encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Crocodile | 5 | Pool ambush in the Long Hall, 42 HP |
| Giant Poisonous Snake | 12 | Woods Wandering Monster Table, result 2–3, 26 HP |
| Giant Dog | 13 | Meadow, 70 HP |
| Giant Tick | 13 | Three ticks in the dog's fur, with 19, 18, and 17 HP |

The crocodile's weapon restrictions and drowning rules stay with the pool encounter. The snake's table gives poison damage on a successful save and leaves the failed-save result unstated. The dog keeps the printed single prime in its movement. The tick profile keeps each printed HP value and follows the abbreviated encounter's wording for the drain. The 5e Giant Tick remains a separate Monstrosity record. [Exact pages and hashes](source-evidence.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

The four new paintings use plain parchment. The crocodile and snake keep visible space around their tail tips. The module gives no colors for these animals.
