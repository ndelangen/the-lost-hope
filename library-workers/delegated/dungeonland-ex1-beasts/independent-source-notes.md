## Giant Crocodile

Printed p. 5 (p. 5), “The Long Hall,” in the pool encounter. One Giant Crocodile, AC 4, move 6″/12″, HD 7, 42 HP, two attacks. Bite deals 3–18, tail smash 2–20. It surprises on 1–3 on a d6. The core 1e *Monster Manual* uses the same movement, AC, HD, and basic damage, and lists animal intelligence. [Dungeonland p. 5][croc] · [1e Monster Manual, Crocodile][mm]

This crocodile waits quietly on either flank of the swimmers. Only stabbing weapons affect it in this underwater encounter. Water resistance and distortion impose −2 to hit. It tries to drag anyone it bites to the pool bottom, where drowning takes 2–4 rounds. Cumulative damage of 10 or more makes it release the victim and attack another. It can still use its tail against opponents in the 180-degree arc behind it. The room gives no treasure and no crocodile-specific coloration. [Dungeonland p. 5][croc]

## Giant Poisonous Snake

Printed p. 12 (p. 12), Woods Wandering Monster Table, result 2–3. One Giant Poisonous Snake, AC 5, move 15″, HD 4+2, 26 HP, one attack for 1–3 damage. The poison deals 3–18 damage even on a successful save. The encounter line stops there and gives no separate failed-save consequence, onset, or save modifier. The original 1e *Monster Manual* poisonous variant describes the same successful-save damage and lists Animal intelligence. The text gives no individual markings. [Dungeonland p. 12][snake] · [1e Monster Manual, Giant Snake][mm]

## Giant Dog

Printed p. 13 (p. 13), Wilds of Dungeonland, Area A “Meadow.” One Giant Dog, AC 3, move printed as 18′, HD 10+10, 70 HP, one attack for 5–30. The digit is followed by a single prime in this stat line. The nearby Giant Ticks use a double prime. [Dungeonland p. 13][meadow]

The dog is furry and nearly elephant-sized. It approaches to play unless harmed. Its play has a 1-in-20 chance of knocking a character over for 1–6 damage. Once provoked, an attack score of 20 grabs the opponent in its jaws. It flees at double speed to its place behind Area C and deals 2–12 additional damage per round while it holds the victim. Once out of sight, it stops and eats the captured victim. The encounter gives no separate escape check. It settles after eating one week of normal rations or two weeks of iron rations, or after a turn of playing with a thrown stick, staff, or pole. [Dungeonland p. 13][meadow]

## Giant Tick

Same Meadow page. Searching the dog’s fur reveals three “Giant Ticks,” each AC 3, move 3″, HD 3, with 19, 18, and 17 HP respectively. Each has one attack dealing 1–4. The initial hit attaches the tick, followed by 1–6 blood drain. Each bitten character has a 50% chance of contracting the encounter’s unnamed disease. EX1 states the drain happens thereafter and supplies no interval, detachment rule, or disease progression. The 1e *Monster Manual* p. 94 separately specifies drain each melee round and describes detachment and the disease. Treat that source as context for the abbreviated EX1 entry. [Dungeonland p. 13][meadow] · [1e Monster Manual, Giant Tick][mm]

Catalog this AD&D encounter animal as a Beast. EX1 does not use a modern creature-type field. The separately cataloged 5e *Bigby Presents: Glory of the Giants* **Giant Tick** is a Monstrosity and belongs to its own edition and publication. None of the four AD&D entries already appears under *Dungeonland* in the primary library. [Dungeonland p. 13][meadow] · [D&D Beyond Giant Tick][bigby]
