# Gloaming rules sources

- *Underdark* (October 2003), pp. 12–13, gives the playable species and racial traits. It explicitly grants low-light vision. The outsider type also supplies 60-foot darkvision. The species entry gives LA +2 and lists no racial Hit Dice. [Underdark]() · [Monster Manual]()
- Gloamings fly at 40 ft. with average maneuverability. The core rule limits flight to a light load or less. Abrupt bright light blinds for one round, and the −1 circumstance penalty on attack rolls, saves, and checks lasts as long as the bright light does. [Underdark, p. 13]() · [Core flight rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#fly)
- The Gloaming region adds Common and defines bonus languages. Portal Sensitive allows a DC 20 Search check for active or inactive portals and grants the check when someone with the feat passes within 5 ft. The +2 caster-level bonus applies when discerning portal properties with *analyze portal*. [Gloaming region, p. 23]() · [Portal Sensitive, p. 27]()
- The collected Wizards errata archive has no *Underdark* file. The June 2008 FAQ has no entry for Gloaming or Portal Sensitive. [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Iskra and Kes are original NPCs. CR 2 is a provisional encounter estimate for each. ECL 3 follows from 1 class Hit Die and LA +2. The portrait depicts Iskra with her luminescence off, using the book’s anatomical description and Big Linda’s parchment treatment. [Gloaming description, p. 12]()
