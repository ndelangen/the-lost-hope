# Gloaming encounters

Both Gloamings have 60-ft. darkvision, low-light vision, a 20-ft. land speed, and a 40-ft. fly speed with average maneuverability. They need a light load to fly. They can set their skin light from off to torch brightness with a standard action. Sudden bright light blinds them for one round, and bright light imposes a −1 circumstance penalty on attack rolls, saves, and checks.

## Iskra Sablewing, shaft guide

**Sorcerer 1, provisional CR 2.** AC 14, HP 6, initiative +7. Fortitude +2, Reflex +3, Will +2. Light crossbow +4 for 1d6 piercing. *Mage armor* raises AC to 18 for one hour. *Silent image* has Will DC 14 on interaction.

The ladder ends above a ledge. Iskra sets her glow to torch brightness before flying down the open shaft. She shows the party where to land and touches down before carrying any pack that would push her above her 19½-lb. light load. In bright light her crossbow attack falls to +3, Search drops to −1, and the three saves become +1, +2, and +1 before situational bonuses.

## Kes Hollowstep, portal scout

**Rogue 1, provisional CR 2.** AC 17, HP 8, initiative +4. Fortitude +2, Reflex +6, Will +0. Short sword +1 or shortbow +5, each for 1d4 piercing. Sneak Attack adds 1d6 when eligible.

Kes follows the gallery wall with his glow off. When he passes within 5 ft. of a hidden active or inactive portal, roll Search against DC 20. With Search +6, he needs a 14 or higher. If a shutter suddenly exposes him to daylight, he takes cover until his sight returns. With Search +5 in sustained bright light, he needs a 15 or higher.
