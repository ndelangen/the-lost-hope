# Grimlock

This batch adds the playable Grimlock from the October 2003 *Underdark*. Diamond Hand and Cold Water are original NPCs. Diamond Hand’s plain-parchment portrait represents the 3.5 species profile.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and both encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/grimlock-draft.png). The [comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Diamond Hand beside the approved Big Linda portrait.

In a temporary copy, search found the species by name, blindsight, scent, and both NPC roles. The portrait loaded for the 2003 profile and as its species thumbnail. The existing monster entry, including its versions and portraits, matched the baseline. [Verification](verification.json)
