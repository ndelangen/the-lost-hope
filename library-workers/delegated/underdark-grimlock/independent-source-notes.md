# Grimlock rules sources

- The playable Grimlock is a Medium Monstrous Humanoid with 2d8 racial Hit Dice and LA +2. The racial traits give +4 Strength, +2 Dexterity, +2 Constitution, −2 Wisdom, and −4 Charisma, with +4 natural armor and +10 to Hide in mountains or underground. The two racial Hit Dice grant one feat, and battleaxe proficiency is a racial bonus feat. [Racial traits, pp. 14–15]()
- Blindsight reaches 40 ft. with line of effect. Losing either hearing or smell suppresses blindsight and leaves the benefit of Blind-Fight. Losing both leaves the Grimlock effectively blinded. Scent normally detects an opponent within 30 ft. A move action reveals the direction, and the Grimlock can pinpoint the source within 5 ft. [Racial traits, p. 14]() · [Core special abilities](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm)
- *Underdark* gives playable Grimlocks Common and Undercommon. The Grimlock region adds Terran and gives its own bonus-language list. The core rulebook has a separate Grimlock ability and language list. The core monster’s Alertness and bonus Track belong to its stat block. Cold Water chooses Track as his racial Hit Die feat. [Grimlock region, p. 23]() · [Monster Manual, pp. 140–141]()
- The collected Wizards errata archive has no *Underdark* file. The June 2008 FAQ mentions a Grimlock in an example about polymorph and equipment, with no correction to the Grimlock traits. [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The portrait follows the written description of an eyeless face without sockets, scarred and slightly scaled gray skin, and a leather harness. Vance Kovacs illustrated the Grimlock in the original book. Both staged CRs are provisional estimates for the original NPCs. [Description and illustration, pp. 14–15]()
