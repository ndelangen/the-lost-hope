# Grimlock encounters

Both NPCs locate foes within 40 ft. with blindsight and line of effect. Beyond 40 ft., targets have total concealment. If either hearing or smell is lost, blindsight stops and the Grimlock functions as if it had Blind-Fight. If both senses are lost, it is effectively blinded. Scent normally detects a presence within 30 ft. Finding its direction takes a move action, and within 5 ft. the Grimlock can pinpoint the source.

## Diamond Hand, passage guardian

**Barbarian 1 with 2 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 2.** AC 16, HP 29, initiative +6, speed 40 ft. Fortitude +5, Reflex +5, Will +3. Two-handed battleaxe +7 for 1d8+6 slashing.

Diamond Hand holds the bend so her companion can retreat. If caught in *silence*, she uses scent to locate nearby foes and can reroll the miss chance if concealment foils a melee hit. When she rages, her AC drops to 14, she gains 6 HP, and her battleaxe becomes +9 for 1d8+9. Rage lasts up to eight rounds. She loses the extra HP when it ends and is fatigued for the rest of the encounter.

## Cold Water, fissure tracker

**2 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 1.** AC 16, HP 16, initiative +2, speed 30 ft. Fortitude +2, Reflex +5, Will +3. Two-handed battleaxe +5 for 1d8+4 slashing.

Cold Water follows a fresh scent through the fissure at half speed. Roll Survival +0 against a typical DC of 10. He pauses at the fork to use scent for direction. If an overpowering odor spoils the trail, he returns to familiar ground.
