# Proposed playable profiles, batch 12

50 claims for overlap review from Arcanomicon’s 2021 Tales of Arcana 5E Race Guide, ISBN 978-0-578-91936-2. The book uses the 2014 fifth-edition rules.

I compared the names against eleven prior stages and catalog commit `aabd361`. 48 names have no exact catalog match. 2 already appear as encounters.

I checked each printed trait heading against the book and used the contents to locate its spread. The TSV records alternate spellings and scope where they matter. Keep printed variants within their named profile. Read the book’s common rules on pp. 24–29 and check any trait cross-references before building an NPC. Appendix A’s Wings rules start on p. 469.
