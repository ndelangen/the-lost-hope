# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual lizard source check

TSR first published *Monstrous Manual*, product 2140, in 1993. The checked print scan identifies itself as the January 1994 third printing. Printed p. 226, viewer page 229, contains the table, all four descriptions and the family art. Its image SHA-256 is `d1b3ffc1d8138232fd79c491ec97099c3e8acbd2049a287b121d13bb1c62819a`. Printed p. 227 begins the separate Lizard Man entry. The reflowed version puts the complete Lizard entry on pp. 639–641. All four are solitary, active during the day, carnivorous and neutral, with nil magic resistance. “No. Appearing” gives encounter numbers separately from the solitary organization.

## Fire Lizard

- Any warm land, very rare, Animal (1) intelligence, treasure B, Q ×10, S and T, 1–4 appearing. AC 3, movement 9, HD 10, THAC0 11, three attacks for 1–8/1–8/2–16, Gargantuan (30 ft.), morale steady (11–12), 3,000 XP. Its special attack and defense refer to the prose.
- It rakes with its claws and bites. It can breathe fire simultaneously in a cone 5 feet wide at the mouth, 10 feet at the end, and 15 feet long. The cone deals 2–12 fire damage, half on a successful save vs. breath weapon. The lizard is immune to fire-based attacks. The entry supplies no reuse limit.
- It resembles a wingless red dragon, with gray skin, a mottled red and brown back and reddish undersides. Hatchlings are light gray and darken with age. It prefers subterranean lairs, hunts outside about every two weeks and sleeps half the time. A lair has a 10% chance of 1–4 eggs. The entry treats its resemblance to dragons as a possible evolutionary relationship and places it in the Lizard family.
- The independently staged *Undermountain: Stardock* encounter has one specimen with 60 hp and morale 11. Its scales are reddish and its underside is bright red. It uses the same general attack, cone and fire immunity with its own encounter tactics. The TSR 2140 base profile has no fixed hp. [Stardock source notes]()

## Giant Lizard

- Any warm land, uncommon, Non- (0) intelligence, no treasure, 2–12 (2d6) appearing. AC 5, movement 15, HD 3+1, THAC0 17, one attack for 1–8, Huge (15 ft.), morale average (8–10), 175 XP. Its special attack refers to the prose, and its special defenses are nil.
- The bite traps its victim on an attack score of 20 and deals doubled damage of 2–16. The lizard continues to deal 2–16 damage each round thereafter. The entry gives no repeated attack roll or separate escape roll for this hold.
- It is a scaled-up lizard living in marshes and swamps. It hunts opportunistically and is sometimes kept by lizard men as a mount, beast of burden or food animal. Its subsection gives no fixed colors.

## Minotaur Lizard

- Tropical hills and mountains, rare, Non- (0) intelligence, treasure J–N, Q and C (magic), 1–8 appearing. AC 5, movement 6, HD 8, THAC0 13, three attacks for 2–12/2–12/3–18, Gargantuan (40 ft.), morale average (8–10), 975 XP. Its special attack refers to the prose, and its special defenses are nil. “Huge” in the opening description is ordinary prose. The table gives size G.
- It attacks with claws and teeth. Its ambush imposes −5 on opponents’ surprise rolls. An attack roll of 20 traps the target in its jaws, after which it deals 3–18 automatically each round until the target escapes or dies. The target cannot attack during the following round. The entry gives no escape check or repeated inability to attack beyond that wording.
- The male has minotaur-like horns thought to attract mates. Those horns are not used for attacks. The text places it near copper and red dragons. It gives no color.

## Subterranean Lizard

- Any subterranean terrain, uncommon, Non- (0) intelligence, treasure O, P and Q ×5, 1–6 appearing. AC 5, movement 12, HD 6, THAC0 15, one attack for 2–12, Huge (20 ft.), morale average (8–10), 650 XP. Its special attack refers to the prose, and its special defenses are nil.
- Its suction cup-tipped feet let it run on walls and ceilings. A bite roll of 20 clamps the victim and doubles that bite to 4–24. The victim automatically takes an additional 2–12 each following round. The entry gives no new escape check for this jaw hold.
- Some forms are albino and attack at −1 in daylight or similar light. Other forms have tongues up to 20 feet long. A human-sized or smaller target already caught by the tongue is pulled to the mouth and bitten the next round unless it makes a bend bars roll. The tongue procedure supplies no attack-roll mechanic or listed damage of its own, and its escape roll applies before the bite. The lizards do not willingly leave their caves.

## Illustration and catalog

Printed p. 226 shows an unlabeled, ordinary-looking four-legged lizard with a curved tail, green-blue scales and yellow mottling. Neither a size cue nor a signature identifies the form or artist. It provides a general silhouette for the family. P. 227 has Tony DiTerlizzi’s signed Lizard Man, which his 1993 production checklist identifies by name. That credit concerns the separate humanoid entry.
