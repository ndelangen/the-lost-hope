# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual lizards

Fire, Giant, Minotaur, and Subterranean Lizard come from printed page 226 of the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. All four use the catalog's Animal grouping. Fire Lizard shares a catalog entry with the separately staged *Undermountain: Stardock* specimen. Each publication keeps its own version. *Stardock* specifies one creature with 60 HP. The handbook gives 10 Hit Dice and 1–4 appearing. [Printed page 226](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/a0f6b5e20e2016196f123259bf095c9b.webp) · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Lizard | Printed combat rules |
| --- | --- |
| Fire | The 15-foot cone can accompany its two claws and bite. It deals 2–12 fire damage, halved on a successful breath-weapon save. Fire attacks cannot harm the lizard. |
| Giant | An attack score of 20 doubles the bite to 2–16 and holds the target for 2–16 damage each following round. |
| Minotaur | Opponents take −5 to surprise. A roll of 20 traps the target for automatic 3–18 on following rounds. The target cannot attack the following round. The male's horns are for display. |
| Subterranean | A roll of 20 doubles the bite to 4–24 and deals another 2–12 each following round. The albino daylight penalty and the long-tongued form's bend bars escape belong to separate variants. |

The portraits use plain parchment with full silhouettes. The Fire Lizard follows the printed gray, red and brown coloration and has no wings. The Giant Lizard's dark blue-green and yellow markings draw on the unlabeled family illustration. The Minotaur Lizard shows the male's paired display horns, with an artist-chosen umber palette. The Subterranean Lizard depicts the albino variant and shows the suction pads on its toes. The p. 226 illustration has no confirmed individual artist.
