# Duskling for D&D 3.5

One playable Duskling version from the September 2005 first printing of *Magic of Incarnum*. The race adds one permanent essentia point. Its movement feature gives +5 ft. per invested point with light or no armor and a light load. Reallocation takes a swift action. The optional first-level Barbarian substitution uses a d10 Hit Die and gives +10 ft. and +2 initiative per invested point. Its speed bonus has a wider armor and load allowance. [Original traits, p. 10]() · [Substitution, pp. 43–44]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Iriavel Solthenar is an original Totemist 1 and ropeway guide. His card shows three ways to use two essentia points across movement, teleport range, and Jump. Darevia Thalliar is an original Barbarian 1 using the substitution. Her card gives speed, initiative, and rage totals. Both are provisional CR 1. Iriavel’s full-body portrait uses muted plain parchment. His steely blue-gray skin, long face, and full beard follow the original description. [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Framing and parchment before and after](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [96 px thumbnail](portrait-thumbnails.jpg)
