# Duskling encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. They are Medium Fey with the Incarnum subtype, low-light vision, 30-ft. base land speed, Common, and Sylvan. They are Extraplanar in these Material Plane encounters. Both can reallocate essentia among the eligible options as a swift action. [Original racial traits]() · [Essentia, p. 50]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Iriavel Solthenar, ropeway guide

Totemist 1. Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 15, touch 12, flat-footed 13. HP 11, initiative +2. Fort +5, Ref +4, Will +1. BAB +0, grapple +1. STR 12, DEX 14, CON 16, INT 10, WIS 12, CHA 10.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Club | +1 melee, 1d6+1 bludgeoning, ×2, 5-ft. reach |
| Light crossbow | +2 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment. Reload as a move action. |

Knowledge (nature) +4, Listen +7, Spot +7, Survival +5. Listen and Spot include Alertness. Wild Empathy +1. Class illiteracy applies.

Meldshaper level 1, two unbound melds, two essentia total, capacity 1 per investment. *Blink shirt* teleports Iriavel alone as a standard action. He takes no further actions until his next turn. *Landshark boots* add +4 competence to Jump or +6 when invested.

| Investments | Speed with light gear | Teleport | Jump |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Racial speed and shirt | 35 ft. | 20 ft. | +4 |
| Boots and shirt | 30 ft. | 20 ft. | +6 |
| Racial speed and boots | 35 ft. | 10 ft. | +6 |

The first row is the default. Iriavel can carry a line across the snapped ropeway if the party braces the anchor. [Totemist, p. 29]() · [*Blink shirt*]() · [*Landshark boots*]()

## Darevia Thalliar, forest corridor watch

Barbarian 1 with the Duskling substitution. Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 14, touch 11, flat-footed 13. HP 13 from 1d10+3. Fort +5, Ref +1, Will +1. BAB +1, grapple +3. STR 15, DEX 13, CON 16, INT 10, WIS 12, CHA 8.

| Attack | Ordinary | Raging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greatclub, ×2 | +3 melee, 1d10+3 bludgeoning | +5 melee, 1d10+6 bludgeoning |
| Javelin, ×2, 30-ft. increment | +2 ranged, 1d6+2 piercing | +2 ranged, 1d6+4 piercing |

Climb +5, Knowledge (the planes) +4, Survival +5. Her one essentia normally goes into Incarnum Speed. With her light gear it gives speed 40 ft., initiative +7, and Jump +9. Without the investment she has speed 30 ft., initiative +5, and Jump +5. Moving the point to the racial feature gives speed 35 ft., initiative +5, and Jump +5. Class illiteracy applies.

Rage once per day and at most once per encounter, up to 8 rounds. Raging gives HP 15, AC 12, touch 9, flat-footed 11, grapple +5, Fort +7, Ref +1, Will +3, STR 19, and CON 20. Climb is +7 and Jump is +11 at 40 ft. Remove 2 bonus HP after the rage. Fatigue lasts for the rest of the encounter. It gives −2 Strength and Dexterity and prevents running or charging. [Barbarian substitution, pp. 43–44]()

Darevia needs help finding a delayed family before the forest corridor closes for the night. She guides the party between clearings and guards the narrow opening if something follows.
