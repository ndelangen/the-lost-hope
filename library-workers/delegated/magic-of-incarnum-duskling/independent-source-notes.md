# Independent Duskling reviews

The source reviewer checked the September 2005 first printing of *Magic of Incarnum*. Mark Poole drew the Duskling pair on p. 11. The appearance text gives a robust build, steely blue-gray skin, long hair, and a long, somewhat foxlike face. The traits are on p. 10. The sample blocks confirm Medium size and LA +0. The reviewer found no dedicated title errata or Duskling-specific ruling in Wizards’ collected archive. [Original rules and appearance]() · [Art and planar heritage]() · [Wizards archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The racial speed feature needs light or no armor and a light load. The Barbarian’s Incarnum Speed works with up to medium armor and medium load and replaces Fast Movement. Both changes to land speed are enhancement bonuses. The subtype marks applicable effects and supplies no extra benefits. [Essentia capacity]() · [Substitutions]() · [Incarnum subtype]()

Published samples are Warrior 1, CR 1/2, Barbarian 3, CR 3, and Totemist 4, CR 4. The Warrior’s printed longbow critical entry differs from the core weapon table. Iriavel and Darevia are original and have their own provisional CRs. [Published examples]()

The second reviewer checked both NPCs’ 16 skill points and verified Iriavel’s two-point reserve, capacity, and melds. The review also confirmed Darevia’s d10, speed and initiative totals, and eight-round rage. Both original NPCs resolve only under the 2005 publication.
