# Corsairs of the Great Sea beasts

This bundle adds four original AD&D 2e entries from Nicky Rea's 1994 TSR boxed set. The eel and sharks use their named encounter blocks. The Addazahr uses the matching Monstrous Compendium leaf. The local matches the in the archived Wizards ZIP byte for byte. The summary chart confirms their listed movement, THAC0, attacks, morale, and XP. [Archived publisher download](https://web.archive.org/web/20101014100000id_/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/files/tsr9449corsairs.zip) · [Exact pages and hashes](source-evidence.json) · [Independent source check](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Addazahr (Backbiter) | Unnumbered supplement leaf, p. 100 | 6, Fl 18 (B) | 1 per insect |
| Electric Eel | Adventure Book p. 24 | Sw 12 | 16 |
| Shark | Adventure Book p. 52 | Sw 24 | 40 each |
| Giant Shark | Adventure Book p. 52 | Sw 18 | 68 each |

The eel's shock ranges overlap at exactly 5 ft. in the original, and the profile keeps that wording. Giant Shark can swallow on an attack roll at least four above the minimum needed to hit. The eel's starting distance and the timing of the Addazahr camel encounter are in the encounter sections.

The ordinary Shark reuses the approved *Wakewyrm's Fury* Hunter Shark portrait. The other three are new built-in ImageGen drafts on plain parchment. The revised Addazahr has darker sepia body lines and wing veins for the 96-pixel thumbnail. The printed drawing guides its shape, and the supplement leaf carries no separate artist credit. The Adventure Book lists Karl Waller for interior art.
