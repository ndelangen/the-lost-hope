# Al-Qadim Corsairs of the Great Sea beast source review

Nicky Rea designed *Corsairs of the Great Sea* for AD&D 2e. The original boxed set is TSR 9449, ©1994, ISBN 1-56076-867-3. Its Adventure Book credits Karl Waller for interior art on printed p. 2 (p. 3). The compiled 128-page puts the Adventure Book first and the unnumbered Monstrous Compendium supplement after the Campaign Guide. I visually checked each original stat block. [Archived Wizards download] · [TSR page scan]

## Addazahr (Backbiter)

The stat leaf is unnumbered, p. 100, and carries supplement code 9449XXX1903. Its exact heading is “Addazahr (Backbiter).” Animal intelligence (1), neutral, Tiny (2 in. long), AC 4, 1 HP, move 6 and fly 18 (B), THAC0 20, one attack for 1 damage plus disease, morale Unsteady (6), XP 15. They appear in swarms of 6–60, with the stat block giving individual figures. Special defenses and magic resistance are nil. A hit destroys one. Ordinary or magical fire and cold can harm them. Wetting alone causes no special harm, but a fully submerged insect that cannot get out can drown. [Monstrous Compendium supplement][addazahr]

The bite causes joint pain and swelling in humans and non-burden animals unless they save versus paralyzation. The separate paragraph on mounts and beasts of burden describes back pain and weakness that prevent carrying loads or moving far. That paragraph gives no separate save or onset. *Cure disease* treats either case, and recovery without magic takes approximately two weeks. The leaf mentions an unconfirmed three-day mundane remedy. On Adventure Book p. 8 (p. 9), the afflicted camels show symptoms on the caravan’s second night. A Desert Survival or Healing proficiency check identifies the cause in that encounter. Neither the encounter schedule nor its identification check is a generic bite rule. [Monstrous Compendium supplement][addazahr] · [To Steal the Steel][camel-encounter]

The profile describes a nonaggressive, pale amber, unusually slender insect with fragile translucent-looking wings, most often found in the seasonal grasslands and deserts of Zakhara. The terrain row also includes hills and plains in tropical, subtropical, and temperate climates. The printed leaf shows a line drawing of the insect without a separate figure caption or illustrator credit on that leaf. [Monstrous Compendium supplement][addazahr]

## Electric Eel

The exact encounter heading is “Electric eel (1),” Adventure Book p. 24 (p. 25). Nonintelligent, neutral, AC 9, swim 12, HD 2, 16 HP, THAC0 16, one attack for 1d3, Large (9 ft. long), morale 7, 65 XP. Electric discharge is once per hour and centered on the eel, with a 15-ft. radius. It deals 3d8 electrical damage within 5 ft., 2d8 at 5–10 ft., and 1d8 elsewhere in range. The two printed ranges meet at exactly 5 ft. The source lists no saving throw, damage halving, or separate discharge attack roll. [Of Waters Dark and Deep stat block][electric-eel]

The Reception Pool on printed p. 23 (p. 24) contains a water-filled pit where the eel attacks when disturbed. Someone who falls in starts within 5 ft. of it by default unless that character flees immediately on seeing it. That starting distance applies to the encounter. The text gives length and aquatic context but no colors. [Reception Pool setup][eel-setup] · [Electric Eel stat block][electric-eel]

## Shark and Giant Shark

Both appear on Adventure Book p. 52 (p. 53) under “Sharks (6)” and “Giant Sharks (2).” Both are neutral and use the printed Non- intelligence entry. The nearby p. 51 scene (p. 52) places the malenti’s shark allies in the water waiting for forced swimmers. This describes their role at Terrapin Isle. [Terrapin Isle encounter][shark-encounter] · [Shark stat blocks][sharks]

- **Shark.** AC 6, swim 24, HD 7, 40 HP, THAC0 13, one attack for 3d4, Large (12 ft.), morale 10, 420 XP each. Its line gives no named special attack. [Shark stat blocks][sharks]
- **Giant Shark.** AC 5, swim 18, HD 11, 68 HP, THAC0 9, one attack for 4d4, Gargantuan (30 ft.), morale 11, 3,000 XP each. It can swallow the target when the attack roll is at least four above the minimum roll needed to hit that target. A swallowed creature suffers 20 damage per round and can attack from inside at a cumulative −1 per round. It escapes by killing the shark. The block gives no additional saving throw, size limit, attack type, or precise damage tick within a round. [Shark stat blocks][sharks]

The Adventure Book’s Monster Statistics Chart on p. 64 (p. 65) lists Addazahr, Eel, electric, Shark, and Shark, Giant. Its AC, movement, HD or 1 HP, THAC0, attacks, morale and XP agree with the corresponding full profiles. It omits the eel and sharks’ fixed encounter HP and gives only their basic attack damage. The existing primary catalog has *Giant Shark* in later editions and no exact *Corsairs* entries for these four. [Monster Statistics Chart][summary]
