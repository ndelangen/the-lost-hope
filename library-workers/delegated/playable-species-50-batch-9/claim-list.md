# Proposed playable profiles, batch 9

50 claims for ownership review before dispatch. Each entry records its canonical name, aliases, publication, edition, and printed trait location. Both volumes are Tyler Kamstra’s player conversions for the 2014 fifth-edition rules.

Compared canonical names with eight prior staged rosters and the committed catalog at `41d0241`. 6 names have no exact catalog match and 44 match only non-player entries.

[Download the claims TSV](claims.tsv)

Use the consulted player trait blocks and the supplement rules for Tiny characters, equipment, spell components, and healing where applicable. Each species has one claim for its printed forms and any related feat. Keep encounter stats separate. The first volume locators use the v2 text. The sequel uses its own folios.
