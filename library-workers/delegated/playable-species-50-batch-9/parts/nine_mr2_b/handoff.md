# Monstrous Races 2, batch 9 group B

These five profiles use Tyler Kamstra's player trait blocks. The printed page numbers below come from the full-text mirror. Each has one linked original NPC and one square ImageGen portrait. [Printed text]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/en/product/248038/monstrous-races-2)

| Profile | Printed rules | Worked NPC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spawn of Kyuss | pp. 68–69, Spawn of Kyuss Traits | Rusk Ditchwatch, Fighter 2. Hide, Dexterity, shield, and Defense give AC 17. HP 22. Handaxe +5 for 1d6+3. Worm DC 13. |
| Trapper | p. 71, Trapper Traits | Lintel, Barbarian 2. Natural AC 15. HP 25. Smother +5 for 1d8+3 and 2 more while raging. A hit qualifies for the bonus-action grapple at Athletics +5. |
| Vargouille | pp. 71–72, Vargouille Traits | Pip Sablewing, Rogue 1. Tiny, AC 12, HP 10. Finesse bite +4 with piercing and poison dice. Both trait DCs are 12. |
| Vegepygmy | p. 73, Vegepygmy Traits. p. 89, Vegepygmy Chief. | Dewcap Reed, standard Fighter 1. Leather and shield give AC 15. HP 12. One-handed spear +4 for 1d6+4 with Dueling. |
| Xvart | p. 76, Xvart Traits | Tiv Ragpocket, Rogue 1. Leather and Dexterity give AC 14. HP 10. Shortbow +5 for 1d6+3. |

I used the trait block where the commentary differs. Trapper has blindsight. The Vargouille's printed ability increase field is blank, and the darkvision mentioned in the play advice has no corresponding trait or range. The printed Kiss targets a visible creature within 5 ft. and leaves the exact Restoration spell open. I read the unqualified Shriek as affecting Pip as well as everyone else in range, and used piercing for Pip's Sneak Attack.

Vegepygmy retains the standard and Thorny forms together. Chief stays an optional feat for the standard form. Xvart's Warlock of Raxivort is class advancement with suggested invocations and an open patron choice.
