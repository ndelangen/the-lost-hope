I preserved Cow, Deep Rothe, Ox, Rothe, and Stench Kow under the one Cattle profile. Flintmoss knows the Wisdom-based cantrip from Deep Rothe and does not use it in the encounter because the build has no way to handle its material component. The Monk's bonus strikes use its class die and bludgeoning damage as directed by the supplement. [Player rules and Monk guidance]() · [2014 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

Ashwhistle uses the printed master bond and sunlight timing. The build treats it as nonspeaking and leaves the unspecified deadline for finding a new master to the table. Bellcurrent uses ordinary breath holding and unarmored AC because the printed trait block supplies no special breath duration or natural armor formula. Cindercoil's Infest uses its bite to hold a target under the ordinary Tiny-to-Small grapple limit. The printed package remains narrow and lacks blindsight. The author's design discussion calls it weak and suggests optional benefits, which the build leaves out.
