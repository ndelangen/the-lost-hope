# Roc, Roper, Scarecrow, Shield Guardian, and Slaad

These five player profiles use Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races* v2 text for the 2014 rules. Each has a linked NPC and one original square parchment portrait. [Published player text]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 combat rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

| Profile and folio | Worked example |
| --- | --- |
| Roc, p. 178 | Galehook, Barbarian 1. AC 15 from 13 + Dex 2. HP 14 from d12 + Con 2. Talons hit at +5 and deal 1d8 + 3, or 1d8 + 5 in Rage. Medium body and 50-ft. flight. |
| Roper, pp. 178–179 | Lintel, Barbarian 1. AC 16 from 15 + Dex 1. HP 14 from d12 + Con 2. Athletics +5, tendril break DC 12. Grappling and Reeling each use the single attack. The optional bite uses normal reach. |
| Scarecrow, p. 184 | Tatter Reed, Rogue 1. AC 14 from leather 11 + Dex 3. HP 9 from d8 + Con 1. Rapier +5 for 1d8 + 3 and qualifying Sneak Attack. Gaze DC 12. |
| Shield Guardian, p. 187 | Slate Ward, Fighter 1. AC 20 from its body 18 + shield 2. HP 12 from d10 + Con 2. Mace +5 for 1d6 + 3 Strength + 2 Dueling. Its racial reaction grants a nearby ally +2 AC against one attack. |
| Slaad, pp. 189–190 | Lichen Mouth, green Slaad Fighter 1. AC 15 from leather 11 + Dex 2 + shield 2. HP 11 from d10 + Con 1. Claw +4 for 1d6 + 2, fire bolt +4 for 1d10. One shared profile holds the printed Blue, Red, Green/Gray/Death, and Tadpole options. |

I treated the Scarecrow's mention of a stalagmite as a copy error. Its extracted language line appears before the heading, and the play advice confirms that it cannot speak. The printed player Shield Guardian begins without its amulet bond, grants no poison immunity in the trait block, and recovers HP by resting. I read the Slaad's printed *fire bold* as *fire bolt*. Gray and Death progression changes appearance and standing under the same printed statistics.
