# Monstrous Races 2 player conversions

Five profiles and their original NPC builds use Tyler Kamstra's DMs Guild player conversions for 2014 fifth edition. I read the full-text mirror for the trait blocks and checked the shared rules and contents against the DMs Guild preview. Printed folios are listed here and in the source evidence. ## Printed gaps and table readings

- Elder Brain Mind Blast specifies a cone before referring to a line. Vellum uses the 15-ft. cone. It relies on blindsight and uses ki and class strikes without hands or speech. The profile treats the specifically granted innate psionics as an exception to the restriction on ordinary verbal components. Vellum has yet to reach either spell's unlock level.
- The Quickling design notes mention darkvision. Bram uses the printed player traits, which omit it.
- Sea Spawn groups all three printed adaptations. Neris selects bite and tentacle and uses the ordinary breathing rules, since the trait block grants swim speed without an Amphibious trait.
- The Shadow Mastiff design discussion suggests a dim-light armor bonus. Its player trait block provides Shadow Blend and Sunlight Weakness. Sable Bell uses that printed block and carries no hand-held gear.
- Slithering Tracker has a specific Life Leech grapple despite having no hands. For Fenline's Damage Transfer, split the damage first, round down, then apply each recipient's applicable damage modifiers. The book does not specify this order.
