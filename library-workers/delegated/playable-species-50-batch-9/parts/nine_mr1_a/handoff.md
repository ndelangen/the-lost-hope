# Monstrous Races v2 player conversions

Five original NPCs use Tyler Kamstra's DMs Guild player traits and 2014 fifth-edition classes. The printed folios below refer to the v2 text. Siltcap uses Violet Fungus. Its profile also includes Gas Spore and Shrieker. [V2 rules]() · [DMs Guild listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [Class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Profile | Printed folio | Build check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bullywug | p. 28 | Quib Reedsocket, Fighter 1. AC 16 adds Dexterity +2, shield +2, and Defense +1 to leather AC 11. HP 12. Spear +5 for 1d6 + 3. |
| Fomorian | pp. 82–83 | Marn Splitknuckle, Barbarian 2. AC 14 is 10 + 2 Dexterity + 2 Constitution. HP 23 comes from 14 + 9. Maul +5 for 2d6 + 3, or 2d6 + 5 while raging. Evil Eye DC 11 uses Wisdom +1. |
| Fungi | pp. 84–85 | Siltcap, Violet Fungus Monk 2. AC 15 is 10 + 3 Dexterity + 2 Wisdom. HP 17 comes from 10 + 7. Rotting Touch +5 for 1d6 + 3 necrotic. Martial Arts and Flurry bonus attacks use 1d4 + 3 bludgeoning under the supplement's p. 7 rule. |
| Gorgon | pp. 108–109 | Brackplate, Fighter 2. Metal Skin AC 18. HP 20 comes from 12 + 8. Hoof +5 for 1d8 + 3. Petrifying Breath DC 12 uses Constitution +2. |
| Hell Hound | p. 117 | Coalwhistle, Fighter 1. Natural AC 15 is 13 + 2 Dexterity. HP 11. Bite +5 for 1d4 + 3 slashing and 1d4 fire. Fire Breath DC 11 uses Constitution +1. |

The Bullywug trait block gives swamp camouflage advantage. Quib's Stealth proficiency comes from Criminal. The Fomorian trait block omits darkvision and gives the Evil Eye curse no fixed duration. The Hell Hound trait block omits the fire resistance described in the design notes.

Two encounter readings are recorded in the source evidence. If Siltcap dies, its revival uses the oldest qualifying kill within the preceding day and takes one day after its own death. Brackplate's breath specifies one repeat save, so a second failed save leaves the target restrained until concentration ends, for up to one minute total. It does not petrify the target.
