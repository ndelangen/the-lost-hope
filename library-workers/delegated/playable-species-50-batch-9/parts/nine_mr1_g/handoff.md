# Batch 9 awakened beasts and shrub

Five exact-key player profiles, five linked original NPCs, and five square portraits are ready to aggregate. Tyler Kamstra's 2018 DMs Guild conversion uses the 2014 fifth-edition rules. Printed page numbers refer to the v2 text. [V2 player rules]() · [Publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races)

| Death Dog, p. 228 | Marl and Soot, Fighter 2 | AC 12, HP 22, Bite +5 for 1d6+3 piercing. Both heads awake raise passive Perception from 12 to 17. A second Bite takes Action Surge. |
| Giant Fire Beetle, p. 236 | Wick, Barbarian 1 | AC 15, HP 15, blindsight 30 ft., Bite +4 for 1d6+2 slashing or 1d6+4 during Rage. |

The author describes the Awakened Shrub as weak even with its printed player traits. Tanglebud keeps those traits and fights bare. The Blink Dog trait lead-in has a “Blind dogs” typo, while the heading, description, and language entry identify Blink Dog. Martial Arts supplies the Dexterity option for the bite. The supplement's p. 7 bonus-strike rule requires conventional bludgeoning blows, so the Monk examples use a root bump or a shoulder bump. The Death Dog keeps the player version's defensive Two Heads trait. Wick uses the printed illumination radius and one AC formula.
