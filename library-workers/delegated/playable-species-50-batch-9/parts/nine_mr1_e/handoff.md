# Specter, Stirge, Tarrasque, Unicorn, and Water Weird

Five playable conversions from Tyler Kamstra's 2018 *Monstrous Races*. I used the v2 player trait blocks and 2014 core rules for the builds. Each profile links to one class-built NPC. [Printed v2 text]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 combat rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

| Profile and printed folio | Worked build | Source reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specter, pp. 191–192 | Sable, Rogue 1. AC 14, HP 10, rapier +5, and Life Drain +4. | The traits give both a 30-ft. walk and flight. Sable communicates in writing and uses a Charisma-based Life Drain. The trait text has one sunlight rule repeated. Spectre is included as a spelling alias. |
| Stirge, pp. 194–195 | Pip, Rogue 1. AC 14, HP 10, and Blood Drain +5. | The initial finesse strike can deliver Sneak Attack. I count only the later attached drain toward the Constitution threshold and use no attack roll for that drain. |
| Tarrasque, pp. 196–197 | Crag, Barbarian 1. AC 15, HP 15, and bite or claw +5. | The player form is Medium. It uses Unarmored Defense. I read the bonus-action grapple as allowing its bite or forelimbs to hold prey. |
| Unicorn, pp. 202–203 | Lark, Paladin 1. AC 11, HP 12, and horn or hoof +5. | The printed healing spell starts at character level 3. Lark can deliver the paladin's touch with its horn at level 1. Core unarmed strikes qualify for the printed Strength-based charge. |
| Water Weird, p. 207 | Undertow, Barbarian 1. AC 14, HP 14, and Constrict +4. | The trait block gives a 30-ft. walk and no dedicated swim speed. Use the normal swim movement cost within the bound water. The p. 8 rule makes dropping to 0 HP fatal because Undertow cannot fall unconscious. |

I checked the ability scores, proficiencies, AC, HP, attack bonuses, and damage by hand. The original portraits were generated with built-in ImageGen using Big Linda as a paint-and-paper reference. I reviewed them at full size and at 96 px. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)
