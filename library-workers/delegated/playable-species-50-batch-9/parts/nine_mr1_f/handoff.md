# Will-o’-Wisp, Wraith, Wyvern, Xorn and Yeti

Five playable profiles, five original NPC builds, and five plain-parchment portraits are staged for the parent batch. Species, editions and publications use the exact roster keys. The printed v2 traits govern this part. [Monstrous Races v2 text]() · [DMs Guild listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races)

| Profile | Printed trait pages | Worked example and manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Will-o’-Wisp | 208–209 | Tallow of Siltford, Life Cleric 1. AC 13 from Dexterity, 9 HP, Shock +4, Consume Life DC 11, cleric save DC 12. The encounter uses material-free casting. |
| Wraith | 210 | Mara Moorwake, Barbarian 2. AC 16 with a shield, 23 HP, Life Drain +4 for 1d6 + 2 necrotic, or 1d6 + 4 during Rage. The duplicate sunlight wording appears once in the profile. |
| Wyvern | 211 | Brackenwake, Barbarian 2. AC 15, 23 HP, stinger +5 for 1d4 + 3 piercing plus 1d4 poison. During Rage add 2 piercing. |
| Xorn | 211–212 | Pinch-of-Tin, Fighter 2. AC 19 with the shield and AC 17 after the bonus-action strike until the next turn. Two shortswords use +4 to hit and 1d6 + 2 piercing with the fighting style. |
| Yeti | 212–213 | Rusk Palecrag, Fighter 1. AC 15, 12 HP, sword +5 for 1d8 + 5 slashing. Chilling Gaze uses Constitution for DC 12 as a table ruling. |

The Yeti trait box stops after the plus sign in the DC formula and resumes with Claws. The Constitution ruling supplies only the missing modifier. Tallow uses an ordinary 5-ft. reach for Shock. The supplement allows a handless creature to perform somatic gestures appropriate to its form. Ephemeral still bars carried material components and foci. The Wraith, Wyvern and Wisp summaries retain the source's explicit speaking, reading and writing permissions.
