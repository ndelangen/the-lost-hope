# Pegasus, Piercer, Pseudodragon, Purple Worm, and Remorhaz

Five player profiles use Tyler Kamstra’s *Monstrous Races* v2, published through Dungeon Masters Guild for 2014 fifth edition. Each has a linked original NPC and a square parchment portrait. The player anatomy and equipment restrictions are part of each build. [Published traits]() · [2014 player classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 combat](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

| Profile | Printed folio | NPC and manual arithmetic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pegasus | p. 168 | Willowbank, Barbarian 2. AC 14 = 10 + 2 Dex + 2 Con. HP 23 = 14 + 9. Hooves +5 = 3 Str + 2 proficiency, with 1d8 + 3 before Rage. |
| Piercer | p. 170 | Flintdrop, Monk 1. AC 17 = 15 + 2 Dex. HP 10 = 8 + 2 Con. The 30-ft. Drop has +4 to hit and deals 3d6 on a hit. |
| Pseudodragon | pp. 171–172 | Needlewing, Rogue 2. AC 13 = 10 + 3 Dex. HP 17 = 10 + 7. The expressly finesse bite or sting hits at +5 for 1d4 + 3. Sneak Attack adds 1d6 when eligible. |
| Purple Worm | p. 172 | Gritcoil, Barbarian 2. AC 17 = 15 + 2 Dex. HP 25 = 15 + 10. A Strength bite hits at +5 and deals 1d8 + 3, or 1d8 + 5 in Rage. The body has blindsight and no eyes. |
| Remorhaz | p. 175 | Sleetback, Monk 1. AC 15 = 13 + 2 Dex. HP 10 = 8 + 2 Con. Bite +4 uses Dexterity. Heated Body DC 12 = 8 + 2 proficiency + 2 Con. |

The Pegasus trait block ends on p. 168. P. 169 begins the play and design notes. The Piercer profile uses the printed hit and miss effects. Flintdrop adds proficiency to the attack as a table ruling. Falling damage on a miss follows 2014 landing rules. For Gritcoil, blindsight counts as sight within range for Danger Sense. On split-damage physical attacks, apply the relevant ability and Rage bonuses once to the piercing component. Sleetback’s Martial Arts bonus action uses the ordinary bludgeoning class die per v2 p. 7. The Pseudodragon profile includes the searchable spelling Pseudo-dragon and keeps the player sting’s damage to its printed piercing.

I generated one original per NPC with built-in ImageGen, using Big Linda as the paint and paper reference. I checked the anatomy, unbroken parchment field, and silhouette at review size and 96 px.
