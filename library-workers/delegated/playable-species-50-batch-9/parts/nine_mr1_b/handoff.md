# Mephit, Modron, Nightmare, Oni, and Owlbear

The profiles use Tyler Kamstra's playable traits for 2014 fifth edition. Each example uses its own class rules and gear. [Monstrous Races v2 text]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment)

| Profile | Printed source | Worked example |
|---|---|---|
| Mephit | v2 pp. 142–144, Mephit Traits | Siftglass, Dust Mephit Fighter 1. Leather AC 13, HP 12, shortbow +6 for 1d6+2 with Archery, Death Burst DC 12. |
| Modron | v2 pp. 149–150, Modrons Traits | Docket Nine, Monodrone Monk 1. Natural AC 16, HP 10, fists +5 for 1d4+3. Martial Arts supplies one bonus-action strike after a qualifying Attack action. |
| Nightmare | v2 p. 158, Nightmare Traits | Sable Cinder, Barbarian 1. Unarmored AC 14, HP 14, hooves +5 for 1d4+3 bludgeoning plus 1d4 fire. Rage raises bludgeoning damage by 2 on that hit. |
| Oni | v2 p. 161, Oni Traits | Rusk Toll, Fiend Warlock 2. Mage armor AC 15, HP 17, spell DC 13, and eldritch blast +5 for 1d10+3. |
| Owlbear | v2 p. 168, Owlbear Traits | Briarwatch, Barbarian 2. Unarmored AC 15, HP 25, bite or claw +5 for 1d8+3 before Rage. |

- All six Mephit forms share one profile. I read the Dust Mephit's ambiguous falling trigger as dropping to 0 HP. Its repeated saves occur at the end of each affected turn for the worked encounter. The trait block omits the fire vulnerability mentioned in design commentary.
- The Modron profile keeps all five printed forms. Monodrone and Quadrone share a package. For Pentadrone gas I used the opening 15-ft. cone wording and its printed restrained effect.
- The Nightmare player's fiery hooves come from the printed conversion. The Oni's magic comes from its Warlock levels. The Oni trait block provides flight without shapechanging or innate spells. Owlbear uses the class Attack action and can speak Common.
