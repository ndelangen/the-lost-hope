# Half-Giant for D&D 3.5

One playable Half-Giant entry from the April 2004 first printing of *Expanded Psionics Handbook*. The Half-Giant is a Medium Giant with the Psionic subtype and level adjustment +1. Powerful Build expands specific size modifiers and weapon options, while actual size sets space and reach. Fire Acclimated gives +2 on saves against fire spells and effects. [Playable traits, pp. 12–13]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Racial Stomp is a separate once-daily, no-cost psi-like ability. It knocks grounded targets prone and deals nonlethal damage on a failed Reflex save. The encounter cards give the two NPCs different save DCs based on Charisma. [Stomp, p. 133]() · [Published warrior, pp. 200–202]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

**Anet Copperstep** is an original Psychic Warrior and a desert pass rescuer. Her card shows the difference between using Powerful Build while Medium and stacking it with actual Large size during Expansion. The full-body portrait shows Anet on plain parchment. **Nadi Flintwell** is an original Fighter who leads the evacuation of a glass kiln. Both are provisional CR 1.
