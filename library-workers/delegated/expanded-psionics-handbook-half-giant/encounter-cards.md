# Half-Giant encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Giants with the Psionic subtype, low-light vision, 30-ft. base land speed, and normal 5-ft. space and reach. Powerful Build allows their listed Large weapons and the advantageous Large size modifier on opposed checks. They get +2 on saves against fire spells and effects. Both are ECL 2 and provisional CR 1. [Playable traits, pp. 12–13]()

| NPC | AC (touch, flat-footed) | HP | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will | Grapple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anet Copperstep, Psychic Warrior 1 | 14 (10, 14) | 11 | +4 | +5, +0, +1 | +7 |
| Anet during Expansion | 12 (8, 12) | 11 | same order | +5, −1, +1 | +12 |
| Nadi Flintwell, Fighter 1 | 15 (11, 14) | 15 | +1 | +4, +1, +1 | +8 |

Each can use **Stomp** once per day as a standard action. In a 20-ft. cone-shaped spread, a grounded creature that fails its Reflex save falls prone and takes 1d4 nonlethal damage. Success negates both effects. Power resistance does not apply. It spends no power points. [Stomp, p. 133]()

## Anet Copperstep

A traveller is stranded on a crumbling ledge. Anet can get a rescue line within reach if the party holds the upper anchor and keeps people off the scree.

Chain shirt, Large longsword, and rescue line. Climb +5, Jump +5, Listen +3, and Spot +3. Her 2 daily power points come from Naturally Psionic.

- **Large longsword** wielded two-handed, +3 melee, 2d6+4 slashing, critical 19–20/×2, 5-ft. reach.
- **Expansion**. Spend 1 PP as a standard action. Anet and her carried gear enlarge for one round. Space and reach become 10 ft., Climb and Jump become +6, and the Huge longsword deals 3d6+6 slashing at +3 melee. Powerful Build stacks with the new size on the grapple check. The effect ends before her next turn, so use the expanded attack for an attack of opportunity in that window.
- **Stomp**. Reflex DC 10. Keep the cone clear of allies on the ledge. [Expansion, pp. 105–106]()

## Nadi Flintwell

Nadi needs to get workers out of a glass kiln through a narrow side passage. The party can free the exit by moving empty racks while Nadi guides the crew through.

Studded leather, light wooden shield, and Large warhammer. Climb +5 and Jump +5. Against fire spells and effects, the saves are Fort +6, Ref +3, and Will +3.

- **Large warhammer** +5 melee, 2d6+3 bludgeoning one-handed, critical ×3, 5-ft. reach.
- **Stomp**. Reflex DC 11. Nadi waits until the crew is out of the cone.
