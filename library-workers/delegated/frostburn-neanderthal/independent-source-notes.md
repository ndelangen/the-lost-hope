# Neanderthal source check

The September 2004 first printing introduces Neanderthals on p. 36 and gives their player rules on pp. 37–38. The p. 145 Warrior confirms the Human subtype and LA +0. The published Warrior and named NPCs have their own stats. The two staged first-level characters use the player traits and have provisional CR 1. [Player rules]() · [Published Warrior]()

Primitive Weapon Mastery gives only the listed attack bonus. Climate Tolerant gives the environmental save waiver and the prerequisite equivalence. The separate Cold Endurance feat has its own rules. The p. 38 Illiteracy trait requires two skill points for literacy and names Wizard as the exception when taking another class. Independent review checked both builds’ skills, Vela’s literacy spend, Oren’s rage, and the weapon bonuses. [Player rules continued]() · [Cold Endurance]()

Wizards’ February 2006 errata gives no Neanderthal correction. David Griffith illustrated the Neanderthal on pp. 39 and 145. The book’s broad chest and long arms informed Oren’s portrait. His clothing, pose, and club are original. [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP) · [Book illustration]()
