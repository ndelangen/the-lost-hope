# Neanderthal encounters

Both are Medium Humanoids with the Human subtype, provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. Climate Tolerant waives environmental Fortitude saves between −20°F and 140°F. Resolve fire and cold damage normally. The trait counts as Cold Endurance for feat and prestige-class prerequisites. Primitive Weapon Mastery adds +1 to attack rolls with its listed weapons. ## Oren Hearthward, Barbarian 1

Oren keeps the shelter passage open while the party brings in the last travelers. He is illiterate under the racial rule.

- HP 15, AC 13, initiative +1, land speed 40 ft. in light armor with a light load. Fortitude +5, Reflex +1, Will +1.
- Greatclub +6 for 1d10+6 bludgeoning. Thrown javelin +3 for 1d6+4 piercing. The mastery bonus is included in both attack rolls.
- Rage once per day for eight rounds. AC drops to 11, HP rises to a maximum of 17, and the greatclub becomes +8 for 1d10+9. Javelin stays +3 and deals 1d6+6. Oren is fatigued after rage ends.

## Vela Ridgewatch, Ranger 1

Vela follows tracks between the cairns. She spent two skill points on literacy and can read the Common warning marker where the path forks.

- HP 10, AC 15, initiative +3, land speed 30 ft. Fortitude +4, Reflex +5, Will +2. Listen, Spot, and Survival are each +8.
- Shortbow +5 for 1d6 piercing. Point Blank Shot makes it +6 for 1d6+1 within 30 ft.
- Shortspear +4 for 1d6+2 piercing. Longsword +3 for 1d8+2 slashing, since the longsword is outside the mastery list.
