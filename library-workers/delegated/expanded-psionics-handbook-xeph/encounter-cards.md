# Xeph encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Psionic and Xeph subtypes. They have darkvision 60 ft., and their listed saves gain +1 against powers, spells, and spell-like effects. Both are ECL 1 and provisional CR 1. [Playable Xeph, p. 16]()

| NPC | AC (touch, flat-footed) | HP | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will | Jump |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rovan Threadglass, Soulknife 1 | 15 (12, 13) | 11 | +6 | +1, +4, +3 | +4, or +8 with Burst |
| Miren Trackweft, Ranger 1 | 16 (13, 13) | 9 | +3 | +3, +5, +1 | +3, or +7 with Burst |

Each can use **Burst** three times per day. It takes a standard action and increases land speed from 30 to 40 ft. for three rounds. The higher speed gives +4 to Jump. Keep the usual running-start requirements. [Core Jump rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/skills/jump.htm)

## Rovan Threadglass

A lantern counterweight is jammed above a busy square. Rovan can reach the far stair if the party clears the space under the lanterns and holds the loose line.

Climb +4, Tumble +5, and Autohypnosis +5. Studded leather and light crossbow. Rovan has a 3-point reserve from Wild Talent and Naturally Psionic. The mind blade and Burst spend none of it.

- Form a **mind blade** as a move action. +2 melee, 1d6+1 piercing, 19–20/×2, 5-ft. reach. It counts as magic against damage reduction, has hardness 10 and 10 HP, and dissipates on release.
- **Light crossbow** +2 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. range increment. Reload as a move action.

## Miren Trackweft

A wolf doubled back at the reserve boundary and is heading for a snare line. Miren can find the last reliable tracks if the party keeps them clear. Once Miren sees where the wolf is going, use Burst to loop ahead and flag the snares.

Survival +5 and +7 when tracking animals. Spot and Listen +5, or +7 against animals. Hide and Move Silently +6. Wild Empathy +0. Studded leather, longbow, and short sword.

- **Longbow** +4 ranged, 1d8 piercing, critical ×3, 100-ft. range increment. Within 30 ft., attack +5 and damage 1d8+1. Add +2 damage against animals.
- **Short sword** +1 melee, 1d6 piercing, 19–20/×2, 5-ft. reach. Add +2 damage against animals.
