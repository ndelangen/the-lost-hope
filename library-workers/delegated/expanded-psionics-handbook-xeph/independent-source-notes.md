# Independent checks

Source review compared the first-print playable rules on p. 16 with the warrior on p. 217. Both give the same number of Burst uses, duration, bonus type, and scaling by total character level. The 2006 errata has no Xeph or racial Burst change. Jump uses the normal speed adjustment, which gives +4 during a first-level Burst at an unencumbered land speed of 40 ft. The p. 217 Xeph illustration is credited to Steven Belledin. [Playable chapter]() · [Sample warrior]() · [Publisher errata]()

The catalog review checked Rovan's 16 skill ranks, Miren's 28 ranks, both builds' attack and defense totals, and the one species entry with two NPCs. It applied Xeph and Elan in either order in temporary files.
