# Xeph for D&D 3.5

One playable Xeph entry from the April 2004 first printing of *Expanded Psionics Handbook*. Xephs have darkvision 60 ft., Dexterity +2 and Strength −2, a conditional +1 racial saving throw bonus, and one racial power point at 1st level. Soulknife is their favored class. [Playables, p. 16]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Racial Burst grants a three-round competence bonus to movement, three times per day. It adds 10, 20, or 30 ft. at character levels 1, 5, and 9. The playable and warrior entries agree. The racial trait does not specify an action, so the profile applies the core standard-action default. The separately named psionic power is a swift-action enhancement. [Warrior, p. 217]() · [Psionic power, pp. 81–82]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

The plain-parchment portrait shows **Rovan Threadglass**, an original Soulknife and festival rigger. **Miren Trackweft** is an original Ranger trying to guide a lost wolf away from snares. Both have provisional CR 1. The cards show standard and Burst movement, saves, skills, and attacks.
