# The Land Beyond the Magic Mirror beasts

Four AD&D 1e profiles from E. Gary Gygax's 1983 TSR adventure, EX2. The in the archived Wizards ZIP matches the local copy byte for byte. [Archived download](https://web.archive.org/web/20030608000000id_/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/files/2/MagicMirror.zip) · [Publisher preview]() · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

| Creature | Page | Encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Ant | 9 | The first three workers arrive with the fourth Giant Bumble Bee. Three arrive each round thereafter, up to 48. Each has 11 HP. |
| Giant Crab | 19 | The 24 HP crab climbs onto the punt at M. At N a crab can upset or hole the boat before downstream departure. |
| Carnivorous Ape | 24–25 | Three apes with 25 HP each prepare to attack at the banquet. Both forelimbs need to hit to trigger the additional rend. |
| Giant Wolverine | 24–25 | Two wolverines with 22 HP each prepare to attack at the banquet. Their ferocity and the conditions for their rearward musk spray follow the printed block. |

The printed attack order puts the wolverines before the next wave of 26 larvae and the apes after it, even though both beasts try to rise together. The crab and ape have explicit 25% magic resistance in their blocks. The Afterword's wider guidance for qualifying inhabitants is recorded separately. [Exact pages and hashes](source-evidence.json)

The original garden illustration on p. 9 shows giant ants. The original banquet scene has no individual identifications for the ape or wolverine. The module has no printed artist credits for these scenes, and the external attribution is tentative. The four plain parchment portraits are new ImageGen drafts.
