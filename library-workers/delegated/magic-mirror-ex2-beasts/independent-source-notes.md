# The Land Beyond the Magic Mirror beast source check

E. Gary Gygax’s *The Land Beyond the Magic Mirror* is a 1983 TSR Hobbies adventure for AD&D 1st Edition, module EX2, product 9073, ISBN 0-88038-025-X. The title leaf identifies the original copyright. The 32-page Wizards facsimile has 2000 metadata and retains the 1983 interior. The pages used here match their one-based positions, including pp. 24–25, where the art occupies the usual folio area. The archived Wizards ZIP contains the exact local plus two JPEG maps. ## Giant Ant worker

- Printed/p. 9, area B, Garden of Colossal Flowers. The first arrival is three workers. Each has AC 3, MV 18″, HD 2, 11 hp, one attack and damage 1–6. Their block supplies no special attack or defense. The poison and saving-throw penalty above the block are the bumblebee’s.
- The flowers cry out when a character steps into a bed, about five feet from any flower. A bee appears the following round and attacks anyone harming or trying to harm the flowers. Three workers arrive with the fourth bee, three rounds after the first bee appears. Three more workers arrive each following round until 48 have arrived, all using the same stats. They attack and pursue within the garden and leave the party alone outside it.
- The page depicts three giant ants in the foreground. The black-and-white image shows segmented bodies, six legs, antennae and substantial mandibles. Their color is an illustration choice. [Printed p. 9]()

## Giant Crab

- Printed/p. 19, areas M, Large Punt, and N, Reedy Bank. AC 3, MV 9″, HD 3, 24 hp, two attacks, damage 2–8/2–8 and 25% magic resistance. The resistance is explicitly in this crab’s block.
- At M the current carries the punt toward N. When rowed upstream, the oars always stick after three strokes. Freeing them uses the door-opening procedure. While an oar is stuck, a crab has a 1-in-6 chance per round to climb aboard and attack. Crabs can walk on the gelid river surface here and do not overturn the boat during this attack.
- At N a party trying to continue downstream has its boat overturned or holed by a Giant Crab before departure. The reverse route toward M from N is only available when arriving from O and uses the sticking-oar and crab rules. The walking surface and boat outcomes are features of these encounters. The page supplies no crab illustration, body measurements, or color. [Printed p. 19]()

## Carnivorous Ape

- Printed/p. 24, key CA in the Mad Feast Hall, with the attack on p. 25. Three apes have AC 6, MV 12″, HD 5, 25 hp each, three attacks, damage 1–4/1–4/1–8, an additional 1–8 rending damage when both forelimbs hit, and 25% magic resistance.
- The feast’s crowns of delusion make the apes seem like large monkeys. The apes themselves are present and attack. As the meal turns into a fight, they try to rise with the wolverines to attack and create confusion at the west end of the table. The encounter’s printed attack order places the wolverines and apes at different positions, with larvae between them. Preserve both the attempted joint action in the scene and their separate positions if using that list. [Printed p. 24]() · [Printed p. 25]()

## Giant Wolverine

- Printed/p. 24, key GW in the Mad Feast Hall, with the attack on p. 25. Two wolverines have AC 4, MV 15″, HD 4+4, 22 hp each, three attacks and damage 2–5/2–5/2–8. Ferocity grants +4 to hit. Its block has no magic resistance value.
- Its musk targets one creature to the rear. A failed save vs. Poison blinds the target for 1–8 hours. Even a successful save brings 2–8 turns of nausea and a 50% reduction in Strength and Dexterity for that duration. All cloth worn by the target also saves as against acid and rots away on failure. The block gives no spray distance or usage limit.
- The procession makes the wolverines appear to be harmless teddy-bear creatures under the crown effect. The text describes them as evil-natured animals and gives no formal alignment field. Use the wolverine form for a creature portrait. [Printed p. 24]() · [Printed p. 25]()

The four variants fit the library’s Beast category. EX2 gives encounter figures under 1e conventions and no 5e creature type or challenge rating. The adjacent speaking Huge Lion and already cataloged Giant Dragonfly are outside this batch.

The p. 24 banquet illustration is an unlabeled group scene. Neither the ape nor the wolverine can be securely identified in it. The separate illustration on p. 25 depicts larvae. The facsimile has no printed art credits. A reference catalog attributes the cover to Jim Holloway, and Greyhawk Online marks the p. 9, p. 24 and p. 25 attributions to Holloway as tentative. An auction of original work identifies Jeff Easley’s contribution to EX2. The individual page assignments remain unconfirmed.
