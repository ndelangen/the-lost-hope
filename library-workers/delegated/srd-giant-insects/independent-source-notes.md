# SRD giant insect source notes

Both profiles describe creatures produced by a magic item or spell. The SRDs give enough detail to use each creature in that context and supply no natural habitat or fixed live coloration. The local SRD 5.2.1 matches the publisher download byte for byte. The local and publisher SRD 5.1 differ at the binary level. Their extracted text matches after normalizing spaces, and pp. 221–222 match as extracted. [SRD 5.1]() · [SRD 5.2.1]()

## Giant Fly, SRD 5.1

Catalog entry `beast-89cd1b062cb2aba1a35fca42`, version `5fc3090e807dec85104aadb0`, 2014 fifth edition.

- The Giant Fly stat block accompanies the rare Ebony Fly Figurine of Wondrous Power. It is a Large, unaligned beast with AC 11, HP 19 (3d10 + 3), a 30-ft. walking speed and a 60-ft. flying speed. STR 14, DEX 13, CON 13, INT 2, WIS 10, CHA 3. It has 60-ft. darkvision and passive Perception 10. The block leaves Languages as a dash and omits attacks, CR, XP and PB. Leave the 2014 challenge field unset. The separate SRD 5.2.1 profile adds CR 0, XP 0, PB +2 and initiative +1. [2014-era block, p. 222]() · [2024-era block, p. 222]()
- Use an action to speak the command word and throw the figurine to a point on the ground within 60 ft. An occupied space or one too small prevents the transformation. The general figurine rules supply the fly's ability to understand the activator's languages and obey spoken commands. It is friendly and limits itself to self-defense without commands. It serves as a mount for up to 12 hours. It reverts when time runs out, at 0 HP, or if the activator uses an action to repeat the command word while touching it. The figurine can be used again after two days. [Figurine rules, pp. 221–222]()
- The source describes the figurine as a horsefly carved from ebony. The horsefly shape is a basis for depicting the mount. Ebony describes the statuette's material. [Ebony Fly, p. 222]()

## Giant Insect, SRD 5.2.1

Catalog entry `beast-26bce7b50d7f5e067749713b`, version `c6dcfaf0cc27ba83189c2c38`, 2024 fifth edition.

- The level-4 Druid conjuration takes an action, requires verbal and somatic components, reaches 60 ft. and requires concentration for up to 10 minutes. Choose a giant centipede, spider or wasp when casting. It appears in a visible unoccupied space. It is allied and takes its turn immediately after the caster on the caster's initiative count. Verbal orders cost the caster no action. Without orders, the creature Dodges and moves away from danger. It disappears at 0 HP or when the spell ends. The 2014 spell has separate rules for transforming ordinary creatures and is a different printing. [2024-era spell, p. 135]() · [2014-era spell, p. 149]()
- All forms are Large, unaligned beasts with CR None, XP 0 and the caster's PB. AC is 11 plus slot level. HP is 30 at slot level 4 and increases by 10 per additional slot level. The early 2024 *Player's Handbook* added 10 for every spell level. Its errata added the above-level-4 qualification that the SRD prints. The creature walks and climbs 40 ft. The wasp also flies 40 ft. STR 17, DEX 13, CON 15, INT 4, WIS 14, CHA 3. Its saves match its ability modifiers. It has 60-ft. darkvision, passive Perception 12 and understands the caster's languages. [Stat block, p. 136]() · [PHB errata]()
- Spider Climb applies to every form. Multiattack makes half the slot level's number of attacks, rounded down. Both attacks use the caster's spell attack modifier. Poison Jab has 10-ft. reach and deals 1d6 + 3 + slot level piercing plus 1d4 poison. The spider's Web Bolt has 60-ft. range, deals 1d10 + 3 + slot level bludgeoning and sets the target's speed to 0 until the start of the insect's next turn. The centipede's Venomous Spew is a bonus action against a visible creature within 10 ft. A failed CON save against the caster's spell DC inflicts Poisoned until that same turn boundary. The form names are the available appearance evidence in the SRD. [Stat block, p. 136]()
