# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual lamprey source check

TSR’s *Monstrous Manual*, product 2140, first appeared in 1993. This physical copy identifies itself as the January 1994 third printing. The Fish table is on printed p. 117 (viewer 120), with the two lamprey descriptions on p. 118 (viewer 121). The reflowed electronic edition puts the same material on pp. 351 and 353. The first row is titled “Lamprey” in Fish and “Lamprey, common” in the aquatic summoning tables. The compact Fish table gives no fixed HP, intelligence, size class, alignment or modern creature type. The “Sw” movement prefix appears for both aquatic forms. [Fish table][117] · [Aquatic summoning tables][378]

## Combat

The shared “Lamprey” paragraph describes a bite that fastens the lamprey to the victim with its sphincter-like mouth. Starting the next round, an attached lamprey drains blood at 2 HP per Hit Die per round. Giant has its own stat row and shares this description. The 2e text leaves the owner of the Hit Dice implicit. The 1e *Monster Manual* explicitly uses the lamprey’s own dice and gives 10 HP per round for a giant specimen. On that reading, a 1+2 HD common lamprey drains 2 HP per round, with the +2 in its HD entry representing HP added to the die. The 2e fire rule imposes −2 to saves against fire-based attacks on **sea lampreys**. The Fish table and prose give no separate sea stat row or explicit mapping from that term to the Giant row. [Printed p. 118][118] · [1e Monster Manual, Lamprey][first-mm]

The Land Lamprey attaches on a hit that deals 1 damage. It then drains 1 HP per round for three rounds unless killed or removed. Each attached lamprey encumbers the victim as if the victim had lost 1 point of Dexterity. This effect applies while attached and accumulates per lamprey. The table lists two attacks, while the prose describes the attachment and subsequent drain without specifying a separate second action, a second bite while attached, or a removal check. The earlier 1e *Monster Manual II* lists one attack and explicitly makes exposure to fire release the grip. That release rule is absent from 2e. [Printed table][117] · [Printed p. 118][118] · [1e Monster Manual II, Land Lamprey][first-mm2]

## Habitat and artwork

On printed p. 377, general Monster Summoning II lists “Lamprey, land” at slot 5. Printed p. 378 lists “Lamprey, common” in Salt Water I at slot 2 and Fresh Water I at slot 3, and “Lamprey, sea” in Salt Water V at slot 2. No entry there explicitly names Giant Lamprey. These tables establish summoning associations. The Fish descriptions support aquatic common and giant lampreys and a land form, with no further natural-habitat details. [Printed p. 377][377] · [Printed p. 378][378] · [Printed p. 118][118]

Page 117 has no illustration. The pictured fish on p. 118 is a shark. The lamprey prose supplies the aquatic attachment mouth but no colors, dimensions or artist credit for a lamprey. The separate 1e aquatic lamprey image shows a circular sucker, and its prose describes a ring of teeth. The 1e Land Lamprey prose describes a thick, roughly three-foot, air-breathing form that moves like a snake, ranging from light to blackish green. These are older-edition visual references. [Printed p. 117][117] · [Printed p. 118][118] · [1e Monster Manual, Lamprey][first-mm] · [1e Monster Manual II, Land Lamprey][first-mm2]
