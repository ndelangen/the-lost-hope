# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual lampreys

The Lamprey, Giant Lamprey, and Land Lamprey come from the Fish table and prose on pp. 117–118 of the January 1994 third printing. The 1994 aquatic rule gives 2 HP of drain per Hit Die each round after attachment and leaves whose Hit Dice implicit. The 1977 *Monster Manual* specifies the lamprey's own Hit Dice and gives 10 HP per round for the Giant. The profiles identify that source for the 2 and 10 HP rulings. [Printed table](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/bf2949b86e3fe804af0c021045ab0d44.webp) · [Printed prose](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/981f6c8811613d3c1f2b6bf18210924c.webp) · [1977 comparison, p. 59]() · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

The Land Lamprey attaches on a one-point hit, then drains 1 HP per round for three rounds unless removed or killed. Each attached lamprey imposes the equivalent of −1 Dexterity. The table lists two attacks. The prose leaves the second attack unexplained. The sea lamprey's fire-save penalty remains limited to sea lampreys.

The original portraits show each complete body on plain parchment. The attach-and-drain description supports the circular mouths. Proportions, colors and poses are illustration choices. [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json)
