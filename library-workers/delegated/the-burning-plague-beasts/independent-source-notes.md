# The Burning Plague beast source review

Miguel Duran’s *The Burning Plague* is a 2000 Wizards of the Coast adventure written for the original Third Edition rules. The 11-page is printed numbered 2–12, so its first page is printed p. 2. It credits Duran on that page and the metadata dates the file to August 25, 2000. The archived publisher download matches the local reference copy byte for byte (SHA-256 `735fb0e2bf3a2725c98c7faf4285b6b7e5197c9be88fa23a36f195d62e1e34fa`). A readable mirror also reproduces the publisher’s Area 4 text. I checked the stat blocks against the rendered pages. [Archived Wizards ][archive] · [Readable publisher mirror][mirror]

## Dire Weasel

The block starts in “Area 4: The Larder” on printed p. 7 (p. 6) and continues on printed p. 8 (p. 7). CR 2, Medium-Size Animal (5 ft. long), HD 3d8+0, 12 HP, initiative +4, speed 40 ft., AC 16, neutral, Fort +1, Ref +7, Will +4. STR 14, DEX 19, CON 6, INT 2, WIS 12, CHA 11. Hide +9, Move Silently +10, Spot +5, and Weapon Finesse (bite). The HD and Constitution are shown as printed. [Larder stat line][larder] · [Continuation][rats]

- Bite is +7 melee for 1d6 + 3 damage. Attach occurs on a hit, costs the weasel its Dexterity bonus to AC, and leaves it at AC 12. Blood Drain deals 2d4 temporary Constitution damage each round it stays attached. The abbreviated 2000 entry provides no separate removal check or grapple number. [Continuation][rats]
- The weasel has Scent and the adventure’s Contagious quality. Contact calls for a DC 13 Fortitude save under the fictional adventure rules. It hides in sacks in the room’s southwestern corner. A character may oppose its Hide check with Spot. After the party spends two rounds in the room, the weasel leaps at the nearest character on round three. It came with the kobolds and leaves them alone. That timing and allegiance belong to this encounter. [Larder setup][larder] · [Special qualities][rats]

## Rats (8)

The heading on printed p. 8 (p. 7) is “Rats (8)” and prints CR 1. It gives Tiny Animal, HD 1/4 d8 − 1, 1 HP each, initiative +2, speed 15 ft. and climb 15 ft., AC 14, neutral, Fort +1, Ref +4, Will +1. STR 2, DEX 15, CON 8, INT 2, WIS 12, CHA 2. Climb +12, Hide +18, Move Silently +10, and Weapon Finesse (bite). Bite is +4 melee and its damage dice print as 1d3 − 4. The profile has Scent and the same contact-based Contagious quality as the Dire Weasel. [Rat stat block][rats]

Area 4 has four individual rats in Barrel 2, with jerky, and four in Barrel 5, with cheese. They stay inside until disturbed. The room is EL 3, potentially EL 4 with the kobold ambush. The original adventure supplies one CR beside the eight-rat heading and labels only the hit points as “each.” Preserve CR 1 as the printed encounter group label. The group reading is an inference, supported by the room EL and by the revised generic Rat’s individual CR 1/8. The adventure gives no independently identified individual CR and no swarm stat block. ## Later rules comparison

The separately published revised 3.5 SRD gives the generic Dire Weasel 13 HP, CON 10, Bite +6, Fort +3, and Blood Drain 1d4 each attached round. It also provides separate grapple rules for removing an attached weasel. Its generic Rat has CON 10, Fort +2, Hide +14, an explicit 15-ft. swim speed, and CR 1/8. These are edition and profile differences. The Area 4 lines retain their printed 2000 encounter values. I found no source-specific replacement printing for the Area 4 blocks. No creature illustration or color note accompanies the two blocks. [Revised Dire Weasel][weasel-srd] · [Revised Rat][rat-srd] · [Area 4 printed source][larder]
