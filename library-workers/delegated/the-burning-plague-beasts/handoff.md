# The Burning Plague beasts

This batch adds the original D&D 3.0 Dire Weasel and Rat variants from Area 4 of Miguel Duran's 2000 adventure. The archived Wizards matches the local reference byte for byte. Its viewer page numbers are one less than the printed page numbers. [Original ]() · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

| Beast | Printed source | Encounter detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dire Weasel | pp. 7–8, pp. 6–7 | The 12 HP weasel hides in the southwestern sacks and leaps on round three. |
| Rat | pp. 7–8, pp. 6–7 | Eight rats with 1 HP each stay in barrels 2 and 5 until disturbed. |

The Dire Weasel's bite attaches it to the target. While attached, it has AC 12 and deals 2d4 temporary Constitution damage per round. The original adventure prints CR 1 on its collective *Rats (8)* line. The catalog keeps that value in the encounter notes and leaves the individual rat's CR blank. The profiles keep both larder encounters separate from the general creatures in the revised 3.5 SRD. [Exact source pages and file hash](source-evidence.json)

Both new square portraits use Big Linda's plain parchment as the visual reference. The catalog associates them with the new adventure versions. [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [96-pixel previews](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json) · [Portrait index additions](portrait-index-preview.json)

The temporary library copy initially found the generic creatures by name. After applying the patch, it also found each variant by its larder encounter text. It displayed the printed attacks, skills, and saves, and loaded the correct portrait bytes for each variant. The existing publication versions and galleries matched the baseline.
