# Changeling for D&D 3.5

One playable Changeling version from the June 2004 first printing of *Eberron Campaign Setting*. Minor Change Shape is a full-round action at will. It physically changes the body and gives +10 circumstance on Disguise checks when used to create a disguise. Clothing and possessions retain their appearance. The profile includes the first printing’s shape limits and its rule for mimicking a dragonmark’s appearance. [Printed traits, p. 13]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Rem is an original Expert 1 and docket clerk at provisional CR 1/2. Serr is an original Rogue 1 and stage understudy at provisional CR 1. The cards show ordinary and shape-assisted Disguise totals. Rem appears in the full-body portrait on plain parchment.
