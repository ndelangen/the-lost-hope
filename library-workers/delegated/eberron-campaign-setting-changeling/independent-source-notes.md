# Independent Changeling reviews

The source reviewer checked the June 2004 first printing. Andrew Jones drew the p. 12 portrait, as confirmed in Wizards’ gallery. The original description gives pale gray skin, thin fair hair, long limbs, and blank white eyes. The playable traits are on p. 13, viewer p. 16. The February 2006 official errata does not change the entry. [Book image and description]() · [Wizards art gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20161101150242/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/eb/20040618a) · [Errata]()

The first printing says Minor Change Shape is physical and gives +10 circumstance on a Disguise check. It gives the duration, full-round action, death reversion, and true seeing rule. The FAQ allows mimicking a Warforged’s appearance within the normal limits. Game traits remain those of the Changeling. The book also allows copying a dragonmark’s visible form and reserves its powers for actual dragonmarks. [Original traits]() · [Wizards FAQ archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The original setting book publishes Rous at CR 9 on p. 80 and a Wayfinder Explorer at CR 8 on p. 247. *Monster Manual III* gives a separate Warrior 1 at CR 1/2 and Spy Rogue 3 at CR 3 on pp. 24–25, both with LA +0. Rem and Serr are original encounter NPCs. [Rous]() · [Wayfinder Explorer]() · [*Monster Manual III*]()

The second reviewer checked Rem’s 28 skill points, including two for languages, and Serr’s 36 skill points. The reviewer confirmed both sets of combat and Disguise totals and publication scoping.
