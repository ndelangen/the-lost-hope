# Changeling encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. They are Medium Humanoids with the Shapechanger subtype and 30-ft. speed. Both gain +2 racial on saving throws against sleep and charm effects. [Original traits]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Rem, docket clerk

Expert 1. Provisional CR 1/2, ECL 1. AC 13, touch 11, flat-footed 12. HP 7, initiative +1. Fort +1, Ref +1, Will +3. BAB +0, grapple −1. STR 8, DEX 13, CON 12, INT 12, WIS 12, CHA 14. Common, Elven, Dwarven, Giant. Elven comes from Intelligence. Two Speak Language points buy Dwarven and Giant.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Light crossbow | +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment |
| Dagger | −1 melee, 1d4−1 piercing or slashing, minimum 1, 19–20/×2 |

Bluff +8, Decipher Script +5, Diplomacy +7, Disguise +6 or +16 with Minor Change Shape, Gather Information +6, Knowledge (local) +5, Sense Motive +7. Diplomacy includes Skill Focus.

Two copies of a docket differ, and a witness only recognizes the relief clerk. Rem asks the party to bring the disputed copy upstairs and can demonstrate both faces in the same office coat before the hearing.

## Serr, stage understudy

Rogue 1. Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 14, touch 12, flat-footed 12. HP 8, initiative +2. Fort +2, Ref +4, Will +0. BAB +0, grapple +1. STR 12, DEX 15, CON 14, INT 12, WIS 10, CHA 12. Common and Gnome.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Rapier | +1 melee, 1d6+1 piercing, 18–20/×2, 5-ft. reach |
| Shortbow | +2 ranged, 1d6 piercing, ×3, 60-ft. increment |

Bluff +7, Disable Device +5, Disguise +5 or +15 with Minor Change Shape, Hide +6, Listen +6, Move Silently +6, Search +5, Sense Motive +6, Spot +6. Listen and Spot include Alertness. Trapfinding covers searching for traps above DC 20 and disabling magic traps.

Sneak attack adds 1d6 when a target is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC or is flanked. A ranged sneak attack requires a target within 30 ft. Serr can take the missing performer’s place if the party retrieves their costume. Serr changes shape backstage and puts on the costume separately.

## Minor Change Shape

At will as a full-round action. The body changes physically. Apply the appearance limits from *disguise self*, including a change of up to 1 ft. in height within the same basic body type. The listed Disguise totals include the +10 circumstance bonus and precede any situational modifiers. The form lasts until another change. *True seeing* reveals the natural form. Death restores it.
