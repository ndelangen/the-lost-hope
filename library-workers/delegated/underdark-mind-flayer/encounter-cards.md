# Mind Flayer encounters

Both have 60-ft. darkvision, a 30-ft. land speed, and telepathy with creatures that have a language within 100 ft. Mind Blast affects creatures in a 60-ft. cone and stuns for 3d4 rounds on a failed Will save.

A tentacle hit can lead to an immediate grapple check to attach the tentacle. Starting a turn with at least one attached allows one check to attach the others. The target can escape with a single successful opposed grapple or Escape Artist check. Each tentacle attached at the start of the target’s turn gives the Mind Flayer +2 on the opposed check. Extract requires starting a turn with all four attached and winning a grapple check. See the [complete builds](npc-builds.json) for target exceptions.

## Ilyth Voss, sealed-archive warden

**8 racial Hit Dice, CR 8.** AC 16, HP 52, initiative +7, SR 25. Fortitude +4, Reflex +5, Will +9. Tentacle +10 for 1d4+1 bludgeoning. Full attack uses four tentacles. Grapple +7.

Ilyth offers the party a way out before setting foot in the passage. If a fight starts, use DC 18 Mind Blast when the cone is clear of allies.

## Sael Nethrix, seal researcher

**Wizard 1 with 8 racial Hit Dice, CR 9.** AC 16, HP 57, initiative +7, SR 26. Fortitude +4, Reflex +5, Will +12. Tentacle +9 for 1d4 bludgeoning. Full attack uses four tentacles. Grapple +6.

Sael casts *mage armor* before examining the seal, raising AC to 20. If interrupted, Sael casts *grease* at Reflex DC 16. Mind Blast is Will DC 17.
