# Mind Flayer rules sources

- *Underdark* lists 8d8 racial Hit Dice, LA +7, favored class Wizard, and ECL 15 before class levels. The *Monster Manual* gives the detailed traits. Both builds use the core innate spell-like abilities. The *Expanded Psionics Handbook* gives its separate telepath variant a power-point reserve and a different power list. [Underdark, pp. 19–20]() · [Monster Manual, pp. 186–188]() · [Expanded Psionics Handbook, p. 204]()
- Mind Blast uses Charisma and a 4th-level spell equivalent. It stuns for 3d4 rounds on a failed Will save in a 60-ft. cone. The innate Psionics use caster level 8. Sael casts Wizard spells at caster level 1. Spell resistance is 25 + class levels, giving Ilyth 25 and Sael 26. [Mind Blast and Psionics, p. 187]() · [Character traits, p. 188]()
- Improved Grab attaches a tentacle after a hit and a successful grapple check. If at least one is attached at the start of a later turn, one check can attach the rest. Extract requires all four attached at the start of a turn and a successful grapple check. The March 2008 FAQ clarifies that warforged are vulnerable to extraction. The February 2006 errata specify supernatural telepathy at 100 ft. with creatures that have a language. [Combat rules, p. 187]() · [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)
- Ilyth spends 66 racial points on six skills at 11 ranks each. Sael spends 77 racial points on seven skills at 11 ranks each. Sael’s Wizard level adds one rank each to Concentration and Knowledge (arcana), and five to Spellcraft. Spellcraft +12 includes five ranks, +5 Intelligence, and +2 from Knowledge (arcana) synergy. [Racial skills, p. 188]() · [Spellcraft synergy](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/skills/spellcraft.htm#synergy)

Ilyth’s pale eyes, mauve skin, and four facial tentacles follow the written description. Todd Lockwood illustrated the Mind Flayer in the original book. [Description, p. 186]() · [Illustration, p. 187]()
