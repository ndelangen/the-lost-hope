# Vril rules sources

- The May 2007 *Drow of the Underdark* gives the playable traits on p. 126. Wizards’ Vril Warrior errata supplies the referenced stat block and missing abilities. The builds use LA +0 because the player entry gives no adjustment and the published Warrior’s three Hit Dice all come from class levels. The book’s Small carrying-capacity line is incomplete, so the profile uses the core three-quarters multiplier. [Player traits, p. 126]() · [Vril Warrior errata](https://www.scribd.com/document/1073439070/Drow-of-the-Underdark-Errata) · [Core carrying capacity](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/carryingCapacity.htm)
- The errata’s written Shriek progression starts at 1d6, adds 1d6 at 3rd level and every three levels, and caps at 4d6. The Warrior 3 stat block gives 2d6. The parenthetical examples instead put 2d6 at 6th level, 3d6 at 9th, and the cap at 12th. The staged profile follows the written increments and the level-3 stat block, reaching 4d6 at 9th level. [Vril Warrior errata](https://www.scribd.com/document/1073439070/Drow-of-the-Underdark-Errata)
- Shriek takes a standard action once per day, with Reflex for half damage. Dazing Shriek adds a separate Fortitude save for creatures that take damage. Skinshift takes a move action and reduces damage from one chosen physical damage type by 5. Its duration is 3 + Constitution modifier rounds. The poison penalty is −4 against drow sleep poison or spiderkind venom, as shown by both the sample’s Fortitude line and the Drow Slayer feat. [Vril feats, p. 123]() · [Vril Warrior errata](https://www.scribd.com/document/1073439070/Drow-of-the-Underdark-Errata)

Skriv’s long arms, dark stripes, pointed ears, and batlike face follow the book’s description. Steve Ellis illustrated the Vril warrior in the original book. [Description and illustration, p. 123]()
