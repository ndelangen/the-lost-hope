# Vril encounters

Both are Small goblinoids with 60-ft. darkvision, a 30-ft. land speed, and sonic resistance 5. Sudden bright light blinds them for 1 round and imposes −1 on attack rolls, saves, and checks while it lasts. They take −4 on Fortitude saves against drow sleep poison and spiderkind venom.

## Skriv Ashstripe, ledge scout

**Rogue 1, CR 1.** AC 16, HP 9, initiative +7. Fortitude +3, Reflex +5, Will +1. Short sword +1 or shortbow +4, each for 1d4 piercing. Climb +8.

Skriv climbs to the ledge before signaling the party. Once per day, use a move action to gain Skinshift for 6 rounds. Choose bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing and reduce damage from that type by 5. Shriek deals 1d6 sonic damage in a 30-ft. cone or a 15-ft.-radius burst, Reflex DC 13 half.

## Brak Greystripe, bridge sentinel

**Fighter 4, CR 4.** AC 19, HP 42, initiative +6. Fortitude +8, Reflex +3, Will +2. Short sword +9 for 1d4+5 piercing. Javelin +7 for 1d4+3 piercing.

Brak aims a cone down the bridge after the crew takes cover. Shriek deals 2d6 sonic damage, Reflex DC 16 half. Anyone who takes damage also rolls Fortitude DC 16 or is dazed for 1 round. Brak can use Skinshift twice per day for 7 rounds per use.
