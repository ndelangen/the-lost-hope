# Vril

This batch adds the playable Vril from the May 2007 *Drow of the Underdark*. It uses the published character entry and Wizards’ Vril Warrior errata. Skriv Ashstripe and Brak Greystripe are original NPCs. Skriv’s plain-parchment portrait represents the species profile.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and both encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/vril-draft.png). The [comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Skriv beside Big Linda.

In a temporary copy, search found Vril by name, Shriek, Skinshift, and both NPC roles. Skriv’s portrait loaded on the 2007 profile and as its list thumbnail. Both catalog commands returned the complete builds for that publication. [Verification](verification.json)
