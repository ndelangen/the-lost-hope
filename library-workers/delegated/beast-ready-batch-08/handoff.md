# Beast batch 08

Staged 50 Beast encounter profiles against `36d277f`. The roster covers seven revised 3.5 SRD entries, 18 from the *Savage Coast Monstrous Compendium Appendix*, 13 from *Tomb of Annihilation*, seven from the 2009 revision of *Keep on the Shadowfell*, and five from *The Land Beyond the Magic Mirror*. Each profile follows its publication's rules. AD&D entries preserve descending AC and Hit Dice, and 4e entries retain their native level, role, defenses, powers, and XP. [Roster](roster.tsv) · [Source and portrait manifest](manifest.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch) · [Portrait index](portrait-index-preview.json)

*Tomb of Annihilation* explicitly assigns Jackal statistics to Wild Dog and Riding Horse statistics to Zebra. Zebra cites Wongo's Shrine and the corrected +5 Hooves attack from the 2015 errata. The Savage Coast notes keep Echyan's printed alternative AC and flag the lack of swallowed damage and escape procedures. Trosip retains both the Tiny category and the conflicting printed diameter. [Zebra evidence](parts/b08_08/source-evidence.json) · [Echyan evidence](parts/b08_02/source-evidence.json) · [Trosip evidence](parts/b08_05/source-evidence.json)

The batch adds 20 uncompressed square PNGs in the plain-parchment style and reuses 30 verified portraits. The older Kruthik Young image leaves its small limb pair hard to distinguish. The older Lupasus wing edges look feathered at small sizes, while the printed description specifies fine fur. [Comparison with Big Linda](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Portrait sheet 1](portraits-1.jpg) · [Portrait sheet 2](portraits-2.jpg)

In a temporary Library copy, I found Batracine by name and by “sticky tongue,” checked its AD&D rules and new portrait, and opened the credited TSR illustration. Zebra loaded its new portrait with Wongo's Shrine and +5 Hooves. The Kruthik Young preview surfaced two sections titled Powers. The staged 4e profiles now use “Printed powers” for the full procedures. I reopened the entry and checked both headings, the Level 2 Brute label, and the end-of-turn aura. The final console was clean. The independent review matched all 50 portrait lookup keys and image hashes. [Batracine before and after](batracine-before-after.jpg) · [Zebra preview](zebra-preview.jpg) · [Kruthik Young preview](kruthik-young-preview.jpg)

To use the local bundle, apply the rules patch and append the preview index rows. Each index image path is relative to `output/dm-library/5etools`. The manifest identifies the packaged file and SHA-256 for each path.
