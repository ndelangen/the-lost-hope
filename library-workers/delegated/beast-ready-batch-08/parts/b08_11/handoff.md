# EX2 beasts

These five AD&D 1e profiles use the 1983 TSR module *The Land Beyond the Magic Mirror*. I checked the local against the printed folios. The notes keep the generic rules separate from named encounter adjustments. [Original module](https://device.report/m/ea082f82df7b66d6277283b8a1178539dfd94f1cc20e1e7409641c7c12becb29)

| Beast | Printed folios | Key decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Bumblebee | 9, 27 | General ground movement is 6″. Garden defenders move 3″ on the ground and have 36 HP each. The XP line leaves its per-HP coefficient blank. |
| Giant Dragonfly | 22, 27 | The encounter and the appendix prose both give +2 on initiative. I use that modifier and the one printed attack. The road groups have 25% magic resistance. |
| Giant Honey Bee (Soldier) | 27 | One or two attacks. I read the second damage entry as the once-only sting. The poison save takes −1. |
| Giant Honey Bee (Worker) | 27 | One 1–3 sting, usable once, with a normal save against poison. |
| Oliphant | 11, 30 | The ordinary beast has AC 4 and 8+4 HD. The Chessboard Rook has AC 2, 10+5 HD, 65 HP, and 25% magic resistance. The appendix leaves ordinary XP blank. |

For each bee, the block states the poison save outcomes and treats the wound and poison as separate damage. The text gives the two figures without specifying whether to add them together. No numeric morale is printed for these creatures.

The four reuse hashes match the roster. The soldier portrait has a pointed abdomen and stinger, and the worker portrait has prominent pollen baskets. The Giant Dragonfly is an original blue specimen inspired by the forest-road encounter. I used built-in ImageGen with Big Linda as the paint-and-paper reference. I chose the wider framing so all four wings fit inside the 96 px circular crop.
