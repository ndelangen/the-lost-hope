# Seven revised 3.5 SRD beasts

I checked the table and combat text for each beast in the original Wizards of the Coast RTF archive. The source evidence records the exact archive hash, shard, heading and shared rules used.

| Beast | Original RTF heading | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squid | `MonstersAnimals.rtf`, Squid | Its 0-damage Arms hit can start a free grapple against any size. Winning deals Bite damage. Ink Cloud is a 10-ft. cube, usable once per minute. The entry gives no duration for the cloud. |
| Tiger | `MonstersAnimals.rtf`, Tiger | Pounce includes a full attack and two Rakes. An ordinary grapple uses the shared Rake timing. The tiger table calls its bite and claw feats Improved Natural Weapon. The general feat heading reads Improved Natural Attack. |
| Triceratops | `MonstersDi-Do.rtf`, Triceratops | Powerful Charge deals 4d8 + 20. Trample deals 2d12 + 15, with the shared choice of a −4 opportunity attack or a DC 28 Reflex save for half. |
| Tyrannosaurus | `MonstersDi-Do.rtf`, Tyrannosaurus | Improved Grab can hold Large or smaller foes. Swallow Whole requires a held Medium or smaller foe and a new grapple check. The gizzard's damage, escape options and capacity are recorded. |
| War Pony | `MonstersAnimals.rtf`, Pony, War | Two hooves on a full attack. A rider who also attacks uses the DC 10 Ride check for a war-trained mount. The printed load and drag limits are recorded. |
| Wolf | `MonstersAnimals.rtf`, Wolf | A Bite hit permits the free +1 Trip check. A failed check gives the opponent no countertrip. The +4 racial Survival bonus applies when tracking by scent. |
| Wolverine | `MonstersAnimals.rtf`, Wolverine | Damage triggers Rage on its next turn. It gains +4 Strength and Constitution and takes −2 AC. The card labels the resulting attack, grapple, save and HP figures as derived. |

All seven reused portrait hashes matched the roster, and each image depicts the assigned animal. Appearance details describe those portraits. Habitat tags follow the original tables.
