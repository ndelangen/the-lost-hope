# Four Tomb of Annihilation beasts

The stat overlays use the September 2017 first printing. I checked the two Appendix D stat blocks and the two chapter 3 references in the original page layouts. The official adventure errata leaves these four entries as printed. [Tomb of Annihilation](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/toa) · [Adventure errata]()

| Beast | Printed page and page | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stegosaurus | 231, 232 | AC 13, HP 76 (8d12 + 24), CR 4, 1,100 XP. Tail +7 for 26 (6d6 + 5) piercing, 10-ft. reach. |
| Velociraptor | 235, 236 | AC 13, HP 10 (3d4 + 3), CR 1/4, 50 XP. Pack Tactics and Multiattack with Bite and Claws. |
| Wild Dog | 96, 97 | Walled Compound refers to Jackal, *Monster Manual* p. 331. AC 12, HP 3 (1d6), CR 0, 10 XP. Bite +1 for 1 (1d4 − 1) piercing. |
| Zebra | 106, 107 | Wongo's Shrine areas 12B–12C refer to Riding Horse, *Monster Manual* p. 336. AC 10, HP 13 (2d10 + 2), CR 1/4, 50 XP. Hooves +5 for 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning. |

I checked the two *Monster Manual* base blocks in the first-print scan. The Riding Horse originally printed Hooves +2 to hit. The errata corrected it to +5 by 2016, before the adventure appeared. The zebra uses the corrected bonus. The curse that can give a character zebra form remains Wongo's Shrine encounter procedure. [2014 Jackal](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16936-jackal) · [2014 Riding Horse](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16997-riding-horse) · [Monster Manual errata]()

The art for Wild Dog uses a painted-dog phenotype, an editorial choice because the adventure names the animals and specifies their mechanics without describing their appearance. The reused feathered Velociraptor portrait matches the roster hash. Habitat tags cite the encounter tables or are marked editorial. I inspected the square portraits at full size and their 96-pixel circular crops. The three new PNGs are 1254 × 1254. I removed the round frame that appeared in the first drafts so the tan paper reaches each corner.
