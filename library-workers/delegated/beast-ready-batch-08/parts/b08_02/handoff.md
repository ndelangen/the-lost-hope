# Five Savage Coast beasts

| Beast | Printed folio | Encounter check |
|---|---:|---|
| Batracine | 14–15 | 2+2 HD, THAC0 19, and one 1d4 bite. Its sticky tongue has no separate attack procedure. Adults gain the Leap Legacy. |
| Caniquine | 15–16 | 4+1 HD and THAC0 17. A trained mount can kick for 2d6 while stationary. To combine the kick and bite, the rider must signal. |
| Cinnavixen | 17–18 | 1+1 HD and THAC0 19. Each bite adds 1 HP of bleeding per round. Maintain pressure until the wound has been bandaged for over 24 hours. |
| Echyan | 26 | 5 HD and THAC0 15. It attaches on a hit for recurring 2d4 damage. A bend bars roll detaches it. Natural 18–20 can swallow a man-sized or smaller creature. |
| Feliquine | 32–33 | 4+4 HD and THAC0 15. A stationary hind-leg kick deals 2d6 and replaces use of the front claws that round. Surprise rolls receive +1. |

The profile cites each table and its continuation. Roll HP using the book's d8 convention. The additional Legacy and parasite procedures come from pp. 6–9 and 85–86. [Savage Coast Monstrous Compendium Appendix]()

The Echyan entry prints AC 4 (10) and leaves the alternate AC's trigger unspecified. Its swallow effect gives neither internal damage nor an escape procedure. For Cinnavixen, the profile records the bite's 20% vermilia chance and the Parasite entry's general saving throws. The book leaves the interaction between them unspecified.

The portraits follow the printed anatomy and use plain tan parchment. The comparison shows Big Linda, Batracine, Caniquine, Cinnavixen, Echyan, and Feliquine at square and 96-pixel circular sizes.
