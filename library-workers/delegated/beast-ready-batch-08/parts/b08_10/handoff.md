# Keep on the Shadowfell, 2009 revision

Three encounter profiles from the 71-page Wizards H1 . I read each block in the archived file and visually checked the printed folios and power icons. The and printed page numbers agree. [Archived Wizards ]()

| Beast | Printed pages | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kruthik Hatchling | 46–47 | Level 2 Minion, 31 XP. HP 1 with the missed-attack rule. Gnashing Horde applies at the end of the enemy’s turn. The at-will Claw deals 4 damage. Burrow 2 has the tunneling annotation. |
| Kruthik Young | 46–47 | Level 2 Brute, 125 XP. HP 43, bloodied 21. Same aura, with a different Claw damage roll. Its burrow 2 line omits the tunneling annotation, and p. 47 lets it traverse the existing lair tunnels at full speed. |
| Rat Swarm | 28–29 | Level 2 Skirmisher, 125 XP. HP 36, bloodied 18. Its aura uses the basic attack as a free action at the start of the enemy’s turn. Swarm of Teeth inflicts ongoing 3 damage (save ends). Keep half damage from melee and ranged attacks and vulnerable 5 to close and area attacks. |

The Claw and Swarm of Teeth powers are at-will. The area’s recharge power belongs to the Kruthik Adult, outside these profiles.

I verified all three reused portrait hashes. The two kruthik portraits show the armored reptilian forms. Their smaller inner limb pair is hard to distinguish, particularly on the young. The source notes retain the printed six-limbed description.
