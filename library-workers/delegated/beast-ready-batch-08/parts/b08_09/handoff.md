The Kruthik Adult’s Wisdom 12 modifier is printed as +4, even though the usual half-level calculation gives +3. The block keeps +4. The opener text mentions the first PC to enter range, and the Toxic Spikes power gives the two-target procedure. For the adult’s movement in lair tunnels, the detailed feature on p. 47 applies half speed.
