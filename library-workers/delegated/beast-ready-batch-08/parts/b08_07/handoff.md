# Tomb of Annihilation beasts

Four encounter blocks from the September 2017 first printing, with one new plain-parchment portrait each. The page is one greater than the printed page. I checked both the original Appendix D and the 2021 errata. The errata makes no change to these four blocks. [Book contents](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/toa) · [2021 errata]()

| Beast | Appendix D folio | AC | HP | CR and XP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Giant Snapping Turtle | 222 | 17, or 12 while prone | 75 (10d10 + 20) | 3, 700 XP |
| Hadrosaurus | 224 | 11 | 19 (3d10 + 3) | 1/4, 50 XP |
| Jaculi | 225, description on 224 | 14 | 16 (3d10) | 1/2, 100 XP |
| Quetzalcoatlus | 230, description on 229 | 13 | 30 (4d12 + 4) | 2, 450 XP |

- Giant Snapping Turtle's Stable gives an optional DC 10 Constitution save when an effect knocks it prone. If overturned, it needs a DC 10 Dexterity check and all its movement that turn to stand. King Toba has separate encounter changes on p. 83. [Giant Snapping Turtle](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/33030-giant-snapping-turtle)
- Hadrosaurus has Strength 15 (+2). The original page confirms the plus sign that OCR lost. Its appendix Tail attack uses +4 to hit and 1d10 + 2 damage. The Port Nyanzaru racing table has separate procedure and statistics. [Hadrosaurus](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17241-hadrosaurus)
- Jaculi's Spring moves up to 30 ft. in a straight line and makes one Bite. A leap of at least 10 ft. gives the Bite advantage. Every Spring hit adds 2d6 piercing damage. [Jaculi](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/33039-jaculi)
- Quetzalcoatlus's Dive Attack needs at least a 30-ft. dive while flying and a successful Bite. Flyby applies when it flies out of reach. Its Bite reaches 10 ft. [Quetzalcoatlus](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17242-quetzalcoatlus)

Habitat tags follow the adventure's descriptions and checked encounter-table columns. The original portraits use printed anatomy and editorial color choices, with blank parchment instead of Chult scenery. I reviewed the full square images and compared their 96 px circular thumbnails with the Big Linda style reference.
