# Four Savage Coast beasts

Four AD&D 2nd Edition blocks from TSR's 1996 *Savage Coast Monstrous Compendium Appendix*. I checked the stat tables and each following-page continuation. The viewer counts the cover, so its page number is one higher than the printed folio. [TSR appendix ]()

| Creature | Printed pages | Descending AC | Hit points | THAC0 | XP |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Trosip | 112–113 | 10 | 1 fixed | 20 | 35 |
| Voat | 115–116 | 7 | Roll 1d4 | 20 | 65 |
| Vulturehound | 117–118 | 5 | Roll 2d8 for an adult | 19 | 65 |
| Wynzet | 122–123 | 7 | Roll 1d8 | 19 | 120 |

- Trosip suffocation uses a save vs. paralyzation with only magical protection bonuses. Four trosips can attack a man-sized sleeper. More than twelve on one victim require two successful saves. The table prints both Tiny size and a 6′–9′ diameter. The block records both. [Trosip]()
- Voat's bite has a 5% disease chance unless the victim saves vs. poison. Its entry describes the common Phase Legacy and Affliction. The profile includes the campaign book's active Phase procedure. [Voat]()
- Vulturehound can only use its beak when it cannot rear, attacks with front talons in flight, and loses flight after losing more than 75% of starting HP. Renardy Legacies and the pup rules are included. [Vulturehound]()
- Wynzet's first successful throat bite uses a save vs. paralyzation with Dexterity bonuses. A failed save kills the victim, and a success still deals 2d8. The book gives no reset trigger for that first-hit effect. [Wynzet]()

The appendix supplies the beast-specific rules. The same-era *Savage Coast Campaign Book*, Part 2, covers general Legacy uses and durations. All four selected portraits match the roster hashes and depict the printed subjects. [Supporting Legacy rules]()
