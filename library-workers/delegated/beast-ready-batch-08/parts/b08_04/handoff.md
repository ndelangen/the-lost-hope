# Savage Coast beasts

Four AD&D 2e records use the 1996 TSR appendix. Hit points are rolled from the listed Hit Dice.

| Record | Descending AC | HD | THAC0 | XP | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nikt’oo | 5, or 6 near the legs | 3 | 17 | 270 | [pp. 78–79]() |
| Omm-wa | 6, or 10 unarmored | 3 | 17 | 175 | [pp. 81–82]() |
| Succulus | 2 | 5 | 15 | 420 | [pp. 103–104]() |
| Swampmare | 5, or 0 in tree form | 3+1 | 17 | 175 | [pp. 104–105]() |

Omm-wa Burn and Succulus Dig use the Campaign Book where the appendix names the powers without describing them. Succulus Spikes uses the appendix’s monster version. Swampmare keeps the three attacks in the table and the separate Forefeet line that accounts for the four attacks described in water. Its tree form takes at most 1 weapon damage per round. [Appendix Legends and Legacies](), [Campaign Book, Book Two]()

Nikt’oo and Succulus are new square ImageGen originals on plain tan parchment. Both retain their faces, limbs and defining silhouettes in a circular crop. The Nikt’oo prompt specified olive skin over flush bony plates with brown and yellow spots. The Succulus prompt specified a charcoal woolly boar with a coarse mane, long lower tusks and rust-red eyes. Omm-wa and Swampmare retain the indexed images, and their hashes match the roster.
