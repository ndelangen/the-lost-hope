# Beast-ready batch 08, group 06

Five encounter profiles are staged from the September 2017 first printing of *Tomb of Annihilation*. I checked the printed folios directly. The official errata does not change these stat blocks.

| Beast | Printed folio | AC | HP | CR and XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Almiraj | 211 | 13 | 3 | 0, 10 XP |
| Brontosaurus | 215 | 15 | 121 | 5, 1,800 XP |
| Deinonychus | 217 | 13 | 26 | 1, 200 XP |
| Dimetrodon | 217 | 12 | 19 | 1/4, 50 XP |
| Flying Monkey | 220, with prose on 221 | 12 | 3 | 0, 10 XP |

[Stat blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Publisher listing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/toa) · [Official errata]()

Deinonychus Pounce uses a straight 20-ft. approach, a Claw hit on the same turn and a DC 12 Strength save. Its follow-up Bite is a bonus action against the prone target. Brontosaurus Stomp and Tail both reach 20 ft. Almiraj and Flying Monkey require DM permission to be summoned as familiars. The Appendix B encounter rules are labeled separately.

Four original 1254 × 1254 PNGs use the same plain tan parchment as Big Linda. I revised the Almiraj's ears to follow the printed illustration and gave Brontosaurus and Dimetrodon more room around their heads and tails for circular cropping. The existing Deinonychus portrait matched its roster SHA-256.
