# Five Savage Coast beasts

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Juhrion portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompt](portrait-prompts.json)

I checked the original 1996 TSR appendix, including its general Hit Dice, flight and Legacy rules. The encounter blocks keep descending AC and the printed THAC0, morale and XP. Roll HP from the printed Hit Dice. The evidence lists the book folios and the corresponding page numbers. [Original appendix]()

| Entry | Printed folios | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Herathian Voat | 116–117 | Movement 18 for three rounds on all fours. The 30-yard screech forces a save vs. paralyzation against a 1d4-round stun. It regenerates 1 HP per turn. Region 1 Legacies and their XP are conditional. |
| Jorri | 57–58 | Five attacks. A successful bite can lock jaws. A victim who can reach the jaws needs a Bend Bars roll to break the lock. The table says 3–4 ft. long and the prose says 2–4 ft. |
| Juhrion | 58–59 and Vermilia on 85–86 | Two claw hits in one round trigger Maul. Its claw text gives a 50% Vermilia chance and the Parasite entry gives applicable saves. The source leaves the roll order open. |
| Lupasus | 68–69 | Flight caps the load at 200 lb. A rider changes maneuverability from C to D and halves the save interval. Losing half its HP stops flight and losing three quarters stops gliding. |
| Marine Cat | 16–17 | If the cat is held and both foreclaws hit, it can use both rear claws that round. It is surprised only on 1. Swim and Breathe Water are rare Legacy examples. |

The Herathian Voat prose uses Slagovich juggernaut as an alias and also describes a juggernaut created to hunt the voats. The encounter follows the printed heading and rules on those folios. Marine Cat's optional power details come from the separately cited 1996 Campaign Book. [Characters of the Savage Coast]()

Juhrion's 1254 × 1254 original shows the printed hairless red hide, bear shape and hooked claws. I checked its face and silhouette at full size and in a 96-pixel circle against Big Linda. All four reused portrait hashes match the roster. Lupasus's reused wing edges read like layered feathers, while the book specifies fine fur. [New portrait metadata](portrait-files.json)
