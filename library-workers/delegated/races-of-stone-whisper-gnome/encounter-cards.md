# Whisper Gnome encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. Both are Small humanoids with the gnome subtype, 30-ft. speed, 60-ft. darkvision, and low-light vision. Both speak Common and Gnome. [Printed traits, p. 96]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Nella Sedgefold, Rogue 1

Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 16, touch 14, flat-footed 13. HP 8, initiative +3. Fort +2, Ref +5, Will +0. BAB +0, grapple −4. STR 10, DEX 17, CON 14, INT 13, WIS 10, CHA 10.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small rapier | +1 melee, 1d4 piercing, 18–20/×2 |
| Small light crossbow | +4 ranged, 1d6 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment |

Add +1 to the attack rolls above against kobolds and goblinoids. Against Giant-type creatures, AC 20 and touch 18 while able to dodge. Sneak attack adds 1d6 when the target is denied its Dexterity bonus or is flanked. Ranged sneak attacks require a target within 30 ft.

Balance +7, Disable Device +5, Hide +15, Listen +8, Move Silently +11, Open Lock +7, Search +5, Spot +8, Tumble +7. Listen and Spot include Alertness. Trapfinding lets Nella find traps above Search DC 20 and disable magic traps.

With Charisma 10, she can use *ghost sound*, *mage hand*, and *message* once per day each, caster level 1. A creature interacting with her *ghost sound* can attempt Will DC 10 to disbelieve. She needs to get a ledger slip through the postern before closing. She offers to approach while the party checks why the lantern signal never came.

## Tovin Larkwell, Fighter 1

Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 16, touch 13, flat-footed 14. HP 13, initiative +6. Fort +5, Ref +2, Will +0. BAB +1, grapple −2. STR 12, DEX 15, CON 16, INT 12, WIS 10, CHA 8.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small gnome hooked hammer, blunt head | +4 melee, 1d6+1 bludgeoning, ×3 |
| Small gnome hooked hammer, hook | +4 melee, 1d4+1 piercing, ×4 |
| Small light crossbow | +4 ranged, 1d6 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment |

Tovin uses either hammer head on its own with two hands. Weapon Focus is included. Add +1 to the attack rolls above against kobolds and goblinoids. Against Giant-type creatures, AC 20 and touch 17 while able to dodge.

Climb +4, Craft (bellmaking) +5, Hide +9, Jump +4, Listen +2, Move Silently +5, Spot +2. His Charisma 8 gives him the baseline *silence* ability. He asks the party to steady the cracked bell and lower its clapper while he keeps the repair platform quiet.

## Silence, once per day each

Center the ability on the gnome. It takes a standard action at caster level 1. The moving 20-ft.-radius emanation lasts 1 minute and can be dismissed. It stops sound passing through the area and blocks verbal spell components within it. Either NPC can use this ability. [3.5 SRD, *silence*](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/silence.htm)
