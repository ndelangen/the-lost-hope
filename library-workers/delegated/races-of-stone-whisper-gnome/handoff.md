# Whisper Gnome for D&D 3.5

One playable Whisper Gnome profile from the August 2004 first printing of *Races of Stone*. The printed traits give a 30-ft. speed, darkvision and low-light vision, and a total +8 to Hide from the racial and Small size bonuses before other modifiers. [Printed traits, p. 96]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

A Whisper Gnome can use *silence* once per day at any Charisma. It must be centered on the gnome and moves with them in a 20-ft. radius. Charisma 10 or higher grants one daily use each of *ghost sound*, *mage hand*, and *message*. [3.5 SRD, *silence*](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/silence.htm)

Nella Sedgefold is an original Rogue 1 and night courier with Charisma 10. She appears in the full-body plain-parchment portrait. Tovin Larkwell is an original Fighter 1 and bellwright with Charisma 8. Both are provisional CR 1. The cards give their Giant-only dodge totals and attack bonuses against kobolds and goblinoids.
