# Independent Whisper Gnome reviews

The source reviewer checked the August 2004 first printing. William O’Connor’s p. 95 portrait accompanies a physical description of a lean gnome with muted gray-to-green skin and gray or blue eyes. Scott Roller drew the p. 96 chest illustration. The rules on p. 96 give the daily self-centered *silence* independently of Charisma. Core *silence* is a moving 20-ft. emanation when centered on a creature. The other three daily abilities require Charisma 10 or higher. Extra Silence is on p. 139. Silencing Strike is on p. 144. [Artwork and description]() · [Species rules]() · [3.5 SRD, *silence*](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/silence.htm)

The printed language prose gives Gnome and says most Whisper Gnomes also speak Terran. It leaves the formal bonus-language list, creature type, and level adjustment unspecified. Common follows the *Player’s Handbook* default. The later published Draen Ralgael block in *Exemplars of Evil*, pp. 52–53, corroborates the humanoid type, gnome subtype, and Common and Gnome. Draen’s separate CR 5 profile includes the wererat template. The original *Races of Stone* entry supplies no encounter block. The reviewer found no specific correction in Wizards’ collected errata.
