# Dvati encounters

Place two tokens for each pair and give each body its own HP counter. The catalog lists the per-twin stats. **Table ruling for these cards.** Roll initiative once per pair and resolve both turns on that count. Declare shared casting and Echo Attack together. Each pair has LA +1 for one character. [Twin rules, pp. 16–17]()

When both twins flank the same foe, each gains +3 to hit. They may then both spend a move action speaking to use Echo Attack. Roll Perform (sing) once for the Will DC. If the target fails, choose +1 to both twins’ attacks against it or +1 to their AC against its attacks for one round. The target must be able to hear them and be flanked. If they start their turns already flanking, they can each speak and then attack with their standard action. [Pair Link and Echo Attack, p. 17]()

Track damage and healing on the body affected. For an ordinary area effect covering both, save for each twin. A mind-affecting effect on one affects both. Use one save if the same such effect targets both at once. Negative levels apply to both. If a twin dies, the survivor takes 1d4 Constitution and Wisdom damage per day and a cumulative −1 to attacks, skills, and saves. These effects end if the other twin returns to life. [Twin rules, p. 17]()

## Aven and Iven Threadwell, crossroad chorus

Chaotic good Bard 2. Provisional CR 3 for the pair, ECL 3. Each has **9 HP**, AC 15 (touch 12, flat-footed 13), speed 30 ft., initiative +2, Fort +2, Ref +5, Will +3, and darkvision 60 ft. Perform (sing) +11. Their shared Hit Dice rolled 6 and 4. Each gets 5 from the dice and 4 from Constitution.

- **Short sword** +1, 1d6 piercing, 19–20/×2. With Pair Link it attacks at +4. A failed Echo Attack save against the shared 1d20+11 check can raise it to +5. **Shortbow** +3, 1d6 piercing, ×3, range increment 60 ft.
- **Bardic music** two uses per day for the pair. Inspire Courage gives allies who hear it +1 on attacks, weapon damage, and saves against charm and fear while heard and for five rounds after. Countersong and fascinate use Perform (sing) +11.
- **Spells** use three cantrip slots and one shared 1st-level slot per day at caster level 2. The twins normally cast together. If one casts alone, the other takes no action during casting, including free actions. A failed attempt spends the spell.
- **Expeditious retreat** gives both twins on the same plane a 60-ft. land speed for two minutes. **Cure light wounds** restores 1d8+2 HP to one willing living target touched. Choose one of these for the day’s shared 1st-level slot. Cantrips are *detect magic*, *light*, *mage hand*, *message*, and *prestidigitation*.

The twins are directing late travellers around a road closure. They can use *expeditious retreat* to cover both forks or save the slot for an injured traveller. [Bard spellcasting and music](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/bard.htm)

## Mara and Sera Reedbank, split floodgate wardens

Lawful good Fighter 1. Provisional CR 2 for the pair, ECL 2. Each has **7 HP**, AC 15 (touch 11, flat-footed 14), speed 30 ft., initiative +5, Fort +4, Ref +1, Will +1, and darkvision 60 ft. Perform (sing) +2. Each gets 5 from the shared 10 on the Hit Die and 2 from Constitution.

- **Spear** +5 melee, 1d8+4 piercing, ×3. Thrown +3, 1d8+3 piercing, ×3, range increment 20 ft.
- **Pair Link** makes each spear +8 when both flank. They can then spend both move actions for Echo Attack, DC 1d20+2. A failed save lets them choose +9 to hit or AC 16 against that foe for one round.
- **Aid Another** takes a standard action in melee. Roll a spear attack against AC 10. On success, give the other twin +4 on the relevant attack or to AC against the next attack from that opponent.

Mara and Sera work the pin wheels on opposite sides of the floodgate. They can warn each other telepathically through the stone. If a creature forces the crossing, they take opposite sides of it or have one twin help the other get a clear strike. [Pair Link and telepathy, p. 17]()
