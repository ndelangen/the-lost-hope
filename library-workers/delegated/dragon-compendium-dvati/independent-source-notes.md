# Dvati source and review notes

Talon Dunning’s AD&D Dvati includes player options in *Dragon* #271, May 2000, printed p. 81. The AD&D echo-voice and survivor HP loss differ from the 3.5 versions, and its Magic Resistance field says Nil. The playable profile in this batch uses the October 2005 first printing of *Dragon Compendium, Volume I*, pp. 14–17 (pp. 18–21). Paizo’s errata contains no Dvati correction. [Original magazine]() · [Compendium]() · [Paizo errata]()

The book’s example divides the Hit Die result and then applies the full Constitution modifier to each twin. Both encounter builds use even die results. The one-initiative procedure is a table ruling for resolving simultaneous declared actions. The printed rule allows separate actions and reserves simultaneous casting or an idle partner for spells. [Dvati rules, pp. 16–17]()

Tom Fowler’s drawings accompany the Compendium section. The text describes identical slight twins, snow-white skin, black hair, eyes that are solid blue, and three fingers plus a thumb on each hand. The two outfits and stance in the staged portrait are original. [Compendium artwork, pp. 14–17]()

Independent integration review checked both skill budgets, the split Hit Dice, separate HP, shared spell slots, spear values, and Pair Link and Echo Attack. Both pair concepts attach only to the 2005 profile.
