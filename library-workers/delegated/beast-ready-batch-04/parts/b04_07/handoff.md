# Giant Elk, Giant Fire Beetle, and Giant Frog

Six rules summaries for three beasts across the November 2018 *Basic Rules* v1.0 and the 2014 *Monster Manual*. Each pair shares one original portrait. The encounter rules agree between the two printings. The official errata has no entry for these creatures. [2018 Basic Rules]() · [2014 printed folio p. 325](https://online.anyflip.com/sqwmo/hxog/mobile/index.html#p=326) · [Official errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)

- Giant Elk's Ram has 10-ft. reach. Charge adds 2d6 damage and may knock a creature prone. The printed Charge text gives no separate damage type for the extra dice. Hooves requires an already prone creature within 5 ft. and uses a separate action. [Basic Rules p. 132]()
- Giant Fire Beetle sheds bright light for 10 ft. and dim light for another 10 ft. Both descriptions say the two glands keep producing light for 1d6 days after death. The *Monster Manual* names underground habitats and dark forests. [Basic Rules p. 132]()
- Giant Frog's Bite grapples and restrains. Swallow uses a new Bite roll against a Small or smaller target the frog already grapples and replaces the grapple with the swallowed effects on a hit. The swallowed creature takes acid damage at the start of the frog's turn. [Basic Rules p. 133]()

I checked both books by hand and marked editorial appearance and habitat details in the source evidence. I reviewed the 1254 × 1254 ImageGen portraits at full size and 96 px beside Big Linda.
