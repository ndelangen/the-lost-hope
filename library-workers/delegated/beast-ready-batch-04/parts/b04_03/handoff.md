# Dire Wolf, Draft Horse, and Flying Snake

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

The printed combat rules agree across the two publications. I verified the ability scores, defenses, actions, and traits in both sources. The official *Monster Manual* errata lists no creature-specific changes for these three entries. Both books place the Flying Snake in remote jungles. The *Monster Manual* also describes their use as trained messengers. Additional search habitats come from the legacy digital tags or from the editorial inferences noted in the evidence.
