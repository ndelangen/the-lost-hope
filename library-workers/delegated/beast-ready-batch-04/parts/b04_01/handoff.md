# Allosaurus, Ankylosaurus and Black Bear

I checked six source entries against the November 2018 *Basic Rules* v1.0 and the 11th printing of the 2014 *Monster Manual*. The default stat blocks match across each pair. [2018 Basic Rules]() · [Monster Manual errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)

| Beast | 2018 Basic Rules page | 2014 Monster Manual folio | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Allosaurus | 116 | 79 | AC 13, 51 HP, 60-ft. speed. Pounce takes a straight 30-ft. approach and a Claw hit on the same turn. DC 13 Strength or prone, with a bonus-action Bite against that prone target. |
| Ankylosaurus | 117 | 79 | AC 15, 68 HP, 30-ft. speed. Tail +7, 10-ft. reach, 18 bludgeoning and DC 14 Strength or prone on a hit against a creature. |
| Black Bear | 119 | 318 | AC 11, 19 HP, 40-ft. walk and 30-ft. climb. Multiattack uses one Bite and one Claws attack. Both are +4 to hit, as the Monster Manual errata specifies. |

I kept the 2014 *Monster Manual*'s spiked-tail piercing variety with that publication. The default tail attack still deals bludgeoning damage. The habitat tags are editorial encounter suggestions. The dinosaur tags draw on the printed group description, and the Allosaurus also has its printed open-ground hunting context.
