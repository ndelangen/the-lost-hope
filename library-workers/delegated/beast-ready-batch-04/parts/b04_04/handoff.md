# Giant Ape, Giant Badger, and Giant Bat

I checked the September 2014 first printing of the *Monster Manual* against the November 2018 *Basic Rules* v1.0. Printed folio 323 and page 130 agree on all three beasts. The publisher's 2014 *Monster Manual* errata list no changes to these entries. [Printed folio 323](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/index.html#p=324) · [2018 Basic Rules p. 130]() · [Monster Manual errata]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

| Beast | Printed basics | Attacks and traits |
|---|---|---|
| Giant Ape | AC 12, HP 157 (15d12 + 60), CR 7, 2,900 XP. | Multiattack makes two Fist attacks. Its separate Rock attack is +9 with range 50/100 ft. |
| Giant Badger | AC 10, HP 13 (2d8 + 4), CR 1/4, 50 XP. | Keen Smell. A Bite and a Claws attack make up Multiattack. |
| Giant Bat | AC 13, HP 22 (4d10), CR 1/4, 50 XP. | Blindsight 60 ft., Echolocation, Keen Hearing, and Bite +4 for 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing. |

The printed Giant Bat starts in the lower left column and continues at the top of the right. I checked the page image to verify both sound-related traits immediately above Bite. The mirror's text extraction skips those two lines. [Stat blocks](stat-blocks.json)
