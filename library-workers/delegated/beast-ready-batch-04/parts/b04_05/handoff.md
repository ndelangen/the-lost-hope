# Giant Boar, Giant Centipede and Giant Constrictor Snake

Six encounter blocks. The two printed sources agree on all three beasts. I checked the September 2014 *Monster Manual* and November 2018 *Basic Rules* against their own folios and reviewed the official *Monster Manual* errata. [Basic Rules p. 131]() · [Monster Manual errata]()

| Creature | Basic Rules | Monster Manual | AC | HP | CR | XP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Giant Boar | 131 | 323 | 12 | 42 | 2 | 450 |
| Giant Centipede | 131 | 323 | 13 | 4 | 1/4 | 50 |
| Giant Constrictor Snake | 131 | 324 | 12 | 60 | 2 | 450 |

- Giant Boar adds 2d6 slashing damage on a qualifying Charge. DC 13 Strength determines whether a creature falls prone. Relentless applies to damage of 10 or less that would drop the boar to 0 HP.
- Giant Centipede's poison requires a failed DC 11 Constitution save. Paralysis and the one-hour poisoned condition apply when the poison damage itself reduces the target to 0 HP.
- Giant Constrictor Snake grapples on a Constrict hit and restrains that creature until the grapple ends. Escape uses DC 16. [Monster grapple rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monsters#GrappleRulesforMonsters)

Each pair shares an original 1254 × 1254 PNG. I compared Big Linda and all three portraits at 96 pixels and inspected the source-size art. The Centipede PNG was restored from the ImageGen output after a preview command briefly displaced it. All three staged PNGs match their source files byte for byte.
