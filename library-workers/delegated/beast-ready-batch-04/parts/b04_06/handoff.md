The stat block mechanics agree across both printings. Giant Crab's two claws can each grapple one target, with escape DC 11. Giant Crocodile's tail can attack only a target outside its grapple. Giant Eagle understands Common and Auran and speaks Giant Eagle. Its nesting details come from the Monster Manual prose.

Three 1254 × 1254 original PNGs share the project’s plain tan parchment, with one image per beast across the two printings. I compared them with Big Linda at portrait size and 96 pixels. The corrected crab has two pincers and eight walking legs. The source evidence marks the editorial appearance and habitat details.
