# Giant Goat, Giant Hyena, and Giant Lizard

Six existing Beast records have encounter rules. Each animal has one original parchment portrait shared by its two records. I used the printed 2014 *Monster Manual* and 2018 *Basic Rules* . The published *Monster Manual* errata lists no changes to these three entries. [2014 Monster Manual, printed p. 326](https://fliphtml5.com/vmzaj/Monster-Manual_AddPage/328/) · [Monster Manual errata v2.0]()

| Animal | Basic Rules page | Base stats and action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Goat | [133]() | AC 11, HP 19 (3d10 + 3), CR 1/2, 100 XP. Ram +5 for 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning. |
| Giant Hyena | [133]() | AC 12, HP 45 (6d10 + 12), CR 1, 200 XP. Bite +5 for 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing. |
| Giant Lizard | [134]() | AC 12, HP 19 (3d10 + 3), CR 1/4, 50 XP. Bite +4 for 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing. |

The goat's Charge adds 2d4 damage on the hit. The DC 13 Strength save determines prone for a creature hit. The hyena's Rampage uses a bonus action for movement and Bite after it drops a creature to 0 HP with a melee attack on its turn. The lizard's base stats match between books. The *Monster Manual* also prints optional Hold Breath, which brings a 30-ft. swim speed, and Spider Climb. Either or both can apply. The *Basic Rules* profile uses only its printed base.

I checked the source values, timing and conditions by hand. Appearance describes the original portraits. Habitat tags are editorial except where the source evidence cites the lizard's Underdark lore. I inspected the 1254 × 1254 originals and compared their 96-pixel circular previews with Big Linda. The Giant Lizard received an anatomy correction to remove a fifth visible foot.
