# Blood Hawk, Brown Bear, and Constrictor Snake

| Beast | Monster Manual | Basic Rules v1.0, November 2018 | Encounter check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blood Hawk | p. 319 | p. 120 | Pack Tactics applies when a non-incapacitated ally is within 5 ft. of the target creature. Beak is +4 for 1d4+2 piercing. |
| Brown Bear | p. 319 and errata v2.0 | p. 120 | Multiattack is one Bite and one Claws attack, both +6. The 2014 first printing gave +5 for both attacks. |
| Constrictor Snake | p. 320 | p. 122 | Constrict hits at +4 for 1d8+2 bludgeoning and grapples with escape DC 14. The grapple restrains the target and prevents constricting another one. |
