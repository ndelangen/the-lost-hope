# Giant Octopus

Two encounter records with identical mechanics. I verified the 2018 Basic Rules and the 2014 Monster Manual entry, then checked the official errata for a Giant Octopus correction. Neither printing has a source conflict. [Basic Rules, p. 134]() · [Monster Manual, printed p. 326](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page327.html) · [Official errata]()

- **Tentacles.** +5 to hit at 15 ft. for 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning. A hit grapples and restrains a creature. The DC 16 check is for escaping. The octopus can target only that creature with Tentacles while the grapple lasts. [Monster grapple rules, Basic Rules p. 114]()
- **Ink Cloud.** Underwater only. Heavily obscures a 20-ft. radius for 1 minute unless a significant current disperses it first. The octopus may Dash as a bonus action after release. Recharge after a short or long rest. [Legacy rules listing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16888-giant-octopus)

Both printings share one original 1254 × 1254 PNG. I checked all eight arms at full size and the mantle and face at 96 pixels. The darker, heavier form distinguishes this from the ordinary Octopus portrait. Reefs and kelp forests are editorial habitat tags drawn from the giant Pacific octopus.
