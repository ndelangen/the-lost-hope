# Hengeyokai encounter cards

These original D&D 3.0 NPCs each have one class level, ECL 2, and 5 maximum HP in all three forms. Each has two supernatural changes per day. A change takes a full-round action and provokes an attack of opportunity. Gear merges in animal form. Hybrid and animal forms gain low-light vision and can communicate with their own animal kind. [Oriental Adventures, pp. 10–12]() · [Player's Handbook, p. 236]()

## Seki, Badger Hengeyokai Expert 1

Seki starts in hybrid form at a damaged mine entrance. Workers are trapped on the far side. Seki needs the party to keep the main shaft clear while checking a narrow support passage. Provisional CR 1/2.

| Form | Speed | AC, touch, flat-footed | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human, Medium | Walk 30 ft. | 12, 10, 12 | +0 | +1, +0, +0 |
| Hybrid, Medium | Walk 20 ft., burrow 10 ft. | 12, 10, 12 | +0 | +2, +0, +0 |
| Badger, Tiny | Walk 30 ft., burrow 10 ft. | 15, 15, 12 | +3 | +2, +3, +0 |

Human or hybrid club +1 melee, 1d6+1 bludgeoning, critical ×2, reach 5 ft. Badger claw +1 melee, 1d2−1. Full attack with two claws +1 each and secondary bite −4 for 1d3−1. Each natural hit deals at least 1 damage. Badger natural reach is 0 ft.

Human and hybrid skills include Climb +5, Craft (stonemasonry) +4, Listen +2, Profession (mine surveyor) +4, Search +4, and Wilderness Lore +2. In the badger form, use Str 8, Dex 17, and Con 15. Add +10 circumstance to animal-form Disguise.

## Aya, Sparrow Hengeyokai Rogue 1

Aya carries a warning to the next watch post before a washout seals the road. Aya can scout ahead by air while the party keeps the lower path open. Provisional CR 1.

| Form | Speed | AC, touch, flat-footed | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human, Medium | Walk 30 ft. | 13, 11, 12 | +1 | +1, +3, −1 |
| Hybrid, Medium | Walk 20 ft., fly 20 ft. (average) | 14, 12, 12 | +2 | +1, +4, −1 |
| Sparrow, Fine | Walk 1 ft., fly 50 ft. | 24, 24, 18 | +6 | +0, +8, −1 |

Human or hybrid dagger −1 melee or +1 thrown in human form and +2 thrown in hybrid. It deals 1d4−1 piercing, minimum 1, with a 19–20/×2 critical, 5-ft. melee reach, and a 10-ft. range increment. Sneak Attack adds 1d6 on qualifying hits, with a 30-ft. range limit. Sparrow form is for scouting.

Human skills are Balance +5, Climb +3, Disguise +3, Hide +5, Listen +5, Move Silently +5, Spot +5, and Tumble +5. Add +1 to Dex-based skills in hybrid form. Sparrow form uses Str 1, Dex 23, and Con 10, with Hide +26 and Disguise +13. Aya can take a light weapon or an object weighing up to 6 lb into hybrid flight. A shield or buckler prevents flight.
