# Hengeyokai for D&D 3.0

One playable Hengeyokai entry contains all 13 animal choices in the 2001 *Oriental Adventures*. Badger is the default. Both playable tables include Raccoon Dog, which is absent from the shorter encounter list on p. 168. The Badger hybrid portrait represents the shared entry on plain parchment. [Playables, pp. 10–12]() · [Encounter, p. 168]() · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg)

The 2002 errata gives the Dog animal form AC 15 and the Crane and Sparrow hybrids average flight maneuverability. The publisher's 2003 FAQ confirms that the hybrids are Medium and explains the flying hybrids' carrying limits. The FAQ mirror returned a 404 on direct download. The source review used the cached publisher text. [Errata](https://www.scribd.com/document/624226672/OAWeberrata) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md)
