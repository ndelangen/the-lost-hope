# Oriental Adventures Hengeyokai source check

James Wyatt’s *Oriental Adventures* is the 2001 Wizards of the Coast D&D 3.0 book, ISBN 0-7869-2015-7, product code 620-T12015-001-EN. The 256-page original text has matching printed folios and page numbers at the cited locations. The 13-page publisher sample shifts printed folios one page earlier in the , so its pp. 9–11 contain printed pp. 10–12. The book gives one Hengeyokai identity with thirteen animal types in Chapter 1. ## Shared rules and transformation

- −2 Wisdom, Shapechanger creature type, +1 level adjustment. The 3.0 type is susceptible to shapechanger-targeting spells and immune to spells that specifically affect humanoids, including *charm person* and *hold person*. Automatic languages are Common and Hengeyokai. Bonus languages are Giant, Goblin, Nezumi and Spirit Tongue. Favored class is Wu Jen, which is ignored for the multiclass XP penalty. [Printed pp. 10–11][p10]
- The human form is Medium-size with 30-foot base speed and generated ability scores, subject to the racial Wisdom modifier. Each Hengeyokai has one fixed animal form and corresponding fixed hybrid form. Changing is supernatural, follows *polymorph other* with listed exceptions, takes a full-round action and provokes attacks of opportunity. The daily limit is 1 + actual character level. Each transition costs one use, including return to human form. A 1st-level character can transform and return in a day. [Printed pp. 10–11][p10] · [Wizards FAQ, pp. 2–3][faq]
- Animal form uses the listed size, speed, AC, damage and physical scores. These physical scores are fixed even when the character’s human form gains a point from level advancement. Carried and worn equipment melds with the form and magic items stop functioning. Animal form has low-light vision, supernatural communication with animals of its own kind as a familiar does, and +10 to Disguise checks to appear an animal. The encounter entry calls the bonus circumstance. [Printed p. 11][p11] · [Printed p. 12][p12] · [Printed p. 168][p168] · [Wizards FAQ][faq]
- Hybrid form retains low-light vision and communication with animals of the corresponding kind. Its physical score modifier applies to the score the character has when transforming. All listed hybrids are Medium-size according to the FAQ. They can usually wear unmodified light or medium armor and cannot wear heavy armor. Human form equipment stays as it is when changing to hybrid. Changing from animal to hybrid restores equipment and magic item operation. Crane and Sparrow hybrid hands sit at the ends of their wings. During flight they cannot hold a buckler or shield and are limited to light weapons or objects weighing 6 pounds or less. [Printed p. 11][p11] · [Wizards FAQ][faq]
- The printed +1 level adjustment makes a 1st-level Hengeyokai ECL 2 and the sidebar’s minimum starting XP is 1,000. That sidebar uses ECL for XP thresholds and starting equipment, and actual level for feats, skills and other class progression. The FAQ’s example is character level 3 and ECL 4, with four daily transformations and no 4th-level ability increase yet. [Printed p. 11][p11] · [Wizards FAQ][faq]

## NPC, setting and art

The p. 168 Hengeyokai entry is a first-level warrior profile, Medium-Size Shapechanger, CR 1/2, 1d8 HD (4 HP), initiative +0, move 30 feet, AC 13 (+3 ashigaru armor), katana +2 melee (1d10), 5-foot face and reach. Saves are Fort +2, Ref +0, Will −1. Scores are Str 10, Dex 11, Con 10, Int 10, Wis 9, Cha 11. Skills are Climb +3, Disguise +0 before the animal circumstance bonus, and Jump +3. The feat is Weapon Focus (katana). It lists any climate, solitary or bands of 2–20, standard treasure, usual chaotic good or chaotic evil alignment and class advancement. The tactics describe the animal form for reconnaissance, hybrid form for fighting or speaking with animals and humanoids, and human form for moving among humans. [Printed p. 168][p168]

The playable prose describes humans, animals and humanoid-proportioned, animal-featured hybrids as the three appearances. The p. 8 grayscale lineup labels its 5-foot-2-inch figure “Hengeyokai Badger Hybrid Female,” with a striped badger face, two hands and layered armor. It shows no separate artist credit or individual signature. The credits name the interior artist roster. Hengeyokai in this book live alone or in small bands near human settlements and unsettled wilderness, and are absent from the featured Rokugan setting. Their names usually follow human conventions in the lands where they live. The book’s named animal-alignment tendencies are descriptive and appear outside the player trait list. ## Edition boundary

The publisher’s 3.0 errata is dated September 24, 2002 and the FAQ April 4, 2003. The available copies are third-party reproductions of publisher-authored documents. The original book and the 3.0 corrections preserve Shapechanger as a type and level adjustment +1. James Wyatt’s April 2004 *Dragon* #318 3.5 update changes the type to Humanoid (Shapechanger) and removes the level adjustment. No exact Hengeyokai species row appeared in the primary TSV at review. The book-catalog string occurrences are cross-species language references. [Wizards 3.0 errata][errata] · [Wizards FAQ][faq] · [Dragon #318 article][dragon]
