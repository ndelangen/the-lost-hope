# Black Flag — Ebony Fly and Giant Fly

Both Kobold Press reference documents print the Giant Fly with the Ebony Fly variant of Figurine of Wondrous Power, under Equipment & Magic Items. Both checked copies match the publisher downloads byte for byte. Printed and one-based viewer page numbers agree. In v0.2, the general figurine rules start on p. 97, and the Ebony Fly and its stat block share p. 98. In v1.0, the general rules and Ebony Fly are on p. 105, and the stat block is at the top of p. 106. The v1.0 title page identifies the version despite the filename containing `v04`. Page 1 of v0.2 specifies the ORC License. Page 1 of v1.0 specifies CC BY 4.0 for BFRD 1.0. Both documents separately credit SRD 5.1 material under CC BY 4.0. [Kobold Press v0.2]() · [Kobold Press v1.0]()

The item is a rare wondrous figurine priced at 60,000 gp. An action to speak its command word and throw it onto the ground within 60 feet activates it if the landing space is unoccupied and large enough. The fly serves as a mount for up to 12 hours. It reverts at the end of that period, on dropping to 0 HP, or when the user spends an action to repeat the command word while touching it. The two-day wait starts at reversion. General figurine rules make the creature friendly to the user and companions, grant it comprehension of the user’s languages for spoken commands, and limit it to defending itself if left without commands.

I checked both stat blocks in rendered page images. They show a Large Beast with AC 11, HP 19 (3d10 + 3), walking speed 30 ft. and flying speed 60 ft., Perception 10, Stealth 11, and darkvision 60 ft. The STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA modifiers are +2/+1/+1/−4/+0/−4. Languages is a dash in the block. Command comprehension comes from the item rules. The blocks give no CR, attack, special action or habitat. The ebony, horsefly-shaped description refers to the figurine. The creature’s color is unspecified.
