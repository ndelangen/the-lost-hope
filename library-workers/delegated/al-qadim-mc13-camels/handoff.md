# Al-Qadim camels

Four AD&D 2e beasts from the Camel leaf in TSR's 1992 *Monstrous Compendium Al-Qadim Appendix*. The leaf is unnumbered and appears on page 11 of the inspected copy. Its figures match the archived edition text. [Camel leaf]() · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

| Creature | Movement | HD | THAC0 | Attacks and damage | Number appearing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desert Camel | 21 | 3 | 17 | One for 1–4 | 1–12 |
| Mountain Camel | 21 | 3 | 17 | One for 1–4 | 1–12 |
| Racing Camel | 24 | 2+2 | 19 | One for 1–4 | 1–6 |
| War Camel | 21 | 3+3 | 16 | Bite for 1–4 and two hooves for 1–6 each | 1–12 |

The profiles follow the dedicated leaf where the appendix summary differs. They keep the spitting and stampede conditions, the War Camel's hoof effect, and the carrying chart in gold-piece weight. The later *Caravans* guide gives the same Desert and War load numbers in pounds. [Page references and discrepancies](independent-source-notes.md) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

The four square paintings use Big Linda's plain parchment as the style reference. The Mountain Camel has two shaggy humps, the Racing Camel has a lighter frame and small feet, and the Desert and War Camels have distinct builds and tack. The baseline library returned none of the four profiles. After applying the patch in a temporary copy, the library loader found all four by name and by appearance or rules text. It preserved the AD&D movement rates, displayed the printed stats and conditional rules, and resolved each edition's portrait to the expected image hash.
