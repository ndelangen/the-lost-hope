# Esper Genesis profiles

Four playable profiles and four original NPCs use the 2018 Core Manual's class, equipment and esper-power rules. I read the October 2019 v1.4 digital text and checked the publisher's errata for Belare containment and Lunar Eldori timing. The entries keep the roster's 2018 publication key. The 2018 first-print folios were outside this source check. [Core Manual text]() · [Publisher's errata]()

| Profile | Manual comparison | Worked example |
|---|---|---|
| Ashenforged | pp. 20–23. Somatic natural AC applies over weaker armor. | Kesvar Maraval, Warrior 2. AC 16 = 12 + 2 Dex + 2 shield. HP 20 = 12 + 8. Strength and proficiency give the longblade +5 to hit. |
| Belare | pp. 24–27. v1.4 and the errata agree on exposure and exhaustion. The GM decides on player availability. | Varenus, Infiltrator Specialist 3. Natural AC 14 = 10 + 2 Dex + 2 Con. HP 24 = 10 + 7 + 7. Constitution-based Light Beam DC 12 = 8 + 2 Con + 2 proficiency. The suit remains sealed by default. |
| Dendus | pp. 28–31. Larima use Mental Missive with a 10-ft. limit. | Issara Verkle, Specialist 2. Armor jacket AC 14 = 11 + 3 Dex. HP 15 = 9 + 6. Dexterity and proficiency give her shortblade and light pistol +5 to hit. |
| Eldori | pp. 32–35. Each species talent has a separate long-rest use. The Lunar roll type is chosen before rolling. | Teralis Danleth, Warrior 1. AC 13 = 11 + 1 Dex + 1 Defense. HP 12 = 10 + 2 Con. Strength and proficiency give the energy staff +4 to hit. The staff's Energy Burst uses its fixed DC 13. |

I read Belare infravision's heat-signature clause as sharing the 60-ft. range established in the same trait. The book calls it the specified range. I used Esper Genesis's Burst Save, armor and weapon rules in all four builds.
