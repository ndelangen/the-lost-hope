The Ushemoi claim includes Arkamoi, Hadrimoi, Lashemoi and Turlemoi in table order. Bladerager Troll uses the Troll parent. The NPC adds one class level to the printed typical specimen in each case.

Printed p. 4 gives each form a numeric LA and ECL. The introduction on p. 6 describes its class-level heading as a way to advance monsters unsuitable for player use, while the glossary on p. 213 describes numeric LA as a rating for player characters or cohorts. The profiles use the printed ECL and the general 3.5 monster-character rules with DM permission. The classed NPCs follow the class advancement in each entry. The linked scan adds one page before the printed folios. [ECL table]() · [Entry headings]() · [Level adjustment glossary]() · [Revised SRD](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monstersAsRaces.htm)

I kept the typical Shaedling's three listed feats. The entry does not designate which is a bonus feat. The NPC encounter sets the shadow wall at 10 ft. high, within 30 ft., for five rounds because the source gives only its maximum length. I used the Sleeping Curse stat block's single-target rule and applied regular javelin damage on impact. [Shaedling, printed p. 148]()

I checked the class and racial arithmetic against the printed entries and the revised SRD. I generated each square portrait with built-in ImageGen and reviewed the full image and its 96 px thumbnail against the approved Big Linda reference.
