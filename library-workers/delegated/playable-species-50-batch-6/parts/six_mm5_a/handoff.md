I checked the printed ECL table on p. 4 and the four entries on pp. 8–11, 48–51 and 54–55. The profiles apply the 3.5 monster-character rules with DM permission. The book presents these entries as class advancement and gives their numeric level adjustments. It does not print dedicated player packages for them. Elemental Magi covers Ken-kuni, Ken-li, and Ken-sun. Only Orrun, the Ken-kuni, has an NPC and portrait in this batch. [Original Monster Manual V]() · [3.5 monster advancement rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/improvingMonsters.htm)

I used the printed monster ability scores as the start for each original NPC. Sil put the increase at 16 total HD into Dexterity and uses the total HD for Stunning Fist. I treated Orrun's Earthshock as a standard supernatural action because the entry specifies its recharge and omits the action cost. Veysk starts in epic play with 22 racial HD and no class levels. Its printed BAB and saving throws carry through, and its two changed feats grant Multiattack and Alertness. [Builds and concepts](npc-builds.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

I made the portraits with built-in ImageGen using Big Linda as the paint and paper reference. All four are 1254 × 1254 PNGs. I inspected each original and the 96 px comparison. Common visual cues are the avenger's silver wings and two swords, the banshrae's eyes and mouthless face, the Ken-kuni's three horns, and the defiler's two chin tendrils.
