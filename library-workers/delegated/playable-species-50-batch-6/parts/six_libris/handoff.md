# Libris Mortis, batch 6

Three playable profiles, three linked NPCs and three original square portraits. The profiles use the book's 3.5 monster-class progressions. Class levels already include the level adjustment and may exceed the actual Hit Dice. The stock monster entries provide a separate reference for core rules the player sections explicitly invoke.

- **Mohrg.** Orven Glassjaw has eight undead Hit Dice at monster-class level 12, for ECL 12. His Dexterity and +5 natural armor give AC 18. BAB +4 and Strength +6 give his slam +10 to hit. Improved Grapple brings the grapple check to +14. Half his eight Hit Dice and +2 Charisma give tongue DC 16. Create spawn comes at level 20. [Table 2–4, pp. 36–37]()
- **Vampire Spawn.** Free-willed Vessa Dain has four undead Hit Dice at monster-class level 8, for ECL 8. Her Dexterity and +3 natural armor give AC 16. BAB +2, Strength +5 and Weapon Focus give slam +8. Improved Grapple brings the grapple check to +11. Half her four Hit Dice and +6 Charisma give DC 18. The class uses caster level 12 for domination and caster level 5 for gaseous form. [Table 2–6, pp. 38–40]()
- **Wight.** Harl Fenmark has four undead Hit Dice at monster-class level 8, for ECL 8. His Dexterity and +4 natural armor give AC 17. BAB +2, Strength +3 and Weapon Focus give slam +6. Half his four Hit Dice and +3 Charisma give energy drain DC 15. A slain humanoid rises after 1d4 rounds. [Table 2–7, p. 40]()

The three NPCs use human base ancestry. The builds apply the human skill point bonus at levels that grant Hit Dice and use the Hit Dice totals for ordinary ability gains. The optional undead hunger rules are off for these encounters. [Monster-class rules, pp. 34–35]() · [Undead hunger, pp. 9–10]()
