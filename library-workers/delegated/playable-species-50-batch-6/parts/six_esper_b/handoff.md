# Esper Genesis player profiles

| Profile | NPC | Original character rules | AC | HP |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Kesh | Neril fel Tivaran | Core Manual pp. 40–42, Star Kesh, Specialist 1 | 14 | 9 |
| Matokai | Rubakai Kothira | Core Manual pp. 45–47, Stonefang, Warrior 2 | 17 | 22 |
| Promethean | Soreva Vantoris | Core Manual pp. 48–49, Fire-Blooded, Specialist 2 | 14 | 15 |
| Valna | Alsha Latchstep | Core Manual pp. 50–52, Hunter 1 | 15 | 11 |

The profiles use the *Esper Genesis Core Manual* species, class, background and equipment rules. Each NPC starts from a 27-point array before adding species modifiers.

- Neril uses light Armor Jacket AC 11 + 3. HP is 8 + 1. Space Science doubles the Pilot background's Astrophysics proficiency, for +3 Intelligence and +4 proficiency. The Core Manual specifies Charisma for Memory Meld and leaves Minor Alteration's channeling ability unstated. This build assigns Charisma to Minor Alteration as a table ruling, for DC 10.
- Rubakai wears Heavy Trooper armor, AC 16, with another +1 from Defense. HP is 10 + 3 at level 1 and 6 + 3 at level 2. The greataxe uses its published statistics. The portrait gives it the look of the power axe from the Matokai chapter's fiction.
- Soreva uses light Armor Jacket AC 11 + 3. HP is 8 + 1 at level 1 and 5 + 1 at level 2. Natural Aptitude doubles Investigation and Mechanics proficiency. The latter normally uses Wisdom in Esper Genesis. Maximum Effort applies to the trained Athletics and Acrobatics checks at +6 and +7.
- Alsha uses Military Jacket AC 13 + 2, the medium armor Dexterity cap. HP is 10 + 1. Catlike Agility grants a 10-ft. move on Disengage. Empathic Sense gives +7 Insight for mood or general attitude. Other Insight checks use +5.
