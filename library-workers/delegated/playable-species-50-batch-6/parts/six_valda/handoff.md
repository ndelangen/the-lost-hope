# Valda's Spire of Secrets

This part contains three playable profiles, their original NPCs and the matching square portraits. The mechanics come from chapter 1 of the v1.4 revision dated 30 December 2022. The publisher still identifies the original book as the 2014-rules version. [Printed chapter](https://anyflip.com/zmngq/pcdc/basic/) · [Mage Hand Press listing]()

| Profile | Printed pages | NPC | Manual check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mandrake | 10–11 | Alder Wick, forest custodian, Champion Fighter 3 | Autumn gives Entangling Vines a second target at level 3. I treated the second target as subject to the same visibility, size and range requirements. Wisdom 17 and proficiency +2 give the enchanted limb +5 to hit and 1d8+3 damage. 28 HP = 12 + 8 + 8. AC 18 = 14 + 1 Dexterity + 2 shield + 1 Defense. |
| Geppettin | 8–9 | Brass Pin, wooden marionette, Fighter 2 | The v1.4 marionette can be Small or Medium. Brass Pin is Small. The rapier has 10-ft. reach on his turn and its ordinary 5-ft. reach on other turns. 20 HP = 12 + 8. AC 17 = 11 + 3 Dexterity + 2 shield + 1 Defense. |
| Spirithost | 13 | Mira Grayglass, courier and locksmith, Rogue 2 | Spirit Speech uses two questions because proficiency is +2. Dexterity +3 and proficiency +2 give a +5 rapier attack. Expertise doubles +2 for Stealth and thieves' tools. 17 HP = 10 + 7. AC 14 = 11 + 3 Dexterity. |

Chapter 1 p. 11 sets the shared language choices. Build calculations use the 2014 Fighter and Rogue, armor, weapons and spell rules. [Classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [Equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment) · [Spells](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/spells)

Built-in ImageGen made the three 1254 × 1254 portraits from the same Big Linda paint-and-paper reference. I compared them full size and on the 96-pixel sheet. Alder's leaves and root limbs, Brass Pin's loose strings and wooden joints, and Mira's pale translucent features remain legible on the plain parchment.
