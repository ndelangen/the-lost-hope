# Mage Hand Press standalone species

Four 2014 fifth-edition player profiles and one original NPC per profile. The records use the exact batch 6 publication and edition keys. Each NPC concept links to its own printed species and build ID.

| Profile | NPC | Manual arithmetic check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mousefolk | Wick Hemm, Mouseling Rogue 2 | AC 14 from leather and Dexterity +3. HP 17 from 8 + 2 and 5 + 2. Attacks +5, finesse weapon damage includes +3 Dexterity, Stealth and Dexterity-based thieves' tools +7 with expertise. |
| Sirine | Nemi Glassfin, Bard 1 | AC 14 from leather and Dexterity +3. HP 9 from 8 + 1. Rapier +5 for 1d8+3. Charisma DC 13 and +5 spell attack. Two bard slots, separate daily charm person and three d6 Bardic Inspiration uses. |
| Fetchling | Orsen Vale, Fighter 1 | AC 14 from leather and Dexterity +3. HP 12 from 10 + 2. Shortsword +5 for 1d6+5 with Dueling. Evil Eye DC 11 with Charisma +1 and proficiency +2. |
| Myconid | Ripplecap, Druid 1 | AC 14 from hide and Dexterity +2. HP 10 from 8 + 2. Staff +2 or +5 with shillelagh. Druid DC 13 from Wisdom. Pacifying Spores have a fixed DC 11. Hallucination Spores have DC 11 because Ripplecap has Intelligence +1 and proficiency +2. |

Scores before species adjustments, in STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA order. Each costs 27 points.

- Wick: 8/15/14/12/10/13. Mouseling adds Dexterity +2 and Charisma +1.
- Nemi: 8/15/13/8/12/15. Sirine adds Dexterity +1 and Charisma +2.
- Orsen: 8/15/14/10/13/12. Fetchling adds Dexterity +2 and Charisma +1.
- Ripplecap: 10/14/13/12/15/8. Myconid adds Constitution +1 and Wisdom +2.

The Mousefolk entry includes both Mouseling and Ratling. Nemi uses the printed Sirine Fey Creature trait as the creature type. Fetchling's Evil Eye has no rest limit in the article. It gives each creature 24-hour immunity after that creature succeeds on a save. The Myconid article describes fungi and refers to other humanoids but assigns no formal creature type. Ripplecap uses Humanoid as the 2014 player default. For Hallucination Spores, Ripplecap uses the one-minute duration of minor illusion and the concentration requirement printed in the article. The older Myconid article supplies no darkvision, so the NPC uses normal vision.

The publisher articles provide the traits directly, with no printed page numbers. The NPC actions use the 2014 core rules. The portraits share plain warm parchment and keep their identifying shapes in the circular crop.
