# Keeper, Rilmani and Yuan-ti Anathema

Three playable profiles and three linked NPCs for batch 6. Use the exact Fiend Folio (2003) and 3.0 edition keys. Anathema sits under the canonical Yuan-ti entry. The Rilmani profile covers all three printed forms, with the NPC and portrait using Ferrumach.

| Profile | Worked NPC | Printed player rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keeper | Orris Fold, rogue 1 | ECL 8. Four racial Hit Dice, one class level and LA +3. Pages 111–112. |
| Rilmani | Tarsen Iron Accord, Ferrumach fighter 1 | ECL 14. Eight racial Hit Dice, one class level and LA +5. The Cuprilach and Aurumach offsets are +17 and +25. Pages 140–143. |
| Yuan-ti Anathema | Sevath Coil-of-Ash, cleric 1 | Epic ECL 29. Twenty-two racial Hit Dice, one class level and LA +6. Pages 193–195. |

The Keeper entry calls the species blind and describes sonar as its sense. Orris uses that reading and loses blindsight in silence. Tarsen uses the printed fear aura and armor. His mount is a separate option. Sevath adds the normal cleric 1 benefits to the racial block and uses the Destruction and Evil domains. His silver holy token is a mundane divine focus. The spell-like ability list retains the April printing's transitional spell names.
