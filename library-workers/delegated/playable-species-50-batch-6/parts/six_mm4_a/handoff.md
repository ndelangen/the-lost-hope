The five builds use the 2006 book and Revised v3.5 class, equipment, and monster advancement rules. I checked the folios, Hit Dice, ECL, attacks, AC, saves, skills and racial ability DCs by hand. The CR values are encounter estimates. [Monster Manual IV]() · [Revised v3.5 SRD]()

- The book assigns Briarvex +4 LA and says briarvexes are generally unsuitable as player characters. Saltburr is a classed NPC. [Printed p. 27]()
- Windblade covers Windrazor and Windscythe. The Windscythe character package gives +5 LA and the ECL table gives +3. The profile records both and uses the dedicated character package. Tallowwing uses the Windrazor rules. Its Fearsome Critical imposes shaken for one round as a table ruling because the book supplies no duration. [Printed p. 179]() · [ECL table]()
- Rusk uses the adult Varag stat block's 60-ft. speed. The optional character progression gives 40 ft. with no listed increase. [Adult stat block]() · [Character progression]()
- Corruptor of Fate is a Yugoloth in the source. The claim is an independent catalog profile. [Corruptor of Fate]()
