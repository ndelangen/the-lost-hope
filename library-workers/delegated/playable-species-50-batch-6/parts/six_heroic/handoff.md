# Android, Gillfolk and Tengu

Three playable profiles, three linked NPCs and three square portraits are ready for batch 6.

## Rules evidence

- Android. I checked both subraces against the publisher preview. page 2 matches printed page 2. Eda uses the alchemical option. [Original traits, p. 2]()
- Gillfolk. The original preview covers printed pp. 1–3 and identifies Gillfolk on p. 1. The trait folios fall beyond the preview. The profile and Mara's Deep Sea build use the attributed open-content reprint. Its trait section gives Medium size, despite an unrelated sentence in the introduction. At the table, apply Water Born disadvantage to eligible action rolls and read advantage against cold as advantage on applicable saving throws. [Publisher preview]() · [Attributed traits](https://5esrd.old.opengamingnetwork.com/database/race/gillfolk/)
- Tengu. The original preview names Tengu on printed p. 1 and ends at p. 6, before the trait folios. The profile uses the attributed open-content reprint. The licensed conversion listing also confirms both subraces. Pell uses Cosmopolitan. Gifted Linguist gives artisan's-tool proficiency for one check per day, as the reprint specifies. [Publisher preview]() · [Attributed traits](https://5esrd.old.opengamingnetwork.com/database/race/tengus/) · [Licensed conversion listing](https://www.fantasygrounds.com/store/product.php?id=JBE04035E)

## Manual checks

- Eda Venn. Wizard 1 with proficiency +2 and Intelligence +3, giving DC 13 and +5 to spell attacks. Constitution +1 puts her at 7 HP. Her AC is 12 or 15 with mage armor, and her longbow attack is +4 for 1d8+2.
- Mara Shoal. Fighter 2 with 20 HP from 10 + 6 + twice Constitution +2. Leather armor, Dexterity +1, shield +2 and Defense +1 give AC 15. Strength +3 and proficiency +2 give a +5 trident attack for 1d6+3. Direct sunlight lowers applicable passive Perception from 13 to 8.
- Pell Inkclaw. Rogue 2 with 15 HP from 8 + 5 + twice Constitution +1. Leather armor and Dexterity +3 give AC 14. Expertise gives Stealth +7 and Investigation +6. His rapier attack is +5 for 1d8+3, with 1d6 Sneak Attack when eligible.
