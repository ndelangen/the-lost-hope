# Mage Hand Press playable profiles

Five player profiles, five linked level-one NPCs and five original square portraits are ready for integration. I checked each PNG at full size and at 96 pixels against the approved Big Linda portrait. Built-in ImageGen produced all five originals. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [96 px comparison](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [Prompts](prompts.md)

## 2024 rules

- **Nautilid.** Brine Sill's AC 17 comes from the Aqua Suit, Dexterity +2, and a shield. Fighter HP is 11, and Alert makes initiative +4. The spear hits at +5. The article gives a walking speed of 30 ft. and underwater breathing. [Nautilid rules](https://magehandpress.com/2025/09/nautilid-5e2024/)
- **Vect.** Mend-Three uses the published Construct type and base Embedded Armor. AC 15 is 13 + Dexterity +2. Fighter HP is 12, the greatsword hits at +5, and Graze deals 3 on a miss. Appendix H defines what Thermalsight can see and what blocks it. No ability-score swap is active. [Vect rules](https://magehandpress.com/2025/09/vect-5e2024/) · [Dark Matter revised]()
- **Amoeboid.** Pilcrow is a Small Ooze. Rogue HP is 10, leather gives AC 14, Alert gives initiative +5, and the shortbow hits at +5. Reform can heal at most 1d4 and cannot exceed the damage taken. The lore mentions mimicking a copied creature's language, while the trait omits the benefit. The article also leaves the timing for exiting Flexible Form open. The build avoids relying on either rule. [Amoeboid rules](https://magehandpress.com/2025/09/amoeboid-5e2024/)

I cross-checked proficiency, starting HP, abilities, backgrounds, equipment and the differing rest recovery rules. Brine's Guard background is in chapter 4 of the 2024 Player's Handbook. Savage Attacker and Great Weapon Fighting affect Mend-Three's hit damage. Graze deals the ability modifier without rolling damage dice. ## 2014 rules

- **Skathári.** Ruvash Ledgewalker uses the original climbing speed, hooked claws and carapace. AC 17 includes a shield. Fighter HP is 12. Strength +3 and proficiency +2 give claw attacks at +5 for 1d6 + 3 slashing. Falling glide and climbing use the separate racial rules. Their background is Outlander from chapter 4 of the 2014 Player's Handbook. [Skathári rules](https://magehandpress.com/2024/03/skathari/)
- **Avia-Ra.** Senet Dawnreed has AC 18 from chain mail and shield, and 10 cleric HP. Wisdom +3 and proficiency +2 give save DC 13 and spell attack +5. Life Domain increases level-one spell healing by 3, making cure wounds 1d8 + 6 and healing word 1d4 + 6. Senet's first-level bless comes from Life Domain. The species grants its own casting at level three. [Avia-Ra rules](https://magehandpress.com/2024/02/avia-ra/)

I checked the 2014 fighter and cleric features, spells, armor and weapon values against the corresponding core rules.
