# Monster Manual IV portraits and builds

Four 3.5 player profiles and original NPCs are staged. The printed level adjustments support monster-character builds with DM permission. The book lists each creature’s racial Hit Dice, and the build cards show level adjustment and total ECL separately.

| Species | Original NPC | Racial HD | Class levels | LA | ECL | Printed pages |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Joystealer | Iri Quietglass | 6 | 0 | +4 | 10 | [78–79]() |
| Verdant Prince | Tern Boughsure | 16 | 0 | +4 | 20 | [172–173]() |
| Wizened Elder | Burrel Waxbough | 4 | 1 fighter | +3 | 8 | [180–181]() |
| Zern | Vesk Thrum | 8 | 1 fighter | +7 | 16 | [195–196]() |

Iri uses Iron Will in place of the example’s Track feat. Tern swaps Combat Casting for Skill Focus and carries a prepared masterwork staff. Burrel and Vesk each take one fighter level. The source notes record the changes to attacks, saves and hit points. Iri has no Strength score under the incorporeal rules.

I reviewed the four built-in ImageGen originals at full size and at 96 px against Big Linda. Iri’s lower silhouette fades to show incorporeality. Vesk’s spear comes close to the top of the square and remains fully in frame. All four are original 1,254 × 1,254 PNGs.
