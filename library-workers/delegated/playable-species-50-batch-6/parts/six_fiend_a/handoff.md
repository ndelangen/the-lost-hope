# Fiend Folio player profiles

Avatars show Vask Undercoal in Haraknin hound form, Nemm Flinttoe the Dark Creeper, Silek-of-Nine with its red ethergaunt mask closed, and Ruun Cliffseer the Fensir. Each original square ImageGen portrait uses the same warm parchment as Big Linda. I inspected the full images and the 96-pixel versions. The profiles cover all three Canomorph lineages, all three Ethergaunt castes, and both Fensir forms. Dark Creeper is the small humanoid under Dark Ones. [April 2003 Fiend Folio scan]() · [Print and rules citations](source-evidence.json)

- The Vultivor example uses racial d10s and a longer Shadow Jump allowance than the dedicated vorr entry on p. 186. The Canomorph profile gives both source values with their context. Vask uses Ranger 1 to keep his lawful alignment and uses the original 1d6 scimitar damage. His at-will command uses CL 5 and Will DC 12 for the encounter because the printed entry leaves both open.
- Nemm uses the 3.0 rule that replaces a single monster HD with the first class HD. That produces the Fiend Folio's printed ECL 5 for Rogue 1. The encounter supplies a standard action and ten-round duration for Shadow Cloak. [Savage Species p. 13]()
- Silek prepares Silent Spell versions in raised slots to account for the printed inability to speak. Only Wizard 1 advances class saves and skills, while racial casting stacks to CL 10. Ruun uses the same distinction at CL 6. [Original Wizard spells-per-day table](https://a.osmarks.net/content/dandwiki_en_all_maxi_2021-03/A/3e_SRD:Wizard) · [Original Silent Spell rules](https://a.osmarks.net/content/dandwiki_en_all_maxi_2021-03/A/3e_SRD:Silent_Spell)

I manually checked the class and racial dice, AC, attack bonuses, saves, spell slots, caster levels, and printed ECL values. The red mask, hooves, dark wraps, and Fensir hat are visible at 96 pixels.
