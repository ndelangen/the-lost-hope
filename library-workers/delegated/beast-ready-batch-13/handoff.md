# Beast readiness batch 13

This batch stages 50 distinct Beast publication versions against archive `3300aad`. It includes 18 from *Rime of the Frostmaiden*, eight from *Monsters of the Multiverse*, eight from *Volo's Guide to Monsters*, ten from *Tales from the Yawning Portal*, and six from *Out of the Abyss*. The parts cite first-printing folios and applicable errata. For variants that borrow a block, they identify the base rules and the changes printed in the adventure. [Frostmaiden](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/idrotf) · [Multiverse](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/motm) · [Volo](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/vgtm) · [Yawning Portal](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/tftyp) · [Out of the Abyss](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/oota)

- [Beast-only roster](roster.tsv) and [manifest](manifest.json) record source evidence, exact version scopes, and portrait hashes. An independent review checked all 50 scopes against the baseline and earlier bundles.
- [Rules patch](rules.patch) adds 50 source-verified overlays. The catalog TSV retains its original bytes, including blank creature URLs where the catalog supplies only adventure context.
- [Preview portrait index](portrait-index-preview.json) maps 50 versions to 43 distinct files. The batch includes 26 original built-in ImageGen PNGs and 17 reused sources. Art prompts are in the per-part handoffs, portrait manifests, or source notes. [Big Linda style comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Contact sheet 1](portraits-1.jpg) · [Contact sheet 2](portraits-2.jpg) · [Contact sheet 3](portraits-3.jpg)
- Kingsport's new portrait follows the prose description of an eyeless white penguin in a leather harness. The publisher's tendrilled illustration remains available in the gallery. The reused Giant Lightning Eel has warmer parchment, and the Fiendish Giant Spider reuses the parent Giant Wolf Spider's body without the baleful energy described in the adventure.
- The two Rot Grub Swarm versions share a portrait and keep their distinct infestation rules. The Young Winter Wolf uses the adventure's Dire Wolf base. The first-print Steeders remain Beasts. Giant Rocktopus retains the inherited Water Breathing text and labels air breathing as a table ruling because its printed land habitat conflicts with that rule.

In the disposable copy, manual searches found Kingsport, Swarm of Rot Grubs, Giant Rocktopus, and Amphisbaena. Both Rot Grub versions loaded the same portrait and displayed their own rules. The Rocktopus table ruling and Amphisbaena's one-Constrict limit appeared in their source versions. All sampled portraits loaded at 1254 × 1254, and the browser console showed no warnings or errors.
