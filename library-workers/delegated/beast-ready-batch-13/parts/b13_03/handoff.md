# Six Icewind Dale beasts

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Three portrait originals](portrait-files.json)

I checked the August 2020 first printing of *Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden*. The D&D Beyond creature URLs lead to the purchase page, so the printed folios are the rules witness. I found no changes to these six entries in the publisher’s errata index or release notes. [Adventure listing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/idrotf) · [Errata index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae) · [Release notes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/release-issues-support/81555-icewind-dale-rime-of-the-frostmaiden-issues-and)

| Entry | Printed folio | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knucklehead Trout | [295](https://online.anyflip.com/jjlbf/giys/mobile/index.html#p=296) | Separate Bite and Tail actions. Advantage in the fishing contest comes from the lake procedure on p. 11. |
| Mountain Goat | [304](https://online.anyflip.com/jjlbf/giys/mobile/index.html#p=305) | Climb 30 ft., Charge with a DC 12 Strength save against being knocked prone, and Sure-Footed on two save types. |
| Reindeer | [307](https://online.anyflip.com/jjlbf/giys/mobile/index.html#p=308) | Uses the 2014 Monster Manual Elk, p. 322. Adds cold acclimation and a roughly one-in-six chance of antlers that shed dim light within 10 ft. |
| Seal | [308](https://online.anyflip.com/jjlbf/giys/mobile/index.html#p=309) | Breath lasts 15 minutes and Bite deals a fixed 1 piercing damage. The prose identifies local seals as acclimated to cold weather. |
| Sperm Whale | [309](https://online.anyflip.com/jjlbf/giys/mobile/index.html#p=310) | Bite can swallow Large or smaller creatures. Swallow, regurgitation, and escape use distinct timing and movement rules. Tail deals its higher damage to objects. |
| Walrus | [312](https://online.anyflip.com/jjlbf/giys/mobile/index.html#p=313) | Breath lasts 10 minutes. Tusks deal 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage. |

The three square originals came from separate built-in ImageGen calls using Big Linda as the style reference. Each prompt called for one full animal centered on muted matte tan parchment, with the body and extremities clear in a circular crop. The subject briefs called for the trout’s bulbous brick-red forehead and silver-gray belly, the mountain goat’s ivory winter coat and slender black horns, and the sperm whale’s blunt rectangular forehead, narrow lower jaw, and broad tail flukes.

Reindeer and Seal use matching earlier parchment portraits. Walrus shares the Batch 13 Giant Walrus portrait for its species anatomy.
