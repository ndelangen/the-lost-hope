# Out of the Abyss beasts

I checked the September 2015 first printing against page images and confirmed the printing on the credits page. Wizards of the Coast's 2020 errata lists no change to these three entries. The public D&D Beyond chapter and creature links redirected to the purchase page. [Credits page](https://online.anyflip.com/cmjgl/axwd/files/large/22ad38e3c1272b86438dba40f8d9e2d3.webp) · [Official errata]()

- **Giant Riding Lizard, p. 131.** The dedicated chapter 8 card gives 19 HP, darkvision 30 ft., Spider Climb, and one Bite. It supplies its own rules. [Printed card](https://online.anyflip.com/cmjgl/axwd/files/large/e6d61fc908cb29d111ad98eaac4acb0b.webp)
- **Giant Rocktopus, p. 28.** Its base is Giant Octopus from the 2014 Monster Manual, p. 326. The adventure sets walking speed to 20 ft. and climbing speed to 10 ft., removes Hold Breath, and replaces Underwater Camouflage with advantage on all Dexterity (Stealth) checks. The adventure leaves swim speed, Water Breathing, Tentacles, and underwater-only Ink Cloud unspecified, so the rules summary identifies them as inherited. Water Breathing conflicts with the land encounter. The separate table ruling lets the rocktopus breathe air. [Adventure variant](https://online.anyflip.com/cmjgl/axwd/files/large/e67a27943fa66d8a332ff3a565fccc4e.webp) · [Base rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16888-giant-octopus)
- **Male Steeder, p. 231.** The original card lists a Medium Beast with a 60-ft. Leap and Sticky Leg for Small or Tiny prey. The legacy digital index displays Monstrosity. The supplied version follows the first printing. The Bite's printed fixed damage is 7, while 1d8 + 2 averages 6.5. The card preserves both published values. [Printed card](https://online.anyflip.com/cmjgl/axwd/files/large/f41c0aec9f7704f10f336edb84c02ba0.webp)

I checked the four leg pairs and the clean parchment on the new 1254 × 1254 Male Steeder portrait, then compared the silhouette at 96 pixels with Big Linda and the Female Steeder. The reused lizard and octopus portraits match their roster checksums. The lizard is unsaddled. The rusty octopus has a mottled, knobbly mantle. The adventure specifies color-changing camouflage and leaves its resting color and anatomy to the artist.
