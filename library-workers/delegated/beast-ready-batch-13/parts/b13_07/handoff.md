# Batch 13 · Ox, Rothé, Stench Kow, and Swarm of Rot Grubs

The November 2016 first printing gives the Cow block on p. 207 and the three cattle variants on p. 208. The Ox's Huge treatment applies only to carrying capacity. The ordinary Rothé has 30-ft. Darkvision. Stench Kow has 60-ft. Darkvision, three damage resistances, and its own Stench trait. [Publisher index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/vgtm) · [Official errata]()

The original Swarm of Rot Grubs has no follow-up save on a Bites hit. It infests the target with 1d4 grubs, uses per-grub damage at the start of the target's turn, and can kill an infested creature that ends its turn at zero HP. The single-grub contact rule is identified in the evidence.

The Rothé and Stench Kow portraits were generated with built-in ImageGen using Big Linda's muted tan parchment. Both square originals are in `portraits/`. The manifest records the prompts, dimensions, and checksums.
