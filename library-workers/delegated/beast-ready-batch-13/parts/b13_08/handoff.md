# Batch 13, part 08

Verified five Beast profiles against the first printing of Tales from the Yawning Portal, April 2017. The official 2021 errata contains no changes to these profiles. [Adventure](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/tftyp) · [Official errata]()

- Amphisbaena, p. 84. The encounter adapts Giant Constrictor Snake on Monster Manual p. 324. The snake can make two melee attacks each turn, with at most one Constrict. The adventure sets CR 3. [Giant Constrictor Snake](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16878-giant-constrictor-snake)
- Elder Giant Lizard, p. 176. The encounter adapts Giant Crocodile on Monster Manual p. 324. The adult lizards have a 30-foot climb speed, no swim speed, and darkvision to 60 feet. [Giant Crocodile](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16880-giant-crocodile)
- Giant Crayfish, p. 235, and Giant Lightning Eel and Giant Subterranean Lizard, p. 236. All have printed stat blocks. The Tamoachan crayfish guard speaks Olman as an encounter exception. The eel's Lightning Jolt stuns only on a failed save when the target takes lightning damage. The subterranean lizard can replace one attack with Swallow.

The original Amphisbaena and Giant Crayfish portraits use the built-in ImageGen tool and Big Linda's matte parchment as the style reference. The exact prompts are in the source evidence. The snake has one head at each end of its body. The crayfish has two claws and a tail fan. Both are 1254 pixels square.

I checked all five subjects in square and circular crops and verified the reuse hashes. Giant Subterranean Lizard uses the pale portrait to match the Forge of Fury specimen. Giant Lightning Eel uses the existing warmer parchment art with faint blue arcs.
