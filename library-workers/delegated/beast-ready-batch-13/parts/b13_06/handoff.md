The local facsimile credits identify the November 2016 first printing. The publisher's v3.0 errata lists no changes to these four entries. The encounter rules come from the printed book. [Book index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/vgtm) · [Official errata]()

- The aurochs's Charge can knock a creature prone on a failed DC 15 Strength save. The cow's Charge adds piercing damage.
- Deep Rothé uses the Cow block on p. 207 with the Rothé changes on p. 208. It is Medium with 13 HP, 60-ft. darkvision and at-will Dancing Lights using Charisma and no components. Appendix C, p. 224, lists it at CR 1/4 under Underdark Creatures. [Dancing Lights, 2014 rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2057-dancing-lights)
- The dolphin has 60-ft. blindsight and can hold its breath for 20 minutes. Its Charge requires 30 ft. of straight movement and adds 3 (1d6) bludgeoning on a Slam hit.
- The new Cow portrait uses Big Linda as the style reference. The horns, hooves and tail fit inside the centered round crop. [Cow portrait]() · [Round crop]()
- The reused Aurochs and Dolphin portraits match the roster's SHA-256 hashes. Both are 1,254 by 1,254 pixels and use muted plain parchment. [Pair of reused portraits]()
- Deep Rothé shares the square portrait from part b13_04. The animal has a compact build, shaggy dark coat and the curved horns of a musk ox, on the same plain parchment. [Deep Rothé portrait]()
