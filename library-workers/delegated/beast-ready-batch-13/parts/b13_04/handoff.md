# Monsters of the Multiverse beasts

Four records checked against the original first printing. The D&D Beyond index identifies the publication and creature entries. Its detail links require access to the book, so the rules use the printed folios. No official correction to these entries was located. [Publisher index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/motm), [Sage Advice and Errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae)

| Beast | Folio | CR | AC | HP | Encounter rules |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Aurochs | 71 | 2 | 11 | 38 | Gore adds 2d8 piercing after an immediate 20-ft. straight approach. A creature hit after the approach saves against falling prone. |
| Deep Rothé | 71 | 1/4 | 10 | 13 | Gore adds 2d6 piercing after the 20-ft. approach. Can cast *dancing lights* without components. |
| Dolphin | 97 | 1/8 | 12 | 11 | Slam adds 1d6 bludgeoning after an immediate 30-ft. straight approach. |
| Ox | 72 | 1/4 | 10 | 15 | Gore adds 2d6 piercing after the 20-ft. approach. Beast of Burden affects carrying capacity. |

Aurochs and Dolphin reuse their verified natural animal portraits. Deep Rothé and Ox have new square ImageGen portraits, using Big Linda as the parchment and rendering reference. The Deep Rothé prompt specified a short, shaggy bovine with thick curled horns. The Ox prompt specified a broad dun working bovine with long pale horns. The selected Ox framing gives both horn tips room in the circular crop. The two new PNGs also cover the matching Volo’s records.
