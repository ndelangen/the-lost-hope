# Batch 13 Beast rules

Verified Guthash on p. 21, Huge Giant Crab on p. 103, Young Winter Wolf on p. 181, Snow Leopard on p. 183 and Huge Polar Bear on p. 187 of the April 2017 first printing of *Tales from the Yawning Portal*. The adventure sends the pups to the 2014 Dire Wolf stat block and the leopards to the 2014 Tiger stat block. The 2021 adventure errata includes no correction to these encounters. [First-printing facsimile](), [Wizards adventure errata]()

The adventure gives Guthash a +5 Bite attack. Its attack damage follows the corrected Diseased Giant Rat base rules, 4 (1d4 + 2). Guthash and Huge Polar Bear have fixed HP in the adventure. Huge Giant Crab keeps the base Giant Crab's Stealth +4 alongside the encounter's listed changes. [Wizards Monster Manual errata]()

Inspected all five portraits. The three reused files match the roster hashes. Snow Leopard and Young Winter Wolf are new square ImageGen portraits on muted tan parchment. The wolf's white fur and pale blue eyes follow the 2014 Winter Wolf description. The prompts and PNG checksums are in the portrait manifest. [2014 Winter Wolf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17061-winter-wolf)
