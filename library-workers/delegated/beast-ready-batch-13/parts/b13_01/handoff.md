# Beast-ready batch 13, part b13_01

I verified the six rules records in the August 2020 first printing of *Icewind Dale: Rime of the Frostmaiden*. Awakened White Moose has its own block on p. 82. Cave Bear combines the encounter rules on pp. 75–76 with the 2014 *Monster Manual* Polar Bear on p. 334 and has 60-ft. darkvision. The four Chimeric Creations use the table on p. 246 plus the book's Fox and Hare or the 2014 *Monster Manual* Baboon and Cat. The printed Hare block awards 0 XP at CR 0. [Adventure](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/idrotf) · [Official errata index](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae)

The five new square portraits use matte tan parchment. The chimeric portraits depict the baboon's bite, the cat's antennae, the arctic fox's color-shifting coat, and the snowshoe hare's feathered wings. I verified the reused Cave Bear's hash and reviewed all six portraits in a circular crop.
