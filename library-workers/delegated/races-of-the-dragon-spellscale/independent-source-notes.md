# Spellscale source and review notes

The January 2006 first printing of *Races of the Dragon*, ISBN 0-7869-3913-3, prints low-light vision in the playable traits on p. 23. Dragonblood on p. 4 governs dragon-targeted effects while the creature keeps the Humanoid type. The spellscale conversion rite is on pp. 31–32. [Publisher sample]() · [Original printing, p. 23]()

The Sorcerer and Mystic Theurge examples on pp. 135–136 print touch ACs higher than their listed defenses would ordinarily give. The Bard sample prints a claw with no listed source. Isette’s card uses ordinary touch AC and specifies when the force armor helps against incorporeal touch. Neither staged build has a claw attack. [Published examples]()

Dan Frazier illustrated the wardrobe scene on p. 22, and Ed Cox illustrated the Spellscale spot portraits. Isette’s shades of green come from the scope of the p. 23 description. Her clothing and pose are original. [Wizards art gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20161101032141/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/ag/20060105a)
