# Spellscale for D&D 3.5

The first printing of *Races of the Dragon* gives Spellscales +2 Charisma, −2 Constitution, low-light vision, and the Dragonblood subtype. Blood Quickening grants one deity’s benefits after an hour of meditation. The choice lasts 24 hours and can grant a skill bonus, limited feat use, or another benefit. The species profile covers all eleven choices. [Original traits, p. 23](https://fliphtml5.com/brixe/hdka/Races_Of_The_Dragon/23/) · [Meditations, p. 25](https://fliphtml5.com/brixe/hdka/Races_Of_The_Dragon/25/) · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Isette Mossglass**, Sorcerer 1, provisional CR 1. She can use Bahamut’s meditation three times to add 2d6 damage to a selected evil creature targeted by or in the area of her spell.
- **Meren Thistlekey**, Bard 2, provisional CR 2. Hlal raises his storytelling check by 1 and lets him heighten *daze* using his only 1st-level slot for the day.

The portrait shows Isette in muted green scales and a plum doublet on plain tan parchment. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Parchment comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [96 px preview](portrait-thumbnails.jpg)

In the temporary copy, the species appeared in 3.5 search by name, traits, and both NPC roles. Both encounter commands returned the full builds. The new species profile supplies its default portrait and browse thumbnail. The existing Spellscale Vassal monster profile and artwork matched the baseline.
