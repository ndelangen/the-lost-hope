# Spellscale encounters

Each NPC has completed one hour of Blood Quickening. Their chosen benefits last 24 hours. [Blood Quickening, p. 25](https://fliphtml5.com/brixe/hdka/Races_Of_The_Dragon/25/)

## Isette Mossglass, sluice rescue Sorcerer

Neutral good Sorcerer 1, provisional CR 1 and ECL 1. HP 5, AC 12 (touch 12, flat-footed 10), speed 30 ft., initiative +6. Fort +1, Ref +2, Will +2. Concentration, Knowledge (arcana), and Spellcraft +5. Low-light vision.

- **Magic missile** deals 1d4+1 force damage to one creature within 110 ft. Spell resistance applies. If she spends one of her three Bahamut uses on an evil target, add 2d6 damage.
- **Mage armor** lasts one hour. On Isette it raises AC to 16 and flat-footed AC to 14. Ordinary touch AC stays 12. The armor also applies against incorporeal touch.
- **Cantrips** are *detect magic*, *light*, *mage hand*, and *read magic*. Five cantrip slots and four 1st-level slots per day at caster level 1. DC 13 for cantrips and DC 14 for 1st-level spells that allow saves.
- Quarterstaff +0 melee, 1d6 bludgeoning, ×2. Light crossbow +2, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. range increment. Reload as a move action.

Isette is looking for two workers who failed to return from a sluice inspection. She casts *mage armor* on the dry side, asks the party to hold the rope, and takes the first steps down. [Sorcerer rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/sorcererWizard.htm#sorcerer)

## Meren Thistlekey, waystation storyteller

Chaotic good Bard 2, provisional CR 2 and ECL 2. HP 12, AC 15 (touch 12, flat-footed 13), speed 30 ft., initiative +2. Fort +1, Ref +5, Will +4. Perform (storytelling) +12 with Hlal or +11 without it. Diplomacy +12, Listen +6. Low-light vision.

- **Daze** takes a standard action. One Humanoid of up to 4 HD within 30 ft. loses its actions for one round on a failed DC 13 Will save. Spell resistance applies. Spending his sole 1st-level slot to heighten it raises the DC to 14 and takes a full-round action.
- **Cure light wounds** heals a willing living creature for 1d8+2 HP by touch. **Expeditious retreat** raises Meren’s land speed to 60 ft. for two minutes. Each uses his 1st-level slot, the same slot he could use to heighten *daze*. His other cantrips are *detect magic*, *light*, *mage hand*, and *prestidigitation*. He has three cantrip slots.
- **Bardic music** twice per day. Inspire Courage grants +1 morale on attacks, weapon damage, and saves against charm and fear while heard and for five rounds afterward. Countersong helps listeners within 30 ft. save against sonic or language-dependent magical attacks. Fascinate can hold one attentive creature within 90 ft. for up to two rounds outside combat distractions. Use his Perform result as the Will DC.
- Rapier +1 melee, 1d6 piercing, 18–20/×2. Shortbow +3, 1d6 piercing, ×3, 60-ft. range increment.

Meren tells a story while he counts which families are still waiting for their wagons. He passes the passenger roll to the party and keeps his 1st-level slot available in case someone comes back injured.
