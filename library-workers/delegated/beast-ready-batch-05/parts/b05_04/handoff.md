# Giant Wasp, Giant Weasel, and Giant Wolf Spider

I compared the September 2014 first-print *Monster Manual* page images with the November 2018 *Basic Rules* . All three pairs match. The wolf spider's 2018 block continues at the top of the right column. The official *Monster Manual* errata list no changes to these entries. [MM p. 329](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/index.html#p=330) · [MM p. 330](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/index.html#p=331) · [2018 p. 137]() · [Publisher errata]()

| Beast | Printed basics | Main attack |
|---|---|---|
| Giant Wasp | AC 12, HP 13 (3d8), CR 1/2, 100 XP. | Sting +4, 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing, then DC 11 Constitution save for 10 (3d6) poison or half. |
| Giant Weasel | AC 13, HP 9 (2d8), CR 1/8, 25 XP. | Bite +5 for 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing. Keen Hearing and Smell. |
| Giant Wolf Spider | AC 13, HP 11 (2d8 + 2), CR 1/4, 50 XP. | Bite +3, 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing, then DC 11 Constitution save for 7 (2d6) poison or half. Spider Climb, Web Sense, and Web Walker. |

If poison damage drops a target to 0 HP, both the wasp and spider leave it stable, poisoned for an hour even after it regains HP, and paralyzed while that poisoning lasts. The stat blocks include the condition effects and separate the piercing hit from the poison save. [Staged stat blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

The existing Wasp WebP matches the roster's SHA-256. The Giant Weasel and Giant Wolf Spider use new square portraits on plain warm parchment. The wolf spider has a low, stocky body and two prominent forward-facing eyes. I checked both new 1254 px originals and the 96 px square and circular previews. The wolf spider has eight visible walking legs. The same portrait is used for each creature's two printings. [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)
