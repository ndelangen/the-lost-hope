# Hunter Shark, Killer Whale and Plesiosaurus

Six encounter blocks. I compared the September 2014 *Monster Manual* pages with the November 2018 *Basic Rules* . The two sources agree on the mechanics of these three beasts. The *Monster Manual* errata contains no entry for them. [2018 Basic Rules ]() · [Monster Manual errata]()

| Creature | Basic Rules | Monster Manual | AC | HP | CR | XP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hunter Shark | 140 | 330 | 12 | 45 | 2 | 450 |
| Killer Whale | 142 | 331 | 12 | 90 | 3 | 700 |
| Plesiosaurus | 149 | 80 | 13 | 68 | 2 | 450 |

- Hunter Shark's Blood Frenzy applies to melee attacks against creatures below full HP. Its 5-ft. Bite deals 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage. [Printed rules]()
- Killer Whale has 120-ft. blindsight that depends on Echolocation. It can hold its breath for 30 minutes. [Printed rules]()
- Plesiosaurus can hold its breath for an hour. Its 10-ft. Bite deals 14 (3d6 + 4) piercing damage. [Printed rules]()

The two Hunter Shark records share a tiger-barred portrait, and both Killer Whale records share an orca. Plesiosaurus uses the selected existing artwork, whose hash matches the roster.
