# Triceratops and Tyrannosaurus Rex

Four existing Beast entries have encounter rules, with a separate overlay for each publication. Both dinosaurs use *Monster Manual* p. 80. The *Basic Rules* place Triceratops on p. 156 and Tyrannosaurus Rex on p. 157. I checked the actual folios, including each dinosaur's description at the top of the second column in the *Basic Rules*. The two printings agree on the encounter mechanics. The official errata lists no change to either creature. [Monster Manual printed p. 80](https://fliphtml5.com/vmzaj/Monster-Manual_AddPage/82/) · [Monster Manual errata v2.0]()

| Beast | Basic Rules page | Printed values checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Triceratops | [156]() | AC 13, HP 95 (10d12 + 30), CR 5, 1,800 XP. Gore +9 for 24 (4d8 + 6). Stomp +9 for 22 (3d10 + 6). |
| Tyrannosaurus Rex | [157]() | AC 13, HP 136 (13d12 + 52), CR 8, 3,900 XP. Bite +10 for 33 (4d12 + 7). Tail +10 for 20 (3d8 + 7). |

Trampling Charge keeps the printed DC 13 Strength save. Stomp targets a prone creature and becomes a bonus action against a prone target after a qualifying charge. The tyrannosaurus must split its Multiattack between two targets. A Bite hit grapples and restrains a Medium or smaller creature. Escape takes an action and a DC 17 Athletics or Acrobatics check. [Monster grapple rules]()

I matched both indexed 1254 × 1254 portraits to the roster hashes and checked the square and 96-pixel circular views. The long horns and tails sit close to the circular crop. The selected files are reused as supplied, so the new portrait manifest is empty. Habitat tags are editorial.
