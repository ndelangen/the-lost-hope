# Reef Shark, Scorpion, and Sea Horse

Six stat blocks for the November 2018 *Basic Rules* v1.0 and the 2014 *Monster Manual*. Each pair shares one original parchment illustration. I read the four folios and checked the official errata. The encounter statistics match for each pair. [2018 Basic Rules]() · [Monster Manual errata]()

| Beast | Basic Rules page | Monster Manual folio | AC | HP | CR and XP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Reef Shark | 151 | 336 | 12 | 22 (4d8 + 4) | 1/2, 100 XP |
| Scorpion | 152 | 337 | 11 | 1 (1d4 − 1) | 0, 10 XP |
| Sea Horse | 152 | 337 | 11 | 1 (1d4 − 1) | 0, 0 XP |

- The Reef Shark's Pack Tactics checks whether its ally is within 5 ft. of the target. Both books place adult reef sharks at 6 to 10 ft. long. I used a slender blacktip reef shark for the portrait to distinguish it from the heavier Hunter Shark and Giant Shark illustrations. [Basic Rules p. 151]() · [Monster Manual p. 336](https://online.anyflip.com/sqwmo/hxog/mobile/index.html#p=337)
- The Scorpion's Sting deals 1 piercing damage and calls for a DC 9 Constitution save against 1d8 poison damage. The save halves poison damage and the attack applies no poisoned condition. The Sea Horse explicitly gives 0 XP and lists no actions. [Basic Rules p. 152]() · [Monster Manual p. 337](https://online.anyflip.com/sqwmo/hxog/mobile/index.html#p=338)

The source evidence identifies editorial appearance and habitat tags. I reviewed the three ImageGen portraits at full size and 96 px against Big Linda's plain parchment.
