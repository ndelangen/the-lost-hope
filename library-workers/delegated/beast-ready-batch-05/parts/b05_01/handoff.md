# Giant Owl, Giant Poisonous Snake and Giant Rat

Six encounter entries are staged from the 2018 *Basic Rules* v1.0 and the 2014 *Monster Manual*. I inspected the pages directly. All three default stat blocks agree across the two books. [Basic Rules ]() · [Monster Manual folio, 11th printing](https://anyflip.com/kqwdv/eoae/basic/301-350#p=328) · [Official errata]()

| Beast | Basic Rules page | Monster Manual folio | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Owl | 134 | 327 | AC 12, 19 HP, flight 60 ft. Flyby applies when leaving an enemy's reach in flight. Talons +3 for 8 (2d6 + 1) slashing. Speaks Giant Owl and understands Common, Elvish and Sylvan. |
| Giant Poisonous Snake | 134 | 327 | AC 14, 11 HP, 30-ft. land and swim speeds. Bite +6, reach 10 ft., for 6 (1d4 + 4) piercing, plus DC 11 Constitution for 10 (3d6) poison damage or half on a successful save. |
| Giant Rat | 135 | 327 | AC 12, 7 HP, Keen Smell and Pack Tactics. The standard Bite is +4 for 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing. |

The *Monster Manual* entry includes the optional Diseased Giant Rats box. The first printing gives the variant's Bite +3 and 3 (1d4 + 1) piercing damage. The official errata raises these to +4 and 4 (1d4 + 2), which the 11th printing incorporates. Its disease can reduce maximum HP every 24 hours until cured. The variant provides no repeat save or recovery schedule for lost maximum HP. [Official errata, page 1]()
