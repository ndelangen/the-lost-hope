# Giant Spider, Giant Toad, and Giant Vulture

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

The three stat blocks agree in both books. I checked the November 2018 Basic Rules and the 2014 *Monster Manual* folios. The 2018 *Monster Manual* errata lists no changes specific to these three creatures. [Basic Rules p. 136]() · [Monster Manual errata]()

| Entry | Basic Rules 2018 | Monster Manual 2014 | Details checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Spider | p. 136 | [p. 328](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page329.html) | AC 14, HP 26. Its Bite uses DC 11 Constitution. Poison damage that reduces a target to 0 HP stabilizes it and applies the one-hour poison and paralysis rider. Web is a +5 ranged attack that recharges on 5–6. Escape is a DC 12 Strength check. |
| Giant Toad | p. 136 | [p. 329](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page330.html) | AC 11, HP 39. Bite grapples and restrains at escape DC 13. Swallow requires a separate Bite hit against a Medium or smaller target it already has grappled. On Basic Rules p. 136, the Toad's traits and actions continue at the top of the right column. |
| Giant Vulture | p. 136 | [p. 329](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page330.html) | AC 10, HP 22. Multiattack makes one Beak and one Talons attack. In the *Monster Manual*, Talons appears at the top of the right column. |

Each beast uses one original 1254 × 1254 PNG across both printings. The Giant Spider has an orb-weaver's round abdomen and long legs. Its second draft makes all eight feet visible. Each original was reviewed at full size and in a 96-pixel circular preview beside Big Linda. [Portrait dimensions and hashes](portrait-files.json)
