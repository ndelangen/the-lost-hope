# Riding Horse and Warhorse

Four checked stat blocks. Both horses are Large, unaligned beasts with a 60-ft. walking speed. The 2018 Basic Rules already prints the corrected attack bonuses. The earlier Monster Manual folios give +2 for the Riding Horse and +4 for the Warhorse. The official errata changes those numbers to +5 and +6. [Monster Manual errata v2.0, p. 2]()

- **Riding Horse.** CR 1/4, 50 XP, AC 10 and 13 HP. Hooves are +5 to hit and deal 8 (2d4 + 3) bludgeoning. [Basic Rules p. 151]() · [Monster Manual printed p. 336](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page337.html)
- **Warhorse.** CR 1/2, 100 XP, AC 11 and 19 HP. Hooves are +6 to hit and deal 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning. Trampling Charge uses a DC 14 Strength save and can give a bonus Hooves attack against a prone target. The Monster Manual record includes its printed Warhorse Armor table. [Basic Rules p. 157]() · [Monster Manual printed p. 340](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page341.html)

The four records share one original mature light-bay portrait. I checked it against Big Linda’s parchment at full size and in a 96-pixel circle. The appearance and habitat tags are editorial.
