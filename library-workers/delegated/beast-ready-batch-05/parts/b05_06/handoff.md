# Beast-ready batch 05, group 06

Six encounter entries are staged for Poisonous Snake, Polar Bear, and Pteranodon. I checked the September 2014 Monster Manual first printing and November 2018 Basic Rules v1.0 directly, then checked the Monster Manual v2.0 errata. The Basic Rules folios match the viewer pages.

[Stat blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [2018 Basic Rules]() · [2014 Monster Manual](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2014) · [Official errata]()

The base stat blocks agree. Poisonous Snake's DC 10 Constitution save halves only the poison damage. Polar Bear keeps its 30-ft. swim speed and the two attacks printed in both sources. The Monster Manual includes the Cave Bear option, which adds 60-ft. darkvision to that variant. Pteranodon's Flyby applies when it flies out of an enemy's reach.

The two new square PNGs are 1254 × 1254. The small snake’s slender body and red, charcoal and cream bands distinguish it from a giant snake. I checked square and 96-pixel circular crops against Big Linda. The selected Pteranodon WebP matched the roster checksum. The handoff uses that existing portrait for both printings.
