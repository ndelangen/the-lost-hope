# Palace of the Silver Princess beasts

This batch covers the Giant Marble Snake and Giant Marmoset from Jean Wells's 1981 orange-cover Basic D&D module. The available scan matches the local reference byte for byte. Both creatures are absent from the later green-cover revision. [Original module]() · [Wizards reissue](https://web.archive.org/web/20030823060503/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/article.asp?x=dnd/dx20020121x7) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Beast | Printed profile | Encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Marble Snake | p. 27, p. 29 | Ballroom, room 29 on p. 25, p. 27 |
| Giant Marmoset | p. 28, p. 30 | Entrance Level, room 32 Cell D on p. 16, p. 18 |

The snake's whistle calls for a save versus Spell. Its successful strike ends the charm on that target. The ballroom individual has 16 HP. The marmoset's ground attacks can use its furry tail spike. In the trees it hangs by its tail and uses claws and bite. The general profile is Neutral, and the two Cell D guards are Chaotic with 10 HP each. The patch adds Basic D&D as a catalog ruleset and preserves the printed movement pairs. [Rules patch](rules.patch) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

Both portraits are square ImageGen drafts on plain parchment. The snake uses the printed description for its milk-white scales and gold mane. The marmoset uses the written ground-fighting description for its long, spiked tail. With the patch applied, the library found both by name and by rules text, displayed the printed numbers and encounter details, and loaded the expected portrait bytes for each version.
