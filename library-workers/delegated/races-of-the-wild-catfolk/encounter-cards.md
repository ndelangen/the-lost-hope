# Catfolk encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Catfolk subtype, low-light vision, 40-ft. land speed, and level adjustment +1. Their armor class includes the racial +1 natural armor. Both are ECL 2 and provisional CR 2. [Playable Catfolk, p. 92]()

| NPC | AC (touch, flat-footed) | HP | Initiative | Fort, Ref, Will |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tavira of the Sun Reed, Bard 1 | 16 (13, 13) | 7 | +3 | +1, +5, +2 |
| Keshar of the Dry Brook, Fighter 1 | 19 (14, 15) | 11 | +4 | +3, +4, +1 |

## Tavira of the Sun Reed, market mediator

Two trading parties disagree about a toll and the market is about to close. Tavira can compare the terms if the party brings the original tally tablet and a witness.

Diplomacy +10, Bluff +7, Gather Information +7, Perform (oratory) +7, Listen +6, Move Silently +9. Bardic Knowledge +2. Leather armor, dagger, and light crossbow.

- **Spellcasting at caster level 1.** Two cantrips per day from *detect magic*, *light*, *mage hand*, and *message*. One shared 1st-level slot for *comprehend languages* or *cure light wounds*. The first lasts ten minutes and requires touching the text or speaker. The second heals 1d8+1 HP to a living creature touched.
- **Bardic Music once per day.** Begin as a standard action. Inspire Courage gives allies who hear her +1 morale on attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, and saves against charm and fear, while heard and for five rounds afterward. Countersong uses Perform +7 within 30 ft. against sonic or language-dependent magic. Fascinate can hold one attentive target within 90 ft. for up to one round, with a Will save against her Perform result. Nearby combat prevents Fascinate.
- **Light crossbow** +3 ranged, 1d8 piercing, critical 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment. Reload as a move action. **Dagger** −1 melee, 1d4−1 piercing or slashing, minimum 1, critical 19–20/×2. [Bard class rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/bard.htm)

## Keshar of the Dry Brook, perimeter escort

Keshar is bringing a healer from the night market to camp. The party can open the alternate gate while he keeps the route clear.

Chain shirt and two short swords. Climb +4, Jump +8, Listen +3, Move Silently +4. The Jump total includes +4 from his 40-ft. land speed.

- **One short sword** +3 melee for 1d6+2 piercing, critical 19–20/×2.
- **Two-weapon full attack** +1/+1. The main hand deals 1d6+2 and the off hand deals 1d6+1.
- **Charge** +5 for one short sword attack. Against a flat-footed opponent, Catfolk Pounce allows both attacks at +3/+3, with normal damage by hand. Both charge options give −2 AC until the start of Keshar's next turn. [Catfolk Pounce, p. 148]()
