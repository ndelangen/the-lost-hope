# Catfolk for D&D 3.5

One playable Catfolk entry from the February 2005 first printing of *Races of the Wild*. It gives Dexterity +4, Charisma +2, 40-ft. land speed, low-light vision, +1 natural armor, and level adjustment +1. Lionlike, leopard-, tiger-, and cheetah-like appearances share this printed rules set. The plain-parchment portrait shows original leopard-like Catfolk **Tavira of the Sun Reed**. [Playable traits, p. 92]() · [Species rules](book-species.json) · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg)

Catfolk use ordinary unarmed attacks. Catfolk Pounce is an optional feat that lets a character make a full attack at the end of a charge against a flat-footed opponent. Tavira is a Bard who mediates a market dispute. **Keshar of the Dry Brook** is a two-weapon Fighter escorting a healer. Keshar takes the feat. Both NPCs are provisional CR 2. [Feat, p. 148]() · [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [NPC build records](npc-builds.json)

The printed Scout and Infiltrator are separate published builds. The Scout's listed CR is 2 even though the example encounter gives EL 1 for a single Scout. The source notes retain the distinction. [Published encounters, pp. 94–95]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)
