# Independent checks

The source review checked the February 2005 playable rules and the p. 148 feat. Thick nails use ordinary humanoid unarmed attacks. Catfolk Pounce is optional, requires Dexterity 13, and applies to a charge against a flat-footed target. The printed Scout is Ranger 1, CR 2, and the Infiltrator is Rogue 3, CR 4. The book prints a separate EL 1 for the single-Scout example. The 2003 *Miniatures Handbook* has a distinct CR 1/2 Warrior and the same playable traits. The publisher's collected errata has no *Races of the Wild* entry.
