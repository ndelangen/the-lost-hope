# Humblewood Campaign Setting v1.04 — five beasts

Hit Point Press’s original campaign setting credits identify the 2019 first edition, ISBN 978-1-9990962-0-5. The publisher’s digital listing labels the package v1.04. The two mirrors expose the book text and Appendix A’s printed page numbers. I checked both extracts. Their text can interleave columns, and I did not independently inspect page images. The Mountain Lion lore starts on p. 184, with the block on p. 185. [Publisher listing](https://hitpointpress.itch.io/humblewood) · [Book text]() · [Appendix A alternate mirror](https://anyflip.com/dbqdz/wewu/basic/151-200)

## Emberbat — p. 182

Tiny unaligned Beast, CR 0 (10 XP), AC 12, HP 2 (1d4), walk 5 ft., fly 30 ft., blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 11. Echolocation switches off blindsight while deafened. Keen Hearing gives advantage on hearing-based Wisdom (Perception) checks. Ember Flame lights and dismisses at will, with bright light 5 ft. and dim light 5 ft. farther. Bite is printed at +0 to hit, reach 5 ft., 1 piercing plus 1 fire. The trait says the fire bonus applies while lit and is already included in that hit line. The lore places these ash-furred bats in the Scorched Grove and describes a candle-sized flame at the nose.

## Swarm of Emberbats — p. 182

Medium unaligned swarm of Tiny Beasts, CR 1/2 (100 XP), AC 12, HP 22 (5d8), walk 0 ft., fly 30 ft., blindsight 60 ft., passive Perception 10. The swarm resists bludgeoning, piercing and slashing and is immune to charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained and stunned. It shares the lone bat’s Echolocation and Keen Hearing. Flame Essence forces each creature starting its turn within 5 ft. to make a DC 10 CON save or take 2 (1d4) fire. Bites is +4, reach 0 ft., and targets one creature inside the swarm’s space for 5 (2d4) piercing, reduced to 2 (1d4) at half HP or less. Swarm permits shared spaces and passage through a Tiny bat’s opening, and prevents healing or gaining temporary HP. The collective heat is modeled by Flame Essence and the bite itself deals piercing only.

## Forest Prowler — p. 183

Large unaligned Beast, CR 4 (1,100 XP), natural AC 16, HP 75 (10d10 + 20), walk and climb 40 ft., Perception +4, Stealth +6, darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14. Natural Camouflage grants advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in forest. Pounce starts with 10 ft. of movement toward a creature and a Claw hit that turn. DC 14 STR or prone. The following sentence allows a bonus Claw against that target and lists no failed-save prerequisite. Ambuscade can use the bonus action to Dash or Hide. Multiattack is two Claws and one Bite. Both are +6 with 5 ft. reach. Claw deals 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing. Bite deals 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing, plus 4 (1d8) piercing against a prone target. Its spotted coat grows moss and leaves that help it ambush from the forest canopy.

## Mountain Lion — pp. 184–185

Large unaligned Beast, CR 1 (200 XP), natural AC 15, HP 37 (5d10 + 10), walk 40 ft., climb 30 ft., Perception +4, Stealth +4, passive Perception 14. Keen Smell gives advantage on smell-based Wisdom (Perception) checks. Pounce starts with 20 ft. of movement toward a creature and a Claw hit that turn. DC 13 STR or prone. The trait allows a bonus Bite against a prone target. Both basic attacks are +5 with 5 ft. reach. Bite deals 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing and Claw deals 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing. Call Avalanche requires mountainous terrain and recharges after a short or long rest. Choose a visible space within 60 ft. Creatures within 15 ft. of it make a DC 12 DEX save against 13 (3d8) bludgeoning, with half damage on success. The rocky projections in the p. 184 description camouflage the lion on the Crest Mountains’ cliffs.

## Wakewyrm — p. 189

Huge unaligned Beast, CR 6 (2,300 XP), natural AC 16, HP 136 (13d12 + 52), walk 20 ft., swim 50 ft., passive Perception 11. Hold Breath lasts 1 hour. Multiattack allows two Bites or one Bite and one Tail. Bite is +8, reach 20 ft., for 15 (3d6 + 5) piercing. DC 16 STR or grappled, with escape DC 16. It can hold one creature at a time and cannot bite another creature while that grapple lasts. The Bite grapple itself does not restrain. Tail is +8, reach 25 ft., for 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning.

Swallow makes a Bite against a Large or smaller creature already grappled. On a hit it replaces the grapple with swallowing. The swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, has total cover from outside, and takes the printed 11 (3d6) acid at the start of the wyrm’s turns. If an inside creature deals at least 18 damage, the wyrm makes a DC 15 CON save at the end of that turn or regurgitates all swallowed creatures, prone within 10 ft. On the wyrm’s death, restraint ends and escape costs 20 ft. of movement. The lore describes a reptile with snake and eel features, over 100 ft. long, ranging the open ocean and occasionally seen off the Talongrip Coast.
