# Aventi from Stormwrack

This batch adds the D&D 3.5 Aventi from the August 2005 first printing and two original encounters. Aventi breathe air and water without a time limit. Water Spell Power adds +1 caster level only to spells with the [Water] descriptor. Selka’s first-level cleric demonstrates this with create water at CL 2, up to four gallons. [Original rules, p. 34]() · [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

Selka’s portrait uses the approved plain tan parchment and shows the small fins on her forearms and calves. The temporary copy found Aventi by name, trait, and NPC role. Both catalog commands returned both builds, and Selka’s portrait resolved as the new list thumbnail.
