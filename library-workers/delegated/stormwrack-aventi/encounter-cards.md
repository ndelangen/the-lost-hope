# Aventi encounters

Both are Medium Humanoids with the Human and Aquatic subtypes. Each is provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. Both breathe air and water without a time limit and have land and swim speeds of 30 ft. Both can take 10 on Swim checks for hazards even under pressure. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Selka of Tideglass, Cleric 1

Selka swims under the collapsed stair into the cistern’s dry upper chamber. She fills the survivors’ jug and marks an exit for the party.

- HP 10, AC 13, initiative +1, Swim +9. Fortitude +4, Reflex +1, Will +5.
- Create water has the [Water] descriptor. Selka casts it at CL 2 to create up to four gallons at up to 30 ft. Guidance and light stay at CL 1.
- First-level spells are bless, cure light wounds, and endure elements from the Ocean domain slot. All three are CL 1. Cure light wounds restores 1d8+1 HP.
- Spear +1 for 1d8+1 piercing. Turn undead five times per day, with 1d20+2 for the check and 2d6+3 total HD affected.

## Kerrin of Shalebank, Fighter 1

Kerrin swims a rope through the flooded sluice. He secures it at the far gate and guides the party through the current.

- HP 12, AC 14, initiative +6, Swim +15. Fortitude +4, Reflex +2, Will +0.
- Shortspear +5 in melee for 1d6+3 piercing. A piercing thrust keeps its normal damage underwater. Above water he can throw it at +4 for 1d6+3 piercing.
