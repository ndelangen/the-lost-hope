# Aventi source check

The August 2005 first printing gives the player rules on p. 34. The table shortens the type to Humanoid (Aquatic), while the detailed rules give both the Human and Aquatic subtypes. The separate Warrior and Sergeant on p. 138 use both. Human Blood governs race-based effects and items. The Aventi player traits give no standard human bonus feat or extra skill points. [Player rules]() · [Published encounter examples]()

Water Spell Power applies to the [Water] descriptor, which create water has. The core water breathing spell lacks that descriptor even though it appears on the Ocean domain list. Aventernus offers the Ocean and Good domains. The independent review checked Selka’s spell slots, skill points, and turning, and verified Kerrin’s attack and feat calculations. [Create water](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/createWater.htm) · [Water breathing](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/waterBreathing.htm) · [Aventernus, p. 49]()

Wizards’ collected archive has no Stormwrack errata file. The June 2008 FAQ gives no Aventi correction. Eric Polak illustrated the unnamed Aventi paladin on p. 35. The portrait uses the printed description of folded calf and forearm fins. Selka’s clothing, pose, and cup are original.
