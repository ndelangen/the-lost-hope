# Elk, Owl, Pony, and Quipper

| Entry and printing | Page | Encounter check |
|---|---|---|
| Elk, Black Flag v0.2 | 357 | 15 HP. Charge adds 1d6 bludgeoning and uses a DC 12 Strength save. Kick can push the target up to 10 ft. |
| Elk, Monster Manual (2014) | 322 | 13 HP. Charge adds 2d6 and uses a DC 13 Strength save. Hooves requires a prone target. |
| Owl, Black Flag v0.2 | 365 | Fixed Perception and Stealth are both 13. Sight or hearing raises Perception to 18. Flying raises Stealth to 18. |
| Pony, Black Flag v0.2 | 366 | 10 HP. Hooves +4 for 1d4+2 bludgeoning. Draft Animal uses a multiplier of three. |
| Quipper, Black Flag v0.2 | 366 | 8 HP. Bite +2 for 1d4 piercing, with advantage against creatures below maximum HP. |

I compared each entry with the printed block. The two Elk versions share a portrait and keep their own rules. Black Flag's XP comes from its p. 229 CR table. The Black Flag blocks print fixed HP and ability modifiers. The 2014 Elk keeps its Hit Dice and scores. [Black Flag v0.2]() · [2014 Elk](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16857-elk) · [Monster Manual errata]()

Pony's Draft Animal trait calls its subject a draft horse. Since the trait is printed in the Pony block, I applied its multiplier to the pony and left the absolute load unspecified. The habitat tags draw on the 2014 Elk tags, natural-history references, and suggested encounter locations, as identified per entry.
