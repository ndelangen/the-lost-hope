# Panther, Vulture, Weasel, and Wolf

Five encounter stat blocks and four original square portraits. Both Panther printings share the same illustration. The abilities and attack values come from their respective books. [Black Flag v0.2 ]() · [2014 Panther in the Basic Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-p#Panther)

| Record | Printing and page | AC | HP | CR and XP |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| Panther | Monster Manual (2014), p. 333 | 12 | 13 (3d8) | 1/4, 50 XP |
| Vulture | Black Flag v0.2, p. 373 | 10 | 8 | 0, 10 XP |
| Weasel | Black Flag v0.2, p. 373 | 11 | 8 | 0, 10 XP |
| Wolf | Black Flag v0.2, p. 374 | 13 | 14 | 1/4, 50 XP |

- Black Flag Panther's Stealthy Hunter bonus action continues onto p. 366. It also has 60-ft. darkvision and fixed Perception 19 for scent. The 2014 Panther uses Keen Smell and the skill values from p. 333. [Black Flag Panther]() · [2014 printed folio](https://online.anyflip.com/sqwmo/hxog/mobile/index.html#p=334)
- Vulture's Defensive Bile forces a DC 11 Constitution save against incapacitation and deals no damage. The damage and stronger save in the other column belong to Giant Vulture. [Vulture and Weasel]()
- Weasel can take an eligible item instead of dealing Bite damage. The printed attack gives the item limits and adds no separate check.
- Wolf's Bite uses the printed +4 attack and 1d8 + 2 damage. A creature hit must pass a DC 12 Strength save or fall prone. [Wolf]()

Black Flag provides fixed HP and ability modifiers. Its XP comes from the table on p. 229. The habitat tags and portraits are editorial, with the Panther depicted as a melanistic leopard.
