I read the Black Flag blocks from the May 2024 v0.2 , whose printed folios match the page numbers. Its p. 229 table supplies XP, its p. 232 definition covers the Mammoth's resistance to the prone condition, and p. 235 explains the Spider's recharge. I also checked the 2014 Mammoth against the September 2014 first printing and the official errata. [Publisher ]() · [2014 Mammoth](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16950-mammoth) · [Monster Manual errata]()

Black Flag Mammoth has two Gore attacks, Trunk Grab, and resistance to cold and prone. The 2014 Mammoth has its original Gore, Stomp and DC 18 charge, with 11d12 + 55 Hit Dice. The Black Flag Spider's Web Shot slows a target by 10 ft. until a creature removes the webbing as an action. The Black Flag Tiger's bleed calls for one save at the start of the target's next turn.

All four original PNGs are 1254 × 1254. I compared them with Big Linda at portrait size and 96 pixels. The source notes identify appearance and habitat details drawn from the books or added as editorial search terms.
