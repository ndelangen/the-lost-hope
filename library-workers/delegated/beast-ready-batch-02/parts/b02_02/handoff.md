# Baboon, Badger and Crab

Five encounter stat blocks across two printings. The paired Baboon and Badger records each share one portrait. I checked the Black Flag blocks against the publisher’s May 2024 v0.2 and the fifth-edition blocks against the 2014 Monster Manual and official SRD 5.1. The published errata lists no changes to the two Monster Manual entries. [Black Flag v0.2]() · [Monster Manual printed p. 318](https://online.anyflip.com/pqhqn/sezy/files/basic-html/page319.html) · [Official SRD 5.1, p. 367]() · [Monster Manual errata]()

| Printing | Creature | Table details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black Flag v0.2, p. 351 | Baboon | CR 1/8, AC 11, HP 10, Bite +3. Startling Display requires a visible attacker within 5 ft. The reaction imposes disadvantage on that attack if the attacker fails a DC 11 Wisdom save. |
| Monster Manual 2014, p. 318 | Baboon | CR 0, AC 12, HP 3, Bite +1. Pack Tactics checks for a non-incapacitated ally within 5 ft. of the target. |
| Black Flag v0.2, p. 351 | Badger | CR 0, AC 10, HP 9, burrow 10 ft., Bite +2. Grumpy sets target priority. Heightened Smell raises fixed Perception to 16 for smell. |
| Monster Manual 2014, p. 318 | Badger | CR 0, AC 10, HP 3, burrow 5 ft., Bite +2. Keen Smell gives advantage on Perception checks that rely on smell. |
| Black Flag v0.2, pp. 354–355 | Crab | CR 0, AC 11, HP 9, Claw +2. A hit attaches the crab and it deals damage at the start of its own turns. The crab spends 5 ft. of movement on its turn to detach, or any creature can detach it as an action. |

The Black Flag blocks give fixed hit points, ability modifiers, Perception and Stealth. They omit Hit Dice, raw ability scores and alignment. Their XP values come from the native CR table. [Black Flag CR table, p. 229]()

I checked the three original square portraits against Big Linda at full size and 96 pixels. I revised the Crab to make all eight walking legs and both pincers visible. The physical descriptions and habitat tags are editorial and grounded in the pictured animals.
