# Boar, Deer, Eagle and Elephant

Five encounter blocks with four original parchment portraits. Both Boar blocks share the same artwork. The portrait comparison shows Big Linda, Boar, Deer, Eagle and Elephant at two sizes. I checked the 1254 × 1254 originals and 96-pixel previews. [Portrait comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.txt) · [Portrait hashes](portrait-files.json)

- **Boar.** Black Flag has +4 to hit, DC 12 Charge and a 4-damage Relentless threshold. The 2014 block has +3 to hit, DC 11 Charge and a 7-damage threshold. I checked the 2014 print page and the official errata. [2014 rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-b#Boar) · [2014 Monster Manual errata]()
- **Deer.** Kick can knock a Large or smaller creature prone. Skittish allows a bonus-action Dash or Disengage. [Printed block]()
- **Eagle.** Seabird allows 30 ft. of conditional swimming and has separate CON and STR rolls for getting stranded and taking flight. Its description gives no action cost for the latter check. [Printed block]()
- **Elephant.** Trunk Grab uses the bonus action and permits one grapple at a time. Trampling Charge can instead give it a bonus-action Stomp against the prone creature. [Printed block]()

The Black Flag blocks use fixed ability modifiers, HP, Perception and Stealth. The printed XP table supplies XP. The Black Flag blocks give no Hit Dice, raw ability scores or alignment. The habitat and appearance evidence distinguishes printed details from interpretation. [Black Flag XP table]()
