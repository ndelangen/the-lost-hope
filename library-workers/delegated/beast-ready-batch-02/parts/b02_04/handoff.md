# Camel, Frog, Goat, and Hawk

Five encounter profiles and four original parchment portraits. Camel shares one portrait across its two rules versions.

[Stat blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source notes](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait manifest](portrait-files.json)

## Source checks

| Beast and printing | Folio | Mechanics checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camel, Black Flag v0.2 | p. 354 | AC 10, HP 10, fixed Perception 9 and Stealth 10. Camel Resilience halves water needs and grants exhaustion resistance. Bile Spit is a ranged action with a DC 12 Constitution save against its hour-long effect, which can be cleaned away. |
| Camel, Monster Manual (2014) | Printed p. 320 | AC 9, HP 15 (2d10 + 4), passive Perception 9. The Bite is +5 to hit and deals 2 (1d4) bludgeoning. The publisher’s legacy stat block agrees with the printed page, and its official errata lists no Camel change. |
| Frog, Black Flag v0.2 | p. 357 | Grappled resistance, Amphibious, Standing Leap, a 20-ft. swim, and the ordinary Frog’s Bite. |
| Goat, Black Flag v0.2 | pp. 358–359 | Prone resistance, Charge and Ram on p. 358. Terrifying Scream continues at the top of p. 359. Creatures frightened by the scream gain the 24-hour protection against that goat’s scream. |
| Hawk, Black Flag v0.2 | p. 359 | Fixed Perception 14, or 19 by sight. Talons and the Evasive Flight bonus action, available while flying. |

The Black Flag profiles use the printed ability modifiers and fixed Perception and Stealth values. XP follows the native table on p. 229. Their native blocks give fixed HP and omit Hit Dice. Habitat and appearance are documented as editorial search text.
