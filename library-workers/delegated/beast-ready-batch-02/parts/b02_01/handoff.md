# Ape, Axe Beak, and Bat

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

I checked Black Flag v0.2 against Kobold Press's May 2024 reference . Ape is on p. 350, Axe Beak on p. 351, and Bat on p. 352. The CR table is on p. 229. The entries use the printed ability modifiers, fixed Perception and Stealth, and Hit Points. Black Flag gives no Hit Dice or underlying ability scores for these three creatures. [Kobold Press v0.2 ]()

Ape and Axe Beak are both on p. 317 of the 2014 *Monster Manual*. I read the indexed printing and checked its values against the two official legacy blocks and SRD 5.1. Neither block has a change in the publisher's 2018 errata.
