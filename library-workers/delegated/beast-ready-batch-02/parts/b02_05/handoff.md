# Cat, Hyena, Jackal and Lizard

Five encounter blocks and four portraits are staged. I checked the May 2024 Black Flag v0.2 and the Cat on printed page 320 of the 2014 *Monster Manual*, using the 11th printing. The official 2014 reprint agrees with the Cat's printed attack. [Black Flag v0.2]() · [2014 Cat](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16820-cat)

| Entry | Rules page | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black Flag Cat | 354 | AC 11, 8 HP, darkvision 30 ft., Claws +3. Charming Mewl targets one creature the cat can see within 10 ft. and uses DC 11 Charisma. A successful save or the end of the charm grants 24 hours of immunity to this cat's mewl. |
| 2014 Cat | 320 | AC 12, 2 HP or 1d4. Claws +0 for 1 slashing damage. Keen Smell gives advantage on scent-based Perception checks. |
| Hyena | 360 | AC 10, 8 HP, speed 50 ft., Bite +2. Cackling Chorus takes at least three participants. The start-of-turn save can deafen until the start of the creature's next turn. |
| Jackal | 362 | AC 11, 8 HP, Bite +3. DC 10 Constitution against shriveling fever. The disease poisons the target and can drain Strength after a long rest. Two successful saves cure it. |
| Lizard | 363 | AC 10, 9 HP, Bite +2. At 5 HP or fewer, Detach Tail can reduce incoming damage by 2 (1d4) and permit immediate movement without opportunity attacks. The reaction recharges on a short or long rest. |

The Hyena block lists DC 10 and a formula of 8 plus the number of participating hyenas. The table note uses the formula as an explicit ruling, giving DC 11 with three participants. All five entries are CR 0. Black Flag's 10 XP values come from its own p. 229 table. The habitat tags are editorial encounter suggestions.
