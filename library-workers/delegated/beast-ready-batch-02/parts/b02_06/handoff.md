# Crocodile, Mastiff, Mule, and Octopus

Five encounter blocks cover the two Crocodile printings and the Black Flag Mastiff, Mule, and Octopus. I used the printed blocks and compared the 2014 Crocodile with the official Basic Rules and the local 5etools extract. The Black Flag folios match its page numbers. [Black Flag v0.2]() · [2014 Crocodile](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-c#Crocodile) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

| Mule | Black Flag v0.2, p. 364 | AC 10, HP 10, CR 1/8, 25 XP. |
| Octopus | Black Flag v0.2, p. 364 | AC 11, HP 8, CR 0, 10 XP. |

- Both Crocodiles grapple with Bite at escape DC 12. Black Flag uses the d8 bite and adds the Death Roll bonus action. The 2014 bite uses a d10.
- Mastiff's Protect Friend reaction measures 5 ft. from the dog to the attacker. The dog must see the friendly Humanoid who was hit.
- Mule counts as Large for carrying capacity. Black Flag resistance gives advantage on checks and saves to avoid or end exhaustion or prone.
- Octopus uses Stealth 18 underwater. Its Ink Dash is a reaction to damage from a creature it can see while underwater. Swimming as part of that reaction immediately releases a held creature.

The Black Flag blocks use fixed Perception, Stealth, and ability modifiers. XP comes from the table on p. 229. The source supplies flat HP, with Hit Dice, full ability scores, and alignment unspecified. Habitat suggestions are marked as editorial in the evidence, except for the octopus's underwater habitat, which follows the printed traits. [Staged rules](stat-blocks.json)

Four original square PNGs use the Big Linda parchment and paint reference. The Crocodile printings share a single portrait. I reviewed the originals alongside 96 px square and circular previews. The circular crops trim the tips of one mule ear and two octopus arms. The square images show the complete animals.
