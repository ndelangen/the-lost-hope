# Lion, Rat, Raven, and Rhinoceros

Five existing Beast entries have encounter rules and four original parchment portraits. Both Lions share a portrait and retain separate stat blocks. I used the May 2024 Black Flag v0.2 and the 2014 *Monster Manual*. [Black Flag v0.2 ]() · [2014 Monster Manual printed p. 331](https://fliphtml5.com/vmzaj/Monster-Manual_AddPage/333/) · [2014 Lion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16944-lion)

| Rhinoceros, Black Flag | 368 | AC 11, HP 63, CR 2, 450 XP. Walk 40 ft., Perception 11, Stealth 9. |

The Black Flag Lion has Multiattack and a rechargeable bonus-action roar. Pounce and the roar compete for the same bonus action. The 2014 Lion uses Keen Smell and has its own Bite, Claw, and Pounce. The official 2014 errata lists no Lion change. [Black Flag Lion]() · [2014 errata]()

The Rat has advantage on saves to avoid contracting a disease. Its bite only deals damage. The Raven identifies carrion, dead creatures, and certain Undead by scent within 60 ft. Recollection lets it echo up to 20 words it has heard using Mimicry. The Rhinoceros keeps the printed bludgeoning Gore, piercing Charge rider, resistance to stunned, and bonus-action Quaking Stomp. [Rat]() · [Raven]() · [Rhinoceros]()

Black Flag HP, ability modifiers, Perception, and Stealth come straight from the profiles. XP comes from the native CR table. Habitat and appearance tags are editorial. I read the rules and checked the values and timings by hand. I inspected the four 1254 × 1254 PNGs at full size and in 96-pixel circular previews beside Big Linda.
