# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual centipedes

The four columns of the Centipede entry appear on page 42 of the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. Giant Centipede gains a historical version. Huge Centipede, Megalo-centipede, and Tunnel Worm add catalog entries. The manual groups the nonmagical Tunnel Worm with centipedes and describes it as a relative, so this catalog lists it under Beasts. [Printed page 42](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/4a5f464d431b7c7386874e8943b4bd69.webp) · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Printed durability | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Centipede | 2 HP | No direct bite damage. The victim saves at +4 against 2d6 hours of paralysis. The centipede takes −1 to its own saves. |
| Huge Centipede | 1 HP | Six inches long, smaller than the Giant. A failed save immobilizes the victim for 1d6 hours. The victim's +4 carries over by reference to the Giant Centipede. The Huge Centipede takes −2 to its own saves. |
| Megalo-centipede | 3 HD | The table lists 1–3 bite damage. The unmodified poison save determines death on a failure or 2d4 acid damage on a success. |
| Tunnel Worm | 9+3 HD | A +2 lunge seizes the target if it hits. It takes one, two, or three rounds to chew through armor. The table lists 2–8 damage, while the combat text specifies automatic 2d8 per round after the armor is breached. Both are recorded. |

The plain parchment portraits distinguish the reddish forest Giant, the smaller rocky gray Huge, the heavier Megalo-centipede, and the thick-bodied Tunnel Worm with large mandibles. The Megalo-centipede's palette and the Tunnel Worm's body and leg proportions are illustration choices. Tony DiTerlizzi identifies his printed illustration as the Giant Centipede in his art checklist, even though it sits below the Tunnel Worm text.
