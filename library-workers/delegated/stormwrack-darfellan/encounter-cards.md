# Darfellan encounters

Both NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Darfellan subtype. Each is provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. Both swim 40 ft., can take 10 on Swim checks for hazards even under pressure, and gain +2 on attack and damage rolls against sahuagin. Echolocation gives 20-ft. blindsense in water and does not remove concealment. Both breathe air. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Oru Breakwater, Barbarian 1

Oru dives through the flooded cave mouth to find stranded gatherers. Use echolocation to locate nearby creatures, then surface at the air pocket before fighting on the ledge.

- HP 15, AC 13, initiative +1, land speed 30 ft. in light armor with a light load, Swim +16. Constitution 16 gives a budget of 128 rounds before drowning checks on a calm dive. Exertion can shorten it.
- Primary bite +5 for 1d6+6, or +7 for 1d6+8 against sahuagin.
- Rage once per day for 8 rounds. It raises HP to a maximum of 17, lowers AC to 11, and raises the primary bite to +7 for 1d6+9, or +9 for 1d6+11 against sahuagin. Oru is fatigued when rage ends.

## Tava Ebbgate, Fighter 1

Tava guides a rope team across the submerged channel. She emerges on the ledge above the far gate and keeps the exit clear.

- HP 12, AC 13, initiative +5, land speed 20 ft., Swim +16. Constitution 14 gives a budget of 112 rounds before drowning checks on a calm dive.
- Shortspear +6 melee or +4 thrown for 1d6+4 piercing. With the spear set aside, primary bite +5 for 1d6+6.
- On an armed full attack use shortspear +6 for 1d6+4 and secondary bite +0 for 1d6+2. Against sahuagin those become +8 for 1d6+6 and +2 for 1d6+4.
