# Giant Squid, Constrictor Snake, Dire Tiger, Hawk, and Octopus

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Giant Squid portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

I checked the Revised v3.5 SRD's original RTF tables and every accompanying combat paragraph. Four entries are in `MonstersAnimals.rtf`, and Dire Tiger is in `MonstersDi-Do.rtf`. Each evidence record includes the archive SHA-256 and exact heading. [Archived SRD](https://archive.org/details/dnd35srd)

| Entry | Manual check |
| --- | --- |
| Giant Squid | CR 9, 72 HP, grapple +29, ten tentacle attacks and a bite on full attack. A tentacle can reach 30 ft. and grab a target of any size. Each limb has 10 HP for sundering. A severed limb costs the squid 5 HP and regrows in 1d10 + 10 days. The squid usually retreats after losing five. |
| Constrictor Snake | CR 2, 19 HP, grapple +5. Bite can start a free grapple and constriction deals 1d3 + 4. Scent is in the individual table. The general Animal traits give low-light vision unless an entry specifies otherwise. |
| Dire Tiger | CR 8, 120 HP, grapple +24. Improved Grab triggers from Bite. A charge permits full attack and two +18 rakes. The printed Hide racial bonus of +4 becomes +8 in tall grass or heavy undergrowth. |
| Hawk | CR 1/3, 4 HP, 0-ft. reach and average flight. Its two talons form one attack. The 1d4 − 2 damage roll is subject to the general one-point minimum for a hit. |
| Octopus | CR 1, 9 HP. The arms hit for 0 damage and can grab, with bite damage on a successful hold. The printed Grapple is +2. Its listed base attack, Strength, and Small size would give −2 under the general formula, so the record keeps the printed value. |

The two ink-cloud entries give use frequency, size, and concealment. Neither gives a duration. All four reused portrait hashes match the roster. The Dire Tiger's existing parchment is more saturated than the new Giant Squid's. The portrait comparison shows both the square art and how it reads in a 96-pixel circle.
