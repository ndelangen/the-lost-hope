# Five animals from the revised 3.5 SRD

I checked the original Wizards RTF tables, the combat text below each table, and the shared rules for the special attacks. The five profiles use 3.5 Hit Dice, initiative, touch and flat-footed AC, grapple modifiers, and saves. The Environment tags come from the RTF. The source evidence identifies the archive and shard by SHA-256.

[Stat blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

| Beast | RTF heading | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brown Bear | MonstersAnimals.rtf, BEAR, BROWN | Claw hit leads to Improved Grab, with the shared size limit and hold options. The +4 racial Swim bonus is included in Swim +12. |
| Dire Lion | MonstersDi-Do.rtf, DIRE LION | Bite triggers Improved Grab. Pounce gives the full attack and two rakes. The source note records the tension between the bite-grab wording and the shared start-of-turn requirement for Rake. Outside a pounce, the staged summary applies the shared timing. |
| Elephant | MonstersAnimals.rtf, ELEPHANT | The full attack offers either slam and two stamps or one gore. Use the creature’s explicit Trample damage of 2d8 + 15 and Reflex DC 25. The source also gives the Indian elephant’s scores as a variation. |
| Monitor Lizard | MonstersAnimals.rtf, LIZARD, MONITOR | Bite, conditional Hide bonus, and the Swim rules for taking 10 and running in a straight line. |
| Riding Dog | MonstersAnimals.rtf, DOG, RIDING | Carrying limits, the war-trained trip procedure from Wolf, and the rider’s Ride check. The table’s Jump +8 is preserved. The source note records the inconsistency with the general Jump modifiers. |

I checked all five reused portraits against their roster hashes and described the animals as depicted. The existing Dire Lion art has a stronger orange paper tone than the other staged examples.
