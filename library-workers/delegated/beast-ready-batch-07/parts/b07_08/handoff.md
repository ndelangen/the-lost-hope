# Five revised 3.5 SRD beasts

I read the original creature tables and combat text in the RTF archive. The overlays give the listed ability scores, AC breakdowns, saves, skills, feats, and encounter procedures. The source evidence gives the original shard and heading, archive checksum, and any shared rule shards needed to run the creature.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Black Bear | `MonstersAnimals.rtf` | AC 13, HP 19 (3d8 + 6), CR 2. Claw +6 for 1d4 + 4. [Black Bear](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/bearBlack.htm) |
| Dire Bear | `MonstersDi-Do.rtf` | AC 17, HP 105 (12d8 + 51), CR 7. Claw +19 for 2d4 + 10. Improved Grab uses grapple +23 before any claw-only penalty. [Dire Bear](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/direBear.htm) |
| Eagle | `MonstersAnimals.rtf` | AC 14, HP 5 (1d8 + 1), CR 1/2. Fly 80 ft. with average maneuverability, talons +3 for 1d4. [Eagle](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/eagle.htm) |
| Manta Ray | `MonstersAnimals.rtf` | AC 12, HP 18 (4d8), CR 1. Aquatic, swim 30 ft., secondary Ram −1 for 1d6 + 1. Its base size is Large and its printed advancement says 5–6 HD (Medium). [Manta Ray](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/mantaRay.htm) |
| Rat Swarm | `MonstersS.rtf` | AC 14, HP 13 (4d8), CR 2. Automatic 1d6 swarm damage and filth fever. Both disease and Distraction use Fortitude DC 12. [Rat Swarm](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/swarm.htm#ratSwarm) |

Rat Swarm keeps the printed 13 HP even though 4d8 usually averages 18. I recorded both printed Distraction triggers. The creature block says any living creature that begins its turn in the swarm's square. The subtype text adds vulnerability to swarm damage. The overlay includes the swarm's movement, targeting and damage rules, and filth fever's incubation, daily saves and recovery. [Swarm subtype](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/typesSubtypes.htm#swarmSubtype) · [Disease](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#disease)

All five reused portrait hashes match the roster. I checked square views and 96-pixel circular previews. The supplied Dire Bear uses brighter orange parchment, and its muzzle sits close to the circular edge. This part retains the selected source files and has an empty new-portrait manifest.
