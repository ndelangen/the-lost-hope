I checked the stat tables and combat prose in the original RTF archive. The blocks include touch and flat-footed AC, base attack, grapple, three saves, skill modifiers and 3.5 combat procedures. The archive SHA-256 is recorded alongside each RTF heading.

- Dire Ape's Rend requires both claws to hit the same opponent. The RTF table gives 2d6 + 9 and its combat prose gives 2d6 + 12. The profile records both values so a DM can choose before play. [SRD mirror](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/direApe.htm)
- Dire Wolf and Hyena can trip after a bite with modifiers of +11 and +2 respectively. The free attempt skips the melee touch attack and carries no counter-trip if it fails. [Dire Wolf](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/direWolf.htm) · [Hyena](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/hyena.htm)
- Polar Bear's Improved Grab starts after a claw hit and uses the shared 3.5 grapple rules. Its listed Hide −2 improves to +10 in snow. [Polar Bear](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/bearPolar.htm) · [Improved Grab](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#improvedGrab)
