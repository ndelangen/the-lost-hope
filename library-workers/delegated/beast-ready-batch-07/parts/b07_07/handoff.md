# Bat Swarm, Dire Bat, Donkey, Lion, and Porpoise

Five encounters sourced from the original Wizards of the Coast Revised v3.5 SRD RTF archive. The archive and shard hashes identify the exact text. The habitat tags follow the printed Environment fields. I checked the portrait hashes against the roster.

| Beast | RTF | AC, touch, flat-footed | HP | CR |

- Bat Swarm deals automatic 1d6 damage at the end of its move. A living creature starting its turn amid the swarm risks nausea on a failed DC 11 Fortitude save. Damage to a living creature also starts nonstacking bleeding at 1 HP per round until a DC 10 Heal check or magical healing stops it. [Bat Swarm](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/swarm.htm#batSwarm) · [Swarm subtype](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/typesSubtypes.htm#swarmSubtype)
- The archive prints touch AC 14 and flat-footed AC 12 for Bat Swarm, despite the listed bonuses yielding 16 and 14. The staged block keeps the printed AC values. The archive also lists half damage from slashing and piercing. For play, use the general Diminutive swarm immunity to weapon damage, confirmed specifically for Bat Swarm by the 17 February 2006 *Monster Manual* errata. [Publisher erratum]()
- Dire Bat uses 40-ft. blindsense and good flight maneuverability. Its Listen and Spot bonuses depend on blindsense. Donkey retains the printed 50, 100, and 150 lb. load boundaries and 750 lb. drag limit. [Dire Bat](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/direBat.htm) · [Donkey](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/donkey.htm)
- Lion's Pounce allows a full attack plus two rakes on a charge. Its Improved Grab says a successful hold permits raking. The general Rake rule requires starting the turn grappling outside the explicit Pounce provision. The source evidence records the timing ambiguity for a fresh hold. [Lion](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/lion.htm) · [General Rake rule](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#rake)
- Porpoise uses 120-ft. blindsight, which *silence* negates. The printed breath rule gives six times its Constitution score, or 78 rounds. [Porpoise](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/porpoise.htm)

The roster reuses all five portraits. The Dire Bat source has a more saturated orange backdrop than the other reused art.
