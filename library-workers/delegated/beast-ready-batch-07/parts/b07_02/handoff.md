# Deinonychus, Cheetah, Dire Shark, Giant Octopus, and Mule

| Beast | Revised v3.5 SRD source | Encounter check |
|---|---|---|
| Deinonychus | MonstersDi-Do.rtf, Dinosaur | Large, AC 16, grapple +11. On a charge, Pounce permits a full attack of talons, two foreclaws, and bite. The two hind talons count as one attack. |
| Cheetah | MonstersAnimals.rtf, Cheetah | Sprint covers up to 500 ft. on a charge once per hour. A claw or bite hit allows a free trip attempt at +3. The single Attack row omits damage, which the matching Full Attack bite supplies. |
| Dire Shark | MonstersDi-Do.rtf, Dire Shark | Bite triggers Improved Grab. Swallow Whole uses a new grapple check and includes damage, gullet limits, and escape rules. |
| Giant Octopus | MonstersAnimals.rtf, Octopus, Giant | A tentacle can grab a target of any size. The block gives separate ink and jet timings, plus sunder and regrowth rules. The ink cloud has no printed duration. |
| Mule | MonstersAnimals.rtf, Mule | At most 230 lb. is a light load, 460 lb. a medium load, and 690 lb. a heavy load. The maximum drag load is 3,450 lb. |

I compared the records against the original Wizards RTF tables and combat text. The 17 February 2006 *Monster Manual* errata gives Deinonychus a different table with Medium size and 1d8+4 talons. The assigned SRD archive gives a Large form with 2d6+4 talons. The profile preserves the archive values and records the later correction as a source distinction. I checked the new Deinonychus portrait at full size and in a 96-pixel circle. It depicts a lean green pack raptor with a stiff tail and raised hind talons. All four reused portrait hashes match the roster, including the older orange-parchment Cheetah and Dire Shark.
