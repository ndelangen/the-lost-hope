Both badgers and the dire wolverine enter rage on the turn after taking damage in combat. Dire Badger's usable tunnel is specific to its own entry. Leopard gets two rake attacks on its Pounce under the individual rule, with regular grapple rakes subject to the shared timing rule. The ordinary Pony's printed hoof modifier is −3, and it cannot fight with a rider.

All five reused portraits matched the roster SHA-256 values. The dire badger's existing portrait has brighter orange paper than the other portraits. It remains the selected reuse asset.
