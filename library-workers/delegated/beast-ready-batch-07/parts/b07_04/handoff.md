# Revised v3.5 SRD animals

Five records checked against the original RTF archive. Each includes the table statistics and rules from the combat text. All five reuse the roster's existing portraits, with SHA-256 verified for each file.

| Record | CR | AC | HP | Combat details |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Ape | 2 | 14 | 29 | Two claws and a bite on a full attack. Can take 10 on Climb under threat. |
| Crocodile | 2 | 15 | 22 | Bite can trigger Improved Grab. Holds breath for 68 rounds. Submersion affects Hide. |
| Dire Weasel | 2 | 16 | 13 | Attaches on a bite hit. AC drops to 12 while attached and it deals 1d4 Constitution damage each round. The prose mentions clawing and the attack table lists only Bite. |
| Huge Shark | 4 | 15 | 65 | Uses the Huge column of the Shark table. Underwater Blindsense has a 30-ft. radius. Keen Scent has a 180-ft. radius and detects blood in the water up to 1 mile away. |
| Owl | 1/4 | 17 | 4 | Both talons count as one attack. Adds +8 to Spot in shadowy illumination. |

The original Huge Shark column gives Will +4 alongside the Iron Will feat. The d20srd.org mirror gives Will +6, which matches applying the feat. The staged rules use the original +4 and record the difference.
