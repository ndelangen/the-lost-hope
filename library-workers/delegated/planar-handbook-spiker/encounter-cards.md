# Spiker encounters

Both encounters take place on the Material Plane, where Spikers have the Extraplanar subtype. Both NPCs have darkvision 60 ft., acid resistance 5, and DR 2 bypassed by either piercing or slashing. Each is provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. The full builds include skills, saves, feats, and attack details. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Parry Millguard, Fighter 1

Parry holds the mill gate while the workers close the shutters. The party can guard the gate or check the service ladder.

- HP 12, AC 18 (touch 13, flat-footed 15), initiative +4, speed 30 ft.
- The chain shirt caps the Dexterity bonus to AC at +3 and applies a −4 armor check penalty. Reflex and initiative still use the full +4 Dexterity modifier. Without the shirt, AC drops to 15 and Climb rises from +2 to +6.
- In melee, attack with the longsword at +4 for 1d8+2 slashing or spikes at +3 for 1d6+2 piercing. A two-weapon full attack uses longsword +2 for 1d8+2 and light off-hand spikes +1 for 1d6+1. A thrown javelin attacks at +5 for 1d6+2 piercing.

## Grip Stonebrace, Monk 1

A broken grate threatens the cistern stairs. Grip holds the lower landing while the party guides workers to safety.

- HP 10, AC 14 (touch 13, flat-footed 13), initiative +1, speed 30 ft.
- Grip starts a grapple with a +3 melee touch attack. Improved Grapple prevents the target’s attack of opportunity and raises the opposed grapple modifier to +7.
- In an existing grapple, win the opposed check at +7 using Damage Your Opponent to deal 1d6+3 piercing with the spikes in place of unarmed damage. With Attack Your Opponent, roll the light spike attack at −1 against AC to deal the same damage. Holding a creature deals no automatic spike damage.
- Outside a grapple, Grip attacks at +3 with his unarmed strike (1d6+3 bludgeoning) or spikes (1d6+3 piercing). An unarmed flurry attacks at +1/+1.
