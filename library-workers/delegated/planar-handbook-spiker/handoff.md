# Spiker from Planar Handbook

This batch adds the D&D 3.5 Spiker from the July 2004 first printing and two original encounters. Wizards corrected the damage reduction to DR 2/piercing or slashing. Either damage type bypasses it. [Book, pp. 14–15]() · [Wizards errata and FAQ archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

Parry Millguard uses a chain shirt adjusted for a Spiker and can fight with a longsword and off-hand spikes. Grip Stonebrace demonstrates two ways to use spikes in a grapple. Each encounter is provisionally CR 1 and uses a level 1 build. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

Grip’s portrait keeps the full figure in frame on plain tan parchment. The temporary catalog found Spiker by name, rules text, and NPC role, returned both NPC builds from both CLI commands, and resolved the correct portrait bytes. The new profile is the sole Spiker version and supplies its list thumbnail.
