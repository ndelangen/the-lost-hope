# Killoren for D&D 3.5

One playable Killoren entry contains Ancient, Destroyer, and Hunter from the February 2005 first printing of *Races of the Wild*. These are three views of a species that chooses one active aspect each morning. Ancient is the default catalog view. The shared plain-parchment portrait depicts Irimel, an original Killoren in the Hunter aspect. [Playables, pp. 102–103]() · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg)

Ancient adds nature knowledge and increases the enchantment save bonus. Destroyer gains a limited smite against specific creature types. Hunter adds initiative, stealth, and perception bonuses. The three aspect feats in the book are optional. [Species rules](book-species.json) · [Feats, p. 151]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

**Irimel** is a Hunter Rogue who guides travellers through a flooded trail. **Daranvar** is a Destroyer Fighter who keeps a construct out of a seedling grove. Each appears only under the aspect used in their encounter, and each has provisional CR 1. The published CR 2 Traveller is a separate example.
