# Killoren encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Fey with 30-ft. land speed and low-light vision. They are immune to magical sleep, gain +2 on saves against enchantment effects, and take −2 on attack rolls with cold-iron weapons or ammunition. Each has one active aspect chosen that morning. Both are provisional CR 1 and use ECL equal to their class level. [Playable traits, p. 103]()

## Irimel, Hunter

Irimel needs to guide a last group of travellers around a flooded stretch. The old warning lines are still in place, and someone is calling from the fog beyond them.

Hide +8, Move Silently +8, Listen +6, Spot +6, Search +5, Disable Device +5, and Open Lock +6. The Hunter bonus is already included. Trapfinding and ordinary thieves' tools cover the old alarms.

- **Killoren Hunter feat**. Move action. Pinpoint a living creature within 30 ft. with line of effect. An unseen creature retains total concealment.
- **Rapier** +0 melee, 1d6 piercing, 18–20/×2. **Shortbow** +2 ranged, 1d6 piercing, ×3, 60-ft. range increment. Add 1d6 Sneak Attack on qualifying hits. Ranged Sneak Attack is limited to 30 ft.

## Daranvar, Destroyer

A stray construct is heading into a seedling grove. Daranvar can hold the crossing while the party looks for its maker's mark.

Climb +4 and Jump +4. Chain shirt, longspear, and club. Hold the crossing with the spear and use the club in adjacent squares.

- **Longspear** +4 melee, 1d8+3 piercing, ×3, 10-ft. reach. **Club** +3 melee, 1d6+2 bludgeoning, ×2, 5-ft. reach.
- **Destroyer smite**. Two uses per day, at most one per hour. Longspear +6 for 1d8+4 or club +5 for 1d6+3. Valid targets are aberrations, constructs, humanoids, oozes, outsiders, and undead. An invalid target expends the use.
- **Killoren Destroyer feat**. An eligible target hit by the smite must make a DC 12 Will save or be dazed for one round. [Aspect feats, p. 151]()
