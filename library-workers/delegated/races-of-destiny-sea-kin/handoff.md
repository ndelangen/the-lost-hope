# Sea Kin for D&D 3.5

One playable Sea Kin entry from the December 2004 first printing of *Races of Destiny*. Sea Kin have a 30-ft. swim speed and breathe air. Hold Breath gives eight rounds per point of Constitution, with the core Swim rule spending an extra round for standard and full-round actions. [Printed traits, p. 102]() · [Core Swim rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/skills/swim.htm) · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Water Dependency calls for full-body immersion at least weekly. The printed rule specifies water without a salinity qualifier. After a week, missed immersion calls for a daily Constitution check. Full reimmersion restores 1d4 lost Constitution per hour while the character remains in water. [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

The full-body plain-parchment portrait depicts **Neressa**, an original basin healer and Druid. **Emmoran** is an original tide gate warden and Fighter. Both are provisional CR 1. The cards give separate breath budgets, underwater trident use, a shore-only net in Emmoran's encounter, and Neressa's prepared spells.
