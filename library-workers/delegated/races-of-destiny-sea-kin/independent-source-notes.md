# Independent checks

The source review checked the December 2004 first printing. The contents point to p. 99, while the Sea Kin entry starts on p. 100. Playable traits are on printed p. 102, viewer p. 103. Water Dependency requires water and gives no minimum duration or salinity. The recovery line gives a rate per hour of immersion. The source supplies no water breathing, racial ability adjustment, level adjustment, or statted encounter NPC. The publisher's collected errata contains no *Races of Destiny* entry.
