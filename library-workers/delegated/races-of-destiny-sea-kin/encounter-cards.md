# Sea Kin encounter cards

Both original NPCs are Medium Humanoids with the Human subtype, low-light vision, and land and swim speeds of 30 ft. Constitution 14 gives each **112 rounds of held breath**. Each standard or full-round action taken while holding breath spends one extra round. Require full-body immersion at least weekly. Both NPCs are provisional CR 1 and use ECL equal to class level. They can take 10 on Swim checks for hazards and special actions even when threatened.

## Neressa, tidal basin healer

Crews can no longer draw safe water from the tidal basin. Neressa can check a sample and tend the sick while the party examines the intake.

Heal +10, Knowledge (nature) +7, Survival +9, Concentration +6, Spellcraft +5, and Escape Artist +3. Leather armor, druidic focus, and a two-handed spear. Wild Empathy +0. Neressa's animal companion is away for this encounter.

- **Spear** +0 melee, 1d8 piercing, critical ×3, 5-ft. reach.
- **Prepared cantrips.** *Guidance* gives one +1 competence bonus on an attack, save, or skill check within a minute. *Know direction* gives north. *Purify food and drink* treats up to one cubic foot of food or water. Use it on a sample or on the crew's immediate drinking supply.
- **Prepared 1st-level spells.** *Cure light wounds* heals 1d8+1 HP to a living creature touched. *Speak with animals* lasts one minute. Either slot can be converted to *summon nature's ally I*. A porpoise is an option in suitable water for one round. Neressa casts from the surface in this encounter. [Druid spellcasting](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/druid.htm) · [Summon Nature's Ally I](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/summonNaturesAllyI.htm)

## Emmoran, tidal lock warden

The tide gate is jammed and the returning boats are approaching. Emmoran can reach the submerged catch if the party keeps the dry platform clear.

Climb +4, Jump +4, Escape Artist +1. Studded leather, light wooden shield, trident, and net. The Swim total includes the doubled armor check penalty.

- **Trident** +4 melee, 1d8+2 piercing, critical ×2, 5-ft. reach. Emmoran uses this attack underwater with his swim speed.
- **Net** +2 ranged touch, maximum 10 ft., for targets from Small to Large. A hit entangles. The target takes −2 to attack rolls and −4 Dexterity, travels at half speed, and cannot run or charge. Escape Artist DC 20 or Strength DC 25 as a full-round action. Use the net on the dry platform against a target above water. [Underwater combat](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/wilderness.htm#underwaterCombat) · [Net](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/equipment/weapons.htm#net)
