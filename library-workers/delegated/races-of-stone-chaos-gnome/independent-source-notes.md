# Independent Chaos Gnome reviews

The source reviewer checked the August 2004 first printing. The description on pp. 86–87 gives the imago a slight build, bright red-to-blond hair, eyes that change shade with mood, and irregular beadwork on bright clothes. Ron Spencer drew the unnamed Chaos Gnome on p. 87. The playable traits span pp. 87–88. The reviewer found no Chaos Gnome stat block in the book or title-specific correction in Wizards’ archive. [Original art and rules]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Luck of Chaos works on the gnome’s own roll before its outcome is announced. The trait requires that the second result stand and names no specific die type or action. The racial caster-level bonus applies to spells with the chaos descriptor. *Entropic shield* retains CL 1. Charisma 10 or higher grants the other three spell-like abilities. The build review checked the Sorcerer’s spells known and slots, the Cleric’s preparation and turn undead, skill budgets, and the conditional defenses. The same protection spell runs at CL 2 for Orrin and CL 3 for Pella because the Chaos domain supplies a second +1 bonus. The two builds resolve under the same publication-specific profile.
