# Chaos Gnome for D&D 3.5

One playable Chaos Gnome, also called imago, from the August 2004 first printing of *Races of Stone*. Luck of Chaos lets the gnome reroll one of their own rolls once per day before the outcome is announced. They keep the second result. The profile includes immunity to confusion, racial Spell Power for chaos-descriptor spells, and level adjustment +1. [Original traits, pp. 87–88]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Orrin Redwick is an original Sorcerer 1 and lantern mender. His *protection from law* lasts 2 minutes at caster level 2. Pella Reedchime is an original Cleric 1 and roadside mediator. Her Chaos domain raises the same spell to caster level 3 for 3 minutes. Both use *entropic shield* at caster level 1 and are provisional CR 2.
