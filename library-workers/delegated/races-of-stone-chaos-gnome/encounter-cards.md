# Chaos Gnome encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. Each is a Small humanoid with the gnome subtype, 20-ft. land speed, low-light vision, and immunity to confusion effects. Each has one class Hit Die and level adjustment +1. [Original rules, pp. 87–88]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Orrin Redwick, Sorcerer 1

Provisional CR 2, ECL 2. AC 14, touch 14, flat-footed 11. HP 6, initiative +3. Fort +2, Ref +3, Will +2. BAB +0, grapple −5. STR 8, DEX 16, CON 14, INT 12, WIS 10, CHA 16. Common, Gnome, Elven.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small light crossbow | +4 ranged, 1d6 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment |
| Small dagger | +0 melee, 1d3−1 piercing or slashing, minimum 1, 19–20/×2 |
| *Ray of frost* | +4 ranged touch, 1d3 cold, range 25 ft. |

Concentration +6, Craft (paperworking) +5, Listen +2, Spellcraft +8. Familiar slot open. Against Giant-type creatures AC 18 and touch 18 while able to dodge. *Mage armor* lasts 1 hour and raises ordinary AC to 18, giant-only AC to 22, and flat-footed AC to 15.

Base caster level 1, spell DC 13 + spell level. Five daily 0-level slots and four daily 1st-level slots. Known at level 0 are *detect magic*, *light*, *message*, and *ray of frost*. Known at level 1 are *mage armor* and *protection from law*. Racial Spell Power raises *protection from law* to CL 2 for 2 minutes.

Orrin needs a dry workbench to salvage the paper lanterns before the procession. He can repair the lanterns if the party keeps the work area clear.

## Pella Reedchime, Cleric 1

Provisional CR 2, ECL 2. AC 18, touch 12, flat-footed 17. HP 10, initiative +1. Fort +4, Ref +1, Will +4. BAB +0, grapple −4. STR 10, DEX 12, CON 14, INT 10, WIS 15, CHA 12. Common and Gnome.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Small light mace | +1 melee, 1d4 bludgeoning, ×2 |
| Small dart | +2 ranged, 1d3 piercing, ×2, 20-ft. increment |

Concentration +6, Diplomacy +8, Listen +4. Against Giant-type creatures AC 22 and touch 16 while able to dodge. Turn undead 4/day, check 1d20+1, turning damage 2d6+2.

Base caster level 1, spell DC 12 + spell level. Prepare *detect magic*, *guidance*, *light*, *bless*, and *cure light wounds*. The first-level domain slot holds *protection from law*. Chaos and Good domains add +1 caster level to their respective descriptors. Racial Spell Power and the Chaos domain combine for CL 3 and a 3-minute *protection from law*. [3.5 SRD, Cleric domains](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spellLists/clericDomains.htm#chaosDomain) · [*Protection from law*](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/protectionFromLaw.htm)

Pella has stopped both wagons at a bridge to settle the damaged-cargo dispute. She asks the party to count the goods in front of both drivers. If a lawful summoned guard appears, she casts *protection from law* on the person closest to it.

## Shared racial abilities

**Luck of Chaos.** Once per day, reroll a roll you just made before the DM announces success or failure. Keep the second result.

**Spell-like abilities.** *Entropic shield* once per day, CL 1, lasts 1 minute and gives ranged attacks that require attack rolls a 20% miss chance. Both meet Charisma 10 and can also use *daze*, *flare*, and *prestidigitation* once per day each at CL 1. Their *daze* Will saves and *flare* Fortitude saves are DC 13 for Orrin and DC 11 for Pella. [Printed abilities, p. 88]()

## Protection from law

Both versions grant +2 deflection AC and +2 resistance on saves against lawful creatures’ attacks or effects. The spell protects against possession and suppresses ongoing mental control. It also blocks contact by lawful summoned creatures. That contact ward ends if the protected creature attacks a blocked creature or pushes the barrier against it. Spell resistance can bypass the contact ward.
