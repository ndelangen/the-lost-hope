# Southlands Heroes and Unlikely Heroes

Four playable profiles, four encounter NPCs, and four square portraits. The profiles use the printed 2015 and 2016 player rules. The NPCs use the 2014 class rules with fixed HP gains after level 1. [Southlands Heroes, pp. 7–9](https://anyflip.com/fgoih/xnbq/basic) · [Unlikely Heroes, pp. 10–17](https://anyflip.com/lphgc/hojw/basic) · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Profile | Encounter NPC | Rule in play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hiveless Tosculi, pp. 7–9 | Sivi Amberwing, advance scout | Each round of gliding requires a drop of at least one foot for every four feet flown. |
| Jinnborn, pp. 10–12 | Narel Claywind, kiln keeper and caravan escort | Earth Shapers resist acid and can add acid damage to a melee hit. |
| Ramag, pp. 16–17 | Vey Tern, checkpoint guide | Spell Damping covers Strength and Dexterity saves against spells. Feather fall comes from the Wizard class. |
| Kijani, pp. 12–14 | Kesh Reedroot, trail sentinel | Contained Fury applies below half HP. Second Wind can take Kesh out of that range. |

The Tosculi NPC applies the standard Strength modifier to the printed claw damage die. The Kijani portrait follows the approved vine-and-bark brief, with muted green foliage over weathered bark. The revised Tosculi portrait shows all four arms. The earlier satchel version is kept in drafts. The Jinnborn’s earthen skin texture is subtle at thumbnail size.

I checked the NPC numbers by hand and inspected each 1254 × 1254 portrait at full size and in the 96 px strip. The strip starts with Big Linda. In-app search and portrait checks are pending in the separate integration copy.
