# Satarre, Lamia, and Mushroomfolk

- **Satarre.** Vesk Nhal uses the Unbound Satarre rules from *Book of Ebon Tides*, pp. 38–39, under the existing Satarre name. The example uses Portal Sense and Unraveling Touch. [Original book](https://anyflip.com/qkxdf/sqvy/basic/)
- **Lamia.** Tara Embercoil uses the serpentine player rules from *Unlikely Heroes*, pp. 14–16. The last four traits appear at the start of p. 16. [Original book]()
- **Mushroomfolk.** Nerin Alderfold uses Morel from *Underworld Player’s Guide*, pp. 25–27. The profile also includes Acid Cap and Favored. [Original book]()

Each build starts from the 2014 standard array. Vesk uses Fighter 2 and has AC 13, 22 HP, and DC 13 for Unraveling Touch. Tara and Nerin use Rogue 2 and have 17 HP each. Tara has AC 13 and retains her climb and swim speeds. Nerin has AC 14 and Morel’s 35-ft. walking speed.

All three original portraits are 1254 × 1254 PNGs. At full size and at 96 px against Big Linda, the entire figures stay readable on warm plain parchment, with only a faint shadow under each. The review image shows Big Linda, Satarre, Lamia, and Mushroomfolk in that order.
