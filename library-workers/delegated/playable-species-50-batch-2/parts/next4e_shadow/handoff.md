# Fourth-edition Shadow and Fey player options

The five rows preserve the publications and edition in the batch roster. Each playable profile links to an original level 1 NPC with its relevant racial actions and NPC class powers ready to run. The NPCs use the *Dungeon Master’s Guide* classed NPC rules on pages 187–188 and *Player’s Handbook* class powers. The source records carry the printed player-option pages and scan links.

| Player option | NPC | Racial rule used in play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shade | Marren Sill, lantern-room infiltrator | Hides behind cover from an ally with One with Shadow |
| Vryloka | Orla Kest, gallery escort | Triggers Lifeblood when she bloodies or kills an enemy |
| Revenant | Iser Mott, last-stand courier | Can act while dazed and dying, with normal death saves |
| Hamadryad | Tavia Burr, grove negotiator | Uses Wooden Form to resist 5 damage |
| Shadar-kai | Nesha Flint, passage scout | Teleports 3 squares with Shadow Jaunt and briefly becomes insubstantial |

The comparison sheet shows Big Linda followed by the five portraits in table order.
