# Orc’s Head Peninsula playable profiles

Cayma, Ee’aar, Enduk, Gurrash and Manscorpion each have one AD&D 2e playable profile, one linked classed NPC and one original square ImageGen portrait. The portraits use Big Linda’s muted natural pigments, a faint contact shadow and plain warm tan parchment.

I checked the racial adjustments, class limits and thief modifiers in TSR’s original 1996 RTF, then compared each build with its player-character section. Page numbers in the evidence refer to the later Pandius reflow. The NPCs use the core class THAC0 and saving throws, applicable Savage Coast kits, and player flight speeds. The setting’s companion appendix fills in ground movement and the Manscorpion’s sting damage and infravision range. The threshold guard uses the Um-Shedu sleep venom. Its 10-foot tail reach is an encounter ruling.

The five NPCs are Tess Reedknot, a culvert scout, Ilyra Serein, a ridge messenger, Sarruk Reedhorn, an escort, Vorr Deepwake, a marsh-channel guard, and Tamar of Um-Shedu, a threshold guard. Their ability scores include racial adjustments. Portraits and references are collected in the manifests next to this handoff.

I reviewed the artwork at portrait and thumbnail sizes and checked the build numbers by hand. The files are staged locally for the parent’s temporary-copy integration and search checks.
