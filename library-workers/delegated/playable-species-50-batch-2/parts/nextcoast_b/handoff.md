# Savage Coast playable species

Verified Phanaton, Shazak, and Wallara in the 1996 TSR Orc’s Head RTF. Verified Lupin and Rakasta in the original TSR Campaign Book Part 2 RTF. The source records give section names from those files and page numbers from the Pandius reflow.

The five 1st-level NPCs use descending AC and the original class tables. Makurí meets the Phanaton chapter’s Dexterity requirement of 16 and glides with ordinary gear. Werrami can vanish and gains blending at 3rd level. Célan Merle uses the Lupin ranger’s Wisdom +6 tracking rule. Sava Flintstep rolls two war-claw attacks at the same THAC0. The sample builds use the classed NPC rules and read base movement from the setting’s companion monster appendix. The samples with potential exposure to the Red Curse grew up and are encountered outside affected lands.

Each portrait was generated individually. I used Big Linda for the parchment, lighting, and paint treatment. The final Wallara is a tall, slender humanoid with faint scale bands and head hair, following the source’s description. I checked that each subject and its gear fit the square frame and remained legible at thumbnail size.
