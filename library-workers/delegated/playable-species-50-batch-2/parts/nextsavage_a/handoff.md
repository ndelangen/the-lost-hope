# Savage Species portraits and NPCs

Five original portraits and five linked NPC builds are staged here. All five builds use the 3.0 monster-class tables in *Savage Species*. [Savage Species, Appendix 1]()

| Profile | NPC | Monster-class level | Printed CR | Rules used |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Aranea | Tess Serein | 5 | 3 | Hybrid and human forms, web 3/day, 2nd-level sorcerer casting |
| Annis Hag | Marda Flintknuckle | 11 | 4 | Large size, Improved Grab and Rend |
| Barghest | Nark Bluefur | 2 | 2 | Goblin and wolf forms and the player version of Feed |
| Djinni | Safa Crosswind | 4 | 2 | Perfect flight, self-only invisibility and whirlwind 1/day |
| Drider | Orel Slate | 6 | 3 | 4th-level sorcerer casting with web, a climb speed and spell resistance |

The Aranea web and Djinni whirlwind use the detailed 2000 *Monster Manual* rules where their class entries refer to that book. The Drider example follows the class prose's two starting feats. Its web comes from sorcerer casting. [Monster Manual (2000)]()

Each ImageGen original is 1254 × 1254 and was reviewed against Big Linda at full size and at 96 × 96. The Aranea is shown in spider form and the Barghest in wolf form. [Portrait review](portrait-review.png)
