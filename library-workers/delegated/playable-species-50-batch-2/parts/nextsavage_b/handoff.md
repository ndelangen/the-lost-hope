# Five Savage Species portraits and builds

Each 3.0 profile uses its monster class from *Savage Species*. The linked encounter uses the printed CR for its tier.

| Species | Original NPC | Published tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Efreeti | Kesh Brassmark, quartermaster | ECL 3. Produce flame and pyrotechnics at will. Kesh relays orders by telepathy. |
| Flamebrother Salamander | Sava Coil, foundry guard | ECL 4. Heat adds 1d4 fire, and the tail can constrict. |
| Athach | Tov Flintarm, quarry guard | ECL 3. Three weapon arms, plus a secondary poisonous bite. |
| Grig | Fenn Burr, woodland guide | ECL 4. Attuned fiddle, half a Hit Die, and spell resistance 17. |
| Harpy | Ryn Reedcall, lookout | ECL 4. A 40-ft. fly speed and a 10-ft. captivating song. |

The Athach level-2 row appears to have a Fortitude typo. Tov uses the level-3 row. The Harpy Siren example supplies the effects of captivation. Ryn uses the player progression for the song’s range, save DC, and duration.

The 96-pixel comparison places approved Big Linda alongside the five portraits. All five have isolated silhouettes on warm parchment. The Athach’s third arm, the Flamebrother’s coiled tail, the Grig’s fiddle and cricket legs, and the Harpy’s wings and talons remain visible.
