# Kobold Press player profiles

Five source profiles, five original NPCs and five square portraits are ready for the parent batch.

| Catalog entry | Player rules | Example | Manual check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alseid | Margreve Player’s Guide, pp. 8–9 | Vaethilalora (Vael), Fighter 2 | 14 AC, 20 HP, shortbow +7. Quadruped movement and Monstrosity type match the print. |
| Erina | Margreve Player’s Guide, pp. 9–11 | Oolay, Rogue 2 | 14 AC, 15 HP, dagger +5. Spines trigger on Oolay’s turn and the 20 ft. burrow applies to earth and sand. |
| Piney | Margreve Player’s Guide, pp. 11–13 | Hushush, Ranger 1 | 12 AC, 12 HP, quarterstaff +2. Hushush uses the adventuring Piney traits and forest Natural Explorer. |
| Ravenfolk | Book of Ebon Tides, pp. 37–38 | Lampnote, Rogue 2 | 14 AC, 15 HP, shortsword +5. Lampnote uses the Sublime option and its printed DC 13 chord. |
| Trollkin | Midgard Heroes Handbook, pp. 29–30 | Split-Tusk, Fighter 2 | 12 AC including Thick Hide, 22 HP, natural weapons +5. Inhuman Vigor spends one Hit Die. |

The NPCs use the 2014 class rules. Humanoid is the working type for Erina, Ravenfolk and Trollkin because the player trait lists leave it implicit. Oolay chooses Wisdom and Common. The Piney portrait uses barklike skin. Split-Tusk uses Stonehide.

I reviewed the full images and thumbnails. The square portraits show the full figures against plain parchment. The Alseid’s four hooves, Erina’s spines, Piney’s bark grain, Ravenfolk’s beak and Trollkin’s rough hide are visible. Combined preview in [review-strip.png](review-strip.png).
