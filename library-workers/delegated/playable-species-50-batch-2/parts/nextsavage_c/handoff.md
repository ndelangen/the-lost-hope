The Medusa feat count follows the prose, which specifies two at 1st level. Table A–34 marks one feat. The Sea Hag’s Horrific Appearance deals 1 point of Strength damage at level 2. Later text and table entries disagree about when Evil Eye begins, so Alda uses the earlier tier. The Lillend build uses 3.0 bardic music and spells, with flight unlocked at class level 6.

Five 1254 × 1254 PNG originals use warm, plain parchment. The review sheet puts Big Linda first, followed by Kyton, Lillend, Medusa, Nixie, and Sea Hag. The figures and their key anatomy are legible at thumbnail size.
