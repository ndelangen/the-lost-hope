# Four Savage Species profiles

| Playable profile | Original NPC | Class level | CR | Printed rule |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Umber Hulk | Rusk, barricade keeper | 9 | 4 | [pp. 201–202, Table A–51]() |
| Air Elemental | Skirl, beacon tender | 5 | 2 | [pp. 152–153, Table A–1]() |
| Imp | Tack, eavesdropper | 4 | 2 | [pp. 179–180, Table A–29]() |
| Rakshasa | Varek Silt, claims agent | 8 | 6 | [pp. 191–192, Table A–41]() |

The profiles use the player progressions in *Savage Species* (2003), with class level and Hit Dice recorded separately. Rusk follows the printed table for the final tremorsense tier because the surrounding prose points to a level beyond the class. Skirl’s Medium build uses the shared Air Elemental progression. Tack uses the only alternate form granted at his tier. Varek uses 4th-level sorcerer casting and the spell immunity and blessed-crossbow-bolt weakness printed in the original rules.

The proof sheet shows Big Linda first, followed by the four new 1254 × 1254 originals in table order. Each silhouette was checked at full size and at 96 px. Each original has its own checksum in the portrait manifest.
