# Shoal Halfling encounters

Both NPCs are Small Humanoids with the Aquatic and Halfling subtypes, provisionally CR 1 and ECL 1. They breathe air and water, walk and swim 20 ft., and can take 10 on Swim checks for hazards even under pressure. Their listed saves already include +1 racial. Add +2 morale against fear. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Tess Siltstep, Rogue 1

Tess swims through the low sluice and checks the lock from the far side. If something follows, she climbs onto the dry step before using her sling.

- HP 8, AC 17, initiative +4. Fortitude +3, Reflex +7, Will +1. Swim +12, Listen +6.
- Sling +5 for 1d3 bludgeoning, or +6 for 1d3+1 within 30 ft. with Point Blank Shot. The attack bonus includes +1 for Small size.
- Dagger +1 for 1d3 piercing or slashing. Add 1d6 sneak attack against an eligible target without concealment. Ranged sneak attacks are limited to targets within 30 ft.
- Trapfinding. Search +6, Disable Device +6. Climb +4, Jump −2, and Move Silently +8 use no racial skill bonus.

## Nerin Reefbrace, Fighter 1

Nerin holds the flooded arch while the crew opens the route to the sandbar. He keeps his shortspear in melee until everyone can surface.

- HP 12, AC 16, initiative +3. Fortitude +5, Reflex +4, Will +3. Swim +14, Listen +2.
- Shortspear +5 in melee for 1d4+2 piercing. Above water he throws it at +6 for 1d4+2. Both attacks include Weapon Focus and the Small size bonus.
