# Shoal Halfling source check

The August 2005 first printing gives the Shoal Halfling on pp. 45–46. The table on p. 34 lists LA +0. The entry inherits the Player’s Handbook Halfling except for the listed changes. The +1 racial bonus on all saves, the stacking +2 morale bonus against fear, and +2 racial Listen remain. The separate +1 to thrown weapon and sling attacks and the racial Climb, Jump, and Move Silently bonuses are removed. Both builds apply the normal Small size modifier to attacks. [Stormwrack player rules]() · [Core Halfling traits](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/races.htm#halflings)

Pages 213 and 217 give a Shoal Halfling patrol with group EL 9. The two staged NPCs are original first-level builds with provisional individual CR 1. Independent review checked their saves, armor, ranks, attack bonuses, and exact publication scope. Wizards’ collected archive has no Stormwrack errata file, and the June 2008 FAQ has no Shoal Halfling entry.
