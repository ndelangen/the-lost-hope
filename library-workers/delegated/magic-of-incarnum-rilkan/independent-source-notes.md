# Rilkan source and review notes

The September 2005 first printing of *Magic of Incarnum*, ISBN 0-7869-3701-7, gives the playable traits on pp. 12–13. The scale bands sit on otherwise human-like necks and forearms. Mark Poole illustrated a pair on p. 13 and the bard Tarrenta Willet on p. 14. [Original printing]()

Substitution levels are independent. The first-level option reduces later sneak attack by one die. Taking only the third-level option grants bardic knowledge and a bonus feat in place of trap sense, with the usual sneak attack progression. The third-level option permanently removes trap sense from Rogue levels. [Substitution rules, pp. 41–42]() · [Rilkan Rogue, p. 47]()

The published Rogue 3 uses the first and third substitutions but omits its bardic knowledge line. The published Incarnate 4 shapes four soulmelds, so the racial formula gives it +3 competence to Knowledge checks. Its printed prose says +1. The staged Incarnate uses the racial formula, with +2 for two shaped soulmelds. [Published examples, pp. 189–191]()

An independent integration review checked the two skill budgets and combat stats, the flanking conditions, the Incarnate’s two legal unbound melds, publication scoping, and the expected thumbnail. The final patch was tested against the committed baseline containing the preceding Azurin batch.
