# Rilkan encounters

Both NPCs are D&D 3.5, provisional CR 1 and ECL 1. When they succeed at Aid Another to assist each other’s roll, the bonus is +3. When both flank the same target, Kesra’s Improved Flanking gives each +4 to their melee attacks. A non-Rilkan flanking with either still gets the ordinary +2. [Racial Aid, pp. 12–13]() · [Improved Flanking, p. 47]()

## Kesra Tarn, warehouse roof scout

Chaotic good Rogue 1, first-level Rilkan substitution. HP 8, AC 16 (touch 13, flat-footed 13), speed 30 ft., initiative +7. Fort +2, Ref +5, Will +0.

- Rapier +1 melee, 1d6+1 piercing, 18–20/×2. While flanking, +5 to hit.
- Light crossbow +3 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, range increment 80 ft. Reload as a move action.
- Trapfinding. Search +5, Disable Device +5, Open Lock +7. Bluff and Diplomacy +7. All Knowledge checks +2 and treated as trained. This build gains sneak attack at Rogue 3.

Kesra has spotted the apprentices on the wrong side of the warehouse doors. She asks the party to watch the alley so she can open the roof hatch. If the cargo inspector reaches her, she asks him to take the far side of the guard. [Rilkan Rogue, p. 47]()

## Bren Carrow, quay cargo inspector

Lawful neutral Incarnate 1. HP 8, AC 17 (touch 12, flat-footed 15), speed 30 ft., initiative +2. Fort +4, Ref +2, Will +4. Detect chaos at will through Detect Opposition.

- Morningstar +0 melee, 1d8 bludgeoning and piercing, ×2. When he and Kesra flank the same foe, +4 to hit.
- Javelin +2 ranged, 1d6 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft.
- **Silvertongue Mask** and **Dissolving Spittle** stay shaped and unbound in the brow and throat respectively. Move the single point between them as a swift action. The mask starts invested. His Knowledge (arcana and the planes) stays +7 and all other Knowledge checks stay +3.

| Where the point goes | Bluff and Diplomacy | Dissolving Spittle |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Silvertongue Mask | +7 | 1d6 acid |
| Dissolving Spittle | +5 | 2d6 acid |

Dissolving Spittle is a standard action, +2 ranged touch at up to 30 ft., with no save. It provokes attacks of opportunity. Bren handles the manifests with the mask invested. If he needs to take a flank with Kesra, he draws his morningstar and moves into melee. [Incarnate, p. 21]() · [Dissolving Spittle, pp. 64–65]() · [Silvertongue Mask, p. 86]()
