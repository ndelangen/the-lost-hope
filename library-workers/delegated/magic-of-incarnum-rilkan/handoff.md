# Rilkan for D&D 3.5

Rilkans treat every Knowledge check as trained. The racial competence bonus starts at +1 and rises by +1 for every two soulmelds currently shaped. It applies to bardic knowledge too. Rilkan-to-Rilkan Aid Another gives +3 to the ally’s roll. The species has no innate essentia pool. [Original traits, pp. 12–13]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Kesra Tarn**, a Rogue scouting a warehouse roof, keeps trapfinding and trades first-level sneak attack for improved flanking. She and other Rilkans flanking the same target each get +4 to attack.
- **Bren Carrow**, an Incarnate inspecting cargo, can shift his one point of essentia between Silvertongue Mask and Dissolving Spittle. Both melds stay shaped in either setup, so his racial Knowledge bonus stays at +2.

Both are provisional CR 1. Kesra’s full-body portrait shows the bands of pebbly, multicolored scales on her neck and forearms. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Portrait](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Parchment comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [96 px preview](portrait-thumbnails.jpg)
