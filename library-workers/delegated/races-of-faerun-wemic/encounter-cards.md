# Wemic encounters

Both are Large, with 10-ft. space, 5-ft. reach, 60-ft. darkvision, and +4 natural armor. They add +8 racial on Jump, in addition to the core bonus for their land speed.

## Tavaro Sunmane, gully guide

**5 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 3.** AC 15, HP 41, initiative +2, speed 40 ft. Fortitude +6, Reflex +6, Will +5. Grapple +15. A full attack uses two claws at +10 for 1d6+6 each.

Tavaro takes a 20-ft. running start and uses Jump +26 against DC 20 to cross the 20-ft. gap. The run-up and jump spend 40 ft. of movement. He anchors the rope after reaching the far side.

## Nasha Reedstep, ford guard

**Barbarian 1 with 5 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 4.** AC 19, HP 51, initiative +6, speed 50 ft. Fortitude +6, Reflex +6, Will +5. Grapple +17 and Jump +30.

Nasha attacks at +12 with the battleaxe while moving. A full attack uses the battleaxe at +12/+7 for 2d6+7 and one secondary claw at +7 for 1d6+3. During her eight-round rage, AC drops to 17 and she gains 12 HP. Her full attack becomes battleaxe +14/+9 for 2d6+9 and claw +9 for 1d6+4. When rage ends, she loses the extra HP and becomes fatigued for the rest of the encounter.
