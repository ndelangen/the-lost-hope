# Wemic

This batch adds the playable Wemic from the March 2003 *Races of Faerûn*, using 3.5 core movement and the 2004 conversion’s space and reach. Tavaro Sunmane and Nasha Reedstep are original NPCs. Tavaro’s plain-parchment portrait represents the species profile.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and both encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/wemic-draft.png). The [comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Tavaro beside Big Linda.

In a temporary copy, search found Wemic by name, Jump, its secondary claw, and both NPC roles. Tavaro’s portrait loaded on the profile and as its list thumbnail. Both catalog commands returned the complete builds for that publication. [Verification](verification.json)
