# Monstrous Races player profiles

Five profiles use Tyler Kamstra’s *Monstrous Races* for 2014 fifth edition, published on DMs Guild. I read the v2 rules and used the 2014 class and spell rules for the original NPCs. Each build links to the exact playable publication and edition. [v2 rules text]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 SRD]()

| Profile | Printed v2 pages | NPC and manual checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empyrean | 76–77 | Lark Halewake, Thief Rogue 3. AC 14 from leather and Dexterity. HP 21 from 9 + 6 + 6. The rapier is +5 to hit and Lark has unlocked the racial *bless*. |
| Ettin | 78 | Reed-and-Cairn, Barbarian 1. AC 13 and HP 14 from Unarmored Defense and Constitution. The greataxe is +5 to hit, with +3 damage normally and +5 during rage. Passive Perception is 13 or 18 while both heads are awake. |
| Faerie Dragon | 79 | Fig Whistletip, Rogue 1. AC 13, HP 9, and Euphoria Breath DC 11. The printed rule makes the bite eligible for finesse and Sneak Attack. Fig wears no armor and carries no tools or weapons. |
| Flameskull | 80 | Venn Cinderword, Fiend Warlock 2. AC 15 with *mage armor* through Armor of Shadows or 12 without it. HP 15 from 9 + 6. Warlock spell attack +4 and racial *fire bolt* +3. |
| Flumph | 81–82 | Ripple-under-Stone, Rogue 1. AC 12, HP 10, passive Perception 16, and Stench Spray DC 12. The printed size is Small. The roster’s Tiny descriptor conflicts with the printed trait. |

The Ettin trait block leaves out the darkvision discussed in the design notes. Both the Faerie Dragon bite and Flumph tendrils have explicit finesse eligibility. For the split tendril damage, I applied the Dexterity modifier and any qualifying Sneak Attack to piercing. The Flameskull treats Rejuvenation as stabilization at 0 HP. Armor of Shadows dispenses with the material component for *mage armor*, and the supplement’s handless rule permits the somatic component. I used the source’s complete Tiny character rules for the Faerie Dragon and Flameskull.
