# Galeb Duhr through Grick

Five player profiles use Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races* v2 and 2014 fifth-edition class rules. The printed trait blocks govern the builds. [V2 rules text]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Gargoyle | 88 | Rusk Lintel, Fighter 1. Natural AC 14. HP 12. Archery longbow +6, 1d8 + 2. |
| Gibbering Mouther | 100–101 | Hush-of-Five, Barbarian 1. Unarmored AC 14. HP 15. Spittle DC 13 and Gibber DC 11. |
| Grell | 109 | Keth Quiet-Beak, Barbarian 1. Unarmored AC 13. HP 13. Beak +5, 1d8 + 3 or +5 while raging. |
| Grick | 110 | Slant, Monk 1. AC 14 with either unarmored formula. HP 9. Natural strike +5, 1d8 + 3. The monk bonus strike uses 1d4 + 3 bludgeoning. |

Galeb Duhr can start rolling or use the rolling Dash follow-up on a turn when its bonus action is free. The follow-up needs movement already in progress. For both stone elementals, False Appearance uses concentration during the one-minute setup and ends when they move.

The Gargoyle uses the trait block's 30-ft. walk and poison immunity. The Galeb Duhr and Grell omit Mold Earth and lightning resistance, respectively, because those appear only in design notes. The Gibbering Mouther likewise follows its printed player traits. The worked Gibber includes Hush if it can hear its own voice. The source gives no explicit exception for the user of the ability.

The three handless builds use natural attacks or the monk's ordinary body strike. Rusk is the only NPC shown carrying a manufactured weapon. I checked the builds by hand and reviewed all five original 1254-square images at full size and at 96 px against Big Linda.
