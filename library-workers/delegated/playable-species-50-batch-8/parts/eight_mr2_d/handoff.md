# Five Monstrous Races 2 profiles

These are Tyler Kamstra's player conversions for 2014 fifth edition. The original encounter stat blocks and optional design changes were outside the rules used for the builds. The consulted mirror has the sequel title and printed folios. It gives no revision number. [Consulted text]() · [DMs Guild listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/en/product/248038/monstrous-races-2)

| Profile | Printed rules | Original NPC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ki-rin, also Kirin | p. 45, Ki-rin Traits | Tavir Rainbell, Draconic Sorcerer 1. AC 15, HP 9, Charisma DC 13. His racial Sacred Flame and class spell loadout need no material components. |
| Korred | pp. 46–47, Korred Traits and Control Hair | Reva Flintcoil, Fighter 1. AC 15, HP 12, rope DC 11. Her handaxe uses Dueling for 1d6+5 damage with a further +1 from Stone's Strength when active. |
| Leucrotta | p. 48, Leucrotta Traits | Neral Ashcall, Barbarian 1. AC 14, HP 14, mimicry DC 10. Use background languages because the source's Languages sentence stops before naming any. |
| Meenlock | p. 49, Meenlock Traits | Orr Mosswisp, Rogue 2. AC 13, HP 17, paralysis DC 12. The player text explicitly qualifies the claw for Sneak Attack and omits the natural armor discussed in design notes. |
| Mindwitness | pp. 53–54, Mindwitness Traits and p. 97, spell entries | Ixel Waker, Barbarian 2. AC 14, HP 21, Intelligence DC 13. Mind Spike uses the supplement's cantrip. The eye rays and Rage are separate tactical choices. |

The player Korred size is Medium and the block grants no burrow speed. Reva uses the printed traits and carries a one-handed melee axe. The racial Rock attack leaves improvised attack proficiency and damage-modifier handling to the table. Neral and Orr use custom-background languages and carry no manipulation-dependent gear. Orr can read, write and understand those languages, and uses telepathy for conversation. [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 background rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/personality-and-background)

The rules, build arithmetic, portrait anatomy and 96 px readability were checked by hand. Big Linda was the paper and paint reference for the five original ImageGen portraits. The Mindwitness visual check used the anatomical description in *Volo's Guide to Monsters*.
