# Morkoth, Neogi, Neothelid, Tlincalli, and Wood Woad

Five original NPCs use Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races 2* player rules for 2014 fifth edition. The page numbers refer to the printed folios in the readable text mirror. [Printed text]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/en/product/248038/monstrous-races-2)

| Profile | Printed rules | NPC and manual arithmetic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morkoth | pp. 54–55, Morkoth Traits | Oru Fenwake, Monk 2. AC 15 from natural armor and Dex. HP 15 from 9 + 6. Tentacles hit at +4 for 1d8+2. Martial Arts and Flurry use the level-2 monk die for 1d4+2. Hypnosis is DC 11. |
| Neogi | p. 56, Neogi Traits. p. 88, Neogi Master | Skevril Pell, Small adult Rogue 2. Leather plus Dex gives AC 14. HP 17 from 10 + 7. Dagger hits at +5. Enslave is DC 12, with a two-hour concentration limit. |
| Neothelid | pp. 57–58, Neothelid Traits | Ishrek Below, Barbarian 2. AC 15 from natural armor and Dex. HP 25 from 15 + 10. Tentacles hit at +5 and apply rage damage to the bludgeoning once. Acid Breath is DC 13. |
| Tlincalli | p. 70, Tlincalli Traits | Sava of Reed Salt, Fighter 2. Natural armor, Dex, and shield give AC 18. HP 20 from 12 + 8. Shortsword hits at +5 for 1d6+5 with Dueling. Sting poison is DC 12. |
| Wood Woad | p. 75, Wood Woad Traits | Wicket Rootmark, Fighter 3. Natural armor, Dex, and grown shield give AC 16. HP 31 from 13 + 9 + 9. Grown club hits at +5 for 1d4+5 with Dueling. |

The Neogi profile keeps Hatchling and Adult together and treats Neogi Master as a feat. The shared language sentence trails off, so the profile uses each form's printed language rule. The adult's Enslave uses concentration and the charmed condition. Its trait block specifies no rest limit.

Neothelid's breath description says lightning once, while its heading and damage line specify acid. Ishrek uses acid. The mirror's column order puts the end of Tlincalli's Sting and Wood Woad's Tree Stride before their headings. Both are included under their printed folios. Sava applies her Strength modifier once to the piercing portion of the poisoned sting. Wicket reads the Weapons of the Forest recharge as applying to the level-3 magical enhancement.
