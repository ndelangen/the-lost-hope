# Helmed Horror, Hippogriff, Homunculus, Hook Horror, and Hydra

These profiles use Tyler Kamstra's published v2 player traits and the 2014 class and equipment rules. The source records point to the printed folios. [Monstrous Races v2 text]() · [DMs Guild listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 equipment rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/equipment)

| Profile | Printed traits | Encounter build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helmed Horror | p. 118 | Gate Scribe, Fighter 1. Living armor 18 plus shield 2 gives AC 20. HP 12. Longsword +4 to hit and 1d8 + 4 with Dueling. |
| Hippogriff | pp. 118–119 | Flint Quill, Fighter 2. Leather barding 11 plus Dexterity 2 and Defense 1 gives AC 14. HP 20. Beak or claws +5 to hit and 1d8 + 3. |
| Homunculus | p. 121 | Mothpin, Rogue 1. Leather 11 plus Dexterity 3 gives AC 14. HP 10. Shortbow +5 to hit and 1d6 + 3 before Sneak Attack. |
| Hook Horror | pp. 121–122 | Bracken Hook, Barbarian 2. Natural armor and Unarmored Defense each give AC 15. HP 25. Hook +5 to hit and 1d8 + 3, or 1d8 + 5 while raging. |
| Hydra | pp. 122–123 | Splitreed, Barbarian 2. Unarmored Defense gives AC 14. HP 25. Proficiency +2 sets the starting head count to two. A hit from one source that deals 16 damage costs a head. |

Helmed Horror's trait block leaves size and walking speed open. Gate Scribe uses Medium size and the printed 30-ft. flight. Hippogriff's alignment line accidentally says griffon. The Homunculus block specifies Small and a 25-ft. walk, while the design commentary mentions Tiny and 20 ft. Mothpin uses the trait block values and has no spoken language. The Hook Horror's trait block gives 10-ft. darkvision.

For Splitreed, apply the head-loss threshold to damage after reductions. The regrowth die includes Constitution because the trait says to roll it like a resting Hit Die. The usual temporary HP duration governs the extra head. The two heads in the portrait match the starting count.

Five original 1254 × 1254 PNGs were generated with built-in ImageGen. I reviewed the full images and compared them at 96 px beside Big Linda. The Hippogriff's leather barding, Homunculus's shortbow, Hook Horror's two unarmed hooks, and Hydra's two visible heads match the encounter builds.
