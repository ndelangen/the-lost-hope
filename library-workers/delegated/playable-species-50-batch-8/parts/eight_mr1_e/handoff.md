# Nothic through Shambling Mound

Five original NPCs use Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races* player traits. The listed pages are the printed folios of the v2 text. [v2 rules]() · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races) · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 combat rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

| Profile | Printed folio | Build and manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nothic | pp. 158–159 | Vesk Foldnote, Rogue 2. Natural AC 15, HP 9 + 6 = 15, and Rotting Gaze DC 8 + 2 + 2 = 12. Rogue supplies Insight while the printed traits supply Arcana and Stealth. Expertise gives Insight +5 and Stealth +7. Use the finesse dagger for Sneak Attack. |
| Otyugh | p. 167 | Grott the Drainkeeper, Barbarian 2. Natural AC 15, HP 14 + 9 = 23, and Strength melee attack +5. The trait block gives 120-ft. darkvision. After a hit, the bonus-action grapple uses Athletics +5, with advantage during Rage. |
| Peryton | p. 169 | Bramblecrest, Monk 1. AC 13, HP 9, attack +5. The trait block gives a 30-ft. walk and a 40-ft. fly speed while unarmored. A hit after a 30-ft. dive can add 1d6 once that turn. Flyby and Martial Arts each use the bonus action. |
| Rust Monster | p. 180 | Pennywort, Monk 2. Natural AC 15, HP 10 + 7 = 17, and 45-ft. walk from 35 + 10. Dexterity gives bite +4. The extra Monk strikes use the conventional d4 bludgeoning die. Iron Scent tracks ferrous metal and Rust Metal uses the printed nonmagical metal condition. |
| Shambling Mound | p. 186 | Mosswake, Fighter 2. Natural AC 15, HP 11 + 7 = 18, slam +5, and Second Wind 1d10 + 2. The printed blindsight is 30 ft. and the no-hands grapple still uses an opposed check. |

The Peryton and Rust Monster builds use natural attacks and carry no hand-operated equipment. The design notes describe both as handless. Their trait blocks omit a named No Hands line. The Nothic's Otherworldly Perception can locate a moving unseen creature, while gaze actions require a visible target. I used the printed movement and darkvision values where the design notes differ.

The portraits were generated with built-in ImageGen. Big Linda was the reference for warm parchment and painting technique. [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json) · [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [96-pixel comparison](portrait-thumbnails.jpg)
