# Monstrous Races, five v2 player profiles

These five profiles use Tyler Kamstra's 2014 fifth-edition player conversions. Each has one original class-built NPC. The five square portraits use built-in ImageGen with Big Linda as a paper and paint reference. I checked the originals and the 96 px comparison. [Printed rules, v2]() · [DMs Guild listing](https://www.dmsguild.com/product/230312/Monstrous-Races)

| Species and folio | Worked NPC and manual arithmetic |
| --- | --- |
| Intellect Devourer, p. 124 | Quill, Rogue 1. AC 14 from Tiny leather and Dexterity +3, HP 10 from 8 + 2, claws +5 to hit with 1d8 + 3 damage, and Devour Intellect DC 12. The printed finesse exception lets the claws qualify for Sneak Attack. |
| Invisible Stalker, pp. 125–126 | Sift, Barbarian 1. Unarmored AC 14 from Dexterity +2 and Constitution +2, HP 14 from 12 + 2, and slam +4 to hit for 1d8 + 2. The supplement's p. 8 rule makes 0 HP immediately fatal to creatures immune to unconsciousness. |
| Jackalwere, p. 127 | Fen-Copper, Rogue 2. Hybrid AC 14 from leather and Dexterity +3, HP 17 from 8 + 5 + 4, shortbow and rapier at +5, and 1d6 Sneak Attack when eligible. The Small jackal cannot use either weapon. |
| Kraken, p. 130 | Brinewake, Barbarian 1. AC 14 from either natural armor or Unarmored Defense, HP 14 from 12 + 2, unarmed strike +5, and Lightning Storm DC 11. It holds its breath for 3 minutes and surfaces between dives. |
| Manticore, p. 141 | Cinder-Sill, Fighter 2. Natural AC 13, HP 20 from 10 + 6 + 4, melee attacks at +5, and Tail Spikes at +4 in this build. |

I used the printed player traits where the design notes differ. Kraken's notes mention Amphibious and Ink Cloud, which the trait block omits. Manticore's player flight is 30 ft. The Tail Spikes trait gives the range and damage die but omits a modifier and proficiency instruction, so Cinder-Sill uses proficiency and Dexterity. Quill learns Deep Speech through Sage because the possession trait names it while the base language trait gives Undercommon. The possession rules leave the host's post-possession state unstated.
