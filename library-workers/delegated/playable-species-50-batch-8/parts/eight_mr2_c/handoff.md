# Monstrous Races 2 player profiles

These five profiles use Tyler Kamstra's printed player traits for 2014 fifth edition. Guard Drake includes all five chromatic options under one profile, with Brinejaw as the black drake example. Firenewt Giant Strider is recorded as an alias within Giant Strider. [Authored rules text]() · [Alternate scan](https://anyflip.com/rehgh/azoq/basic) · [DMs Guild publication](https://www.dmsguild.com/en/product/248038/monstrous-races-2)

| Profile | Printed folio | Worked NPC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giant Strider | 33 | Emberstep, Barbarian 2. Unarmored Defense gives AC 15 and HP is 25. Fire Absorption expends a reaction and one d12. |
| Flail Snail | 34 | Clink, Barbarian 1. The shell gives AC 17, or 21 when withdrawn. HP is 14. |
| Froghemoth | 35–36 | Fenhook, Barbarian 2. AC 15 and HP 25. The tongue hits at +5 and pulls a target adjacent before the bonus-action grapple attempt. |
| Girallon | 39–40 | Brace, Fighter 2. HP 22. Shield and hide give AC 16, which drops to 14 after the two-weapon bonus attack until the start of its next turn. |
| Guard Drake | 42–43 | Brinejaw, Barbarian 2. Natural armor gives AC 16. HP is 25. The black drake can swim and breathe underwater. |

The two mirrors leave the Flail Snail's ability increase and language fields blank. Clink uses baseline ability scores and learns Common through Hermit. When Antimagic Shell rolls 5–6, the burst can also hit Clink and the original spell resolves normally. Froghemoth's commentary describes Amphibious, which the trait block omits. Fenhook uses its swim speed and the ordinary breath limit. The Guard Drake commentary mentions darkvision and Common. Brinejaw uses the trait block's Draconic and learns Common through Outlander.
