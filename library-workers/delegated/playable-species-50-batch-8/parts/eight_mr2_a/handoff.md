# Monstrous Races 2 first group

Five player profiles use Tyler Kamstra's *Monstrous Races 2* for 2014 fifth edition. Mirehook's Banderhobb trait block is on printed p. 7, viewer page 5 of the DMs Guild preview. I used the printed text mirror for Bodak p. 14, Boggle p. 15, Catoblepas p. 16 and Cave Fisher pp. 17–18. The first-volume rules and the encounter monster blocks have their own entries. [DMs Guild preview]() · [Printed text mirror]() · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes) · [2014 combat rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat)

- **Mirehook, Banderhobb barbarian.** Natural armor and Unarmored Defense each yield AC 14. HP are 14. The +5 tongue attack uses the same Strength-based damage as the bite, changing its type to necrotic. Rage adds 2 damage and grants advantage on the follow-up Athletics check. Mirehook has to choose between starting Rage and grappling with their bonus action.
- **Harrow Pell, Bodak rogue.** Natural AC 15 and 10 HP. The dagger is +5 to hit, and its finesse property qualifies it for Sneak Attack. The printed Death Gaze DC uses Constitution even though the trait also labels Charisma its spellcasting ability. The encounter uses DC 12 from Constitution. Harrow heals through rests under the supplement's rules.
- **Puddlepin, Boggle monk.** Unarmored AC 14 and 10 HP. Shortsword and Martial Arts both use Dexterity and +5 to hit. The build starts with sticky oil. The profile keeps both oils and their separate saves at DC 12. The Rift trait allows a bonus-action trip between fixed frames and contains no recharge limit. The text's design commentary proposes one use per day.
- **Sedge-Eye, Catoblepas fighter.** AC 15 and 12 HP. The tail attacks at +5. Death Ray uses the printed Intelligence formula for DC 9. Stench uses Constitution for DC 12, and requires concentration. The tail has the player trait's bludgeoning damage.
- **Latchline, Cave Fisher barbarian.** AC 16 and 13 HP. The printed player ability increase is blank. This build keeps its standard-array scores. Filament has AC 11, 2 HP and a 225-lb. reeling limit. Spider Climb removes the check with normal climbing movement costs. The encounter uses one filament at a time and notes when a creature remains attached after release.

I reviewed the five square ImageGen originals at full size and at 96 px against Big Linda. Each keeps one readable silhouette on plain warm parchment. The sheets show Big Linda first, followed by Banderhobb, Bodak, Boggle, Catoblepas and Cave Fisher.
