# The Wakewyrm's Fury beasts

The 2020 standalone adventure's reference deck prints five complete beast profiles. The patch adds those five publication records to the existing creatures. I checked the pages directly, including the mirrored order of the front and back panels. The Young Wakewyrm's card prints a single 30-ft. speed. The booklet describes it swimming in Tideswell Cave, and the profile records that encounter detail. Plaintive Cry uses the juvenile's bonus action and the mother's reaction. The Boar card prints both Perception +2 and passive Perception 9. The Boar and Weasel are Wild Shape reference cards for a pregenerated druid. The collected *Humblewood Tales* rules are still unverified and the patch targets the standalone printing.

I used built-in ImageGen for four square portraits with plain gray-tan parchment. The adult Wakewyrm uses the same approved draft from the Campaign Setting batch. The original cards credit Crystal Sully for both wakewyrms and Paul Scott Canavan for Hunter Shark.
