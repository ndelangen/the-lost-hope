# The Wakewyrm’s Fury beast source review

Hit Point Press distributes both the booklet and its eight-page reference deck free through itch.io. The booklet’s copyright page (p. 3) identifies it as the 2020 first printing. The rules on the deck are for the standalone adventure. The card fronts and backs are on pp. 4–5, with the back columns mirrored for duplex printing. [Official booklet and reference cards]()

## Young Wakewyrm

The card gives a Large unaligned Beast at CR 2 (450 XP), AC 15 (natural armor), HP 68 (8d10 + 24), and speed 30 ft. STR 16 (+3), DEX 16 (+3), CON 17 (+3), INT 3 (−4), WIS 10 (+0), CHA 5 (−3), and passive Perception 10. The card prints a single speed even though the adventure describes the creature swimming. Keep the printed speed as given. [Official reference deck, pp. 4–5]()

- Hold Breath lasts 1 hour. Plaintive Cry uses the young creature’s bonus action to call its mother. If she is within 150 ft. and hears the call, she may use her reaction to move up to her speed toward her young.
- Multiattack makes two Bite attacks. Bite is +5 to hit, 10-ft. reach, one creature, 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage. [Official reference deck, p. 5]()
- The coastal poachers hold the juvenile in Tideswell Cave on the Talongrip Coast. It swims just beneath the pool’s surface, lashes out at strangers within bite range unless calmed with a DC 16 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check, and dives and hides when attacked. When released, it swims toward Ship’s Bay following its mother’s calls. The encounter has a separate initiative-count-20 arrival and Skopps control sequence. The card art by Crystal Sully shows a coiled blue-green sea serpent with finlike frills, a spiny back, and yellow luminous markings. [Official booklet, printed pp. 3, 8, 10–11, and reference deck p. 4]()

## Other full beast cards

- **Wakewyrm** is Huge and unaligned, CR 6 (2,300 XP), AC 16, HP 136 (13d12 + 52), and speed 20 ft., swim 50 ft. It holds its breath for 1 hour. Multiattack is bite and tail or two bites. Bite is +8, reach 20 ft., 15 (3d6 + 5) piercing, with a DC 16 Strength save and escape DC 16 for its single grapple. Tail is +8, reach 25 ft., 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning. Swallow uses a bite against a grappled Large-or-smaller creature, then applies blindness, restraint, total cover from external effects, and 11 (3d6) acid at the start of the wakewyrm’s turns. A creature inside dealing at least 18 damage triggers a DC 15 Constitution save at that turn’s end. This card reproduces the adult block from *Humblewood Campaign Setting* v1.04 p. 189. [Official reference deck, pp. 4–5]()
- **Hunter Shark** is Large and unaligned, CR 2 (450 XP), AC 12, HP 45 (6d10 + 12), speed 0 ft., swim 40 ft., with blindsight 30 ft., Perception +2, and passive Perception 12. Blood Frenzy grants advantage on melee attacks against any creature below full HP. Water Breathing limits it to underwater breathing. Bite is +6, reach 5 ft., for 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing. Three patrol 50 ft. from the cave mouth at high tide. [Official reference deck, pp. 4–5 and booklet printed p. 5]()
- **Boar** and **Weasel** have complete compact cards on pp. 6–7, respectively. Their purpose is to support the pregenerated druid’s two Wild Shape forms. Preserve printed values if capturing these card versions, including the boar’s Perception +2 and passive Perception 9, and the weasel’s flat 1 piercing damage. [Official booklet and reference deck]()

## Collected Tales

D&D Beyond’s *Humblewood Tales* contents lists Cosmoth Caterpede, Gargath, Gimlet Swarms, Wakewyrm, and Young Wakewyrm in Appendix A. The entries link to purchase-gated material. The publisher’s separate itch.io listing identifies its collected as v.18. The standalone deck establishes the 2020 Young Wakewyrm rules. The corresponding collected stat block remains unverified.
