# Azurin source and review notes

The September 2005 first printing of *Magic of Incarnum*, ISBN 0-7869-3701-7, gives the playable racial traits on pp. 7–8. Mark Poole’s illustration is on p. 8. The printed page numbers match the scan’s page numbers. The p. 169 subtype rules add no universal Incarnum abilities. [Original printing]() · [Subtype]()

The source reviewer found two problems in the published Warrior 1 and Hextor Cleric 3 examples. Both print Con 13 and use Improved Essentia Capacity, which requires Con 15. The cleric lists two temporary essentia per Channel Incarnum use, where the substitution’s formula yields one for a third-level cleric. Both staged builds use the normal level-1 cap of one point per receptacle. See the printed examples for LA +0 and class Hit Dice. [Improved Essentia Capacity, p. 38]() · [Channel Incarnum, p. 43]() · [Examples, pp. 170–171]()

The integration reviewer checked the patch, publication scoping, skill budgets, and combat math. Lysa’s card describes her aura without assigning a detect-spell strength category to her class feature. [Soulborn class features, p. 25]()
