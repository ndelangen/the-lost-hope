# Azurin encounters

Both NPCs use D&D 3.5 rules from *Magic of Incarnum*. Use provisional CR 1 and ECL 1 for either. Each starts with three essentia, one racial and one from each of their two incarnum feats. Both lock one point into each feat at the start of the day and leave one point in the pool. [Feat rules, p. 34]()

## Lysa Vale, ferry warden

Lawful good Soulborn 1. HP 12, AC 16 (touch 11, flat-footed 15), speed 30 ft., initiative +1. Fort +4, Ref +1, Will +1. Heal +5, Diplomacy +6.

- Longsword +3, 1d8+2 slashing, 19–20/×2. Javelin +2, 1d6+2 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft.
- **Smite Opposition** twice per day with Sapphire Smite. With the longsword, +5 to hit and 1d8+4 damage against chaotic or evil targets. If the target qualifies for neither, resolve the ordinary attack and spend the attempt.
- **Healing Soul** once per day. As a swift action, regain 2 HP.

Lysa has tied the boat back up to give a missing child more time to reach the landing. She asks the party to check the path from the village while she keeps the ramp clear. [Soulborn, pp. 25–26]() · [Healing Soul, p. 38]() · [Sapphire Smite, p. 40]()

## Rena Marr, mill causeway watch

Chaotic good Fighter 1. HP 12, AC 15 (touch 12, flat-footed 13), speed 30 ft., initiative +6. Fort +4, Ref +2, Will +1. Climb +6, Jump +6.

- Greatsword +4, 2d6+4 slashing, 19–20/×2. Javelin +3, 1d6+3 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft.
- **Cobalt Charge** with the greatsword gives +7 to hit and 2d6+5 damage. This includes the normal +2 charge bonus and the feat’s +1 insight to attack and damage. AC falls to 13 until her next turn.
- **Midnight Dodge** gives +1 dodge AC against one target’s attacks. Pick the target on Rena’s turn. Her AC against it is 16, or 14 if she charged. She can change targets on each turn. If she loses her Dexterity bonus to AC, she loses the dodge bonus too.

Rena is getting the workers off the causeway before the sluice gate opens. She can charge if the lane clears. With people in the way, she stays at the gate and asks the party to lead them across. [Cobalt Charge, p. 35]() · [Midnight Dodge, p. 39]()
