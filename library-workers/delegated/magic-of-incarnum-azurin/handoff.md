# Azurin for D&D 3.5

The first printing of *Magic of Incarnum* gives Azurins one bonus feat at 1st level and one permanent point of essentia. The point creates a pool if needed. The book lists no racial ability adjustments or bonus skill points. Both NPCs below invest one point per feat, locked for 24 hours. [Original traits, pp. 7–8]() · [Incarnum feat rules, p. 34]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Lysa Vale**, a Soulborn ferry warden, can smite twice per day and heal herself once. Her racial and feat essentia already work at 1st level. Soulborn meldshaping begins at 4th level.
- **Rena Marr**, a Fighter guarding a mill causeway, gets an attack and damage bonus when she charges. She can choose a new opponent for Midnight Dodge each turn while the investment in the feat stays locked.

Both builds are provisional CR 1. The portrait depicts Lysa. The original description puts the sky-blue sheen in the whites of the eyes.
