# Diabolus source and review notes

Mike McArtor’s D&D 3.5 playable Diabolus appeared in the January 2005 *Dragon* #327, pp. 62–65. The October 2005 *Dragon Compendium, Volume I* first printing reprints it on pp. 6–10, pp. 10–14. The October printing adds LA +1 and the daily tail dose formula, and omits the magazine’s exemptions for interactions with other Diaboli. Paizo’s errata has no Diabolus correction. [Magazine scan]() · [Compendium traits, p. 10]() · [Paizo errata]()

The Compendium says the tail injects poison and limits daily doses. It leaves the spending procedure unstated. Both cards use one dose per successful tail hit until the supply runs out. The 1986 *DM’s Guide to Immortals* and 1994 *Mystara Monstrous Compendium Appendix* entries use a paralyzing tail. The staged encounters use the 3.5 sickened effect. [AD&D Mystara entry]()

Niklas Jannsen is credited on the Compendium’s p. 9 full-body illustration. The 3.5 description gives goatlike legs, small horns, pointed ears, three fingers and a thumb, and a barbed tail. Zareth’s clothing, trident, and pose are original. [Compendium art, p. 9]()

Independent review checked both skill budgets, the trident and tail attack values, spell slots and save DCs, daily doses, and exact publication scoping. The original rules supply LA +1. The encounter CRs are provisional estimates.
