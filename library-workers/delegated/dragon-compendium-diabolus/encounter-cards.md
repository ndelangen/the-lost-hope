# Diabolus encounters

Both NPCs use D&D 3.5 rules and are provisional CR 2, ECL 2. Both have darkvision 60 ft. and +2 racial on saves against spells and spell-like effects. Their tail is a secondary natural attack. In these encounters, a successful tail hit spends one available dose. Failure on the initial Fortitude save sickens the target for 2d4 rounds. Sickened creatures take −2 on attacks, weapon damage, saves, skill checks, and ability checks. The tail has no secondary poison damage. [Compendium traits, p. 10]()

## Zareth Flintmere, dry causeway keeper

Chaotic good Fighter 1. HP 12, AC 15 (touch 12, flat-footed 13), speed 30 ft., initiative +6. Fort +4, Ref +2, Will +1. Against spells or spell-like effects, use Fort +6, Ref +4, Will +3. Intimidate +4 or +6 against animals and humanoids. Jump +5.

- **Trident** +4 melee or thrown, 1d8+2 piercing, ×2, range increment 10 ft.
- **Tail** −2 melee, 1d4+1 piercing, ×2. For a full attack, make the trident attack at +4 and the tail attack at −2. Five doses per day, Fortitude DC 12.

Zareth wants to get a loaded handcart across before the water rises. He signals Vessa with his tail and waves the party toward the cart while he checks the loose stones.

## Vessa Nettle, echo stair interpreter

Chaotic good Sorcerer 1. HP 5, AC 12 (touch 12, flat-footed 10), speed 30 ft., initiative +2. Fort +1, Ref +2, Will +2. Against spells or spell-like effects, use Fort +3, Ref +4, Will +4. Bluff +7, Concentration +5, Spellcraft +5.

- **Trident** +0 melee or +2 thrown, 1d8 piercing, ×2, range increment 10 ft. **Tail** −5 melee, 1d4 piercing, ×2. Four doses per day, Fortitude DC 11.
- **Shield** lasts one minute. It raises AC to 16 and flat-footed AC to 14. Ordinary touch AC stays 12. It blocks *magic missile* and applies against incorporeal touch.
- **Charm person** affects one Humanoid within 25 ft. for an hour, Will DC 15 negates. Spell resistance applies. The target gets +5 on its save if Vessa or her allies attack or threaten it.
- **Cantrips** are *daze*, *detect magic*, *light*, and *mage hand*. *Daze* affects one Humanoid of up to 4 HD within 25 ft., Will DC 14 negates, spell resistance applies, for one round. Vessa has five 0-level and four 1st-level slots per day at caster level 1.

Vessa relays warnings along a stair where voices carry. She can translate a signal from across the landing and show the party which stair is open. Anyone watching the tail can recognize it as communication with a DC 10 Intelligence check. Understanding the message requires the code. [Tail code, pp. 8–9]()
