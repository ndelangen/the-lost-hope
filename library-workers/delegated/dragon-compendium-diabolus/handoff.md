# Diabolus for D&D 3.5

The playable Diabolus appeared in *Dragon* #327 before its October 2005 *Dragon Compendium, Volume I* printing. This batch uses the Compendium, which adds LA +1 and a daily limit for the tail’s poison. Diaboli are Native Outsiders with the Chaotic subtype. They gain +2 on saves against spells and spell-like effects and treat a trident as a simple weapon. [Compendium traits, p. 10]() · [Earlier playable article]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Zareth Flintmere**, Fighter 1, uses the trident alongside a secondary tail attack. His tail has five doses per day and a Fortitude DC of 12.
- **Vessa Nettle**, Sorcerer 1, can use the trident through Weapon Familiarity and can translate the visual tail code. Her tail has four doses per day and a Fortitude DC of 11.

Both encounters are provisional CR 2 and ECL 2. To run the encounters, spend a dose on each successful tail strike while doses remain. A failed initial save sickens for 2d4 rounds. The plain-parchment portrait shows Zareth.
