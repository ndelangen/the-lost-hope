# Avariel encounter cards

## Sael Frostpinion

Fighter 1. Provisional CR 1, ECL 4. AC 17, HP 11, initiative +4. Land 30 ft., fly 50 ft. with average maneuverability. Fort +3, Ref +4, Will +1.

- Rapier +5, 1d6+2 piercing, critical 18–20/×2.
- Dive charge on a clear line with at least a 30-ft. descent. Rapier +7, 2d6+4 piercing on a hit, AC 15 until Sael's next turn.
- Flyby Attack lets Sael use a standard rapier attack during his flying move.

Sael asks the party to watch the trail while he checks the ridge above a looming rockslide.

## Iri Windward

Cleric 2. Provisional CR 2, ECL 5. AC 18, HP 14, initiative +3. Land 30 ft., fly 50 ft. with average maneuverability. Fort +4, Ref +3, Will +6.

- Light mace +2, 1d6+1 bludgeoning.
- Casts bless before the rescue if she has time. Keeps cure light wounds and shield of faith ready. Her domain spell is longstrider, which increases her land speed by 10 ft. for 2 hours.
- Turn undead 4/day. Separate Air domain uses apply to earth and air creatures.

Iri wants someone to open the terrace so she can land beside the stranded porters. [Complete builds](npc-builds.json)
