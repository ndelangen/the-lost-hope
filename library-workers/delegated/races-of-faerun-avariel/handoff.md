# Avariel

This batch adds the Avariel from the March 2003 first printing of *Races of Faerûn*. The profile uses 3.5 core Elf and aerial movement rules and retains the species' 30-ft. diving descent. [Original rules, printed p. 32]()

Sael Frostpinion and Iri Windward are original encounters. The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds their builds and the species profile. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) maps Sael's [square portrait](portraits/avariel-draft.png) to that publication. The patch is based on catalog commit `636bc8a`.

I reviewed the source pages, the staged catalog records, and the [portrait at thumbnail size](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) by hand. Search, runtime portrait loading, and automated verification are deferred.
