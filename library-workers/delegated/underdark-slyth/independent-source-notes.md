# Slyth source check

The October 2003 first printing gives the player profile on pp. 17–18. The counter runs one ahead of the printed folios. The book describes humanoid shapechangers without naming a formal subtype. Its description of an oozelike alternate form grants no general Ooze traits. The named Slyths in the settlement listings have class levels but no full combat sheets. [Player rules]() · [Settlement listings]()

The player entry gives a standard action to shift and a full-round action to return. It caps the form at 10 minutes per class level and establishes a cooldown equal to time spent in form. It gives no separate reversion procedure at the cap. Both cards use a planned return before the cap. The independent review checked form-dependent AC, casting limits, spells, and skill ranks. [Form rules continued]() · [Mage armor](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/mageArmor.htm)

Wizards’ collected archive has no Underdark errata file. The June 2008 FAQ has no Slyth entry. Vance Kovacs illustrated the center figure on p. 18. The book’s rounded features, bald head, and brown skin informed Plip’s normal-form portrait. Her outfit, staff, and pose are original.
