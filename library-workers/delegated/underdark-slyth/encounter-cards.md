# Slyth encounters

Both are Medium Humanoids with one class Hit Die, LA +2, ECL 3, and provisional CR 2. They walk 30 ft. and can breathe underwater indefinitely. They are immune to polymorphing and poison and have sonic resistance 5. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

Enter amorphous form as a standard action. In it, swim 30 ft. and pass through openings as small as 2 inches across. Return with a full-round action that does not provoke. These first-level builds can spend up to 10 minutes in the form. After returning, wait for the time actually spent in it before shifting again.

Armor and gear meld. Magic items stop functioning. Dexterity and ongoing force armor still apply. Amorphous Slyths cannot attack or cast spells with components, lose their other supernatural abilities, and cannot be flanked, stunned, or critically hit.

## Plip Reedstone, Druid 1

Plip swims through the grate to the back of the sluice release. She returns to normal in the chamber before operating it.

- HP 10, initiative +3, Fortitude +4, Reflex +3, Will +5. AC 15 in leather, AC 13 in amorphous form.
- If the swim lasts six minutes, wait six minutes after returning before shifting again.
- In normal form, quarterstaff +0 for 1d6 bludgeoning. Entangle is Reflex DC 14 and needs vegetation. Cure light wounds restores 1d8+1 HP.

## Murra Stonewake, Wizard 1

Murra casts mage armor, then swims through the seep opening to see whether it leads into the vault.

- HP 6, initiative +7, Fortitude +2, Reflex +3, Will +4. AC 13 normally, or AC 17 in either form while mage armor lasts.
- If the scout lasts three minutes, wait three minutes after returning before shifting again.
- In normal form, light crossbow +3 for 1d8 piercing. Grease is Reflex DC 14 and lasts one round when a duration applies.
