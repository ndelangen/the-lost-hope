# Slyth from Underdark

This batch adds the D&D 3.5 Slyth from the October 2003 first printing with two original encounters. Plip’s leather stops contributing to AC in amorphous form. Murra keeps the force armor from a previously cast mage armor. Both have a 10-minute cap at class level 1 and wait after returning for as long as they actually spent in the form. [Original rules, pp. 17–18]() · [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

Plip’s portrait uses the approved plain tan parchment and shows her normal form. The temporary copy found Slyth by name, trait, and encounter role, returned both builds from both catalog commands, and resolved Plip’s portrait as the new list thumbnail.
