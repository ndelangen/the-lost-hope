# Feral Gargun encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. Both are Medium Monstrous Humanoids with 30-ft. speed, darkvision 60 ft., cold resistance 5, and 5-ft. natural reach. Goliath Blood counts them as Goliaths for race-keyed effects. Both use running-jump rules from a standstill, can attempt to climb 15 ft. as a move action with their full Climb modifier, and are acclimated to high altitude. ## Sava of Rethma, glacier wayfinder

Two racial Hit Dice and Ranger 1. Provisional CR 3, ECL 5. AC 16, touch 12, flat-footed 14. HP 31, initiative +2. Fort +6, Ref +7, Will +4. BAB +3, grapple +11 with Powerful Build. STR 19, DEX 14, CON 18, INT 10, WIS 13, CHA 6. Common and Gol-Kaa.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Large battleaxe in one hand | +7 melee, 2d6+4 slashing, ×3, 5-ft. reach |
| Primary claws | +8 melee each, 1d6+4 piercing and slashing each, 5-ft. reach. Use both on a full attack when fighting with claws alone. |
| Free claw with battleaxe | +3 melee, 1d6+2 piercing and slashing, as part of a full attack |

Climb +10, Jump +10, Listen +3, Spot +5, Survival +3. Includes Alertness and Weapon Focus (claw). Track is the Ranger bonus feat. Favored enemy Giant adds +2 weapon damage and +2 to Bluff, Listen, Sense Motive, Spot, and Survival against giants. Wild Empathy −1.

The ice shelf is breaking away from the old route. Sava asks the party to move the waymarks to the higher path. She checks the ridge for giant tracks before anyone crosses.

## Gorren of Vekra, river salvage guide

Feral Gargun monster-class level 2, one racial Hit Die. Provisional CR 1, ECL 2. AC 12, touch 11, flat-footed 11. HP 14, including Toughness, initiative +1. Fort +3, Ref +3, Will +2. BAB +1, grapple +4. STR 17, DEX 12, CON 16, INT 10, WIS 10, CHA 8. Common and Gol-Kaa.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Shortspear in one hand | +4 melee, 1d6+3 piercing, ×2, 5-ft. reach. Thrown +2 at a 20-ft. increment with the same damage. |
| Primary claws | +4 melee each, 1d4+3 piercing and slashing each, 5-ft. reach. Use both on a full attack when fighting with claws alone. |
| Free claw with shortspear | −1 melee, 1d4+1 piercing and slashing, as part of a full attack |

Climb +7, Jump +3, Survival +4. Powerful Build and 1d6 claws arrive at monster-class level 3. The printed table gives CR 1 at level 2. [Progression table, p. 91]()

Gorren saw where the ferry dropped a toll chest. He can climb down to the submerged ledge while the party holds the raft to the pier.
