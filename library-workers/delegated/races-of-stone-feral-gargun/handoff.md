# Feral Gargun for D&D 3.5

One playable Feral Gargun profile from the August 2004 first printing of *Races of Stone*. The full package has two racial Hit Dice, level adjustment +2, cold resistance 5, and two 1d6 claws. Powerful Build permits Large weapons while natural reach remains 5 ft. Goliath Blood applies to effects keyed to race. [Original traits, pp. 91–92]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

The optional four-level monster class reaches the full package in stages. At level 2, it has one Hit Die, two 1d4 claws, and altitude acclimation. The second Hit Die, Powerful Build, and 1d6 claws arrive at level 3. [Progression table, p. 91]()

Sava of Rethma is an original glacier wayfinder with two racial Hit Dice and Ranger 1, provisional CR 3 and ECL 5. Her portrait is full-body on plain parchment. Gorren of Vekra is an original river salvage guide at monster-class level 2, provisional CR 1 and ECL 2. The printed table gives CR 1 for that stage. Both cards show the primary claw attacks and how a free claw works alongside a weapon.
