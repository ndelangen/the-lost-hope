# Independent Feral Gargun reviews

The source reviewer checked the August 2004 first printing. Thomas Baxa drew the Feral Gargun on p. 90, and David Day drew the separate fallen-log illustration on p. 91. The description gives a tall, thick-furred humanoid with a long bear-like face. The playable rules span pp. 91–92. The reviewer found no Feral Gargun-specific correction in Wizards’ archive. [Original portrait]() · [Original rules]() · [Wizards FAQ archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The full profile has 2d8 racial Hit Dice, +0 base Fortitude, +3 Reflex and Will, and LA +2. The first two progression levels have only one Hit Die. The second Hit Die, Powerful Build, and 1d6 claws arrive at third. Natural armor totals +2 at fourth. The rules name Gol-Kaa in the language description. Common on the encounter cards follows the core character language rule. [Progression table]() · [Core character languages]()

The 2004 official web adventure *Raiders of the High Citadel* provides separate Feral Gargun Barbarian 2 stat blocks at CR 4, including armored guards and an unarmored barracks version. Some weapon damage lines for attack and full attack disagree in that adventure. Sava and Gorren are original builds using the playable traits and core natural weapon rules. [Published adventure]() · [3.5 SRD natural weapons](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#naturalWeapons)

The second reviewer checked Sava’s 16 skill points, favored enemy, size and attack modifiers, and Gorren’s 8 skill points, 14 HP with Toughness, and earlier claw progression.
