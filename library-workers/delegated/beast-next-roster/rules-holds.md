# Remaining Beast rules sources

All 43 exact printings still need rules evidence. The frozen roster records the canonical entry and printing ID for each hold. [Roster](roster.json)

| Records | Printing | Evidence still needed |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 21 | *Monster Vault 2*, Black Flag Roleplaying | Final Animals appendix, pp. 322–329, for 17 entries. The four main bestiary blocks are Death Butterfly Swarm, Greater Death Butterfly Swarm, Rattus Eel, and Swarm of Rattus Eels. The publisher's encounter builder lists the precise forms but leaves AC and HP blank. [Encounter builder](https://encounter-builder.koboldpress.com/) · [Licensed module](https://www.fantasygrounds.com/store/product.php?id=KPFGTOVMV2) |
| 18 | *Humblewood Tales*, 2014 fifth edition compatible | Appendix A of the collected v.18 or equivalent hardcover. Several earlier publications have matching art or shared digital IDs. The collected rules remain unverified. [Digital contents](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/hwt) · [Publisher ](https://hitpointpress.itch.io/humblewood-tales) |
| 1 | Ghamoo-Ra, *D&D: World of Warcraft*, 2024 fifth edition | The full Blackfathom Deeps block around p. 175, including actions and legendary actions below the visible portion of the review. [Review frame](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLLV1EZN71E&t=1390s) · [Digital entry](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/6888405-ghamoo-ra) |
| 1 | Swarm of Contaminated Insects, *Monsters of Drakkenheim*, 2024 fifth edition compatible | Final p. 33. The available beta cannot verify the final mechanics. [Final digital entry](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5525128-swarm-of-contaminated-insects) |
| 1 | Spiderfrog, *Muk's Guide to Everything He Learned From Tasha*, 2014 fifth edition | Full block on indexed p. 33. The official sample stops at printed p. 5. [Official sample]() |
| 1 | Boar, *Dark Pyramid of Sorcerer's Isle*, 2014 fifth edition | An official correction for Tusk, which Charge refers to and p. 33 omits. The Basic Rules Boar matches the printed stats and traits and is a usable table fallback under its own printing. [Basic Rules Boar](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16812-boar) |

The single and swarm forms of the Dusktail share the same publisher illustration. Adult and young Wakewyrms retain separate portraits and canonical IDs. The Greater Death Butterfly Swarm is CR 7 in *Monster Vault 2* and CR 6 in other publications, so its earlier stat blocks leave the Black Flag rules unverified.
