# Monster Vault beasts

Five Monster Vault records checked against Nosolorol’s licensed Spanish edition, *Cubil de Monstruos*. The English catalog confirms names and habitats. CR and combat rules come from the Spanish folios. All five portraits match the checked roster hashes. [Spanish edition](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant), [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)

| Beast | Spanish folio | CR | AC | HP | Combat details |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Cat | 342 | 0 | 11 | 8 | Its bonus-action meow can charm a visible creature within 10 ft. |
| Crab | 338–339 | 0 | 11 | 9 | Claw attaches on a hit. It deals automatic damage at the start of the crab’s turns and travels with the target. |
| Crab, Giant | 339 | 1/4 | 15 | 13 | Each pincer can hold one target. A grappled creature that breathes can lose speech and begin suffocating on a failed Strength save. |
| Crocodile | 339–340 | 1/2 | 12 | 25 | Bite grapples and restrains. Death Roll deals 2d4 slashing damage, or half on a successful save. |
| Crocodile, Giant | 340 | 5 | 14 | 120 | Tail cannot target a creature it is grappling. Death Roll deals 2d6 slashing damage, or half on a successful save. |

The ordinary Crocodile’s printed Bite loses the die from its damage expression. The profile uses the fixed 6 piercing damage printed before the incomplete expression. The giant form keeps the printed Strength modifier +8 and damage modifiers +5. General grapple escapes use the fixed DC and an action. [Monster Vault errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)
