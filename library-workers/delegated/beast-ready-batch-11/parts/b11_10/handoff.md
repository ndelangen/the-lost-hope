# Five insects from Cubil de Monstruos

I checked the licensed Spanish translation of Kobold Press’s Monster Vault, translated by Andrea Iglesias and published by Nosolorol in 2025. The Spanish folios supply the rules. Kobold Press’s English index supplies the English names and terrain tags. XP follows the table on Spanish p. 17. The viewer page is two higher than the printed folio. [Nosolorol edition](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/) · [Publisher errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

| Beast | Printed pages | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insect, Giant Fire Beetle | 344 | Illumination, Bite, and Igniting Mucus. The actions continue at the top of the right column. The profile covers the two ignition triggers, the ongoing fire damage, and both ways to remove the hazard. |
| Insect, Giant Scorpion | 344–345 | Two Claws and one Sting. Each claw can hold one target, with escape DC 12. The Spanish Sting has 5-ft. reach. Reflexive Sting triggers from an attacker within 10 ft. The fixed escape procedure follows the publisher’s general clarification. |
| Insect, Giant Wasp | 343 | The right column gives the Sting sequence and the damage-triggered pheromone reaction. Poison damage must reduce the target to 0 HP to trigger the hour-long poisoned and paralysis effects. |
| Insect, Scorpion | 344 | Poison damage and poisoned condition resistance. Sting has a DC 10 Constitution save for the poison damage. |
| Insect, Swarm of Insects | 344 | Bites, shared space, no HP recovery, and condition immunities. A failed DC 11 Constitution save against Crawling Swarm costs the spell’s action while preserving the slot or daily use. |

I verified the five reuse hashes. The existing swarm portrait depicts a spiral of mixed insects. Its profile keeps the printed walking and climbing speeds. The older scorpion portraits use a more orange parchment tone.
