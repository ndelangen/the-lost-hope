# Batch 11, part b11_05

Staged Deer, Ankylosaurus, Plesiosaurus, Triceratops and Tyrannosaurus Rex under their exact Monster Vault scopes. The rules come from Nosolorol’s licensed Spanish edition, *Cubil de Monstruos*, pp. 108–111 and 339. Kobold Press’s English index confirms names, book, challenge ratings and habitats. [Spanish publisher](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/) · [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

The Spanish Deer prints Perception 16, which differs from Perception 12 in the Black Flag reference document. All four dinosaurs convert incoming critical hits to normal hits. The Tyrannosaurus’s Rending Shake continues onto p. 111 and specifies the saves for the throw and a second creature’s collision.

I checked the five reused square portraits against the roster’s subjects and SHA-256 hashes. The four dinosaur portraits use warmer parchment than the Deer portrait.
