# Batch 11 Beast rules

Verified both horses on p. 337, Hyena on pp. 342–343, Giant Hyena on p. 343 and Giant Centipede on pp. 343–344 of *Cubil de Monstruos*, Nosolorol's licensed Spanish edition of *Monster Vault*. The English catalog confirms names and habitats. XP comes from the printed challenge rating table on p. 17. [Spanish publisher](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant), [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)

The Spanish War Horse uses a 30-ft. Trampling Charge trigger. Both hyenas print Cackling Chorus as DC 10 and as 8 plus the number of participating hyenas. The summaries preserve both values. The May 2025 errata gives no correction to these entries. [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

All five roster hashes matched. The horse entries share the same light bay portrait. Scale barding is part of the War Horse stat block.
