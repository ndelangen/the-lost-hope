# Beast-ready batch 11, group 06

Five profiles are staged from Nosolorol's licensed 2025 *Cubil de Monstruos*, the Spanish edition of Kobold Press's 2024 *Monster Vault*. I checked the Spanish text and page images. Kobold Press's English index supplies the English names and habitats. The Spanish book's p. 17 table supplies XP.

[Stat blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Spanish publisher](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/) · [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

Velociraptor's Bite can grapple and restrain a Medium or smaller creature, and its Maul action targets a creature it is grappling. Both eagles' Seabird rules specify where the movement must begin and end, the Constitution save against sinking, and the Strength check to fly out. The Giant Eagle's two Talons attacks and Elephant's standalone Stomp target follow the published Spanish book. The source notes record the differences in the English Black Flag reference document v0.2.

All five reused portraits match their listed SHA-256 values and depict the scoped animals. The existing Velociraptor and Giant Eagle portraits use brighter orange parchment.
