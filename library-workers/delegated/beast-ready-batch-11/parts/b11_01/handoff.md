# Beast-ready batch 11, part b11_01

I verified five stat blocks in Nosolorol's licensed Spanish edition of *Monster Vault*, ISBN 979-13-87882-04-4. The staged English summaries follow *Cubil de Monstruos*, pp. 335, 350, and 356–357. The Kobold Press catalog confirms the English names and habitats. [Spanish publisher](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [English catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/) · [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

Baboon is CR 1/8 and 25 XP. The p. 32 overview says CR 0, but the p. 335 stat block and terrain tables agree on CR 1/8. Ape retains the printed Strength +5 and +3 in its damage rolls. Giant Ape's Multiattack calls for two Fists or two Rocks, and its Throw takes a bonus action.

I checked all five portrait subjects and matched their SHA-256 digests to the roster. Giant Ape uses the approved tan-parchment silverback from batch 04.
