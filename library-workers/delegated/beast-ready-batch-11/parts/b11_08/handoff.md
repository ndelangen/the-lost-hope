# Batch 11, part 08

Staged five Monster Vault profiles translated from the licensed Spanish edition, Cubil de Monstruos. The Spanish print supplies the rules and exact fractional challenge ratings. Kobold Press's English catalog confirms the catalog names and habitats. Its May 29, 2025 errata lists no changes for these five profiles. [Nosolorol edition](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [Kobold Press catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/) · [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

- Hippocampus, p. 229. Shared Breathing supplies an air-breathing rider while the hippocampus is neither fighting nor traveling at high speed. Rearing Kick is a bonus action. On a failed Strength save, the hippocampus chooses between knocking the target prone and pushing it away.
- Giant Goat and Draft Horse, p. 338. The goat can react to damage with Terrifying Scream. The horse's Draft Animal trait applies to pushing, dragging, and lifting. The horse's profile prints CR 1/4, displayed as 0.3 in the English catalog.
- Hawk and Blood Hawk, p. 342. The ordinary hawk's block crosses columns. Both can Disengage or Dodge as a bonus action while flying. Blood Hawk's profile prints CR 1/8, displayed as 0.1 in the English catalog.

All five reused portraits match their roster hashes. Giant Goat, Blood Hawk, and Draft Horse use muted Batch 04 assets. The Hippocampus retains its orange parchment, with its head and fin close to the circular crop edge.
