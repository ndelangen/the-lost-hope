# Five Monster Vault beasts

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

I translated the mechanics from Nosolorol’s licensed Spanish *Cubil de Monstruos* and checked each English name, creature type, challenge rating, and habitat in Kobold Press’s catalog. The Spanish book prints CR 1/4 for Boar and CR 1/8 for Camel. The publisher’s online catalog rounds those to 0.3 and 0.1. [Spanish edition](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [English catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)

| Entry | Spanish folio | page | Rules checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bear, Brown | 349 | 351 | Heightened Smell sets Perception to 18 for smell. Multiattack lists Bite and Claws, with escape DC 14 for its grapple. |
| Bear, Polar | 349 | 351 | Cold resistance, swim speed 30 ft., and escape DC 15 for its grapple. |
| Boar | 345 | 347 | Charge adds 1d6 slashing damage and can knock a creature prone. Relentless has a 4-damage threshold and recharges after a short or long rest. |
| Boar, Giant | 345 | 347 | Relentless has a 10-damage threshold. Challenging Snort can frighten a visible creature and impose disadvantage on its next save against being knocked prone by the boar. |
| Camel | 338 | 340 | Camel Resilience covers exhaustion and water needs. Bile Spit imposes disadvantage on Charisma checks for up to an hour on a failed Constitution save. |

Both Spanish bear blocks list one Bite and one Claws attack but require two Claws hits for the grapple. I preserved the printed contradiction and cited the English Black Flag Reference Document’s reading as a table clarification. Under that reading, Bite and Claws must both hit the same creature. The Monster Vault errata explains how a creature can escape these grapples. [Black Flag Reference Document]() · [Kobold Press errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/)

All five approved parchment portraits match their subjects and roster hashes.
