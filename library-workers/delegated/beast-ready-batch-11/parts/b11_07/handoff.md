# Batch 11 · Giant elk, frogs, and goat

The five rules profiles come from Nosolorol's licensed 2025 Spanish edition of the Monster Vault. Exact English catalog names remain the record keys. The book supplies fixed Perception and Stealth, six ability modifiers, and fixed HP. The encounter profiles use the XP table on p. 17. [Spanish publisher](https://www.nosolorol.com/talesofthevaliant) · [English catalog](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)

The standard giant frog can swallow a Small or smaller target. The poisonous giant frog can swallow a Medium or smaller target, and its Reeling Tongue also targets Medium or smaller in the Spanish printing. English Black Flag v0.4 lists Large or smaller for the poisonous frog's tongue. The staged rule follows the licensed printing. [English rules reference]()

All five assigned portraits passed subject and checksum checks. The indexed giant elk and giant frog portraits have warmer orange parchment than the plain tan Frog and Goat portraits.
