# Plane Shift Zendikar beasts, group B

These six encounter entries use the James Wyatt supplement's April 2016 first printing and the September 2014 first printing of the Monster Manual. Zendikar page numbers count the cover as page 1. The Monster Manual numbers are printed folios. I checked the two printings by hand and compared the base blocks with the 2014 D&D Beyond rules.

| Beast | Zendikar page | Monster Manual base and printed page | AC | HP | CR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Oxen | 34 | Giant Goat, 326 | 11 | 19 | 1/2 |

| Terastodon | 34 | Mammoth, 332 | 13 | 126 | 6 |

Woodcrasher Baloth keeps the Triceratops's 50-ft. walk and gains the supplement's 30-ft. climb. Shoal Serpent keeps the Plesiosaurus's 20-ft. walk, 40-ft. swim and hour-long breath hold. The original Heartstabber Mosquito blood-drain threshold, Terastodon Stomp's shorter reach and Terra Stomper's separate Multiattack targets are carried through.

The source evidence identifies which appearance details and habitat tags come from the text or illustrations and which are editorial. The Terastodon's flooded woodland tag comes from the illustration on page 34.

The six original PNGs are 1254 × 1254. I compared them with Big Linda at portrait size and 96 pixels. The final Terastodon has four tusks and four feet, and the final Terra Stomper has six legs.
