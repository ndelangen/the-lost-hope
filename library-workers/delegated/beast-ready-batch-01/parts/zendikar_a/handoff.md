# Zendikar beasts

Five encounter blocks use James Wyatt's April 2016 first printing of *Plane Shift: Zendikar*. Page numbers count the cover. I checked the base rules against the official D&D Beyond 2014 Basic Rules listings and used the 5etools index as a secondary folio check for the 2014 *Monster Manual*. [Publisher ]()

| Catalog entry | page | 2014 *Monster Manual* base | AC | HP | CR |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Baloth | 33 | Triceratops, p. 80 | 13 | 95 | 5 |
| Drake (Large) | 24 | Giant Eagle, p. 324 | 13 | 26 | 1 |
| Drake (Small) | 24 | Pteranodon, p. 80 | 13 | 13 | 1/4 |
| Gnarlid | 34 | Brown Bear, p. 319 | 11 | 34 | 1 |
| Great Cat | 34 | Saber-Toothed Tiger, p. 336 | 12 | 52 | 2 |

- The regular Baloth uses Trampling Charge and walks at 50 ft. The Turntimber Woodcrasher has its own entry and the added 30-ft. climb speed.
- The larger Drake keeps the Giant Eagle's neutral good alignment and languages because the publisher gives no replacement. Small in the smaller Drake's name is relative. The Pteranodon block is Medium and keeps Flyby.
- The Gnarlid block is for its enlarged state. Magic that reduces its size halves maximum HP and damage from each attack. The source describes its ability to grow using green mana and supplies no activation or recharge rules.
- Zendikar compares the Great Cat with the Saber-Toothed Tiger. The encounter block uses that base and describes its Claw attack as the foreleg-blade strike. Habitat tags for the Gnarlid and Great Cat include editorial inferences noted in the evidence.

Four square PNGs match the plain parchment reference. The two Drakes share the same original portrait and keep separate encounter stats. I checked the artwork at source size and 96 pixels.
