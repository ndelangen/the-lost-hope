# Plane Shift Kaladesh beast rules and portraits

Five existing Beast records have encounter stat blocks. Four original portraits cover those records, with one image shared by the two drakes. I used James Wyatt's February 2017 first printing and checked the three referenced stat blocks against the 2014 *Monster Manual*. The Kaladesh page numbers count the cover as page 1. | Entry | Kaladesh page | Rules source and check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drake (Large) | 29 | Giant Eagle, *Monster Manual* p. 324. AC 13, HP 26, CR 1. Beak and Talons at +5. |
| Drake (Small) | 29 | Pteranodon, *Monster Manual* p. 80. AC 13, HP 13, CR 1/4. The smaller drake inherits the Pteranodon's Medium size. |
| Gremlin | 26 | Printed Kaladesh block. AC 11, HP 27, CR 1/2. The printed Hit Dice are 5d8 + 5. Siphon uses DC 11 Dexterity for carried devices. |
| Sky Leviathan | 28 | Printed Kaladesh block. AC 14, HP 247, CR 10. Bite is printed at +9. Swallow starts with a DC 18 Dexterity save. |
| Sky Whale | 28 | Giant Crocodile, *Monster Manual* p. 324. AC 14, HP 85, CR 5. Flipper and Tail both use +8 and DC 16 Strength against falling prone. |

The Sky Whale adds 50-ft. hover flight to the Giant Crocodile statistics. It retains the base walking and swimming speeds, Hold Breath, and Stealth +5. That reading differs from the existing index, which lists flight alone. The Kaladesh Flipper replaces Bite and uses the Tail attack mechanics, so the whale has no bite grapple or associated restraint.

The supplement gives Gremlins climbing behavior in its description and only a walking speed in the stat block. Siphon can eventually ruin a device. The source supplies no fixed number of applications. The Sky Leviathan uses its own swallow effects, including the 30-damage threshold from one creature inside during one turn and the DC 21 Constitution save to avoid disgorging creatures.

The habitat tags come from Kaladesh's descriptions. The evidence file calls out editorial search tags and appearance details. The stat block overlays keep the supplement and stat block references attached to each exact catalog entry.

I checked the printed statistics and attack text by hand. I also inspected each 1254 × 1254 portrait and compared the 96-pixel circular versions with Big Linda. I widened the Sky Whale framing after its nose clipped in the first thumbnail.
