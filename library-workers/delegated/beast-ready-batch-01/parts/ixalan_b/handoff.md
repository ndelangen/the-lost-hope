# Ixalan dinosaurs

| Entry | First-printing Ixalan | Encounter rules |
|---|---|---|
| Brontodon | p. 27, The Rumbling Herds | Brontosaurus, *Volo's Guide to Monsters*, first printing, p. 139. CR 5. Both attacks reach 20 ft. |
| Ceratops | p. 28, Horned Herbivores | Triceratops, *Monster Manual*, first printing, p. 80. CR 5. The printed Trampling Charge save DC is 13. |
| Ferocidon | p. 30, Teeth and Frills | Allosaurus, *Monster Manual*, first printing, p. 79. CR 2. Pounce uses a 30-ft. approach and a Claw hit. |
| Gishath, Sun's Avatar | p. 32, dedicated stat block | CR 10. Bite can start the grapple. Swallow requires its own hit against an eligible creature already grappled. The two Multiattack targets must differ. |
| Hadrosaur | p. 29, herbivore paragraph above Soaring Flocks | Hadrosaurus, *Volo's Guide to Monsters*, first printing, p. 140. CR 1/4. |

page numbers count the cover. The base book page numbers are the printed folios. I compared the summaries with the printed encounter blocks and checked attack bonuses, dice, save DCs, and durations by hand. Brontodon's sparse-forest tag, Ferocidon's jungle-edge tag, Gishath's setting tags, and Hadrosaur's wetland tags are editorial. Their evidence is identified per entry.
