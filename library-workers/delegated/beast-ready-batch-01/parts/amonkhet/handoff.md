# Plane Shift: Amonkhet

Five encounter blocks for the July 2017 first printing, by James Wyatt with Ashlie Hope. The page numbers count the cover as page 1. Each encounter follows its referenced 2014 Monster Manual block, with the changes given in Amonkhet. [Wizards of the Coast ]()

| Record | page | Base block | AC | HP | CR and XP |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Ammit | 30 | Giant Crocodile, p. 324 | 14 | 85 | 5, 1,800 XP |
| Cerodon | 37 | Mammoth, p. 332 | 13 | 126 | 6, 2,300 XP |
| Hippopotamus | 38 | Elephant, p. 322 | 12 | 76 | 4, 1,100 XP |
| Large Drake | 33 | Giant Eagle, p. 324 | 13 | 26 | 1, 200 XP |
| Small Drake | 33 | Pteranodon, p. 80 | 13 | 13 | 1/4, 50 XP |

- Ammit keeps the Giant Crocodile’s Huge Beast type and unaligned alignment. The bestiary describes ammits as demonic but supplies no mechanical type override. I based the original portrait on the crocodilian bestiary description and the leonine body in James Wyatt’s story. The existing indexed publisher art is the adjacent Archfiend of Ifnir illustration by Slawomir Maniak. That credit and image reference are preserved. [Ammit story description](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/brazen-2017-05-03)
- Cerodon keeps Trampling Charge and adds Siege Monster. Its 10-ft. Gore and 5-ft. Stomp have different ranges. The desert and Hekma tags follow the Amonkhet entry. [Mammoth base block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-m#Mammoth)
- Hippopotamus swaps Gore for Bite, including the trigger in Trampling Charge. It also gains a 30-ft. swim speed and can hold its breath for 30 minutes. I kept the printed DC 12 and applied the official damage-expression correction for the Elephant’s Gore and Stomp. [Elephant base block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-e#Elephant) · [Monster Manual errata](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/sae/monster-manual-2014)
- The Drakes share one original portrait. Large Drake retains the Giant Eagle’s neutral good alignment, language line, and action names. Small Drake retains the Pteranodon’s Medium mechanical size and Flyby. [Giant Eagle base block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-g#GiantEagle) · [Pteranodon base block](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/monster-stat-blocks-p#Pteranodon)

I checked the movement, hit dice, damage dice, modifiers, traits, attack reaches, and saves against the 2014 stat blocks. I reviewed all four original ImageGen portraits at full size and 96 px against the Big Linda parchment reference.
