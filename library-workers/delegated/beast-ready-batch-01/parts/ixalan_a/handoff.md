# Aegisaur, Aerosaurs, Altisaur and Armasaur

Five encounter blocks follow the bases assigned by the January 2018 first printing of James Wyatt's *Plane Shift: Ixalan*. I read the setting descriptions and checked the 2014-rule base stat blocks. Pages 27–29 below are counted from the cover. The base pages are printed folios. [Ixalan ]() · [Volo’s contents]()

| Entry | Ixalan page | Base and manual check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aegisaur | 28 | 2014 *Monster Manual* p. 79, Ankylosaurus. Huge, AC 15, 68 HP, CR 3. Tail +7, reach 10 ft., DC 14 Strength or prone on a hit against a creature. |
| Aerosaur (Large) | 29 | *Volo’s Guide to Monsters* p. 140, Quetzalcoatlus. Huge, AC 13, 30 HP, fly 80 ft., CR 2. Flyby and a 30-ft. dive that adds 3d6 to a successful bite. |
| Aerosaur (Small) | 29 | 2014 *Monster Manual* p. 80, Pteranodon. Medium, AC 13, 13 HP, fly 60 ft., CR 1/4. Flyby and Bite +3 for 2d4 + 1 piercing. |
| Altisaur | 27 | *Volo’s Guide to Monsters* p. 139, Brontosaurus. Gargantuan, AC 15, 121 HP, CR 5. Constitution save +6. Stomp and Tail both reach 20 ft. Stomp also calls for a DC 14 Strength save. |
| Armasaur | 28 | *Volo’s Guide to Monsters* p. 140, Stegosaurus. Huge, AC 13, 76 HP, CR 4. Tail +7 for 6d6 + 5 piercing. |

The Aerosaur names use the existing relative form labels. The printed bases determine the actual combat sizes, and the two entries share one portrait. The Aegisaur keeps the printed +7 to hit. The Large Aerosaur's dive prints extra damage without a separate damage type. The habitat labels for armored herbivores are drawn from the plains section, with Grassland added as an editorial search term.
