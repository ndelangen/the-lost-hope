# Black Flag beasts

Five encounter stat blocks use the May 2024 Black Flag Roleplaying Reference Document v0.2. I checked the actions, saves, and fixed values against the publisher . XP follows the table on page 229. The creature blocks give fixed HP, with no Hit Dice. The habitat labels are editorial encounter suggestions.

| Beast | page | Mechanics checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plesiosaurus | 259 | Two bites, Hardy, a one-hour breath hold, and the underwater Flipper Slam bonus action. Off-balance halves speed and penalizes the first attack roll on each affected turn. |
| Triceratops | 259 | Two Gores, a Strength save against Trampling Charge, and the Wisdom save against Warning Bugle. The Wisdom save governs fear. The free Stomp under the bugle rule still requires a failed charge save. |
| Tyrannosaurus Rex | 259 | Bite and Tail must target different creatures. The bite grapples and restrains eligible targets. Rending Shake has separate Strength and Dexterity saves for the throw and potential collision. The published attack bonuses and damage modifiers are retained as printed. |
| Velociraptor | 260 | Pack Tactics, Bite and Claws, the bite grapple and restraint, and Maul as its own action against the grappled target. |
| Stirge | 340 | Blood Drain attaches on a hit, then causes direct HP loss on the stirge’s turns. Any creature can spend an action to detach it. Reactive Wiggle can redirect an attack onto its host. |

## Artwork

The new Velociraptor and Stirge are original square 1254 × 1254 PNGs on warm tan paper. The raptor’s raised sickle claws and the stirge’s proboscis are clear in the thumbnail check. The stirge’s outer wing tips clip in the circular preview and are fully visible in the square original.

Plesiosaurus, Triceratops, and Tyrannosaurus Rex already have generated parchment portraits on their corresponding Monster records. Their silhouettes and anatomy fit the Black Flag Beast entries. Their paper is more golden than Big Linda’s, and the portrait manifest identifies the existing image files for reuse.
