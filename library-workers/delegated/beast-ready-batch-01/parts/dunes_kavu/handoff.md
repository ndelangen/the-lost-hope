# Amonkhet and Dominaria beasts

Four encounter stat blocks and four original 1254 × 1254 portraits. All use the first-printing Plane Shift . Page numbers count the cover as page 1. [Amonkhet, July 2017]() · [Dominaria, July 2018]()

| Beast | Source | Table details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| River Serpent | Amonkhet p. 38. Giant Constrictor Snake in 2014 Monster Manual p. 324 and SRD 5.1 p. 374. | CR 2, AC 12, HP 60. Bite reaches 10 ft. Constrict hits within 5 ft. and grapples and restrains, escape DC 16. |
| Serpopard | Amonkhet p. 37. | CR 3, AC 12, HP 52. Bite uses a DC 14 Constitution save for poison. Printed Pounce calls for a Claw hit that the book does not supply. |
| Kavu Predator | Dominaria p. 24. | CR 2, AC 15, HP 75. Walk and climb 30 ft. Pounce with a DC 14 Strength save, two Claws via Multiattack, and a bonus Bite against the prone target after Pounce. |
| Steel Leaf Kavu | Dominaria p. 24. | CR 4, AC 16, HP 142. Raking Charge begins with Bite and uses a DC 15 Strength save. Rend targets a prone creature and is available as a bonus action through the charge. |

I checked each printed block for ability modifiers, skills, speeds, AC, hit dice, XP, attack bonuses, damage and save timing. For the CR 2 River Serpent I used the Giant Constrictor Snake rules. Amonkhet’s behir-based serpent is the separate Giant River Serpent catalog entry. The stat block gives the Serpopard its printed Bite and flags the omitted Claw where the DM will see it. [Amonkhet’s river reskin]() · [Official SRD base rules]() · [Serpopard print block]()

I compared each portrait with Big Linda at full size and 96 pixels. The ordinary River Serpent uses a constrictor silhouette and draws its muted blue scales from the credited Amonkhet art. The two kavu use different builds and poses to distinguish the arboreal hunter from the heavy charger. The searchable habitats are derived from the setting text.
