# Sailback, piranha swarm, two trilobites, and Tyrant

James Wyatt's *Plane Shift: Ixalan*, first printing, January 2018, uses the following 2014 *Monster Manual* blocks as reskins. I checked each printed block and compared the rules to Wizards' 2014 Basic Rules. [Ixalan ]() · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Staged rules](stat-blocks.json)

| Ixalan entry | Ixalan page | Base and printed folio | Encounter numbers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sailback | 30 | Giant Crocodile, p. 324 | AC 14, HP 85, CR 5, 1,800 XP. |
| Swarm of Piranhas | 40 | Swarm of Quippers, p. 338 | AC 13, HP 28, CR 1, 200 XP. |
| Trilobite | 40 | Crab, p. 320 | AC 11, HP 2, CR 0, 10 XP. |
| Trilobite (Giant) | 40 | Giant Crab, p. 324 | AC 15, HP 13, CR 1/8, 25 XP. |
| Tyrant | 31 | Tyrannosaurus Rex, p. 80 | AC 13, HP 136, CR 8, 3,900 XP. |

- Sailback can hold its breath for 30 minutes. Its bite grapples and restrains on a hit with escape DC 16. Its tail knocks a creature prone on a failed DC 16 Strength save and cannot target prey held by the Sailback.
- Swarm of Piranhas has advantage against creatures below their hit point maximum and bites for 2d6 piercing at 14 HP or less. Its immunity to grappling comes from the 2016 errata that predates Ixalan. [Official Monster Manual errata v1.1]()
- Both trilobites breathe in air or water and share dark segmented-shell artwork. The ordinary creature deals a flat 1 bludgeoning damage with its inherited Claw. On a hit, the giant version deals 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning and grapples with escape DC 11. It can hold one target with each grasping appendage.
- Tyrant's two Multiattack attacks require different targets. Its bite grapples only a Medium or smaller creature, with escape DC 17. The description of frightening roars supplies setting flavor.

The artwork follows the plain parchment and paint of Big Linda, with new subjects and poses. I checked the four source PNGs at full size and at 96 px. Both trilobite sizes share one image. The individual source records identify the printed habitats and label inferred search terms, such as the Tyrant's forested valleys. Existing book images and credits stay in the gallery.
