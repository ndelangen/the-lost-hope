# Five Ixalan beasts

[Encounter blocks](stat-blocks.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait comparison](portrait-review.jpg) · [ImageGen prompts](portrait-prompts.json)

The five records use the January 2018 first printing of James Wyatt's *Plane Shift: Ixalan*. Page numbers for that supplement count the cover as page 1. The supplement supplies the reskin instructions, and the 2014 *Monster Manual* supplies the mechanics. I checked the numbers and complete traits and actions against Wizards' SRD 5.1 and the legacy Basic Rules stat blocks. The 5etools index provided a secondary check for the *Monster Manual* page locations. [Plane Shift: Ixalan]() · [Official SRD 5.1]()

| Ixalan entry | Source page | 2014 base | Manual rules check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hammerskull | 28–29 | Giant Goat, p. 326 | AC 11, 19 HP, CR 1/2. Ram is +5 to hit. A 20-ft. straight approach before a same-turn hit adds 2d4 damage. A creature hit must pass a DC 13 Strength save to stay standing. |
| Horned Frog | 39 | Giant Frog, p. 325 | AC 11, 18 HP, CR 1/4. Bite is +3 to hit and restrains through its grapple, escape DC 11. Swallow takes a separate Bite hit on a grappled Small or smaller target. |
| Macaw and Parrot | 39 | Raven, p. 335 | Both are Tiny mechanically, with AC 12, 1 HP, and CR 0. Beak deals a flat 1 piercing damage. Mimicry uses a DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check. |
| Piranha | 40 | Quipper, p. 335 | AC 13, 1 HP, CR 0. Bite is +5 to hit for a flat 1 piercing damage. Blood Frenzy applies to melee attacks against creatures below full HP. |

The four 1254-pixel square originals use Big Linda as the parchment and paint reference. Macaw and Parrot share one portrait. I inspected all four at source size and at 96 pixels.
