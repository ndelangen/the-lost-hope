# Shifter for D&D 3.5

One playable Shifter version from the June 2004 first printing of *Eberron Campaign Setting*. It covers Beasthide, Cliffwalk, Longstride, Longtooth, Razorclaw, and Wildhunt. Shifting is a free action, normally once per day. Duration uses the current Constitution modifier, and each shifter feat adds one round. [Original traits, pp. 19–20]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Iona Fernridge is an original Wildhunt Ranger 1. Her card gives the starting and shifted Constitution, HP, Fortitude, and Survival totals, plus scent and tracking ranges. Dane Millward is an original Razorclaw Fighter 1. His card gives the first printing’s mixed sword-and-claw attack and the extra round and healing from Healing Factor. Both are provisional CR 1.
