# Shifter encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. They are Medium Humanoids with the Shapechanger subtype, 30-ft. land speed, low-light vision, and Common. Both can shift once per day as a free action. [Original rules, pp. 19–20]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

## Iona Fernridge, Wildhunt Ranger 1

Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 15, touch 13, flat-footed 12. HP 10, initiative +3. Fort +4, Ref +5, Will +1. BAB +1, grapple +2. STR 12, DEX 17, CON 14, INT 10, WIS 13, CHA 8.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Short sword | +2 melee, 1d6+1 piercing, 19–20/×2 |
| Longbow | +4 ranged, 1d8 piercing, ×3, 100-ft. increment |

Climb +7, Handle Animal +3, Jump +7, Listen +7, Spot +7. Alertness is included and Track is the Ranger bonus feat. Wild Empathy +0. Favored enemy magical beasts gives +2 to weapon damage and to Bluff, Listen, Sense Motive, Spot, and Survival against magical beasts.

| Wildhunt | Ordinary | While shifting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CON | 14 | 16 |
| HP | 10 | 11 |
| Fortitude | +4 | +5 |
| Survival | +7 | +5 |
| Scent | — | 30 ft., 60 ft. upwind or 15 ft. downwind |

Shifting lasts 6 rounds, using the improved Constitution modifier. Remove 1 bonus HP at the end. A move action gives the direction of a scent. Within 5 ft. it can be pinpointed. Tracking a fresh scent normally starts at Survival DC 10 and adds +2 per hour. Ordinary surface and visibility penalties do not apply. Once the party finds the missing pack train’s gear, Iona can use its scent to follow the trail across bare rock.

## Dane Millward, Razorclaw Fighter 1

Provisional CR 1, ECL 1. AC 15, touch 12, flat-footed 13. HP 12, initiative +2. Fort +4, Ref +2, Will +1. BAB +1, grapple +3. STR 15, DEX 14, CON 14, INT 10, WIS 12, CHA 8.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Short sword, ordinary | +4 melee, 1d6+2 piercing, 19–20/×2 |
| Short sword, shifted | +5 melee, 1d6+3 piercing, 19–20/×2 |
| Claws, shifted | +4 melee and 1d4+3 piercing and slashing per claw. One as a standard action or two as a full attack. |
| Mixed full attack, shifted | Sword +3 for 1d6+3 and light off-hand claw +2 for 1d4+1. The first-printing −2 penalty is included in both. |
| Light crossbow | +3 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment. Reload as a move action. |

Balance +3, Climb +7, Jump +7. Shifting raises STR to 17, grapple to +4, and Climb and Jump to +8. Weapon Focus (short sword) is included. Healing Factor adds a round to the shift, for 6 rounds total, and heals 1 HP when Dane stops shifting alive. [Healing Factor, p. 55]()

The mill latch is giving way. Dane asks the party to hold it in place while the repairers file through. He shifts to guard the opening.
