# Independent Shifter reviews

The source reviewer checked the June 2004 first printing. Lucio Parrillo drew the Shifter on printed p. 19. Wizards’ art gallery confirms the credit. The text describes a lithe humanoid with a broad flat nose, heavy brows, pointed ears, and long sideburns. All six shifting traits appear on pp. 19–20. [Original art and rules]() · [Wizards art gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20161101150242/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/eb/20040618a)

The first printing uses −2 on all attacks for the Razorclaw light off-hand claw paired with a weapon. Longtooth specifies a −5 secondary bite when paired with a weapon. The cards use the core natural weapon and off-hand Strength rules where applicable. The February 2006 errata addresses Shifter Ferocity and Weretouched Master. The original racial traits on pp. 19–20 remain as printed. [Original traits, p. 20]() · [Official errata]() · [3.5 SRD natural weapons](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#naturalWeapons)

*The Forgotten Forge* has two published Razorclaw Shifter Warrior 2 NPCs at CR 1. Their five-round shift illustrates Constitution and Healing Factor. The published stat block uses an extraordinary label for Razorclaw and prints claw attack and damage totals that differ from the base trait and standard calculation. This batch uses the rules on pp. 19–20 for the original NPCs. [Published encounter]()

The second reviewer checked Iona’s 24 skill points and shifted Survival, HP, Fortitude, and duration. Dane uses eight skill points and qualifies for Healing Factor. The review also confirmed the original mixed attack, publication scoping, and the list thumbnail effect of the current sort. [Rules patch](rules.patch) · [Verification](verification.json)
