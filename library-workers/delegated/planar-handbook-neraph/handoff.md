# Neraph for D&D 3.5

The July 2004 first printing of *Planar Handbook* gives Neraph Camouflage one charge or thrown attack per enemy per encounter. That attack denies the target its Dexterity bonus to AC. A proficient annulat thrower also reduces an existing cover bonus by 2. [Original traits, p. 13]() · [Annulat, pp. 68–69]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Teo Spent River**, Ranger 1, uses an annulat. His example breaks down a throw against a target with both Dexterity and cover bonuses.
- **Mai the Houseless**, Rogue 1, can add sneak attack on a qualifying camouflaged javelin throw within 30 ft. That spends her camouflage use against the target for the encounter.

Both builds are provisional CR 1, ECL 1. The encounters are on the Material Plane, where both Limbo-origin Neraphim have the Extraplanar subtype. Teo is the subject of the plain-parchment portrait.
