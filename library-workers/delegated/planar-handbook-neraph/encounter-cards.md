# Neraph encounters

Both NPCs are D&D 3.5, provisional CR 1 and ECL 1. They have darkvision 60 ft. and are Outsiders (Extraplanar) on the Material Plane. Mark each enemy once a charge or thrown attack uses Neraph Camouflage. The target loses its Dexterity bonus to AC against that attack. Track the use separately for each enemy. [Neraph traits, p. 13]()

## Teo Spent River, lamp ramp outrider

Chaotic good Ranger 1. HP 10, AC 17 (touch 12, flat-footed 15), speed 30 ft., initiative +2. Fort +4, Ref +4, Will +1. Jump +10, Search +7, Spot +7, Survival +5.

- **Longsword** +3 melee, 1d8+2 slashing, 19–20/×2. Charge at +5 and lower AC to 15 until his next turn.
- **Annulat** +3 ranged, 1d6+2 slashing, 19–20/×2, range increment 30 ft. Point Blank Shot gives +4 and 1d6+3 within 30 ft. before range penalties. Apply 2 less to a target’s cover bonus.
- **Worked throw.** At 20 ft., roll +4 against a target whose usual AC is 16 (base 10, +2 Dexterity, +4 cover). Camouflage removes Dexterity and the annulat leaves +2 cover, for AC 12 against that throw. This spends his one camouflage use against that enemy.
- **Favored Enemy.** Against Outsiders with the Chaotic subtype, add +2 weapon damage and +2 to Bluff, Listen, Sense Motive, Spot, and Survival. Track is a bonus feat. Wild empathy +0.

Teo needs to bring a replacement lamp across a ramp with a damaged rail. He asks the party to check the rail and holds his camouflage until a clear charge or throw opens. [Annulat, pp. 68–69]()

## Mai the Houseless, split ledge courier

Chaotic neutral Rogue 1. HP 7, AC 18 (touch 13, flat-footed 15), speed 30 ft., initiative +3. Fort +1, Ref +5, Will +1. Jump +9, Search +7, Spot +7, Disable Device +5, Open Lock +7. Trapfinding covers harder and magical traps.

- **Short sword** +1 melee, 1d6+1 piercing, 19–20/×2. Charge at +3 and lower AC to 16 until her next turn.
- **Javelin** +3 ranged, 1d6+1 piercing, ×2, range increment 30 ft. Within 30 ft., Point Blank Shot gives +4 and 1d6+2 before range penalties.
- **Camouflaged throw.** Against an eligible target within 30 ft., denying Dexterity to AC can add +1d6 sneak attack. Spend the one camouflage use against that creature. She can still charge or throw normally against it on later turns.

Mai has a message for the next watch and is checking the marked footholds. The party can watch the approach while she gets across the split ledge. [Neraph Camouflage, p. 13]()
