# Neraph source and review notes

The July 2004 first printing of *Planar Handbook*, ISBN 0-7869-3429-8, describes the Neraph on pp. 12–13. The rules give a Limbo-origin Outsider with the Extraplanar subtype away from home. The published Cado block on p. 48 prints the Native subtype. This batch follows the player entry and uses the Material Plane for both encounters. The November 2004 errata does not change the Neraph or annulat. [Publisher preview]() · [Original player rules]() · [Published Cado example]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The racial ability and Cado’s description both give one camouflage attack per enemy per encounter. The learned Neraph Charge and Neraph Throw feats on p. 40 have their own text and explicitly require the target to see the attacker. The encounter cards use the racial ability. [Racial ability, p. 13]() · [Optional feats, p. 40]()

David Roach’s p. 12 illustration shows the large toadlike head, coarse red skin, and bone-studded gear. Vinod Rams shows Cado’s annulat on p. 48. Teo’s pose and outfit are original. Independent review checked both skill budgets, both attack routines, the cover calculation, and publication scoping. [Neraph art]() · [Annulat illustration]()
