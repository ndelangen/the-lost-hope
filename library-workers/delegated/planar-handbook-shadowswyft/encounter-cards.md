# Shadowswyft encounters

Both NPCs use D&D 3.5 rules and are provisional CR 2, ECL 2. They have low-light vision and darkvision 120 ft. In these Material Plane encounters they are Outsiders (Extraplanar). [Shadowswyft traits, p. 14]()

| State | Defense and movement | Checks and attacks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In ordinary conditions | AC 16, touch 13, movement 40 ft. | Use the listed attacks and checks. |
| First round after abrupt bright light | Blinded. AC 11, touch 8, movement 20 ft. | Spot and other vision-dependent checks fail. Search and most Str- or Dex-based skill checks that can still be attempted take −4. Opponents have total concealment and a 50% miss chance if their square is correctly targeted. |
| After the first round, still in bright light | Dazzled. AC 16, touch 13, movement 40 ft. | −1 on attacks, Search, and Spot. |

Sunlight and a *daylight* spell are the printed examples. Leaving the affected area ends the lingering dazzle. [Condition rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/conditionSummary.htm#blinded) · [Dazzled](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/conditionSummary.htm#dazzled)

## Mheer Wink, covered stair runner

Chaotic good Rogue 1. HP 7, AC 16 (touch 13, flat-footed 13), speed 40 ft., initiative +9. Fort +1, Ref +5, Will +1. Hide +10, Move Silently +10, Jump +8, Search and Spot +5, Disable Device +5, Open Lock +7. Trapfinding covers difficult and magical traps.

- **Short sword** +1, 1d6+1 piercing, 19–20/×2. **Light crossbow** +3, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, range increment 80 ft. Reload as a move action.
- **Sneak attack +1d6** against an eligible creature denied Dexterity to AC or flanked by her melee attack. The ranged limit is 30 ft. Total concealment while blinded prevents sneak attack.
- **Dazzled values.** Short sword +0, crossbow +2, Search +4, Spot +4. Damage stays the same.

Mheer has the spare key for the covered stairs. She checks the landing in dim light. If the upper shutter opens into direct sun, she feels for the rail and asks the party to guide her into shade.

## Pohl Nod, gloam road lookout

Chaotic good Ranger 1. HP 9, AC 16 (touch 13, flat-footed 13), speed 40 ft., initiative +5. Fort +3, Ref +5, Will +1. Hide and Move Silently +10, Search and Spot +5, Survival +5. Track as a bonus feat, wild empathy +0.

- **Longsword** +2, 1d8+1 slashing, 19–20/×2. **Longbow** +4, 1d8 piercing, ×3, range increment 100 ft. Point Blank Shot gives +5 and 1d8+1 within 30 ft.
- **Favored enemy undead.** Add +2 weapon damage and +2 to Bluff, Listen, Sense Motive, Spot, and Survival against undead.
- **Dazzled values.** Longsword +1, longbow +3 or +4 within 30 ft., Search +4, Spot +4. At 20 ft. against undead, the normal longbow attack is +5 for 1d8+3. The dazzled attack is +4 for the same damage.

Pohl is watching two stone arches along the road. He asks the party to move the last travellers from arch to arch and stays where he can watch the approach.
