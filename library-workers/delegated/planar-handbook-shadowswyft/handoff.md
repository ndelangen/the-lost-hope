# Shadowswyft for D&D 3.5

The July 2004 first printing of *Planar Handbook* gives Shadowswyfts 120-ft. darkvision, low-light vision, a 40-ft. land speed, and Light Blindness. Abrupt bright light blinds for one round. After that, the Shadowswyft stays dazzled while in the affected area. [Original traits, p. 14]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

- **Mheer Wink**, Rogue 1, loses sneak attack against opponents she cannot see during the blinded round. Her card gives ordinary, blinded, and dazzled values.
- **Pohl Nod**, Ranger 1, keeps his favored-enemy damage when dazzled. His card shows the change to his longbow attack at close range.

Both builds are provisional CR 2, ECL 2, with one class Hit Die and LA +1. They are Extraplanar in these Material Plane encounters. Mheer is the subject of the full-body portrait.
