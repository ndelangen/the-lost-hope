# Shadowswyft source and review notes

The July 2004 first printing of *Planar Handbook*, ISBN 0-7869-3429-8, prints the Shadowswyft traits on p. 14. The printed page matches the page. The November 2004 errata has no Shadowswyft correction. [Publisher preview]() · [Original rules]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The race has 120-ft. darkvision and low-light vision. The bright-light trigger and its two successive conditions use the printed trait and the core condition rules. The 2008 FAQ treats any additional *sunbeam* or *sunburst* vulnerability as an optional house rule. The staged builds use the printed Light Blindness effect. [Original traits]() · [Core conditions](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/conditionSummary.htm#blinded)

Steve Prescott illustrated an unnamed Shadowswyft rogue on p. 14. Page 13 describes slender bodies, medium-brown to black skin, and slanted, almost entirely black eyes. Mheer’s pose and costume are original. Independent review checked both skill budgets, first-round AC and movement, dazzle penalties, attack values, and exact publication scoping.
