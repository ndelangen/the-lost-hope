# D&D 3.0 encounter cards

These original first-level NPCs can be reused with a different name or hook. Both have provisional CR 1/2. Their statistics use the original 3.0 species rules. [Species rules, pp. 13–16]() · [NPC classes, Dungeon Master's Guide pp. 38–39]()

## Tesk Reedmark, Nezumi trail scout

**Warrior 1.** Medium Humanoid (Nezumi). AC 13 (touch 11, flat-footed 12), HP 6 (1d8+1), initiative +1, speed 40 ft. Fort +3, Ref +1, Will +0. Add +2 to saves against poison or disease. Str 12, Dex 13, Con 13, Int 9, Wis 10, Cha 6. Low-light vision. Speaks Rokugani and Nezumi.

**Skills and gear.** Climb +3, Hide +4, Listen +2, Move Silently +3, Spot +2. Alertness. Leather armor, longspear, javelins. The Nezumi's protection against gaining Shadowlands Taint still applies. Tesk has not taken the optional Scent feat.

- **Longspear.** Melee +2, reach 10 ft. The spear cannot strike an adjacent target. Hit 1d8+1 piercing, critical ×3.
- **Claw or bite.** Melee +2, reach 5 ft. Hit 1d4+1 normal damage, one such unarmed strike per round.
- **Javelin.** Ranged +2, range increment 30 ft. Hit 1d6+1 piercing, critical ×2.

Tesk saw someone move the flood markers and wants to keep travelers off a washed-out trail. Tesk will guide the party there if someone stays to warn incoming travelers. If attacked, Tesk holds a narrow passage long enough to withdraw.

## Nemi, Vanara canopy guide

**Expert 1.** Medium Humanoid. AC 13 (touch 11, flat-footed 12), HP 5 (1d6+1), initiative +1, speed 30 ft., climb 20 ft. Fort +1, Ref +1, Will +2. Str 8, Dex 13, Con 12, Int 13, Wis 11, Cha 8. Low-light vision. Speaks Common, Vanara, and Sylvan.

**Skills and gear.** Balance +9, Climb +13, Hide +7, Listen +6, Move Silently +7, Spot +6, Wilderness Lore +4. Alertness. Leather armor, club, light crossbow. Nemi can take 10 on a Climb check under pressure for a result of 23. Accelerated climbing covers 30 ft. and uses a +8 check after the −5 penalty.

- **Club.** Melee −1, reach 5 ft. Hit 1d6−1 bludgeoning, minimum 1, critical ×2.
- **Light crossbow.** Ranged +1, range increment 80 ft. Hit 1d8 piercing, critical 19–20/×2. Reload as a move-equivalent action.

Nemi needs a repair crew across the ravine before another bridge support fails. Nemi offers to lead the party up the canopy route if they bring a coil of rope and check the upper anchor. In a fight, Nemi stays near a climbable exit and protects the crew from cover.
