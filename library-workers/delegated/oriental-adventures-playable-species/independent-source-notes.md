## Nezumi

- **Character rules.** Medium-size humanoid, +2 Constitution, −2 Charisma, 40 feet base speed. Low-light vision sees twice as far as a human in poor light and retains color and detail. +2 racial bonus on Hide and Move Silently checks and on saving throws against poison and disease. Nezumi cannot acquire a Taint score and suffer no ill effects from ordinary Shadowlands exposure. The *cloud of Taint* spell and special attacks of Shadowlands creatures can still harm them. A successful unarmed strike with claw or bite deals 1d4 normal damage. The species text limits this to one unarmed attack per round, choosing claw or bite. Automatic languages are Rokugani and Nezumi. Bonus languages are Bakemono and Shadowlands. A multiclass Nezumi ignores Rogue when determining the XP penalty. [Printed p. 13][nezumi] · [Printed p. 14][nezumi2]
- **Keen Scent.** The Nezumi may select the Scent feat if Wisdom is at least 11. A character with the feat can detect an opponent’s presence as a free action at 30 feet normally, 60 feet upwind or 15 feet downwind. Strong odors double these ranges and overpowering odors triple them. Finding direction takes a move-equivalent action. Within 5 feet, the Nezumi can pinpoint the source. Tracking uses a Wisdom check, ordinarily DC 10 for a fresh trail. Odor strength, number of creatures and trail age can change the DC, which rises by 2 per hour since the trail was left. Otherwise the Track rules apply, with surface conditions and poor visibility ignored. Familiar odors can be recognized. Water can ruin the trail, especially running water. A powerful false scent impedes detecting or identifying creatures, and the book calls the resulting tracking baseline Wilderness Lore DC 20 in that sentence. [Printed p. 14][nezumi2]
- **Appearance and home.** Upright ratlike humanoids average about 5 feet 6 inches and 155 pounds. They have long snouts, prominent incisors, pink ears and palms, mostly bare pink tails, rough fur from white through gray and brown to black, five-fingered clawed hands with opposable thumbs and bent legs with three toes. Fur patterns can run in families. Pierced ears and bone or tooth ornaments are typical options. In Rokugan, mobile packs range through the Shadowlands, sometimes crossing into Scorpion and Unicorn lands. Nezumi in the book are allied with the Crab and commonly work with Hiruma scouts. [Printed p. 13][nezumi]

## Vanara

- **Character rules.** Medium-size monkeylike humanoid, +2 Intelligence, +2 Wisdom, −2 Strength, 30 feet base speed and 20 feet climb speed. +8 racial bonus on all Climb checks, using the higher of Strength or Dexterity modifiers. A Vanara can choose to take 10 when rushed or threatened. Accelerated climbing moves 30 feet and requires one Climb check per round at −5. Low-light vision works as described for the Nezumi. +4 racial bonus on Balance and Jump checks. +2 racial bonus on Hide and Move Silently checks. Automatic languages are Common and Vanara. Bonus languages are Giant, Goblin, Spirit Tongue and Sylvan. A multiclass Vanara ignores Shaman when determining the XP penalty. The trait list gives no tail action or racial attack. [Printed p. 16][vanara2]
- **Appearance and home.** The book describes a 4 feet 6 inches to 5 feet 6 inches range and typical weight of 90–140 pounds. Their light fur ranges from white through light blue to brown and black. Protruding muzzles, furred cheeks, wide lipless mouths, large ears, long fingers and toes and long semi-prehensile tails distinguish them. They have human-proportioned limbs and torsos. They live in deep forests and high mountains, chiefly hunting and gathering. The chapter says they are absent from Rokugan. Its language paragraph calls the language Vanaran and says it uses Common script, while the trait entry names it Vanara. [Printed p. 15][vanara] · [Printed p. 16][vanara2]

Chapter 5 gives additional character-generation tables for starting age, aging, and random height and weight. The pictured heights are examples and use different figures from the species descriptions. Neither playable trait entry assigns racial Hit Dice, a challenge rating or a level adjustment. [Printed p. 8][art] · [Printed pp. 69–70][vitals]

## Nezumi encounter entry

Printed p. 178 profiles a first-level warrior under Medium-Size Humanoid (Nezumi). It has CR 1/2, 1d8+1 HD (5 HP), initiative +0, speed 40 feet, AC 13 (+3 ashigaru armor), and 5-foot face and reach. It chooses nagamaki +2 melee (2d4), unarmed strike +1 melee (1d4) or composite shortbow +1 ranged (1d6). Saves are Fort +3, Ref +0, Will +0. Scores are Str 10, Dex 11, Con 12, Int 11, Wis 10, Cha 8. Skills are Climb +2, Hide +1, Jump +2 and Move Silently +1. Its feat is Weapon Focus (nagamaki). The entry lists Shadowlands terrain, standard treasure, usual chaotic neutral alignment, and advancement by character class. Groups are solitary, team (2–9), band (10–60) or pack (76–95). Page 179 describes using cover to ambush and harrying with ranged weapons before withdrawing. Several species traits are repeated there. This warrior’s feat entry contains Weapon Focus only. No corresponding Vanara encounter block appears in the book. [Printed p. 178][npc] · [Printed p. 179][npc2]

## Original illustrations and edition boundary

The grayscale lineup on printed p. 8 depicts a labeled Nezumi Female (5 feet 3 inches) and Vanara Male (4 feet 9 inches). The Nezumi appears second from left, with a long ratlike muzzle and bent legs. The Vanara appears far right, with a monkeylike face and a staff. The publisher preview preserves the lineup on p. 7. The figure page shows no individual artist credit or visible signature. The credits distinguish Raven Mimura’s cover credit from the roster of interior artists. The painted group on p. 179 is labeled as Oni. [Full scan, printed p. 8][art] · [Publisher preview, p. 7][preview-art] · [Book credits][credits] · [Printed p. 179][npc2]

James Wyatt’s April 2004 *Dragon* #318 article “Oriental Adventures Update: Eastern Flavor” removes all Vanara ability-score adjustments for 3.5. The 2001 entries use their original modifiers. The primary bestiary TSV and book-catalog JSON had no Nezumi or Vanara matches at review. [Dragon #318 article reproduction][dragon]
