# Nezumi and Vanara playable species

Two missing playable species from James Wyatt's 2001 *Oriental Adventures* for D&D 3.0. Nezumi traits come from printed pp. 13–14. Vanara anatomy and setting are on p. 15, and its playable traits are on p. 16. The p. 178 Nezumi Warrior is a published encounter reference. Tesk and Nemi are original, reusable NPCs. [Publisher preview]() · [Original book scan]() · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Species | Edition-specific traits | Runnable NPC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nezumi | 40-ft. speed, low-light vision, stealth and poison/disease save bonuses, and protection from acquiring Shadowlands Taint through exposure. Scent is an optional feat at Wisdom 11. | Tesk Reedmark, Warrior 1. A trail scout who can warn or guide the party. |
| Vanara | 30-ft. walk, 20-ft. climb, a climbing bonus, and the original −2 Str, +2 Int, +2 Wis. The book permits an accelerated 30-ft. climb with a −5 penalty. | Nemi, Expert 1. A canopy guide and bridge keeper who needs help moving a crew. |

The generated portraits use the anatomy described in the book and show both full figures on plain parchment. Clothing, poses, and equipment in the paintings are illustration choices. The published p. 8 lineup is recorded as a reference. [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json)
