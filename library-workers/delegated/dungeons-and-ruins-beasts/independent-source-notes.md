# Dungeons & Ruins underground mounts

The March 2025 Kobold Press book has both stat blocks in Chapter 1, printed pp. 28–29. The Scribd copy has the printed footers and matches the publisher’s ISBN 978-1-964506-04-3. The text extraction mixes the two columns, so some gear and vehicle lines fall inside the blocks. Search for `Earthworm, Giant CR` and `Round Beetle, Giant CR` to find the blocks. I found no publisher errata for these entries and did not verify the page artwork. [Book text on Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/850759299/Campaign-Builder-Dungeons-and-Ruins) · [Kobold Press sell sheet]()

## Earthworm, Giant (p. 28)

The catalog and book use the same heading. It is a Large Beast, CR 1/2, AC 12 (natural armor), HP 22, speed 40 ft. and burrow 20 ft. Perception 10, Stealth 11, immune to prone, keensense 30 ft., tremorsense 60 ft., no languages. The book gives modifiers in STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA order as +2/+1/+3/−5/+0/−4. The p. 29 mount table lists 500 gp and a 480 lb. carrying capacity. [Book pp. 28–29](https://www.scribd.com/document/850759299/Campaign-Builder-Dungeons-and-Ruins)

- Water Aversion keeps the worm out of bodies of water. If water fills its tunnel as it burrows, it must Dash away on its next turn.
- Bite is +4, reach 5 ft., one target, for 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning.
- Thrash recharges on 4–6. Each creature within 5 ft. makes a DC 13 DEX save against 7 (2d6) bludgeoning, with half damage on success. A failure by 5 or more also knocks the creature prone.
- Reflexive Writhe sets its AC to 14 against the triggering attack from a creature it can see.

## Round Beetle, Giant (p. 29)

The book prints `Round Beetle, Giant`, and the catalog labels the record `Beetle, Giant Round`. The block gives exact CR 1/4, which the catalog index serializes as 0.3. It is a Large Beast, AC 12 (natural armor), HP 14, speed 30 ft. and climb 30 ft. Perception 10, Stealth 11, keensense 10 ft., no languages. Its modifiers in the same order are +3/+1/+2/−5/+0/−4. The mount table lists 200 gp and a 480 lb. carrying capacity. [Book p. 29](https://www.scribd.com/document/850759299/Campaign-Builder-Dungeons-and-Ruins)

- Knockdown Charge requires 20 ft. or more of straight movement toward a creature. DC 12 STR or prone. If the target is prone, the beetle can Bite it as a bonus action. The charge does not require a prior Bite hit.
- Bite is +5, reach 5 ft., one creature, for 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing.
- Poison Spit is a reaction to taking damage. Creatures in the 15 ft. line make a DC 12 DEX save. Failure deals 1d6 poison and poisons for 1 minute. Success halves the damage and avoids the condition. A poisoned creature can end the condition with a DC 12 CON save at the end of each turn.

## Riding and appearance

The p. 28 rule applies when an underground mount climbs or burrows. The rider makes a WIS (Animal Handling) check at DC 10 + 1 per 5 ft. traveled or is thrown off. A secure saddle takes two rounds to fasten and gives advantage on checks or saves to stay mounted. Both creatures are presented as underground mounts. The text supports a large worm and a rounded beetle with natural armor, and gives no color or pattern for either.
