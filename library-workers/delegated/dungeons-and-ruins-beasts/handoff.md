# Dungeons & Ruins beasts

Both beast profiles come from the 2025 first printing of Kobold Press's *Campaign Builder: Dungeons & Ruins*. The publicly indexed copy presents the publisher's book text and credits with ISBN 978-1-964506-04-3. Its text extraction interleaves the page columns, so the profiles follow each stat block and the shared mount section. [Publisher catalog]() · [Book text, pp. 28–29](https://www.scribd.com/document/850759299/Campaign-Builder-Dungeons-and-Ruins)

- **Giant Round Beetle** has CR 1/4, AC 12, 14 HP, Knockdown Charge and a Poison Spit reaction. The catalog retains the name `Beetle, Giant Round` from the publisher's index. The profile displays the printed fraction where the index had rounded it to 0.3. [Publisher index](https://koboldpress.com/tools/search-tov-kobold-press-monsters/)
- **Giant Earthworm** has CR 1/2, AC 12, 22 HP, Thrash, and an AC 14 reaction that applies to one attack. It avoids water and must Dash away if its tunnel floods while it is burrowing.

Both creatures are underground mounts with a 480-lb. carrying capacity. Each profile includes the rider's climbing or burrowing check and the Secure Saddle rule. The publisher lists no errata for their stat blocks or the mount rules on pp. 28–29. [Published errata](https://koboldpress.com/errata/#:~:text=Dungeons%20and%20Ruins)

Both existing parchment portraits were reused. Their colors are artistic choices. The beetle gallery's other images are credited Giant Fire Beetle references from separate books. [Portrait sheet](portrait-comparison.jpg) · [Portrait hashes](portrait-reuse.json)
