# Dream Dwarf for D&D 3.5

One playable Dream Dwarf profile from the August 2004 first printing of *Races of Stone*. The original rules give the subrace 90-ft. darkvision, Stonecunning, and Dream Sight, which makes ethereal creatures visible and distinguishable. The 20-ft. land speed holds in medium or heavy armor and under medium or heavy loads. [Printed traits, p. 89]() · [Species rules](book-species.json)

Spell Power gives +1 effective caster level for divinations or spells with the earth descriptor while in contact with the ground. The example Druid’s *speak with animals* lasts two minutes while grounded and one minute at her base caster level. Her *entangle* uses caster level 1 in either case. [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md)

Maera Dol-Vel is an original Druid 1 and spring tender at provisional CR 1. Her full-body portrait uses plain parchment and the small abstract embroidery described in the source. Ornek Tarn-Iol is an original Expert 1 and seam reader at provisional CR 1/2. His card gives the ordinary Search total and the Stonecunning total for unusual stonework.
