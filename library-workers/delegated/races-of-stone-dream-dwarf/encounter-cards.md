# Dream Dwarf encounter cards for D&D 3.5

Both NPCs are original. Both are Medium humanoids with the dwarf subtype, 90-ft. darkvision, 20-ft. land speed, and Dream Sight. Ethereal creatures appear translucent and indistinct to them. Stability adds +4 on ability checks to resist a bull rush or trip when they stand firmly on the ground. [Original traits, p. 89]() · [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

Maera wants to know why the animals stopped drinking at the spring. She can speak with a badger from the bank while the party checks the spillway. She uses *entangle* in the reeds if she needs time to withdraw.

## Ornek Tarn-Iol, Expert 1

Provisional CR 1/2, ECL 1. AC 13, touch 11, flat-footed 12. HP 9, initiative +1. Fort +3, Ref +1, Will +3. BAB +0, grapple +1. STR 12, DEX 13, CON 16, INT 12, WIS 12, CHA 8. Common, Dwarven, Terran.

| Attack | Roll and damage |
| --- | --- |
| Heavy mace | +1 melee, 1d8+1 bludgeoning, ×2 |
| Light crossbow | +1 ranged, 1d8 piercing, 19–20/×2, 80-ft. increment. Reload as a move action. |

Appraise +5, Craft (stoneworking) +5, Knowledge (architecture and engineering) +5, Listen +5, Search +8 or +10 for unusual stonework, Sense Motive +5, Spot +5. Stonecunning gives the +2 on unusual stonework. It also allows a check within 10 ft. as if actively looking, and covers finding stonework traps.

Ornek saw an ethereal traveler pass through the old tunnel entrance. The entrance has since been bricked up. Bring him the old floor plan and he will check the new masonry for the seam.
