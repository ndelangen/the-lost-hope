# Independent Dream Dwarf reviews

The source reviewer checked the August 2004 first printing. Jim Nelson drew the meditating Dream Dwarf on p. 88. The accompanying prose describes dark gray to deep brown skin, dark hair, pale eyes, and clothing decorated with abstract motifs. Page 89 gives the complete racial traits. The text identifies Dream Dwarf as a dwarf subrace and leaves the type, racial Hit Dice, and level adjustment implicit. The reviewer found no Dream Dwarf-specific correction in Wizards’ collection. [Art and description]() · [Rules]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Dream Sight reveals ethereal creatures, which look translucent and indistinct. The entry specifies no separate range, activation, or cross-plane targeting rule. Spell Power raises effective caster level when the dwarf is in contact with the ground and casts a divination or an earth-descriptor spell. It applies to level-dependent variables and caster level checks. It stacks with other Spell Power abilities. [Rules]()

The original book includes a Dream Dwarf druid story seed on p. 191. The seed supplies no stat block. Kyliki Turco, the published Earth Dreamer example, is a gnome. Maera and Ornek are original encounter builds. [Dream Dwarf seed]() · [Earth Dreamer example]()

The second reviewer checked both builds, including Maera’s spell preparation, grounded ranges and durations, and Ornek’s Search +8 and Stonecunning Search +10. Maera uses 16 skill points and Ornek uses 28. The reviewer confirmed the patch applies and keeps the Dream Dwarf profile distinct from Dwarf.
