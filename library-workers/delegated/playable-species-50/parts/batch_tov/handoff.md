# Tales of the Valiant

Five playable profiles are staged with one original level 1 NPC and one original square portrait each. [Species rules](book-species.json) · [NPC builds](npc-builds.json) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json) · [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg)

- **Dragonborn.** Varesh Cinderkeel uses void ancestry for a necrotic cone or line. The ancestry introduction mentions Natural Adaptation, but the printed trait block provides no separate effect. [Labyrinth Worldbook, pp. 136–137](https://www.scribd.com/document/897921400/Labyrinth-Worldbook-Mpzq99)
- **Gearforged.** Brasswell hides an integrated shortsword in an articulated arm. The profile includes the other chassis upgrades and uses the printed Humanoid type. [Labyrinth Worldbook, pp. 137–139](https://www.scribd.com/document/897921400/Labyrinth-Worldbook-Mpzq99)
- **Satyr.** Tam Mossdial can land a horn strike and move 15 feet as a reaction. The build uses Strength for the horns and reserves the same reaction for Last Stand when injured. [Labyrinth Worldbook, p. 140](https://www.scribd.com/document/897921400/Labyrinth-Worldbook-Mpzq99)
- **Syderean.** Mira Glassbeck uses the Spellborn adaptation to resist damage from nonmagical weapons and change the damage type of an attack or spell. The base lineage traits come from the Player’s Guide. [Labyrinth Worldbook, pp. 141–142](https://www.scribd.com/document/897921400/Labyrinth-Worldbook-Mpzq99) · [Player’s Guide, p. 110](https://anyflip.com/fflfu/egji/basic/101-150)
- **Sapopova.** Tavi Reedcap is an amphibious Toadfolk lookout with full-color darkvision. The included profile covers Frogkin and Toadfolk from the publisher’s preview. [Player’s Guide 2 Preview, p. 17]()

The NPCs use the first-level Black Flag class rules. I reviewed the source summaries, encounter math, and artwork by eye, including the 96-pixel preview. Automated verification is deferred.
