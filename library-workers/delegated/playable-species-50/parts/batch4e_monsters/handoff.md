# Five playable options from the 4e Monster Manual

The June 2008 Monster Manual appendix gives playable-with-permission racial traits for these five species. Every profile has an original level 1 encounter NPC and one square portrait on Big Linda's plain parchment. The encounter blocks use the 2008 Dungeon Master's Guide NPC rules and Player's Handbook class powers. [Monster Manual, pp. 276–280]() · [NPC design, pp. 187–188]()

| Species | Original NPC | Encounter trait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doppelganger | Vessa Hallow, courier liaison | Change Shape impersonation |
| Githyanki | Kareth Venn, crossing scout | Telekinetic Leap carries one ally over a gap |
| Gnoll | Rasha Longstride, outrider | Blood Fury and Ferocious Charge |
| Kobold | Nik Coppernotch, switchback guide | Trap Sense and Shifty |
| Warforged | Brasswake, doorholder | Warforged Resolve while bloodied |

The Doppelganger uses the June 2008 appendix name and rules. The 2009 Eberron Player's Guide calls the related player profile Changeling and gives it its own traits. The January 2010 update uses Changeling in the Eberron player feat references. The staged Warforged likewise uses the appendix's bloodied-only Resolve and its 3 + half level temporary HP. [Eberron Player's Guide, pp. 28–33]() · [January 2010 update]()
