# Fourth-edition player profiles

This part adds Deva, Githzerai, Minotaur, Shardmind, and Wilden. Each has one original level 1 NPC using fourth-edition character math and one square portrait. [Player’s Handbook 2, Deva]() · [Player’s Handbook 3, four player races]()

| Profile | NPC | Encounter feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deva | Selian Dawnledger, invoker | Adds a die to a roll after seeing the result. |
| Githzerai | Reth Quietmere, monk | Raises all defenses against a hit and repositions on second wind. |
| Minotaur | Brann Lowvault, fighter | Knocks an enemy prone on a charge and can strike when reduced to 0 HP. |
| Shardmind | Nura Glasswake, wizard | Disperses into shards and reforms nearby after granting combat advantage. |
| Wilden | Briar Reedfoot, warden | Chooses one aspect per extended rest. Hunter lets Briar follow a moving opponent. |

I read the printed rules and checked the build arithmetic, first-level powers, and art by hand. The Minotaur scan shows 2d6 paragon damage for Goring Charge despite the text layer’s 1d6. Search, portrait loading in the app, and automated verification remain deferred.

OpenAI ImageGen made the portraits using Big Linda as the parchment reference. The comparison reads left to right, Big Linda, Deva, Githzerai, then Minotaur, Shardmind, Wilden.
