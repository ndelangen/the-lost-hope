# Daelkyr Half-Blood

The October 2005 first printing of *Magic of Eberron* gives this Medium Aberration a personal symbiont. Lysa Fenwick's throwing scarab supplies one ranged shard each round. Deren Valehart's crawling gauntlet supplies one natural claw. Each host starts with the symbiont attached and gains 2 HP from Symbiont Mastery. [Player traits]() · [Symbiont Mastery]()

The attached throwing scarab uses Lysa's normal ranged attack, with a 20-ft. range increment. The shard deals 1d6 slashing and 1d6 acid. A critical multiplies the slashing damage. The published rogue lists the shard without Strength damage, which the new build follows. The scarab's direct HP and AC sit beside the host's stats. A visible attached symbiont can be targeted separately. [Throwing scarab]() · [Published example]() · [Symbiont general rules](https://anyflip.com/groks/gypc/basic/301-325)

Deren uses his own Strength and base attack with the gauntlet's claw. The gauntlet's detached creature stats stay in its source. Each encounter is rated provisionally at CR 3 using the symbiont entries' host +1 guidance. The sourcebook's Rogue 3 example prints CR 3 with its scarab already attached. [Crawling gauntlet]() · [Published example]()

The original portrait shows Lysa with a small blue scarab on the back of her hand, based on the book's description. Steve Prescott drew the published half-blood and psiforged plate. Francis Tsai drew the symbionts. [Wizards' art gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20161101192337/http://archive.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/ag/20051010a&page=4)
