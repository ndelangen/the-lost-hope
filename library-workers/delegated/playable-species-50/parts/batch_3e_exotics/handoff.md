# Karsite, Lumi, Maug, and Nerra

Four playable species for the 50-entry batch. Each has one original encounter build and a square portrait staged for integration. All four use D&D 3.5.

| Species | Original encounter | Table rule to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Karsite | Ilyra Keene, shieldwright and Fighter 2 | A melee hit can suppress one magical item. A spell stopped by spell resistance can heal Ilyra. |
| Lumi | Aurin Vell, oath witness with two racial Hit Dice | The floating head denies flanking bonuses. At-will light, healing, and glitterdust support the escort. |
| Maug | Basalt Wren, ramp engineer and Fighter 1 | Pulverize can remove an object's hardness briefly. Rapid Repair can restore hit points during a rest. |
| Nerra, Varoot | Sive, threshold guide and Rogue 2 | Mirror Jump links reflective entry points to mirrors. Reflective spell resistance can send a targeted spell back to the caster. |

The Karsite encounter uses its book's PC-class CR formula. The Lumi uses the official errata for Extraplanar and the core spell text for disrupt undead. Both Fiend Folio profiles use revised core rules. Maug retains two construct Hit Dice and LA +3. Sive follows the printed Varoot PC example, where the first class level replaces the single outsider Hit Die. Sive uses 3.5 disguise self and the revised wounding property. The other Nerra variants are identified in the source notes and outside this Varoot profile.
