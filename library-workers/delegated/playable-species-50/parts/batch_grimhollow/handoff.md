# Grim Hollow player profiles, 2021

Five species from Ghostfire Gaming’s original 2014-compatible guide, each with one original NPC and a plain-parchment portrait. The revised D&D Beyond guide has different heritage rules and retains its separate catalog entries. [Publisher overview](https://ghostfiregaming.com/blog/discovering-the-races-of-grim-hollow/)

| Species | Original source | NPC | Encounter use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Disembodied | [The Player’s Guide, p. 15](https://anyflip.com/usqxr/dvib/basic) | Elian Voss, Divination Wizard 3 | A witness investigator who can wait out danger on the Ethereal Plane. |
| Dreamer | [The Player’s Guide, p. 14](https://anyflip.com/gtpst/dzwp/basic) | Vela Stonewake, Fighter 2 | A shield-bearing expedition archivist who listens for clues in dreams and can recover a Hit Die with a nap. |
| Laneshi | [The Player’s Guide, pp. 10–11](https://anyflip.com/gtpst/dzwp/basic) | Teral Brineford, Ranger 2 | A warrior-caste shore escort who can swim away after a hit. The profile also includes the Mystic caste. |
| Ogresh | [The Player’s Guide, pp. 11–12](https://anyflip.com/aysnx/chqg/basic) | Marel Broadbank, Bard 2 | A negotiator who can charm a nonhostile listener and learn one relevant fact. |
| Wechselkind | [The Player’s Guide, p. 9](https://anyflip.com/lphgc/qpve/basic) | Pip Cinderbutton, Rogue 2 | An infirmary runner who moves through crowds and is immune to disease. Artificial Form counts this original profile as Humanoid. Pip takes Dwarvish from the [2014 Noble background](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/personality-and-background#Noble). |
