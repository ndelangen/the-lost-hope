# Five revised SRD playable profiles

The revised 3.5 SRD gives explicit player rules for each of these species. The matching monster records already exist. This batch adds the playable profiles, one original encounter each, and five square portraits.

- **Centaur.** Myrine Ashrein changes between a composite bow and a lance. The profile preserves the printed carrying limit and the lance charge rule. [Centaurs as characters](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/centaur.htm#centaursAsCharacters)
- **Hound Archon.** Brother Brindle tracks in canine form and protects the group with *aid* and *magic circle against evil*. His teleport carries him and up to 50 lb. of objects. [Hound Archons as characters](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/archon.htm#houndArchonsAsCharacters)
- **Janni.** Nura bint Sahl takes a linked party across planes. Time on an Elemental Plane matters because her protection runs out after 48 hours. [Jann as characters](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/genie.htm#jannAsCharacters)
- **Lizardfolk.** Vesh Reedcurrent uses Swim and Hold Breath to free a submerged ferry line. The source grants a Swim bonus and a land speed. [Lizardfolk as characters](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/lizardfolk.htm#lizardfolkAsCharacters)
- **Pixie.** Bren Hazelwing combines invisibility and illusions to cover a courier route. The build uses the +4 level adjustment and ordinary arrows. [Pixies as characters](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/sprite.htm#pixiesAsCharacters)

I compared each profile against the source's player section, walked through the encounter math by hand, and inspected the parchment portraits at full and thumbnail sizes. Catalog integration and runtime verification are pending.
