# Five 4e playable species

The staged records add five playable profiles and five level 1 NPCs. The NPCs use player class rules and the listed equipment. The two Athasian builds leave campaign themes open.

| Profile | Original source | Encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genasi | [Forgotten Realms Player’s Guide, pp. 10–11]() | A firesoul swordmage shields the forge crew. |
| Kalashtar | [Eberron Player’s Guide, pp. 30–31](https://anyflip.com/yhggl/brmq/basic) | A cleric relays warnings by telepathy and protects nearby allies against Will attacks. |
| Mul | [Dark Sun Campaign Setting, pp. 20–21]() | A fighter holds a choke point and uses Incredible Toughness to clear a disabling condition. |
| Thri-kreen | [Dark Sun Campaign Setting, pp. 22–23]() | A clutch scout jumps gaps and uses a minor claw attack when surrounded. |
| Bladeling | [Manual of the Planes, pp. 116–117](https://anyflip.com/gwttt/kvvs/basic/101-150) | A rogue clears a gate after moving allies away from Razor Storm. |

The original Bladeling Characters entry is on printed p. 117. See the [rules and edition distinctions](source-evidence.json) and the [NPC builds](npc-builds.json).
