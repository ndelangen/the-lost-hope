# Book of Ebon Tides

Five playable profiles, five original encounter NPCs, and five square portraits. Each profile fills an existing catalog entry for *Book of Ebon Tides* under Fifth edition compatible. The NPCs use the 2014 class rules and fixed HP gains after level 1. [Book preview]() · [Player traits in the printed book]() · [2014 class rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

| Profile | Encounter NPC | Rule to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bearfolk, pp. 22–24 | Halla Duskbark, waystone warden | Shadowborn can Hide as a bonus action in dim light or darkness. |
| Darakhul, pp. 24–27 | Rell Ashgate, ferry keeper | The playable Darakhul is Humanoid and can be affected by rules that target Undead. Bearfolk and Shadow Goblin are the heritages printed here. |
| Quickstep, pp. 32–33 | Copperflash, court courier | Startling Speed gives attackers disadvantage after sufficient movement. Daily rounds scale with character level. |
| Ratatosk, pp. 33–35 | Minnet Branchbell, branch guard | Tiny Ekorre have special weapon rules. Small Tradvakt use Constitution for War Chatter. |
| Shadow Goblin, pp. 29–30 | Merrin Netward, road mediator | The playable Stink Eye uses an action and affects the target’s next qualifying roll. |

I compared the rules to the 2022 text, checked each NPC’s bonuses by hand, and viewed the portraits as a full strip and at 96 pixels. The portrait order in the strip matches the table. The NPCs use character levels without assigned challenge ratings. Automated checks and in-app review are deferred.
