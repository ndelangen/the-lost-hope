# Humblewood birdfolk

Corvum, Gallus, Luma, Raptor, and Strig fill five existing Humblewood catalog entries. Each entry keeps both printed subraces together and has one original NPC build. The player rules use the publisher's v1.04 text and the 2014 rules. Glide requires a fall. Wing Flap belongs to Gallus and Luma. For NPC talon attacks, Appendix C adds Strength or Dexterity to the base 1d4 damage. The builds use the player Glide rule and carry only gear that it allows. Appendix C also lets birdfolk NPCs glide with heavy weapons, heavy armor, or shields when that gear appears in their stat block. [Hit Point Press campaign book](https://hitpointpress.com/products/humblewood-campaign-book) · [v1.04, pp. 12–21 and 210–211]() · [D&D Basic Rules (2014)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/classes)

The [player rules](book-species.json), [NPC builds](npc-builds.json), [source evidence](source-evidence.json), and [portrait mapping](portrait-files.json) are ready for staging. The five NPCs are an appraiser, an orchard keeper, a songkeeper, a canopy lookout, and a bridge keeper. Their CRs are estimates.
