# Tiefling encounters

Both are Medium Native Outsiders on the Material Plane. Each has one class level, LA +1, ECL 2, and provisional CR 2. They have darkvision 60 ft. and resistance 5 each to cold, electricity, and fire. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

Both can use darkness once per day at CL 1. Touch an object as a standard action. It creates a 20-ft. radius of shadowy illumination for 10 minutes and can be dismissed. Creatures in the area have concealment, with a 20% miss chance even against darkvision. A lightproof cover blocks the effect while the object is covered. [Darkness spell](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/darkness.htm)

## Sella Cinderlock, Rogue 1

Sella checks the lockhouse gate for a trap. If the party needs to retreat, she casts darkness on a copper token and leaves it at the bend.

- HP 8, AC 16, initiative +4, speed 30 ft. Fortitude +2, Reflex +6, Will +0.
- Rapier +1 for 1d6+1 piercing. Shortbow +4 for 1d6 piercing, or +5 for 1d6+1 within 30 ft.
- Add 1d6 sneak attack when the target loses its Dexterity bonus to AC or Sella flanks it. Ranged sneak attacks are limited to 30 ft. Concealment prevents sneak attack even if Sella can see the target with darkvision.
- Trapfinding. Search +7, Disable Device +7, and Open Lock +8.

## Verren Vale, Fighter 1

Verren puts darkness on the latch while the last workers reach the cross-passage. He holds the gate in melee until the others clear it.

- HP 12, AC 17, initiative +2, speed 30 ft. Fortitude +4, Reflex +2, Will +0.
- Longsword +5 for 1d8+3 slashing. Javelin +3 for 1d6+3 piercing.
- Blind-Fight lets him reroll the miss chance once when concealment causes a melee miss. The miss chance still applies, and his javelin gets no reroll.
