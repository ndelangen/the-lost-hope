# Tiefling source check

The July 2004 first printing introduces Tieflings on p. 15 and gives their player traits on p. 16. The November 2004 errata has no Tiefling correction. The profile grants darkness once per day at character level. The separate Monster Manual warrior has its own entry. [First printing]() · [Wizards errata archive](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

The core 3.5 spell creates shadowy illumination around a touched object. The resulting concealment gives a 20% miss chance even against darkvision. Sneak attack requires a target without concealment. Blind-Fight gives a melee miss-chance reroll. The independent review also checked both builds’ attacks, skills, armor, and publication scope. [Darkness](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/darkness.htm) · [Rogue](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/rogue.htm#sneakAttack) · [Blind-Fight](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/feats.htm#blindFight)

Emily Fiegenschuh illustrated the unnamed rogue on p. 16. The portrait uses small horns and reddish eyes from p. 15’s list of possible features. Sella’s clothing, pose, and copper token are original. [Book illustration]()
