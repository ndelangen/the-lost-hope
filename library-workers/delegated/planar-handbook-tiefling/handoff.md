# Tiefling from Planar Handbook

This batch adds the 2004 D&D 3.5 Tiefling profile and two original encounters. Each first-level build uses darkness once per day at caster level 1. Level adjustment raises ECL to 2 without changing the ability’s caster level. The 3.5 spell grants concealment even against darkvision. [Original rules, pp. 15–16]() · [Encounter cards](encounter-cards.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

Sella uses a token to cover a retreat. She needs a target without concealment to use sneak attack. Verren’s Blind-Fight feat gives him one reroll when concealment causes a melee miss. [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Source evidence](source-evidence.json)

Sella’s portrait appears on the 2004 profile and uses Big Linda’s plain parchment. The 2024 Player’s Handbook profile remains the default. The list thumbnail and Mina Letterturn’s existing encounter are preserved. The temporary copy found the new profile by name, trait, and NPC role and returned both new encounters from both catalog commands.
