# Fey’ri rules sources

- The March 2003 *Races of Faerûn* gives the player traits, including at-will *alter self* as a spell-like ability to assume humanoid form indefinitely. The 2004 update to the earlier *Monsters of Faerûn* creature instead gives Change Shape and DR 10/magic. This staged 3.5 profile applies those two conversions to the 2003 player traits. [Player traits, pp. 119–120]() · [Monster Update, p. 5]()
- Change Shape keeps the Fey’ri’s type and ability scores and adopts the new form’s movement. It gives +10 on Disguise, and *true seeing* reveals the natural form. Vaelis’s human disguise walks at 30 ft. Her natural winged form flies at 40 ft. with poor maneuverability, subject to the core light-load limit. [Change Shape, pp. 306–307]() · [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP) · [Core flight rules](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#fly)
- Choose four Demonic Abilities total. Damage reduction, *dimension door*, and *enervation* are mutually exclusive choices, each raising LA to +3. Vaelis’s four choices give LA +2. Nyrel chooses *dimension door* for LA +3. The racial spell-like abilities use the character’s class level in these two builds, so Nyrel’s *dimension door* reaches 440 ft. and has no passenger allowance at caster level 1. Both CRs are provisional. [Demonic Abilities, p. 120]() · [Dimension Door](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/dimensionDoor.htm)
- The 2004 *Player’s Guide to Faerûn* offers a campaign-wide lesser planetouched option. Both builds use the native Outsider Fey’ri from the primary sources. [Lesser planetouched, pp. 190–191]()

Vaelis’s batlike wings and pointed tail follow the written description. Vinod Rams illustrated several Fey’ri in the original book. [Description, p. 118]() · [Illustration, p. 119]()
