# Fey’ri

This batch adds the playable Fey’ri from the March 2003 *Races of Faerûn* with an explicit 3.5 adaptation. Vaelis Ashthread and Nyrel Duskmantle are original NPCs. Vaelis’s plain-parchment portrait shows her natural winged form.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and both encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/feyri-draft.png). The [comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Vaelis beside Big Linda. The [portrait revision](portrait-revision.jpg) shows the first pose and the staged folded-wing pose.

In a temporary copy, search found the species by both apostrophe spellings, Change Shape, Dimension Door, and both NPC roles. The portrait loaded for the 2003 profile and as its list thumbnail. Both catalog commands returned the complete builds for that publication. [Verification](verification.json)
