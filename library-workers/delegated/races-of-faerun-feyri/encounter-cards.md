# Fey’ri encounters

Both have 60-ft. darkvision, low-light vision, and a 30-ft. land speed. They fly at 40 ft. with poor maneuverability in their natural winged form with a light load. The 3.5 adaptation uses Change Shape as a standard action. A humanoid guise uses that form’s movement and grants +10 to Disguise.

## Vaelis Ashthread, canopy scout

**Rogue 1, provisional CR 2, ECL 3.** AC 16, HP 7, initiative +8. Fortitude +1, Reflex +6, Will +1. Shortbow +4 or rapier +0, each for 1d6 piercing. Sneak Attack adds 1d6 to eligible hits.

Vaelis passes the checkpoint in a human disguise at Disguise +14. Once she reaches the far side, she returns to her natural form before flying across the gap. She has *darkness* and *detect thoughts* once per day each at caster level 1. *Detect thoughts* is Will DC 12.

## Nyrel Duskmantle, courtyard courier

**Sorcerer 1, provisional CR 2, ECL 4.** AC 13, HP 5, initiative +7. Fortitude +1, Reflex +3, Will +2. Light crossbow +3 for 1d8 piercing. *Mage armor* raises AC to 17 for 1 hour.

Nyrel uses *dimension door* once per day at caster level 1 to cross up to 440 ft. alone. After arriving, Nyrel acts again on the next turn. The other racial spells are *charm person* (Will DC 14) and *detect thoughts* (Will DC 15), once per day each.
