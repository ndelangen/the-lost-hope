# Uldra from Frostburn

This batch adds the D&D 3.5 Uldra from the September 2004 first printing with two original encounters. Both builds distinguish protection from cold environments from resistance 5 against cold attacks. Rimewillow uses Frosty Touch with a held staff. Stillrime uses it on unarmed strikes.
