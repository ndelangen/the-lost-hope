# Uldra encounters

Both are Small Fey with one class Hit Die, LA +1, ECL 2, and provisional CR 2. Both walk 20 ft. and have darkvision 120 ft. and low-light vision. Cold environments harm neither the Uldra nor its equipment. Attacks that deal cold damage meet cold resistance 5. [Full builds](npc-builds.json)

Frosty Touch takes a free action to activate. It adds 1 cold damage to unarmed strikes while the hands are frosty and to melee weapon hits while the weapon is held. The bonus does not stack with cold damage granted by a magic weapon.

Both have racial abilities at CL 1. Ray of frost is available three times per day, attacks at +3 ranged touch, and deals 1d3 cold. Speak with animals is available once per day for one minute. Touch of fatigue is available once per day at +1 melee touch. It lasts one round on a failed Fortitude DC 13 save. [Ray of frost](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/rayOfFrost.htm) · [Touch of fatigue](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/touchOfFatigue.htm)

## Rimewillow, Druid 1

Rimewillow asks a fox which trail is open, then leads the party through the cold hollow.

- HP 11, AC 15, initiative +2. Fortitude +5, Reflex +2, Will +5.
- Knowledge (nature) +12 and Survival +9.
- Small quarterstaff +1 for 1d4 bludgeoning with one end. Add 1 cold on a hit when Frosty Touch is active and she is holding the staff.
- Entangle is Reflex DC 14 and needs vegetation. Cure light wounds restores 1d8+1 HP.

## Stillrime, Monk 1

Stillrime guards the pass shelter while the party guides stranded travelers inside.

- HP 11, AC 16, initiative +2. Fortitude +5, Reflex +4, Will +5.
- Unarmed strike +1 for 1d4 bludgeoning. A flurry makes two attacks at −1 each. With Frosty Touch, add 1 cold to each hit.
- Stunning Fist is available once per day. Declare it before the attack. If the attack deals damage, the target must pass Fortitude DC 13 or be stunned for one round.
