# Uldra source check

The September 2004 first printing describes Uldras on pp. 38–40 and gives the player rules on p. 40. The separate Warrior on pp. 158–159 has printed CR ½. The staged characters are original and each has provisional CR 2. [Player rules]() · [Published Warrior]()

Cold environment protection covers the Uldra and its equipment, while attacks face cold resistance 5. Frosty Touch gives +1 cold to unarmed attacks and held melee weapons. The p. 158 Warrior and Grimsickle’s p. 186 encounter show the cold bonus on the melee weapon. Their ranged weapons have no such bonus. The player entry uses Hit Dice for racial caster level and Wisdom for save DCs. The independent review checked both builds’ skill points, attacks, and DCs. [Player rules]() · [Grimsickle encounter]()

Wizards’ February 2006 errata changes Midgard Dwarf and Marzanna, with no Uldra change. David Griffith illustrated the Uldra in the group image on p. 39. The pale skin, blue hair, large ears, and pointed hat informed Rimewillow’s portrait. The pose, clothing, and staff are original.
