## Rats (8)

- **Adventure instance.** Encounter 3, *Rat Race*, p. 5, occurs in mapped area 2 after all party members arrive, at a time the DM chooses. The encounter is EL 2. Lightning opens a crevice and floods the nest, causing eight rats to rush into the chamber. Four have 2 hp each and four have 1 hp each. They attack the nearest visible targets in groups of at most three per target. An injured rat that survives flees on its next move action. The text confirms a successful bite deals at least 1 damage despite the printed 1d3−4. These are eight individual Rats. The text’s collective movement carries no Rat Swarm rules. [Adventure p. 5]()
- **Linked base.** *Monster Manual* v.3.5 p. 278 gives each Rat CR 1/8, Tiny Animal, HD ¼d8 (typical 1 hp), initiative +2, land, climb and swim speeds 15 ft. each, AC 14/touch 14/flat-footed 12, base attack +0/grapple −12, bite +4 melee (1d3−4), space 2½ ft./reach 0 ft., no special attacks, low-light vision and scent. Fort +2, Ref +4, Will +1. Str 2, Dex 15, Con 10, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 2. Balance +10, Climb +12, Hide +14, Move Silently +10 and Swim +10. Weapon Finesse. The animal type provides neutral alignment. The base rat usually flees and bites only as a last resort. The adventure replaces that default behavior for the flooded nest. [Monster Manual p. 278]()

## Medium Monstrous Spider

- **Adventure instance.** Encounter 5, *What a Tangled Web*, is EL 1 and spans pp. 5–6. A single 11 hp web-spinner hides on the webbed ceiling of the short Hightower passage. Its web-assisted skills are Hide +15 and Move Silently +11. The nearly invisible doorway web requires Spot DC 20 to notice. Touching it applies the net effects and holds the target in place at the doorway. Only one creature can be caught in the doorway web at once. The web has DR 5/—, 6 hp, break DC 16 and Escape Artist DC 12. Weapons that strike it stick and require a DC 16 Strength check to free. [Adventure pp. 5–6]()
- **Tactics.** The spider attacks when a character is stuck in or attacks the doorway web, with surprise if possible. It always attacks the nearest target and ignores flanking and target priority. It can throw a web only when no character is currently entangled in a web. The webbed lumps are remains of rats and humanoids, rather than additional active creatures. [Adventure p. 6]()
- **Linked base.** *Monster Manual* v.3.5 p. 288 gives Medium Vermin, CR 1, 2d8+2 (11 hp), initiative +3, speed 30 ft. and climb 20 ft., AC 14/touch 13/flat-footed 11, base attack and grapple +1 each, bite +4 (1d6 plus poison), space and reach 5 ft., poison and web, darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft. and vermin traits. Fort +4, Ref +3, Will +0. Str 11, Dex 17, Con 12, Int —, Wis 10, Cha 2. Climb +11, Hide +7, Jump +0 and Spot +4 before applicable footnotes, plus bonus Weapon Finesse. The web-spinner’s +8 when using its webs explains the adventure’s adjusted skills. Poison is Fortitude DC 12 with 1d4 Strength damage both initially and secondarily. [Monster Manual p. 288]()
- **Linked web rules.** The p. 289 web-spinner may throw a web eight times per day with a 10-foot range increment, 50-foot maximum and a target at most one size category larger. The family rule’s usual Escape Artist and break actions, ceiling-web movement at climb speed, and ability to pinpoint creatures touching its web remain part of the linked rules. The scenario supplies its specific doorway restrictions and throwing tactic. [Monster Manual p. 289]()

The two profiles fit the library’s Beast category as animal forms. Their original types remain Tiny Animal and Medium Vermin. The encounter-specific HP, trap and tactics come from the adventure, and the remaining entries come from its explicit Monster Manual cross-references.
