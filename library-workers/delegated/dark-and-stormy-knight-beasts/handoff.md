# A Dark and Stormy Knight beasts

Two encounter profiles from Owen K. C. Stephens's 2005 D&D 3.5 adventure. The patch follows the archived Wizards release. The local reference is a later with identical extracted text across all eight pages. The source notes record both hashes. [Original release](https://web.archive.org/web/20060304014750id_/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=dnd/oa/20050329a) · [Original ZIP](https://web.archive.org/web/20060304014750id_/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/files/A_Dark_and_Stormy_Knight.zip) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md)

| Creature | Adventure source | Individual and encounter values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rat | p. 5, Rat Race in map area 2 | Eight CR 1/8 rats in an EL 2 encounter. Four have 2 HP each and four have 1 HP each. |
| Medium Monstrous Spider | pp. 5–6, What a Tangled Web | One CR 1 spider with 11 HP in an EL 1 encounter. |

The rat keeps the original bite formula and the encounter's minimum of 1 damage. The spider keeps its Monster Manual baseline and the adventure's separate ceiling-web modifiers, doorway web, and attack trigger. [Exact source pages and hashes](source-evidence.json) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

The two square portraits are newly generated on plain parchment. The adventure credits Nick Isaac for cartography and has no creature illustrations on the encounter pages. The rat's wet fur is an illustration choice based on the flooded nest. [Portrait sheet](portrait-sheet.jpg) · [96-pixel previews](portrait-thumbnails.jpg) · [Prompts](portrait-prompts.json) · [Portrait index additions](portrait-index-preview.json)

Both profiles passed search by name and encounter text in a temporary library copy. Their portraits loaded with the expected hashes. The patch marks only the new Rat as an encounter variant. The default Rat still opens Basic Rules (2018) and uses the same thumbnail as the baseline. Selecting A Dark and Stormy Knight by its version key opens its own rules and parchment portrait.
