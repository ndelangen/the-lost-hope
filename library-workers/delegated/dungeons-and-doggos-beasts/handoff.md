# Dungeons & Doggos beasts

The bundle adds four Black Flag Beast profiles from Brian Suskind's May 6 and July 10, 2026 articles for Kobold Press. Each entry links to its own section of the original article. The sources print challenge ratings and ability modifiers, with no XP or ability scores. [Dwarf dogs](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-dig-in-the-dirt-with-a-dwarfs-best-friends-may-6-2026/) · [Halfling and gnome dogs](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-loyal-dogs-of-the-halflings-and-gnomes-july-10-2026/) · [Independent source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

| Beast | CR | Printed movement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kamrat | 1/2 | Walk 30 ft. |
| Olhund | 1 | Walk 60 ft. |
| Storgin | 1/4 | Walk 40 ft., climb 15 ft. |
| Pennig | 1/2 | Walk 50 ft. |

The Kamrat's 5-ft. burrow speed is available only through the Digger variant, which replaces Reflexive Escape. The Olhund keeps its printed 60-ft. speed. For its single optional variant, the GM chooses whether to remove Smashing Paw or Intimidating Rumble. The article leaves the resulting Multiattack unspecified when Paw is removed and leaves Protective Strike's distance reference implicit. The Pennig's alternate AC 15 applies with scale mail barding.
