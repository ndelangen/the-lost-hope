# Dungeons & Doggos source review

Brian Suskind wrote both Kobold Press articles, dated May 6 and July 10, 2026. The stat blocks use the Black Flag Roleplaying format, with signed ability modifiers and separate Perception and Stealth values. Record those values as given. Both articles use the same Great Dane header photograph and give no photographer credit or breed-specific illustration. [May article](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-dig-in-the-dirt-with-a-dwarfs-best-friends-may-6-2026/) · [July article](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-loyal-dogs-of-the-halflings-and-gnomes-july-10-2026/)

## Kamrat

Tiny Beast, CR 1/2. AC 13 (natural armor), HP 23, speed 30 ft., Perception 12, Stealth 15, blindsense 40 ft., no languages. STR −1, DEX +5, CON +2, INT −4, WIS +2, CHA −1. Heightened Hearing sets Perception to 17 for hearing. Bite is +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target, 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing. Reflexive Escape lets it move up to 5 ft. as a reaction when attacked by a creature it can see. The attack misses if that movement takes it out of reach. [Kamrat stat block](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-dig-in-the-dirt-with-a-dwarfs-best-friends-may-6-2026/#h-kamrat-cr-1-2)

A variant replaces Reflexive Escape with exactly one of the following. Dedicated Target uses a bonus action to select a visible creature and gives advantage on attacks and checks to track or follow it while seen and for three hours afterward. Frenetic gives a bonus-action Disengage or Hide. Digger grants a 5-ft. burrow speed. The prose describes a long, low dog with large forepaws, pointed ears, a smooth brown, black, or gray-blue coat, and echo-sensing chuffs used in mines and tunnels. [Kamrat description and variants](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-dig-in-the-dirt-with-a-dwarfs-best-friends-may-6-2026/#h-kamrat-tunnel-companions)

## Olhund

Medium Beast, CR 1. AC 14 (natural armor), HP 40, speed 60 ft., Perception 11, Stealth 16, immune to frightened. No special senses or languages. STR +4, DEX +2, CON +4, INT −3, WIS +1, CHA −2. Heightened Hearing and Smell sets Perception to 16 for either sense. The prose describes a slow gait as its typical trained behavior. The 60-ft. speed is the printed combat value. [Olhund description and stat block](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-dig-in-the-dirt-with-a-dwarfs-best-friends-may-6-2026/#h-olhund-guardian-ale-hound)

Multiattack uses Smashing Paw and Bite once each. Both attacks are +5 to hit with reach 5 ft. against one target. Bite deals 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing. Paw deals 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning and knocks a Large or smaller creature prone on a failed DC 14 STR save. The hound can opt to knock a creature unconscious when it would kill it. Intimidating Rumble is a bonus action (recharge 6) against one visible creature within 30 ft. It frightens on a failed DC 13 CHA save for up to 1 minute, with new saves at the ends of the target’s turns. [Olhund stat block](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-dig-in-the-dirt-with-a-dwarfs-best-friends-may-6-2026/#h-olhund-cr-1)

An olhund can take one variant in exchange for either Paw or Rumble, selected by the GM. Removing Paw leaves a gap in the published Multiattack instruction. The article supplies no replacement attack. Den Tactics grants advantage on attacks and ability checks within its chosen den. A new den can be accepted through the PG2 Creature Training downtime activity or chosen by the hound. Grappling Charge requires a straight approach of at least 20 ft. and a Bite or Paw hit on the same turn. On a failed DC 14 STR save, the target is prone and grappled (escape DC 14, one grapple at a time). Protective Strike lets the olhund make a reaction Bite against an attacker that hits a target the olhund can see. The article describes the attacker as “within 5 feet of it” and leaves the pronoun’s referent implicit. The breed is a heavy, floppy-eared, loose-skinned tavern guardian with a thick brown, black, or reddish brindled coat. [Olhund description and variants](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-dig-in-the-dirt-with-a-dwarfs-best-friends-may-6-2026/#h-olhund-guardian-ale-hound)

## Storgin

Medium Beast, CR 1/4. AC 12 (natural armor), HP 15, speed 40 ft., climb 15 ft., Perception 13, Stealth 12. No special senses or languages. STR +2, DEX +2, CON +2, INT −4, WIS +1, CHA −2. Beast of Burden treats it as Large for carrying capacity. Heightened Hearing and Smell sets Perception to 18 for either sense. In sunlight it has disadvantage on attacks and Perception 8 by sight. The gnomish tunnel hound has a printed climb speed. Its stone-eating name comes from a legend. [Storgin description and stat block](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-loyal-dogs-of-the-halflings-and-gnomes-july-10-2026/#h-storgin-stone-eaters)

Bite is +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target, 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing. A Medium or smaller creature hit is grappled (escape DC 12). Bite is unavailable against other targets while that grapple lasts. Crushing Jaws reacts only when the grappled creature hits the storgin, dealing 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing to that creature. The prose describes a short-legged, deep-chested dog with a wiry gray, brown, or black coat. It hunts vermin, scouts, carries packs, and hauls ore wagons. [Storgin stat block](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-loyal-dogs-of-the-halflings-and-gnomes-july-10-2026/#h-storgin-cr-1-4)

## Pennig

Medium Beast, CR 1/2. AC 12, or 15 with scale mail barding. HP 22, speed 50 ft., Perception 11, Stealth 12. No special senses or languages. STR +3, DEX +2, CON +2, INT −3, WIS +1, CHA −2. Barding is optional. The article gives a conditional AC and specifies no speed adjustment. Heightened Hearing and Smell sets Perception to 16 for either sense. Pack Tactics gives advantage on attacks against a creature if a non-incapacitated ally is within 5 ft. of that creature. [Pennig stat block](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-loyal-dogs-of-the-halflings-and-gnomes-july-10-2026/#h-pennig-cr-1-2)

Bite is +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target, 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing. A creature hit must make a DC 13 STR save or fall prone. Trained Shift is a reaction granting the rider advantage on the check or save against a dismount or knockdown effect. The prose describes a rangy, long-legged halfling riding hound with a broad back, dense double coat, long rough muzzle, and curled or plumed tail. Coats may be gray and white, red and white, or sable. [Pennig description and stat block](https://koboldpress.com/dungeons-doggos-loyal-dogs-of-the-halflings-and-gnomes-july-10-2026/#h-pennig-riding-hound)
