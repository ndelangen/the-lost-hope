# AD&D 2nd Edition Monstrous Manual shark source check

TSR’s *Monstrous Manual*, product 2140, was first published in 1993. The inspected physical-book scan identifies itself as the January 1994 third printing. The Fish table and general swallow rules are on printed p. 117 (viewer page 120). The Shark and Giant Shark prose and shared art are on printed p. 118 (viewer page 121). ## Shark

3–12 appearing, AC 6, Sw 24, one attack and average morale (10). The table supplies these branches.

| HD | THAC0 | Bite damage | XP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 | 17 | 2–5 | 65 |
| 4 | 17 | 2–5 | 120 |
| 5 | 15 | 2–8 | 175 |
| 6 | 15 | 2–8 | 270 |
| 7 | 13 | 3–12 | 420 |
| 8 | 13 | 3–12 | 650 |

The game entry says a shark can detect the scent of blood a mile away. Blood and the kill send sharks into a feeding frenzy. Sharks approach, bite and withdraw, allowing up to ten ordinary sharks to attack one human-sized opponent. The prose gives no frenzy attack bonus, extra attacks or rule to swallow a target.

## Giant Shark

1–3 appearing, AC 5, Sw 18, one attack and steady morale (11). The prose calls these giant sharks megalodons.

| HD | THAC0 | Bite damage | XP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 11 | 4–16 | 2,000 |
| 11 | 9 | 4–16 | 3,000 |
| 12 | 9 | 5–20 | 5,000 |
| 13 | 7 | 5–20 | 6,000 |
| 14 | 7 | 6–24 | 7,000 |
| 15 | 5 | 6–24 | 8,000 |

The Giant Shark never enters the ordinary Shark’s frenzy. It swallows most creatures whole on an attack roll four greater than the minimum number needed to hit that target. This measures the hit margin. The text supplies no exact target-size cutoff, special save or fixed die result.

The general Fish rule on p. 117 supplies the consequences. A swallowed target takes normal bite damage, then 1 point of digestive damage per round plus a cumulative 5% chance of suffocating each round. The target can cut free with a sharp-edged weapon, and others may cut or tear it free from outside. The fish’s loss of 50% of its hit points frees the target. The Giant Shark subsection supplies the trigger and does not override those consequences.

## Habitat, illustration and related sources

The compact Fish table does not give fixed HP, size categories or dimensions, alignment, intelligence, activity, frequency, treasure, magic resistance or a precise habitat. Printed p. 378 places ordinary Shark in salt-water Monster Summoning IV (3–5 HD), V (5–6 HD), and VI (7–8 HD), and Giant Shark in VIII. The appendix corroborates a broad salt-water association for both forms. It gives no geographic range, normal depth or habitat restrictions. The unlabeled p. 118 illustration depicts an upswept shark with a slate-blue back, pale underside, gill slits and pointed teeth. It establishes a painted family appearance, with no size or label distinguishing either shark and no visible signature to support an individual artist attribution. [Printed family art](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/981f6c8811613d3c1f2b6bf18210924c.webp) · [Volume credits](https://online.anyflip.com/vjcbu/qkrw/files/large/efaf128f9ae2ed636f64e32847d3e254.webp)

In *Corsairs of the Great Sea* (TSR 9449, 1994), Adventure Book pp. 51–52 describe six 7 HD Sharks and two 11 HD Giant Sharks at Terrapin Isle. Ordinary sharks have 40 hp each, 3d4 bite damage, Large size (12 ft.) and 420 XP each. Giant sharks have 68 hp each, 4d4 bite damage, Gargantuan size (30 ft.) and 3,000 XP each. Both are neutral with Non- intelligence. The 7 and 11 HD branches have compatible AC, speed, THAC0, damage ranges and morale with the Manual. The adventure Giant Shark likewise swallows at a hit margin of four, but a swallowed target takes 20 per round, attacks inside at a cumulative −1 per round and escapes by killing the shark. Those procedures and the fixed dimensions belong to the adventure profiles. [Corsairs source notes]()

No exact TSR 2140 records match the primary index. Separately staged profiles include both *Corsairs* sharks and Black Flag v0.2 Giant Shark, while the main catalog has 5e Giant Shark and the v3.5 SRD’s differently named Huge Shark.
