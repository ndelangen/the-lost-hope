# AD&D 2e Monstrous Manual sharks

The Shark and Giant Shark, called megalodon in the text, come from the Fish entry on printed pp. 117–118 of the January 1994 third printing of TSR's *Monstrous Manual*. Each profile has six Hit Dice variants with its own THAC0, bite damage, and XP. The Fish entry gives no individual size or depth bands. The Salt Water summoning tables on p. 378 list both forms. | Profile | Hit Dice | Printed combat rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shark | 3–8 | Detects the scent of blood up to a mile away and can enter a feeding frenzy. Up to ten sharks can take turns biting a man-sized target. |
| Giant Shark | 10–15 | Swallows most creatures on an attack roll four above the minimum needed to hit. The shared Fish rules give initial bite damage, 1 damage per round, cumulative 5% suffocation per round, and release when the shark has lost half its HP. |

*Corsairs of the Great Sea* remains a separate adventure version for each shark. It gives a 7 HD Shark with 40 HP and an 11 HD Giant Shark with 68 HP. Its Giant Shark uses 20 damage per swallowed round and an escape condition tied to killing the shark. The standalone patches share the ordinary Shark's insertion point in the TSV. A temporary copy with both publication rows verified that the handbook and adventure remain separately selectable. [Corsairs source evidence](../al-qadim-corsairs-beasts/source-evidence.json)

The two plain parchment portraits show the full fins and tail. The ordinary shark is lean and narrow-snouted. The Giant has a broader head and deeper body. The Fish illustration is unsigned and unlabeled, so these proportions and color choices belong to the new portraits.
