# Kuo-toa encounters

Both NPCs breathe air and water, swim at 50 ft., and have darkvision to 60 ft. Their Keen Sight can reveal moving invisible or ethereal targets. Sudden bright light blinds them for one round. They take −1 on attack rolls, saves, and checks while the bright light lasts.

## Lilli Seachild, shore warden

**Fighter 1 with 2 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 3.** AC 21, HP 28, initiative +5. Fortitude +5, Reflex +4, Will +5. Shortspear +7 for 1d6+3 piercing.

Lilli holds the landing with a freshly coated shield. While the shield is coated, an enemy who misses her in melee makes a DC 14 Reflex save. On a failure, its weapon sticks to the shield or, if it used a natural weapon, the attacker becomes grappled. A successful catch uses up the coating. A DC 20 Strength check frees a stuck weapon or limb.

If suddenly exposed to daylight, she tries to retreat toward the submerged passage. In sustained bright light her shortspear drops to +6, Search drops to +9, and her saves become Fortitude +4, Reflex +3, and Will +4.

## Rripp Siltwatch, sluice scout

**Rogue 1 with 2 racial Hit Dice, provisional CR 3.** AC 21, HP 23, initiative +7. Fortitude +2, Reflex +8, Will +6. Rapier +5 for 1d6+1 piercing. Sneak Attack adds 1d6 when eligible.

Rripp watches for a moving invisible scout from below the sluice. The scout can hide from Keen Sight by keeping still. Rripp takes 10 with Swim +12 for a result of 22 when crossing the current. If caught in a snare, he uses Escape Artist +16. Webs do not impede him.
