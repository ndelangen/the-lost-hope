# Kuo-toa rules sources

- *Underdark* gives the playable Kuo-toa two racial Hit Dice and LA +3, so ECL starts at 5. The core entry confirms the Aquatic subtype. The Monstrous Humanoid type supplies 60-ft. darkvision. Amphibious lets Kuo-toa breathe air and water indefinitely. Their swim speed is 50 ft. [Racial traits, pp. 16–17]() · [Monster Manual, pp. 163–164]()
- Keen Sight can reveal a moving invisible or ethereal creature or object. A perfectly still target can escape detection. The rules give no special range for Keen Sight or ability to strike an ethereal target. Light Blindness blinds for one round on sudden exposure, then imposes −1 on attacks, saves, and checks while the bright light lasts. The core monster uses the narrower dazzled condition after the first round. [Racial traits, p. 16]() · [Monster Manual, pp. 163–164]()
- A coated shield can catch one weapon or limb. The playable save DC is 11 + Constitution modifier. Cooperative Lightning Bolt requires at least two Kuo-toa clerics, and its Reflex save DC is 11 + the highest participating Wisdom modifier + the number of whips. Both formulas come from the racial traits. [Racial traits, p. 16]()
- The racial and regional language lists differ. Both original builds use the racial list and select Common with an Intelligence bonus. The Wizards archive has no *Underdark* errata file, and the June 2008 FAQ gives no relevant correction. [Racial languages, p. 17]() · [Kuo-Toa region, p. 23]() · [Collected errata](https://media.wizards.com/2014/downloads/dnd/3ECollectedErrata.ZIP)

Lilli’s fine scales, short rounded torso, broad hands, and flipper-like feet follow the written description. Vance Kovacs illustrated the Kuo-toa in the original book. Both staged CRs are provisional encounter estimates. [Description and illustration, p. 15]()
