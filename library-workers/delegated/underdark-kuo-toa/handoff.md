# Kuo-toa

This batch adds the playable Kuo-toa from the October 2003 *Underdark*. Lilli Seachild and Rripp Siltwatch are original NPCs. Lilli’s plain-parchment portrait represents the 3.5 species profile.

The [rules patch](rules.patch) adds the species and the two encounters. The [portrait index preview](portrait-index-preview.json) identifies the [square original](portraits/kuo-toa-draft.png). The [style comparison](portrait-comparison.jpg) shows Lilli beside Big Linda.

In a temporary copy, search found the species by name, Keen Sight, Adhesive, and both NPC roles. The portrait loaded for the 2003 profile and as the species thumbnail. The existing monster entry, including its versions and portraits, matched the baseline. [Verification](verification.json)
