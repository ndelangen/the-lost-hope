## Advanced Huge Monstrous Crab

- Area 17, Ledge, starts on p. 20. The full adventure stat block is on p. 21. It is CR 8, neutral, Huge vermin (aquatic). Initiative +0. Darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent, Listen +0, Spot +4. AC 22, touch 8, flat-footed 22, 107 hp (16 HD), immune to mind-affecting effects, Fort +12, Ref +5, Will +5.
- Speed 30 ft. (six squares). The block prints no separate swim speed. One claw or two claws, each +18 melee for 2d6+8. Space and reach 15 ft., base attack +12, grapple +32. Str 26, Dex 11, Con 14, Int —, Wis 11, Cha 2. Hide −4, Spot +4 and bonus Toughness.
- A claw hit allows improved grab as a free action without provoking attacks of opportunity. Winning the grapple establishes a hold and allows constrict. A successful grapple check deals the block’s explicit 4d6+8 constrict damage. Amphibious lets it survive indefinitely on land. Use the specific adventure block for this crab. The encounter also points to *Stormwrack* p. 142, which presents a lower-HD CR 7 Huge Monstrous Crab.
- The crab guards Wave in its nest beneath a magical water membrane at the boiling lake. It tries to keep at least five feet from the dome walls and fights to repel intruders. Puncture, collapse and flooding are the dome’s mechanics.
- The black-and-white illustration on p. 20 is signed Erol Otus and shows a broad, jointed crab with prominent pincers facing adventurers. Pages 20–21 give no color or shell material. Wave’s history on p. 33 describes adamantine plating on the different crab Dravenda killed with the trident. [Adventure p. 20]() · [Adventure p. 21]() · [Adventure p. 33]() · [Stormwrack p. 142]()

## Large Monstrous Scorpion

- The adventure’s p. 27, area 26C, Desert Terrace, specifies three scorpions at 32 hp each and explicitly points to *Monster Manual* v.3.5 p. 287. **CR 3** applies to each individual scorpion. **EL 6** is the *Terraces of Doom* heading’s encounter-level figure per terrace. The scorpions move to surround intruders as soon as a character sets foot on the terrace. After another successful grapple check, one can pull its captured target 25 ft. away from the group as a standard action. They fight to the death. The scene describes a dry ledge covered by a thin layer of sand with scattered bones. The illustration on this page does not depict the scorpions. [Adventure p. 27]()
- **Linked base rules.** *Monster Manual* printed/p. 287 gives Large vermin, 5d8+10 (32 hp), initiative +0, speed 50 ft., AC 16 (−1 size, +7 natural), touch 9, flat-footed 16, base attack +3, grapple +11, single claw +6 melee (1d6+4), full attack two claws +6 melee (1d6+4 each) and sting +1 melee (1d6+2 plus poison), space 10 ft., reach 5 ft., constrict 1d6+4, improved grab, poison, darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft. and vermin traits. Fort +6, Ref +1, Will +1. Str 19, Dex 10, Con 14, Int —, Wis 10, Cha 2. Climb +8, Hide +0, Spot +4, no feats. Warm deserts, solitary or colony (2–5), 1/10 coins and 50% each goods and items, advancement 6–9 HD (Large), no level adjustment. The Large column omits an alignment row, as does the original SRD RTF. The “Always neutral” line directly above belongs to the Tiny, Small and Medium table.
- **Linked abilities.** A claw hit enables improved grab, using Strength or Dexterity for its grapple check, whichever is better. Constrict deals automatic claw damage on a successful grapple check. The p. 288 poison table gives the Large form Fortitude DC 14 and 1d4 Constitution damage both initially and secondarily. The family has +4 racial bonuses on Climb, Hide and Spot checks. The adventure adds the movement procedure during a grapple and leaves the base attack and poison rules in place.
