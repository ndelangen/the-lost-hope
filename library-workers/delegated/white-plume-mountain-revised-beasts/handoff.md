# White Plume Mountain (Revised) beasts

Two creature profiles from the 2005 D&D 3.5 revision of *White Plume Mountain*. The in the archived Wizards ZIP matches the local reference byte for byte. Both creatures have the original 3.5 Vermin type and are grouped with beasts in the library. [Archived publisher download](https://web.archive.org/web/20090603202048id_/http://www.wizards.com/dnd/files/WhitePlumeMountainRevised.zip) · [Source notes](independent-source-notes.md) · [Rules patch](rules.patch)

| Creature | Rules source | Encounter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advanced Huge Monstrous Crab | Full CR 8 block on adventure p. 21 | Area 17, pp. 20–21. One 107 HP guardian that tries to stay at least 5 feet from the membrane walls. |
| Large Monstrous Scorpion | CR 3 base profile from the 3.5 *Monster Manual*, p. 287, cross-checked against the SRD | Area 26C, adventure p. 27. Three scorpions with 32 HP each in an EL 6 encounter. |

The crab keeps its printed 4d6+8 constrict damage, amphibious trait, and 30-foot movement rate. The scorpions retain the referenced base poison and grapple rules. Their tactics come from the terrace encounter, including pulling a held character 25 feet as a standard action after another successful grapple check. [Page references and exact file hashes](source-evidence.json)

Erol Otus signed the original crab scene on p. 20. The adamantine-plated crab in Wave's history on p. 33 is another creature. The two plain parchment portraits are newly generated, with wider margins around the claws and tail. The scorpion's amber-brown coloring is an illustration choice. The loader displayed the printed crab values, the scorpion's base stats, and the area's EL separately from each scorpion's CR.
