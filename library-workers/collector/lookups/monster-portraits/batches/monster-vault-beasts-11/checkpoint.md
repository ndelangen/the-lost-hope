# Monster Vault beasts, eleventh batch

Hunter Shark, Reef Shark, Constrictor Snake, and Flying Snake have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits based on their Kobold Press tokens. The Hunter has a longer blue-gray profile, and the Reef Shark has a compact profile with dark fin tips. The Flying Snake's detached tail segment was removed from the final illustration. The first draft and corrected draft are both backed up. Each gallery has its Kobold Press art and a related D&D illustration.

Chromium verified all four galleries and eight alternates. All fourteen backed-up raster files, including the two draft copies, have verified checksums. All four R2 originals match their lock hashes.

178 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 10 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. Giant Constrictor Snake, Giant Poisonous Snake, Poisonous Snake, and Swarm of Poisonous Snakes are next, saved in next-beast-batch.json.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `43b1748` records the registry and lockfile.
