# Monster Vault beasts, first batch

Giant Ape, Giant Badger, and Giant Bat have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits based on their matching Kobold Press Foundry tokens. The two Swarm of Bats entries share the previously generated square WebP, now registered and locked in R2. Each of the four Monster Vault galleries shows the Kobold Press token and a credited D&D alternate.

Chromium verified the four galleries and eight alternates. The three new PNGs, shared WebP, and eight alternates have checksum-verified copies outside the worktree. All four R2 originals match the lock hashes.

218 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 50 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. The next four beasts are Black Bear, Brown Bear, Polar Bear, and Giant Boar, saved in next-beast-batch.json. Check their publication equivalents and tokens for compatible art.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `f7390da` records the registry and lockfile.
