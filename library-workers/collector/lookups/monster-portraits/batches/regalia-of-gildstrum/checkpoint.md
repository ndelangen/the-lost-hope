# Regalia of Gildstrum portrait checkpoint

Spark Dog and Tuberkith have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits. The Spark Dog is an original interpretation based on the Kobold Press catalog, and its local alternate is credited Blink Dog art. The Tuberkith is based on William O'Brien's published illustration, which also appears in the local gallery.

Chromium verified both galleries. Both originals are synced to R2, and all four images have verified backups outside the worktree. The media gate, 887 tests, and production build passed.

Local commit `0d90763` records the registry and lockfile.
