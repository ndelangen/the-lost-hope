# Monster Vault beasts, sixth batch

Blood Hawk, Draft Horse, Riding Horse, and War Horse have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits. The Blood Hawk follows the Kobold Press token's red plumage. The horses show three distinct roles through their builds and tack. Each gallery has matching Kobold Press Foundry artwork and a credited D&D alternate.

Chromium verified all four galleries and eight alternates. All twelve images have checksum-verified backups outside the worktree. All four R2 originals match their lock hashes.

198 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 30 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. Giant Hyena, Giant Centipede, Giant Fire Beetle, and Giant Scorpion are next, saved in next-beast-batch.json.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `60597ac` records the registry and lockfile.
