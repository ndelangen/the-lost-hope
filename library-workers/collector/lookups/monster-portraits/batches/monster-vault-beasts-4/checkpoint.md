# Monster Vault beasts, fourth batch

Velociraptor and Giant Eagle have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits based on their Kobold Press tokens. Triceratops and Tyrannosaurus Rex share their existing square parchment illustrations with the matching D&D monster entries. Each gallery has its Kobold Press token and a related D&D illustration or token. The Giant Eagle also has its earlier Lost Hope mountain illustration as an alternate.

Chromium verified all four galleries and nine alternates. All thirteen images have checksum-verified backups outside the worktree. The two new portraits and two preserved WebPs match their R2 lock hashes.

206 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 38 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. Giant Elk, Giant Frog, Giant Poisonous Frog, and Giant Goat are the next Monster Vault beasts, saved in next-beast-batch.json.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `5cd3d97` records the registry and lockfile.
