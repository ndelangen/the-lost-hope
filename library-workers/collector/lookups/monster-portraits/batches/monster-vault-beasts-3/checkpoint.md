# Monster Vault beasts, third batch

Giant Crab and Giant Crocodile have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits based on their Kobold Press tokens. Ankylosaurus and Plesiosaurus share their existing square parchment illustrations with the matching D&D monster entries. Each gallery has two credited alternates. The Ankylosaurus alternates include Emily Willoughby's original life reconstruction under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Chromium verified all four galleries and eight alternates. All twelve images have checksum-verified backups outside the worktree. The two new portraits and two preserved WebPs match their R2 lock hashes.

210 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 42 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. Triceratops, Tyrannosaurus Rex, Velociraptor, and Giant Eagle are the next Monster Vault beasts, saved in next-beast-batch.json.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `a2ba378` records the registry and lockfile.
