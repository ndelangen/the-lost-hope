# Lost Mine of Phandelver portrait checkpoint

Brughor Axe-Biter, King Grol, Lhupo, Targor Bloodsword, Yeemik, and Yegg now have original 1254 × 1254 parchment portraits made with built-in ImageGen. The portraits follow their descriptions in the 2014 adventure. Each gallery includes a labeled illustration of its base creature as an alternate. The six generated originals and four distinct source images were backed up and checked byte for byte.

Chromium verified all six portraits and alternates. The R2 sync uploaded the six original PNGs, and the media gate confirmed 283 originals in total. The contact sheet and King Grol's before-and-after screenshots are in the local review directory.

There are 151 monster entries without local art. Domains of Dread, Monsters of Drakkenheim, Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse, and Storm King's Thunder each have six. Fetch origin main, verify the worktree base, and refresh the queue before continuing.
