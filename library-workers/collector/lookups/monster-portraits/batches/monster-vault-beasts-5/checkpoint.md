# Monster Vault beasts, fifth batch

Giant Elk, Giant Frog, Giant Poisonous Frog, and Giant Goat have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits. The two frogs share the Kobold Press token as an alternate. The poisonous frog's distinct green-and-black markings are an original interpretation inspired by Hans Hillewaert's poison dart frog photograph. Each gallery has two credited alternates.

Chromium verified four galleries and eight alternate placements. The four portraits and seven distinct alternate files have checksum-verified backups outside the worktree. All four R2 originals match their lock hashes.

202 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 34 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. Blood Hawk, Draft Horse, Riding Horse, and War Horse are next, saved in next-beast-batch.json.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `4f28d65` records the registry and lockfile.
