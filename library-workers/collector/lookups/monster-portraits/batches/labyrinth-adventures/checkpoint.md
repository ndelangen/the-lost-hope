# Labyrinth Adventures portrait checkpoint

The Clockwork Cadaver, Controller, Parasite, and Queen have original 1254 × 1254 parchment portraits based on the adventure's descriptions. I found no matching source portraits in the material I could verify.

Chromium verified all four galleries. The four PNGs are backed up outside the worktree and synced to R2. The media gate verified all 359 registered originals. All 887 tests and the production build passed.

Local commit `d38975a` records the registry and lockfile.

There are 75 monster entries without local art. The Sunken Pyramid has four outstanding entries and is the largest remaining group.
