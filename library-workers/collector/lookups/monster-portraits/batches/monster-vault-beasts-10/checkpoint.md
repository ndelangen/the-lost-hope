# Monster Vault beasts, tenth batch

Giant Seahorse and Giant Shark have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits based on their Kobold Press tokens. Swarm of Rats and Swarm of Ravens share their existing square parchment illustrations with the matching D&D monster entries. Each gallery has its Kobold Press artwork and a related D&D illustration.

Chromium verified all four galleries and eight alternates. All twelve images have checksum-verified backups outside the worktree. Both new portraits and both preserved swarm WebPs match their R2 lock hashes.

182 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 14 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. Hunter Shark, Reef Shark, Constrictor Snake, and Flying Snake are next, saved in next-beast-batch.json.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `f578718` records the registry and lockfile.
