# Beast and monster artwork

The missing-art queue is empty. The catalog has artwork for all 574 beasts and 5,932 monsters. Of those, 1,661 entries have generated portraits and 4,845 have source artwork.

The final three batches covered 50, 50 and 24 beast entries in commits `47b0414`, `f311218` and `83805dd`. They added 119 new originals, reused five portraits for equivalent entries, and preserved 204 credited alternate placements. External backups hold the new originals and earlier drafts alongside copies of the alternate art.

Use the wyverns and ice cat from the final batch as references for muted gray-tan paper. Seven drafts in batch 2 were revised to soften orange backgrounds. The before-and-after comparisons are in the local review folder.
