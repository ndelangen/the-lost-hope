# Revised v3.5 SRD beasts, third batch

Dire Rat, Dire Shark, Dire Tiger, and Dire Weasel have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits. The Dire Weasel uses a lower stalking pose to distinguish it from the Giant Weasel. Both drafts are backed up.

Chromium verified four galleries and eight credited alternates. The four originals, eight source files, and two drafts have checksum-verified external backups. All four originals match their R2 locks.

154 beasts still lack local art, including 30 with a Revised v3.5 SRD version. All 5,932 monster entries have artwork. Dire Wolverine, Dog, Donkey, and Elasmosaurus are next.

All 887 tests, the production build, and the media gate passed. Local commit `178e9f2` records the image registry and R2 lock.
