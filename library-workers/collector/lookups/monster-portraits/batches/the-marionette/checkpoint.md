# The Marionette portrait checkpoint

Isabella’s ghost and the Toy Soldier have original 1254 × 1254 parchment portraits. Isabella appears in her white dress with a doll. The soldier salutes beside its controller. I found no source portraits of these exact entries. Each gallery includes two labeled alternates depicting related creatures.

Chromium verified both galleries and all four alternates. All six images are backed up outside the worktree. Both originals are synced to R2, and the media gate verified all 376 registered originals. All 887 tests and the production build passed.

Local commit `daf7224` records the artwork registry and lockfile.

There are 58 monster entries without local art. The Innocent comes first among the largest groups, with two outstanding portraits.
