# Monster Vault beasts, thirteenth batch

Giant Spider, Giant Wolf Spider, Saber-Toothed Tiger, and Giant Vulture have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits. The two spiders share a Kobold Press token. The related D&D illustrations guided their distinct shapes. The Giant Spider has long legs and a round purple abdomen, and the Giant Wolf Spider sits low on stockier, hairy legs. Its revised portrait shows the hind leg that was hidden in the first draft.

Each gallery has two credited source alternates. Chromium verified all four galleries and eight alternate placements. Thirteen backed-up files match their checksums, including both wolf spider drafts. The four installed originals match their R2 locks.

170 beast entries still lack local artwork. Giant Weasel and Dire Wolf are the final two Monster Vault entries in the queue. All 5,932 monster entries have local art.

All 887 tests, the production build, and the media gate passed. Local commit `02d23b2` records the registry and R2 lock.
