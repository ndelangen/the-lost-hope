# Monster Vault beasts, eighth batch

Scorpion and Giant Lizard have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits. Giant Wasp and Swarm of Insects share their existing square parchment art with the matching D&D monster entries. The smaller tawny scorpion has its own illustration, separate from the dark Giant Scorpion. Each gallery has its Kobold Press artwork and a related D&D illustration.

Chromium verified all four galleries and eight alternates. All twelve images have checksum-verified backups outside the worktree. The two new portraits and two preserved WebPs match their R2 lock hashes.

190 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 22 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. Giant Octopus, Giant Owl, Swarm of Quippers, and Giant Rat are next, saved in next-beast-batch.json.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `a67d51d` records the registry and lockfile.
