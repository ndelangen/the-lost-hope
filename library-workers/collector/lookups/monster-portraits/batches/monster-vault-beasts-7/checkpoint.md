# Monster Vault beasts, seventh batch

Giant Hyena, Giant Centipede, and Giant Scorpion have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits based on their Kobold Press tokens. The Giant Fire Beetle shares its existing square parchment illustration with the matching D&D monster entry. Each gallery has its Kobold Press artwork and a related D&D illustration.

Chromium verified all four galleries and eight alternates. All twelve images have checksum-verified backups outside the worktree. The three new portraits and preserved Beetle WebP match their R2 lock hashes.

194 beast entries still lack local artwork, including 26 from Kobold Press's Monster Vault. All 5,932 monster entries have local art. Giant Wasp, Scorpion, Swarm of Insects, and Giant Lizard are next, saved in next-beast-batch.json.

The media gate, all 887 tests, and the production build passed. Local commit `5c399c3` records the registry and lockfile.
