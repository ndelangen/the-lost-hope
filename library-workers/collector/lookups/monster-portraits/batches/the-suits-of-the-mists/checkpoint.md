# The Suits of the Mists portrait checkpoint

The Elf Scout and Mist Zombie (Lesser Strahd Zombie) have original 1254 × 1254 parchment portraits. The scout depicts a Greenhall archer with a purple wooden pendant. Each entry has two labeled alternates from the related Scout or Strahd Zombie source.

Chromium verified both galleries and all four alternates. The six image files are backed up outside the worktree. Both originals are synced to R2, and the media gate verified all 374 registered originals. All 887 tests and the production build passed.

Local commit `7166b78` records the artwork registry and lockfile.

There are 60 monster entries without local art. The Marionette comes first among the largest remaining groups, with two outstanding portraits.
