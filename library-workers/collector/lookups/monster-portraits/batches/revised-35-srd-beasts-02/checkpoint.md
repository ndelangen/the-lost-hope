# Revised v3.5 SRD beasts, second batch

Dire Bat, Dire Bear, Dire Boar, and Dire Lion have new 1254 × 1254 square parchment portraits. Sam Wood's Dire Bear illustration from the Wizards Monster Manual v.3.5 gallery guided the new bear portrait and appears as a credited alternate in both publication versions.

Chromium verified four galleries and ten alternate placements across five publication versions. The four originals and eight distinct source files have checksum-verified external backups. All four originals match their R2 locks.

158 beast entries still lack local art, including 34 with a Revised v3.5 SRD version. All 5,932 monster entries have artwork. Dire Rat, Dire Shark, Dire Tiger, and Dire Weasel are next.

All 887 tests, the production build, and the media gate passed. Local commit `bce787a` records the image registry and R2 lock.
