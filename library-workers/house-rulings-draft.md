# Shop items: house rulings (approved by Norbert on 2026-11-26, now in the library)

Most of the 599 shop items with no printed rules are already play-ready: their one-line summary
says what they do in the fiction, and none of them needs a roll. Ninety-three items now carry
the rules the SRD 5.2.1 prints for them. The items below are the few whose effect will come up as
a question at the table ("how far does it carry?", "can it be resisted?"). Norbert approved all
ten; each item's page shows its ruling under "House rule", so it is never mistaken for printed
rules. Change a ruling in the catalog entry, and here.

| Item | Summary today | Proposed house rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thunderstone (40 gp) | A single-use signal stone that cracks with a thunderclap if dropped. | Dropping or throwing it (range 20/60 ft.) is a Utilize action. The crack is audible 600 feet away. Creatures within 10 feet make a DC 10 Constitution save or have the Deafened condition until the end of their next turn. The stone is spent. |
| Drowsy shield (80 gp) | Softens sound when set upright between a sleeper and a doorway. | While it stands upright, creatures behind it have Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing to notice sounds from the far side, and sounds from behind it don't wake a sleeper. |
| Nightmare hook (100 gp) | Pulls a sleeping creature out of a nightmare without waking others nearby. | A Utilize action on a sleeping creature within reach ends any effect that is giving it a nightmare (including the Dream spell's nightmare) unless the effect's source succeeds on a DC 13 Charisma save. Others don't wake from it. |
| Dream admission ticket (35 gp) | Lets one willing sleeper follow a companion into the same dream. | Single use. Both sleepers must hold it as they fall asleep within 5 feet of each other. They share one dream until either wakes; neither can be harmed in it. |
| Lucid lollipop (5 gp) | A sleeper knows they are dreaming until the sugar taste fades. | Lasts 1 hour of sleep. The sleeper has Advantage on saves against effects that act through dreams. |
| Sill marker charm (21 gp) | Makes the sound of the latch if the window opens before dawn within the same building. | The holder hears it anywhere in the building, even asleep, and wakes. One window per charm; it resets each dusk. |
| Spark drops, dozen (3 gp) | Crackling lights flash at the lips. | Each drop sheds Dim Light in a 5-foot radius for 1 minute. Not bright enough to read by. |
| Sheet ghost charm (15 gp) | A harmless ghost shape hangs in the air where the charm is pinned. | The shape is an illusion that a DC 10 Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals as false. It makes no sound and can't move. |
| Scroll of a Silent Appointment (28 gp) | Two willing readers learn which appointment their shared ward admits them to. | Works only inside a warded place both readers can enter; the GM decides which of the two appointments the ward admits. Single use. |
| Sleepwalk slippers (55 gp) | Track the wearer's route in faint chalk dust until dawn. | Anyone can follow the trail without a check while it lasts; rain or sweeping erases it. |
