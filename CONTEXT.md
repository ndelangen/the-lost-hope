# The Lost Hope Campaign

The player-facing campaign archive for reconstructing established events, active objectives, and
unanswered story threads.

## Quests

**Quest**:
An umbrella record that synthesizes a story thread the party may follow. Every quest has exactly
one primary type based on the party's current objective.

**Mystery**:
A quest whose primary objective is to understand an unanswered story question.
_Avoid_: Investigation, lore quest

**Mission**:
A quest whose primary objective is to complete an explicit assignment given to the party.
_Avoid_: Task, job, commission

## Locations

**Location parent**:
The larger place that contains a location. Containment does not establish a travel route.

**Location connection**:
A known route from one location to another, such as a door, passage, or portal. A recorded route
does not establish that travel in the opposite direction is possible.

**Portal**:
A magical location connection that transports travelers to its destination.

**Location illustration**:
An artistic depiction of a place. It is decorative and does not establish campaign canon.
_Avoid_: Avatar, map

**Location icon**:
A compact symbol used to identify a place in references, directories, navigation, and map pins.
_Avoid_: Avatar, illustration

**Location map**:
A spatial diagram showing the geography or layout of a place.
_Avoid_: Illustration
