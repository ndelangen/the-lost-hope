# Campaign questions

Open questions for the author. Also used by the agent when editing data.

**Maintenance:** Remove a line when answered. Add new questions with the known context and session;
include why the answer matters when it blocks a modeling or naming decision. Record confirmed canon
in `src/data/` when answered. Do not use this file as an engineering backlog.

**Play sessions:** Sessions **1–11** are dated and have event notes.

---

## How to write a question

Every question must include **what we already know** — many sit unanswered across sessions.

```markdown
- **What did the tentacles want?**
  - _Context:_ Session 1, The Nest. Voices compelled Revin; tentacles tried to drag him out; party saved him; evil laughter; PCs slept. Light had no clue when asked. Festival nights later seemed safe.
  - _Why asking:_ Tentacle Night quest still open.
  - _Session:_ 1
```

## Sessions 5–10

- **Who is Giggles, and why did nobody in Fairhaven seem to know him?**
  - _Context:_ Giggles posted the alchemist job and delivered most of its briefing from Mortimer’s shop. When the party asked around town, nobody recognized Giggles; people instead became confused about Giggles and Gadgets. Giggles may also have known about Penelope’s supposedly unique laughing-potion formula.
  - _Why asking:_ Giggles’ identity, relationship with Mortimer, and knowledge of the stolen formula remain unexplained.
  - _Session:_ 6 ([n2-e044](/events/giggles-hires-the-party-against-a-rival-alchemist))

- **What is the personal name of the Mayor of Fairhaven?**
  - _Context:_ The mayor made the major Festival of the Heroes announcement at Fairhaven Town Square and became upset when Swift repeatedly tried to join the list of heroes.
  - _Session:_ 7 ([n2-e063](/events/swift-disrupts-the-festival-of-the-heroes-announcement))

- **What else happened in Session 7?**
  - _Context:_ Session 7 records Swift taming and refusing the broom, the Festival announcement, Fix’s rival party joining the guild under her fake tattoo, and Devan revealing Jim’s name to Fix.
  - _Session:_ 7

- **Why could Light not give Fix a normal guild tattoo?**
  - _Context:_ In Session 7, Light could not apply his usual Adventurers' Guild tattoo to Fix for an undisclosed reason, so he fabricated one. The DM has said Fix has a special connection to an unidentified organization of necromancers. Players suspect that connection explains the tattoo problem, but this is unconfirmed and the PCs do not know it. Beasts and Dwarf did not notice the deception.
  - _Why asking:_ The reason may connect Light, Fix, or the Eyeless Hand and is established as unresolved canon.
  - _Session:_ 7 ([n2-e058](/events/fix-joins-the-guild-with-her-rival-party-under-a-fake-tattoo))

- **Which campaign days contain Sessions 5–10?**
  - _Context:_ Session ownership is now corrected from dated WhatsApp recaps: Session 4 reaches Fairhaven on the current Day 12, Sessions 5–7 occupy Day 13, Session 8 is Day 14, Session 9 is provisionally Days 14–16, and Session 10 spans Days 16–17. The DM later recalled four days travelling to Fairhaven, five or six days in Fairhaven, four days fleeing, three days in Verdant Haven, three days at the mountain, one day settling in ShadowPeak, two days in ShadowPeak, and three days aboard the flying bazaar. In the same exchange he confirmed that Jim received Roberto’s map pages on the second day in Verdant Haven and then said seven days had elapsed since, which cannot be reconciled additively with all of those phase totals.
  - _Why asking:_ The event timeline requires campaign-day numbers and should be corrected if unrecorded rests occurred.
  - _Session:_ 5–10
